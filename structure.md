## Hard Constraints

- At least 1,000 words body prose per page
- No dead links — mention in plain text until the page exists
- Present multiple perspectives on contentious events
- Avoid mechanical repetition across pages
- Index pages auto-generated via `_includes/content-list.html` — do not maintain by hand
- Never address readers or reference the real world
- Medieval fantasy — not grim or bleak; honest about hardship without exaggeration
- Anomalous scripts (Han, Hangul, Hiragana/Katakana, full-width punctuation) inside English prose are copy-paste corruption — correct them
- No slang, modern idioms, or anachronisms
- The tree is extensible — new subdirectories may be added under any category
