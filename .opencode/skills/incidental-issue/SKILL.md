---
name: incidental-issue
description: When you stumble across an issue unrelated to your current task, record it silently and report it only after the main work is done
---

If during your work you notice a bug, dead link, inconsistency, style violation, or any other issue that is NOT directly related to the task you were asked to do:

1. **Do not act on it.** Do not fix it, investigate further, or open a ticket.
2. **Record it silently.** Note the file path, line number, and what the issue is — but do not mention it mid-task.
3. **Finish your assigned task first.** Complete all required work as instructed.
4. **Report at the end.** After your main task is fully complete and verified, append a section titled "Unrelated issues noticed" listing each issue with its location and a short description. Use your final message to the caller to deliver this report.

The caller may want to give specific instructions on how each issue should be handled, so do not act on them unilaterally.
