# Skill (`content-page`)

Write information-dense prose in the manner of Wikipedia: paragraphs under `##` and `###` headings, no bullet lists for body prose. Link the first mention of any existing page as `[Name](/content/category/subcategory/page)` — full path with leading slash. Never use bold, italic, or the `#` top-level heading (handled by layout).

## Frontmatter

Every content page begins with:

```yaml
---
layout: default
title: "Page Title"
description: "1–2 sentences, 120–200 characters, in-world voice, unique per page."
---
```

## Hard Constraints

- At least 1,000 words of body prose per page.
- No dead links — if a page does not yet exist, mention it in plain text until it does.
- Present multiple perspectives on contentious events.
- Avoid mechanical repetition across pages.
- Index pages are auto-generated by `_plugins/generate_category_indices.rb`; never maintain them by hand.
- Never address readers or reference the real world.
- Medieval fantasy tone — not grim or bleak; honest about hardship without exaggeration.
- No slang, modern idioms, or anachronisms.
- Prejudices are background reality, not narrative centrepieces; consolidate them on [content/culture/prejudices.md](/content/culture/prejudices).
- The content tree is extensible — new subdirectories may be added under any category.
