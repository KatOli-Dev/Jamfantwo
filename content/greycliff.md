## Overview

Greycliff is the largest fishing and whaling town on the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and it is the principal settlement of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/grey-coast-holdings). The town stands on a high cliff of grey stone that overlooks the [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean), and its harbour is tucked into a narrow inlet that cuts deep into the cliff, sheltered from the wind and the swell that batter the open coast. The town is smaller and more rugged than the cities of the eastern coast, but it is the wealthiest and most important settlement on the western shore, and its prosperity is built upon the cold, rich waters of the Greyring.

## The Harbour and the Fisheries

The harbour of Greycliff is a natural inlet that penetrates the cliff for several hundred yards, forming a deep, sheltered basin that is accessible at all states of the tide. The inlet is narrow, and the entrance is marked by lighthouses that are visible for many miles across the open ocean, for the fog that blankets the western coast can reduce visibility to almost nothing, and the harbour is one of the few safe refuges on the entire coastline. The quays line both sides of the inlet, and they are occupied by the fishing boats, the whaling vessels, and the small craft that serve the town.

The fisheries of Greycliff are the most productive on the western coast, and the cold, nutrient-rich waters of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean) yield a bounty of fish that is the foundation of the town's economy. The fishing fleet of Greycliff is the largest on the western coast, and the boats venture far from land in pursuit of the shoals that move through the deep water beyond the continental shelf. The catch is landed at the harbour, processed in the salting houses and the smokehouses that line the inlet, and exported to the inland markets and the eastern coast, where the dried and salted fish of Greycliff is a valued commodity.

Whaling is the other great industry of Greycliff, and the whaling vessels of the town are the most seaworthy and the most daring on the western coast. The great whales of the Greyring are hunted for their oil, bone, and ambergris, and the products of the hunt are exported far inland, where they command high prices. The whalers of Greycliff are among the most skilled and the most hardy mariners in the known world, and their voyages into the open ocean are the closest thing to deep-water exploration that the Greyring has seen.

## The Town

Greycliff is built of the same grey stone that forms the cliffs, and the buildings are sturdy and low, with thick walls, small windows, and steep roofs weighted with stone to resist the gales that sweep across the coast. The streets are narrow and winding, sheltered from the wind, and the buildings huddle together as if for warmth. The town is dominated by the harbour and the industries that serve it, and the salting houses, the smokehouses, and the warehouses are the largest and most substantial buildings in the settlement.

The population of Greycliff is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the town are known for their hardiness and their independence. The [cancrids](/content/cancrids) of the rocky intertidal zone are occasionally encountered at the margins of the settlement, and a small community of [goblins](/content/goblins) is present in the ruins and the caves along the cliff, where they scavenge and trade as opportunity allows.

## Significance

Greycliff is the principal settlement of the western coast of Aethelar, and its fisheries and whaling operations are the foundation of the economy of the entire western shore. The town is the largest and most prosperous member of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/grey-coast-holdings), and its harbour is the primary safe refuge for vessels on the western coast. The trade that flows through Greycliff, though small compared to that of the eastern ports, is the sole channel through which the products of the Greyring reach the markets of the interior, and the dried fish, the whale oil, and the ambergris of Greycliff are among the most valued commodities of the western trade.
