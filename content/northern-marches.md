## Overview

The Northern Marches is a frontier principality of the northern forests of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), encompassing the settled lands that lie between the ancient forests of the north and the open country of the central grasslands. The principality is the northernmost political entity of Aethelar, and its territory includes the river valleys, the forest margins, and the foothills that form the transition between the temperate north and the wilder regions beyond. The Northern Marches is a frontier realm, and its primary function is the management of the boundary between the settled land and the forest, the defence of the northern approaches, and the maintenance of the trade routes that connect the north to the rest of the continent.

## The Forest and the Frontier

The ancient forests that cover the northern reaches of Aethelar are the defining feature of the Northern Marches, and the relationship between the principality and the forest is the central concern of its governance. The [elves](/content/elves) of the deep forest are the neighbours of the principality, and the co-operation between the humans and the elves in the management of the forest is a model of the kind of accommodation that has sustained the northern frontier for centuries. The logging and the hunting that the principality conducts are managed in consultation with the elves, and the boundary between the settled land and the forest is maintained by mutual agreement.

The frontier that the Northern Marches guards is not a political boundary but a geographical one, for the forest is the home of the [elves](/content/elves) and the [ursines](/content/ursines) and the other peoples of the woodland, and the principality is the guardian of the transition between the forest and the settled land. The defence of the frontier is maintained by a standing force that is funded by the principality, and the fortifications of the northern settlements are maintained in good order.

## The Cities and the Administration

The principal city of the Northern Marches is [Thornwick](/content/thornwick), the oldest human settlement in the northern river valleys and the administrative centre of the principality. [Eldergrove](/content/eldergrove) is the other significant city of the principality, situated at the edge of the ancient forests, and its trade in timber and forest products is a valued component of the economy. [Ashford](/content/ashford) and [Fernmoor](/content/fernmoor) are the other members of the principality, and their positions at the river crossing and the forest boundary respectively give them a strategic significance within the frontier.

The principality is governed from Thornwick by a prince who is appointed by the council of the northern cities, and the administration is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the collection of taxes, the maintenance of the roads and the fortifications, and the administration of justice. The governance of the Northern Marches is oriented toward the frontier, and the administration is concerned chiefly with the management of the boundary and the defence of the northern approaches.

## Trade and the People

The trade of the Northern Marches is dominated by the products of the forest and the agriculture of the river valleys, and the timber, the game, and the grain that are produced in the principality are exported to the markets of the south through the river and the road network that connects the north to the rest of the continent. The population of the Northern Marches is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [elves](/content/elves) who are involved in the management of the forest and the trade between the principality and the elven communities of the deep woodland.

## Significance

The Northern Marches is the frontier principality of the northern forests, and its management of the boundary between the settled land and the forest is a critical function that has sustained the northern frontier for centuries. The principality's trade in timber and forest products is a significant component of the economy of Aethelar, and its co-operation with the elves of the forest is a model of the accommodation between peoples that is characteristic of the northern frontier. The significance of the Northern Marches lies in its frontier role and its capacity for co-operation with the forest peoples, and the principality is a symbol of the balance between settlement and wilderness that defines the northern reaches of the continent.
