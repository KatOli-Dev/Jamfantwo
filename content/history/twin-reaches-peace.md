## Treaty That Ended the Twin-Reaches Wars

The Twin-Reaches Peace is the treaty that concluded the [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars), signed at the [temple of the Sea Mother](/content/religion/temple-of-the-sea-mother)'s Court at [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in the early years of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale)'s reign. The peace took its name from the two parties to the conflict - the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) - and from the inland valleys that the wars had disputed. The academies record the peace as one of the most consequential instruments of the present age and the foundation of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace) that followed.

The peace was negotiated over several years through the mediators of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm). The mediators included scholars who had studied the older river agreements and the ancient accords between the cities, bringing a knowledge of traditions the parties had observed for several centuries. The negotiation is regarded as a model of patient and careful diplomacy.

## Mediators and the Transit Fee Compromise

Sera opened the negotiations soon after her accession to the throne of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and appointed the council of the Republic to act in concert with the mediators of the academies. The mediators met with the parties in the temple of the [Sea Mother's Court](/content/religion/sea-mothers-court) at [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and conducted the negotiations over several years. They travelled between the Republic and the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) to consult with the magistrates and councils of both parties and reported the state of the negotiations to Sera and to the council of the Pact. The negotiations proceeded in rounds, with each round producing a draft of a particular section of the treaty that the parties would then review.

The negotiations were complicated by each party's insistence upon the rights it claimed at the beginning of the war. The Republic insisted upon the customs dues by virtue of the ancient accord the founders of the Republic had made with the founders of the cities of the warm coast. The Pact insisted upon the right to export its goods without hindrance by virtue of the natural liberty of trade. The mediators proposed a compromise that abolished the customs dues and replaced them with a small transit fee shared between the two parties. The compromise was accepted by Sera and by the council of the Pact.

## Trade, Navigation, and Military Provisions

The Twin-Reaches Peace is a long document covering the trade, navigation, customs, and territory of the parties. It abolishes the customs dues the Republic had long claimed and replaces them with a small transit fee collected at the gates of the principal towns along the trade routes. The fee is shared between the Republic and the Pact in a proportion of two to one in favour of the Republic, as set by the mediators. The peace also establishes rules of navigation upon the rivers and seas the parties share, granting vessels of both parties equal access to the principal harbours, and rules for the military forces the parties may maintain upon the trade routes, limiting the number of armed vessels each party may keep in the waters of the inland valleys.

The peace establishes the office of the mediator, appointed by the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) to oversee the execution. The mediator inspects the trade routes at regular intervals, reports to the parties upon the state of trade and navigation, and has the power to mediate any dispute that arises between the parties and to bring them to a settlement permitted by the peace. The office of the mediator has been the foundation of the success of the peace.

## Public Ceremony at Khalisar

The peace was signed in a public ceremony at the temple of the Sea Mother's Court at [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of both parties. Sera signed for the Republic, and the head of the council of the Pact signed for the Pact. The mediators signed as witnesses, and the academies recorded the ceremony in the archives.

The ceremony was followed by a procession through the streets of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), including the magistrates and principal citizens of both parties. The citizens of Khalisar greeted the procession with great public celebration, including the singing of hymns in the temples and feasting provided by the council of Khalisar. The celebration continued for three days.

## The Mediator's First Year

The execution required several years of careful work by the magistrates and the mediator. The mediator inspected the trade routes section by section, recording the state of trade and navigation. Violators of the peace were brought before the magistrates of the nearest town, who imposed the prescribed fines. The mediator also mediated disputes between merchants and captains, bringing parties to settlements permitted by the peace. In the first year of the execution, the mediator resolved more than a dozen disputes without any party resorting to the blocking of trade routes. The mediator reported to the parties at regular intervals, and the parties reviewed the reports and revised regulations as needed. Revisions have been few and carefully considered.

## Model for Later Agreements

The Twin-Reaches Peace has been in force for several centuries and is the foundation of the public order of the inland valleys and the warm coast. It has been revised from time to time to reflect changes in trade and navigation, but revisions have been few and carefully considered. The magistrates of both parties mark the anniversaries of the signing with ceremonies.

The peace has served as a model for agreements the parties have signed with the other principal cities of the three continents, including the [Eastern Compact](/content/history/eastern-compact) with the city of [Serren](/content/location/settlement/city/serren) and the [Harbour Accord](/content/history/harbour-accord) with the ports of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). These agreements have followed the structure of the Twin-Reaches Peace, establishing similar transit fees and similar offices of mediator. The peace is the foundation of the public order of the trade routes of the present age and the measure by which the magistrates and councils of both parties judge public acts.

## Foundation of the Long Peace

The Twin-Reaches Peace is one of the most consequential instruments of the present age and the foundation of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace) for several centuries. The transit fees, navigation rules, military provisions, and the office of the mediator are managed in accordance with its terms. The magistrates of both parties mark the anniversaries of the signing with ceremonies attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every city of both parties. These ceremonies are among the most important public observances of the present age. The peace is placed in the broader chronology of the age in the [World Timeline](/content/history/world-timeline).
