## The Queen Who Ended the Wars

[Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) is recorded in the chronicles of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and every other principal city of the three continents as the queen who concluded the [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars) and began the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace). Sera was a junior magistrate of the Republic at the time of her accession and had no experience of war. She judged that the war had reached a stalemate and that the Republic could not win without incurring disproportionate costs. She opened negotiations with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) through the mediators of the academies, and the negotiations continued for several years before the peace was signed at the temple of the Sea Mother's Court at [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

The reign of Sera lasted more than thirty years, the longest of the recorded reigns of the Amurra Republic. It is remembered as a period of consolidation and rebuilding and is the foundation of the public order of the Republic for several centuries. Sera is the measure by which the magistrates and councils of the Republic judge public acts.

## From Junior Magistrate to Throne

Sera was the youngest child of the previous queen and had been educated in the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) before returning to the Republic to serve as a magistrate. She was appointed to the magistracy of the southern district and served for several years, where she gained a reputation for fair judgment in trade disputes and for careful management of the district's accounts. The death of her elder sister made her heir to the throne. The accession took place in the midst of the wars, and the chronicles of the Republic record it as a moment of great uncertainty. Sera had no military experience, and her appointment was the result of the accident of birth rather than any particular fitness for the office.

Sera quickly demonstrated a capacity for administration and a willingness to consult with the magistrates and councils the Republic had established. She appointed the most experienced magistrates to the principal offices of state, including the offices of the treasury, the harbour, and the foreign relations, and listened to their advice. She also established a regular council of advisers that met weekly to review the affairs of the Republic.

## Mediating with the Vashari Pact

The negotiations with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) were the most important undertaking of Sera's reign and occupied the first decade. Sera opened the negotiations through the mediators of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm). The mediators were scholars of the academies who brought a knowledge of the ancient accords and a reputation for impartiality trusted by both parties. The negotiations were conducted in rounds, with the mediators travelling between the Republic and the Pact to carry proposals and counter-proposals.

The negotiations were complicated by each party's insistence upon the rights it claimed at the beginning of the war. The Republic insisted upon the customs dues by virtue of the ancient accord the founders of the Republic had made with the founders of the cities of the warm coast. The Pact insisted upon the right to export its goods without hindrance by virtue of the natural liberty of trade. The mediators proposed a compromise that abolished the customs dues and replaced them with a small transit fee shared between the two parties. The compromise was accepted by Sera and by the council of the Pact. The peace was signed at the temple of the Sea Mother's Court at Khalisar and is known as the [Twin-Reaches Peace](/content/history/twin-reaches-peace).

## Restoring Fleets and Towns

The years following the peace were a period of rebuilding over which Sera presided with care and patience. The Republic rebuilt the fleets broken by [Thessa](/content/people/historical/thessa-nordhaven) and the towns damaged in the wars. Sera appointed commissioners to oversee the rebuilding, who worked in concert with the magistrates and merchants of the principal towns. The commissioners set priorities for the reconstruction, giving the first attention to the harbours and the granaries that were essential to the resumption of trade. The rebuilding was the work of two generations.

Sera also oversaw the construction of the roads and bridges that the [Laying the Stone Roads](/content/history/laying-the-stone-roads) required and established the office of the road wardens that has maintained the roads ever since. The transit fees the peace had established provided the revenue, and Sera used the revenue with care, setting aside a portion each year for the maintenance of the roads rather than spending the entire sum on new construction.

## Endowment of the Khalisar Academy

Sera supported the founding of the Academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and appointed scholars from the Republic to serve in it. The academy was housed in a complex of buildings the council of Khalisar set aside for the purpose and was endowed by Sera with a portion of the transit fees the Republic received. The first curriculum included the study of the chronicles, the mathematics of trade, and the natural philosophy of the tides and the stars. The academy soon attracted scholars from every city of the three continents and became the principal centre of the learned traditions of the present age.

The academy has served as the principal source of mediators in every subsequent dispute between the Republic and the Pact. It is the legacy of Sera's reign.

## Mourning and Commemoration

Sera died in the thirtieth year of her reign and was mourned by the magistrates and merchants of every city of the three continents. She was buried in the royal tomb at the capital of the Republic, marked by a stele bearing an inscription composed by the council of the Republic in her honour. The inscription records the principal acts of Sera's reign and is regarded as among the finest public inscriptions of the present age.

The memory of Sera is preserved in the chronicles of every city of the three continents. The principal public observance of the Republic is the anniversary of Sera's accession, attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every city of the Republic.

## Model of Just Rule

Sera is one of the most admired queens of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), and her reign is the foundation of the public order of the Republic and of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace) for several centuries. The peace Sera negotiated, the rebuilding she oversaw, the roads she financed, and the academy she founded are the legacy of her reign. Sera is the measure by which the magistrates and councils of the Republic judge public acts.
