## The Brief Engagement

The War of the [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) Pass is the name given by the academies to a brief but sharp engagement between the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) and a coalition of lowland princes, fought in the later period of the recorded history of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The war lasted less than a year and was concluded by a peace that has endured to the present day. The chronicles of the holds and the lowland princes agree upon the principal events but disagree upon their meaning, and the disagreement itself reflects the importance the war has assumed in the traditions of both regions.

The war was fought at the approaches of [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass), the broad rocky pass at the head of the highlands that carries the road from the cold north into the warm coast. The pass has been the principal route of the land trade between the cold north and the warm coast since the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era) and has been the subject of careful engineering by the magistrates of both regions. The war is remembered differently by dwarves and humans: dwarven chroniclers emphasise the perfidy of the lowland princes who broke the ancient accord; human chroniclers dwell on dwarven greed and inflexibility. These competing narratives have shaped the attitudes of both peoples toward each other in the generations since.

## The Outbreak of the War

The war began with a dispute over the tolls the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) had long collected from merchants crossing [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass) into the warm coast. The holds claimed the right to collect the tolls by virtue of the ancient accord the founders of the holds had made with the magistrates of the principal towns of the lowland principalities. The tolls were the foundation of the maintenance of the road that crossed the pass. The coalition of lowland princes disputed the claim on the grounds that the accord had been made before the coalition had been formed and that the coalition was not bound by an instrument to which it had not been a party. The dispute was referred to the magistrates of the principal towns of the lowlands, who decided against the holds. The holds refused to accept the decision, and the coalition dispatched a levy to enforce the ruling.

The levy was met upon the approaches of the pass by the garrison of the holds. The levy was insufficient for the task the council of the coalition had set it, as the approaches of the pass are narrow and the holds had fortified them with care. The levy was held at the foot of the pass for several weeks while the council reinforced it with a second body of troops drawn from the principal towns of the lowlands.

## The Course of the War

The course of the war is conventionally divided into two phases: the coalition's investment of the pass and the holds' counter-offensive that broke the investment and forced the coalition to seek terms. The first phase lasted for the first half of the war, marked by the siege of the watch-towers the holds maintained at the northern and southern ends of [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass). The second phase lasted for the second half, marked by the arrival of the levies of the allied holds and the sortie the garrisons of the towers conducted against the lines of the coalition.

During the first phase, the coalition controlled the approaches of the pass, and the watch-towers were invested from the lowland side. The coalition maintained a close blockade, and the garrison of the northern tower was cut off from relief for several weeks. The garrison held out with a stubbornness the hold chronicles celebrate, though the lowland chroniclers regard their descriptions as excessive. The phase was marked by hardship on both sides.

The second phase began with the arrival of the levies of the allied holds, summoned by the king of Stoneheim and marched by the lesser passes the coalition had not blockaded. The levies descended upon the coalition's lines from the high ground to the north, and the garrison of the northern tower conducted a sortie at the same hour. The coalition, beset upon two sides, withdrew from the approaches with heavy loss.

## The Negotiation of the Peace

With the coalition's withdrawal from the approaches of [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass), the council of the coalition requested a suspension of arms. The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) granted the request, as the counter-offensive had cost them a number of their best warriors and the king of Stoneheim judged that the holds could not press the war further without disproportionate costs. The negotiations were conducted at the great hall of Stoneheim, mediated by the elders of the dwarven communities of the lower slopes who had remained neutral.

Each party insisted upon the rights it had claimed at the beginning of the war. The coalition demanded the abolition of the tolls by virtue of the natural liberty of trade; the holds insisted upon the tolls by virtue of the ancient accord. The mediators proposed a compromise that reduced the tolls to a small transit fee shared between the holds and the magistrates of the principal towns of the lowlands. The compromise was accepted by the king of Stoneheim and by the council of the coalition. The peace was signed in the great hall of Stoneheim in the presence of the elders of the dwarven communities and the magistrates of the principal towns and is known as the Peace of the Stoneheim Pass.

## The Aftermath

The aftermath was marked by a long period of reconstruction upon the approaches of [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass) and in the principal towns of the lowland principalities. The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) rebuilt the watch-towers the war had damaged and re-established the tolls the peace had confirmed. The lowland principalities rebuilt the levies the war had cost them and entered into the arrangements the peace had established. The war left a permanent mark upon the relations of the mountain and the lowlands, characterised by a mixture of rivalry and cooperation.

The peace that has prevailed since the conclusion of the war has held for several generations and is regarded by the academies of the cold north and the warm coast as a model of settlement achieved through patient negotiation. The transit fee has become a substantial source of revenue for the holds and the principal towns alike, used to maintain the road, the watch-towers, and the quarters of the pass.

## The War in the Chronicles of the Holds

The chronicles of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) record the war as a brief but glorious engagement, celebrating the stubbornness of the garrison of the northern watch-tower and the discipline of the levies of the allied holds. The chronicles place the war in the context of the long history of the mountain and the lowlands and describe it as a confirmation of the mountain's capacity to defend itself. The names of the warriors who fell in the war are recited at annual ceremonies maintained in their honour.

## The War in the Chronicles of the Lowlands

The chronicles of the lowland principalities record the war as a brief but instructive misadventure, describing the haste with which the council of the coalition dispatched the levy as a measure of the council's overconfidence. The chronicles place the war in the context of the long history of the trade of the pass and describe it as a confirmation of the lowlands' need to maintain the freedom of trade. The names of the magistrates who counselled against the war are invoked by the magistrates of the principal towns whenever a dispute arises over the tolls of the pass. The academies of the lowlands regard the chronicles as a warning against precipitate action.

## Significance

The Stoneheim Pass War is the most recent of the major conflicts fought at the approaches of [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass) and is held in particular memory by the elders of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) and the magistrates of the lowland principalities. The war lasted less than a year and was concluded by a peace that has endured to the present day, and the peace is the foundation of the trade that flows between the cold north and the warm coast. The war is commemorated in the ceremonies both regions maintain, attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every hold and every town of the two regions.
