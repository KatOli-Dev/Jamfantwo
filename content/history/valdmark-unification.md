## The Joining of the Northern Holdings

The Valdmark Unification was the process by which the independent holdings of the Valdmark region of northern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) were united under a single government in the late fifth century of the present age. The unification was the culmination of a generation of diplomatic efforts and military campaigns that brought the scattered holdings together into the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) realm, a kingdom that commands the northern coast of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) and the passes of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range).

The Valdmark region is a land of deep valleys and high mountains, where the population is concentrated in the valleys and the coastal plains. The holdings of the region had been independent for many centuries, each governed by its own chieftain or council of elders, and each maintaining its own customs and its own traditions. The unification was a difficult and complex process that required the negotiation of differences of culture, language, and interest.

## The Condition of the Holdings Before the Unification

The holdings of the Valdmark region were small and poor by the standards of the principal powers of the present age. The land was mountainous and the soil was thin, and the holdings depended upon fishing, hunting, and herding for their survival. The trade with the south and the west was limited, and the holdings were isolated from the currents of commerce that flowed through the principal cities of Aethelar.

The holdings were also divided among themselves. The chieftains of the holdings were constantly competing with one another for the control of the passes and the harbours, and the competition sometimes led to armed conflict. The wars between the holdings were small in scale but frequent, and they consumed the resources and the energy of the people of the region.

The holdings were also vulnerable to the pressure from the neighbouring powers. The [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) was expanding its trade into the northern waters, and the merchants of the League were seeking to establish posts upon the Valdmark coast. The [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) was also seeking to extend its influence into the region, and the kings of Karhold had made several attempts to bring the holdings under their authority.
