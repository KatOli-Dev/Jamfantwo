## Compilation of Recorded Events

The timeline that follows records the major events of the history of the known world, from the earliest period for which any record survives to the present day. The chronology is constructed from the chronicles of the various nations and peoples of the three continents, and the dates are reckoned according to the calendars of the chroniclers whose records are the source. Where the chronicles disagree upon the date or the character of an event, the disagreement is noted and no judgment is offered upon which account is to be preferred. The timeline is a work of compilation rather than interpretation, intended to provide a single sequence by which the events of the different nations may be related to one another.

## Earliest Kingdoms and Institutions

The earliest period of which any record survives is the Age of Founding, the era in which the great kingdoms of the present age took shape and in which enduring institutions were first established. The chronology is uncertain, for the records are fragmentary and the dates vary by as much as a century between the chroniclers of different nations. The events recorded include the foundation of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) in the eastern lowlands of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the establishment of the first dwarven halls in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands), and the first settlements of the [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) upon the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana).

The Age of Founding is also the period in which the great temples of [the Sky Walkers' Way](/content/culture/sky-walkers-way) were first established upon the central grasslands, and the foundation of the pantheon is recorded by the chroniclers of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) as the central event of the age. The elven communities of the central woodlands date their traditions to this period, though the elven chroniclers reckon the age by a different chronology and the events do not always coincide with those of the human chroniclers. The dwarven chronicles place the foundation of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) and the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) in this age, and these two polities are the oldest of the dwarven communities of which any continuous record survives.

## Sera's Conquest and Legal Code

The most celebrated event of the early period is the unification of the warring principalities of the central river valleys of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) under the rule of [Sera Ambervale](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale), reckoned as the founder of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael). The chronicles of the kingdom record that unification was achieved through military conquest, diplomatic marriage, and the negotiation of treaties. The code of law Sera instituted remained the foundation of the legal system of the kingdom for more than six centuries. She died in the sixty-eighth year of her life, and her reign is dated from the year the chroniclers of the kingdom record as the first year of the Vaelic era.

The reign of Sera is one of the best documented of any ruler of the ancient age. The royal archives of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth) preserve the records in unusual detail, and the chronicles of the neighbouring kingdoms corroborate the main events with independent testimony. Sera is invoked by the rulers of [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) in every age as the model of the just and effective sovereign, and the memory of her reign is the standard by which the success of her successors is measured.

## Trading Posts and Regular Routes

The maritime expansion of the trading peoples of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the central event of the middle period. It began with the establishment of the first trading posts upon the coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and continued with the opening of regular trade routes connecting the ports of the eastern Aethelaran coast to the city-states of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) littoral. The expansion was accompanied by the codification of the [Trade Tongue](/content/language/pseudo/trade-tongue), a system of hand signals that became a practical medium of exchange between merchants who had no spoken tongue in common, and the rise of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) as the foremost commercial power of the eastern coast.

The maritime expansion is also the period in which the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) became the principal conduit of trade between the eastern and western waters. The establishment of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) is recorded by the chroniclers of the strait towns as the central event of the age. The compact is the alliance of the coastal settlements that line the strait, and its terms have been renewed at intervals throughout the centuries that followed. The expansion was not without its conflicts; the wars between the maritime powers of the eastern coast and the corsairs of the western islands are among the most extensively documented conflicts of the period.

## The Oracles of Nessara

The history of the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) in the middle period is dominated by the rise and consolidation of the city-states and by the influence of [Aeliana Truevoice](/content/people/historical/aeliana-truevoice) upon the affairs of the coast. The Oracle was not a single individual but a succession of seers who spoke from the temple upon the headland of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara). Their prophecies influenced the decisions of rulers, the movements of armies, and the beliefs of peoples across the northern coast. The first Oracle appeared in the year that the chroniclers of the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) record as the two hundred and sixth year of the league.

The league itself was established in the early part of this period as an alliance of the principal city-states of the coast and is the oldest of the political entities of the northern [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani). The league was not a unified state but a confederation that preserved the independence of the member cities, and its principal function was the regulation of the trade between the coast and the interior. The league has endured through the centuries following its foundation and remains the principal political entity of the northern coast in the present age.

## Alliance of the Inland Caravan Cities

The establishment of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) is the central event of the history of inland [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) in the middle period. The pact was established by the representatives of the inland caravan cities at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and is the alliance that has governed the caravan trade between the coast and the interior for more than three centuries. The founding charter was agreed by the representatives of the original member cities, and the principles of co-operation, mutual support, and collective defence established at that time have been the foundation of the alliance ever since.

The history of the pact has been marked by the recurring challenge of desert raiders and wild peoples of the deep interior. The most serious threat was a confederation of raiders based in the deep [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and the campaign conducted by the pact's force against them is commemorated in the traditions of the inland cities and the records of the council. The pact has also been called upon to mediate disputes between the member cities and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), and the mediation of the council has been a significant factor in the maintenance of the peace and co-operation that sustain the caravan trade.

## Two Dwarven Realms in the Same Age

The history of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is dominated by the activities of the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), whose underground halls and mountain fortresses have been carved into the living rock over centuries of patient labour. The foundation of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) is recorded by the dwarven chroniclers as the central event of the early history of the mountain, and the federation has been the largest of the dwarven polities of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) for the entire period of recorded history. The [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) was founded in the same age upon the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the two realms have maintained a friendship and commercial partnership that has endured across the centuries.

The mountain has been the scene of numerous conflicts between the dwarven communities and the human and elven settlements of the lower slopes. The treaties negotiated between the mountain and the lowland towns are among the most extensively documented diplomatic instruments of the continent. The mountain has been at peace with the lowland towns for several generations, and the trade between the mountain and the lowlands is the foundation of the prosperity of both. The most recent of the major conflicts was the [Stoneheim Pass War](/content/history/stoneheim-pass-war), a brief but sharp engagement between the holds and a coalition of lowland princes. The peace that has prevailed since its conclusion has been among the most enduring of the mountain's history.

## Nordhaven and the Velden Compact

The history of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) is the history of the maritime communities that line southern [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and northern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) is the principal power on the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) shore, while the settlements of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) regulate the eastern approaches where the sea narrows into the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). Their chroniclers record the establishment of the first permanent harbours, the opening of trade between the continents, and the gradual charting of waters whose storms and reefs had defeated earlier mariners.

The coasts have been the scene of recurring conflicts between settled maritime communities and pirates operating from the islands of the sea. Piracy has never been entirely suppressed, for the broken shoreline and scattered islands provide more anchorages than any fleet can patrol. The most successful campaign was led by [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) in the middle period, with pilots and support ships supplied by the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact). It destroyed the largest pirate stronghold and established the convoy customs still observed by merchant captains. The victory secured the principal crossing for a generation without bringing every remote harbour under lasting control.

## Monotheistic Faith in the Central Lowlands

[The Still Flame](/content/religion/still-flame) is the monotheistic religion that has spread through the central and southern regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) in the later period. The faith was founded in the central lowlands by a prophet whose name is recorded in the scriptures of the church, and it spread rapidly through the towns of the river valleys and along the trade routes connecting the interior to the coast. The faith has been adopted as the principal religion of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and of several of the smaller kingdoms of the interior.

The establishment of the monotheistic church in regions where older polytheistic religions had been established was not accomplished without opposition. The conflicts between the faithful of [the Still Flame](/content/religion/still-flame) and the adherents of [the Sky Walkers' Way](/content/culture/sky-walkers-way) were most intense in the early period of the faith's expansion. The reconciliation achieved between the adherents of the two religions in the present age is a measure of the maturity the religions have attained. The faith has also made some progress in the coastal regions of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), though the older animistic traditions of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) have proved more resistant to monotheistic teaching.

## The Highland Federation

The history of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) is the history of the highland settlements that command the passes from the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) into the basin of the inland seas in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The federation of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) was established by the communities of the highland settlements and the basin settlements that wished to coordinate the defence of the passes and the regulation of the trade that flowed through them. The federation has endured through the centuries that have followed its foundation and remains the principal political entity of the far south.

The inland seas have been the scene of a distinctive maritime culture, and the boats that sail the inland waters are among the most distinctive vessels of the known world. The maritime culture of the inland seas has been influenced by the wider maritime culture of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and the merchants of the southern coast are among the most active traders of the continent. The inland seas are also the home of the [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans), whose slow and careful temperament is well suited to the demands of the highland terrain. The tortolans are found throughout the settlements of the Southern Reach.

## The Three-Banners War and Its Aftermath

The history of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) is the history of the peoples who have inhabited the grasslands of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the wars that have been fought between the competing confederations of the plains. The most celebrated of these wars is the [Three-Banners War](/content/history/three-banners-war), the conflict fought between the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) and two rival confederations that contested the control of the central grasslands in the middle period. The war lasted seven years and resulted in the consolidation of the Confederation's control over the central grasslands, a position it has maintained to the present day.

Wars of the plains have been less frequent in the later period, as the Confederation has established institutions that have made such wars unnecessary, and the negotiation of treaties between the Confederation and neighbouring kingdoms has reduced occasions of conflict. The Confederation remains a formidable military power, and the mounted warriors of the plains are among the most accomplished cavalry in the known world. The annual hunt of the [plains thunder boar](/content/species/beasts/plains-thunder-boar) is the occasion upon which the warriors of the Confederation demonstrate their prowess and a ceremonial occasion that binds the peoples of the plains to the traditions of the grasslands.

## Island Confederation of the Auric Ocean

The [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) are the scattered island communities of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The islands were first settled by fisherfolk of the eastern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) coast in the early period and were organised into a confederation in the middle period under the leadership of the principal island. The confederation has endured through the centuries that have followed its foundation and remains the principal political entity of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean).

The maritime culture of the isles has been a major influence upon the wider maritime culture of the eastern waters, and the navigators and chroniclers of the isles are among the most accomplished in the world. [Marisel Auric](/content/people/notable/marisel-auric) is the present chief chronicler of the confederation, and the chronicle compiled under her direction is the standard reference for the history of the isles. The isles are also the home of the [harpies](/content/species/sapient/harpies), whose presence upon the smaller islands is a feature of the maritime landscape that the islanders have long accommodated.

## Peace, Trade, and Natural Science

The present age is the period in which the major events of recorded history have given way to the more ordinary rhythms of peace and trade that have characterised the past several generations. The great wars of the earlier periods have not been repeated, and the treaties negotiated in the aftermath of the last of the major conflicts have endured to the present day. The trade that flows between the continents has increased substantially, and the cities of the principal ports have grown in proportion to the increase of commerce. The present age is not without its difficulties: the relations between the maritime powers of the eastern coast and the corsairs of the western islands remain a source of tension, and the rivalry between the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) for control of the eastern trade continues to shape the politics of the continent.

The present age is also the period in which the academies of the known world have made substantial advances in the understanding of the natural sciences. The treatises published under the direction of [Halen Amurra](/content/people/notable/halen-amurra) are among the most significant contributions to [Tide-Reading](/content/culture/tide-reading) and [Spherical Cartography](/content/culture/spherical-cartography) made in the present century. The work of the academy of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) has been complemented by the work of the other academies of the continent, and the intellectual life of the present age is among the most vigorous that recorded history records.

## A Chronicle for Reference

The timeline compiled above is the chronicle of the known world from the earliest period of which any record survives to the present day. The events recorded are the foundation of the world in which the present age is conducted, and the institutions established in the earlier periods continue to govern the affairs of the nations of the three continents. The timeline is a work of compilation rather than interpretation, and the conclusions that the events warrant are left to those who consult it.
