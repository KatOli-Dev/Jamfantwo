## Overview

The timeline that follows records the major events of the history of the known world, from the earliest period for which any record survives to the present day. The chronology is constructed from the chronicles of the various nations and peoples of the three continents, and the dates that are given are reckoned according to the calendars of the chroniclers whose records are the source. Where the chronicles disagree upon the date or the character of an event, the disagreement is noted in the entry, and the chronicler offers no judgment upon which account is to be preferred. The timeline is a work of compilation rather than of interpretation, and its purpose is to provide the reader with a single sequence by which the events of the different nations may be related to one another.

## The Age of Founding

The earliest period of which any record survives is the Age of Founding, the era in which the great kingdoms of the present age took shape and in which the institutions that have endured to the present day were first established. The chronology of this period is uncertain, for the records are fragmentary and the dates that are given by the chroniclers of the different nations vary by as much as a century. The events that are recorded in the chronicles of this age are the foundation of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) in the eastern lowlands of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the establishment of the first dwarven halls in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands), and the first settlements of the [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) upon the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana).

The Age of Founding is also the period in which the great temples of the [Sky Walkers' Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/sky-walkers-way) were first established upon the central grasslands, and the foundation of the pantheon is recorded by the chroniclers of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) as the central event of the age. The elven communities of the central woodlands also date their traditions to this period, though the elven chroniclers reckon the age by a different chronology and the events that they record do not always coincide with those of the human chroniclers. The dwarven chronicles place the foundation of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) and the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) in this age, and the two polities are the oldest of the dwarven communities of which any continuous record survives.

## The Unification of the Amber Vale

The most celebrated event of the early period is the unification of the warring principalities of the central river valleys of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) under the rule of [Sera, Queen of the Amber Vale](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale), the sovereign who is reckoned as the founder of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael). The chronicles of the kingdom record that the unification was achieved through a combination of military conquest, diplomatic marriage, and the negotiation of treaties with the neighbouring kingdoms, and the code of law that the queen instituted remained the foundation of the legal system of the kingdom for more than six centuries. The queen died in the sixty-eighth year of her life, and her reign is dated from the year that the chroniclers of the kingdom record as the first year of the Vaelic era.

The reign of Sera is one of the best documented of any ruler of the ancient age, for the royal archives of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth) preserve the records of her reign in unusual detail, and the chronicles of the neighbouring kingdoms corroborate the main events of her life with independent testimony. The chronicler notes that the queen is invoked by the rulers of [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) in every age as the model of the just and the effective sovereign, and the memory of her reign is the standard by which the success of her successors is measured.

## The Maritime Expansion

The maritime expansion of the trading peoples of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the central event of the middle period of the recorded history of the world. The expansion began with the establishment of the first trading posts upon the coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and it continued with the opening of the regular trade routes that connected the ports of the eastern Aethelaran coast to the city-states of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) littoral. The expansion was accompanied by the codification of the [Trade Tongue](/content/language/pseudo/trade-tongue), the shared system of hand signals that became a practical medium of exchange between merchants who had no spoken tongue in common, and the rise of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) as the foremost commercial power of the eastern coast.

The maritime expansion is also the period in which the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) became the principal conduit of the trade between the eastern and the western waters, and the establishment of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) is recorded by the chroniclers of the strait towns as the central event of the age. The compact is the alliance of the coastal settlements that line the strait, and the terms of the compact have been renewed at intervals throughout the centuries that have followed. The chronicler notes that the expansion was not without its conflicts, and the wars between the maritime powers of the eastern coast and the corsairs of the western islands are among the most extensively documented of the conflicts of the period.

## The Sahrani City-States and the Oracle

The history of the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) in the middle period is dominated by the rise and the consolidation of the city-states that line the coast, and by the influence of the [Oracle of Nessara](/content/people/historical/oracle-of-nessara) upon the affairs of the coast. The Oracle was not a single individual but a succession of seers who spoke from the temple upon the headland of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara), and the prophecies that were delivered from the temple influenced the decisions of rulers, the movements of armies, and the beliefs of peoples across the northern coast. The first Oracle appeared in the year that the chroniclers of the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) record as the two hundred and sixth year of the league.

The league itself was established in the early part of this period as an alliance of the principal city-states of the coast, and the league is the oldest of the political entities of the northern Sahrani. The chronicler notes that the league was not a unified state but a confederation that preserved the independence of the member cities, and the league's principal function was the regulation of the trade that flowed between the coast and the interior. The league has endured through the centuries that have followed its foundation, and the league remains the principal political entity of the northern coast in the present age.

## The Vashari Pact and the Desert Trade

The establishment of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) is the central event of the history of the inland [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) in the middle period. The pact was established by the representatives of the inland caravan cities at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and the pact is the alliance that has governed the caravan trade between the coast and the interior for more than three centuries. The founding charter of the pact was agreed by the representatives of the original member cities, and the principles of co-operation, mutual support, and collective defence that were established at that time have been the foundation of the alliance ever since.

The history of the pact has been marked by the recurring challenge of the desert raiders and the wild peoples of the deep interior, and the collective force of the pact has been dispatched on numerous occasions to suppress the threat and to secure the routes. The most serious of these threats was a confederation of raiders that was based in the deep [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and the campaign that was conducted by the pact's force against the raiders is commemorated in the traditions of the inland cities and in the records of the council. The pact has also been called upon to mediate disputes between the member cities and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), and the mediation of the council has been a significant factor in the maintenance of the peace and the co-operation that sustain the caravan trade.

## The Founding of the Dwarven Holds

The history of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is dominated by the activities of the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), whose underground halls and mountain fortresses have been carved into the living rock over centuries of patient labour. The foundation of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) is recorded by the dwarven chroniclers as the central event of the early history of the mountain, and the federation has been the largest of the dwarven polities of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) for the entire period of the recorded history. The [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) was founded in the same age upon the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the two realms have maintained a friendship and a commercial partnership that has endured across the centuries.

The mountain has been the scene of numerous conflicts between the dwarven communities and the human and elven settlements of the lower slopes, and the treaties that have been negotiated between the mountain and the lowland towns are among the most extensively documented of the diplomatic instruments of the continent. The chronicler notes that the mountain has been at peace with the lowland towns for several generations, and the trade that flows between the mountain and the lowlands is the foundation of the prosperity of both. The most recent of the major conflicts of the mountain was the [Stoneheim Pass War](/content/history/stoneheim-pass-war), a brief but sharp engagement between the holds and the forces of a coalition of lowland princes, and the peace that has prevailed since the conclusion of that war has been among the most enduring of the mountain's history.

## The Stormfall Sea and Its Coasts

The history of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) is the history of the maritime communities that line southern [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and northern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) is the principal power on the Morvind shore, while the settlements of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) regulate the eastern approaches where the sea narrows into the Velden Strait. Their chroniclers record the establishment of the first permanent harbours, the opening of trade between the continents, and the gradual charting of waters whose storms and reefs had defeated earlier mariners.

The coasts have been the scene of recurring conflicts between settled maritime communities and pirates operating from the islands of the sea. Piracy has never been entirely suppressed, for the broken shoreline and scattered islands provide more anchorages than any fleet can patrol. The most successful campaign was led by Nordhaven in the middle period with pilots and support ships supplied by the Velden Compact; it destroyed the largest pirate stronghold and established the convoy customs still observed by merchant captains. The victory secured the principal crossing for a generation without bringing every remote harbour under lasting control.

## The Still Flame

The [Still Flame](/content/belief/religion/monotheist/still-flame) is the monotheistic religion that has spread through the central and southern regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) in the later period of the recorded history, and the history of the faith is among the most extensively documented of the religious histories of the world. The faith was founded in the central lowlands by a prophet whose name is recorded in the scriptures of the church, and the faith spread rapidly through the towns of the river valleys and along the trade routes that connected the interior to the coast. The faith has been adopted as the principal religion of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and of several of the smaller kingdoms of the interior.

The faith has not been without its conflicts, for the establishment of the monotheistic church in regions where the older polytheistic religions had been established was not accomplished without opposition. The chronicler notes that the conflicts between the faithful of the Still Flame and the adherents of the [Sky Walkers' Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/sky-walkers-way) were most intense in the early period of the faith's expansion, and the reconciliation that has been achieved between the adherents of the two religions in the present age is a measure of the maturity that the religions have attained. The faith has also made some progress in the coastal regions of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), though the older animistic traditions of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) have proved more resistant to the monotheistic teaching.

## The Southern Reach and the Inland Seas

The history of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) is the history of the highland settlements that command the passes from the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) into the basin of the inland seas in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The federation of the Southern Reach was established by the communities of the highland settlements and the basin settlements that wished to coordinate the defence of the passes and the regulation of the trade that flowed through them. The federation has endured through the centuries that have followed its foundation, and the federation remains the principal political entity of the far south.

The inland seas have been the scene of a distinctive maritime culture, and the boats that sail the inland waters are among the most distinctive of the vessels of the known world. The chronicler notes that the maritime culture of the inland seas has been influenced by the wider maritime culture of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and the merchants of the southern coast are among the most active of the traders of the continent. The inland seas are also the home of the [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans), whose slow and careful temperament is well suited to the demands of the highland terrain, and the tortolans are found throughout the settlements of the Southern Reach.

## The Wars of the Great Grass Plains

The history of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) is the history of the peoples who have inhabited the grasslands of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and of the wars that have been fought between the competing confederations of the plains. The most celebrated of the wars of the plains is the [Three-Banners War](/content/history/three-banners-war), the conflict that was fought between the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) and the two rival confederations that contested the control of the central grasslands in the middle period. The war lasted for a period of seven years and resulted in the consolidation of the Confederation's control over the central grasslands, a position that the Confederation has maintained to the present day.

The chronicler notes that the wars of the plains have been less frequent in the later period, for the Confederation has been successful in establishing the institutions that have made the wars unnecessary, and the negotiation of the treaties between the Confederation and the neighbouring kingdoms has reduced the occasions of conflict. The Confederation remains, however, a formidable military power, and the mounted warriors of the plains are among the most accomplished cavalry in the known world. The annual hunt of the thunder boar is the occasion upon which the warriors of the Confederation demonstrate their prowess, and the hunt is also a ceremonial occasion that binds the peoples of the plains to the traditions of the grasslands.

## The Founding of the Auric Isles

The [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) are the scattered island communities of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and the history of the isles is the history of the maritime peoples who have inhabited them. The islands were first settled by the fisherfolk of the eastern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) coast in the early period of the recorded history, and the islands were organised into a confederation in the middle period under the leadership of the principal island. The confederation has endured through the centuries that have followed its foundation, and the confederation remains the principal political entity of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean).

The chronicler notes that the maritime culture of the isles has been a major influence upon the wider maritime culture of the eastern waters, and the navigators and the chroniclers of the isles are among the most accomplished in the world. The [Marisel](/content/people/notable/marisel) is the present chief chronicler of the confederation, and the chronicle that has been compiled under her direction is the standard reference of the history of the isles. The isles are also the home of the [harpies](/content/species/sapient/harpies), whose presence upon the smaller islands is a feature of the maritime landscape that the islanders have long accommodated.

## The Present Age

The present age is the period in which the major events of the recorded history have given way to the more ordinary rhythms of peace and trade that have characterised the past several generations. The great wars of the earlier periods have not been repeated, and the treaties that were negotiated in the aftermath of the last of the major conflicts have endured to the present day. The trade that flows between the continents has increased substantially, and the cities of the principal ports have grown in proportion to the increase of the commerce. The chronicler notes that the present age is not without its difficulties, for the relations between the maritime powers of the eastern coast and the corsairs of the western islands remain a source of tension, and the rivalry between the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) for the control of the eastern trade continues to shape the politics of the continent.

The present age is also the period in which the academies of the known world have made substantial advances in the understanding of the natural sciences, and the treatises that have been published under the direction of [Halen, Magister of Amurra](/content/people/notable/magister-halen-of-amurra) are among the most significant contributions to the [Tide-Reading](/content/belief/science/physical/tide-reading) and the [Spherical Cartography](/content/belief/science/theoretical/spherical-cartography) that have been made in the present century. The work of the academy of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) has been complemented by the work of the other academies of the continent, and the intellectual life of the present age is among the most vigorous that the recorded history records.

## Significance

The timeline that has been compiled above is the chronicle of the known world from the earliest period of which any record survives to the present day. The events that have been recorded are the foundation of the world in which the present age is conducted, and the institutions that were established in the earlier periods are the institutions that continue to govern the affairs of the nations of the three continents. The chronicler notes that the timeline is a work of compilation rather than of interpretation, and the reader is invited to draw such conclusions as the events may warrant. The history of the known world is a long and a complex one, and the timeline is offered as a guide by which the reader may navigate the events that the chronicles of the different nations have recorded.
