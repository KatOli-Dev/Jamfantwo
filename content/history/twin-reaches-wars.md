## Overview

The Twin-Reaches Wars is the conventional name for the long conflict waged in the generations following the founding of the principal cities between the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) for control of the inland valleys and the trade routes that crossed the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) into the warm coast. The wars lasted several generations and were fought by land and by sea. They are remembered as the most destructive conflicts of the early history of the present age. The academies date the wars by the reign of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale), whose reign is conventionally placed in the latter half of the conflict and whose accession is taken as the beginning of the end of the wars.

The wars are documented in the chronicles of both parties, which agree upon the principal events but disagree upon their meaning. The chronicles of the Amurra Republic present the wars as a defensive struggle against the aggression of the Vashari Pact; the chronicles of the Vashari Pact present the wars as a defensive struggle against encirclement by the Amurra Republic. The academies note that both accounts contain elements of truth and that the wars were the product of a long-standing rivalry for the trade of the inland valleys rather than the deliberate aggression of either party.

## The Outbreak of the War

The wars began with a dispute over the customs dues the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) collected from merchants crossing the Stoneheim Highlands into the warm coast. The Republic claimed the right to collect the dues by virtue of the ancient accord the founders of the Republic had made with the founders of the cities of the warm coast. The [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) disputed the claim on the grounds that the accord had been made before the Pact was founded and that the Pact was not bound by it. The dispute was referred to the magistrates of the principal towns, who decided against the Pact. The Pact refused to accept the decision, and the Republic imposed a blockade upon the ports of the Pact.

The blockade was effective, as the Pact depended upon the export of grain and salt to the warm coast and the Republic controlled the principal outlets. The Pact retaliated by sending raiders into the valleys of the Republic, burning warehouses and driving off cattle of the principal merchants. The Republic responded by sending an army into the valleys, occupying the principal towns of the Pact and imposing a garrison upon each. The war had begun and continued for several generations with varying fortunes.

## The Course of the War

The course of the war is conventionally divided into three phases: the Republic's ascendancy, the Pact's recovery, and the negotiations that led to peace. The first phase lasted for the first generation and was marked by the victories of the Republic's armies in the valleys and its fleets upon the sea. The Republic controlled the principal trade routes and towns of the valleys, imposed garrisons sustained by the customs dues, and maintained a fleet that controlled the principal harbours of the warm coast, preventing the Pact from exporting its goods by sea. This phase was marked by great hardship in the Pact.

The second phase lasted for the second and third generations and began with the appointment of [Thessa](/content/people/historical/thessa-nordhaven) to the command of the Pact's fleet. Thessa was a veteran of the smaller engagements of the wars who had won the confidence of the merchants of the Pact by her refusal to accept the Republic's terms. She rebuilt the fleet from the resources of the Pact's shipyards and trained the crews in new tactics she had observed in the Republic's fleet. Thessa met the Republic's fleet in the waters off the southern coast and won a decisive victory that broke the Republic's control of the harbours. This victory is recorded in the chronicles of the Pact as the turning of the war.

## The Phase of the Negotiations

The third phase began with the accession of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) to the throne of the Amurra Republic. Sera had been a junior magistrate with no experience of war. She judged that the war had reached a stalemate and that the Republic could not win without incurring disproportionate costs. She opened negotiations with the Pact through the mediators of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and the negotiations continued for several years.

Each party insisted upon the rights it claimed at the beginning of the war. The Republic insisted upon the customs dues by virtue of the ancient accord; the Pact insisted upon the right to export its goods without hindrance by virtue of the natural liberty of trade. The mediators proposed a compromise that abolished the customs dues and replaced them with a small transit fee shared between the two parties. The compromise was accepted by Sera and by the council of the Pact. The peace was signed at the temple of [the Sea Mother's Court](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/sea-mothers-court) at Khalisar and is known as the [Twin-Reaches Peace](/content/history/twin-reaches-peace).

## The Aftermath

The aftermath of the wars was marked by a long period of reconstruction in both parties. The Pact rebuilt the towns that had been occupied by the Republic, and the Republic rebuilt the fleets that had been destroyed by Thessa. The transit fee the peace had established became a substantial source of revenue for both parties, used to repair the roads, bridges, and harbours of the trade routes. The wars left a permanent mark upon the relations of the two parties, characterised by a mixture of rivalry and cooperation.

The wars are remembered in the chronicles of both parties as a long and unnecessary conflict, and the memory has been the foundation of the reluctance of both parties to take up arms against each other in the generations that have followed. The peace has held for several centuries. The academies of the warm coast and the cold north have preserved the chronicles of the wars, and these are consulted by every magistrate who must judge a dispute upon the trade routes.

## Significance

The Twin-Reaches Wars are the most destructive conflicts of the early history of the present age and the foundation of the political relations of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) for several centuries. The peace negotiated by [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) is the foundation of the trade of the inland valleys and the warm coast and the measure by which the magistrates and merchants of both parties judge the disputes of their own day. The citizens of both parties mark the anniversaries of the principal battles with ceremonies attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every city of the two parties.
