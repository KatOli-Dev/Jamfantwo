## The Founding of the Northern Realm

The [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) Pact was the agreement that established the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) as a unified kingdom in the northern regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the neighbouring coasts of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The pact was negotiated in the early fifth century of the present age, after a century of conflict and competition among the northern chieftains for the control of the trade routes and the resources of the north. The pact established the terms of the union of the northern territories and has served as the foundation of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) for the centuries that followed.

The pact is preserved in the archives of the Karhold court at [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) and in the records of the principal chieftains who were parties to the agreement. The text of the pact is written in the old runes of the north and in the common tongue of the southern trade, and the two versions are considered equally authoritative. The academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) have studied the pact and regard it as one of the most important instruments of the northern peoples.

## The State of the North Before the Pact

Before the pact, the northern territories were divided among a number of independent chieftains, each governing a portion of the coast or the interior. The chieftains were constantly at war with one another, competing for the control of the fishing grounds, the hunting territories, and the trade routes. The wars were costly and destructive, and the chieftains were unable to present a united front against the threats from the south and the east.

The chieftains were also divided by differences of culture and language. The chieftains of the coast were descended from the settlers who had crossed the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) from [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and they spoke the northern dialect of the common tongue. The chieftains of the interior were descended from the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) and from the peoples of the Aethelar north, and they spoke a number of distinct languages that were not mutually intelligible. The differences made communication and cooperation difficult, and the chieftains were often suspicious of one another.

The pressure from the south and the east was the principal factor that drove the chieftains to consider a union. The [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) was expanding its trade into the northern waters, and the merchants of the League were seeking to establish posts upon the northern coast. The [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) was also expanding its influence into the northern grasslands, and the elders of the Confederation were seeking to bring the northern kindreds under their authority.

## The Negotiations

The negotiations that led to the Karhold Pact were initiated by the chieftain Kar [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim), who was the most powerful of the northern chieftains of the period. Kar had seen that the northern territories could not survive as a collection of independent chieftaincies and that a union was necessary if the north was to resist the pressure from the south and the east. Kar sent messages to the other chieftains, proposing a council to discuss the terms of a union.

The council was held at Kar's stronghold, which was located at the foot of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) where the passes from the south met the coastal route. The site was chosen because it was accessible to all the chieftains and because Kar's prestige was sufficient to guarantee the safety of the participants. The council lasted for the whole winter, as the chieftains debated the terms of the union.

The principal issue was the nature of the union. The chieftains of the coast favoured a strong central authority that would command the warriors of all the territories and that would have the power to tax the territories for the common defence. The chieftains of the interior favoured a looser confederation that would preserve their independence and their autonomy.

## The Terms of the Pact

The compromise that emerged from the council was the establishment of the Karhold Realm as a kingdom under a single ruler, with a council of chieftains that would advise the king and that would have the power to approve the principal decisions of the realm. The king would be chosen by the council of chieftains from among the members of the principal families, and the king would be responsible for the defence of the realm and for the conduct of foreign relations.

The first provision of the pact established the boundaries of the realm. The realm was defined as the territory of the signatory chieftains, which extended from the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) in the west to the [Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range) in the east and from the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) in the south to the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) in the north. The boundaries were confirmed by the council and were recorded in the text of the pact.

The second provision established the succession to the throne. The king was to be chosen by the council of chieftains from among the members of the principal families, and the choice was to be confirmed by the assembly of the freeholders of the realm. The king would serve for life, and the succession would pass to the nearest adult relative of the appropriate sex, subject to the approval of the council.

The third provision established the rights and the obligations of the chieftains. The chieftains retained the governance of their own territories, including the right to collect taxes and to administer justice. The chieftains were required to provide warriors for the defence of the realm and to pay a portion of the taxes that they collected to the royal treasury. The chieftains were also required to attend the council and to participate in its deliberations.

## The Ratification and the Coronation

The pact was ratified by the signatory chieftains in the spring of the year 412, at a ceremony held at Kar's stronghold. The ceremony was attended by the chieftains and their retinues, by the representatives of the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) and the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and by the mediators of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic). The ceremony included the reading of the text of the pact, the exchange of gifts, and a feast that lasted for three days.

The first king of the Karhold Realm was Kar Stoneheim, who was chosen by the council of chieftains and confirmed by the assembly of the freeholders. Kar was crowned at the stronghold that became the capital of the realm, and he took the name King Kar the First. The coronation was the occasion of a great celebration, and the chronicles record that the festivities lasted for a week.

King Kar's reign was the foundation of the Karhold Realm. He established the institutions of the kingdom, including the royal court, the treasury, and the military command. He also established relations with the neighbouring powers, including the Northern Coast League and the Plains Confederation, and he negotiated a treaty that recognised the independence and the boundaries of the realm.

## The Legacy of the Pact

The Karhold Pact has been in force for several centuries and is one of the most enduring instruments of the northern peoples. The pact established the Karhold Realm as a unified kingdom and laid the foundations for the prosperity and the security of the north. The institutions that the pact established, including the council of chieftains, the succession procedure, and the system of rights and obligations, have served as the foundation of the realm for the centuries that have followed.

The pact also established the Karhold Realm as a distinct power in the north, independent of the Northern Coast League and the Plains Confederation. The realm has maintained its independence through the centuries, and the kings of Karhold have defended the northern territories against the threats from the south and the east.

The memory of the pact is preserved in the ceremonies of the Karhold Realm. The anniversary of the ratification is marked by a gathering at the capital of the realm, attended by the chieftains of the territories and by the royal court. The gathering includes the reading of the text of the pact, the renewal of the oaths of the chieftains, and a feast that celebrates the unity of the realm.
