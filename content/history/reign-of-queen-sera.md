## Overview

[Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) is recorded in the chronicles of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and of every other principal city of the three continents as the queen who concluded the [Wars of the Twin Reaches](/content/history/wars-of-the-twin-reaches) and began the [Long Peace of the Twin Cities](/content/history/long-peace-of-the-twin-cities). Sera was a junior magistrate of the Republic at the time of her accession to the throne, and Sera had no experience of war. Sera judged that the war had reached a stalemate, and that the Republic could not win without incurring costs that would be disproportionate to the benefits. Sera opened negotiations with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) through the mediators of the academies, and the negotiations continued for several years before the peace was signed at the temple of [the Faith of the Sea Mother](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-faith-of-the-sea-mother) at [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

The reign of Sera lasted more than thirty years, and the reign is the longest of the recorded reigns of the Amurra Republic. The reign is remembered as a period of consolidation and rebuilding, and the reign is the foundation of the public order of the Republic for several centuries. The chronicler notes that Sera is held in particular esteem by the magistrates and the merchants of the Republic, and Sera is the measure by which the magistrates and the councils of the Republic judge the public acts of their own day.

## The Accession

Sera was the youngest child of the previous queen, and Sera had been educated in the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) before returning to the Republic to serve as a magistrate. Sera was appointed to the magistracy of the southern district of the Republic, and Sera served in that office for several years before the death of her elder sister made Sera the heir to the throne. The accession took place in the midst of the wars, and the chronicles of the Republic record the accession as a moment of great uncertainty. The chroniclers of the academies note that Sera had no military experience, and that Sera's appointment to the throne was the result of the accident of birth rather than of any particular fitness for the office.

Sera quickly demonstrated a capacity for administration and a willingness to consult with the magistrates and the councils that the Republic had established. Sera appointed the most experienced of the magistrates to the principal offices of the state, and Sera listened to the advice that the magistrates gave her. The chronicler notes that Sera's willingness to listen was the foundation of her success, and the success is the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates of the Republic.

## The Negotiations

The negotiations with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) were the most important undertaking of Sera's reign, and the negotiations occupied the first decade of the reign. Sera opened the negotiations through the mediators of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and the mediators served as the channel through which the two parties communicated. The mediators were scholars of the academies, and the scholars brought to the negotiations a knowledge of the ancient accords and a reputation for impartiality that the parties trusted.

The negotiations were complicated by the insistence of each party upon the rights that it claimed at the beginning of the war. The Republic insisted upon the customs dues by virtue of the ancient accord that the founders of the Republic had made with the founders of the cities of the warm coast. The Pact insisted upon the right to export its goods without hindrance by virtue of the natural liberty of trade. The mediators proposed a compromise that abolished the customs dues and replaced them with a small transit fee that was shared between the two parties, and the compromise was accepted by Sera and by the council of the Pact. The peace was signed at the temple of [the Faith of the Sea Mother](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-faith-of-the-sea-mother) at Khalisar, and the peace is known in the chronicles as the Wars of the Twin Reaches.

## The Rebuilding

The years following the peace were a period of rebuilding, and Sera presided over the rebuilding with care and patience. The Republic rebuilt the fleets that had been broken by [Admiral Thessa](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet), and the Republic rebuilt the towns that had been damaged in the wars. Sera appointed commissioners to oversee the rebuilding, and the commissioners worked in concert with the magistrates and the merchants of the principal towns. The chronicler notes that the rebuilding was the work of two generations, and the rebuilding is recorded in the chronicles of the Republic as a period of patient and necessary labour.

Sera also oversaw the construction of the roads and the bridges that the [Building of the Stone Roads](/content/history/building-of-the-stone-roads) required, and Sera established the office of the road wardens that has maintained the roads ever since. The transit fees that the peace had established provided the revenue for the construction, and Sera used the revenue with the care that the magistrates and the merchants had come to expect. The chronicler notes that Sera's care with the public revenue was the foundation of the prosperity of the Long Peace, and the prosperity is the measure of Sera's success.

## The Founding of the Academy

Sera supported the founding of the Academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), and Sera appointed scholars from the Republic to serve in the academy. The academy was housed in a complex of buildings that the council of Khalisar set aside for the purpose, and the academy was endowed by Sera with a portion of the transit fees that the Republic received. The academy soon attracted scholars from every city of the three continents, and the academy became the principal centre of the learned traditions of the present age.

The chronicler notes that the founding of the academy was one of the most important of the works of Sera's reign, and the academy is the foundation of the learned culture of the present age. The academy has served as the principal source of the mediators in every subsequent dispute between the Republic and the Pact, and the academy is held in high esteem by every magistrate and every merchant of the three continents. The chronicler observes that the academy is the legacy of Sera's reign, and the legacy is the measure of Sera's public spirit.

## The Death and the Memory

Sera died in the thirtieth year of her reign, and the death was mourned by the magistrates and the merchants of every city of the three continents. Sera was buried in the royal tomb at the capital of the Republic, and the tomb is marked by a stele that bears the inscription that the council of the Republic composed in her honour. The inscription records the principal acts of Sera's reign, and the inscription is held to be among the finest of the public inscriptions of the present age.

The memory of Sera is preserved in the chronicles of every city of the three continents, and the chronicles are consulted by every magistrate and every merchant who must judge a question of public order. The principal public observance of the Republic is the anniversary of Sera's accession, and the observance is attended by the magistrates and the principal citizens of every city of the Republic. The chronicler notes that the observance is among the most important of the public observances of the present age, and the observance is the measure of the regard in which Sera is held.

## Significance

Sera is one of the most admired of the queens of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), and Sera's reign is the foundation of the public order of the Republic and of the [Long Peace of the Twin Cities](/content/history/long-peace-of-the-twin-cities) for several centuries. The peace that Sera negotiated, the rebuilding that Sera oversaw, the roads that Sera financed, and the academy that Sera founded are the legacy of Sera's reign. The chronicler notes that Sera is held in particular memory by the magistrates and the merchants of every city of the three continents, and Sera is the measure by which the magistrates and the councils of the Republic judge the public acts of their own day.
