## Overview

The Founding Era is the period, generally placed in the early centuries of recorded history, in which the principal cities of the three continents established themselves as centres of trade, of governance, and of the learned traditions. The era is named for the rapid appearance within a few generations of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim), and the other great cities of the present age. The chroniclers of the academies date the era by the reign of [Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale), whose accession is taken as the conventional beginning of the present age, but the founding of the cities themselves is generally placed several generations earlier.

The era is poorly documented in the surviving records, for the cities were founded in a time when the writing of chronicles was the privilege of the temples and the royal houses. The chronicles that survive are often hagiographic, and they present the founders as near-mythical figures who accomplished their work by virtue of single-handed heroism. The chroniclers of the academies discount the more extravagant of these accounts, and they have reconstructed a more sober history from the comparison of the chronicles with the archaeological evidence of the earliest streets, the earliest walls, and the earliest harbours of the cities. The reconstruction is incomplete, but it is the best that the present age can offer.

## The Founding of Khalisar

The founding of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is attributed in the chronicles to the merchants who settled upon the natural harbour of the southern reach of the Greyring in the generation before the era. The settlement was small at first, consisting of a few warehouses, a small temple, and a modest mole. The settlement grew rapidly, for the harbour was the best along the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast and the merchants who traded with the inland valleys found it convenient to keep their goods in the warehouses of the settlement rather than to carry them further.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed to the accord that the merchants made with the salt-makers of the coast and with the farmers of the inland valleys. The accord established the standards of the salt and the grain, the rules of the trade, and the procedures by which the disputes were adjudicated. The accord is the foundation of the public order of the Sahrani coast, and the accord has been revised from time to time but never replaced. The chronicler notes that the accord is one of the most ancient of the public instruments of the present age, and the accord is preserved in the archives of the [Khalisar Council](/content/government/local/khalisar-council).

## The Founding of Vassholm

The founding of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) is attributed in the chronicles to the fishermen and the seal-hunters who settled upon the natural harbour of the southern flank of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) in the same generation. The settlement was founded upon a small island at the mouth of the harbour, and the island was connected to the mainland by a causeway that could be raised in time of danger. The settlement grew rapidly, for the harbour was a useful refuge for the vessels that worked the coast and the seas to the north.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed to the accord that the fishermen made with the merchants who traded with the inland valleys and with the dwarven holds of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). The accord established the standards of the fish and the seal-oil, the rules of the trade, and the procedures by which the disputes were adjudicated. The accord is the foundation of the public order of the cold north, and the accord has been revised from time to time but never replaced. The chronicler notes that the accord is one of the most ancient of the public instruments of the present age, and the accord is preserved in the archives of the magistrates of Vassholm.

## The Founding of Stoneheim

The founding of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) is attributed in the chronicles to the dwarven clans who migrated from the older holds of the north to the southern flank of the Stoneheim Highlands in the same generation. The migration is described in the dwarven chronicles as a deliberate choice, made by the elders of the clans in council, to seek new quarries and new pastures for the goats and the sheep that the dwarven households depended upon. The chroniclers of the academies note that the migration may also have been driven by pressure from the orc and goblin tribes of the older mountains, but the dwarven chronicles are reticent upon the matter, and the question is not settled.

The settlement was founded upon a high plateau that commanded the principal passes of the southern [Stonespire](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire), and the dwarven engineers cut the first terraces and the first roads into the rock. The settlement grew rapidly, for the quarries of the southern Stonespire were rich and the goats and the sheep flourished upon the upland pastures. The chronicler notes that the dwarven chronicles are more detailed for the period of the founding than the chronicles of Khalisar or Vassholm, for the dwarven tradition of record-keeping is older than the human tradition of the coastal cities.

## The Other Foundations

The other principal cities of the present age were founded in the same generation or in the generation that followed, and the chronicles of each city preserve a similar account of merchants or settlers who chose a favourable site and who established an accord with their neighbours. [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) was founded upon a bend of the [Greywash River](/content/location/natural/feature/greywash-river); [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) was founded upon the northern bank of the strait; [Serren](/content/location/settlement/city/serren) was founded upon a fertile plain of the interior. Each foundation is attributed in the chronicles to a particular group of settlers and to a particular accord with the neighbours, and each accord has been preserved in the archives of the magistrates.

The chronicles of the academies note that the foundations of the cities were not isolated events but part of a broader movement of settlement that followed the cessation of the migrations of the elder peoples. The migration had brought the [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans), the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves), and the [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) into the lands that they now occupy, and the settlements that grew into the cities of the present age were the points at which the migration settled. The chronicler observes that the foundation of the cities is the beginning of the recorded history of the present age, and the period of the migration itself is lost in the older traditions of the temples.

## The Legacy of the Era

The legacy of the Founding Era is the set of public instruments by which the cities have been governed for several centuries. The accords between the merchants and the farmers, between the fishermen and the dwarven holds, and between the dwarven clans and the peoples of the plains have been the foundation of the public order of the three continents, and the accords have been revised from time to time but never replaced. The chronicler notes that the accords are among the most ancient of the public instruments of the present age, and the accords are the measure by which the magistrates and the councils judge the public acts of their own day.

The era is also remembered for the temples that were founded in the same generation, and the temples of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) are among the most ancient of the present age. The temples are the custodians of the older traditions of the polytheist faiths and of the older rites of the [Grey Sea Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/grey-sea-way) and the other ancient religions. The chronicler notes that the temples have preserved a great deal of the older learning that would otherwise have been lost, and the temples are held in high esteem by every scholar and every magistrate of the present age.

## Significance

The Founding Era is the beginning of the recorded history of the present age, and the era is the foundation upon which every subsequent history of the three continents is constructed. The cities that were founded in the era are the cities of the present day, and the public instruments that were established in the era are the public instruments by which the cities are still governed. The chronicler notes that the era is held in particular honour by the magistrates and the councils of the cities, and the magistrates and the councils mark the anniversaries of the foundations with the appropriate ceremonies. The ceremonies are among the most important of the public observances of the present age, and the ceremonies are attended by the magistrates and the principal citizens of every city of the three continents.
