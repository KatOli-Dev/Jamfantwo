## Overview

The Founding Era is the period, generally placed in the early centuries of recorded history, in which the principal cities of the three continents established themselves as centres of trade, governance, and learned traditions. The era is named for the rapid appearance within a few generations of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim), and the other great cities of the present age. The academies date the era by the reign of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale), whose accession is taken as the conventional beginning of the present age, but the founding of the cities themselves is generally placed several generations earlier.

The era is poorly documented in the surviving records. The chronicles that survive are often hagiographic, presenting the founders as near-mythical figures who accomplished their work by single-handed heroism. The academies discount the more extravagant accounts and have reconstructed a more sober history by comparing the chronicles with the archaeological evidence of the earliest streets, walls, and harbours of the cities. This reconstruction is incomplete but represents the best understanding that the present age can offer. The writing of chronicles was the privilege of temples and royal houses at the time, and few civic records from the period survive.

## The Founding of Khalisar

The founding of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is attributed to merchants who settled upon the natural harbour of the southern reach of the Greyring in the generation before the era. The settlement was small at first, consisting of a few warehouses, a small temple, and a modest mole. The harbour was the best along the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast, and merchants who traded with the inland valleys found it convenient to store their goods in the settlement rather than carry them further.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed to an accord between the merchants, the salt-makers of the coast, and the farmers of the inland valleys. The accord established standards for salt and grain, rules of trade, and procedures for adjudicating disputes. This accord remains the foundation of the public order of the Sahrani coast. It has been revised from time to time but never replaced, and it is preserved in the archives of the [Khalisar Council](/content/government/local/khalisar-council) as one of the most ancient public instruments of the present age.

## The Founding of Vassholm

The founding of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) is attributed to fishermen and seal-hunters who settled upon the natural harbour of the southern flank of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) in the same generation. The settlement was founded upon a small island at the mouth of the harbour, connected to the mainland by a causeway that could be raised in time of danger. The harbour provided a useful refuge for vessels working the coast and the northern seas.

The city's growth is attributed to an accord between the fishermen, the merchants who traded with the inland valleys, and the dwarven holds of the Stoneheim Highlands. The accord established standards for fish and seal-oil, trade rules, and dispute procedures. Like the Khalisar accord, it has been revised but never replaced, and it is preserved in the archives of the magistrates of Vassholm.

## The Founding of Stoneheim

The founding of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) is attributed to dwarven clans who migrated from older northern holds to the southern flank of the Stoneheim Highlands in the same generation. The dwarven chronicles describe the migration as a deliberate choice by the elders in council to seek new quarries and pastures for their goats and sheep. The academies note that the migration may also have been driven by pressure from orc and goblin tribes of the older mountains, though the dwarven chronicles are reticent on the matter.

The settlement was founded upon a high plateau commanding the principal passes of the southern [Stonespire](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire). Dwarven engineers cut the first terraces and roads into the rock. The quarries were rich and the upland pastures flourished. The dwarven chronicles are more detailed for this period than the human chronicles of the coastal cities, as the dwarven tradition of record-keeping predates the human tradition.

## The Other Foundations

The other principal cities of the present age were founded in the same generation or the one that followed, and each preserves a similar account of merchants or settlers who chose a favourable site and established an accord with neighbours. [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) was founded upon a bend of the [Greywash River](/content/location/natural/feature/greywash-river); [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) was founded upon the northern bank of the strait; [Serren](/content/location/settlement/city/serren) was founded upon a fertile interior plain. Each foundation is attributed in the chronicles to a particular group of settlers and a particular accord preserved in the archives.

The foundations were not isolated events but part of a broader movement of settlement that followed the cessation of the migrations of the elder peoples. The migrations had brought [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans), [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves), and [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) into the lands they now occupy, and the settlements that grew into cities were the points at which the migrations settled. The foundation of the cities marks the beginning of recorded history; the period of migration itself is lost in the older traditions of the temples.

## The Legacy of the Era

The legacy of the Founding Era is the set of public instruments by which the cities have been governed for several centuries. The accords between merchants and farmers, fishermen and dwarven holds, and dwarven clans and plains peoples have been the foundation of public order across the three continents. These instruments are among the most ancient of the present age and serve as the measure by which magistrates and councils judge public acts.

The era is also remembered for the temples founded in the same generation. The temples of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) are among the most ancient of the present age. They are the custodians of the older traditions of the polytheist faiths and the older rites of the [Grey Sea Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/grey-sea-way) and other ancient religions. The temples have preserved a great deal of older learning that would otherwise have been lost, and they are held in high esteem by scholars and magistrates throughout the three continents.

## Significance

The Founding Era is the beginning of the recorded history of the present age and the foundation upon which every subsequent history of the three continents is constructed. The cities founded in the era are the cities of the present day, and the public instruments established in the era remain in force. The magistrates and councils of the cities mark the anniversaries of the foundations with ceremonies attended by the principal citizens of every city of the three continents. These ceremonies are among the most important of the public observances of the present age.
