## First Eastern Crossing to the Kharesh

Kaelen's Voyage is the conventional name for the expedition by which [Kaelen](/content/people/historical/kaelen-navigator) and the pilots of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) first reached the great island of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) in the early generations of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace). The voyage was the result of several generations of patient exploration by the pilots of the warm coast and opened a new chapter in the maritime trade of the three continents. The academies date the voyage by the year Kaelen returned to Khalisar with the first cargo of spices and woods from the Kharesh.

The voyage is recorded in the chronicles of the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch) and of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm). It is among the most admired achievements of the pilots of the present age, and the pilots mark the anniversaries of Kaelen's return with ceremonies.

## Suspicions of a Great Eastern Land

The pilots of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) had long suspected that a great land lay to the east beyond the horizon of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). They had observed the flight of migratory birds passing over the ocean in spring and autumn, always flying eastward in the spring and returning from the east in the autumn, and had heard legends of the Grey Sea Way that spoke of a great island in the eastern waters where the spirits of the sea were said to dwell. Earlier attempts to sail east had been turned back by contrary winds and uncertain currents, and several pilots had never returned, their fates unknown.

The growth of trade in the Long Peace provided the resources for a more ambitious attempt. The council of Khalisar voted to equip a small fleet of three vessels for the voyage, appointed Kaelen to command, and provided the supplies and instruments required, including the best compasses and charts the academies could furnish. The vessels were the Starfinder, the Seahawk, and the Grey Gull, all stoutly built for deep water. Kaelen was chosen for the command because of a reputation for careful navigation and a willingness to learn from the failures of earlier pilots. The investment was considerable and reflected the public spirit of the magistrates of Khalisar.

## Sailing East on the Trade Winds

Kaelen set out from [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in the early spring, sailing east upon the trade winds that blow steadily across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) at that season. Kaelen kept a careful record of the course, winds, and currents and consulted the records of earlier pilots preserved in the academy archives. By sailing south of the earlier track, Kaelen found a steadier wind and a more favourable current that carried the fleet further in a single day than any earlier pilot had managed. The fleet made good progress, and Kaelen sighted the high mountains of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) in the late summer, after more than four months at sea.

Kaelen landed upon the southern coast of the Kharesh and was received by the Kharesh Spirits, the indigenous people of the island. The Spirits were initially cautious, but Kaelen offered gifts of steel tools and woven cloth, which the Spirits accepted and reciprocated with fruits and woven baskets. Kaelen spent several months learning their language and customs and trading for the spices and unusual woods the island produced, including the aromatic bark of the redwood tree and the hard, dark timber of the ironwood, both unknown in the markets of the three continents. The fleet was loaded with the first cargo of these goods, carefully packed to survive the voyage. Kaelen set sail for the return in the early winter and reached [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in the following spring, having survived storms and a period of calm that had threatened to strand the fleet far from land.

## Celebration and Profit in Khalisar

The return of Kaelen was greeted with great public celebration in [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), including a procession through the streets, a service in the temple of the Sea Mother's Court, and a public reading of the account of the voyage. The council of Khalisar received the cargo, paid Kaelen the promised reward, and voted to equip a second voyage in the following year.

The cargo was sold at a great price in the markets of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), providing the resources for the second voyage and for subsequent voyages. The trade with the Kharesh became one of the most profitable trades of the warm coast and the foundation of prosperity for the merchants of Khalisar.

## Permanent Factory and New Learning

Kaelen's Voyage opened a new chapter in the maritime trade of the three continents, and the trade has continued without interruption ever since. The merchants of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) established a permanent factory upon the southern coast of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), which has been the principal point of contact between the people of the three continents and the Kharesh Spirits. The factory is governed by a consul appointed by the council of Khalisar, who adjudicates disputes between merchants and the Spirits and who maintains friendly relations with the elders of the Spirits' communities. The consul also regulates the exchange of goods and ensures that the trade is conducted fairly on both sides.

The voyage also affected the learning of the academies. Scholars of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) studied Kaelen's account and revised the maps and charts of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), correcting errors that had persisted for generations. The language and customs of the Kharesh Spirits became part of the curriculum of the academies, and scholars compiled the first dictionaries of the Spirits' speech. The botanical specimens Kaelen brought back prompted the establishment of a garden of exotic plants at the academy of Khalisar, which continues to be maintained.

## Foundation of the Kharesh Trade

Kaelen's Voyage is among the most important maritime achievements of the present age and the foundation of the trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) for several centuries. The voyage of Kaelen, the factory upon the southern coast, and the consul who governs it are the legacy of the expedition. The pilots mark the anniversaries of the return of Kaelen with ceremonies attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every city of the warm coast. These ceremonies are among the most important public observances of the warm coast. The voyage is placed in the broader chronology of the age in the [World Timeline](/content/history/world-timeline).
