## Overview

The Auric Isles is a confederation of island communities of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), encompassing the settlements that are situated on the volcanic islands and the remote landmasses that rise from the deep waters of the central and eastern Auric. The confederation is not a kingdom but a commercial and a cultural alliance, and its member communities retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, navigation, and mutual support. The Auric Isles is the primary political entity of the ocean, and its collective power in the maritime trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) is a significant component of the economy of the known world.

## The Member Islands

The Auric Isles includes the principal island settlements of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and the membership is drawn from the communities that share in the ocean trade. [Tidesmeet](/content/tidesmeet) is the largest and the most influential member of the confederation, and its harbour is the primary refuge and resupply point for the vessels that travel the Auric trade routes. [Windward](/content/windward) is the most remote member, situated on a distant island of the eastern Auric, and its participation in the confederation is nominal, owing to its distance from the other islands. The smaller islands of the chain, though not individually named in the records of the continental peoples, are full members of the confederation, and their communities are represented in the council.

The confederation is governed by a council of representatives from each member island, and the council meets regularly at [Tidesmeet](/content/tidesmeet) to co-ordinate the trade, the navigation, and the mutual support of the island communities. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the confederation has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the confederation is a model of co-operation among isolated communities, and the council is the primary institution of the island peoples.

## Trade and the Ocean

The trade of the Auric Isles is built upon the position of the islands astride the trade routes of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and the goods that flow through the harbours of the confederation include the spices and the dyes of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), the grain and the metalwork of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and the products of the islands themselves, which include the tropical fruits, the shells, and the other materials that are found only in the island communities. The confederation co-ordinates the maritime trade of the islands, managing the lighthouses, the pilotage services, and the navigation aids that are essential to the safety and the efficiency of the ocean routes.

The [merfolk](/content/merfolk) of the Auric waters are regular contacts of the island communities, and the relationship between the islanders and the merfolk is one of cautious but peaceful coexistence, for the two peoples share the same waters and the same dependence on the sea. The [cyclopes](/content/cyclopes) of the remote islands are occasional contacts of the confederation, and the relationship between the islanders and the cyclopes is distant but respectful, for the cyclopes are solitary and the islanders are few.

## Defence and the People

The defence of the Auric Isles is maintained by a collective fleet that is funded by the contributions of the member islands, and the fleet protects the trade routes of the ocean from pirates and raiders. The population of the confederation is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the islands are known for their seamanship and their boatbuilding, and their vessels are a common sight in the ports of both Aethelar and Sahrana. The [tortolans](/content/tortolans) of the warm coasts and islands are full members of the confederation, and their participation is a valued feature of the alliance.

## Significance

The Auric Isles is the primary political entity of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and its collective power in the maritime trade of the ocean is a significant component of the economy of the known world. The confederation's co-operation among isolated island communities is a model of the kind of political order that has sustained the island peoples for many centuries, and its regulation of the ocean trade and the navigation aids is a critical function that ensures the safety and the efficiency of the routes that connect the continents. The significance of the Auric Isles lies in its capacity for collective action and its role as the guardian of the ocean trade, and the confederation is a symbol of the maritime tradition and the enterprise of the island peoples.
