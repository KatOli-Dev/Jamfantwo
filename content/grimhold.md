## Overview

Grimhold is a [dwarven](/content/dwarves) fortress-city in the deep interior of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated in a region where the mountain ranges are at their most rugged and the passes are at their most difficult. The city is carved into the rock of a great peak that dominates the surrounding ranges, and its fortifications are among the most extensive and the most impressive of any dwarven settlement. Grimhold is both a city and a fortress, and its primary function is the defence of the deep interior and the maintenance of the routes that connect the interior ranges to the coast.

## The Fortress

The fortress of Grimhold is the dominant feature of the city, and its walls and towers are carved from the living rock of the peak, rising from the mountainside in a series of terraces and battlements that command every approach to the city. The gates of Grimhold are cut through the rock of the mountain, and the passages that lead to them are narrow and easily defended, for the city was built in an age when the interior was less secure and the threat of attack from the wild peoples of the deep interior was a constant concern. The fortifications have been maintained and reinforced over the centuries, and the garrison of Grimhold is among the largest and the most professional in the dwarven world.

The halls of Grimhold are carved into the interior of the peak, and the dwellings, the forges, and the storehouses are all situated within the mountain, protected by the rock from both the weather and the enemy. The great hall of Grimhold is a martial space, and the forges that line its walls are devoted chiefly to the production of weapons and armour, for the city's industry is oriented toward defence rather than trade. The storehouses of Grimhold are stocked with provisions for a long siege, and the water supply is drawn from the underground streams that flow through the mountain, making the city self-sufficient in the essentials of survival.

## The Deep Interior

The deep interior of Morvind is the most remote and the most rugged region of the continent, and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/frostwall-clans), the dwarven clans that inhabit the deep interior, are the guardians of this region. Grimhold is the principal fortress of the Frostwall Clans, and the city serves as the base for the defence of the interior and the maintenance of the routes that connect the interior ranges to the coast. The routes that pass through the deep interior are few and difficult, but they are the only overland connections between the eastern and western portions of the Morvind ranges, and their maintenance is a vital function of the Grimhold garrison.

The wild peoples of the deep interior, including the [orcs](/content/orcs) and the [lupines](/content/lupines) of the cold steppes, are a constant presence in the region, and the relationship between the dwarves and the wild peoples is one of mutual wariness. The fortress of Grimhold is a deterrent against any threat from the interior, and the garrison is prepared to defend the passes and the routes that are the lifelines of the dwarven interior.

## The City and the Clans

Grimhold is the capital of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/frostwall-clans), and the city is the seat of the council of clan chiefs that govern the deep interior. The population of Grimhold is entirely [dwarven](/content/dwarves), and the community is oriented toward the military function that is the reason for the city's existence. The dwarves of Grimhold are among the most hardy and the most martial in Morvind, for the deep interior is a demanding environment and the defence of the region is a constant responsibility.

## Significance

Grimhold is the principal fortress of the deep interior of Morvind, and its garrison is the guardian of the routes that connect the interior ranges to the coast. The city is the capital of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/frostwall-clans), and its military function is the foundation of its significance. The fortress of Grimhold is a symbol of the dwarven commitment to the defence of the interior, and the city is a testament to the capacity of the dwarven people to establish and to maintain secure settlements in the most remote and the most demanding environments.
