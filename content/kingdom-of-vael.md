## Overview

The Kingdom of Vael is a major human kingdom of the temperate river valleys of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and it is among the oldest and most prosperous of the political entities on the continent. The kingdom occupies the valley of the River Vael, the greatest waterway of Aethelar, and its territory extends from the foothills of the mountain spine in the west to the coast of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) in the east. The capital of the kingdom is [Vaelmouth](/content/vaelmouth), the port city that stands at the mouth of the river, and the seat of the king and the court is situated in the oldest quarter of the city, overlooking the harbour and the river.

## The Valley and the Realm

The valley of the River Vael is the most fertile region of Aethelar, and the alluvial soils that line the banks of the river are among the most productive in the continent. The agriculture of the valley is the foundation of the kingdom's prosperity, and the grain, the livestock, and the other products of the valley are the primary exports of the realm. The river is navigable for much of its length, and the trade that flows along its waters connects the interior of the kingdom to the coast and the markets of the wider world, making the Kingdom of Vael a major commercial power as well as an agricultural one.

The kingdom is administered from [Vaelmouth](/content/vaelmouth) through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the collection of taxes, the maintenance of the roads and the river, and the administration of justice. The king of Vael is the supreme authority of the realm, and the court is the centre of the political and the social life of the kingdom. The nobility of Vael are the landowners of the valley, and their estates are the primary units of the agricultural economy, producing the grain and the livestock that are the wealth of the realm.

## Trade and the Coast

The trade of the Kingdom of Vael is dominated by the agricultural products of the valley, and the grain, the timber, and the manufactured goods that are produced in the kingdom are exported through the harbour of [Vaelmouth](/content/vaelmouth) to the markets of the eastern coast and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The kingdom is a rival of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/kingdom-of-aldreth) in the trade of the eastern coast, and the competition between the two kingdoms is a driving force in the economy of the continent. The merchants of Vael are among the most influential in Aethelar, and their operations extend from the harbour to the farthest reaches of the trade network.

## The People and the Defence

The population of the Kingdom of Vael is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the valley are among the most prosperous and the most settled in Aethelar. The [elves](/content/elves) of the northern forests are occasional visitors to the valley, and the trade between the humans and the elves is a minor but valued part of the kingdom's commerce. The [dwarves](/content/dwarves) of the mountain spine are present in the western portions of the kingdom, where the foothills of the range provide a habitat for the dwarven communities that are involved in the metal trade.

The defence of the kingdom is maintained by a standing army that is funded by the taxes of the valley, and the fortifications of Vaelmouth and the other settlements of the realm are maintained in good order. The kingdom has been at peace with its neighbours for many generations, but the rivalry with the Kingdom of Aldreth and the occasional tensions with the [Thessaran League](/content/thessaran-league) require the maintenance of a credible military force.

## Significance

The Kingdom of Vael is the major human kingdom of the temperate river valleys and one of the most prosperous and the most influential political entities in Aethelar. Its agricultural wealth and its commercial power are the foundation of its significance, and its trade connects the interior of the continent to the markets of the coast and the wider world. The kingdom is a symbol of the endurance and the prosperity of the human civilisation of Aethelar, and its long history and its stable governance are a model of the political order that has sustained the continent for centuries.
