## Overview

Valdheim is a city in the principal river valley of the interior of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated on the banks of the largest river that flows through the interior ranges. The city is the primary settlement of the [Valdmark](/content/valdmark), the human realm of the interior, and its position in the most fertile valley of the interior has made it the agricultural and the commercial centre of the region. Valdheim is smaller than the coastal cities, but it is the largest and the most prosperous settlement in the Morvind interior, and its trade connects the interior to the coast through the river and the overland routes.

## The River Valley

The river valley that surrounds Valdheim is the most fertile region in the interior of Morvind, and the alluvial soils that line the banks of the river are among the most productive in the continent. The agriculture of the valley is the foundation of the city's prosperity, and the fields that stretch out from the city walls produce grain, vegetables, and the fodder that sustains the livestock of the region. The river is navigable for much of its length, and the barges that carry the goods of Valdheim to the coast and the goods of the coast to the interior are a common sight on the waterway.

The climate of the valley is milder than the surrounding highlands, for the river moderates the temperature and the surrounding peaks shelter the valley from the worst of the wind. The growing season is longer than on the higher plateaus, and the agriculture of the valley is more varied and more productive, for the soil is deeper and the water is more reliable. The valley is the breadbasket of the Morvind interior, and the grain that is produced here is exported to the coast and the other regions of the continent.

The valley is also a route of communication, for the river is navigable for much of its length, and the barges that carry the goods of Valdheim to the coast are a common sight on the waterway. The river descends from the interior ranges through a series of rapids and gorges, and the navigable portion begins at Valdheim, where the current slows and the water deepens. The barges are floated downstream to the coast, carrying the grain and the wool of the valley, and they are towed back upstream by teams of oxen that walk the towpaths along the banks, carrying the metalwork and the manufactured goods of the coast to the interior.

## Trade and the City

The trade of Valdheim is dominated by the agricultural products of the valley and the mineral products of the surrounding mountains, and the city is the primary point of exchange between the interior and the coast. The grain, the wool, and the leather of the valley are traded at Valdheim for the metalwork, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that are imported from the coast and the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) cities of the interior. The river is the primary route of trade, and the wharves and quays that line the riverbank are the centre of the city's commerce. The barges that carry the trade are broad, flat-bottomed vessels that are suited to the shallow waters of the upper river, and the bargees who work them are a familiar sight on the waterway, their vessels loaded with the grain and the wool of the valley on the downstream journey and the metalwork and the goods of the coast on the return.

The overland routes that connect Valdheim to the coast and the interior are also a significant part of the trade, for the river is not navigable beyond the city, and the goods that are destined for the higher valleys and the mountain settlements are carried by pack trains and wagons. The roads that lead from Valdheim into the interior are maintained by the council of the Valdmark, and the road wardens who patrol them are responsible for the upkeep of the surfaces and the bridges that carry the roads across the streams and the gullies of the highlands.

Valdheim is a well-built, prosperous city, and its architecture reflects the character of the interior, with sturdy buildings of stone and timber, steep roofs, and streets that are broad enough for the wagons and the livestock that pass through on their way to and from the market. The market square of Valdheim is the largest in the interior, and the market that is held there is a weekly affair that draws farmers, traders, and craftsmen from the surrounding country. The population of Valdheim is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of dwarves who are involved in the metal trade. The dwarven community of Valdheim is the link between the city and the dwarven settlements of the interior ranges, and the dwarven merchants who reside in the city are among the most influential figures in the trade that connects the valley to the mountains.

## Significance

Valdheim is the capital of the [Valdmark](/content/valdmark) and the primary settlement of the Morvind interior, and its agricultural products are the foundation of the economy of the interior region. The city is the commercial centre of the interior, and its trade connects the valley to the coast and the other regions of the continent through the river and the overland routes. The prosperity of Valdheim is built upon the fertility of the valley and the industry of its people, and the city is the most important and the most prosperous settlement in the Morvind interior.

## History

Valdheim was founded in the early centuries of human settlement in the interior of Morvind, and the site was chosen for the fertility of the valley and the navigability of the river. The earliest settlement was a farming village, and the abundance of the harvest encouraged the growth of the community. The barges that carried the grain to the coast were the foundation of the trade that transformed the village into a town and then a city, and the wharves and the warehouses that line the riverbank are the visible evidence of the prosperity that the trade has brought.

The Valdmark was established with Valdheim as its capital, and the city has been the seat of the realm's governance for centuries. The council of the Valdmark meets in the great hall that stands at the centre of the market square, and the representatives of the valley and the surrounding highlands gather there to settle the business of the realm. The relationship between Valdheim and the dwarven settlements of the interior ranges has been a defining feature of the history of the city, and the trade in metalwork and minerals has been a source of mutual benefit that has sustained the prosperity of both the human and the dwarven communities of the interior.
