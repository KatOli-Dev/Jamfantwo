## Overview

The Vashari Pact is the alliance of the inland caravan cities at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh), the vast desert interior of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). The pact is not a kingdom but a commercial and a defensive alliance, and its member cities retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, caravan management, and defence. The Vashari Pact is the primary political entity of the inland Sahrani, and its collective power in the caravan trade that connects the coast to the interior is the foundation of the economy of the desert regions.

## The Member Cities

The Vashari Pact includes the principal inland cities and settlements at the edge of the Kharesh, and the membership is drawn from the communities that share in the caravan trade. [Vashari](/content/vashari) is the largest and the most influential member of the pact, and its position at the terminus of the great caravan routes gives it a commercial importance that is unmatched by any other inland city. [Shurra](/content/shurra) is a valued member, the oasis settlement deep in the Kharesh that is a vital waypoint on the caravan routes. [Kheled](/content/kheled) is the upland settlement of the desert massifs, and its minerals and its springs are a valued resource for the caravans. [Dunesend](/content/dunesend) is the caravan stop at the edge of the Kharesh, and its services are a critical component of the caravan trade.

The pact is governed by a council of representatives from each member city, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the caravan trade, the management of the routes, and the defence of the inland cities. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the pact has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective force in times of need. The governance of the pact is a model of co-operation among independent cities, and the council is among the most effective political institutions in the inland region.

## The Caravan Trade

The caravan trade is the foundation of the Vashari Pact's economy, and the goods that flow through the caravan routes include the spices, the dyes, and the precious stones of the interior, which are carried by the caravans from the oases and the upland settlements to the markets of the coast, and the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that are carried in the opposite direction. The pact co-ordinates the caravan trade, managing the routes, the provisioning, and the protection of the caravans that travel the Kharesh, and its regulation of the trade ensures that the goods flow efficiently and safely between the coast and the interior.

The pact's relationship with the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/kharesh-tribes), the confederation of nomadic peoples of the desert, is one of mutual dependence, for the tribes provide the knowledge of the desert that the caravans require, and the pact provides the goods and the market that the tribes need. The co-operation between the pact and the tribes is the foundation of the caravan trade, and the two entities work together to maintain the routes, the water sources, and the shelters that sustain the traffic between the coast and the interior.

## Defence and the People

The defence of the inland cities is maintained by a collective force that is funded by the contributions of the member cities, and the fortifications of the individual cities are maintained by each member. The desert is a source of danger as well as trade, and the defence of the caravan routes against raiders and the wild peoples of the deep desert is a constant concern of the pact. The population of the pact is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [salawa](/content/salawa) who are involved in the caravan trade and the guidance of the desert routes.

## Significance

The Vashari Pact is the primary political entity of the inland Sahrani, and its collective power in the caravan trade that connects the coast to the interior is the foundation of the economy of the desert regions. The pact's co-operation with the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/kharesh-tribes) in the caravan trade is a critical component of the economy of Sahrana, and its regulation of the caravan routes is a vital function that sustains the commerce between the coast and the interior. The significance of the Vashari Pact lies in its capacity for collective action and its control of the caravan trade, and the pact is a symbol of the endurance and the enterprise of the inland cities of the Sahrani desert.
