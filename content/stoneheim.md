## Overview

Stoneheim is the greatest [dwarven](/content/dwarves) hall of [Morvind](/content/morvind) and the capital of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim), the largest dwarven realm in the known world. The city is carved into the living rock of the interior ranges, and its halls and chambers extend for many miles into the mountains, forming a subterranean complex that is the most extensive and the most magnificent of any dwarven settlement. Stoneheim is the centre of dwarven civilisation in Morvind, and its wealth, its craftsmanship, and its political influence are unmatched by any other dwarven community.

## The Halls

The halls of Stoneheim are the grandest of their kind, and the great hall is a cavernous space that is supported by pillars of carved stone, each one a masterpiece of dwarven engineering. The hall is the heart of the city, and the forges, the council chambers, and the oldest dwellings are all situated within it. The chambers and passages that branch from the hall extend in every direction, following the veins of ore and the natural cave systems that the dwarves have shaped and enlarged over centuries.

The dwellings of Stoneheim are carved into the walls of the halls and the passages, and the deepest and the most ancient are those of the oldest families, who have inhabited the city for the longest and who hold the most prestigious positions in the community. The deeper dwellings are warmer, for the rock of the mountain retains a constant temperature, and the deepest chambers are those that are closest to the forges and the mines, where the heat of the molten metal and the labour of the smiths warm the stone.

## Mining and Metalwork

The mining operations of Stoneheim are the most extensive of any dwarven city, and the ore that is extracted from the depths beneath the city includes iron, copper, tin, silver, gold, and the rarer metals that are found only in the deepest veins of the Morvind ranges. The mines extend for miles below the city, and the tunnels that connect them to the halls above are works of engineering in their own right, wide enough for the passage of ore carts and ventilated by shafts that are driven through the rock to the surface above.

The metalwork that is produced in the forges of Stoneheim is the finest in the known world, and the smiths of the city are renowned for their skill in the working of iron, steel, and the precious metals. The weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects that are produced in Stoneheim are exported to every continent, and the reputation of the city's smiths is the foundation of the wealth and the influence of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim).

## The City and the Realm

Stoneheim is the capital of the Holds of Stoneheim, the dwarven realm that encompasses the interior ranges of Morvind, and the city is the seat of the king and the council that govern the realm. The population of Stoneheim is almost entirely [dwarven](/content/dwarves), with a small community of [humans](/content/humans) who are involved in the trade between the city and the coast, and a few [gnomes](/content/gnomes) who are drawn by the metalwork and the craftsmanship for which the city is known.

## Significance

Stoneheim is the greatest dwarven city in the known world, and its mineral wealth, its craftsmanship, and its political influence are the foundation of the economy and the culture of the dwarven people of Morvind. The city is the centre of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim), the largest dwarven realm, and its halls are the most extensive and the most magnificent of any subterranean settlement. The prosperity of Stoneheim is built upon the wealth of the mountains and the skill of its people, and the city is a symbol of the endurance and the achievement of the dwarven civilisation.
