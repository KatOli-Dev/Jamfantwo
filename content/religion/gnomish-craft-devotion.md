## The Making as a Form of the Prayer

The gnomish peoples of the woodland hills practise a form of the spiritual life that is centred upon the craft of the making, the work of the hands that transforms the raw material into the finished object. The gnomish craft devotion is not a religion in the manner of the temple faiths, and the gnomes do not maintain the priests or the doctrines or the sacred texts. The craft devotion is the understanding that the act of the making is a form of the prayer, and that the object that is well made is an honour to the divine.

The gnomes are a people of the hands, and the making is the central activity of the gnomish life. The gnomish craftsman who works in the forge or the workshop is engaged in a practice that is both practical and spiritual, and the attention that the craftsman brings to the work is the attention that the gnome brings to the divine.

## The Spirit in the Material

The gnomes believe that the material from which the object is made contains a spirit that is released and shaped by the work of the craftsman. The spirit of the wood, the spirit of the metal, and the spirit of the stone are the presences that the gnome honours in the work, and the gnome who works the material with the respect and the skill is the gnome who releases the spirit in the best form.

The spirit of the wood is the presence that the gnome experiences in the grain and the texture of the timber. The gnomish woodworker who selects the piece of the wood for the carving is attending to the spirit that the wood contains, and the carving that follows is the revelation of the form that the spirit has intended.

The spirit of the metal is the presence that the gnome experiences in the forge and the anvil. The gnomish smith who heats the metal in the forge and shapes it on the anvil is in the dialogue with the spirit of the metal, and the object that emerges from the forging is the resolution of the dialogue.

The spirit of the stone is the presence that the gnome experiences in the cutting and the polishing of the gem. The gnomish lapidary who cuts the stone is revealing the light that the stone has contained, and the polished gem is the expression of the spirit that the stone has held.

## The Workshop as the Sacred Place

The workshop is the sacred place of the gnomish craft devotion, and the gnome who enters the workshop enters a space that is set apart for the work of the making. The workshop is furnished with the tools and the materials that the craftsman requires, and the gnome who works in the workshop is engaged in the practice that is the devotion.

The tools of the workshop are the instruments of the devotion, and the gnome who uses the tools does so with the care and the respect. The tool that is well maintained is the tool that serves the devotion, and the tool that is neglected is the tool that fails in the service.

The gnome who begins the work in the workshop speaks the words of the opening, acknowledging the spirits of the material and the tools and asking for the guidance of the hands. The gnome who finishes the work speaks the words of the closing, thanking the spirits for the assistance and the material for the gift.

## The Practice of the Attention

The practice of the attention is the central discipline of the gnomish craft devotion. The gnome who works at the craft must attend to the work with the full attention, and the gnome who allows the attention to wander is the gnome who produces the flawed work.

The attention is the means by which the gnome enters into the relationship with the spirit of the material. The gnome who attends to the grain of the wood, the temper of the metal, and the clarity of the stone is the gnome who perceives the spirit that the material contains.

The practice of the attention is also the means by which the gnome cultivates the stillness of the mind. The gnome who is absorbed in the work does not think of the other things, and the absorption in the work is the form of the meditation that the craft devotion teaches.

## The Teaching of the Master

The craft devotion is transmitted from the master to the apprentice through the practice of the teaching. The master gnome who takes the apprentice into the workshop teaches the skills of the craft and the discipline of the attention, and the apprentice who learns from the master learns the devotion as well as the trade.

The teaching is not conducted through the lectures or the texts. The master shows the apprentice how the work is done, and the apprentice learns by the watching and the doing. The master speaks the words of the opening and the closing, and the apprentice learns the words by the repetition.

The apprenticeship is the period during which the apprentice learns the devotion as well as the craft, and the apprentice who completes the apprenticeship is the gnome who has been initiated into the practice.

## The Festival of the Making

The gnomish communities observe the festival of the making at the turning of the year, the occasion on which the craftsmen display the work that has been produced through the year. The festival is held in the central clearing of the community, and the gnomes bring the objects that they have made to be seen and admired by the others.

The festival is not a competition, and the gnomes do not judge the work of the others as the better or the worse. The festival is the celebration of the making, and the gnome who looks at the work of the other gnome is attending to the spirit that the other gnome has released through the craft.

## The Craft Devotion and the Other Traditions

The gnomish craft devotion is not a tradition that seeks the converts, and the gnomes do not maintain the correspondence with the human traditions. The gnomes regard the craft devotion as the practice of the gnomish people, and the human faiths are the practices of the human peoples.

The gnomes respect the human traditions without adopting them, and the gnomes who trade with the human settlements have learned enough of the human beliefs to conduct the relations with the courtesy. The gnomes recognise in the Still Flame and the Forged Flame the kindred understanding that the work of the hands is a form of the devotion, and the gnomes have exchanged the craftsmen with the Forged Flame on the occasion.
