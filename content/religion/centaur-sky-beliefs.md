## The Reading of the Sky and the Plains

The centaur kindreds of the Great Grass Plains maintain a body of the beliefs and the practices that are centred upon the sky and the stars and the open grasslands. The centaur sky beliefs are not a religion in the manner of the temple faiths or the contemplative orders, and the centaurs do not maintain the priests or the doctrines or the sacred texts. The sky beliefs are the understanding of the order that governs the plains, the recognition of the patterns that the sky reveals and the guidance that the patterns provide for the centaur way of life.

The centaurs are a people of the open plains, and the sky is the constant presence in the centaur life. The sky by day is the source of the light and the warmth, and the sky by night is the map by which the centaurs navigate the vast expanse of the grasslands. The stars are the fixed points in the changing world, and the centaurs who read the stars know the directions and the seasons and the times of the movements.

## The Celestial Order

The centaurs believe that the sky is governed by an order that is reflected in the patterns of the stars and the movements of the celestial bodies. The order is not a law that is imposed by a deity but a rhythm that is inherent in the nature of the sky, and the centaurs who attend to the rhythm are the people who live in the harmony with the world.

The stars are the principal signs of the order, and the centaurs have named the constellations that appear in the night sky of the plains. The constellations are the figures of the centaur mythology, representing the heroes and the creatures and the events of the stories that the kindreds preserve. The constellation of the Great Horse, the constellation of the Hunter, and the constellation of the Running Water are the most important of the centaur constellations, and the rising and the setting of the constellations mark the seasons of the centaur year.

The moon is the second of the celestial bodies that the centaurs attend to, and the phases of the moon are the markers of the passage of the month. The full moon is the time of the gathering, when the kindreds come together for the rites and the celebrations and the renewal of the bonds. The new moon is the time of the quiet, when the kindreds are still and the attention is turned inward.

## The Reading of the Signs

The centaurs read the signs that the sky and the plains provide, and the reading of the signs is the skill that the centaur learns from the youth. The elder centaur teaches the younger centaur to read the clouds for the coming of the weather, to read the stars for the direction of the travel, and to read the behaviour of the animals for the signs of the danger and the opportunity.

The reading of the signs is not a divination in the manner that the human fortune-tellers practise. The centaurs do not believe that the signs reveal the future or the will of the divine. The signs reveal the condition of the world, and the centaur who reads the signs knows what is happening and what is likely to happen based on the patterns that the centaur has learned to recognise.

The centaur who reads the signs is the centaur who is prepared for the changes that the plains bring. The reading of the signs is the means by which the centaur lives in the harmony with the world, and the centaur who neglects the signs is the centaur who suffers the consequences of the unpreparedness.

## The Seasonal Gatherings

The seasonal gatherings are the principal rites of the centaur sky beliefs, conducted at the times of the solstice and the equinox and at the times of the full moons that mark the important transitions of the centaur year. The kindreds gather at the traditional meeting places on the plains, and the elders lead the observances that mark the passage of the season.

The gathering at the summer solstice is the largest of the seasonal gatherings, for the summer is the time of the abundance and the kindreds come together to celebrate the bounty of the plains. The gathering includes the sharing of the food, the telling of the stories, and the observance of the rising of the sun on the longest day.

The gathering at the winter solstice is the most solemn of the seasonal gatherings, for the winter is the time of the hardship and the kindreds come together to support one another through the difficult season. The gathering includes the reading of the signs for the coming of the spring and the renewal of the bonds that hold the kindreds together.

## The Plains as the Sacred Ground

The plains are the sacred ground of the centaur sky beliefs, and the centaurs regard the grasslands as the gift of the sky. The plains are the place where the centaurs live and the place where the centaurs die, and the centaur who is born on the plains and who lives the centaur life on the plains and who dies on the plains is the centaur who has lived the life that the sky intends.

The centaurs do not build the permanent structures on the plains, for the building of the structure would be the fixing of the centaur life in the place where the centaur way of life is the movement. The centaur encampment is a temporary arrangement, and the centaurs who leave the encampment leave no trace of the presence.

## The Centaur Beliefs and the Human Traditions

The centaur sky beliefs are not a tradition that seeks the converts, and the centaurs do not maintain the correspondence with the human traditions. The centaurs regard the sky beliefs as the understanding that is given to the centaur people, and the human traditions are the understandings that are given to the human peoples.

The centaurs respect the human traditions without adopting them, and the centaurs expect the humans to reciprocate the respect. The centaurs who trade with the human settlements of the plains have learned enough of the human beliefs to conduct the relations with the courtesy, but the centaurs do not adopt the human faiths and they do not expect the humans to adopt the centaur beliefs.
