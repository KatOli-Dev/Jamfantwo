## The Three Continents

The established geography of the known world recognises three canonical continents: [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The names Eastern Reach, Thessara, Ashara, and Kharos describe regions of Aethelar; Nordheim and Stormfall describe regions of Morvind; and Southern Lands is the northern cartographers' collective name for the remote southern region of Sahrana around the inland seas. These regional names appear as separate entries in some atlases because their peoples, languages, and political customs differ, but they do not represent additional continents.

The three continents are separated by the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean), and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). Smaller seas, straits, and inland waters make the boundaries of the continents useful for broad description but less important to travellers than the routes that join their regions. The interior of every continent remains less accurately charted than its principal coasts.

## Aethelar and Its Regions

[Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the largest of the three continents and occupies the central position between the northern waters and the warmer southern seas. Its eastern coast faces the Auric Ocean and its western coast faces the Greyring Ocean. A mountain spine crosses the interior from northwest to southeast, dividing river systems and creating the passes that have connected the eastern and western shores since the earliest settlements. The continent contains the greatest concentration of human kingdoms, elven forests, dwarven highlands, and settled river valleys in the known world.

The [Eastern Reach](/content/location/natural/region/eastern-reach) is the long eastern region of Aethelar, where broad river plains, estuaries, reefs, and port councils control the approaches to the Auric Ocean. Its name comes from the mariners who travelled east along the Aethelaran shore and used the reach speech in the ports and river towns. It is not land beyond the ocean; it is the outer belt of Aethelar's eastern coast and the connected river country behind it.

[Thessara](/content/location/natural/region/thessara) is the highland and harbour region of eastern Aethelar. The [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) forms its watershed, and the passes between its valleys connect the eastern ports with the interior. The [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) is a league of Aethelaran coastal cities, while Thessari is the regional language of the highlands and the eastern trade. The name does not denote a separate eastern continent.

[Ashara](/content/location/natural/region/ashara) is the warm river region of southern and eastern Aethelar, where the [Sunken Crown](/content/location/natural/feature/sunken-crown) supplies three great basins with water. Its wetlands, forests, lagoons, and seasonal floodplains give it a distinct regional economy based on rice, resin, timber, river transport, and marsh products. Ashari is the principal river speech, and its councils govern water and flood works without a single continental crown.

[Kharos](/content/location/natural/region/kharos) is the volcanic western region of Aethelar, extending along the Greyring coast and into the dry highlands of the interior. The [Ember Ridge](/content/location/natural/feature/ember-ridge), its hot springs, and its glass groves distinguish it from the forested and riverine regions farther east. Kharoshi is spoken in its highland settlements and western ports. The Kharos glass groves must not be confused with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), which is the desert interior of Sahrana.

## Morvind and Its Regions

[Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) lies north of Aethelar across the Stormfall Sea. It is a cold, mountainous continent with narrow coastal lowlands, deep river valleys, and extensive dwarven workings. Its eastern ranges contain the largest concentration of dwarven population in the known world, while human settlements occupy the coasts and the agricultural valleys. Ore, metalwork, timber, fish, and preserved food move from Morvind toward the markets of Aethelar and Sahrana.

[Nordheim](/content/location/natural/region/nordheim) is the northern region of Morvind, centred on the Frostwall Tundra, the southern forests, and the [Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range). Its people use Nordhal in the southern harbours and Frostic in the tundra and highland refuges. Nordheim is a regional name for the northern country of Morvind, not a fourth continent.

The [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is the southern maritime region of Morvind, stretching from the Greyring Ocean to the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). Its fjords and ports face the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), and the kingdom of [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) coordinates the principal coastal settlements. Stormfall is also used in the names of the coast, its trade, and its regional speech; the Stormfall Sea itself is a body of water, not a separate continent.

## Sahrana and the Southern Lands

[Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) lies south of Aethelar across the Auric Ocean. Its northern coast contains fertile city-states and its interior is dominated by the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), a desert of dunes, rocky uplands, and caravan routes. The [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) supports intensive agriculture and maritime trade, while the desert routes connect the coast to the inland cities and the far south.

The [Southern Lands](/content/location/natural/region/southern-lands) is the northern navigators' name for the far southern region of Sahrana, beyond the Kharesh and around the [Greater Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea) and [Lesser Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/lesser-inland-sea). The region is cooler and wetter than the northern desert, with highlands, open woodland, fertile basins, and lakeside settlements. The [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) governs these communities as a federation from Vereth. Southern Lands therefore describes a remote Sahrani region and its surrounding waters, not a separate continent.

## The Great Waters

The [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) lies east of Aethelar and separates it from Sahrana. Warm currents and seasonal winds make it the busiest long-distance sea in the known world. The [Auric Passage](/content/location/route/trade/auric-passage) carries grain, cloth, iron, and manufactured goods southward and spices, dyes, and precious stones northward. The [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) lie along the principal routes and provide pilots, fishing grounds, and sheltered anchorages.

The [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) lies west of Aethelar and south of Morvind. Its cold currents support fisheries and whaling along the western coasts, but fog, long swells, and limited harbours make it less travelled than the Auric. The [Drowned Isles](/content/location/natural/feature/drowned-isles) are an uncertain archipelago within its waters, shown differently on the charts of the Velden and Morvind schools.

The [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) separates Morvind from Aethelar. Its shallow floor, shoals, and autumn gales make the crossing hazardous, but its ports carry the metals and fish of Morvind southward and the grain and cloth of Aethelar northward. The [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) connects the Stormfall Sea to the Auric Ocean between the southeastern coast of Morvind and the northeastern coast of Aethelar. It is the principal northern chokepoint of intercontinental trade.

## The Routes between Regions

The geography of the three continents is understood through the routes that join their regions. The [Thessaran Coastal Route](/content/location/route/trade/thessaran-coastal-route) follows eastern Aethelar from the harbour country of the Eastern Reach through the southern ports and the highland approaches of Thessara. The [Grassland Traderoad](/content/location/route/trade/grassland-traderoad) crosses the Great Grass Plains of central Aethelar, while the [Deep Realm Tradeway](/content/location/route/trade/deep-realm-tradeway) and the [Stoneheim Highland Road](/content/location/route/trade/stoneheim-highland-road) connect Aethelaran and Morvindi mountain settlements to the coasts.

In Sahrana, the [Northern Coast Route](/content/location/route/trade/northern-coast-route) links the city-states of the Green Crescent, and the [Kharesh Caravan Way](/content/location/route/trade/kharesh-caravan-way) carries goods between the coast, the desert oases, and the Southern Lands. The [Sahrani Desert Trail](/content/location/route/trade/sahrani-desert-trail) follows a more direct southern course for merchants travelling toward the remote highlands. The routes are governed by different kingdoms, leagues, councils, and tribal authorities, but their goods and travellers form one connected commercial system.

## Cartographic Disputes

The three-continent model describes the accepted physical arrangement, not every claim made by a mapmaker. Cartographers disagree over the eastern extent of the Eastern Reach, the uncharted interior beyond the Kharesh caravan routes, and the precise boundaries between the named regions of Aethelar and Morvind. These disputes concern coastlines, watersheds, and political control; they do not establish additional canonical continents.

The academies also differ over how the Southern Lands should be represented. Northern charts once left the far south blank or marked it as a separate landmass because the Kharesh crossing was difficult and the inland seas were known only through caravan accounts. Recent surveys and the archives of the Southern Reach place the region within Sahrana, although the southern coast and the highland interior remain incompletely mapped. The uncertainty explains the older use of the name without changing the continental classification.

The broad outlines of the three continents are established by independent surveys, shipping logs, caravan records, and the observations compiled in the [Known-World Codex](/content/magic/relic/codex-of-the-known-world). The boundaries of named regions remain open to correction, but Aethelar, Morvind, and Sahrana are the canonical continents used by historians, governments, merchants, and navigators.
