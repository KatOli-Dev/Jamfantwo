## The Uncovering of the Hidden

The myth of the First Veil is the central teaching of the tradition of the Sunless Veils, the faith that is also known as the Veiled One or the First Veilers. The myth tells of a time when the world was covered by a great veil that hid the true nature of reality from the perception of the peoples, and of the moment when the first veil was lifted and the truth was revealed. The myth is not a story of creation in the ordinary sense, for the Sunless Veils teach that the world was not created at a specific time but has always existed in a state of being that is partially hidden and partially revealed.

The myth is preserved in the Veil Cloisters, the establishments where the practitioners of the Sunless Veils maintain the tradition and instruct the novices. The myth is not written down in the manner of the scriptures of the other religions, for the Sunless Veils teach that the truth cannot be captured in words and that the written text is itself a veil that obscures the reality that it attempts to describe. The myth is transmitted orally, from teacher to student, and the version that each student receives is tailored to the student's capacity to understand.

## The State of the Veiled

The myth begins with a description of the world before the first veil was lifted, a world in which the peoples perceived only the surfaces of things and could not see the deeper reality that lay beneath. The sun gave light, and the moon gave light, and the stars gave light, but the light was reflected from the veil and did not penetrate to the truth. The peoples of the world lived in a condition of partial awareness, the myth says, like people who see the shadows on the wall of a cave and who mistake the shadows for the reality.

The veil was not a physical substance, the myth teaches, but a condition of perception, a limitation of the mind that prevented the peoples from seeing the world as it truly was. The veil was not imposed by any external power; it was inherent in the nature of the mind itself, and the lifting of the veil required a transformation of the mind that the peoples could not achieve by their own efforts alone.

The Sunless Veils teach that the state of the veiled is the ordinary condition of human existence, and that the practices of the faith are the means by which the practitioner can begin to lift the veils that obscure the truth. The First Veil was the first lifting, the first moment when a being saw through the veil and perceived the reality that lay beyond it, and the myth describes this moment as the beginning of the possibility of liberation.

## The First Lifting

The first being to lift the veil was not a god or a spirit or a being of a higher order, the myth says, but a human being who lived in the time before the memory of the peoples extends. The first veiler was a woman known in the tradition as the Unnameable, for the Sunless Veils teach that the name of the first veiler is itself a veil that must be lifted. The Unnameable was an ordinary person, the myth says, not distinguished by any particular virtue or power, and the lifting occurred not as a result of any special effort but as a gift that was given without reason and without cause.

The Unnameable was sitting in the shade of a tree, the myth says, watching the light of the afternoon sun as it filtered through the leaves, when the veil lifted and she saw the world as it truly was. The moment is described in the tradition as the moment of the clear seeing, and the descriptions of the experience are consistent across the different versions of the myth: the Unnameable saw that the world was not composed of separate things but of a single reality that manifested in infinite forms, and she saw that the forms were not solid and permanent but fluid and changing, and she saw that the separation between herself and the world was an illusion that the veil had created.

## The Teaching of the Unnameable

The Unnameable attempted to describe what she had seen to the other members of her community, the myth says, but she found that words could not capture the experience and that the others could not understand what she was trying to convey. The Unnameable realised that the teaching could not be transmitted through language alone, and she developed the practices that became the foundation of the Sunless Veils: the disciplines of meditation and attention and contemplation that prepare the mind for the lifting of the veils.

The Unnameable taught that the veils are not lifted all at once, the myth says, but one by one, and that each lifting reveals a deeper level of reality while also revealing that there are further veils to be lifted. The First Veil was the beginning of the journey, and the journey is infinite, for the reality that lies beyond the veils cannot be fully known by a finite mind, and the practitioner who lifts one veil discovers that there are others beyond it.

The Sunless Veils teach that the Unnameable did not establish a religion in the manner of the great temple faiths. The Unnameable taught a practice, not a doctrine, and the tradition that bears her teaching is a tradition of practice rather than of belief. The practitioner of the Sunless Veils is not required to accept any particular account of the nature of the world; the practitioner is required to practise the disciplines that the Unnameable developed, and the understanding that arises from the practice is the only understanding that matters.

## The Succession of the Veilers

The Unnameable transmitted her teaching to a small group of students, the myth says, and the students transmitted the teaching to their own students, and the succession has continued through the ages to the present day. Each generation of veilers has added its own understanding to the tradition, and the practices have been refined and adapted to the conditions of the different times and places in which the tradition has been maintained.

The succession is not a hierarchy in the manner of the ecclesiastical orders of the temple faiths. The veilers are not ranked according to their authority or their knowledge, and there is no central authority that determines the orthodoxy of the teaching. The veilers are connected by the practice that they share, and the tradition is maintained by the mutual recognition of the practitioners who have experienced the lifting of the veils.

The myth of the First Veil is retold by each generation of veilers to the next, and each retelling is different, for the myth cannot be captured in a fixed form. The veilers say that the myth is alive, and that it changes with each telling, and that the changes are not corruptions of the original but developments that reveal aspects of the truth that the earlier versions had not expressed.

## The Relation to the Other Traditions

The Sunless Veils regard the other religious traditions of the world with a certain distance, for the other traditions are concerned with the surfaces of things, the veils that the Sunless Veils seek to lift. The Still Flame teaches of the warmth that underlies the world, and the Sunless Veils acknowledge that the warmth is real but maintain that it is itself a veil that obscures a deeper reality. The Shield and Song teaches of the watch and the song, and the Sunless Veils acknowledge that the watch and the song are practices that can prepare the mind for the lifting of the veils, but they maintain that the watch and the song are not the final truth.

The Sunless Veils do not claim that their tradition is superior to the other traditions, and they do not seek to convert the practitioners of the other faiths. The Sunless Veils teach that the different traditions are appropriate for different minds and different stages of development, and that the practitioner who is not ready for the lifting of the veils should follow the tradition that suits the practitioner's current condition. The Sunless Veils are not a path for everyone, the tradition teaches, and the attempt to force the practice on those who are not ready for it is itself a form of violence that the veilers must avoid.

## The Continuing Lifting

The myth of the First Veil does not belong to the past, the tradition teaches, for the lifting of the veils continues in the present, and every practitioner who experiences the clear seeing participates in the same moment that the Unnameable experienced. The First Veil was not a single event that happened once and then was finished; it was the beginning of a process that continues through the ages, and each practitioner who lifts a veil is adding to the cumulative understanding of the tradition.

The Sunless Veils teach that the world is not a place of fixed and permanent objects but a place of infinite depth and infinite mystery, and the practice of the veilers is the practice of exploring this depth. The First Veil was the first step on a journey that has no end, and the veilers who follow the path that the Unnameable opened are travelling still.
