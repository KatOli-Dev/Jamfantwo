## Overview

Karhold is the greatest [dwarven](/content/dwarves) city of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and it is the capital of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/deep-realm-of-karhold). The city is carved into the living rock of the mountains, its halls and chambers extending for miles into the interior of the range, and its wealth is built upon the mineral deposits that are worked in the depths beneath the city. Karhold is one of the oldest dwarven settlements in the known world, and its halls are among the most extensive and the most magnificent of any dwarven city, rivalled only by the great halls of [Morvind](/content/morvind).

## The Halls

The halls of Karhold are carved into the granite of the mountain spine, and they are arranged in a pattern that reflects the geology of the range, following the veins of ore and the natural cave systems that the dwarves have enlarged and shaped over centuries. The great hall of Karhold is the largest single chamber in the city, a cavernous space that is supported by pillars of living rock and lit by the glow of the forges that line its walls. The hall is the heart of the city, the gathering place for the community, and the site of the forges, the council chambers, and the oldest dwellings.

The dwellings of Karhold are carved into the walls of the halls and the passages that branch from them, and each dwelling is a self-contained apartment with its own hearth, its own water supply, and its own ventilation shaft. The deeper dwellings are warmer, for the rock of the mountain retains a constant temperature throughout the year, and the deepest chambers are those of the oldest families, who have inhabited the city for the longest and who hold the most prestigious positions in the community.

## Mining and Metalwork

The mining operations of Karhold are the foundation of its wealth, and the ore that is extracted from the depths beneath the city includes iron, copper, tin, and the precious metals that are among the most valued products of the mountain spine. The mines extend for miles below the city, following the veins of ore deeper and further as the older workings are exhausted, and the miners of Karhold are among the most skilled and the most experienced in the known world. The ore that is extracted is smelted in the forges of the great hall, and the metalwork that is produced in Karhold is noted for its quality and its durability, and it is exported to the markets of the lowlands and the coast.

The metalwork of Karhold includes tools, weapons, armour, and the decorative objects for which the dwarven craftsmen are renowned, and the reputation of Karhold's smiths extends throughout Aethelar and beyond. The city is also a centre for the working of stone, and the stonemasons of Karhold are responsible for the construction and the maintenance of the city's halls, the fortifications, and the roads and bridges that connect the mountain spine to the lowlands.

## The City and the Realm

Karhold is the capital of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/deep-realm-of-karhold), the dwarven kingdom that encompasses the mountain spine of Aethelar, and the city is the seat of the king and the council that govern the realm. The population of Karhold is almost entirely [dwarven](/content/dwarves), with a small community of [humans](/content/humans) who are involved in the trade between the city and the lowlands, and a few [gnomes](/content/gnomes) who are drawn by the metalwork and the craftsmanship for which the city is known.

## Significance

Karhold is the greatest dwarven city of Aethelar and the capital of the Deep Realm, and its mineral wealth and its craftsmanship are the foundation of the economy of the mountain spine. The city is the most important dwarven settlement on the continent, and its influence extends throughout the range and into the lowlands, where the metalwork and the stone of Karhold are valued commodities. The city is a symbol of the endurance and the skill of the dwarven people, and its halls are among the most impressive achievements of subterranean architecture in the known world.
