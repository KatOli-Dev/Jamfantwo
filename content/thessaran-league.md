## Overview

The Thessaran League is a confederation of eastern coast port cities of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), united by their common interest in the maritime trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) and their shared dependence on the sea for their prosperity. The league is not a kingdom but a commercial and a defensive alliance, and its member cities retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, navigation, and defence. The Thessaran League is one of the most influential political entities in Aethelar, and its collective power in the maritime trade of the eastern coast rivals that of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/kingdom-of-aldreth).

## The Member Cities

The Thessaran League includes the principal port cities of the eastern coast, and the membership is drawn from the settlements that share in the Auric trade. [Eldreth](/content/eldreth) is the largest and the most influential member of the league, though the city is also the capital of the Kingdom of Aldreth, and its dual role as a member of the league and the capital of a kingdom is a source of complexity in the politics of the eastern coast. [Caerwyn](/content/caerwyn) is a valued member, renowned for its shipyards and the vessels they produce, and [Thornhaven](/content/thornhaven) is a significant member, commanding the river and the mountain passes of the eastern interior. [Sallowmere](/content/sallowmere) is the southernmost member, and its trade with the warm plains and the southern regions is a valued component of the league's commerce.

The league is governed by a council of representatives from each member city, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the trade, the defence, and the navigation of the eastern coast. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the league has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the league is a model of co-operation among independent cities, and the council is among the most effective political institutions in Aethelar. The council meets in the great hall of Eldreth, a building that was donated to the league by the city in the early years of the alliance, and the hall has served as the seat of the council ever since.

The council elects a presiding officer, known as the Thessar, who serves for a term of three years and is responsible for the conduct of the council's business and the implementation of its decisions. The Thessar is chosen from among the representatives, and the office rotates among the member cities, a convention that ensures that no single city dominates the governance of the league. The Thessar is assisted by a body of administrators who manage the day-to-day affairs of the league, including the maintenance of the lighthouses, the collection of the contributions, and the co-ordination of the collective fleet.

## Trade and Navigation

The trade of the Thessaran League is the collective commerce of its member cities, and the goods that flow through the harbours of the league include the products of every continent and the islands. The league co-ordinates the maritime trade of the eastern coast, managing the lighthouses, the pilotage services, and the navigation aids that are essential to the safety and the efficiency of the trade routes. The league also maintains a collective fleet that protects the trade routes from pirates and raiders, and the fleet is funded by the contributions of the member cities and commanded by an admiral who is appointed by the council.

The league is a rival of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/kingdom-of-vael) in the trade of the eastern coast, and the competition between the league and the kingdom is a driving force in the economy of the continent. The league's control of the maritime trade routes and the kingdom's control of the river trade create a balance of power that has prevented either from dominating the eastern coast entirely, and the relationship between the two is one of competition and occasional co-operation. The rivalry has been a source of tension at times, but it has also been a spur to enterprise, for the competition between the league and the kingdom has driven the expansion of the trade and the improvement of the routes that serve it.

## Significance

The Thessaran League is the primary confederation of the eastern coast port cities, and its collective power in the maritime trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) is among the greatest of any political entity in Aethelar. The league's co-ordination of the trade, the navigation, and the defence of the eastern coast is a model of co-operation among independent cities, and its council is one of the most effective political institutions on the continent. The significance of the league lies in its commercial power and its capacity for collective action, and the league is a symbol of the maritime tradition and the commercial enterprise that are the hallmarks of the eastern coast.

## History

The Thessaran League was founded in the aftermath of a period of raiding and disorder that threatened the trade of the eastern coast, when the port cities recognised that their individual efforts to protect their commerce were insufficient, and that a collective response was required. The founding charter of the league was signed in the great hall of Eldreth, and the original members were the principal port cities of the coast, each of which contributed a representative to the council that was established to govern the new alliance.

The league grew in power and influence over the centuries that followed, as the maritime trade of the eastern coast expanded and the member cities prospered. The collective fleet was established in the early years of the league, and the fleet has been the primary instrument of the league's power, protecting the trade routes from the pirates and the raiders who have threatened them. The league has negotiated treaties with the kingdoms and the powers that border the eastern coast, and the treaties have secured the rights of navigation and the access to the ports that are essential to the trade. The history of the league is a record of successful co-operation among independent cities, and the league is regarded as one of the most enduring and the most effective political institutions in the known world.
