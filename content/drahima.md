## Overview

Drahima is a coastal city-state on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), renowned throughout the known world for its dye works and the vivid colours that are produced in its workshops. The city stands on a broad, open bay on the coast where the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) meets the fertile strip of the northern coast, and its position near the sources of the dye-producing plants of the interior foothills has made it the primary centre of the dye trade in Sahrana. Drahima is a prosperous, industrious city, and its wealth is built upon the production and the export of the dyes that are among the most valued products of the Sahrani coast.

## The Dye Works

The dye works of Drahima are the most extensive and the most productive in the known world, and the dyes that are produced in the city's workshops are prized for their colour, their durability, and their variety. The dyes are derived from the plants that grow in the foothills and the upland valleys of the Sahrani interior, including the indigo, the madder, and the other dye-producing plants that are cultivated in the region, and the dye works of Drahima are the primary processing centre for these materials. The process of dye production is complex and labour-intensive, involving the cultivation and the harvesting of the plants, the extraction and the preparation of the dye, and the fixing and the finishing of the dyed materials, and the workers of Drahima are skilled in every stage of the process.

The dyes that are produced in Drahima are exported to every continent, and the dyed cloth and the raw dye that are shipped from the city's harbour are among the most valuable products of the Sahrani trade. The colours that are produced in the dye works of Drahima are noted for their depth and their permanence, and the dyes of Drahima are used in the production of the finest cloth and the most valued garments in the known world. The reputation of the city's dye works is the foundation of its wealth, and the dyers of Drahima are among the most skilled and the most respected craftsmen in Sahrana.

## The Harbour and the Trade

The harbour of Drahima is a broad, open bay that is sheltered by a headland that extends from the southern end of the city, and the approach is marked by lighthouses that are visible for many miles along the coast. The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses that are situated along the waterfront are filled with the dyes and the dyed materials that are the city's primary export. The trade that flows through the harbour is dominated by the export of the dyes and the import of the raw materials and the goods that the city needs, including the cloth that is brought from the northern continents to be dyed in the workshops of Drahima.

## The City

Drahima is a substantial, well-built city, and its architecture reflects the industrious character of the community, with buildings of light-coloured stone, broad streets, and the workshops and the warehouses that are the centre of the city's industry. The dye works are the dominant feature of the city, and the vats and the drying frames that are situated along the waterfront and in the industrial quarters are a vivid and a distinctive sight, for the colours of the dyes permeate the streets and the buildings, giving the city a brightness that is unlike any other in the known world. The population of Drahima is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a small community of traders and merchants from the northern continents.

## Significance

Drahima is the primary centre of the dye trade in Sahrana, and its dyes are among the most valued products of the Sahrani coast. The city is a member of the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast), and its dye production is a significant component of the economy of the Sahrani coast and the continent as a whole. The significance of Drahima lies in its dye works and the quality of its dyes, which are unmatched by any other city in the known world, and the city is a symbol of the industry and the craftsmanship of the Sahrani people.
