## The Ornament of the Body

Jewellery and adornment are the arts of decorating the human body with objects of beauty and significance. The arts are practised by every thinking people in the known world, for the desire to ornament the body appears to be as universal as the desire to cover it. The forms and the materials of jewellery vary greatly across the regions and the cultures, but the purpose is the same: to mark the status, the wealth, the allegiance, and the identity of the wearer.

The materials of the jeweller are the precious metals and the precious stones that the earth provides. Gold and silver are the principal metals of the jeweller's craft, worked by the techniques that the [Metalwork and Jewellery](/content/art/metalwork-and-jewellery) tradition has developed. The precious stones are the diamonds and the rubies, the sapphires and the emeralds, the garnets and the amethysts, the pearls and the coral, that are gathered from the mines and the coasts of the three continents.

## The Jewellery of the Northern Courts

The courts of the north, the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) and the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), have a tradition of jewellery that is suited to the conditions of the north. The northern jewellers work in gold and silver, but they also work in the materials that the north provides, the amber of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) and the jet of the Morvind uplands. The northern jewellery is characterised by its boldness and its weight, with large stones set in heavy mounts that are designed to be seen from a distance.

The northern peoples wear jewellery as a sign of status and as a store of wealth. The chieftain of the clan wears a torque of twisted gold around the neck, the weight of the torque measuring the wealth of the chieftain and the size of the clan. The warriors of the north wear arm-rings of silver and bronze, each ring representing a victory in battle or a service to the chieftain. The women of the north wear brooches and pendants of silver and gold, decorated with the patterns of the northern tradition that are the signs of the family to which the woman belongs.

The amber of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is the most distinctive material of the northern jewellery. The amber is the fossilised resin of the ancient forests, washed up on the shores of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and gathered by the people of the coast. The amber is cut and polished and set in silver and gold, and the amber jewellery of the north is among the most valued of the northern exports.

## The Jewellery of the Aldreth Realm

The [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) realm has a tradition of jewellery that is more refined than the tradition of the north. The jewellers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) work in gold and silver, inlaid with the precious stones that are imported from every region of the known world. The Aldreth jewellery is characterised by its delicacy and its precision, with the stones set in the mounts that allow the light to pass through them and to reveal the colour and the clarity that the stone contains.

The jewellers of Eldreth are the most accomplished in the human world. They have mastered the technique of enamelling, the fusion of powdered glass to a metal surface that produces a coloured decoration that is as durable as the metal itself. The enamel of Eldreth is used for the decoration of the pendants and the brooches that are the principal forms of the Aldreth jewellery, and the colours of the Eldreth enamel are the standard of quality for the enamellers of the known world.

The jewellery of the Aldreth court is the expression of the status and the wealth of the nobility. The noble of the Aldreth court wears a chain of gold around the neck, with the links that are shaped and decorated in the pattern that is the sign of the noble house. The rings of the Aldreth nobility are set with the stones that are the symbols of the office or the title that the wearer holds, and the ring that is given to the new member of a noble house is the sign of the bond that connects the member to the house.

## The Jewellery of the Sahrani Courts

The courts of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) have a tradition of jewellery that is distinct from the traditions of the north. The Sahrani jewellers work in gold and silver, but they also work in the materials of the desert, the turquoise of the mines of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the coral of the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast). The Sahrani jewellery is characterised by its use of colour and its love of pattern, with the stones set in the mounts that are decorated with the filigree and the granulation that are the techniques of the Sahrani tradition.

The women of the Sahrani courts wear jewellery of great complexity, with the earrings and the necklaces that are composed of many elements arranged in the patterns that are the tradition of the Sahrani style. The jewellery of the Sahrani women is also a form of protection, for the amulets and the charms that are incorporated into the jewellery are believed to ward off the evil eye and the other dangers that the desert presents.

The turquoise of the Kharesh mines is the most distinctive material of the Sahrani jewellery. The turquoise is a stone of a blue-green colour that is found in the veins of the desert mountains, and it is valued by the Sahrani jewellers for the colour that matches the colour of the desert sky at dawn. The turquoise is set in silver and gold, and the turquoise jewellery of the Sahrani is among the most valued of the Sahrani exports.

## The Jewellery of the Plains Confederation

The [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) has a tradition of jewellery that is based on the materials that the plains provide. The plains jewellers work in the silver that is mined in the mountains of the plains, and they use the beads that are made from the shells of the rivers and the bones of the animals of the plains. The plains jewellery is characterised by its use of the beadwork and the quillwork that are the traditional techniques of the plains decorative tradition.

The plains people wear jewellery that is arranged in the patterns that carry the meanings that the plains people understand. The beadwork of the plains jewellery uses the colours that represent the elements of the plains world, the red of the earth, the blue of the sky, the yellow of the sun, and the white of the snow. The patterns of the plains jewellery are the same patterns that the [Carpet and Rug Weaving](/content/art/carpet-and-rug-weaving) tradition uses, and the jewellery of the plains is the expression of the identity of the wearer in the same manner that the carpet is the expression of the identity of the weaver.

## The Symbolic Meaning of Jewellery

Jewellery in the known world is not merely decorative but carries symbolic meaning that the people of the region understand. The ring that is worn on a specific finger indicates the marriage status of the wearer, with the ring that is placed on the hand of the spouse at the ceremony of the [Hands Joined](/content/culture/hands-joined) being the bond that connects the two people for the duration of their lives.

The pendant that is worn around the neck may carry the symbol of the faith of the wearer, the flame of the [Still Flame](/content/religion/still-flame) or the wave of the [Sea Mothers Court](/content/religion/sea-mothers-court) or the star of the [Watcher](/content/religion/watcher). The pendant is the sign of the wearer's devotion, and the material from which the pendant is made measures the depth of the wearer's commitment.

The jewellery that is given as a gift at the ceremonies of the [First-Grain Feast](/content/culture/first-grain-feast) and the [Crown Harvest](/content/culture/crown-harvest) is the expression of the bond between the giver and the receiver. The gift of a piece of jewellery is a gift of value, and the recipient is expected to wear the jewellery as a sign of the respect that the recipient holds for the giver.

## The Guilds of the Jewellers

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the jewellers are among the most exclusive of the trade organisations in the known world. The guild of the jewellers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) is the most influential, and the standards that the Eldreth guild sets are the standards that the jewellers of the other cities follow. The guild of the jewellers of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is the second most influential, and the Sahrani jewellers are the masters of the techniques that are distinct to the Sahrani tradition.

The training of the jeweller is a long and demanding process. The apprentice must master the techniques of the metalworking and the stone setting, the enamelling and the filigree, and the design and the composition that the craft requires. The apprentice must also learn the meanings of the symbols and the patterns that the jewellery of the region uses, for the jeweller must be able to produce the jewellery that the customer requires for the specific purpose that the jewellery will serve.

The jeweller of the known world is the maker of the objects that are the most intimate and the most personal of the works of the craftsman. The jewellery that the jeweller produces is worn against the skin of the wearer, and it becomes a part of the wearer's identity. The relationship between the jeweller and the customer is therefore a relationship of trust, and the jeweller who is known for the quality of the work and the honesty of the dealing is the jeweller who is most respected.
