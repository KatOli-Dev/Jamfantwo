## The Craft of Wood

Woodcarving and furniture making are the arts of shaping wood into objects of use and beauty. Wood is among the most versatile of materials, available in every region where trees grow, and the working of wood is among the most widespread of the crafts. Every thinking people has developed techniques for the shaping of wood, and the character of the woodwork of a region reflects the forests that grow there and the needs of the people who inhabit them.

The raw material of the woodcarver and the furniture maker is timber, and the quality of the timber determines the quality of the finished object. The timber must be seasoned, dried slowly over a period of years, to prevent the warping and cracking that would ruin the work. The seasons of timber are the principal resource of the woodworking crafts, and the merchants who trade in seasoned timber are among the wealthiest in the known world.

## The Forests and Their Timbers

The forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) provide the finest timbers for woodcarving and furniture making. The oak of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is hard and close-grained, suitable for the carving of detailed ornament and for the construction of furniture that must last for generations. The elm of [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) is more flexible, suitable for the curved forms that the chair-makers require. The walnut of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) is dark and rich in colour, valued for the veneers that decorate the finest furniture.

The forests of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) produce timbers of a different character, shaped by the cold climate and the thin soil of the mountain slopes. The pine of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) is light and straight-grained, suitable for the construction of the simple furniture that is the standard of the northern households. The birch of the Morvind uplands is hard and pale, used for the turned bowls and the tool handles that the northern craftsmen produce.

The forests of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) produce timbers that are suited to the climate of the south. The acacia of the desert margins is hard and durable, resistant to the insects that attack the softer woods of the north. The palm of the Sahrani oases is light and fibrous, used for the construction of the lattice screens and the carved panels that are characteristic of the Sahrani decorative tradition.

## The Woodcarver's Art

Woodcarving is the art of cutting and shaping wood with chisels and knives to produce decorative forms. The woodcarver works with the grain of the wood, following the natural direction of the fibres, for to cut against the grain is to risk splitting the wood and ruining the work. The woodcarver must understand the character of the wood, the hardness and the grain and the tendency to split, and must choose the tools and the techniques that suit the material.

The woodcarvers of the [Elven Woodland Art](/content/art/elven-woodland-art) tradition are the most accomplished in the known world, for the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) have worked with wood for longer than any other people and have developed techniques that human carvers cannot match. The elven carvers work with the living wood, carving the branches and the trunks of the trees that grow in the elven groves, shaping the growth of the tree itself rather than cutting the timber and carving it after it is dead. The elven carvers say that the tree cooperates with the carver, directing the growth of the branches into the forms that the carver intends.

The woodcarvers of the human realms work in a different manner, cutting the timber and carving it in the workshop. The human carvers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) produce the carved panels and the decorative mouldings that adorn the interiors of the great houses and the temples. The human carvers are organised into guilds that maintain the standards of the craft, and the apprenticeship for a woodcarver is no less than seven years.

## The Furniture of the Great Houses

The furniture of the great houses of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) is among the finest in the known world. The cabinet-makers of the Aldreth court produce chests and cupboards, tables and chairs, beds and desks, all constructed from the finest timbers and decorated with carving, inlay, and veneer. The furniture of the Aldreth court is the standard by which the furniture of the other realms is measured.

The cabinet-makers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) are the most skilled in the human world. They have developed techniques for the construction of furniture that is both strong and elegant, with joints that do not loosen over time and surfaces that are smooth to the touch. The Eldreth cabinet-makers use the dovetail joint for the construction of drawers and chests, a joint that is both strong and decorative, and they have developed methods for the cutting of the dovetails that are the envy of cabinet-makers across the known world.

The furniture of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) is different in character from the furniture of the north. The Thessaran furniture is lighter and more graceful, suited to the warm climate of the [Thessaran Basin](/content/location/settlement/region/thessaran-basin). The Thessaran cabinet-makers use the woods of the south, the citrus woods and the olive woods that are available in the basin, and they decorate their furniture with the geometric patterns that are characteristic of the Thessaran decorative tradition.

## The Furniture of the Plains and the Nomadic Peoples

The [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) have a different relationship with furniture, for the nomadic life requires furniture that can be dismantled and carried. The chests and the storage boxes of the plains are constructed from light timbers, bound with leather and metal, and decorated with the geometric patterns that are characteristic of the plains decorative tradition.

The furniture of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) is adapted to the life of the sea. The Vashari seafarers use folding furniture that can be stored in the limited space of a ship and that can be set up quickly when the ship is at anchor. The Vashari furniture is constructed from the hardwoods of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast), woods that are resistant to the salt and the humidity of the sea air.

## The Decorative Techniques of Woodwork

The decoration of wood is accomplished by several techniques, each of which requires a different set of skills. Carving is the oldest technique, in which the surface of the wood is cut away to produce a pattern or a figure. Inlay is the setting of materials of different colours into the surface of the wood, and the [Inlay Art](/content/culture/inlay-art) of the dwarves has been adapted by human woodworkers for the decoration of furniture.

Veneering is the technique of applying a thin layer of decorative wood to the surface of a less expensive wood, a method that allows the cabinet-maker to use the most beautiful timbers without the expense of constructing the entire piece from them. The veneers of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) are the finest in the known world, cut so thin that the grain of the wood is revealed in all its beauty.

Turning is the technique of shaping wood on a rotating lathe, producing the symmetrical forms that are used for the legs of tables and chairs, for the balusters of staircases, and for the bowls and the platters of the household. The turners of [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) are the most skilled in the world, and the turned legs of Valdmark furniture are the standard of quality for the furniture makers of the known world.

## The Guilds and the Training of the Woodworkers

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the woodworkers are among the oldest of the trade organisations, for the working of wood has been practised since before the founding of the first cities. The guilds maintain the standards of the craft, regulate the training of the apprentices, and settle disputes between the workshops. The guilds also maintain the patterns and the techniques of the craft, preserving the knowledge for the generations that follow.

The apprenticeship for a woodworker is no less than seven years, and the apprentice must produce a masterpiece before being admitted to the rank of journeyman. The masterpiece is a piece of work that demonstrates the apprentice's mastery of the techniques of the craft, and it is judged by the master woodworkers of the guild. The masterpiece is the measure of the apprentice's skill, and a masterpiece that is judged to be of insufficient quality is a cause of shame for the apprentice and the master who trained him.
