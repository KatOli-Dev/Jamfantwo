## The Art of the Written Letter

Calligraphy is the art of beautiful writing, the shaping of letters into forms that are not merely legible but pleasing to the eye. The art is practised wherever writing is used, and the character of the calligraphy of a people reflects the script they employ, the tools they use, and the purposes that writing serves. The art of illumination is the decoration of written pages with colours, gold, and ornament, transforming the written text into a work of visual art.

The written word is the foundation of the civilisations of the known world, and the calligrapher is the servant of the written word. The letters that the calligrapher forms must be clear and consistent, for the reader must be able to read the text without effort. But the calligrapher also seeks to make the letters beautiful, to give to the text a visual rhythm that matches the rhythm of the language, and to dignify the words that the text contains.

## The Scripts of the Known World

The known world employs several scripts, each with its own character and its own tradition of calligraphy. The script of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) realm is a rounded, open hand that is suited to the writing of the Aldreth language. The script is written with a broad-nibbed pen that produces the thick and thin strokes that give the letters their character. The calligraphers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) are the masters of this script, and the manuscripts that are produced in the Aldreth scriptoria are among the finest in the known world.

The script of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) is more angular, written with a narrower nib that produces strokes of a more uniform width. The Thessaran script is compact and efficient, suited to the writing of the legal documents and the commercial records that the league produces in great quantity. The calligraphers of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) and [Thessara](/content/location/natural/continent/thessara) are the masters of this script, and the records of the league are maintained in a hand that is as legible as it is elegant.

The script of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) is cursive, written with a reed pen that produces flowing, connected letters that are suited to the writing of the Kharesh language. The Kharesh script is written from right to left, and the calligraphers of the Kharesh have developed a tradition of calligraphy that emphasises the rhythm and the flow of the line rather than the precision of the individual letter.

## The Materials of the Calligrapher

The calligrapher depends upon the quality of the materials, and the production of the tools and the surfaces for writing is an industry that spans the known world. The pens of the calligrapher are cut from the feathers of the larger birds, the goose and the swan and the raven, and the cutting of the nib is a skill that the calligrapher must master before the writing can begin. The reed pens of the south are cut from the reeds that grow along the [Greywash River](/content/location/natural/feature/greywash-river) and in the marshes of the [Thessaran Basin](/content/location/settlement/region/thessaran-basin).

The ink of the calligrapher is made from lampblack, the soot of burnt oil, mixed with gum arabic and water to produce a liquid that flows smoothly from the pen and dries to a dense black. The ink is ground on a stone slab with a pestle, and the quality of the ink depends upon the fineness of the grinding and the proportions of the mixture. The calligraphers of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) produce the finest ink in the known world, and the Amurran ink is exported to every continent.

The surface for writing is parchment, made from the skin of sheep or goats, prepared by soaking in lime, scraping, stretching, and polishing. The parchment of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) is the finest in the known world, for the skins of the northern sheep are thin and strong, and the parchment-makers of the Marches have developed techniques for the preparation of the skins that produce a surface of exceptional smoothness and whiteness.

## The Illumination of the Page

Illumination is the decoration of the written page with colours and gold, and it is practised by a specialised group of craftsmen who work alongside the calligraphers in the scriptoria. The illuminator works after the calligrapher has finished the text, adding the decorated initials, the borders, and the illustrations that transform the page into a work of art.

The colours of the illuminator are the same pigments that the painter uses, but the illuminator works on a smaller scale and with a precision that the painter does not require. The gold that the illuminator uses is gold leaf, beaten to a thinness that allows it to be applied to the parchment with a brush. The gold is applied over a ground of gesso, a mixture of chalk, glue, and sugar, and it is burnished with a smooth stone to produce a surface that reflects the light.

The illuminated manuscripts of the [Still Flame](/content/religion/still-flame) are the most numerous and the most elaborate of the illuminated works in the known world. The temples of the Still Flame maintain scriptoria in which the sacred texts are copied and illuminated, and the manuscripts that are produced in these scriptoria are the standard of quality for the art of illumination. The illuminators of the Still Flame are organised into a guild that maintains the traditions of the craft, and the guild also maintains the patterns and the formulas for the colours and the gold.

## The Scriptoria of the Great Centres

The scriptoria of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) are the largest and the most productive in the known world. The scriptoria are housed in the buildings of the temples and the academies, and they employ calligraphers, illuminators, and binders who work together to produce the manuscripts that the realm requires. The scriptoria of Eldreth produce not only the sacred texts of the Still Flame but also the legal documents, the historical chronicles, and the works of natural philosophy that are the foundation of the learning of the present age.

The scriptoria of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are smaller but more innovative, for the Amurran calligraphers and illuminators are known for their willingness to experiment with new styles and new techniques. The Amurran scriptoria produce manuscripts that are unlike the manuscripts of any other centre, combining the scripts and the decorative traditions of several cultures into a style that is uniquely Amurran.

The scriptoria of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) are the principal centres of the Sahrani tradition of calligraphy and illumination. The Sahrani calligraphers write in the cursive script of the Sahrani language, and the Sahrani illuminators decorate the pages with the geometric patterns that are characteristic of the Sahrani decorative tradition. The Sahrani manuscripts are among the most valued of the exports of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), and the merchants who trade in them pay prices that rival those of the northern manuscripts.

## The Binder's Art

The binder is the craftsman who assembles the written pages into a book, a task that requires skills that are distinct from the skills of the calligrapher and the illuminator. The pages are folded into gatherings, sewn onto cords or tapes, and attached to boards of wood or pasteboard that form the covers of the book. The covers are covered with leather or cloth, and the spine is reinforced with bands of leather or metal.

The binders of [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) are the most accomplished in the known world, producing bindings that are as beautiful as the texts they contain. The Valdmark binders use the leathers of the northern animals, the calf and the goat and the seal, and they decorate the bindings with the tools and the stamps that produce the patterns that are characteristic of the Valdmark style.

The binders of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) produce bindings of a different character, suited to the conditions of the sea. The Vashari bindings are made from the skins of the sea creatures, the seal and the porpoise, and they are treated with oils that protect the leather from the salt and the humidity of the sea air. The Vashari bindings are as durable as they are beautiful, and the manuscripts that are bound in the Vashari style are valued for their ability to withstand the conditions of the sea voyage.

## The Social Role of the Calligrapher

The calligrapher occupies a respected place in the societies of the known world, for the written word is the foundation of the administration, the law, and the learning of the present age. The calligrapher is the servant of the [Chroniclers College](/content/government/local/chroniclers-college), producing the records that preserve the history of the realm. The calligrapher is also the servant of the [Memory Houses](/content/religion/houses-of-memory), producing the texts that preserve the names of the departed.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the calligraphers are among the most exclusive of the trade organisations, for the skills of the calligrapher require a long training and a natural gift that not every aspirant possesses. The guilds maintain the standards of the craft, regulate the training of the apprentices, and preserve the styles and the techniques that are the foundation of the art. The calligrapher who has been admitted to the guild is a craftsman of the highest rank, and the work of the guild calligrapher is valued above all others.
