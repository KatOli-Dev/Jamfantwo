## The Working of Hide

Leatherworking is the art of preparing animal hides and shaping them into objects of use and beauty. The art is among the most ancient of human crafts, for the hide of the hunted animal was among the first materials that the early peoples used for clothing, shelter, and tools. The leatherworking traditions of the known world have developed over many centuries, and the craftsmen who work in leather produce objects that range from the simplest of belts to the most elaborate of saddles and armour.

The raw material of the leatherworker is the hide of the animal, the skin that covers the body of the beast. The hide must be preserved by the process of tanning, which converts the raw skin into a material that is durable and flexible and resistant to decay. The tanning of hides is a separate craft from the working of leather, and the tanners of the known world are organised into [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) that are distinct from the guilds of the leatherworkers.

## The Tanning of Hides

The tanning of hides is accomplished by the application of substances that bind to the proteins of the skin and prevent them from decaying. The most common tanning agent is the bark of the oak tree, which contains tannins that are extracted by soaking the bark in water. The hides are soaked in the tannin solution for a period of months, and the tannins penetrate the skin and bind to the proteins, converting the raw hide into the material that the leatherworker requires.

The tanners of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) produce the finest oak-tanned leather in the known world, for the oak of the heartwood is rich in tannins and the water of the heartwood streams is pure and soft. The leather of the heartwood is used for the finest products of the leatherworker's craft, the saddles of the nobility and the bindings of the books that are produced in the scriptoria of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth).

The tanners of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) use a different method of tanning, suited to the conditions of the plains. The plains tanners use the brains of the animals that are killed for the hide, rubbing the brain into the skin and working it with the hands to produce a leather that is soft and white and that does not require the soaking in the tannin solution. The brain-tanned leather of the plains is the material that is used for the garments and the moccasins of the plains people.

## The Leatherworking of the Northern Peoples

The northern peoples of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) have a tradition of leatherworking that is suited to the cold climate of the north. The northern leatherworkers work in the hides of the northern animals, the elk and the deer and the seal, producing garments and footwear that are warm and durable and resistant to the conditions of the northern winter.

The northern leatherworkers use the technique of the sewing of the leather with the sinew of the animals, a thread that is strong and that does not rot in the conditions of the northern climate. The seams that the northern leatherworkers produce are tight and waterproof, and the garments that are sewn with the sinew thread are the standard of the northern peoples for the clothing that must withstand the conditions of the winter.

The northern leatherworkers also produce the leather that is used for the covers of the northern shields, the leather that is stretched over the frame of the shield and that provides the protection that the warrior requires. The shields of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) are covered with the leather of the northern seal, a leather that is thick and strong and that can withstand the blows of the weapons that the enemies of the clans use.

## The Leatherworking of the Plains

The [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) has a tradition of leatherworking that is based on the materials that the plains provide. The plains leatherworkers work in the hides of the bison and the deer of the plains, producing garments and equipment that are suited to the life of the plains. The plains people use the leather for the clothing, the moccasins, the leggings, and the tunics that are the dress of the plains, and the leather is decorated with the beadwork and the quillwork that are the characteristic decorations of the plains tradition.

The plains leatherworkers also produce the containers that are used for the storage and the transport of the goods of the plains. The parfleche is a container of raw hide, folded and sewn into a shape that is suited to the packing on the back of a horse or a camel. The parfleche is decorated with the geometric patterns that are the tradition of the plains, painted in the colours that the plains people use for the decoration of their equipment.

The plains leatherworkers are also the makers of the saddles that the people of the plains use for the riding of the horses. The plains saddle is a structure of wood and leather, designed to distribute the weight of the rider across the back of the horse and to provide the stability that the rider requires. The design of the plains saddle is the result of centuries of development, and the saddle of the plains is the standard of quality for the saddles of the known world.

## The Leatherworking of the Sahrani Deserts

The [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) and the other peoples of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) have a tradition of leatherworking that is based on the hides of the animals that are kept by the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) peoples. The Sahrani leatherworkers work in the hides of the goat and the camel, producing the leather that is used for the tents and the equipment of the Sahrani nomads.

The Sahrani leatherworkers use a method of tanning that is different from the methods of the north and the plains. The Sahrani tanners use the alum that is found in the deserts of the south, a mineral salt that produces a leather that is white and soft and that does not shrink in the heat of the desert. The alum-tanned leather of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) is the material that is used for the garments of the Sahrani nobility and for the bindings of the books that are produced in the scriptoria of the Sahrani cities.

The Sahrani leatherworkers are also the makers of the water bags that the people of the desert use for the transport of water across the dry regions of the south. The water bag is made from the hide of the goat, sewn into a shape that holds the water without leaking and that allows the evaporation of a small amount of water through the surface of the bag, cooling the contents and keeping the water fresh. The design of the Sahrani water bag is a masterpiece of the leatherworker's craft, and the water bags of the Sahrani are used by travellers across every continent.

## The Decoration of Leather

The decoration of leather is accomplished by several techniques, each of which requires a different set of skills. The tooling of leather is the most common technique, in which the surface of the leather is impressed with patterns and designs by the use of metal stamps that are heated and pressed into the leather. The tooling of leather is practised in every region where leather is worked, and the patterns of the tooling reflect the decorative traditions of the region.

The embroidery of leather is another technique, in which the leather is decorated with the needle and the thread in the manner of the [Textile Arts](/content/art/textile-arts). The embroidery of leather is practised chiefly by the peoples of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), who use the coloured threads to produce the patterns that are characteristic of their traditions.

The painting of leather is a third technique, in which the surface of the leather is painted with the pigments that the painter uses for the decoration of the other materials. The painting of leather is practised in the courts of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and in the workshops of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), where the painted leather screens and panels are used for the decoration of the walls of the palaces and the temples.

## The Guilds and the Standards of the Leatherworkers

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the leatherworkers are among the oldest of the trade organisations in the known world. The guilds maintain the standards of the craft, regulate the training of the apprentices, and settle disputes between the workshops. The guild of the leatherworkers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) is the largest and the most influential, and the standards that the [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) guild sets are the standards that the leatherworkers of the other cities follow.

The training of the leatherworker begins with the apprenticeship, a period of no less than seven years in which the apprentice learns the preparation of the leather, the cutting and the sewing, and the techniques of decoration. The apprentice must produce a masterpiece before being admitted to the rank of journeyman, and the masterpiece is judged by the master leatherworkers of the guild. The mastery of the leatherworker's craft is an achievement that requires the patience and the dedication that the craft demands.
