## The Woven Fabric of Civilisation

Textile arts encompass the production of cloth and the decoration of cloth through weaving, embroidery, tapestry, and a range of associated techniques. The art of turning fibre into fabric is among the most ancient of human crafts, predating the working of metal and the making of pottery in many regions. Every community that has access to plant or animal fibre produces some form of textile, and the character of the textiles of a region reveals much about the climate, the economy, and the aesthetic values of the people who produce them.

The raw materials of the textile arts are fibres derived from plants and animals. Flax, grown in the temperate regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), produces the linen that is the most common textile of the northern lands. Cotton, cultivated in the warm climates of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the [Thessaran Basin](/content/location/settlement/region/thessaran-basin), produces a softer and more absorbent cloth suited to the heat of the south. Wool, from the sheep of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) and the goats of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), provides warmth and durability for the cold climates of the north.

## Weaving and the Loom

Weaving is the process of interlacing two sets of threads, the warp and the weft, at right angles to produce a cloth. The warp threads are held under tension on a frame called a loom, and the weft threads are passed through the warp by means of a shuttle. The simplest looms are the backstrap looms of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), in which the warp tension is maintained by the body of the weaver. The most complex are the great horizontal looms of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) weaving houses, which require two weavers to operate and produce cloth of a width sufficient for the robes of the nobility.

The patterns of woven cloth are produced by the selection of coloured threads and by the order in which the warp threads are lifted to allow the passage of the weft. The weavers of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) have developed techniques for weaving complex patterns that include the representation of ships, waves, and sea-creatures in the cloth itself. The [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari) loom patterns are closely guarded secrets, passed from master weaver to apprentice within the families that have practised the craft for generations.

The [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) of the Aethelaran hill country produce a distinctive woven cloth of nettle fibre, softer and more lustrous than linen, that is highly valued for the garments of the nobility. The nettle fibre is extracted from the stems of cultivated nettle plants, spun on gnomish spinning wheels of a design that is unique to the gnomish communities, and woven on small looms that produce cloth of a fineness that human weavers cannot match. The gnomish nettle cloth is traded across the known world and commands prices that rival those of silk.

## Embroidery and the Needle Arts

Embroidery is the decoration of cloth with needle and thread, and it is practised in every region of the known world. The techniques of embroidery range from the simplest chain stitch, which any child can learn, to the complex goldwork of the court embroiderers of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), which requires years of training to master.

The embroidery of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) is characterised by the use of wool yarn on a linen ground, with designs that follow the geometric patterns of the northern tradition. The embroiderers of the [Frostwall Marches](/content/location/settlement/region/frostwall-marches) produce ceremonial hangings that depict the history of the clans, the battles of the ancestors, and the animals of the northern wastes. The hangings are displayed in the long halls of the clan chiefs and are brought out only for the major festivals of the year.

The embroidery of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) is more delicate, worked in silk thread on a silk ground, with designs that incorporate floral and foliate patterns derived from the gardens of the league. The silk is imported from the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), where the silkworms are cultivated in the temperate climate of the islands. The embroiderers of [Thessara](/content/location/natural/region/thessara) are mostly women, trained in workshops that are attached to the households of the nobility. The work of the Thessaran embroiderers is among the most valued of the luxury goods of the known world, and a robe embroidered in the Thessaran style is a mark of wealth and status wherever it is worn.

## Tapestry and the Narrative Hanging

Tapestry is a form of weaving in which the weft threads are of many colours, each colour being used only where it is needed in the design, so that the pattern appears as an integral part of the cloth rather than as a decoration applied to the surface. A tapestry is woven on a loom, but the weaver follows a full-scale drawing called the cartoon, which is placed behind the warp threads as a guide.

The great tapestries of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) are among the largest woven objects in the known world, measuring as much as twenty feet in length and ten feet in height. The tapestries depict scenes from history and mythology, the battles of the [Three-Banners War](/content/history/three-banners-war) and the legends of the founding of the realm. The weavers of the [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) tapestry houses work in teams, with the master weaver responsible for the faces and the hands and the journeymen responsible for the backgrounds and the borders.

The [Dwarven Craft Traditions](/content/art/dwarven-craft-traditions) include a distinctive form of tapestry woven from metal thread on a warp of stone fibre. The metal thread is drawn from silver and copper wire, beaten flat and wound around a core of silk. The stone fibre is produced from a type of asbestos that occurs in the mountains of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), spun into thread that is resistant to fire and to decay. The dwarven metal tapestries are among the most durable works of art in the known world, and examples that are centuries old remain as bright and as strong as the day they were woven.

## Dyeing and the Colour of Cloth

The colour of cloth is determined by the dyes in which it is steeped, and the art of the dyer is as important as the art of the weaver in the production of fine textiles. The dyes of the known world are derived from plants, from minerals, and from the bodies of certain animals. The red of the madder root, the blue of the woad plant, and the purple of the murex shell are the principal colours of the textile arts.

The dyers of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) are the most skilled in the known world, having developed techniques for the production of colours that do not fade in the sun and do not run in the wash. The Amurran dyers guard their formulas as state secrets, and the export of dyed Amurran cloth is one of the principal sources of the republic's wealth. The crimson of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) is the most expensive colour in the known world, commanding prices that exceed those of gold by weight.

The [Sahrani](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) tradition of dyeing favours the warm colours of the desert, the reds, oranges, and yellows that are produced from the safflower and the pomegranate. The [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) dyers also produce a distinctive black from the gallnuts of the desert acacia, and the black cloth of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is the standard of quality for the garments of the Sahrani nobility.

## The Social Place of the Textile Arts

Textile arts are practised by both men and women in the known world, though the division of labour varies from region to region. In the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), the weaving of the great tent cloths is the work of women, who are also responsible for the embroidery of the ceremonial garments. In the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), the weaving of metal thread tapestries is the work of dwarven men, who have developed the strength and the precision required to work with the stiff metal threads.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the textile arts are among the oldest and most powerful of the trade organisations. The guild of the weavers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) dates from the founding of the city, and its records preserve the names of the master weavers who have served the guild for centuries. The guild maintains the standards of the craft, regulates the training of apprentices, and settles disputes between the weaving houses. The guild also maintains the patterns and the formulas of the dyes, preserving the knowledge of the craft for the generations that follow.
