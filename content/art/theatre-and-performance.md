## The Art of Living Presentation

Theatre and performance are the arts of presenting stories and spectacles before an audience through the actions and voices of performers. The art is among the most immediate of the arts, for it is experienced in the moment of its creation and cannot be preserved in the manner of a painting or a carved stone. The theatre of the known world varies greatly from region to region, shaped by the language, the religion, and the social structure of the people who practise it.

The origins of theatre lie in the ceremonies of the temples, the re-enactments of the myths and the histories that gave the people their identity. The ceremonies of the [Still Flame](/content/religion/still-flame) include dramatic performances in which the priests and the acolytes enact the stories of the founding of the faith and the lives of the saints. The ceremonies of the [Sea Mothers Court](/content/religion/sea-mothers-court) include the performance of the covenants between the sea mothers and the peoples of the coast, performed by the priests of the court on the shores of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea).

## The Theatre of the Aldreth Realm

The theatre of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is the most developed in the known world, with a tradition that stretches back to the early days of the realm. The [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) theatre is performed in purpose-built structures called playhouses, which are circular or polygonal buildings with a raised stage at one end and a yard in front of the stage where the common people stand to watch. The playhouses of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) are the largest and the most elaborate in the known world, accommodating audiences of several thousand.

The plays of the Aldreth theatre are written in verse, for the Aldreth playwrights regard verse as the natural language of the stage. The plays treat the great themes of human life, love and death, power and ambition, honour and betrayal, and they draw their stories from the history of the realm and the myths of the [Watcher](/content/religion/watcher) faith. The plays of the Aldreth theatre are performed by companies of players who travel from city to city, bringing the drama to the audiences of the towns and the villages.

The actors of the Aldreth theatre are organised into companies that are licensed by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the players. The company is led by a master player who is responsible for the training of the apprentices and the management of the company's affairs. The actors are trained in the arts of the voice, the movement, and the gesture, and the best of them are renowned across the realm for the power of their performances.

## The Dance of the Elven Groves

The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) have a tradition of dance that is unlike the theatre of the human realms. The elven dance is performed in the clearings of the forest, before audiences that gather among the trees. The dance is not a representation of a story but an expression of the relationship between the dancers and the forest, a movement that follows the rhythm of the natural world rather than the rhythm of a written text.

The elven dancers train for years to master the movements of the dance, which require a precision and a grace that human dancers rarely achieve. The dance is accompanied by the music of the elven instruments, the flutes and the strings that produce the melodies that guide the movement of the dancers. The dance of the elves is regarded as a form of worship by some, a communion with the spirit of the forest that the elves call the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court).

The elven dance is also performed at the festivals of the elven year, the celebrations of the turning of the seasons and the growth of the forest. The [Long Night Festival](/content/culture/long-night-festival) of the elves includes dances that continue through the hours of darkness, and the dancers are said to be guided by the light of the stars that shine through the canopy of the trees.

## The Carnival of the Amurran Republic

The [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) has a tradition of street performance that is unlike the formal theatre of the Aldreth realm. The Amurran carnival is a festival of performance that takes place in the streets and the squares of the city, with performers of every kind competing for the attention of the crowds. The carnival includes acrobats and jugglers, dancers and musicians, puppet shows and mask plays, and the carnival is the occasion at which the Amurran love of spectacle and innovation is expressed in its fullest form.

The mask plays of the Amurran carnival are the most distinctive of the Amurran performance traditions. The performers wear masks that represent the characters of the play, the fool and the hero, the merchant and the noble, the wife and the husband. The mask allows the performer to assume the character in a way that is different from the unmasked acting of the Aldreth stage, and the Amurran mask plays require a physicality and a precision of gesture that the mask demands.

The puppet shows of the Amurran carnival are also distinctive, using puppets of carved wood and painted cloth that are manipulated by strings or by the hands of the puppeteer. The puppets of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are the most sophisticated in the known world, with joints that allow the puppet to move in a manner that is almost lifelike. The puppet shows of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are performed in booths that are set up in the squares of the city, and the stories that the puppets tell are the same stories that the actors perform on the stage.

## The Story-Dance of the Sahrani Courts

The courts of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) have a tradition of performance that combines music, dance, and poetry into a single art form that the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) call the story-dance. The story-dance is performed by a company of dancers and musicians, who enact the stories of the Sahrani courts, the loves and the wars of the nobility, the legends of the Sahrani heroes, and the history of the Sahrani people.

The story-dance is performed in the courtyards of the palaces, on a stage that is decorated with the tiles and the mosaics that are characteristic of the Sahrani decorative tradition. The performers wear costumes of silk and wool, dyed in the colours of the Sahrani palette, and they move to the music of the Sahrani instruments, the drums and the flutes and the stringed instruments that produce the rhythms and the melodies of the south.

The story-dance of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) is also performed in the [Sands Assembly](/content/culture/sands-assembly), the great gathering of the Sahrani tribes that takes place in the desert. The performance at the Sands Assembly is the occasion at which the master dancers and musicians of the Sahrani courts compete for the honour of the most accomplished company, and the competition is fierce and the standards are high.

## The Northern Saga-Performances

The northern peoples of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) have a tradition of performance that is centred on the recitation of the sagas. The saga-performer stands before the company in the hall, speaking the words of the saga in a voice that carries to every corner of the room. The performer uses gesture and movement to illustrate the action of the story, but the voice is the principal instrument, and the performer must be able to command the full range of tones from the whisper to the shout.

The saga-performers of the north are the heirs of the ancient tradition of the skalds, the poets who served the chieftains of the northern clans. The skalds composed the sagas and the poems that preserved the history of the clans, and they performed them in the halls of the chieftains before the assembled company. The tradition of the skald continues in the present age, and the saga-performers of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) are the most respected of the northern performers.
