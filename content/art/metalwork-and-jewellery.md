## The Working of Metal as Art

Metalwork is the art of shaping metal into objects of use and beauty, and it is among the most valued of the crafts in the known world. The metals that the earth provides, gold and silver, copper and bronze, iron and steel, are transformed by the skill of the smith into objects that serve every purpose of human life, from the blade of the plough to the crown of the king. The art of the metalworker is practised in every region where metal ores are available, and the quality of the metalwork of a people is a measure of their technical achievement and their aesthetic sensibility.

The practice of metalwork depends upon the properties of the metals and the techniques that have been developed to shape them. The soft metals, gold and silver and copper, can be shaped by hammering alone, a technique called cold-working that is practised by every people who have access to these metals. The harder metals, bronze and iron and steel, must be heated to a high temperature before they can be shaped, a technique called hot-working that requires specialised equipment and a precise understanding of the behaviour of the metal at different temperatures.

## The Smiths of the Stoneheim Holds

The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) are the most accomplished metalworkers in the known world, a reputation that rests upon their mastery of the techniques of smelting, alloying, and forging. The dwarven smiths have developed methods for the extraction of metals from their ores that produce a purity and a consistency that human smiths cannot match. The dwarven smiths have also developed alloys that are unknown to the human smiths, combinations of metals that produce properties of strength, flexibility, and resistance to corrosion.

The dwarven approach to metalwork is shaped by the nature of the materials and the purpose of the work. Dwarven metalwork is characterised by its solidity and its precision. A dwarven smith does not waste metal on unnecessary decoration; every line of the decoration follows the structure of the object and serves the purpose of the object. The [Inlay Art](/content/culture/inlay-art) is the principal decorative technique of dwarven metalwork, and the inlay of silver into bronze and of gold into iron is the standard of dwarven decorative metalwork.

The great forges of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) are the centre of dwarven metalwork. The forges are built into the heart of the mountain, where the natural heat of the earth assists the smiths in the heating of the metal and where the water of underground rivers provides the power for the hammers and the bellows. The forges of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) are the largest and the most productive in the known world, and the metalwork that is produced in them is exported to every continent.

## The Silversmiths of the Amurran Republic

The [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) has a tradition of silver-working that is distinct from the dwarven tradition. The Amurran silversmiths work in the precious metals, silver and gold, producing objects of delicacy and refinement that serve the luxury trade of the known world. The Amurran silversmiths have developed techniques for the working of metal that are unknown to the dwarves, including the technique of granulation, in which tiny spheres of gold are fused to a gold surface to produce a textured pattern of great fineness.

The Amurran silversmiths are organised into a guild that maintains the standards of the craft and regulates the training of the apprentices. The apprenticeship for an Amurran silversmith is no less than ten years, and the master silversmiths are among the most respected craftsmen in the republic. The guild also maintains the formulas for the alloys and the solders that the silversmiths use, preserving the knowledge of the craft for the generations that follow.

The silver of the Amurran mines is the purest in the known world, and the Amurran silversmiths attribute the quality of their work to the quality of the metal. The silver is extracted from the ores of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) and refined in the workshops of the republic to a purity that other smiths cannot achieve. The Amurran silver is traded across the known world, and the objects that are made from it are among the most valued of the luxury goods of the present age.

## The Jewellery of the Northern Courts

The courts of the north, the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) and the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), have a tradition of jewellery described in the account of [Jewellery and Adornment](/content/art/jewellery-and-adornment). The northern jewellers work in gold and silver alongside the metals of the north, the platinum and the palladium found in the ores of the [Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range). These metals are white and lustrous, more resistant to tarnish than silver and more durable than gold. The northern jewellers have developed techniques for working these metals that are unknown to the smiths of the south, and the white metal jewellery of the north is among the most valued exports of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans).

## The Metalwork of the Sahrani Courts

The courts of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) have a tradition of metalwork that is distinct from the metalwork of the north and the west. The [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) metalworkers work in brass and bronze, metals that are well suited to the climate of the south, where the humidity of the coast and the dust of the desert would tarnish the softer metals. The Sahrani metalworkers have developed techniques for the casting of brass and bronze that produce objects of great complexity, including the intricate latticework that is characteristic of the Sahrani decorative tradition.

The metalworkers of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Qassir](/content/location/settlement/city/qassir) are the most skilled in the Sahrani tradition, producing vessels, lamps, and decorative objects that are exported to every region of the known world. The Sahrani metalworkers use the lost-wax casting technique, in which a model of the object is made in wax, the wax is covered in clay, the clay is fired to harden it and to melt the wax, and the molten metal is poured into the cavity that the wax has left. The lost-wax technique produces objects of great detail and precision, and the Sahrani metalworkers have refined the technique to a level that other smiths admire but cannot match.

## The Jewellery of Personal Adornment

The art of the jeweller is closely related to the art of the metalworker, for the jeweller works in the precious metals and the precious stones to produce objects of personal adornment. The jewellers of the known world produce rings and bracelets, necklaces and earrings, brooches and pendants, crowns and diadems. The [Jewellery and Adornment](/content/art/jewellery-and-adornment) of the known world is a subject with its own extensive traditions and techniques.

The jewellers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) are the most accomplished in the human world, producing pieces that combine gold and silver with the precious stones of the three continents. The [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) jewellers are organised into a guild that maintains the standards of the craft and regulates the training of the apprentices. The jewellers of Eldreth are known for their mastery of the technique of enamelling, the fusion of powdered glass to a metal surface to produce a coloured decoration that is as durable as the metal itself.

## The Guilds and the Standards of the Craft

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the metalworkers and the jewellers are among the most powerful of the trade organisations in the known world. The guilds maintain the standards of the craft, regulate the training of the apprentices, and settle disputes between the workshops. The guilds also maintain the formulas for the alloys and the solders that the metalworkers use, preserving the knowledge of the craft for the generations that follow.

The guild of the smiths of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) is the oldest of the metalworkers' guilds, and its records preserve the names of the master smiths who have served the hold for centuries. The guild of the silversmiths of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) is the most influential, for the Amurran silversmiths control the trade in silver across the known world. The guild of the jewellers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) is the most exclusive, for the number of master jewellers is limited by the guild to ensure that the quality of the work is maintained.
