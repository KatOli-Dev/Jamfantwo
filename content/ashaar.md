## Overview

Ashaar is the southernmost settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/southern-reach), situated on the shore of the Lesser Inland Sea in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), beyond the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh). The town is the centre of the fishing and the agriculture of the southern basin, and its position on the shore of the smaller of the inland seas gives it a role in the economy of the Southern Reach that is distinct from that of [Vereth](/content/vereth), the larger settlement on the Greater Inland Sea to the north. Ashaar is the most remote of the Southern Reach settlements, and its distance from the caravan routes and the northern world has preserved a way of life that is more self-sufficient and more traditional than that of the other communities.

## The Lesser Inland Sea and the Harbour

The Lesser Inland Sea is the smaller of the two inland seas of the Southern Reach, and its waters are warmer and more sheltered than those of the Greater Inland Sea to the north. The sea is navigable throughout its extent, and the boats that travel its waters connect the lakeside communities that dot its shores. The harbour of Ashaar is a natural anchorage that is sheltered by a curving headland, and the quays line the inner shore, where the fishing boats and the smaller trading vessels of the southern basin are moored.

The fishing grounds of the Lesser Inland Sea are productive, and the catch is the primary food source of the southern communities. The fishing fleet of Ashaar is the largest on the southern sea, and the boats venture out daily, returning with a catch that is preserved by salting and drying. The preserved fish of the Lesser Inland Sea are consumed locally and traded to the other settlements of the Southern Reach through the boat routes that connect the two seas.

## Agriculture and the Land

The land around Ashaar is the most fertile in the Southern Reach, and the town is the centre of the agriculture of the southern basin. The fields that surround the town are watered by the rivers that flow from the surrounding highlands into the Lesser Inland Sea, and the soil is deep and rich, suited to a great variety of crops. The farmers of Ashaar cultivate grains, vegetables, and fruits, and the orchards that line the river valleys produce figs, dates, and the citrus fruits that are among the most prized products of the south. The agriculture of Ashaar is the foundation of the food supply of the Southern Reach, and the surplus that is produced is traded to the other settlements through the inland sea routes.

The highlands that rise to the south of Ashaar are the southernmost extent of the Southern Reach, and their slopes are clothed in open woodland and grassland that support the herds of the highland farmers. The wool and the leather of the highland herds are among the goods that are traded through the caravan routes to the north, and the herdsmen of the highlands are a distinct community within the Southern Reach, whose way of life is shaped by the terrain and the climate of the uplands.

## The Town

Ashaar is a well-built, prosperous town, and its architecture reflects the agricultural wealth of the surrounding land, with substantial buildings of pale stone, broad streets, and a market square that is the centre of the town's commercial and social life. The buildings are constructed in the southern tradition, with thick walls, shaded courtyards, and high windows, and the gardens that surround the houses are irrigated by the waters of the rivers that flow through the town. The town is surrounded by walls of stone that are maintained in good order, and the gates are guarded by a modest force that is funded by the local council.

The population of Ashaar is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people are a mixture of farmers, fishermen, and craftsmen, whose way of life is oriented toward the land and the sea. A small community of [tortolans](/content/tortolans) is found on the shore of the Lesser Inland Sea, and their long-lived and deliberate nature is well suited to the slow pace of the agricultural life. The tortolans of Ashaar are the most numerous of any settlement in the Southern Reach, and their knowledge of the waters and the land is valued by the human community.

## Trade and the Inland Seas

The trade of Ashaar is dominated by the commerce of the inland seas, and the town is the primary source of the agricultural products and the preserved fish that are traded across the Southern Reach. The boats that travel between the two seas carry the goods of Ashaar to [Vereth](/content/vereth) and the other settlements of the Greater Inland Sea, and the return cargo includes the manufactured goods and the northern products that arrive through the caravan routes. Ashaar is not directly connected to the caravan trade, for the routes from the north terminate at [Torem](/content/torem) and Vereth, and the goods that reach Ashaar from the north are carried by the inland sea routes.

## Significance

Ashaar is the southernmost settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/southern-reach) and the centre of the agriculture and the fishing of the southern basin. The town's position on the shore of the Lesser Inland Sea and its distance from the caravan routes have preserved a way of life that is more self-sufficient and more traditional than that of the other settlements, and its prosperity is built upon the fertility of the land and the richness of the waters. The significance of Ashaar lies in its role as the breadbasket of the Southern Reach and the most remote settled community in the known world.
