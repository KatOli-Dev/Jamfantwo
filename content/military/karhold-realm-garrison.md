## The Frozen Watch

The [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) is the most
northerly of the settled realms of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind),
a cold and mountainous land where winter lasts for nine months of the year and
where the sun does not rise for weeks at a time during the darkest months. The
military forces of [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) are shaped by these conditions. The [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm)
does not maintain a large field army in the manner of the southern realms, for
the population is too small and the resources too scarce. Instead, the realm
relies on a system of garrison forces that defend the fortified settlements and
the passes that lead into the interior.

The Karhold garrisons are professional soldiers who serve for long terms,
often for their entire adult lives. They are experts in winter warfare, in the
defence of fortifications, and in the skirmish tactics that are suited to the
broken terrain of their homeland. Their equipment is adapted to the climate,
with heavy furs worn over mail armour and weapons that are designed to be used
with gloved hands.

## Organisation

The garrison forces of Karhold are organised around the strongholds that
command the valleys and passes of the realm. Each stronghold is commanded by a
Warden, who is responsible for the defence of his sector and for the
maintenance of the soldiers under his command. The Wardens report to the Jarl
of Karhold, who exercises overall command of the military forces of the realm.

The largest garrison is at [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold)
itself, the seat of the Jarl and the administrative centre of the realm. The
Karhold garrison numbers approximately five hundred soldiers, a mixture of
heavy infantry, archers, and a small cavalry force that is used for scouting
and for the pursuit of raiders. The other garrisons are smaller, ranging from
one hundred to two hundred soldiers, depending on the importance of the
stronghold.

## The Winter Warriors

The soldiers of the Karhold Realm are recruited from the local population, men
who have grown up in the cold and who are accustomed to the conditions that
would incapacitate a soldier from a warmer climate. Recruits are selected for
their physical endurance, their mental resilience, and their ability to work as
part of a team. Training takes place in winter, when the conditions are most
demanding, and recruits who cannot meet the standards are sent home.

A Karhold garrison soldier is trained in the use of the spear, the axe, and the
bow, and he is instructed in the tactics of winter warfare. He learns to ski,
to build snow shelters, and to navigate in conditions of poor visibility. He is
taught to recognise the signs of an avalanche, to find water in a frozen
landscape, and to treat the injuries and illnesses that are common in cold
environments.

## Equipment

The equipment of the Karhold garrison is designed for warmth and functionality.
Body armour consists of a hauberk of mail, worn over a padded gambeson and
covered with a coat of heavy fur. The helmet is of iron, lined with fur, and
fitted with a mail curtain that protects the neck and shoulders from the wind.
Gloves of leather and fur are worn at all times, and boots are of leather,
stuffed with dried grass for insulation.

The primary weapon of the Karhold infantry is the long axe, a weapon that is
effective against armour and that can be used to break through the doors and
palisades that are common in the fortifications of the north. The axe is
supplemented by a spear and a knife, and archers carry composite bows that are
made from horn and sinew that does not become brittle in the cold.

## Fortifications

The strongholds of the Karhold Realm are built of stone, with walls that are
twenty feet thick at the base and that taper to fifteen feet at the top. The
walls are roofed with slate, providing cover for the defenders and preventing
the accumulation of snow in the walkways. The strongholds are heated by large
fireplaces that are built into the walls, and they are stocked with provisions
that can sustain the garrison for a full winter.

The strongholds are positioned to command the valleys and passes that are the
only routes through the mountains. An enemy advancing into the Karhold Realm
must either take the strongholds or bypass them, and bypassing a stronghold
means leaving a fortified enemy force in the rear of the advancing army. The
strongholds have never been taken by assault, and the sparse population of the
realm means that they have never been starved into submission.

## The Winter Patrol

The garrisons maintain patrols that range out from the strongholds during the
months when travel is possible. The patrols are small, usually ten to twenty
soldiers, and they are tasked with monitoring the approaches to the stronghold,
gathering intelligence about enemy movements, and maintaining contact with the
other garrisons. The patrols travel on skis in winter and on foot in summer,
and they are trained to survive for weeks in the wilderness if necessary.

The winter patrols are the most demanding duty in the Karhold Realm. The
soldiers must travel through snow and ice, in conditions of extreme cold and
poor visibility, knowing that a mistake could mean death. The patrols are
voluntary, and the soldiers who serve on them are paid a bonus that reflects
the risks they take.

## The Reserve

In addition to the professional garrison forces, the Karhold Realm maintains a
militia of all able-bodied men between the ages of eighteen and fifty. The
militia is called up only in times of extreme danger, when the realm is facing
an invasion that the regular garrisons cannot handle alone. The militiamen are
armed with the weapons they possess, and they are organised by the Wardens of
their districts.

The militia has not been called up in living memory, but the preparations for
its summoning are maintained. The weapons and equipment that the militia would
need are stored in the strongholds, and the Wardens keep records of the men who
would be called. The existence of the militia provides a final line of defence
that few potential invaders would be willing to test.

## Relations with the Frostwall Clans

The Karhold Realm shares a border with the [Frostwall
Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans), and the relationship is
one of cautious coexistence punctuated by periods of conflict. The garrison
forces of Karhold are positioned primarily along this border, watching for
raids and incursions. The Wardens maintain contact with the clan chieftains,
and there are established protocols for resolving disputes without resort to
war.

The border with the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) is the most dangerous posting in the
Karhold Realm. The clans are fierce warriors who know the terrain as well as
the Karhold soldiers do, and they are not constrained by the rules of the
[Greywash Compact](/content/history/greywash-compact). The garrisons that face
them are the best equipped and the most experienced in the realm, and they are
commanded by Wardens who have spent their entire careers on the border.
