## The Character of War

The peoples of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) have waged war since before the
[Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era), and the art of conflict has shaped every realm that endures in the
present age. Warfare across the known world is neither uniform in method nor
purpose. The open grasslands of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) demand one kind of
soldier, the frozen passes of the [Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range) another, and the busy
sea-lanes of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) yet another. Each realm has developed martial
traditions that reflect its geography, its resources, and its history of
conflict with neighbours both near and far. The [Three-Banners War](/content/history/three-banners-war), the
[Stoneheim Pass War](/content/history/stoneheim-pass-war), and the [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars) each left lasting marks on how
armies are raised, equipped, and commanded. Understanding the military
landscape of the known world requires an examination of these diverse
traditions and the common threads that bind them.

## Raisings and Standing Forces

Few realms maintain a full-time standing army of the size seen in the [Amurra
Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), where the legion is a
permanent institution supported by a system of citizen obligation and state
funding. Most realms rely on a combination of small professional cadres and
larger levy systems that summon freeholders, craftsmen, and labourers in times
of need. The [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches)
maintain a dedicated force of rangers who patrol the frontier year-round, but
the bulk of their defensive strength comes from farmsteads whose men know how
to use a spear and a bow from childhood. In [Vael](/content/government/national/vael),
the river kingdom supports a standing army paid from the treasury in
[Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), a force that can be
augmented by noble retinues when the kingdom faces a major threat.

The [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation)
raises its levy through a system that assigns each clan and settlement a quota
of riders based on population and grazing land. The [Stoneheim
Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) call up their host by
ancient agreements between the holds, each sending a number of warriors
proportional to its output of ore and finished metal. The [Kharesh
Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) organise their raiding
parties around kinship groups, with successful war leaders attracting followers
through reputation and the promise of plunder. Every system of raising soldiers
reflects the social structure of the realm that employs it, and no two are
precisely alike.

## The Influence of Terrain

The geography of the three continents dictates the shape of their armies and
the nature of their campaigns. Aethelar, the most temperate and populous
continent, supports the largest and most varied forces. Its river valleys and
coastal plains allow for the movement of large armies, while its mountain
ranges — the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) and
the [Thunderback Range](/content/location/natural/feature/thunderback-range) —
create natural bottlenecks that have been fortified over centuries. The
[Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the
[Greater Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea)
provide avenues for naval power, and the realms that command the best harbours
have historically held advantages in trade and war alike.

Sahrana, the desert continent, favours light cavalry and camel-borne raiders
who can cross vast distances with minimal supply. The [Vashari
Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) has mastered this form of
warfare, fielding mounted archers who strike from range and withdraw before a
heavier force can respond. Fortifications in Sahrana cluster around oases and
the few perennial rivers, making the control of water sources a strategic
imperative in every conflict.

Morvind, the cold and mountainous northern continent, imposes severe limits on
military operations. The [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans)
and the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) field
forces that are smaller than those of the southern realms but are hardened by
the environment. Armour in Morvind tends toward heavier furs and layered mail,
and weapons are designed to be effective at close quarters where cold and
blizzard conditions can make archery unreliable.

## The Role of Mercenaries

[Mercenary companies](/content/military/mercenary-companies) have been a feature of warfare since before the [Long
Peace](/content/history/long-peace), and their importance has only grown as
treasure accumulated in the coffers of the established realms. A well-regarded
company can tip the balance of a campaign, and the great powers of Aethelar
maintain standing relationships with several such forces. The reliability of
mercenaries varies considerably; some companies have served the same employer
for generations, while others have been known to change sides for a better
offer. The [Greywash Compact](/content/history/greywash-compact) included
provisions regulating the conduct of mercenaries, and subsequent agreements
have attempted to standardise their contracts, though enforcement remains
uneven.

## Naval Power

Control of the seas is as important as control of the land for the trading
realms. [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) fields the largest
fleet in the known world, a force of deep-water warships that protects the
eastern trade routes and projects power across the [Stormfall](/content/location/natural/continent/stormfall) Sea.
[Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) matches [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) in the
northern waters, its fleet designed for the rougher conditions of the
[Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range) approaches.
The [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) and the
[Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league)
maintain galley fleets suited to the calmer waters of the [Greater Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea)
and the sheltered coastal routes. [Naval warfare](/content/military/naval-warfare) in the known world ranges from
ramming actions between war galleys to boarding actions between deep-water
ships, and the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) have
developed a distinctive style of coastal patrol that relies on swift,
shallow-draft vessels.

## Fortifications and Siegecraft

The fortresses of the known world are as varied as the armies that besiege
them. Dwarven stonework in the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds)
is considered the finest, with gates and walls that have withstood assault for
centuries. The river forts of [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) command the crossing points of the great
rivers, while the border keeps of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach)
guard the passes through the [Thunderback Range](/content/location/natural/feature/thunderback-range). Siegecraft has advanced
steadily since the Founding Era, with engineers developing ever more powerful
torsion engines, rams, and siege towers. The [Stoneheim
Pass War](/content/history/stoneheim-pass-war) demonstrated that even the best
fortifications can be taken with sufficient determination and ingenuity, but
the cost in lives is always high.

## The Conduct of War

Rules governing the conduct of war exist in every realm, though they are
observed unevenly. The [Greywash Compact](/content/history/greywash-compact) codified certain expectations: that
non-combatants should be spared where possible, that surrendering garrisons
should be granted quarter, and that prisoners of sufficient rank should be
ransomed rather than executed. In practice, these rules are frequently bent or
broken, especially in conflicts between realms that share no cultural or
commercial ties. The [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) do not consider
themselves bound by the Compact, and wars along the fringes of the known world
tend to be more brutal than those fought in the civilised heartland of
Aethelar.

## The Costs of Conflict

Warfare is expensive. The raising, equipping, and supplying of an army consumes
resources that could otherwise be spent on building roads, dredging harbours,
or stocking granaries. The [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars)
impoverished both participants for a generation after the peace was signed, and
the [Three-Banners War](/content/history/three-banners-war) left entire regions
depopulated. Some realms have learned to measure the cost of war carefully,
while others continue to pursue glory or vengeance at ruinous expense. The
wisest commanders understand that a war is won not when the enemy surrenders
but when the peace that follows is sustainable.
