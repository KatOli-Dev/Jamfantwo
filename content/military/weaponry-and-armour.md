## The Smith's Art

The weapons and armour of the known world are the products of generations of
craftsmanship, refined through centuries of use in battle and tested in the
fires of conflict. Every realm has developed its own traditions of smithing,
shaped by the materials available, the demands of the local climate, and the
tactical preferences of the soldiers who carry the weapons. The dwarven smiths
of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) are
rightly celebrated for their skill, but the human smiths of
[Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth),
[Vael](/content/government/national/vael), and the [Southern
Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) produce work of the highest
quality.

The materials of weapon-making are iron and steel, with bronze used for certain
specialised applications. Iron is smelted from ore in furnaces that reach
temperatures high enough to separate the metal from the rock, and the resulting
bloom is hammered to remove impurities and to shape the metal into bars. Steel
is made by adding carbon to iron in a controlled process, producing a metal
that is harder and tougher than iron alone. The best steel comes from the
[Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), where the ores are rich and the smiths have mastered the
techniques of carburisation and quenching.

## Swords

The sword is the most prestigious weapon in the known world, carried by kings,
commanders, and warriors of every realm who can afford one. The design of
swords varies widely. The longsword of the southern realms has a straight blade
of about three feet, double-edged, with a cruciform hilt. It is used for both
cutting and thrusting, and it requires considerable training to use
effectively. The longsword is the weapon of the heavy infantry and the cavalry,
and it is often passed down through families as an heirloom.

The curved sabre of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact)
and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) is
designed for cutting from horseback. The curve of the blade concentrates the
force of the blow at a single point, allowing the rider to strike with
devastating effect while passing at speed. The sabre is lighter than the
longsword and is worn suspended from a waist belt, ready for quick drawing.

The short sword of [Vael](/content/government/national/vael), the vaelspatha, is a stabbing weapon designed for use
in the close confines of a shield wall or a riverboat deck. It has a broad
blade of about eighteen inches, tapering to a sharp point. The vaelspatha is
carried as a secondary weapon, used when the spear is lost or when the
fighting becomes too close for the longer weapon to be effective.

## Spears and Polearms

The spear is the most common weapon in the known world, carried by the infantry
of every realm. It is cheap to produce, easy to teach, and effective in both
offence and defence. The spear is typically eight to ten feet in length, with a
head of iron or steel that is socketed onto the shaft. The shaft is of ash,
oak, or another dense wood, chosen for its strength and flexibility.

The long spear of the [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) Holds is twelve feet in length, used in the
dense phalanx formation that is the hallmark of dwarf infantry. The length of
the spear allows the rear ranks to reach over the front ranks, presenting a
wall of points that no enemy can approach without risk. The Stoneheim spear is
tipped with a broad head that can penetrate armour and that makes a wound that
is difficult to treat.

The pike is an even longer weapon, eighteen to twenty feet in length, used by
the infantry of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic).
The pike is unwieldy in individual combat but devastating in formation, and the
[Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) legions have used their pike phalanxes to defeat cavalry charges that
would have broken any other infantry.

## Bows and Missile Weapons

The bow is the most important missile weapon in the known world, used for
hunting, for war, and for sport. The self bow, made from a single piece of
wood, is the simplest form. It is used by the hunter and the levy soldier, and
it is adequate for shooting at short ranges. The longbow, used by the archers
of [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) and the [Northern
Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), is a self bow of
exceptional size, requiring great strength to draw but delivering an arrow that
can penetrate mail at long range.

The composite bow is a more sophisticated weapon, built from layers of horn,
sinew, and wood. The composite bow is shorter than the longbow but stores more
energy, delivering a faster arrow that retains its force at longer ranges. The
composite bow is the weapon of the horse archer, used by the Plains
Confederation, the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes).

The crossbow is used by some realms, particularly in siege warfare. The
crossbow fires a bolt that is shorter and heavier than an arrow, and it can
penetrate armour that would stop a bowshot. The crossbow is slower to load
than a bow, but it requires less training to use effectively, and it is valued
by commanders who need to field large numbers of missile troops quickly.

## Armour

Body armour has developed in response to the weapons it must resist. The simplest
form is the padded jack, a garment of multiple layers of cloth or leather that
provides protection against cuts and bludgeons. The jack is worn by levy
soldiers who cannot afford better, and it is often used as padding under mail
or plate.

Mail is the most common form of armour for professional soldiers. A hauberk of
mail is made from thousands of iron rings, each linked through its neighbours
to form a flexible, protective garment. Mail provides excellent protection
against cuts and reasonable protection against thrusts, and it allows the
wearer to move freely. The finest mail comes from the Stoneheim Holds, where
the rings are riveted rather than butted, making them almost impossible to
break.

Plate armour offers the highest level of protection, covering the body in
shaped steel plates that deflect blows and resist penetration. Full plate
armour is expensive and heavy, and it is worn only by the wealthiest warriors
and by the cavalry of the southern realms. A suit of plate armour must be
fitted to the individual wearer, and it requires regular maintenance to keep it
in good condition.

## Shields

The shield is the oldest form of defence, carried by soldiers of every culture.
The round shield, three feet in diameter, is the most common form, made from
wood planking covered with leather or rawhide and reinforced with an iron rim
and a central boss. The round shield is used by the infantry of Vael, the
cavalry of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and the warriors of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans).

The kite shield, longer and narrower than the round shield, provides better
protection for the legs and is used by cavalry. The rectangular shield, or
scutum, is used by the [Amurra Republic legion](/content/military/amurra-republic-legion), providing cover for the entire
body when the legionaries form their shield wall. The dwarves of Stoneheim use
a round shield of iron-bound wood that is smaller than the human round shield
but thicker and heavier.
