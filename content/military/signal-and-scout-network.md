## The Eyes of the Realm

Communication and intelligence are as important to the conduct of war as
soldiers and weapons. A commander who knows where the enemy is and what the
enemy intends can concentrate his forces at the decisive point, catch the enemy
by surprise, and avoid ambushes and traps. The realms of the known world have
developed a variety of systems for gathering intelligence and transmitting
messages, ranging from the simple signal fires of the northern frontier to the
organised messenger services of the southern kingdoms.

The need for rapid communication is greatest in times of war, but the networks
that support it are maintained in peacetime as well. A realm that neglects its
communications in peacetime will find itself unable to respond when war breaks
out, and the cost of rebuilding a neglected network is far higher than the cost
of maintaining one that already exists.

## The Semaphore Towers of Aldreth

[Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) has developed the most
sophisticated signalling system in the known world, a chain of semaphore towers
that stretches from [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) to the
borders of the realm. Each tower is equipped with a semaphore mast, a vertical
pole with movable arms that can be set in different positions to represent
letters and numbers. The operators read the message from the previous tower,
repeat it, and pass it along the chain.

The semaphore system can transmit a message from the border to [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) in a
matter of hours, a journey that would take a rider three days. The system is
used for military communications, for the transmission of government orders,
and for the dissemination of news. The towers are staffed by trained operators
who serve under the authority of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) postal service, and they are
maintained to a high standard.

## The Signal Fires of the Northern Marches

The [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) use a
simpler but equally effective system of signal fires. Beacon points are
established on hilltops along the frontier, each within sight of the next. When
a threat is detected, the nearest beacon is lit, and the fire is seen by the
next beacon, which is lit in turn. Within an hour, the entire frontier knows
that danger is approaching.

The signal fire system is limited in the amount of information it can transmit.
A single fire means a general warning; two fires mean a major invasion; three
fires mean that the situation is desperate and that every available man should
take up arms. The system is used in conjunction with riders who carry more
detailed messages, but the signal fires provide the initial warning that
allows the defences to be alerted before the enemy arrives.

## The River Messenger Service of Vael

[Vael](/content/government/national/vael) makes use of the river that gives the
kingdom its name as a highway for communications. The River Messenger Service
operates a fleet of fast boats that carry messages up and down the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael),
travelling with the current in one direction and using sails or oars against it
in the other. The boats are small and light, crewed by two or three men, and
they can make the journey from the headwaters to
[Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth) in four days.

The messenger boats travel day and night, using lamps to navigate in the dark,
and they carry dispatches for the government, the army, and the commercial
houses that trade along the river. The service is reliable and efficient, and
it has been operating without interruption for over two hundred years. The
boats are protected by the River Guard, and interference with a messenger boat
is a serious crime.

## The Desert Riders of Vashari

The [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) relies on mounted
messengers to carry communications across the vast distances of the [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana)
desert. The messengers are riders who know the desert intimately, who can find
water where others see only sand, and who can navigate by the stars when the
sun has erased all landmarks. They ride the hardy desert horses and camels of
the region, and they can cover distances that would be impossible for a
messenger in any other environment.

The [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari) messenger service is organised into stages, with riders passing
dispatches from one stage to the next. Each stage is a day's ride, and at each
stage fresh horses and riders are waiting. A message can be carried from one
end of the Pact's territory to the other in ten days, a journey that would take
an ordinary traveller a month.

## The Scout Network of the Plains

The [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation)
maintains a network of scouts who watch the approaches to the grassland and
report the movements of potential invaders. The scouts are riders who know the
plains as a sailor knows the sea, reading the terrain and the weather and the
signs of passage that are invisible to an untrained eye. They operate in small
groups, moving constantly and living off the land.

The scouts report to the clan chieftains and to the [Confederation Council](/content/government/local/plains-confederation-council),
using riders to carry their messages. The information they provide allows the
Council to decide whether to call out the levy and where to concentrate the
riders. The scout network has been instrumental in every successful defence of
the plains, and it has never been successfully deceived by an invader.

## The Mountain Beacons of Stoneheim

The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) use a
system of signal beacons that is similar to that of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) but is
adapted to the mountainous terrain of the dwarf realm. The beacons are
positioned on peaks and ridges, each visible from the next, and they can carry
a warning from the outermost hold to [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) itself in a matter of hours.

The dwarven beacons are built of stone and are permanently manned, unlike the
temporary beacon points used in the Northern Marches. The keepers of the
beacons are veterans of the [Stoneheim Host](/content/military/stoneheim-host), men who have been given light duty
in their old age and who take their responsibility seriously. The beacons are
lit only in times of extreme danger, and their lighting is a signal that the
host must be assembled immediately.

## The Intelligence Trade

In addition to the official communication networks, there exists a shadowy
trade in intelligence that operates in every realm. Spies and informants gather
information about the military preparations of neighbouring realms, about the
movements of armies, and about the plans of commanders. The information is sold
to the highest bidder, and it is used by the realms that can afford it to gain
an advantage over their rivals.

The intelligence trade is dangerous. A spy who is caught can expect execution,
and the realms that employ spies go to great lengths to protect their sources
and to deceive the spies of their enemies. The trade flourishes despite the
risks, for the value of good intelligence is incalculable and the consequences
of being blind to the enemy's movements can be catastrophic.
