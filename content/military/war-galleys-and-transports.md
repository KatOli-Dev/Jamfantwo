## The Ships of War

The distinction between a warship and a merchant vessel is not always clear in
the known world, for many merchant ships are built to be converted for military
service if the need arises. But there exist vessels that are designed from the
keel up for war, ships whose speed, strength, and carrying capacity for soldiers
make them specialised instruments of naval combat. These vessels fall into two
broad categories: the war galley, built for speed and ramming, and the troop
transport, built for capacity and endurance.

The design of war galleys and transports has developed over centuries of
experience, and the ships that sail in the present age are the products of that
development. The shipwrights of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth),
[Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven), the [Thessaran
League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league), and the [Northern Coast
League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) each have their own
traditions and their own specialised designs.

## The Trireme

The trireme is the most common type of war galley in the waters of the [Greater
Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea) and the
southern coasts. It carries three banks of oars on each side, with one man to
each oar, for a total of one hundred seventy rowers. The rowers are seated
on benches that are arranged in a stepped pattern, allowing each bank of oars
to operate without interference from the others.

The trireme is built for speed. Its hull is long and narrow, with a
length-to-beam ratio of approximately six to one. The hull is light, with the
planking fastened to the frames by mortise-and-tenon joints that give the
structure strength without excessive weight. A trireme at full speed can
travel at eight knots, a speed that no sailing ship can match in calm
conditions.

The trireme's primary weapon is the ram, a bronze-sheathed projection from
the bow that is designed to punch through the planking of an enemy hull. The
ram is positioned at the waterline, and it is driven by the momentum of the
ship. A well-executed ramming attack can sink an enemy vessel with a single
blow.

## The Quinquereme

The quinquereme is a larger version of the trireme, with five banks of oars on
each side. The quinquereme is slower than the trireme, but it carries a larger
complement of marines and can serve as a platform for light catapults. The
quinquereme is used as a flagship and as a transport for significant forces of
infantry.

The quinquereme is also more seaworthy than the trireme. Its broader beam and
higher freeboard allow it to operate in rougher conditions, and its greater
storage capacity allows it to carry provisions for longer voyages. The
quinquereme is the preferred vessel for long-range operations, where the
trireme's speed advantage is outweighed by the quinquereme's endurance.

## The Deep-Water Warship

The navies of Aldreth and Nordhaven rely on deep-water warships rather than
galleys for their primary combat power. These ships are built for the open
ocean, with deep keels, high freeboards, and multiple masts that carry a
substantial spread of sail. The deep-water warship is slower in calm conditions
than a galley, but it can make way in any wind, and it can remain at sea for
months.

The deep-water warship carries a crew of sailors to handle the ship and a
complement of marines to fight. The marines are armed with bows, spears, and
swords, and they are trained to fight from the deck and to board enemy vessels.
The ship is armed with light catapults that can be used against enemy crews and
rigging.

The deep-water warship is the most expensive type of vessel to build and
maintain, but it is also the most capable. Aldreth's deep-water warships are
the largest in the known world, and they have dominated the [Stormfall
Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) for centuries.

## The Transport Ship

Transport ships are designed for the movement of soldiers, horses, and supplies
across the sea. A typical transport is a beamy, slow vessel with a deep hold
and a large deck area. The transport carries a crew of sailors to handle the
ship, but the primary capacity is for passengers and cargo. A large transport
can carry up to five hundred soldiers or two hundred horses.

Transports are not built for combat. They are slow, unmanoeuvrable, and
vulnerable to attack. A transport that encounters an enemy warship is at the
mercy of the enemy unless it is escorted. The transport's only defence is its
speed when sailing downwind and its ability to carry a large number of
soldiers who can fight if the ship is boarded.

The design of transports has been improved significantly since the
[Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars), when inadequate
transports contributed to the loss of an entire army. Transports of the present age are
built with stronger hulls, better ventilation for the horses, and improved
facilities for the storage of provisions.

## The Horse Transport

The transport of horses by sea presents particular challenges. Horses are
large, heavy animals that require space, ventilation, and careful handling.
A horse transport is designed with stalls that are arranged along the deck,
with the horses secured in place to prevent them from falling when the ship
rolls. The stalls are provided with hay nets and water troughs, and the deck
is designed to allow the horses to be loaded and unloaded efficiently.

Horse transports are crewed by grooms and handlers who are experienced in the
care of horses at sea. The journey is stressful for the animals, and a
significant number of horses may be lost if the voyage is long or the weather
bad. The horse transports of the [Plains
Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) are regarded
as the best in the known world, for the Plainsfolk have more experience with
the movement of horses than any other realm.

## The Landing Craft

The assault landing is one of the most difficult operations in naval warfare.
The attacking force must be transported from ship to shore under fire, and the
soldiers must reach the beach in a condition to fight. The landing craft used
for this purpose are small, shallow-draft boats that can be beached on the
shore, allowing the soldiers to disembark directly onto dry land.

Landing craft are carried on the decks of transport ships and are lowered into
the water when the fleet reaches its destination. The boats are rowed to the
shore, with the soldiers providing covering fire from their bows. The boats are
vulnerable to attack from the shore, and the landing is a desperate business
that requires courage and determination.

## The Fire Ship

The fire ship is a specialised vessel that is used to attack enemy fleets at
anchor. An old hulk or a cheaply built vessel is filled with combustible
material — pitch, oil, dry wood, and sulphur — and set alight. The fire ship
is then sailed or towed into the midst of the enemy fleet, where the flames
spread to the enemy ships.

Fire ships are unpredictable and dangerous to their users as well as to the
enemy. The wind and current may carry the fire ship away from its target, or
the enemy may be able to tow it aside before it reaches the fleet. The use of
fire ships is a desperate measure, employed when the attacking fleet cannot
defeat the enemy by conventional means.
