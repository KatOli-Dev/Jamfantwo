## The Academy Forts

The [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) occupies a
region of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) beyond the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), a land of rolling
hills, inland seas, and fertile valleys that has been settled for centuries. The Southern
Reach is famous throughout the known world for its military academies,
institutions that train the officers and soldiers who serve not only the Reach
itself but also many of the other realms of Aethelar. The academies produce
officers who are skilled in strategy, tactics, and the arts of command, and
they are the foundation of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach)'s military strength.

The Frontier Guard is the standing force that protects the borders of the
Southern Reach. It is a professional force, recruited from the graduates of the
academies and from the population of the Reach, and it is organised into
regiments that are stationed along the frontier. The Guard is tasked with
defending against incursions from the less settled lands to the south and east,
with maintaining order in the border regions, and with providing assistance to
the settlements that lie in the path of potential invasion.

## The Military Academies

The Southern Reach is home to five military academies, each with its own
specialisation and its own traditions. The oldest is the Academy of the Silver
Pike, which trains infantry officers and which has produced some of the finest
commanders in the known world. The Academy of the Red Horse trains cavalry
officers, emphasising the shock charge and the pursuit of a defeated enemy. The
Academy of the Grey Falcon trains scouts and skirmishers, producing soldiers
who are skilled in reconnaissance and irregular warfare.

The academies accept students from the age of sixteen, and the course of study
lasts four years. The students are instructed in military history, in
logistics, in the handling of weapons, and in the command of soldiers. They are
subjected to a discipline that is strict even by the standards of the military,
and those who cannot meet the standards are dismissed. Graduates of the
academies are commissioned as officers in the Frontier Guard, and they carry
the reputation of their academy with them throughout their careers.

## The Cadet System

In addition to the officer academies, the Southern Reach maintains a system of
cadet schools that train soldiers for the ranks. The cadet schools accept young
men and women from the age of fourteen, providing them with a basic education
and with training in the skills they will need as soldiers. The cadets serve
for a period of three years, after which they are assigned to regiments of the
Frontier Guard.

The cadet system is designed to produce soldiers who are literate, disciplined,
and loyal to the Reach. The cadets are taught to read and write, to perform
basic arithmetic, and to understand the principles of military organisation.
They are also trained in the use of weapons, in formation drill, and in the
construction of field fortifications. A cadet who completes the training is a
valuable asset to any regiment.

## Organisation of the Guard

The Frontier Guard is organised into twelve regiments, each of approximately
one thousand soldiers. Four regiments are stationed on the southern frontier,
watching the approaches from the southern highlands and the lands beyond. Four
regiments are stationed on the eastern frontier, where the Reach borders the
less settled regions of the interior. Three regiments are stationed in the
interior of the Reach, ready to respond to threats anywhere in the realm. One
regiment is stationed at the capital, providing a guard for the government and
a reserve that can be committed in an emergency.

Each regiment is commanded by a Colonel, who is appointed by the Council of the
Southern Reach. The Colonel is responsible for the training, discipline, and
equipment of his regiment, and he answers directly to the Marshal of the Reach,
the senior military officer in the realm.

## Equipment

The soldiers of the Frontier Guard are equipped to a standard that is
consistent across all regiments. The infantry carry a spear and a sword, wear a
mail hauberk and a helmet, and carry a round shield. The cavalry carry a lance
and a sword, wear a heavier hauberk, and ride horses that are bred in the
valleys of the Reach. The archers carry composite bows and are trained to shoot
at moving targets from a distance.

The equipment of the Guard is manufactured in the workshops of the Reach, which
produce weapons and armour of high quality. The forges of the Reach are
renowned for their steel, which is second only to that produced in the
[Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds). The workshops
also produce the uniforms, tents, and other equipment that the Guard requires.

## The Border Watches

The Frontier Guard maintains a network of border watches, small fortified posts
that are positioned at intervals along the frontier. Each watch is manned by a
squad of ten to twenty soldiers, who patrol the surrounding area and report any
signs of enemy activity. The watches are connected by a system of signal fires
and messenger riders, and they can summon reinforcements from the nearest
regiment if necessary.

Service on the border watches is considered the most demanding duty in the
Guard. The watches are located in remote and often dangerous areas, and the
soldiers who serve on them must be self-reliant and resourceful. The watches
are rotated regularly, with each soldier serving a tour of six months on the
border before returning to the main garrison.

## Relations with the Settlements

The Frontier Guard maintains close relations with the settlements of the
border regions. The Guard provides protection against raiders and bandits, and
it assists the settlements in times of natural disaster. The settlements, in
turn, provide the Guard with intelligence, supplies, and recruits. The
relationship is mutually beneficial, and it is one of the foundations of the
Southern Reach's stability.

The Guard also maintains a system of subsidies for the border settlements,
providing funds for the construction of fortifications and for the training of
local militias. The subsidies encourage the settlements to take responsibility
for their own defence, reducing the burden on the Guard and ensuring that the
border is defended by those who have the most to lose if it falls.

## The Reputation of the Guard

The Frontier Guard is respected throughout the known world for its discipline,
its professionalism, and its effectiveness. Graduates of the Southern Reach
academies serve in the armies of many other realms, and they carry the
reputation of the Guard with them. The Guard has never been defeated in a major
battle, and it has successfully defended the Southern Reach against every
invasion that has been attempted in the past three centuries.
