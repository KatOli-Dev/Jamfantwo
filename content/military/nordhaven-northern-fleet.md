## The Northern Seas

The fleet of [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) is built for
waters that no southern sailor would willingly cross. The northern reaches of
the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the
approaches to the [Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range)
are subject to fierce storms, shifting ice floes, and fogs that can reduce
visibility to a few yards. The ships that sail these waters must be stronger,
the crews harder, and the captains more skilled than those who ply the milder
southern routes. The [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) [Northern Fleet](/content/government/local/northern-fleet) meets these requirements and has
done so since the earliest days of the realm.

Nordhaven's position on the northern coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar)
gives it control over the sea-lanes that connect the continent to the
[Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) and the [Frostwall
Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) of
[Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The fleet protects
this trade, enforces the realm's claims to fishing grounds and sealing
stations, and provides a defence against the raiders who occasionally descend
from the northern islands. Without the fleet, Nordhaven would be vulnerable to
attack from the sea and cut off from the resources that sustain its economy.

## Ship Design

Nordhaven warships are built to a different standard than those of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth). The
hulls are shorter and broader, with a deeper draught that provides stability in
heavy seas. The bows are reinforced with extra planking and iron strakes to
withstand contact with floating ice, and the rudders are built larger and
stronger to maintain control in rough conditions. The masts are stepped more
securely, and the rigging is of heavier cordage that can endure the strain of
gale-force winds.

The typical Nordhaven warship carries a crew of eighty to one hundred sailors
and marines. The marines are armed with spears, axes, and bows, and they are
trained to fight in the rolling conditions of a ship's deck. Every member of
the crew, from the captain to the youngest cabin boy, is expected to be able to
handle a weapon and to fight if boarding is required. The distinction between
sailor and marine is less rigid in the Northern Fleet than in the navies of the
south, and every hand is expected to contribute to the defence of the ship.

## Organisation

The Northern Fleet is organised into three divisions. The Home Division guards
the approaches to [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven)
itself and patrols the coastal waters of the realm. The Far Patrol operates in
the waters between [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), keeping the sea-lanes open for
merchant shipping and deterring the raiders who operate from the islands and
skerries of the northern ocean. The Ice Watch is a smaller force that sails as
far north as the ice permits, monitoring the movements of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans)
and maintaining a presence in waters that few other ships visit.

Each division is commanded by a Sea-Captain who holds authority from the Jarl
of Nordhaven. The Sea-Captains are experienced mariners who have spent decades
at sea, and they are chosen for their knowledge of the northern waters and
their ability to command in the harsh conditions that prevail there. Below the
Sea-Captains, individual ship captains are responsible for the day-to-day
operations of their vessels and for the discipline and training of their crews.

## The Shipyards of Nordhaven

The shipyards of Nordhaven are located in a sheltered harbour that is protected
from the worst of the northern storms by a headland of dark granite. The yards
are smaller than those of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) but are no less skilled in their craft. The
shipwrights of Nordhaven have developed techniques for working with the
tough, dense timber that grows in the northern forests, timber that resists
rot and stands up to the punishment of ice and storm.

The yards also produce the specialised equipment that the Northern Fleet
requires. Ice axes, boarding hooks, and reinforced ramming prows are forged in
the smithies that line the harbour. The ropewalks produce cables of braided
leather and horsehair that do not become brittle in freezing temperatures, and
the sail lofts stitch canvas that has been treated with tallow to repel water
and resist mildew.

## The Crews

The sailors of the Northern Fleet are drawn from the coastal population of
Nordhaven and from the fishing villages that dot the northern coast. They are
hardened by a life spent at sea in difficult conditions, and they take pride in
their ability to handle ships in weather that would keep southern sailors in
port. Service in the fleet is voluntary, and the pay is supplemented by a share
of the customs duties collected from the merchant ships that the fleet
escorts.

Training is continuous and practical. New recruits are assigned to experienced
crews and learn their trade through direct experience, handling lines, standing
lookout, and learning to read the signs of weather in the sky and the sea. A
recruit who shows promise may be sent to the Navigation Hall in Nordhaven to
study the use of the lead line, the compass, and the charts that the fleet has
accumulated over generations.

## The Ice Patrol

The most dangerous duty in the Northern Fleet is service with the Ice Watch.
Ships assigned to this division sail into waters where the sea freezes in
winter and where icebergs drift south on the currents. The Ice Watch maintains
a chain of lookout posts on the islands and headlands of the far north,
watching for the movement of enemy ships and for the advance of the ice that
marks the changing seasons.

The Ice Watch also undertakes rescue operations, searching for merchant ships
that have been caught by the ice or damaged in storms. The crews of the Ice
Watch are among the most skilled in the fleet, and they are paid a premium for
the risks they take. Service with the Ice Watch is considered a mark of honour,
and those who complete a tour are regarded with respect throughout the fleet.

## The History of the Northern Fleet

The Northern Fleet was founded during the reign of
[Thessa Nordhaven](/content/people/historical/thessa-nordhaven), who recognised
that the growing power of Nordhaven depended on control of the northern sea-
lanes. The early fleet consisted of a handful of converted merchant ships, but
Thessa's successors invested heavily in naval expansion, and by the end of the
[Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era) the fleet was the dominant naval
power in the northern waters.

The fleet played a decisive role in the [Three-Banners
War](/content/history/three-banners-war), where it disrupted the supply lines
of the enemy and transported Nordhaven's armies to the battlefields of the
south. The fleet also fought in the [Stoneheim Pass
War](/content/history/stoneheim-pass-war), providing naval support for the
dwarven forces and preventing enemy reinforcements from reaching the
battlefield by sea. The Northern Fleet has maintained its readiness through the
[Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace), and it remains the most capable
naval force in the northern half of the known world.

## The Role of the Fleet in Peace

In times of peace, the Northern Fleet serves as an instrument of diplomacy and
commerce. The fleet escorts merchant ships, enforces the realm's claims to
fishing waters, and maintains a presence in the ports of allied and neutral
realms. The fleet also undertakes voyages of exploration, charting the coasts
of the northern continent and searching for new routes to the lands beyond.

The fleet's peacetime duties also include the suppression of piracy, a problem
that has plagued the northern waters since the earliest days of navigation. The
Northern Fleet maintains a dedicated anti-piracy squadron that patrols the
shipping lanes and hunts down pirate vessels. The squadron has been successful
in reducing piracy to a manageable level, though it has not eliminated the
problem entirely.
