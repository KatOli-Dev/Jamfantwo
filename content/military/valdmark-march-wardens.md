## The Watchers of the Border

[Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) lies at the eastern edge of
the settled river valley of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) interior, a realm of forests, hills, and river valleys
that forms a buffer between the established valley settlements and the wilder cold steppes
beyond. The March Wardens are the military force that guards this buffer,
protecting [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) from incursions by the tribes and raiders that inhabit the
eastern wilderness. The Wardens are not a standing army in the manner of the
legions of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic);
they are a force of mounted soldiers who patrol the border constantly, living
in the saddle and responding to threats as they arise.

The March Wardens are recruited from the population of Valdmark, a people who
are themselves descended from the frontiersmen who first settled this land. The
Wardens are tough, self-reliant, and skilled in the arts of bushcraft and
horsemanship. They know the border region intimately, every ford, every pass,
every hidden valley where an enemy might attempt to cross.

## Organisation

The March Wardens are organised into companies, each responsible for a sector
of the border. There are eight companies in total, each commanded by a Warden-
Captain who reports to the Lord Warden of Valdmark. The Lord Warden is the
senior military officer in the realm, appointed by the ruler of Valdmark, and
he is responsible for the overall defence of the border.

Each company consists of one hundred twenty Wardens, divided into four troops
of thirty. The troops are further divided into patrols of ten, which are the
basic operating unit. A patrol operates independently for weeks at a time,
ranging across its assigned sector and reporting its findings to the troop
commander. The patrols are self-sufficient, carrying their own provisions and
equipment, and they are trained to survive in the wilderness for extended
periods.

## Recruitment and Training

Recruits for the March Wardens are drawn from the young men and women of
Valdmark who have grown up on the frontier and who possess the skills that the
Wardens require. The selection process is rigorous; only those who can ride,
shoot, and navigate in the wilderness are accepted. The recruits undergo a
year of training before they are assigned to a patrol.

The training includes instruction in horsemanship, archery, tracking, and
survival skills. The recruits learn to read the signs of passage, to identify
the tracks of men and horses, and to follow a trail across difficult terrain.
They are taught to recognise the plants that are safe to eat and those that are
poisonous, to find water in dry country, and to treat the injuries that are
common on the frontier.

## Equipment

The March Wardens are equipped for mobility and endurance. They ride hardy
ponies that are bred in the hills of Valdmark, animals that are sure-footed and
capable of travelling long distances on limited forage. The Wardens carry
composite bows for ranged combat and long knives for close action. Some carry
spears or light axes, depending on their personal preference.

Armour is light, consisting of a leather jerkin reinforced with plates of horn
or boiled leather. The Wardens do not wear heavy armour, for it would impede
their mobility and exhaust their horses. The emphasis is on speed and
endurance, not on protection. A Warden who is caught by a larger force relies
on his horse to carry him to safety, not on his armour to protect him.

## The Patrol

The patrol is the heart of the Warden system. A patrol of ten Wardens rides out
from its base and spends two to three weeks in the field, covering a circuit of
the border sector. The Wardens watch for signs of enemy activity, investigate
reports of incursions, and maintain contact with the settlements and
farmsteads that lie along the border.

The patrols maintain a network of caches where they can replenish their
supplies of food and ammunition. The caches are hidden in remote locations,
known only to the Wardens, and they are restocked by supply parties that travel
from the Warden bases. The cache system allows the patrols to remain in the
field for extended periods without returning to base, and it ensures that the
Wardens can respond quickly to threats anywhere along the border.

## The Watchtowers

The border of Valdmark is marked by a chain of watchtowers that provide the
Wardens with vantage points from which to observe the surrounding countryside.
The towers are built of stone, with a platform at the top that offers a view of
the terrain for miles in every direction. Each tower is manned by a small
garrison of two or three Wardens, who serve a tour of a month before being
relieved.

The watchtowers are equipped with signal fires that can be lit to warn of
approaching danger. The fires are visible from the neighbouring towers and
from the Warden bases, and they provide a means of communication that covers
the entire border in a matter of hours. The watchtowers are the eyes of
Valdmark, and they have saved the realm from surprise attack on many
occasions.

## Relations with the Settlements

The March Wardens maintain close relations with the settlements and farmsteads
of the border region. The settlers are the first to notice signs of enemy
activity, and they provide the Wardens with intelligence that is essential for
the defence of the realm. The Wardens, in turn, provide protection and
assistance, helping the settlers to defend their homes and to recover from
raids.

The relationship is one of mutual dependence. The settlers cannot survive
without the protection of the Wardens, and the Wardens cannot function without
the support of the settlers. This interdependence has created a bond between
the Wardens and the frontier population that is stronger than any formal
military command structure.

## The History of the Wardens

The March Wardens were established in the aftermath of a major invasion that
overran the eastern border of Valdmark and threatened to destroy the realm
entirely. The invasion was repelled, but the losses were severe, and the rulers
of Valdmark determined that such a catastrophe would never be allowed to happen
again. The Wardens were created as a permanent border force, and they have
maintained the security of Valdmark ever since.

The Wardens have fought in every major conflict that has touched the eastern
regions of the Morvind interior. They have repelled invasions, conducted raids into enemy
territory, and served as scouts and guides for the armies of the allied realms.
Their reputation for skill and courage is second to none, and they are regarded
with respect and admiration throughout the known world.
