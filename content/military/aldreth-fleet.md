## The Foundation of Naval Power

The fleet of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is the largest and
most capable naval force in the known world, a position the eastern trading
realm has held since the closing years of the [Long
Peace](/content/history/long-peace). Aldreth's wealth derives from sea-borne
commerce, and the fleet exists to protect that commerce from pirates,
competitors, and the naval forces of rival realms. Without the fleet, the
merchant ships of Aldreth would be vulnerable to attack along every route they
sail, and the treasure that flows into the treasury at
[Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) would dwindle to nothing.

The Aldreth Fleet is not merely a defensive force. It projects power across the
[Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), enforces
trade agreements, and maintains the flow of goods that sustains the Aldreth
economy. The fleet's commanders are among the most influential figures in the
realm, and the shipyards of Eldreth employ thousands of carpenters, sailmakers,
and ropeworkers. A significant portion of the realm's budget is devoted to
naval expenditure, and the fleet is maintained at full readiness at all times,
even during periods of peace.

## Organisation and Command

The Aldreth Fleet is divided into four squadrons, each responsible for a
different area of operation. The Home Squadron guards the approaches to
Eldreth and the coastal waters of Aldreth itself, providing close protection
for the merchant shipping that enters and leaves the great harbour. The Eastern
Squadron patrols the trade routes toward the [Auric
Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) and the far eastern reaches of
the Stormfall Sea, where piracy has historically been a problem. The Western
Squadron operates toward the [Greater Inland
Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea) and the approaches
to [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), keeping the
sea-lanes open for Aldreth merchantmen. The Southern Squadron ranges along the
coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), protecting
trade with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) and
the ports of the southern continent.

Each squadron is commanded by a Fleet-Captain who reports to the Lord Admiral
of Aldreth, the highest naval authority in the realm. The Lord Admiral is a
member of the ruling council and is appointed by the Merchant-Prince of Aldreth
from among the most experienced captains. Below the squadron commanders,
individual ships are commanded by captains who hold their commissions from the
Lord Admiral and who are subject to strict regulations governing discipline,
navigation, and the treatment of prisoners and prizes.

## Ship Types

The backbone of the Aldreth Fleet is the deep-water warship, a vessel of
between one hundred and fifty and two hundred fifty tons burden, carrying a
crew of sixty to one hundred sailors and marines. These ships are rigged with
two or three masts and are capable of extended voyages across open water. They
carry a complement of marines who serve as boarding parties and as missile
troops, and they are armed with light catapults mounted on the forecastle and
aftercastle for use against enemy crews and rigging.

Alongside the deep-water warships, the fleet maintains a substantial number of
smaller vessels. Cutters and schooners serve as scouts, messengers, and patrol
vessels, operating in the coastal waters where larger ships cannot easily
manoeuvre. The fleet also operates a class of fast dispatch vessels, called
eldreth flyers, that are designed for speed above all else. These ships carry a
single mast with an enormous spread of canvas and are used to carry urgent
messages and valuable cargoes that must reach their destination in the shortest
possible time.

## The Shipyards of Eldreth

The shipyards of Eldreth are the largest in the known world, a sprawling
complex of docks, sheds, and workshops that stretches along the eastern shore
of the harbour for nearly a mile. The yards are capable of building a deep-
water warship from keel to completion in under four months when working at full
capacity, and they maintain a stock of seasoned timber, canvas, cordage, and
iron that allows construction to continue even when supply routes are
disrupted.

The yards are organised into specialised sections. The frame yard produces the
curved timbers that form the skeleton of a ship, using patterns that have been
refined over generations of shipwrights. The planking yard cuts and shapes the
strakes that form the hull, using sawpits and adzes to achieve the smooth
curves that make Aldreth ships among the fastest afloat. The rigging loft
produces ropes and cables of every size, from the thin lines used for signal
flags to the heavy cables that anchor a ship in a storm. The foundry casts the
iron fittings, anchors, and catapult components that complete the vessel.

## Recruitment and Crew

Sailors in the Aldreth Fleet are volunteers who serve for a term of five years,
with the option to re-enlist for additional terms. The pay is good, the
conditions are better than those on most merchant vessels, and there is the
prospect of prize money from captured enemy ships. These incentives attract a
steady flow of recruits from the seafaring population of Aldreth and from the
coastal towns of neighbouring realms.

Training is rigorous and continuous. Recruits spend their first three months
ashore, learning the fundamentals of seamanship: how to handle lines, how to
reef sails, how to steer, and how to fight. They are then assigned to a ship,
where they continue their training under the supervision of experienced
sailors. Promotion is based on ability and experience, and it is possible for a
common sailor to rise to the rank of captain through merit alone, though such
rises are rare and require exceptional skill.

## History of the Fleet

The Aldreth Fleet was founded during the early years of the realm's existence,
when the first Merchant-Prince recognised that the city's future lay on the
water. The early fleet consisted of converted merchant ships, but dedicated
warships were built as soon as the resources became available. The fleet played
a decisive role in the [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars),
blockading the ports of the enemy and preventing reinforcements from reaching
their armies in the field.

During the [Three-Banners War](/content/history/three-banners-war), the Aldreth
Fleet suffered its greatest defeat when a squadron was caught in a storm off
the coast of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and lost
seven ships with all hands. The disaster led to significant reforms in ship
design and navigation, and the current fleet is built to higher standards of
seaworthiness than any of its predecessors. The lessons of that storm have been
passed down through generations of shipwrights and captains, and the fleet has
not suffered a similar catastrophe since.
