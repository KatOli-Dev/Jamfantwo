## Overview

Ravenscrag is a mountain settlement in the far interior of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated in a remote valley of the interior ranges where the mountains are at their most rugged and the approaches are the most difficult. The settlement is the most remote permanent community in Morvind, and its people are among the most hardy and the most self-reliant in the known world. Ravenscrag is a small, isolated settlement, and its primary function is the mining of the mineral deposits that are found in the surrounding peaks and the maintenance of the highland routes that connect the far interior to the rest of the continent.

## The Valley and the Mines

The valley in which Ravenscrag stands is a deep, narrow gorge that is carved into the interior ranges by a river that flows from the highest peaks, and the settlement is built on the floor of the gorge and on the terraces that line its walls. The valley is sheltered from the worst of the wind, and the river provides water and the modest agriculture that sustains the community, but the growing season is short and the crops are limited to the hardiest varieties. The mines that surround Ravenscrag are the primary reason for the settlement, and the ore that is extracted from the peaks includes iron, copper, and the precious metals that are found in the deepest veins of the interior ranges.

The mining operations of Ravenscrag are conducted on a small scale, for the deposits are not as rich as those of [Stoneheim](/content/stoneheim) or [Khazgar](/content/khazgar), and the remoteness of the settlement makes the export of ore difficult and expensive. The ore that is extracted is processed in the small forges of the settlement and worked into the tools, the weapons, and the implements that the community needs, and the surplus is exported through the highland routes to the markets of the interior. The [corvids](/content/corvids) of the high forests are occasional visitors to the settlement, and the trade between the mountain dwarves and the forest corvids is a small but valued connection to the wider world.

## The Routes and the Isolation

The routes that connect Ravenscrag to the rest of Morvind are few and difficult, and the settlement is cut off from the wider world for much of the year, when the snow blocks the passes and the river freezes. The overland routes that lead to the interior are navigable only during the brief summer, and the journey to the nearest settlement is a matter of many days of hard travel through the most demanding terrain in the continent. The isolation of Ravenscrag is the defining feature of the settlement, and the people have learned to live on what the valley and the mines provide, supplementing their resources with the occasional trade that arrives during the summer months.

## The Settlement

Ravenscrag is a small, compact settlement, and its buildings are constructed of the dark stone of the gorge, with walls that are thick and roofs that are weighted with stone to resist the wind and the snow. The buildings are built into the terraces that line the walls of the gorge, and the streets are steep and narrow, connected by stairs and paths that are cut into the rock. The population of Ravenscrag is chiefly [dwarven](/content/dwarves), with a small community of [humans](/content/humans) who are involved in the management of the routes and the trade with the interior.

## Significance

Ravenscrag is the most remote permanent settlement in Morvind, and its people are the guardians of the far interior and the highland routes that connect it to the rest of the continent. The settlement is a dependency of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/frostwall-clans), and its mineral output is a small but valued contribution to the economy of the deep interior. The significance of Ravenscrag lies in its survival and its productivity in an environment that is among the most demanding in the known world, and the settlement is a testament to the endurance and the self-reliance of the people of the Morvind interior.
