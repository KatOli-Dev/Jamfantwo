## Overview

Windward is a settlement on a remote island of the eastern [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), situated far from the trade routes that connect the continents and the larger islands. The island is one of the most isolated inhabited places in the known world, and its distance from the trade routes has given the settlement a character that is self-sufficient and distinct from the other island communities. Windward is small and remote, and its people are among the most self-reliant in the known world, for the contact with the wider world is infrequent and the community must sustain itself on what the island and the surrounding waters provide.

## The Island and the Wind

The island on which Windward stands is a small, rugged landmass that rises from the deep waters of the eastern [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and its shores are steep and rocky, with few beaches and fewer natural harbours. The settlement is situated on the leeward side of the island, where the cliffs provide shelter from the prevailing winds that give the island its name, and the harbour is a small, sheltered cove that is accessible only through a narrow entrance between the rocks. The wind is the dominant feature of the island's climate, for the eastern Auric is a region of steady and sometimes fierce winds that blow from the east across the open ocean, and the vegetation of the island is shaped by the wind, with the trees growing low and bent in the direction of the prevailing gusts.

The island is too small and too remote to support agriculture on any significant scale, and the community sustains itself through fishing and the gathering of the resources that the island and the surrounding waters provide. The fishing grounds around the island are productive, for the deep waters of the eastern Auric sustain a variety of fish and marine life, and the catch is the primary food source of the settlement. The island also provides a modest quantity of fresh water, drawn from the springs that emerge from the volcanic rock, and the community manages the water carefully, for it is the most precious resource on the island.

## Trade and the Outside World

The trade of Windward is minimal, for the island is far from the trade routes and the contact with the wider world is infrequent. The vessels that call at the harbour are few, and they are chiefly the explorers and the occasional traders who venture into the remote waters of the eastern Auric. The goods that are exchanged at Windward include the fish and the marine products of the island, which are traded for the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the community cannot produce for itself, and the trade is conducted on a small scale and at irregular intervals.

The [cyclopes](/content/cyclopes) of the remote islands and coastlines are the nearest neighbours of the Windward community, and the occasional encounters between the islanders and the cyclopes are rare and generally distant, for the cyclopes are solitary and the Windward islanders are few. The [tortolans](/content/tortolans) of the warm coasts and islands are occasional visitors to the harbour, and the trade between the two peoples is a minor but valued connection to the wider world.

## The Settlement

Windward is a small, compact settlement, and its buildings are constructed of the dark stone of the cliffs, with low profiles and thick walls that withstand the wind and the salt. The buildings are clustered on the leeward slope above the harbour, and the streets are narrow and sheltered, for the wind is a constant presence and the settlement is built to minimise its exposure. The population of Windward is small and entirely [human](/content/humans), and the people are known for their extraordinary self-reliance and their deep attachment to the remote island that is their home.

## Significance

Windward is the most remote permanent settlement in the known world, and its people are the guardians of one of the most isolated inhabited places in the eastern [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The settlement is a member of the [Auric Isles](/content/auric-isles), the confederation of island communities, though its distance from the other islands means that its participation in the confederation is nominal. The significance of Windward lies in its survival, for the settlement is a testament to the capacity of humans to endure and to thrive in the most remote and the most demanding environments that the known world can offer.
