## Overview

The Valdmark is the human realm of the principal river valley of the interior of [Morvind](/content/morvind), encompassing the settled lands that line the banks of the largest river of the interior. The realm is the only significant human political entity in the Morvind interior, and its capital is [Valdheim](/content/valdheim), the largest and most prosperous city of the interior. The Valdmark is an agricultural and a commercial realm, and its prosperity is built upon the fertility of the river valley and the trade that flows through the interior, connecting the coast to the deep interior and the mountain ranges.

## The Valley and the Agriculture

The river valley that is the heart of the Valdmark is the most fertile region in the interior of Morvind, and the alluvial soils that line the banks of the river are among the most productive in the continent. The agriculture of the valley is the foundation of the realm's prosperity, and the grain, the vegetables, and the fodder that are produced in the valley are the primary exports of the realm. The river is navigable for much of its length, and the barges that carry the goods of the valley to the coast and the goods of the coast to the interior are a common sight on the waterway.

The climate of the valley is milder than the surrounding highlands, for the river moderates the temperature and the surrounding peaks shelter the valley from the worst of the wind. The growing season is longer than on the higher plateaus, and the agriculture of the valley is more varied and more productive than that of the rest of the interior. The valley is the breadbasket of the Morvind interior, and the grain that is produced here is exported to the coast and the other regions of the continent, sustaining the populations that cannot grow their own food.

## The Settlements and the Trade

The Valdmark includes the principal settlements of the interior, and the membership is drawn from the towns and the cities that share in the agricultural and the commercial economy of the valley. [Valdheim](/content/valdheim) is the capital and the largest city, and its market is the primary point of exchange for the goods of the interior. [Frostbeak](/content/frostbeak) is a valued member, commanding the highland pastures that are the source of the wool and the leather that are among the realm's exports. [Coldmoor](/content/coldmoor) is the most remote member, situated on the cold steppes of the far interior, and its pastoral economy is a valued supplement to the agriculture of the valley.

The trade of the Valdmark is dominated by the agricultural products of the valley and the mineral products of the surrounding mountains, and the realm is the primary point of exchange between the interior and the coast. The grain, the wool, and the leather of the valley are traded at Valdheim for the metalwork, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that are imported from the coast and the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) cities of the interior. The river is the primary route of trade, and the wharves and quays that line the riverbank at Valdheim are the centre of the realm's commerce.

## Governance and the People

The Valdmark is governed from [Valdheim](/content/valdheim) by a lord who is appointed by the council of the valley settlements, and the administration is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the collection of taxes, the maintenance of the river and the roads, and the administration of justice. The population of the Valdmark is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the valley are among the most prosperous and the most settled in the Morvind interior. The [dwarves](/content/dwarves) of the surrounding mountains are present in the valley, where they trade their metalwork for the agricultural products of the realm, and the [lupines](/content/lupines) of the cold steppes are occasional visitors to the valley settlements, where they trade the fur and the meat of the steppe for the grain and the manufactured goods of the valley.

## Significance

The Valdmark is the only significant human political entity in the Morvind interior, and its agricultural wealth and its commercial role are the foundation of its significance. The realm's trade connects the coast to the deep interior, and its grain sustains the populations of the interior that cannot grow their own food. The significance of the Valdmark lies in its position as the agricultural and the commercial centre of the interior, and the realm is a symbol of the human capacity to establish and to maintain prosperous settlements in the demanding environment of the Morvind interior.
