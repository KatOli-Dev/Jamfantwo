## Overview

The Council of Khalisar is the governing body of [Khalisar](/content/khalisar), the greatest of the Sahrani coastal city-states, and it is the primary political institution of the wealthiest and most influential settlement on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). The council is not a monarchy but an elected body of officials and appointed elders, and its governance is based on the principles of merit, consensus, and the representation of the commercial and the civic interests of the city. The Council of Khalisar is the most powerful political entity in Sahrana, and its decisions shape the economy and the culture of the Sahrani coast and the continent as a whole.

## The Council and the Governance

The Council of Khalisar is composed of elected officials who are chosen from the merchant and the civic families of the city, and appointed elders who are drawn from the most experienced and the most respected members of the community. The council meets in the council hall, a substantial building that is situated in the centre of the city, and the decisions of the council are made by vote. The council oversees the administration of the city, the regulation of the trade, the maintenance of the harbour and the fortifications, and the administration of justice, and its decisions are binding on all the inhabitants of the city.

The governance of the council is based on the principles of merit and representation, and the officials are chosen for their ability and their integrity rather than for their birth or their wealth. The council is the primary political institution of the city, and its effectiveness and its fairness are the foundation of the prosperity and the stability of Khalisar. The council's governance is a model of the kind of political order that has sustained the Sahrani city-states for many centuries, and its reputation for competence and integrity is recognised throughout the known world.

## The City and the Trade

The trade that flows through the harbour of [Khalisar](/content/khalisar) is the greatest of any Sahrani city, and the council's regulation of this trade is the primary function of its governance. The council oversees the collection of the tolls and the taxes on the trade, the regulation of the merchants and the shipping, and the maintenance of the harbour and the warehouses that are essential to the commerce of the city. The wealth that accumulates in the treasury of the city is used to fund the construction of public works, the maintenance of the trade routes, and the support of the learning and the arts that are the pride of the city.

The council's influence extends beyond the walls of the city, for Khalisar is the leading member of the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast), the alliance of the Sahrani coastal city-states, and the council's decisions shape the policy and the direction of the league. The council's leadership in the league is based on the wealth and the influence of the city, and the other members of the league look to Khalisar for direction and for support.

## The People and the Defence

The population of Khalisar is the most diverse of any Sahrani city, and the council's governance reflects this diversity, for the officials and the elders are drawn from the various communities that constitute the population of the city. [Humans](/content/humans) are the majority, but [dwarves](/content/dwarves), [elves](/content/elves), and the peoples of the southern regions are present in significant numbers, and the council's representation of these communities is a valued feature of its governance. The defence of the city is maintained by a standing force that is funded by the treasury, and the fortifications of Khalisar are maintained in good order.

## Significance

The Council of Khalisar is the most powerful political entity in Sahrana, and its governance of the greatest city-state on the northern coast is a critical component of the economy and the culture of the continent. The council's effective and fair governance is a model of the political order that has sustained the Sahrani city-states for many centuries, and its leadership in the League of the Northern Coast is a significant force in the politics of the coast. The significance of the Council of Khalisar lies in its competence and its integrity, and the council is a symbol of the civic tradition and the commercial enterprise that are the hallmarks of the Sahrani civilisation.
