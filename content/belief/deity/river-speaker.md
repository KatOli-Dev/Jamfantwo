## Overview

River Speaker is the fourth of the greater spirits of the [Sky Walkers' Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/sky-walkers-way), the spirit of the rivers that thread the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) from the foothills of the mountain spine to the broad valleys draining toward the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). River Speaker is the moving water of the long rivers, the brown silt that the current carries in the season of the rains, the deep pools where the fish gather in summer, and the fords where the pastoralists cross from one grazing ground to another. The kindreds whose grounds lie along the great rivers invoke River Speaker at the flood and at the drought, at the crossing of the fords, and at the reading of the water. The figure is older than the first ferry and the first fisher's weir, and the [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) of the river kindreds have honoured the figure since before the first formal confederation of the kindreds beneath the open sky.

River Speaker is patient and observant, and the rites attending the figure are marked by the same steady quality as the flow of the long rivers across the grasslands. The figure is invoked at the morning of the first crossing of the season, at the evening of the last fishing of the year, at the rising of the water in spring, and at the falling of the water in autumn. Pastoralists ask for clear water, for a steady flow, and for wisdom to read the signs of the river. The figure is honoured by every act of stewardship of the long rivers, and the enduring productivity of the fisheries and fords of the grasslands is the clearest evidence of the figure's favour.

## The Figure and Her Domain

River Speaker is conceived as the living presence of the moving water. Her domain extends from the small brooks gathering in the foothills of the western mountains to the broad rivers the grasslands send eastward to the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). She is the spring snowmelt that swells the upper tributaries, the brown water of the season of the rains, the deep green of the summer pools, and the long slow current of autumn when the water has returned to its ordinary depth. The figure is not a person in the human sense but a presence, invoked by reading the water and managing the fords as much as by formal rite.

The principal dwelling of River Speaker is the long rivers themselves, the broad ribbons of water crossing the grasslands from one horizon to the other. She is invoked at customary fords, marked places where the pastoralists cross from one grazing ground to another. The fords are not built upon in the manner of bridges; they are natural shallow places, and their tending is a responsibility shared among the kindreds of both banks.

The figure is also invoked in the songs of the ferrymen, who work the small boats carrying pastoralists and goods across the deeper reaches. The songs are passed from parent to child, preserving the names of the spirits of the pools and reaches.

## Festival and Observances

The festival of River Speaker falls at the height of the spring runoff, when the rivers are at their fullest and the fish are running upstream to spawn. The rites are conducted at the principal ford of each river by the elder of the kindred, who speaks the traditional words asking River Speaker to carry the flood gently and to grant a fair catch. The spring festival is observed with great solemnity in every river kindred.

The rite of the first crossing is conducted at the opening of the grazing season, when pastoralists lead their herds across the fords to spring pasture. The elder asks River Speaker to watch over the crossing and grant that the water remain low enough for the herds to pass safely. The rite of the last crossing follows at the close of autumn, when the herds are brought back to winter shelter. These two rites form the pivot of the pastoral year along the rivers.

A distinctive observance is the reading of the water, conducted by watchkeepers of the kindred at the gathering of the spring floods. Watchkeepers observe the conditions of the rivers and weather, a form of devotion practised by every household of the river kindreds. They have developed a refined understanding of currents and floods, and their predictions are relied upon by every pastoralist and fisher who works upon the long waters.

## The Order of the Greater Spirits

River Speaker is the fourth of the greater spirits of the [Sky Walkers' Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/sky-walkers-way), her position being the supplementing of the open country. [Sky Walker](/content/belief/deity/sky-walker) is the first, spirit of the open heavens and the weather. [Pasara](/content/belief/deity/pasara) is the second, spirit of the grazing beasts and the fertility of the herds. [Stone Watcher](/content/belief/deity/stone-watcher) is the third, spirit of the standing stones and the memory of the ancestors. River Speaker is the fourth, spirit of the rivers that cross the grasslands. The four are acknowledged collectively at seasonal gatherings, but each has her own day of particular observance. The order is fixed by the pastoral cycle, and River Speaker is honoured last because the rivers are the last of the four gifts the spirits bestow.

Beneath the four greater spirits are a multitude of lesser powers, many local to a single reach, pool, or ford. To know the name of the spirit of a place is to hold a claim upon its favour, and elders of a kindred are charged with remembering these names and teaching them to the young.

## The Centaur Kindreds of the Rivers

The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) are the principal adherents of River Speaker, their observance adapted to the life of the river kindreds. Centaur kindreds of the rivers move along the banks in a seasonal cycle mirroring the rise and fall of the water, camping at customary fords and pools where fishing is best.

Centaur songs of the river are among the most refined of the pastoral literature, preserving the names of reaches, patterns of currents, and the turning of fishing seasons in a form carried between kindreds by wandering singers. Centaurs are the first of the river peoples to read the flood and the last to leave the bank when the water rises. The relationship between centaurs and human pastoralists is one of quiet cooperation, and centaur songs are learned by human fishers and adapted to local grounds.

The figure is also honoured in the practice of fishing weirs, maintained at customary reaches where fish gather in the season of the run. The weirs are constructed of stakes and woven withes, and the fish are taken in baskets set at the openings. Weir-fishing is regulated by customary law setting the seasons, methods, and proportion of the catch that must be returned to the water.

## The Fords and the Crossings

The fords of the long rivers are the principal points of exchange between kindreds of the two banks, and the crossings are sacred to the observance of River Speaker. The fords are natural shallow places where the river may be crossed without a structure. Custom requires that the ford be maintained by the common labour of communities on both banks. The maintenance of crossings is held to be a religious obligation taking precedence over every private claim.

The fords of the grasslands are particularly important during seasonal movements of herds, when pastoralists must cross from one grazing ground to another. The timing of crossing is determined by the reading of the water that the watchkeepers provide. Pastoralists speak the customary words of acknowledgement as they enter the water.

The figure is also honoured in the observation of the long rivers, which elders use to read the rise and fall of the water and the approaching of the seasons. The rivers change with the season, from clear low water of late summer to brown flood of the spring runoff. Elders have developed customary knowledge governing the reading of the rivers and timing of crossings.

## Significance

River Speaker is the fourth of the greater spirits of the [Sky Walkers' Way](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/sky-walkers-way), and the figure's observance is the foundation of the river life of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The reverence for the long rivers that thread the grasslands, the formalisation of the stewardship of the fords and fisheries through religious obligation, and the integration of the household observance into the everyday practice of the pastoral year have contributed to the enduring productivity of the river kindreds. River Speaker is honoured not by a ruling class or a priesthood but by the steady labour of the ferrymen and watchkeepers who depend upon the rivers for their livelihood, whose continuation depends upon the rivers remaining patient.
