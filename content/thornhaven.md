## Overview

Thornhaven is an eastern trade city of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the mouth of a great river that descends from the mountain spine and crosses the eastern lowlands before reaching the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The city is a major node in the trade network of the eastern coast, for its river connects the coast to the interior highlands and the mountain passes, and the goods that travel its waters pass through Thornhaven on their way to and from the sea. The city is smaller than [Eldreth](/content/eldreth) or [Vaelmouth](/content/vaelmouth), but its position at the junction of river and mountain gives it a strategic importance that belies its size.

## The River and the Passes

The river that serves Thornhaven is one of the principal waterways of eastern Aethelar, navigable for a considerable distance inland, and its valley forms the most direct route from the coast to the mountain passes that connect the eastern and western halves of the continent. The trade that flows through the river valley is diverse, including the metalwork and stone of the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) settlements in the mountains, the wool and leather of the upland pastures, and the grain and timber of the lowland valleys. These goods are gathered at Thornhaven, where they are loaded onto seagoing vessels for transport to the markets of the coast and the wider world.

The mountain passes that lie at the head of the river valley are among the most important routes of communication in Aethelar, and the control of these passes has long been a source of contention between the powers that border them. Thornhaven serves as the eastern terminus of the overland trade that crosses the passes, and the city's merchants are skilled in the management of the caravans that make the journey, organising the transport, the provisioning, and the exchange of goods between the two coasts.

## The Harbour and the City

The harbour of Thornhaven is a broad, open roadstead that is sheltered by a chain of small islands that lie offshore, and the approach to the harbour is marked by lighthouses that are among the oldest on the eastern coast. The waterfront is lined with the warehouses and offices of the trading houses that manage the commerce of the city, and the quays are busy with the loading and unloading of the vessels that call at the port.

The city itself is built on a series of low hills that rise above the river mouth, and the older quarters are fortified by a wall that dates from an earlier age of conflict. The streets are steep and winding in the older parts, and broader and more regular in the newer districts that have grown up along the river and the harbour. The population of Thornhaven is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) who are involved in the trade of metalwork and the management of the mountain routes.

## Significance

Thornhaven is the primary gateway between the eastern coast and the interior of Aethelar, and its trade connects the coast to the mountain passes and the western shore beyond. The goods that flow through the city are the foundation of its prosperity, and the merchants of Thornhaven are among the most influential in the trade network of the continent. The city is a member of the [Thessaran League](/content/thessaran-league), and its position in the league is strengthened by its control of the river and the passes, which are the most valuable assets of the city.
