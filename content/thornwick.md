## Overview

Thornwick is a northern city of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated in one of the broad river valleys that thread between the ancient forests of the north. The city is among the oldest [human](/content/humans) settlements in Aethelar, and its foundations date to the earliest period of human expansion into the temperate regions of the continent. The river that flows past its walls is one of the principal waterways of the north, and the valley that surrounds it is among the most fertile regions in Aethelar, with rich alluvial soils that have sustained agriculture for centuries. Thornwick is a quiet, prosperous city, proud of its age and its heritage, and its long history is visible in every street and wall.

## The River Valley

The river valley that surrounds Thornwick is broad and well-watered, and the alluvial soils that line its banks are among the most productive in the continent. The agriculture of the valley is the foundation of the city's prosperity, and the fields that stretch out from the city walls produce grain, vegetables, and the fodder that sustains the livestock of the region. The river itself is navigable for much of its length, and the trade that flows along its waters connects Thornwick to the other settlements of the valley and to the markets of the coast.

The age of the settlement is reflected in the landscape, for the land around Thornwick has been cultivated for so long that the pattern of the fields, the hedgerows, and the lanes is indistinguishable from the natural contours of the valley. The oldest farms are those closest to the city, and their boundaries have been fixed for centuries, while the newer clearings are further out, where the forest once stood and has been gradually pushed back by the expansion of the cultivated land.

The river that flows past Thornwick is one of the principal waterways of the north, and its course through the valley is broad and gentle, well-suited to the barges that carry the agricultural produce of the region to the markets of the coast and the south. The river is also a source of fish, and the eels that are caught in the weirs below the city are a valued delicacy that is exported to the markets of the south. The watermills that line the riverbank are among the oldest in Aethelar, and the flour that is ground in the mills of Thornwick is noted for its quality and its consistency.

## The City

Thornwick is a well-fortified city, and its walls are among the oldest and the thickest in Aethelar, built of the stone that is quarried from the hills above the valley and reinforced many times over the centuries. The streets within the walls are narrow and winding, for the city was built in an age when space was limited and defence was the primary concern, and the buildings are close-set and ancient, their foundations dating to the earliest period of the settlement. The newer districts, built outside the walls during the long periods of peace, are broader and more regular, and the contrast between the old city and the new reflects the changing fortunes of Thornwick over its long history.

The city is the seat of the administration of the [Northern Marches](/content/northern-marches), and the public buildings, the barracks, and the courthouse are situated in the oldest quarter, where the streets are widest and the buildings most substantial. The market square of Thornwick is one of the largest in the north, and the market that is held there each week is a gathering point for the farmers, the traders, and the craftsmen of the valley.

The walls of Thornwick are a notable feature of the city, for they are among the oldest and the thickest fortifications in Aethelar, and the stones that are used in their construction are quarried from the hills above the valley. The walls have been reinforced and extended many times over the centuries, and the layers of construction are visible in the different colours and the different styles of the stonework. The gates of the city are four in number, one for each of the cardinal directions, and the gates are surmounted by the guard towers that are the most prominent features of the walls. The northern gate is the oldest, and its arch is decorated with the carved figures that are the work of the earliest stonemasons of the city.

## History and the Northern Marches

The founding of Thornwick is dated to the earliest period of human expansion into the temperate regions of Aethelar, and the city is regarded as the oldest human settlement in the northern river valleys. The early settlement was a fortified camp that was established on the banks of the river, and the camp grew into a permanent settlement as the fertility of the valley became apparent and the population of the region increased. The construction of the first walls was undertaken in the year 142, and the walls have been the defining feature of the city ever since.

The role of Thornwick as the administrative centre of the [Northern Marches](/content/northern-marches) was established in the year 287, when the council of the marches was formed and the courthouse was built in the oldest quarter of the city. The council is responsible for the governance of the northern region, and the administration of the marches is conducted from the public buildings that are situated around the courthouse. The barracks of the garrison are the most substantial of the public buildings, and the garrison of Thornwick is the largest military force in the northern interior.

## Trade and Significance

The trade of Thornwick is dominated by the agricultural produce of the valley, and the grain, the livestock, and the manufactured goods of the city are exported to the markets of the south and the coast. The river is the primary route of trade, and the barges that carry the goods of Thornwick downriver are a common sight on the waterway. The city is also a centre for the crafts that serve the agricultural economy, and the smiths, the wheelwrights, and the carpenters of Thornwick are noted for their sturdy, well-made products. The leather workers of the city are also noted for their skill, and the harness and the saddles that are produced in the workshops of Thornwick are valued by the farmers and the herders of the valley.

## Culture and the Heritage

The culture of Thornwick is shaped by the age of the city and the pride that the inhabitants take in their heritage, and the community is noted for its respect for tradition and its attachment to the old ways. The festivals of Thornwick are the oldest in the north, and the most important is the harvest festival, which is held in the market square at the end of the growing season and which includes the procession of the guilds, the feasting, and the contests of skill that are a tradition of the city. The procession of the guilds is the most distinctive feature of the festival, for each guild marches in the order of its founding, and the banners and the regalia that are carried in the procession are among the oldest and the most valued possessions of the community.

The stonemasons of Thornwick are the most respected craftsmen in the city, for the maintenance of the walls and the public buildings is a continuous and a demanding task, and the skills of the masons are passed down from generation to generation. The masons' guild is the oldest guild in the city, and its members are responsible for the construction and the repair of the fortifications, the public buildings, and the bridges that cross the river. The guild hall of the masons is situated in the oldest quarter of the city, and the hall is a notable building of stone that is decorated with the carved figures that are the hallmark of the Thornwick tradition.

Thornwick is significant as the oldest human settlement in the northern river valleys and the administrative centre of the [Northern Marches](/content/northern-marches). Its long history and its position in the most fertile valley of the north give it an importance that is cultural as well as economic, and the city is regarded throughout Aethelar as a symbol of the endurance and the continuity of human settlement on the continent.
