## Overview

The Auric Ocean lies to the east of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and separates it from [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). It is the warmer of the two oceans, its equatorial currents giving rise to spirited storms during the summer months. Despite these seasonal challenges, the Auric Ocean carries the heaviest maritime traffic in the known world, as merchant vessels ply the routes between Aethelar and Sahrana. The ocean takes its name from the golden sunsets visible along its western shores, a phenomenon well recorded in the chronicles of coastal peoples and celebrated in the poetry and song of every nation that borders its waters.

## Currents and Navigation

The prevailing currents of the Auric flow from south to north along the eastern coast of Aethelar, carrying warm water into the temperate latitudes and giving the coastal regions a mild and agreeable climate. These same currents make the return voyage from Sahrana a relatively swift passage, and the calmer months outside the storm season are favoured by merchants and pilgrims alike. The northward current is broad and steady, a river within the sea that experienced mariners have learned to use to great advantage, shaving weeks from the journey north. The southward return, against the current, requires patience and a knowledge of the counter-currents that run nearer the coast, secrets closely guarded by the captains who sail the route regularly.

Mariners who sail the Auric have developed sophisticated methods for reading the weather, observing the colour of the water and the behaviour of seabirds to anticipate changing conditions. The deep blue of the open ocean gives way to greenish hues where the current shoals, and the appearance of certain seabirds signals the approach of weather that no cloud formation can foretell. Storms in the Auric are spirited but generally brief, building rapidly in the heat of the afternoon and dissipating by morning, though the great storms of midsummer can last for days and are respected by even the most seasoned captains.

## Trade and Significance

The Auric Ocean is the principal highway of trade between the northern and southern continents. The goods of Sahrana, including rare spices, dyes, and precious stones, travel northward across its waters to the markets of Aethelar, while the products of the northern continent, including grain, cloth, iron, and crafted goods, flow south in return. The volume of this trade has grown steadily over the centuries, and the ports along its shores are among the wealthiest and busiest in the known world. The nations that border the Auric have long invested in the construction of harbours, lighthouses, and the training of skilled mariners, and the competition between them is conducted chiefly through commerce rather than conflict.

The [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), which connects the Auric Ocean to the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea), is a chokepoint of immense commercial value. Goods bound for [Morvind](/content/morvind) must pass through this narrow channel, and the tolls levied upon the traffic provide a steady income to the settlements on either shore. The alternative route, around the southern coast of Sahrana and through the open ocean, is far longer and far more dangerous, and only the most desperate or daring captains have attempted it.

## Islands and Shores

The Auric Ocean is dotted with islands, particularly in its northern and central reaches, where volcanic activity has created chains of rugged, fertile land that rise sharply from the deep water. These islands are inhabited by fishing and trading communities, and several have developed distinct cultures shaped by their isolation and their position astride the trade routes. The islanders are noted for their seamanship and their boatbuilding, and their vessels are a common sight in the ports of both Aethelar and Sahrana. Some of the larger islands support inland populations that have no contact with the sea, living instead in the mountainous interiors where they cultivate terraced fields and maintain traditions that predate the arrival of the trading peoples.

The eastern shore of Aethelar and the northern coast of Sahrana are the two great maritime regions of the Auric, and their ports are the primary destinations for the ocean's trade. The Aethelaran shore is the more heavily developed, with a chain of port cities stretching from the northeastern tip of the continent to its southern coast, each serving as a node in the network of maritime commerce. The Sahrani coast is less densely settled but no less wealthy, as the city-states of the north draw their prosperity from the trade that crosses the ocean and the caravans that venture into the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh).

## Peoples

The coastal waters of the Auric Ocean are home to [merfolk](/content/merfolk), who inhabit the shallow seas and shorelines around all three continents. The merfolk of the Auric are the most colourful and most numerous of their kind, reflecting the warmth and richness of the waters they inhabit. Fishermen and sailors of the coastal towns are familiar with the merfolk, and a cautious but generally peaceful coexistence has developed between the two peoples where their activities overlap. The merfolk are known to warn sailors of approaching storms, a courtesy that has fostered a measure of goodwill between the peoples of the sea and those who travel upon it.
