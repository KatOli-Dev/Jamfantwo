## The Council at the Oasis

The Sands' Assembly is the great gathering of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), the meeting of the council of elders held at a designated oasis at a time determined by the movement of the tribes and the condition of the water sources. The Assembly is the most important event in the governance of the confederation, attended by the elders of every kindred and family within reach of the gathering place. It is regarded by the tribes as the central observance of the political year and is the source of the unity of the confederation that extends across the [Sahrani Wastes](/content/location/settlement/region/sahrani-wastes).

The Assembly is held at an oasis designated by the council of elders drawn from the leaders of the individual kindreds and families. The designation is made after consultation with the elders of the several kindreds and after a careful reading of the condition of the water sources and the movement of the tribes. The Assembly is held at a time when the tribes can gather without endangering their herds or their families, and the timing measures the tribes' care for the desert and its ways.

## The First Meetings of the Desert Peoples

The Sands' Assembly traces its foundation to the early generations of the nomadic peoples of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), when the kindreds of the desert first recognised that the common challenges of the desert required a common response. The early kindreds met at the oases the desert provided, and the meetings were the means by which the kindreds regulated the use of the water sources and the passage of the caravans. These meetings were refined over generations into the tradition observed in the present age, preserved by the elders and the storytellers who are the guardians of the oral histories of the tribes.

The first Assembly was a small gathering of the elders of the principal kindreds of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), held at an oasis the elders had agreed upon by common consent. The first Assembly was conducted without ceremony, and the elders discussed the affairs of the kindreds in the customary order. The successors of this first community refined the Assembly into the tradition observed in the present age, and the tradition is the source of the unity of the confederation. The Assembly has been held without interruption through every generation since the first gathering, and its continuity measures the durability of the institutions of the tribes.

## Choosing and Preparing the Meeting Place

The designated oasis is one the tribes have agreed upon by common consent, and the agreement is entered in the oral traditions of the tribes. The Assembly is held at the oasis for a period of several days, and the tribes encamp around the oasis in the customary order of the kindreds. The oasis is maintained by the elders of the kindred that hosts the Assembly, and the maintenance is regarded by the tribes as a privilege rather than a duty. The oasis is provided with the water and shade the Assembly requires, and the host kindred is responsible for the well-being of the elders and families attending. The hosting of the Assembly is one of the most important customary obligations of the kindreds, measuring the tribes' care for the confederation.

## The Leaders' Deliberations and Decisions

The Assembly convenes the council of elders of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), composed of the leaders of the individual kindreds and families. The decisions of the council are made by consensus, and the governance of the confederation is based on the accumulated wisdom of the elders and the deep knowledge of the desert that is the heritage of the nomadic peoples. The council co-ordinates the management of the grazing lands and the water sources, the regulation of the caravan routes, and the defence of the desert and its peoples.

The council meets at the Assembly for a period of several days, the meetings conducted in the customary order of the kindreds. The council opens with a recitation of the oral traditions of the tribes, regarded by the elders as the most important part of the meeting. The council then considers the affairs of the confederation and resolves any disputes that have arisen between the kindreds in the months preceding the Assembly. The council also plans the management of the desert for the coming season, and these plans are the source of the tribes' care for the water sources and the grazing lands.

## Resolving Conflicts over Water and Grazing

The Assembly is the occasion at which disputes between the kindreds of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) are resolved, one of the principal functions of the Assembly. Disputes are heard by the council of elders in the customary order. The elders hear the parties and the witnesses before retiring to consider the matter, returning with a judgement recorded in the oral traditions of the tribes. The judgement is binding on all members of the confederation.

Disputes brought before the council include those over water sources, grazing lands, and the passage of caravans. The disputes are heard with great care, and the elders take counsel with one another before rendering a judgement. The judgement is entered in the oral traditions by the storytellers who attend the Assembly, who are the guardians of the record. The disputes are resolved with a fairness that the tribes regard as the measure of the confederation's care for its members.

## Reciting the Legends of the Desert

The Assembly is also the occasion for the recitation of the oral traditions of the Kharesh Spirits, conducted by the elders and storytellers who are the guardians of the legends. The recitation is regarded by the tribes as the second of the principal observances of the Assembly, the source of the tribes' continuity with the desert and its powers. The elders of every kindred gather at the Assembly to confirm the legends and settle such disputes as arise between the accounts of different families.

The recitation is conducted in the customary order of the kindreds, with the elders reciting the legends of the spirits of particular places. The recitation measures the tribes' knowledge of the desert and its ways and is the source of the tribes' care for the water sources and the grazing lands. The legends are entered in the oral traditions by the storytellers, and the recitation is regarded by the tribes as a privilege rather than a duty.

## Conferring with the Vashari Representatives

The Assembly is the occasion at which the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) confer with the representatives of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities that depends upon the desert routes for its commerce. The conference between the tribes and the pact is one of the principal observances of the Assembly, the source of the co-operation that sustains the caravan trade between the coast and the interior. The pact maintains a presence at the Assembly, and the representatives are received by the council of elders in the customary manner.

The conference considers the regulation of the caravan routes, the maintenance of the water sources, and the fees paid to the tribes for the guides that conduct the caravans across the desert. The conference is conducted with great care, and the council and the representatives take counsel with one another before reaching an agreement. The agreement is entered in the oral traditions of the tribes and in the records of the pact, forming the source of the tribes' and the pact's care for the desert trade.

## Planning the Stewardship of the Wastes

The Assembly is the occasion at which the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) plan the management of the [Sahrani Wastes](/content/location/settlement/region/sahrani-wastes) for the coming season. The management of the desert is the most important customary obligation of the confederation, measuring the tribes' stewardship of the most demanding environment in the known world. The council considers the condition of the water sources, the movement of the game, and the shifting of the dunes, reaching a consensus upon the measures the tribes must take.

The management includes the regulation of the grazing lands, the maintenance of the water sources, and the protection of the caravan routes. The tribes are bound by the consensus of the council to observe the measures agreed upon. The management is the source of the tribes' continuity with the desert and measures the confederation's care for the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and its peoples.

## Final Rites and Departure from the Oasis

The closing of the Assembly is the final observance of the gathering, the occasion at which the tribes prepare to depart from the designated oasis and return to the scattered camps of the [Sahrani Wastes](/content/location/settlement/region/sahrani-wastes). The closing is marked by customary ceremonies including the recitation of prayers and songs the tribes have maintained. The closing is also the occasion at which the council of elders makes the final decisions of the Assembly, recorded by the storytellers who have attended the proceedings.

The departure from the oasis is gradual, with encampments departing in the customary order determined by the seniority of the kindreds. The departure is marked by customary farewells and the exchange of gifts between departing kindreds and the kindred that has hosted the Assembly. The closing is a melancholy occasion tempered by the recognition that the Assembly will be renewed the following year, and this renewal is the source of the tribes' continuity with the confederation.

## The Assembly as the Heart of the Confederation

The Sands' Assembly is the great gathering of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) and the central observance of the political year of the confederation. The Assembly includes the convening of the council of elders, the resolution of the disputes of the kindreds, the recitation of the traditions of the Kharesh Spirits, the conference with the representatives of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), and the management of the desert for the coming season. The Assembly has been the source of the unity of the confederation through every generation since the first gathering, and it is held in high esteem by every elder and storyteller of the tribes. The Assembly endures as a ceremony of the desert, affirming that the prosperity of the caravan trade and the stewardship of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) rest upon the patient consultation of the elders and the consent of the peoples who belong to the confederation.
