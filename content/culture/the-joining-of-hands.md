## Overview

The custom is observed in every nation of the temperate zone, though the precise form of the ceremony varies from one people to another, and the chronicler notes that no two communities observe the custom in precisely the same manner. The custom is above all an acknowledgement of the bond between the two persons who are to be married and of the obligations that the bond entails, and the rituals that accompany the ceremony are directed toward the formal recognition of the marriage by the community.
