## Overview

The Sahrani Caravan Feast is the principal culinary tradition of the inland cities and the caravan routes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), a body of practice and custom that has grown up over the centuries around the necessities of the desert trade. The chronicler notes that the feast is one of the most distinctive of the regional cuisines of the known world, and the dishes that have been developed for the occasion are among the most carefully prepared of any in the inland regions.
