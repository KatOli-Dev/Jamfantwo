## Overview

The Plains' High Days are the principal summer festivals of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), observed by the peoples of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) during the weeks when the grass is at its tallest and the herds are in their best condition. The High Days are not a single festival but a cycle of celebrations extending over several weeks, observed at the great camps where the peoples of the plains gather for the summer grazing. The High Days are among the most distinctive of the regional festivals of the known world, expressing the bond between the peoples of the plains and the grasslands that sustain them.

## Origins and Meaning

The origins of the High Days lie in the practical necessities of the pastoral life. Summer is the season when the herds are most numerous and the people have the leisure to devote to celebrations marking the turning of the seasons. The early peoples of the grasslands developed a cycle of festivals to mark the principal events of the pastoral year, refined over generations into the tradition observed in the present age. The customs of the High Days have been adopted by the settled communities of the river valleys bordering the plains, and the festivals are observed in the towns of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) that lie at the edge of the plains.

The High Days begin upon the day the elders of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) have appointed for the gathering. The gathering is the occasion upon which the peoples of the plains come together from the scattered camps of the grasslands, held at a broad meadow that has been grazed to a short turf in the preceding weeks. The site is established as a customary location for the gathering, recognised for its suitability for the assemblies and celebrations the High Days require.

## The Gathering

The gathering is the first principal event of the High Days, attended by the encampments of the principal families and clans of the plains. The encampments are arranged according to the customary order of the clans, determined by seniority and the customary precedence the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) has established. The order is a matter of considerable importance to the clans whose encampments are positioned nearest the centre of the gathering.

The gathering is also the occasion for the formal proceedings of the council of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), meeting in the great tent erected at the centre of the gathering. The council considers matters brought before it by the elders of the clans and makes decisions required by the customary law of the Confederation. The council also receives visitors from neighbouring kingdoms, who are received with the customary hospitality of the plains. The gathering is a political as well as a social occasion, and the proceedings of the council are among the most important political events of the plains year.

## The Horse Fair

The horse fair is the first principal celebration of the High Days, held upon the first day in a broad field set aside for the purpose. The peoples of the plains exchange the horses that are the foundation of the pastoral economy. Horses are brought by the breeders of the plains and examined by buyers from the settled communities of the river valleys. The examination includes inspection of the teeth and hooves and testing of the wind, conducted with a care learned from long experience of the trade.

The horse fair is also an occasion for customary contests demonstrating the qualities of the horses offered for sale — races and displays of horsemanship conducted in open ground and judged by appointed elders of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation). Betting is permitted according to the customary forms the plains have established. The horse fair is one of the most important commercial events of the plains year, and the horses that change hands at the fair are the foundation of the cavalry of the neighbouring kingdoms.

## The Hunt of the Thunder Boar

The hunt of the thunder boar is the second principal celebration, the occasion upon which the mounted warriors of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) demonstrate their prowess in the chase. The hunt is conducted on a day appointed by the elders in the grasslands surrounding the gathering. Senior warriors of the principal clans lead the hunt, accompanied by beaters who drive the boar from the coverts toward the riders. The hunt is a ceremonial pursuit conducted according to customary forms, having acquired a significance independent of the practical purpose of the chase.

The warrior who delivers the final stroke is honoured by the assembly with the title of the hunt. The meat of the boar is distributed according to customary order, and the feast that follows is one of the principal celebrations of the High Days, held in the great tent at the centre of the gathering. The feast is also the occasion for the recitation of customary songs celebrating the prowess of the hunters.

## The Wedding Moot

The wedding moot is the third principal celebration, the occasion upon which the marriages arranged in the months preceding the gathering are solemnised in the presence of the assembly. The moot is conducted on a day appointed by the elders of the clans whose members are to be married, typically the day following the hunt. Marriages are solemnised according to the customs of [Hands Joined](/content/culture/hands-joined), and the ceremonies are conducted at the gathering place of the clans, attended by the families and the wider community.

The wedding moot is also the occasion for customary gifts presented by the clans to the newly married couples — livestock and household goods required for the establishment of new households. The gifts are presented in a formal ceremony conducted by the elders. The feast that follows is one of the principal social occasions of the plains year. The wedding moot measures the importance the plains attach to the formation of new households and is one of the principal expressions of the continuity of the pastoral life.

## The Closing

The closing of the High Days is the final event of the cycle, the occasion upon which the peoples of the plains prepare to depart from the gathering and return to the scattered camps of the grasslands. The closing is marked by customary ceremonies established by the elders of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), including the recitation of customary prayers and songs. The closing is also the occasion upon which the councils of the clans make the final decisions of the gathering, recorded by the chroniclers who have attended the proceedings.

The departure from the gathering is gradual, with encampments departing in the customary order determined by seniority. The departure is marked by customary farewells and the exchange of gifts between departing clans and those that remain. The closing is a melancholy occasion tempered by the recognition that the gathering will be renewed the following year.

## Significance

The Plains' High Days are the principal summer festivals of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), among the most distinctive of the regional celebrations of the known world. The High Days express the bond between the peoples of the plains and the grasslands that sustain them, and the celebrations measure the prosperity the pastoral life has brought to the peoples of the central lowlands. The High Days hold both civic and religious significance, recognised by the authorities of the Confederation, and the celebrations are one of the customary acts of the community at the height of the pastoral year. The High Days endure as a celebration of the grasslands, affirming that the pastoral life is the foundation of the culture of the plains.
