## The Sacred Bond

The custom of guest right is among the most ancient and the most sacred of the institutions that the peoples of the known world maintain. Guest right is the formal recognition of the bond that is created when a traveller is received into the household of a host, a bond that imposes obligations upon both parties and that is enforceable under the customary law of every community. The guest right is the foundation of the travel and the trade that connect the peoples of the different lands, and the violation of the guest right is regarded everywhere as one of the most serious offences that a person can commit.

The guest right is established by the formal acceptance of the hospitality of the host. The traveller who comes to the door and is admitted into the household is entitled to the protection of the host for the duration of the visit, and the host is entitled to the respect of the guest and the observance of the customs of the household. The guest right is not a matter of choice or of personal inclination; it is a binding obligation that the customary law of every community enforces.

## The Three Days and the Three Gifts

The customary law of the temperate lands specifies the duration of the guest right as three days and three nights, the period that is considered sufficient for the traveller to rest and to conduct the business that has brought the traveller to the community. The host is required to provide the guest with food and shelter for the period of the guest right, and the guest is required to accept the hospitality of the host and to depart at the conclusion of the period.

The guest right is established by the exchange of the traditional gifts that symbolise the bond between the host and the guest. The host offers the guest the customary cup of water or ale, the gift that signifies the host's acceptance of the guest into the household. The guest offers the host the customary gift of the traveller, a token of the guest's gratitude and respect. The exchange of the gifts is the formal moment of the establishment of the guest right, and the bond that is created by the exchange is binding upon both parties.

## The Guest Right of the Vael Valley

The guest right of the Vael river valley is governed by the teachings of the [Still Flame](/content/religion/still-flame), which require the faithful to welcome the stranger as the stranger would welcome the flame itself. The guest right is enforced by the [Vael Parish Councils](/content/government/local/vael-parish-councils), which have the authority to impose penalties for the violation of the guest right.

The host who violates the guest right by failing to provide the required hospitality or by causing harm to the guest is subject to the penalties of the customary law. The penalties include the payment of compensation to the guest, the exclusion of the host from the community, and, in the most serious cases, the exile of the host from the valley. The guest who violates the guest right by abusing the hospitality of the host or by causing harm to the host or to the property of the host is subject to the same penalties.

## The Guest Right of the Plains

The guest right of the [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) and the human herders of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) is adapted to the conditions of the nomadic life, in which the traveller is a common figure and the provision of hospitality is a matter of survival. The guest right of the plains is established by the sharing of the milk or the water at the fire of the camp, the traditional gesture of welcome.

The guest right of the plains is enforced by the elders of the tribe, who have the authority to impose the penalties for the violation of the custom. The violator of the guest right is subject to the exclusion from the protection of the tribe, which is the most serious penalty in the nomadic culture. The violator who is excluded from the tribe is left to the mercy of the elements and the predators of the plains, a fate that few survive.

## The Guest Right of the Dwarven Halls

The guest right of the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) is governed by the traditions of the hall, which require that the visitor to the mountain be welcomed with the proper ceremonies and provided with the hospitality that the dwarven tradition requires. The guest right of the dwarves is established by the formal presentation of the visitor to the thane, who speaks the words that accept the visitor into the protection of the hall.

The guest right of the dwarves is enforced by the thane and by the authority of the [Stoneheim Thanes Council](/content/government/local/stoneheim-thanes-council) or the [Karhold Warden Councils](/content/government/local/karhold-realm-warden-councils), according to the hall. The violator of the guest right is subject to the penalties that the thane and the council impose, which may include the payment of compensation, the exclusion from the hall, and the reporting of the violation to the other halls of the dwarven people.

## The Guest Right of the Desert

The guest right of the [Kharesh](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) and the [Vashari](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) desert nomads is governed by the customs of the desert, which regard the guest right as the most sacred of the obligations. The guest right of the desert is established by the sharing of the salt, the traditional symbol of the bond between the host and the guest.

The guest right of the desert is enforced by the elders of the tribe and by the customary law of the [Kharesh Blood Debt](/content/law/kharesh-blood-debt). The violator of the guest right is subject to the blood debt, the obligation that the kin of the violated party are entitled to seek from the kin of the violator. The blood debt is the most serious of the penalties in the desert culture, and the fear of the blood debt is the principal guarantee of the observance of the guest right.

## The Guest Right of the Sahrani Coast

The guest right of the [Sahrani city-states](/content/culture/sahrani-city-life) is governed by the laws of the salt, the traditional symbol of the commercial bond that connects the merchants of the coast. The guest right of the Sahrani coast is established by the offering of the coffee and the dates, the traditional refreshment of the Sahrani hospitality.

The guest right of the Sahrani coast is enforced by the [Sahrani Salt Court](/content/government/local/sahrani-salt-court), which has the authority to impose the penalties for the violation of the custom. The violator of the guest right is subject to the exclusion from the trade of the city, which is the most serious penalty in the mercantile culture of the coast. The violator who is excluded from the trade is unable to conduct the business that is the foundation of the wealth and the status in the Sahrani society.

## The Universal Bond

The guest right is the universal bond that connects the peoples of the known world, the institution that makes the travel and the trade between the communities possible. The guest right is the recognition of the common humanity of the host and the guest, the acknowledgement that the stranger who comes to the door is entitled to the same consideration as the member of the community. The guest right is enforced by the customary law of every people, and the violation of the guest right is punished by the penalties that each culture has developed for the protection of the sacred bond.
