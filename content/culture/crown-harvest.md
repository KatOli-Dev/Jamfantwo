## The Late-Summer Convocation

The Crown Harvest is the annual late-summer ceremony of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) at which the laws of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) are recited and grain from each district is offered as a token of common inheritance. It is a multi-day cycle of observances held at the old coronation hall and the principal granaries of the surrounding districts. The hall is where Sera first took the crown and where her water ordinances were first recorded. The Crown Harvest is regarded as the central observance of the agricultural year by every household of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) that works the soil of the vale. The timing of the ceremony — when the grain has been safely gathered and the granaries filled — reflects the vale's dependence upon the harvest.

## Founding under Sera's Reign

The Crown Harvest traces its foundation to the early reign of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale), who united the warring principalities of the central river valleys and founded the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael). She instituted the ceremony to bind the conquered principalities to the new kingdom's laws and institutions, and she presided over the first Crown Harvest in person at the coronation hall that had been erected for the occasion. The ceremony was designed to remind the peoples of the vale that their inheritance was held in common, and it has been observed without interruption since that first gathering, maintained by the governors and local councils of the vale through every season of peace and conflict the kingdom has known. The refinement of the ceremony over generations into its present form is attributed to the consistent stewardship of these local authorities, who have preserved the observances without the direct involvement of the crown.

## The Hall of Records and Assembly

The old coronation hall stands in the heart of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) and serves as the principal repository of the vale's collective memory. Erected early in [Sera's reign](/content/history/seras-reign), it houses copies of her first water ordinances, the boundary stones of the vale's districts, harvest accounts kept by local councils, and the agreements by which former rivals entered the kingdom. The governors and magistrates of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) consult these records in any dispute touching upon the vale's inheritance. The hall is regarded as the most sacred of the vale's public buildings and is the place to which every household returns at the close of the harvest. It has been maintained by the governors and local councils in every generation since Sera's death.

## The Reading of Sera's Ordinances

The central observance of the Crown Harvest is the recitation of Sera's laws from memory in the great chamber of the coronation hall. The senior governor of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) opens the recitation, and the local councils of each district continue in customary order. Reciters are chosen from among the district elders for their knowledge of the laws. Delegates and householders from every district attend, and the recitation is followed by a brief silence in which the laws are held in mind without being spoken — the elders of the vale regard this silence as the most important part of the ceremony. The recitation is considered the most important public observance of the year, a measure of the vale's continuity with the institutions Sera established.

## The Presentation at the Granaries

The second principal observance is the offering of grain from each district at the principal granaries of the vale. Householders bring grain in customary order as a free-will offering — not a tax — and the offering is regarded as a privilege. The governors and local council elders receive the offering and enter it in the harvest accounts preserved at the coronation hall. These accounts are the most complete records of any district of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and are consulted in any dispute touching upon the harvest. The offering concludes with a feast at the great granary attended by delegates and householders.

## The District Accountings

The third principal observance is the delivery of district reports in the great chamber of the coronation hall. The local councils report on the condition of bridges, granaries, and channels for the past year. These reports are the means by which the vale maintains the water law instituted by [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale). Disputes that resisted resolution in the villages are often concluded during the Crown Harvest, when precedent can be checked against the hall's records. The reports are followed by discussion in which governors and delegates plan maintenance works for the coming year.

## The Kingdom's Relation to the Vale

The Crown Harvest is a ceremony of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) specifically, not observed by the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) at large, though senior magistrates attend as a mark of the kingdom's respect for the heartland from which it was founded. Ambassadors of neighbouring kingdoms also attend. They are shown the records and harvest accounts of the vale and are expected to observe without comment or interference.

## Songs and Remembrance of the Founder

The valley regards the Crown Harvest as the principal living legacy of [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale). Her name is invoked at every ceremony and entered in the hall's records beside those of the governors and elders who have served since her death. Customary songs recounting Sera's life and reign are sung at the Crown Harvest feasts. These songs are taught to children at the household hearth and sung in the presence of the governors and delegates at the harvest gatherings.

## The Ceremony's Place in the Year

The Crown Harvest is the greatest annual gathering of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) and the central observance of the agricultural year of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael). It encompasses the recitation of Sera's laws, the offering of grain, the reports of the districts, and the feasts that follow. The continuity of the ceremony — unbroken since Sera's reign — reflects the durability of the institutions she established. The Crown Harvest is held in high esteem by every household that draws its living from the soil of the vale. It endures as a reminder that the prosperity of the kingdom rests upon the cultivation of the land and the law that Sera instituted.
