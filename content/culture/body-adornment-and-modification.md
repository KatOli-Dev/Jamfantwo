## The Canvas of the Body

The adornment of the body is a practice observed by every people of the known world, the means by which the individual expresses the identity, the status, the achievements, and the adherence to the customs of the community. The forms of body adornment vary considerably from one culture to another, reflecting the different materials that the environment provides and the values that each culture holds most important. The practice of body adornment is among the most ancient of the human traditions, predating the recorded history and persisting through the changes of the ages.

The principal forms of body adornment in the known world are the modification of the skin through tattooing and scarification, the piercing of the body for the insertion of ornaments, the painting of the body with the pigments of the earth and the plants, and the wearing of the ornaments of the metal and the stone. Each form of adornment carries a meaning that the members of the community understand, and the accumulation of the adornments over the lifetime of the individual records the history of the individual's life.

## The Tattoos of the Plains Peoples

The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) and the human herders of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) practise the art of the tattoo as the principal form of the body adornment. The tattoos of the plains are applied with the needles of the bone and the pigments of the earth and the plants, and the patterns that are inscribed on the skin record the identity and the achievements of the individual.

The tattoos of the centaurs are applied to the flanks and the shoulders, the areas of the body that are most visible in the movement of the running. The patterns of the centaur tattoos indicate the tribe of the individual, the lineage within the tribe, and the achievements of the individual in the hunt and in the war. The tattoos are applied over the course of the individual's life, beginning with the first tattoo at the coming of age ceremony and adding the further tattoos as the individual accumulates the achievements that the culture values.

The tattoos of the human herders are applied to the face and the hands, the areas of the body that are most visible in the daily life of the camp. The patterns of the human tattoos indicate the family of the individual, the status within the family, and the skills that the individual has mastered. The tattoos of the human herders are considered the markers of the adult status, and the individual who has not received the tattoos of the adulthood is regarded as a child regardless of the age.

## The Scarification of the Sahrani Coast

The [Sahrani city-states](/content/culture/sahrani-city-life) practise the art of the scarification as the principal form of the body modification. The scarification of the Sahrani coast is applied with the knives of the metal and the ash of the desert plants, and the patterns that are incised into the skin record the lineage and the status of the individual.

The scarification of the Sahrani merchants is applied to the arms and the chest, the areas that are visible in the conduct of the trade. The patterns of the Sahrani scarification indicate the trading house of the individual, the rank within the house, and the voyages that the individual has completed. The scarification is applied at the coming of age ceremony and is supplemented at the completion of each significant voyage, the new pattern marking the achievement of the individual.

The scarification of the Sahrani women is applied to the face and the hands, the areas that are visible in the domestic life of the household. The patterns of the women's scarification indicate the family of the individual and the status of the marriage. The scarification is applied at the marriage ceremony, and the pattern is considered the mark of the adult woman.

## The Piercings of the Northern Coast

The northern peoples of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) practise the art of the piercing as the principal form of the body modification. The piercings of the northern coast are inserted with the needles of the bone and the ornaments of the metal and the stone, and the patterns of the piercings record the achievements of the individual in the sea and in the war.

The piercings of the northern men are concentrated in the ears and the nose, the areas that are visible in the conduct of the trade and the negotiation. The materials of the piercings indicate the wealth and the status of the individual, the gold and the silver marking the wealth of the successful trader and the bone and the stone marking the skill of the hunter. The piercings are accumulated over the course of the individual's life, each new piercing marking a significant achievement.

The piercings of the northern women are concentrated in the ears and the lips, the areas that are visible in the domestic life of the household. The materials of the women's piercings indicate the status of the family, and the size of the piercings indicates the number of the children that the woman has borne.

## The Painting of the Dwarven Rituals

The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) practise the art of the body painting as the principal form of the adornment for the rituals and the ceremonies. The pigments of the dwarven painting are derived from the minerals of the mountain, the oxides of the iron and the copper that produce the deep colours that the dwarven tradition requires.

The painting of the dwarves is applied to the face and the hands, the areas of the body that are visible in the rituals of the forge and the hall. The patterns of the dwarven painting indicate the lineage of the individual and the craft that the individual practises. The painting is applied for the major ceremonies of the dwarven calendar, including the binding ceremony, the marriage ceremony, and the funeral.

## The Ornaments of Every Culture

The wearing of the ornaments of the metal and the stone is a practice observed by every culture of the known world. The ornaments of the metal include the rings and the bracelets and the necklaces of the precious metals, and the ornaments of the stone include the beads and the pendants of the gems and the minerals. The ornaments are worn by both the men and the women, though the forms and the materials vary according to the culture and the status.

The ornaments of the Vael valley are typically of the silver and the bronze, the metals that the smiths of the valley work. The ornaments of the plains are of the bone and the horn and the leather, the materials that the nomadic life provides. The ornaments of the dwarven people are the masterworks of the [Inlay Art](/content/culture/inlay-art), the works of the metal and the stone that are the pride of the dwarven culture.

## The Meaning of the Adornment

The adornment of the body is the language that the individual speaks about the identity and the status and the achievements of the individual. The modifications that are inscribed on the skin and the ornaments that are worn on the body record the history of the individual's life and the connections of the individual to the community. The customs that govern the body adornment are transmitted from one generation to the next, and the patterns that are applied to the body of the newest member of the community are the same patterns that the ancestors have worn since the time immemorial. The adorned body is the body of the member of the community, marked by the signs of the belonging and the achievements that the community values.
