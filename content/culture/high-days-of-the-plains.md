## Overview

The High Days of the Plains are the principal summer festivals of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), observed by the peoples of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) during the weeks when the grass is at its tallest and the herds are in their best condition. The High Days are not a single festival but a cycle of celebrations that extends over a period of several weeks, and the celebrations are observed at the great camps where the peoples of the plains gather for the summer grazing. The chronicler notes that the High Days are among the most distinctive of the regional festivals of the known world, and the celebrations are an expression of the bond between the peoples of the plains and the grasslands that sustain them.

## Origins and Meaning

The origins of the High Days lie in the practical necessities of the pastoral life, for the summer is the season when the herds are most numerous and the people have the leisure to devote to the celebrations that mark the turning of the seasons. The early peoples of the grasslands developed a cycle of festivals to mark the principal events of the pastoral year, and the festivals were refined over the generations into the tradition that is observed in the present age. The tradition is not confined to the grasslands, for the customs of the High Days have been adopted by the settled communities of the river valleys that border the plains, and the festivals are observed in the towns of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) that lie at the edge of the plains.

The High Days are reckoned to begin upon the day that the elders of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) have appointed for the gathering, and the gathering is the occasion upon which the peoples of the plains come together from the scattered camps of the grasslands. The gathering is held at a place that has been designated for the purpose, and the place is typically a broad meadow that has been grazed to a short turf in the weeks that precede the gathering. The place is established as a customary site for the gathering, and the establishment is a recognition of the suitability of the site for the assemblies and the celebrations that the High Days require.

## Significance

The High Days of the Plains are the principal summer festivals of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and the festivals are among the most distinctive of the regional celebrations of the known world. The High Days are an expression of the bond between the peoples of the plains and the grasslands that sustain them, and the celebrations are a measure of the prosperity that the pastoral life has brought to the peoples of the central lowlands. The chronicler notes that the High Days have acquired a civic as well as a religious significance that is recognised by the authorities of the Confederation, and the celebrations are one of the customary acts of the community at the height of the pastoral year. The High Days endure as a celebration of the grasslands and as a reminder that the pastoral life is the foundation of the culture of the plains.
