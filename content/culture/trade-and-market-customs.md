## The Foundation of Exchange

Trade is one of the fundamental activities of civilised life in the known world, the means by which the products of one region are exchanged for the products of another and the relationships between the peoples of the different lands are maintained. The customs that govern trade and the markets are among the most important of the institutions that the peoples have developed, reflecting the values of the mercantile cultures and the practical requirements of the exchange of goods.

The market is the principal institution of the trade, the place where the buyers and the sellers gather to conduct the exchange. The market is governed by the customary law of the community, which specifies the hours of the market, the quality of the goods that may be offered, and the penalties for the violation of the rules. The market is supervised by the market master, who is appointed by the community to ensure the conduct of the trade according to the law.

## The Markets of the Vael Valley

The markets of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) river valley are held on the appointed days of the week in the principal towns of the valley. The market day is the principal social occasion of the week, when the farmers bring their surplus produce to the town and the townspeople gather to buy and sell. The market is held in the central square of the town, supervised by the market master who is appointed by the [Vael Parish Councils](/content/government/local/vael-parish-councils).

The goods that are offered in the Vaelic markets include the grain, the vegetables, and the fruits that the farms of the valley produce; the dairy products that the households prepare; the meat of the animals that are slaughtered for the market; and the craft goods of the town's artisans. The prices are determined by the negotiation between the buyer and the seller, a process that is governed by the customary rules of the market that require the seller to state a fair price and the buyer to pay without deception.

The Vaelic market is also the place where the debts are settled and the contracts are made. The merchants who trade in the market keep the accounts of the credit and the debit, and the settlement of the accounts is conducted at the appointed intervals of the year. The [Contract Law](/content/law/contract-law) of the valley specifies the terms of the agreements and the penalties for the breach of the contract.

## The Trade of the Plains

The trade of the [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) and the human herders of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) is conducted at the traditional meeting places on the borders of the plains, where the tribes and the merchants of the settled lands assemble at the appointed times. The trade is governed by the customary law of the plains, which specifies the terms of the exchange and the penalties for the violation of the agreements.

The principal exports of the plains are the horses that the centaurs breed, the wool and the hides that the human herders produce, and the dairy products that the households prepare. The principal imports are the grain, the metal tools, the cloth, and the luxury goods that the settled lands provide. The trade is conducted by the barter or the exchange of the goods, though the use of the currency of the settled lands is becoming more common in the present age.

## The Harbour Trade of Aldreth

The harbour trade of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is the principal commercial institution of the eastern coast, the channel through which the goods of the interior reach the wider world and the goods of the distant lands enter the continent of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The harbour trade is governed by the [Aldreth Commercial Code](/content/law/aldreth-commercial-code), which specifies the duties that are payable on the goods that pass through the harbour, the quality standards that the goods must meet, and the penalties for the violation of the trade regulations.

The principal exports of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are the grain of the Vael valley, the timber of the northern forests, the metals and the stone goods of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands), and the craft goods of the city's artisans. The principal imports are the luxury goods of the [southern lands](/content/location/natural/region/southern-lands), the spices and the dyes of the [Sahrani coast](/content/culture/sahrani-city-life), the silk of the distant eastern lands, and the salt and the fish of the northern waters.

## The Caravan Trade of the Desert

The caravan trade of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the [Sahrani Wastes](/content/location/settlement/region/sahrani-wastes) is the principal means by which the goods of the coast reach the interior of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the goods of the interior reach the markets of the coast. The caravans are organised by the merchants of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) city-states, who hire the guides and the guards from the desert tribes to conduct the caravan across the sands.

The trade of the caravan is governed by the [Sahrani Caravan Law](/content/law/sahrani-caravan-law), which specifies the rights and the obligations of the caravan master, the guides, the guards, and the merchants. The caravan law requires the caravan master to ensure the safety of the caravan, the guides to provide accurate knowledge of the routes and the wells, and the guards to protect the caravan from the bandits and the predators of the desert.

## The Guilds and the Standards

The guilds are the principal institutions of the trade in the settled lands, the organisations of the craftsmen and the merchants that regulate the practice of the trades and maintain the quality of the goods. The guilds are chartered by the authorities of the kingdoms, and they operate under the terms of the [Guild Charters](/content/law/guild-charters) that specify the rights and the responsibilities of the members.

The guilds maintain the standards of the quality that the goods must meet, the conditions of the apprenticeship that the craftsmen must serve, and the rules of the conduct that the members must observe. The guilds also maintain the funds for the relief of the members who have fallen into hardship and the schools for the education of the apprentices in the skills of the trade.

## The Currency and the Exchange

The currency of the known world is based on the coinage of the principal kingdoms, the silver and the copper coins that are stamped with the marks of the issuing authority. The most widely accepted coin is the silver crown of Aldreth, which serves as the standard of the value for the trade across the continent. The other kingdoms issue their own coins, and the exchange between the currencies is conducted at the rates that the merchants of the principal markets establish.

The use of the credit and the bill of exchange is common in the larger commercial centres, where the merchants maintain the accounts of the credit and the debit and settle the balances at the appointed intervals. The [Banking and Credit](/content/economy/banking-and-credit) institutions of the known world are concentrated in the principal ports, where the merchants and the bankers have developed the systems of the credit that aid the trade across the distances.

## The Customs of the Commerce

The trade and the market customs of the known world reflect the values of the mercantile cultures and the practical requirements of the exchange of goods. The honesty of the merchant and the fairness of the price are the foundations of the trust that the trade requires, and the customary law of every community enforces the standards of the conduct that the trade demands. The market is the place where the goods of the world are exchanged and the relationships between the peoples are maintained, the institution that connects the villages of the valley to the ports of the coast and the caravans of the desert to the markets of the interior.
