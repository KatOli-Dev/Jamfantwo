## Overview

Fernmoor is a northern settlement of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the boundary where the ancient forests give way to the open grasslands of the central plains. The town stands on a low rise that overlooks the transition from woodland to grass, and its position at the meeting of two kinds of country has made it a natural point of exchange between the forest peoples and the plains peoples. Fernmoor is smaller than the cities of the river valleys, but it is a thriving and well-situated community, and its market is a gathering point for the diverse peoples who inhabit the boundary country.

## The Boundary Country

The land around Fernmoor is a transition zone, where the dense canopy of the ancient forest thins and gives way to the open grass of the central plains. The forest here is not the deep, shadowed woodland of the north but a patchwork of groves and clearings, with the trees growing in clusters that are separated by meadows and grassland. The undergrowth is thick and varied, and the ferns that give the town its name grow in abundance, covering the moister ground in a carpet of green that is among the most distinctive features of the landscape.

The boundary country is rich in game and forage, for the meeting of forest and grassland creates a diversity of habitats that supports a greater variety of plants and animals than either the deep forest or the open plain. The hunting and the gathering that the land provides are a significant part of the economy of Fernmoor, and the game of the boundary country, including deer, boar, and the smaller animals of the woodland edge, is a valued commodity in the markets of the region.

## Trade and the Market

The market of Fernmoor is the primary institution of the town, and it is a meeting point for the peoples of the forest and the plains. The [humans](/content/humans) of the town and the surrounding farms bring grain, vegetables, and the manufactured goods that they produce, and they exchange these for the timber, the game, and the forest products of the woodland peoples and the wool, the leather, and the livestock of the plains peoples. The [elves](/content/elves) of the forest are occasional visitors to the market, and the [centaurs](/content/centaurs) of the grasslands are regular traders, and the market of Fernmoor is one of the few places where the settled, the forest, and the nomadic peoples meet on equal terms.

The town is also a centre for the processing of the ferns and the herbs that grow in the boundary country, and the herbalists and the dyers of Fernmoor are skilled in the use of the plants that are gathered from the woodland edge. The dyes produced from the plants of the boundary country are a minor but valued export, and the natural colours of the Fernmoor dyes are noted for their subtlety and their durability.

## The Town

Fernmoor is a small, open town, and its buildings are a mixture of the timber-frame construction of the forest country and the stone and plaster of the plains. The town is not walled, for the boundary country has been peaceful for many generations, and the streets are broad and open, lined with the gardens and the orchards that the inhabitants tend. The market square is the centre of the town, and it is a broad, open space that can accommodate the stalls, the pens, and the crowds that gather for the weekly market.

## Significance

Fernmoor is the primary point of exchange between the forest peoples and the plains peoples of northern Aethelar, and its market is a vital link in the trade network of the region. The town is small, but its position at the boundary of two kinds of country gives it a commercial and a social significance that belies its size. The coexistence of the settled, the forest, and the nomadic peoples at Fernmoor is a model of the kind of interchange that has shaped the culture and the economy of the boundary country for centuries.
