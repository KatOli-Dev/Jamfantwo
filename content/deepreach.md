## Overview

Deepreach is a [dwarven](/content/dwarves) mining settlement in the central highlands of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated in a region where the mountain spine is rich in mineral deposits but too remote for the establishment of a major city. The settlement is smaller and more specialised than [Karhold](/content/karhold), and its primary function is the extraction and the preliminary processing of the ores that are found in the surrounding mountains. Deepreach is a community of miners and the craftsmen who serve them, and its prosperity is built upon the wealth of the earth and the labour of those who work it.

## The Mines

The mines of Deepreach are the reason for the settlement, and they are the dominant feature of the community. The mine entrances are cut into the sides of the valleys that surround the settlement, and the tunnels that extend from them follow the veins of ore deep into the mountains. The ore that is extracted from the mines of Deepreach includes iron, copper, and the rarer metals that are found in the central highlands, and the deposits are among the richest in the mountain spine outside of Karhold itself.

The mining operations of Deepreach are conducted on a smaller scale than those of Karhold, for the settlement is younger and the workings less extensive, but the miners of Deepreach are skilled and experienced, and the ore that is produced is of a quality that is valued in the markets of the lowlands. The mines are worked by the community, with each family responsible for a particular set of tunnels, and the ore that is extracted is shared according to the contribution of each family to the collective effort.

## The Settlement

Deepreach is a compact, purpose-built settlement, and its architecture is entirely subterranean, in the manner of the dwarven tradition. The dwellings, the workshops, and the storehouses are carved into the rock of the mountain, and the settlement is connected to the mine entrances by a network of tunnels that allow the miners to move between their homes and their workings without surfacing. The great hall of Deepreach is smaller than that of Karhold, but it is the centre of the community, and the forges, the council chambers, and the gathering places are all situated within it.

The population of Deepreach is entirely [dwarven](/content/dwarves), and the community is close-knit and self-sufficient, for the settlement is remote and the contact with the outside world is limited to the trade caravans that call at the settlement to collect the ore and deliver the supplies that the community cannot produce for itself. The caravans travel the mountain roads that connect Deepreach to Karhold and to the lowland markets, and the trade that they carry is the sole link between the settlement and the wider world.

## Significance

Deepreach is the most important mining settlement in the central highlands of Aethelar, and the ore that is extracted from its mines is a significant contribution to the mineral output of the mountain spine. The settlement is a dependency of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/deep-realm-of-karhold), and its ore is processed and distributed through the trade network of the realm. The prosperity of Deepreach is built upon the wealth of the surrounding mountains, and the community is a model of the dwarven capacity to establish productive settlements in the most remote and demanding environments.
