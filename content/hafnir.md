## Overview

Hafnir is a port on the southeastern coast of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated near the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden) where the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) meets the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The city stands on a natural harbour that is one of the best on the southeastern coast, and its position near the strait has made it a significant point in the trade network that connects Morvind to the markets of Aethelar and Sahrana. Hafnir is smaller than [Nordhaven](/content/nordhaven), but its proximity to the strait gives it a commercial importance that is disproportionate to its size.

## The Harbour and the Strait

The harbour of Hafnir is a deep, sheltered bay that is protected from the open sea by an island that lies across the entrance, and the approach is straightforward in most weather, though the currents that run through the nearby strait can affect the passage. The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses that serve the strait trade are situated along the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels that are preparing to enter the strait or that have just passed through it, and the port is a natural staging point for the trade that flows between the two seas.

The proximity of the strait is the defining feature of Hafnir's economy, for the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden) is the only passage between the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and all vessels that travel between the two must pass through these waters. The pilots of Hafnir are among the most experienced in the strait trade, and the town is a base for the pilotage services that guide vessels through the narrow and treacherous channel.

## Trade and the Strait Connection

The trade of Hafnir is dominated by the strait traffic, and the goods that pass through the harbour include the metalwork of the Morvind interior, the grain and cloth of Aethelar, and the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). The town is a centre for the transhipment of goods between the vessels that trade on the Stormfall and those that trade on the Auric, and the warehouses of Hafnir are filled with the goods that are transferred between the two routes. The tolls and the pilotage fees that are collected at Hafnir are a significant source of the town's revenue, and the management of the strait trade is the primary occupation of the merchants and the officials of the town.

The town is also a centre for the repair and the supply of vessels that have passed through the strait, and the shipyards and the chandleries of Hafnir are a significant part of the local economy. The vessels that navigate the strait are often in need of repair after the difficult passage, and the shipwrights of Hafnir are skilled in the rapid and the reliable maintenance that the strait trade demands.

## The City

Hafnir is a well-built, prosperous town, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the strait trade has brought, with substantial buildings of stone, well-paved streets, and a commercial district that is oriented toward the harbour. The population of Hafnir is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) who are involved in the metal trade and the management of the overland routes that connect the port to the interior.

## Significance

Hafnir is the primary Morvind-side port of the strait trade, and its position near the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden) gives it a commercial importance that is disproportionate to its size. The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), the joint governance of the strait towns, and its role in the pilotage and the transhipment of the strait trade is a significant component of the economy of the strait region. The prosperity of Hafnir is built upon the trade that flows through the strait, and the town is a key point in the trade network that connects Morvind to the wider world.
