## Overview

The Holds of Stoneheim is the greatest [dwarven](/content/dwarves) realm in [Morvind](/content/morvind), encompassing the underground cities, the mining settlements, and the mountain territories that are inhabited and governed by the dwarven people of the continent. The realm is the largest and the most powerful dwarven political entity in the known world, and its capital is [Stoneheim](/content/stoneheim), the greatest dwarven hall of Morvind. The Holds of Stoneheim are the foundation of the dwarven civilisation in Morvind, and their mineral wealth and their craftsmanship are the basis of the continent's prosperity and its reputation.

## The Territory and the Holds

The territory of the Holds of Stoneheim extends across the interior ranges of Morvind, encompassing the vast underground networks that are the home of the dwarven people. The realm includes [Stoneheim](/content/stoneheim), the capital and the greatest city, and [Khazgar](/content/khazgar), the city renowned for its metalwork, as well as [Ironhollow](/content/ironhollow), the industrial settlement that is the primary source of the iron that feeds the forges of the realm. The holds are the individual dwarven communities that constitute the realm, and each hold is a self-governing entity that manages its own affairs while contributing to the collective defence and the commerce of the realm.

The [geography](/content/geography) of the interior ranges determines the distribution of the dwarven population, and the holds are situated where the veins of ore and the natural cave systems provide the resources and the space that the dwarven way of life requires. The deepest and the most extensive of the cave systems are those of the central interior, where Stoneheim and Khazgar are situated, and the eastern ranges, where Ironhollow is located, are the richest in iron ore.

## Mining and Metalwork

The mineral wealth of the Holds of Stoneheim is the greatest of any political entity in the known world, and the ore that is extracted from the mines of the realm includes iron, copper, tin, silver, gold, and the rarer metals that are found only in the deepest veins of the Morvind ranges. The metalwork that is produced in the forges of Stoneheim and Khazgar is the finest in the known world, and the weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects that are produced by the dwarven smiths are exported to every continent. The iron that is produced in the smelteries of Ironhollow is the raw material that feeds the forges of the realm, and the quantity and the quality of the Ironhollow output is unmatched by any other source in Morvind.

The trade that connects the Holds of Stoneheim to the rest of Morvind and the wider world is managed through the overland routes that connect the interior to the coast, and the metalwork and the stone of the realm are transported overland to the ports of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea), where they are loaded onto vessels for shipment to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and beyond. The return cargo includes the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the dwarven communities cannot produce for themselves.

## Governance and the King

The Holds of Stoneheim are governed by a king who is chosen from the hereditary ruling family of Stoneheim, and the king is the supreme authority of the realm. The court is situated in the great hall of Stoneheim, and the council of hold-elders that advises the king is drawn from the leaders of the individual holds throughout the realm. The administration of the realm is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the mining operations, the maintenance of the halls and the roads, and the administration of justice. The governance of the Holds of Stoneheim is the most stable of any political entity in Morvind, and the dwarven tradition of hereditary rule and council governance has sustained the realm for many centuries.

## Significance

The Holds of Stoneheim is the greatest dwarven realm in the known world, and its mineral wealth and its craftsmanship are the foundation of the economy and the reputation of Morvind. The realm's trade connects the interior to the coast and the wider world, and its metalwork is among the most valued products in the known world. The significance of the Holds of Stoneheim lies in its scale and its quality, for the realm is the largest and the most productive dwarven political entity, and its long history and its stable governance are a model of the political order that has sustained the dwarven people for centuries.
