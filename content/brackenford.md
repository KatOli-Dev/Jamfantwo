## Overview

Brackenford is a western inland town of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the foot of the mountain spine where the western foothills meet the lowland plains. The town stands at a crossing of the river that descends from the mountains, and its position at the meeting of the highland and the lowland has made it a natural point of exchange between the mountain peoples and the plains peoples. Brackenford is smaller than the cities of the eastern coast, but it is a thriving and well-situated community, and its trade connects the western interior to the mountain passes and the regions beyond.

## The River and the Foothills

The river that flows past Brackenford descends from the mountain spine, and its waters are swift and clear at the point where they enter the lowlands, fed by the snowmelt and the springs of the highlands. The crossing at Brackenford is a ford that has been used since the earliest days of settlement in the region, and the bridge that now spans the river is a sturdy structure of stone that was built with the assistance of [dwarven](/content/dwarves) engineers. The foothills that rise behind the town are covered in a thick growth of bracken and scrub, and the name of the town is derived from the ferns that cover the slopes.

The foothills of the mountain spine are a region of transition between the highland and the lowland, and the land around Brackenford is a mixture of wooded slopes, open meadows, and the cleared ground that is used for agriculture. The farms that surround the town produce grain, vegetables, and the fodder that sustains the livestock of the region, and the hills provide timber, game, and the other products of a woodland economy.

## Trade and the Market

The trade of Brackenford is a mixture of the agricultural products of the lowlands and the mineral products of the mountains, and the town is a natural point of exchange between the two. The metalwork, the stone, and the mineral products of the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) settlements in the mountains are traded at Brackenford for the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods of the lowlands, and the market square of the town is the primary point of exchange for the western interior. The [dwarves](/content/dwarves) of the mountain spine are regular visitors to the market, and the trade between the humans and the dwarves is the foundation of the town's commerce.

The town is also a centre for the woodworking that is the natural industry of a foothill settlement, and the carpenters, the coopers, and the wheelwrights of Brackenford are noted for their sturdy, well-made products. The timber of the foothills is exported to the lowlands and the coast, where the straight-grained wood of the mountain slopes is valued for its durability.

## The Town

Brackenford is a small, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the character of the foothill country, with timber-framed buildings that are clad in the wood of the hills, their roofs pitched to shed the rain and the snow, and their foundations set on the stone that is quarried from the slopes above. The streets are broad and well-paved, and the town is oriented toward the bridge and the river, with the market square situated at the crossing. The population of Brackenford is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of dwarves who are involved in the mineral trade and the maintenance of the bridge and the mountain roads.

## Significance

Brackenford is the primary point of exchange between the western interior and the mountain spine of Aethelar, and its trade connects the lowlands to the mountain passes and the regions beyond. The town is a significant settlement of the western interior, and its position at the foot of the mountains gives it a commercial importance that belies its modest size. The prosperity of Brackenford is built upon the trade that flows through the town, and the community is a model of the coexistence between the human and the dwarven peoples that is characteristic of the foothill country.
