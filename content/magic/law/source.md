## Overview

The source of the Art is resonance, a property of the world by which like things act upon like. The chroniclers of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) state the principle in the form that has come to be the most widely cited: that every thing in the world has its likeness, and every likeness is a path by which the thing may be reached. Resonance is not a force that flows between things, and resonance is not a substance that can be gathered and spent. Resonance is the principle of sympathy itself, and the practitioner of the Art is one who has learned to walk the paths of sympathy without losing the way.

This page sets down the principle as it is observed and as it is taught, with the principal variants of the formulation and the principal disputes about its meaning. The disputes are not resolved here, for the chronicler is bound to record rather than to adjudicate.

## Resonance and Sympathy

The principle of resonance is older than any of the traditions that teach the Art. The earliest of the practitioners whose names have been preserved spoke of sympathy rather than of resonance, and the older word is still preferred by the [Forest Fellowship of the Heartwood](/content/government/local/forest-fellowship-of-the-heartwood) and by several of the more conservative lineages of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael). The shift in vocabulary was made by the chroniclers of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) in the generation after the [Reign of Queen Sera](/content/history/reign-of-queen-sera), and the shift has been adopted by most of the academies that have come into existence since.

The two words are taken by the traditions to mean the same thing, though the [Forest Fellowship](/content/government/local/forest-fellowship-of-the-heartwood) and the [Council of Khalisar](/content/government/local/council-of-khalisar) have occasionally argued for a distinction. The Fellowship treats sympathy as the narrower term, denoting the act by which one pattern reaches another, and treats resonance as the wider term, denoting the underlying order that makes sympathy possible. The Council treats the two as synonyms, and the Council holds that the distinction introduces a complexity that the practice does not require. The disagreement is not merely verbal, for the position one takes on the distinction affects the working one prefers to use when the pattern to be reached is far from the pattern of the practitioner.

## What Resonance Is Not

The traditions agree that resonance is not a thing that can be measured in the manner of weight or length, and the traditions agree that resonance is not a thing that can be stored in a vessel. The practitioners of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) have been particularly insistent on this point, and the practitioners of the Compact have published several accounts of failed experiments in which an attempt was made to gather resonance into a containment and to release it at need. The accounts agree that the containment gathered nothing, and the accounts agree that the release produced nothing, and the accounts are taken by the chroniclers as the strongest evidence that resonance is not a substance.

The traditions also agree that resonance is not the gift of any god. The priests of [Sahran](/content/location/settlement/city/sahran) and the temples of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) have on occasion claimed that the Art is a divine favour, and the claim has been received by the practitioners of the Art with courtesy and with a polite refusal to engage. The chronicler notes that the temples have not pressed the claim, and the temples have contented themselves with the observation that the Art is a part of the order of the world, and the order of the world is a matter upon which the temples and the practitioners may agree.

## Resonance in the World

The principle of resonance is observed in many places and at many scales, and the practitioner of the Art does not look upon resonance as a peculiar property of the Art alone. The chronicler of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) has recorded that the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) responds to the moon in a pattern that resembles the response of water in a bowl, and the chronicler of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) has recorded that the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) responds to the wind in a pattern that is repeated at every scale. The Art is not the source of these observations, and the Art is not the explanation of them. The Art is the discipline by which a practitioner learns to walk the paths that the patterns reveal.

The traditions treat the world as a fabric of patterns in which every part is in sympathy with every other part, and the practitioner is one who has learned to read the fabric and to follow the threads. The reading is not a passive observation, for the reading itself changes the reader. The chronicler observes that the practitioner who has read a pattern deeply enough to reshape it is no longer the same practitioner who began the reading, and the change is part of the [cost](/content/magic/law/cost) of the Art.

## The Path and the Practitioner

The older accounts describe the practitioner as one who walks the paths of resonance, and the description is preserved in the discipline of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the [Forest Fellowship](/content/government/local/forest-fellowship-of-the-heartwood). The paths are not physical, and the paths are not visible to the uninitiated. The paths are the relations between patterns, and the practitioner follows a path from the pattern of the practitioner to the pattern of the subject. The following is a work of attention and of attunement, and the work is the foundation of the reshaping that follows.

The practitioners of the academies of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) prefer a different figure: that of the practitioner as one who adjusts a tuning until the practitioner and the subject vibrate together. The two figures describe the same discipline in different terms, and the two figures are taken by the chroniclers to be equivalent in practice. The choice of figure is, however, a matter of dispute between the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the academies, and the dispute has been carried on for several generations without resolution.

## Disputes and Uncertainties

The traditions agree that resonance is the source of the Art, and the traditions agree that resonance is the principle of sympathy. The traditions disagree about how far the sympathy extends, and the traditions disagree about whether the sympathy may be increased by any act of will or by any act of the Art. The [Council of Khalisar](/content/government/local/council-of-khalisar) has maintained that the sympathy is fixed in the order of the world, and the Council holds that the practitioner may only follow the paths that already exist. The [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) has maintained that the sympathy may be deepened by long practice, and the Court holds that the practitioner of sufficient skill may reach patterns that an untutored practitioner cannot. The two positions are not in practice far apart, for both agree that the practitioner must work within the sympathy that is given, and both agree that the practitioner cannot create a sympathy that does not exist.

The chronicler observes that the disputes are of greater interest to the traditions themselves than to the practitioner at work. The practitioner at work is occupied with the reading and the reshaping, and the practitioner at work has little time for the questions that occupy the chroniclers. The questions are not, however, without importance, for the answers that the traditions give shape the disciplines that the traditions teach, and the disciplines in turn shape the workings that the traditions make their own.
