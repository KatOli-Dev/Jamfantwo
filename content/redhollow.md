## Overview

Redhollow is a southern settlement of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the frontier of the plains where the cultivated land meets the warm, dry country of the far south. The town stands in a shallow valley where the red soil of the dry country meets the darker earth of the grasslands, and the name of the settlement is derived from the reddish hue of the ground that surrounds it. Redhollow is the smallest and the most remote of the settlements of the southern plains, and its people are hardy and self-reliant, accustomed to the demands of a life at the edge of the cultivated land.

## The Frontier

The land around Redhollow is a transition zone, where the fertility of the southern plains gives way to the aridity of the dry country, and the agriculture of the region is limited to the hardy crops that can tolerate the poor soil and the scarce water. The farms that surround the town are smaller and less productive than those of the central grasslands, but they are sufficient to sustain the community, and the people of Redhollow supplement their agriculture with the hunting and the foraging that the dry country provides.

The frontier that Redhollow marks is the southern limit of the settled land of Aethelar, and the town is the last outpost of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation) before the dry country and the warm regions beyond. The traffic that passes through the town is light, for the routes that cross the dry country are few and the trade is modest, but the town serves as a staging point for the caravans that venture into the warm regions, and the inns and the stables of Redhollow are a valued refuge for those who travel the southern roads.

## Trade and the Town

The trade of Redhollow is limited to the products of the surrounding farms and the goods that are imported from the central grasslands. The town is a centre for the trade in the hardy livestock that is raised on the frontier, and the cattle and the goats that are bred in the region are valued for their resilience and their adaptability to the dry conditions. The wool of the frontier sheep is coarser than that of the central grasslands, but it is durable and warm, and it is a minor export that finds a market among the traders who pass through the town.

Redhollow is a small, rugged town, and its buildings are constructed of the red stone and the sun-baked clay that are the materials of the region, with thick walls and small windows that provide insulation against the heat and the wind. The streets are broad and dusty, and the town is oriented toward the road that connects it to the central grasslands, with the market square and the inns situated along the main street. The population of Redhollow is small and entirely [human](/content/humans), and the people are known for their hardihood and their independence.

## Significance

Redhollow is the southernmost settlement of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), and its position at the frontier of the plains gives it a strategic significance that belies its modest size. The town is a staging point for the trade and the travel that crosses the dry country, and its people are the guardians of the southern frontier, maintaining the roads, the wells, and the shelters that sustain the traffic between the grasslands and the warm regions beyond. The prosperity of Redhollow is modest, but the community is proud of its role as the frontier settlement of the southern plains.
