## The Forest Industries of Aethelar

The forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) are among the most extensive and productive in the known world, covering the slopes of the mountains, the valleys of the great rivers, and the coastal plains of the northern and eastern shores. The timber that the forests produce is the foundation of the shipbuilding industry, the construction of buildings and fortifications, and the production of fuel for the hearths and furnaces of the cities and towns. The logging industry is one of the oldest of the extractive trades, and the relationship between the human settlements and the forests that surround them is a subject of regulation and negotiation that involves the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds), the sovereigns of the regions, and the elven communities that have their own traditions of forest management.

The forests of Aethelar are not uniformly distributed across the continent. The great forest of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) covers the central highlands of the continent, an expanse of ancient woodland that has never been fully cleared or settled. The [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) occupies the northern slopes of the Aethelaran massif, a forest of oak, ash, and beech that provides the finest timber for shipbuilding and construction. The forests of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) extend along the coast of the Greyring Ocean, a region of pine and spruce that is the principal source of the timber that is exported to the treeless regions of the southern continent.

## The Aethelar Heartwood

The [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is the largest continuous forest on the continent of Aethelar, covering an area that is measured in hundreds of square leagues. The Heartwood is a mixed forest of oak, ash, beech, elm, and yew, with undergrowth of hazel and holly and a ground cover of ferns and mosses. The trees of the Heartwood are tall and straight, growing in the rich soil of the central highlands where the rainfall is plentiful and the climate is mild. The forest has been the source of timber for the settlements of Aethelar since the first human communities were established on the continent, and the logging operations that take place within it are regulated by the authorities of the surrounding kingdoms and the guilds of the timber merchants.

The logging in the Heartwood is conducted on a rotational basis, with the forest divided into compartments that are harvested in sequence over a cycle of several decades. The compartment that is to be harvested is surveyed by the foresters, who mark the trees that are to be felled and the trees that are to be left for seed and for the maintenance of the forest canopy. The marked trees are felled by the woodcutters, who work in teams of four or five men, using axes and saws of forged iron to bring down the trees and to trim the branches from the trunks. The felled timber is hauled by teams of oxen to the nearest road or river, where it is loaded onto wagons or lashed into rafts for transport to the mills and the markets.

The organisation of the woodcutters and the timber hauliers in the Heartwood is regulated by the guilds of the lumber trade, which set the standards for the felling and the trimming of the timber and the rates at which the woodcutters are paid. The guilds also maintain the roads and the bridges that carry the timber from the forest to the settlements, and the tolls that are collected on the timber roads are the principal source of revenue for the guilds.

## Brackenshield Wood and Northern Ship Timber

The [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) is the second great forest of Aethelar, occupying the northern slopes of the central massif and extending to the shores of the Greyring Ocean. The forest is composed primarily of oak, the most valuable timber tree for the construction of ships and buildings, and the oak of Brackenshield is recognised as the finest in the known world. The oak of Brackenshield is slow-grown, dense, and resistant to rot, and the curved branches and forked trunks of the Brackenshield oak are sought after by the shipwrights of the coastal cities for the frames and the keels of the vessels they build.

The logging in Brackenshield Wood is more intensive than the logging in the Heartwood, for the demand for Brackenshield oak is high and the forest is smaller and more accessible. The woodcutters of Brackenshield are organised into guilds that are among the oldest in the timber trade, with traditions and customs that date back to the earliest centuries of the human settlement of Aethelar. The guilds of Brackenshield regulate the felling of the oak, requiring that the woodcutters take only the trees that have reached their full maturity and that they leave the younger trees to grow to replace them.

The timber from Brackenshield Wood is transported to the coast along the roads and the rivers that connect the forest to the ports of the northern coast. The timber that is destined for the shipyards of Eldreth and the other shipbuilding cities is carried by wagon to the rivers and lashed into rafts that are floated downstream to the coastal towns, where the timber is loaded onto ships for the voyage to the shipyards. The journey from the forest to the shipyard can take months, and the timber that is lost to theft and damage along the way is a significant cost that the timber merchants factor into the price of the wood.

## The Northern Marches and the Export Trade

The forests of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) are the most commercially significant in the known world for the volume of timber that they produce for export. The Northern Marches extend along the coast of the Greyring Ocean, a region of pine, spruce, and fir that grows quickly in the cool, damp climate of the northern coast. The timber of the Northern Marches is not as dense or as durable as the hardwood timber of the Heartwood and Brackenshield, but it is much more abundant and much cheaper, and it is the principal source of the timber that is exported to the treeless regions of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the islands of the Greyring Ocean.

The logging in the Northern Marches is conducted on an industrial scale that is unmatched in the other forest regions of Aethelar. The woodcutters of the Northern Marches work in large camps that move through the forest as the accessible timber is exhausted, felling the trees and processing them into logs and planks at the camp before transporting them to the coast. The camps are established by the timber merchants who have purchased the rights to the timber from the sovereign of the Northern Marches, and the merchants employ the woodcutters and the sawyers who work in the camps.

The timber of the Northern Marches is exported primarily to Sahrana, where the demand for timber for construction and for the fuel of the smelters and the furnaces is high and the local supply is limited. The timber is carried along the [Northern Coast Route](/content/location/route/trade/northern-coast-route), the sea route that follows the coast of Aethelar from the ports of the Northern Marches to the ports of the south and then across the [Auric Passage](/content/location/route/trade/auric-passage) to Sahrana. The timber is loaded onto the vessels of the timber merchants, who carry it to Khalisar and the other Sahrani ports where it is sold to the merchants of the interior.

## Elven and Human Cooperation in Forest Management

The relationship between the human settlements and the elven communities of the forests is a distinctive feature of the timber industry of Aethelar. The elves of the Aethelar Heartwood and the Brackenshield Wood have their own traditions of forest management that are older than the human settlement of the continent, and the elven communities have developed a way of living in the forest that takes the timber and the other products of the woodland without reducing the forest or diminishing its vitality.

The human woodcutters and the elven foresters have established a system of cooperation that governs the logging in the forests where the elven communities are present. The elven foresters survey the forest and mark the trees that may be taken and the trees that must be left, and the human woodcutters respect the elven markings as binding. The elven foresters also supervise the replanting of the areas that have been harvested, ensuring that the forest will regenerate and that the next generation of timber trees will be as fine as the current one.

The cooperation between the humans and the elves is not without its tensions, for the human demand for timber is higher than the elven foresters consider sustainable and the elven restrictions on logging are a source of frustration for the human woodcutters and the timber merchants. The guilds of the timber trade and the elven councils of the forest maintain a dialogue that has kept the tensions from breaking into open conflict, and the system of cooperation has endured for centuries. The [Heartwood Fellowship](/content/government/local/heartwood-fellowship), the institution that represents the elven communities in the negotiations with the human authorities, is the principal forum in which the disputes over the forest are resolved and the agreements that govern the logging are reached.

## Timber for Shipbuilding

The shipbuilding industry of the coastal cities is the largest single consumer of Aethelaran timber, and the quality of the timber that the shipyards require is the highest of any use. The shipwrights who build the vessels that carry the trade of the three continents need timber that is straight and strong and free of knots and defects, and they need the curved pieces that form the frames and the keels of the vessels. The timber that meets these requirements is the most expensive and the most carefully selected of the products of the forest, and the guilds of the timber merchants maintain the highest standards for the timber that is sold to the shipyards.

The oak of Brackenshield Wood is the timber that the shipwrights prefer for the frames and the keels of the largest vessels, for the Brackenshield oak is the strongest and the most durable of the hardwoods that grow in Aethelar. The elm of the Heartwood is used for the planking of the hulls, for elm is resistant to the rot and the worm that attack the timber in the warm waters of the Auric Ocean. The pine of the Northern Marches is used for the masts and the spars and the decking, for pine is light and strong and easy to work.

The timber that the shipyards of Eldreth and Khalisar and Nordhaven consume in a single year is measured in thousands of trees, and the rate at which the timber is taken from the forests is a matter of concern for the authorities who regulate the timber trade. The guilds of the shipwrights and the guilds of the timber merchants have established quotas that limit the amount of timber that may be taken from each forest in a given year, and the quotas are enforced by the foresters of the sovereigns who own the forests.

## Construction Timber and Fuelwood

The timber that is used for construction in the cities and the towns of the three continents is the largest portion of the total timber harvest, measured by volume if not by value. The timber that frames the buildings of Eldreth and Khalisar and Nordhaven, the timber that floors the houses and roofs the halls, and the timber that furnishes the workshops and the warehouses comes from the forests of Aethelar, and the demand for construction timber grows with the growth of the cities.

The construction timber is of a lower quality than the shipbuilding timber, and the standards that the guilds apply to the construction timber are less stringent than the standards for the shipbuilding timber. The timber that is used for the frames of buildings can have knots and small defects that would render it unsuitable for a ship's keel, and the timber that is used for flooring and panelling can have a less straight grain than the timber that is used for a ship's planking. The differentiation of the timber by quality is one of the functions of the guilds of the timber merchants, who sort the timber at the mills and the yards and sell it at different prices according to its quality.

The fuelwood that heats the houses and the workshops of the cities and the towns is the third great product of the forests of Aethelar. The fuelwood is taken from the branches and the tops of the felled trees, from the trees that are too small or too crooked to be used for timber, and from the woodlands that are managed specifically for the production of fuel. The [prices and livelihood](/content/economy/prices-and-livelihood) of the households of the northern towns are significantly affected by the cost of fuelwood, and the price of fuelwood in the winter months is a matter of concern for the councils of the northern cities.
