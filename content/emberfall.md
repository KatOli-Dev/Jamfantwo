## Overview

Emberfall is a southern trade city of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the junction of the overland routes that cross the southern portion of the continent. The city stands at the point where the roads from the eastern coast, the western coast, and the southern plains converge, and its position at the meeting of these routes has made it the primary hub of southern inland trade. Emberfall is a bustling, well-connected city, and its prosperity is built upon the traffic that flows through its streets and the commerce that accumulates at the junction.

## The Roads and the Junction

The roads that converge on Emberfall are the principal overland routes of southern Aethelar, and the city stands at the point where they meet, a junction that has been a natural gathering point for travellers and traders since the earliest days of human settlement in the region. The east road runs to [Sallowmere](/content/sallowmere) and the eastern coast, the west road runs to [Brackenford](/content/brackenford) and the western interior, and the north road runs to [Sunmere](/content/sunmere) and the southern plains. The traffic that passes through Emberfall includes the goods of the eastern coast, the western interior, and the southern plains, and the city's position at the junction of these routes makes it a natural point of exchange.

The roads that enter the city are broad and well-maintained, for the traffic that uses them is heavy and constant, and the tolls on the roads are a significant source of the city's revenue. The caravans that travel the roads are a common sight in the streets of Emberfall, and the inns, the taverns, and the stables that serve the travellers are a significant part of the city's economy.

## Trade and the Market

The trade of Emberfall is the most diverse of any southern inland city, for the goods that pass through the city come from every region of the southern continent. The agricultural products of the southern plains, the manufactured goods of the eastern coast, the timber and the metalwork of the western interior, and the exotic products of the warm regions beyond the frontier are all traded at Emberfall, and the market square of the city is one of the busiest in the south. The market that is held there is a daily affair, for the constant traffic of the roads ensures a steady flow of goods and customers.

The city is also a centre for the financial services that support the trade of the southern routes, and the bankers and the money-changers of Emberfall are among the most influential in the southern economy. The warehouses and the counting houses that line the main streets are substantial buildings, and the trading houses that occupy them are among the most prosperous in the southern inland.

## The City

Emberfall is a large, bustling city, and its architecture reflects its function as a trade hub, with broad streets, substantial buildings, and a commercial district that dominates the city centre. The city is built around the junction of the roads, and the main streets radiate outward from the central market square in the pattern of the routes that converge on the city. The population of Emberfall is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) who are involved in the metal trade and the maintenance of the roads.

## Significance

Emberfall is the primary hub of southern inland trade, and its position at the junction of the overland routes makes it the most connected settlement in the southern interior. The city is a significant member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), and its trade and financial services are a valuable component of the economy of the southern region. The prosperity of Emberfall is a direct consequence of its position, and the city has grown and thrived on the commerce that flows through its streets.
