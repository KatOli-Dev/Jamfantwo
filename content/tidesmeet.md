## Overview

Tidesmeet is a settlement on a volcanic island in the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), situated at the meeting point of two oceanic currents that converge near the island and give the settlement its name. The island is one of the larger of the volcanic chain that rises from the deep waters of the central Auric, and its position astride the trade routes between [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and [Sahrana](/content/sahrana) has made it a natural stopping point for vessels crossing the ocean. Tidesmeet is the largest and most important of the island settlements, and its harbour is the primary refuge and resupply point for the vessels that travel the Auric trade routes.

## The Island and the Harbour

The island on which Tidesmeet stands is a volcanic peak that rises from the deep water of the central [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and its slopes are steep and rugged, covered in a thick growth of tropical vegetation that is sustained by the warm climate and the abundant rainfall. The harbour of Tidesmeet is a natural anchorage that is sheltered by the curve of the island's caldera, and the approach is marked by a lighthouse that is visible for many miles across the open ocean. The harbour is deep and well-protected, and it can accommodate the largest vessels that travel the Auric trade routes, though the entrance is narrow and requires careful navigation.

The convergence of the two currents near the island is the source of the name Tidesmeet, and the meeting of the warm and the cooler waters creates a zone of turbulent and unpredictable conditions that can be dangerous for vessels that are unfamiliar with the local waters. The pilots of Tidesmeet are skilled in the navigation of the currents, and their services are sought by every captain who must approach or leave the harbour, for the passage through the convergent zone requires local knowledge and experience.

## Trade and the Ocean Routes

The trade of Tidesmeet is built upon its position astride the Auric trade routes, and the settlement is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling between [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). The vessels that call at the harbour are provisioned with water, food, and the materials that are needed for the continuation of the voyage, and the services that the settlement provides are the foundation of its economy. The goods that pass through the harbour include the spices and the dyes of Sahrana, the grain and the metalwork of Aethelar, and the products of the other islands of the chain, which are gathered at Tidesmeet for transhipment to the continental markets.

The island itself produces a variety of tropical fruits, fish, and the other products of the volcanic soil and the surrounding waters, and these are exported to the continental markets as minor but valued commodities. The fishing grounds around the island are rich, for the convergent currents sustain a diverse and abundant marine life, and the catch is a significant supplement to the economy of the settlement. The [merfolk](/content/merfolk) of the Auric waters are occasional visitors to the harbour, and a cautious but peaceful coexistence has developed between the islanders and the merfolk of the surrounding waters.

The pilots of Tidesmeet are among the most skilled navigators in the Auric, and their knowledge of the convergent currents and the approaches to the harbour is a valuable asset that is passed from generation to generation. The pilotage service is managed by the settlement, and the fees that are charged for the pilotage are a significant source of income for the community. The pilots are held in high regard by the captains who call at the harbour, and the reputation of the Tidesmeet pilots is a factor in the decision of many vessels to stop at the island rather than to attempt the crossing of the convergent zone without local guidance.

## The Settlement

Tidesmeet is a small, well-built settlement, and its architecture reflects the character of the volcanic island, with buildings of dark stone and timber, broad streets, and open squares that take advantage of the sea breezes. The buildings are constructed to withstand the occasional storms that sweep across the island, with thick walls and steep roofs, and the settlement is oriented toward the harbour, with the market square and the warehouses situated along the waterfront. The population of Tidesmeet is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a small community of traders and sailors from the continental ports.

The market square of Tidesmeet is the centre of the settlement, and the goods that are bought and sold in the market include the provisions and the supplies that are needed by the vessels that call at the harbour, the fish and the tropical fruits that are produced on the island, and the goods that are transhipped between the continents and the other islands of the chain. The warehouses that line the waterfront are the storage points for the goods that are awaiting transhipment, and the warehouses are managed by the merchants and the agents of the continental trading houses who maintain a permanent presence in the settlement.

## History

The settlement of Tidesmeet was established by the first mariners who recognised the value of the natural harbour and the position of the island astride the trade routes. The initial settlement was a small provisioning station that served the vessels that were beginning to cross the Auric in the early centuries of the continental trade, and the community grew as the volume of the trade increased and the services that the settlement provided became more elaborate. The lighthouse that marks the approach to the harbour was one of the first substantial structures to be built, and it remains one of the most important, for the convergent currents and the open ocean make the approach to the island a demanding passage.

The admission of Tidesmeet to the [Auric Isles](/content/auric-isles) was a natural development, for the settlement was the largest and the most important of the island communities, and its harbour was the primary refuge for the vessels that travelled the ocean. The leadership of Tidesmeet in the confederation has been a factor in the co-ordination of the island trade and the maintenance of the navigation aids that are essential to the safety of the ocean routes. The council of the confederation meets at Tidesmeet, and the settlement is the effective capital of the island communities.

## Significance

Tidesmeet is the largest and most important of the island settlements of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and its harbour is the primary refuge and resupply point for the vessels that travel the Auric trade routes. The settlement is a member of the [Auric Isles](/content/auric-isles), the confederation of island communities, and its role in the ocean trade is a vital component of the economy of the Auric. The significance of Tidesmeet lies in its position astride the trade routes and its harbour, which is the finest and most accessible of any island in the central Auric, and the settlement is a symbol of the maritime connections that link the continents through the ocean.

The role of Tidesmeet as the meeting point of the continental trade and the island trade gives it a cultural character that is distinct from the other settlements of the Auric, for the population is a mixture of the islanders and the continental traders who have settled in the community, and the traditions and the customs of the settlement reflect the diversity of its people. The settlement is a testament to the capacity of the island peoples to sustain a prosperous and a significant community in the most remote and the most demanding environment of the open ocean.
