## Overview

Skaldshavn is a fishing and trading port on the northern coast of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated on a stretch of coast where the mountains descend almost to the water's edge and the harbours are few and difficult of access. The town stands on a narrow shelf of land between the cliffs and the sea, and its harbour is a deep, sheltered inlet that is carved into the rock of the coast by the same geological forces that built the mountains above. Skaldshavn is smaller than [Nordhaven](/content/nordhaven), but it is a thriving and self-sufficient community, and its fishing fleet and its trading vessels are among the most seaworthy in Morvind.

## The Harbour and the Fishing

The harbour of Skaldshavn is a narrow inlet that penetrates the coast for several hundred yards, and its entrance is so constricted that only one vessel may pass at a time. The harbour is deep and well-sheltered, and the water within is calm even when the storms rage outside, for the cliffs that enclose the inlet absorb the force of the wind and the swell. The quays line both sides of the inlet, and the fishing boats and the trading vessels that are moored there are hauled out of the water during the winter months, when the ice can form within the harbour itself.

The fishing grounds off Skaldshavn are among the most productive in Morvind, for the cold, nutrient-rich waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) sustain vast shoals of fish that are the foundation of the town's economy. The fishing fleet of Skaldshavn is small but hardy, and the boats venture far from land in pursuit of the shoals, braving the storms and the cold that would deter less experienced mariners. The catch is landed at the harbour, salted and dried in the curing houses that line the inlet, and exported to the inland markets and the ports of Aethelar.

## Trade and the Town

The trade of Skaldshavn is secondary to its fishing, but it is nonetheless significant, for the town is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling along the northern coast, and the goods that pass through its harbour include the metalwork of the interior and the manufactured goods of Aethelar. The town is also a centre for the building of the small, sturdy fishing boats that are used along the Morvind coast, and the boatbuilders of Skaldshavn are noted for their skill in the construction of vessels that are suited to the demanding conditions of the northern waters.

Skaldshavn is a small, rugged town, and its buildings are constructed of the dark stone of the cliffs, with low profiles and thick walls that withstand the wind and the salt. The streets are steep and narrow, climbing the cliff from the harbour to the plateau above, where the newer districts of the town are situated. The population of Skaldshavn is small and entirely [human](/content/humans), and the people are known for their hardihood, their skill in the difficult waters they work, and their deep attachment to the coast that sustains them.

## Significance

Skaldshavn is one of the most important fishing ports on the northern coast of Morvind, and its catch is a valued contribution to the economy of the continent. The town is a member of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/kingdom-of-nordhaven), and its position on the remote northern coast gives it a strategic significance as a refuge and a stopping point for vessels navigating the difficult waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea). The prosperity of Skaldshavn is modest compared to that of the larger ports, but the community is proud of its independence and its mastery of one of the most demanding environments in the known world.
