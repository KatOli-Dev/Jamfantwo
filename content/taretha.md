## Overview

Taretha is a coastal city-state on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), renowned throughout the known world for its libraries and its scholarship. The city stands on a sheltered bay on the coast where the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) meets the fertile strip of the northern coast, and its position on the coast and its proximity to the interior have made it a natural gathering point for the knowledge and the learning of the Sahrani civilisation. Taretha is a prosperous, cultivated city, and its wealth is built not upon trade alone but upon the accumulation and the preservation of the knowledge that is the pride of the Sahrani people.

## The Libraries

The libraries of Taretha are the most extensive in the known world, and they are the primary institution of the city and the reason for its renown. The libraries contain collections of manuscripts, scrolls, and codices that have been gathered from every continent and every age, and the holdings include works of history, philosophy, mathematics, medicine, and the natural sciences, as well as the literary and the religious texts that are the cultural heritage of the Sahrani people. The libraries are housed in a series of substantial buildings that are situated in the centre of the city, and the architecture of the library complex is noted for its beauty and its suitability for the preservation of the collections, with thick walls, shaded reading rooms, and the climate-controlled chambers that protect the most fragile materials. The oldest of the library buildings is a structure of great antiquity, and its walls are inscribed with the texts that were carved into the stone by the earliest scholars of the city, a record of the knowledge that was gathered in the first centuries of the settlement.

The libraries of Taretha are open to scholars from every continent, and the city has a long tradition of hospitality toward those who come to study and to learn. The scholars who work in the libraries are supported by the city, and the community of learning that has grown up around the libraries is the most distinguished in the known world. The libraries are also a centre for the copying and the translation of manuscripts, and the scribes and the translators of Taretha are among the most skilled in the known world, producing copies of the works that are in the collections and translating texts between the languages of the different continents. The scriptorium of the libraries is a vast hall where dozens of scribes work in silence, their quills and their brushes producing the copies that are sent to the institutions and the scholars who request them, and the quality of the Taretha copies is recognised as the standard against which all other manuscript production is measured.

The libraries are governed by a council of senior scholars who are responsible for the acquisition, the preservation, and the organisation of the collections. The council oversees the cataloguing of the holdings, the acquisition of new materials, and the admission of scholars to the reading rooms. The council also maintains the network of contacts that the libraries have established with the institutions and the scholars of the wider world, and the exchange of manuscripts and copies that flows through these contacts is a vital source of the new materials that are added to the collections each year.

## The Harbour and the Trade

The harbour of Taretha is a modest but sufficient anchorage that is sheltered by a pair of headlands, and the trade that flows through the port is secondary to the scholarly activity that is the primary function of the city. The trade of Taretha includes the export of the manuscripts and the copies that are produced in the libraries, which are valued by the scholars and the institutions of the northern continents, and the import of the books and the materials that are needed for the work of the libraries. The city is also a centre for the trade in the pharmaceutical and the medicinal products that are derived from the knowledge of the Sahrani herbalists and physicians, and the drugs and the remedies that are produced in Taretha are valued throughout the known world.

## The City

Taretha is a beautiful, well-built city, and its architecture reflects the cultivated and the intellectual character of the community, with elegant buildings of light-coloured stone, shaded courtyards, and the gardens and fountains that are the hallmark of the Sahrani architectural tradition. The streets are broad and quiet, for the city is oriented toward study rather than commerce, and the public buildings are dominated by the library complex and the associated institutions of learning. The population of Taretha is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a diverse community of scholars and students from every continent. The scholars who reside in the city are a cosmopolitan community, and the presence of so many learned people from so many different lands gives the city a character that is unlike any other in Sahrana, for the conversations that take place in the reading rooms and the courtyards are a meeting of minds that spans the breadth of the known world.

The gardens of Taretha are a particular feature of the city, and the cultivation of the gardens is a tradition that dates to the earliest years of the settlement. The gardens are planted with the trees and the flowers that are native to the Sahrani coast, and the irrigation that sustains them is a work of considerable engineering, for the water is drawn from the springs that rise in the foothills behind the city and conducted through a system of channels and cisterns to the gardens and the fountains. The gardens are open to the scholars and the citizens of the city, and they are a place of contemplation and of conversation, where the learned people of Taretha gather to discuss the ideas that are the substance of their work.

## Significance

Taretha is the primary centre of learning in Sahrana and the home of the most extensive libraries in the known world, and its significance lies in the preservation and the advancement of the knowledge that is the cultural heritage of the Sahrani people and the wider world. The city is a leading member of the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast), and its scholarly activity is a valued contribution to the culture and the civilisation of the Sahrani coast. The significance of Taretha lies in its libraries and its scholarship, which are unmatched by any other city in the known world, and the city is a symbol of the learning and the refinement of the Sahrani civilisation. The knowledge that is preserved in the libraries of Taretha is a resource that benefits every continent, and the scholars who study there carry the learning of the city to the farthest corners of the known world.
