## A Survey of Sapient Species

The known world is home to a diversity of sapient species, each with its own form, nature, and manner of life. What follows is a broad survey of the species known to inhabit the continents and seas. Detailed accounts of individual species may be found on their respective pages.

## The Three Widespread Species

The term common peoples refers to those species whose populations are most widespread and most extensively encountered. They are found on all three continents and across the islands and coasts of the known world: [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans), [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves), and [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves). Each has established enduring populations on multiple continents, and their interactions with one another and with the lands they inhabit have shaped much of the region's natural history.

[Humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) are the most numerous and most widely distributed, owing to their short generation time, high birth rate, and generalised constitution, which allows them to thrive in nearly any climate. [Elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) are the longest-lived but the fewest, for their rate of reproduction is very low and their populations grow slowly. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) stand between the two in both lifespan and fecundity, and their restriction to mountainous and subterranean habitats limits their range relative to humans, though they are well established wherever terrain suits them.

## Gnomes, Orcs, and Goblins

Beyond the common peoples, the world is home to several species whose populations are smaller or more localised: [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes), [orcs](/content/species/sapient/orcs), and [goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins). Their communities tend to be concentrated in particular regions rather than spread across the whole of the world.

[Gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) are a small folk reported in the woodlands and hills of several continents, bearing some resemblance to dwarves in miniature, though the nature of any kinship is unknown. [Orcs](/content/species/sapient/orcs) are a large and powerful people found chiefly in wilder, less settled regions, with a constitution well suited to harsh conditions and violent exertion. [Goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins) are a smaller and more numerous people reported in a variety of habitats, often in places that other peoples do not occupy. Less is known of all three compared to the common peoples.

## Merfolk, Cancrids, and Cephalopods

In addition to land-dwelling peoples, the world is home to species whose lives are tied to oceans and coastlines. The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) are the most widely known, inhabiting coastal waters and shallow seas around all three continents. Their bodies are adapted to an aquatic life in a manner that admits no comparison with any land-dwelling people. The [cancrids](/content/species/sapient/cancrids) are a crustacean people of the rocky intertidal coasts, and the [cephalopods](/content/species/sapient/cephalopods) are a tentacled people of the deep ocean floor, known chiefly through [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) accounts.

## Southern-Adapted Species

The warm regions are home to several species adapted to heat, whose ranges are confined to southern latitudes. The [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga) are a serpent-bodied people of the warm southern coasts and rivers, possessing keen intellects and an extensive knowledge of herbal remedies. The [lacertians](/content/species/sapient/lacertians) are a reptilian people of the swamps and warm wetlands, skilled in crafting leather and bone. The [saurians](/content/species/sapient/saurians) are a large reptilian people of the warm southern rivers, dwelling in riverbank settlements and known for their formidable strength. The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) are a jackal-headed people of the arid [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) interior, organised into clans that follow traditional migration routes. The [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) are a tortoise-like people of the warm coasts and islands, long-lived and slow of movement, their shells serving as both shelter and portable fortification. The [felinids](/content/species/sapient/felinids) are a cat-headed people of the grasslands and savannas, organised into hunter-gatherer societies with keen senses and swift reflexes.

## Woodland and Open-Country Peoples

The woodlands and open country are home to a great variety of peoples, each adapted to a particular kind of terrain. The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) are a horse-bodied people of the central grasslands, roaming in herds across the open plains. The [halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings) are a small, pastoral folk of the lowland valleys, tending flocks and cultivating garden plots. The [satyrs](/content/species/sapient/satyrs) are a goat-legged folk of the woodland fringes, known for their music and merriment. The [minotaurs](/content/species/sapient/minotaurs) are a bull-headed people of the labyrinthine highlands, dwelling in elaborate tunnel complexes carved into the rock. The [ursines](/content/species/sapient/ursines) are a bear-like people of the northern forests, solitary and territorial. The [corvids](/content/species/sapient/corvids) are a crow-like people of the high forests, nesting in the tallest trees and communicating through complex calls. The [cervines](/content/species/sapient/cervines) are a deer-headed people of the deep forests, graceful and wary of open ground. The [lepidians](/content/species/sapient/lepidians) are a moth-like people of the temperate forests, nocturnal and seldom seen, their wings bearing intricate patterns. The [limifelids](/content/species/sapient/limifelids) are a semi-aquatic people of the wetlands, marshes, and river deltas, moving with fluid grace and leaving trails of slime where they travel.

## Stone, Darkness, and Thin Air Dwellers

The mountains and subterranean world are home to several species adapted to stone, darkness, and thin air. The [trolls](/content/species/sapient/trolls) are a large, reclusive folk of the mountains and deep forests, remarkable for their regenerative abilities. The [goliaths](/content/species/sapient/goliaths) are a large, solitary people of the highest peaks, adapted to thin air and extreme cold. The [giants](/content/species/sapient/giants) are an enormous humanoid people of the most remote mountains, few in number and rarely seen. The [harpies](/content/species/sapient/harpies) are a winged people of the cliffs and high peaks, adapted to flight, their aeries inaccessible to earthbound peoples. The [kobolds](/content/species/sapient/kobolds) are a small, reptilian people of caves and shallow tunnels, industrious miners and craftsmen. The [arachne](/content/species/sapient/arachne) are a spider-bodied people of the deep caverns, weaving silk structures of considerable complexity. The [kryn](/content/species/sapient/kryn) are an insectoid people of the deep underground, living in colonies with a rigid social hierarchy. The [noctules](/content/species/sapient/noctules) are a bat-like people of the deep caves, navigating by echolocation in complete darkness. The [ogres](/content/species/sapient/ogres) are a brutish people of the wild margins, larger and less organised than orcs. The [cyclopes](/content/species/sapient/cyclopes) are one-eyed giants of remote islands and coastlines, possessing great strength and a reputation for skilled craftsmanship.

## Freshwater and Wetland Peoples

The rivers, lakes, and wetlands are home to several species whose lives are tied to fresh water. The [kappa](/content/species/sapient/kappa) are an amphibious people of the rivers and lakes of the interior, known for their mischievous nature and strength in water. The [anurans](/content/species/sapient/anurans) are an amphibious people of the marshes and still waters, their songs carrying across the wetlands at dusk. The [murids](/content/species/sapient/murids) are a small, rat-like people of the underground and the margins of settled communities, prolific breeders and resourceful survivors. The [lupines](/content/species/sapient/lupines) are a wolf-headed people of the cold northern steppes, organised into packs that follow the seasonal migrations of herd animals.

## Coexistence, Overlap, and Separation

Where the ranges of different peoples overlap, their interactions are shaped by the natures of each. Differences in lifespan, rate of reproduction, and environmental tolerance determine in large part whether species coexist closely or remain separate. Humans, being generalists, share habitat with both elves and dwarves across broad portions of the world; the chief factor in their coexistence is the tendency of each species to favour different ground within a shared region. Elves and dwarves overlap less frequently and generally separate by elevation and vegetation.

Where species are more particular in their needs, they tend to be more isolated from others. Orcs and goblins, favouring wild and marginal lands, have less contact with the common peoples. Merfolk, bound to the sea, are apart from all land-dwelling species by the nature of their habitat. Freshwater and marsh-dwelling species interact with land peoples primarily at river crossings, fords, and lakeshores, where trade in fish, reeds, and freshwater goods takes place. The world is thus a mosaic of overlapping and separate ranges, in which the degree of contact between species is governed less by disposition than by the lands they can inhabit.

Beyond the sapient peoples, the known world is home to many remarkable beasts. The [Aethelar stag](/content/species/beasts/aethelar-stag) is a noble beast of the deep woodlands, while the [amber crawler](/content/species/beasts/amber-crawler) is the largest freshwater crustacean of the inland waters. The [basilisk](/content/species/beasts/basilisk) is a serpent whose gaze is said to petrify, while the [cockatrice](/content/species/beasts/cockatrice) combines the features of a cockerel and a lizard into a creature of ill omen. The [dune worm](/content/species/beasts/dune-worm) burrows through the deepest deserts, and the [greyring whale](/content/species/beasts/greyring-whale) navigates the cold western waters. The [griffin](/content/species/beasts/griffin) is a winged lion that nests upon the highest crags, and the [hydra](/content/species/beasts/hydra) is a many-headed serpent of the marshes. The [Kharesh glass spider](/content/species/beasts/kharesh-glass-spider) weaves its webs in the deep desert, and the [manticore](/content/species/beasts/manticore) is a maneater with the body of a lion and a tail of venomous spines. The [Morvind snow cat](/content/species/beasts/morvind-snow-cat) is the apex predator of the alpine heights, the [owlbear](/content/species/beasts/owlbear) is a fierce predator of the deep forests, the [phoenix](/content/species/beasts/phoenix) is a fire-bird reborn from its own ashes, the [plains thunder boar](/content/species/beasts/plains-thunder-boar) is the great quarry of the grassland hunt, the [storm petrel](/content/species/beasts/storm-petrel) is a familiar seabird of the northern waters, the [unicorn](/content/species/beasts/unicorn) is a pure-white horse of the deep woodlands, and the [wyvern](/content/species/beasts/wyvern) is a two-legged dragon of the rocky highlands. These creatures are known through travellers' accounts and the records of naturalists, though reliable information about many of them remains scarce.
