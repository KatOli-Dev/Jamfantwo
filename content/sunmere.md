## Overview

Sunmere is the largest city of the southern plains of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated on the banks of a broad, slow river that winds through the warm grasslands of the south. The city is the primary settlement of the southern plains and the principal market for the agricultural and pastoral products of the region, and its prosperity is built upon the fertility of the warm soil and the industry of the farmers and the herders who work it. Sunmere is a warm, sunlit city, and its climate and its way of life are distinctly different from those of the cities of the temperate north.

## The Southern Plains

The southern plains of Aethelar are a region of warm, open grassland that stretches from the foothills of the mountain spine to the southern coast, and the land is among the most productive in the continent for the cultivation of the warm-weather crops that are the staples of the southern diet. The soil is dark and rich, and the river that flows past Sunmere provides the water that sustains the agriculture of the surrounding country. The farms that line the river produce grain, fruits, and the vegetables that are the primary exports of the city, and the pasture that lies beyond the cultivated land supports the herds of cattle and sheep that are the other great resource of the region.

The pastoral peoples of the southern plains include the [centaur](/content/centaurs) kindreds that range across the open grass, and the relationship between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is one of cautious co-operation, with each people pursuing its own way of life and meeting at the markets of Sunmere to exchange the products of their different economies.

The climate of the southern plains is warm and dry for much of the year, and the growing season is longer than that of the temperate north, allowing for two harvests of the principal grain crops in a single year. The river that flows past Sunmere is the lifeline of the agriculture, for the rainfall alone is insufficient to sustain the cultivation of the warm-weather crops that are the staples of the southern diet, and the irrigation channels that have been dug from the river to the fields are among the oldest and the most important works of engineering in the region. The management of the water is a responsibility of the city, and the overseers of the irrigation are among the most important officials of the community.

## Trade and the Market

The trade of Sunmere is dominated by the agricultural products of the surrounding plains, and the grain, the fruit, and the livestock that are produced in the region are gathered at the city for sale and for transport to the markets of the north. The river is the primary route of trade, and the barges that carry the goods of Sunmere downriver to the southern coast and upriver to the interior are a common sight on the waterway. The city is also a centre for the processing of the agricultural products of the region, and the mills, the presses, and the workshops that line the riverbank are a significant part of the city's economy.

The market square of Sunmere is one of the largest in the southern plains, and the market that is held there is a weekly affair that draws farmers, herders, and traders from the surrounding country. The [centaurs](/content/centaurs) of the plains are regular visitors to the market, and the trade between the settled and the nomadic peoples is the lifeblood of the city's commerce.

The river trade of Sunmere is conducted by the barges that carry the goods of the city downriver to the southern coast and upriver to the interior, and the barge operators are a significant community within the city. The barges are broad, flat-bottomed vessels that are suited to the shallow waters of the river, and they are poled and towed by the crews that operate them. The river is the most economical route for the bulk goods that form the core of the trade of Sunmere, and the volume of traffic on the waterway is a measure of the prosperity of the city.

## History and the Foundation

The founding of Sunmere is dated to the early centuries of the human settlement of the southern plains, when the site was chosen for its position on the river and the fertility of the surrounding soil. The early settlement was a farming community, and the first permanent buildings were constructed on the banks of the river. The growth of the city was rapid, for the productivity of the land and the mildness of the climate attracted settlers from the temperate north, and the population of Sunmere swelled within a few generations. The city was granted its charter by the council of the southern plains in the year 298, and the charter established the rights of the city to manage the irrigation and to collect the tolls on the river trade.

The history of Sunmere is a history of agricultural prosperity, for the city has never been threatened by war or disturbance, and the community has devoted its energies to the improvement of the land and the expansion of the trade. The construction of the great irrigation channel that runs from the river to the eastern fields was the most significant public work in the history of the city, and the channel was completed in the year 412 after a decade of labour by the engineers and the workmen of the community.

## The City

Sunmere is a large, open city, and its architecture reflects the warm climate of the southern plains, with broad streets, shaded courtyards, and buildings of light-coloured stone that reflect the sun. The buildings are constructed with wide windows and high ceilings that allow the air to circulate, and the roofs are tiled and fitted with the wind-catchers that are a distinctive feature of the southern architectural tradition. The population of Sunmere is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a growing community of [halflings](/content/halflings) who are drawn by the fertile land and the mild climate.

## Architecture and the City

The architecture of Sunmere is the most distinctive of any city on the southern plains, for the buildings are designed to provide relief from the heat of the summer, and the construction reflects the warmth and the brightness of the climate. The walls are thick and the windows are screened with the lattice shutters that allow the air to circulate while keeping out the sun, and the courtyards are planted with the shade trees and the fountains that cool the air and provide the water that is the most precious resource of the city. The roofs are fitted with the wind-catchers that channel the breeze into the interiors, and the wind-catchers are the most distinctive feature of the skyline of Sunmere, their tapered silhouettes visible from many miles across the plain.

The public buildings of Sunmere are situated around the market square, and the council hall, the granary, and the exchange are the most substantial buildings in the city. The granary is the largest building in Sunmere, for the storage of the grain that is the primary product of the region is a critical function of the city, and the granary is built of stone with walls that are thick enough to keep the interior cool and dry. The exchange is the centre of the financial services that support the trade of the southern plains, and the merchants and the money-lenders of Sunmere conduct their business in the cool of its shaded arcade.

## Culture and the Centaurs

The culture of Sunmere is shaped by the warmth of the climate and the diversity of the peoples who inhabit the southern plains, and the community is noted for its hospitality and its openness to strangers. The cuisine of Sunmere is the most varied in the south, for the warm-weather crops that are grown in the fields provide a greater range of ingredients than the staples of the temperate north, and the cooks of the city are skilled in the preparation of the spiced dishes and the fruit preserves that are the specialities of the southern tradition.

The relationship between the settled humans and the [centaur](/content/centaurs) kindreds of the plains is a central feature of the cultural life of Sunmere, and the market is the meeting point where the two peoples interact. The centaurs are nomadic herders who range across the open grass in patterns that are governed by the seasons and the availability of the grazing, and their visits to the market are occasions of commerce and of social exchange. The humans of Sunmere have learned to respect the ways of the centaurs, and the trade between the two peoples is conducted with a courtesy and a formality that reflect the long history of the relationship. The centaurs provide the wool, the leather, and the livestock that are the pastoral products of the plains, and the humans provide the grain, the manufactured goods, and the tools that the nomadic life requires.

## Significance

Sunmere is the largest and most prosperous city of the southern plains, and its agricultural products are a significant component of the economy of Aethelar. The city is the primary settlement of the southern region, and it is a key member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), the alliance of the grassland cities and the pastoral peoples. The prosperity of Sunmere is a direct consequence of the fertility of the warm soil and the industry of its people, and the city is a symbol of the agricultural wealth of the southern plains.
