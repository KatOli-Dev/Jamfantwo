## Overview

Sunmere is the largest city of the southern plains of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated on the banks of a broad, slow river that winds through the warm grasslands of the south. The city is the primary settlement of the southern plains and the principal market for the agricultural and pastoral products of the region, and its prosperity is built upon the fertility of the warm soil and the industry of the farmers and the herders who work it. Sunmere is a warm, sunlit city, and its climate and its way of life are distinctly different from those of the cities of the temperate north.

## The Southern Plains

The southern plains of Aethelar are a region of warm, open grassland that stretches from the foothills of the mountain spine to the southern coast, and the land is among the most productive in the continent for the cultivation of the warm-weather crops that are the staples of the southern diet. The soil is dark and rich, and the river that flows past Sunmere provides the water that sustains the agriculture of the surrounding country. The farms that line the river produce grain, fruits, and the vegetables that are the primary exports of the city, and the pasture that lies beyond the cultivated land supports the herds of cattle and sheep that are the other great resource of the region.

The pastoral peoples of the southern plains include the [centaur](/content/centaurs) kindreds that range across the open grass, and the relationship between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is one of cautious co-operation, with each people pursuing its own way of life and meeting at the markets of Sunmere to exchange the products of their different economies.

## Trade and the Market

The trade of Sunmere is dominated by the agricultural products of the surrounding plains, and the grain, the fruit, and the livestock that are produced in the region are gathered at the city for sale and for transport to the markets of the north. The river is the primary route of trade, and the barges that carry the goods of Sunmere downriver to the southern coast and upriver to the interior are a common sight on the waterway. The city is also a centre for the processing of the agricultural products of the region, and the mills, the presses, and the workshops that line the riverbank are a significant part of the city's economy.

The market square of Sunmere is one of the largest in the southern plains, and the market that is held there is a weekly affair that draws farmers, herders, and traders from the surrounding country. The [centaurs](/content/centaurs) of the plains are regular visitors to the market, and the trade between the settled and the nomadic peoples is the lifeblood of the city's commerce.

## The City

Sunmere is a large, open city, and its architecture reflects the warm climate of the southern plains, with broad streets, shaded courtyards, and buildings of light-coloured stone that reflect the sun. The buildings are constructed with wide windows and high ceilings that allow the air to circulate, and the roofs are tiled and fitted with the wind-catchers that are a distinctive feature of the southern architectural tradition. The population of Sunmere is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a growing community of [halflings](/content/halflings) who are drawn by the fertile land and the mild climate.

## Significance

Sunmere is the largest and most prosperous city of the southern plains, and its agricultural products are a significant component of the economy of Aethelar. The city is the primary settlement of the southern region, and it is a key member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), the alliance of the grassland cities and the pastoral peoples. The prosperity of Sunmere is a direct consequence of the fertility of the warm soil and the industry of its people, and the city is a symbol of the agricultural wealth of the southern plains.
