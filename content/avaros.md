## Overview

Avaros is a great agricultural city of the central grasslands of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated in the heart of the most productive farming region of the continent. The city stands on a low rise in the middle of the open plain, where the grassland stretches in every direction to the horizon, and its position at the centre of the grasslands has made it the primary market and the principal depot for the grain, the livestock, and the other products of the region. Avaros is a large, prosperous city, and its wealth is built upon the fertility of the soil and the industry of the farmers who work it.

## The Grasslands and the Agriculture

The central grasslands of Aethelar are among the most productive agricultural regions in the known world, and the land around Avaros is the richest portion of the grasslands. The soil is deep and dark, formed over centuries from the decay of the prairie grasses that cover the plain, and it produces grain in abundance, along with the other crops that are grown in the region. The agriculture of the grasslands is a mixture of large-scale grain production and the pastoralism of the [centaur](/content/centaurs) kindreds, and the two systems coexist in a relationship that is generally beneficial to both.

The grain that is produced on the farms around Avaros is the primary export of the city, and the granaries of Avaros are among the largest in the known world, capable of storing enough grain to feed the continent for many months. The grain is purchased by the merchants of the city from the farmers of the surrounding country, stored in the granaries, and sold to the traders who transport it to the markets of the coast and the interior. The grain trade is the foundation of the economy of Avaros, and the city's prosperity is directly tied to the fertility of the land and the success of the harvest.

## The City

Avaros is a large, open city, and its architecture reflects the character of the grasslands, with broad streets, low buildings, and wide-open spaces that accommodate the market, the granaries, and the stockyards that are the primary institutions of the settlement. The buildings are constructed of the light-coloured stone that is quarried from the occasional outcrops that rise from the plain, and the roofs are pitched to shed the rain that comes in sudden, heavy storms during the summer months.

The market square of Avaros is one of the largest in the known world, and the market that is held there is the primary point of exchange for the agricultural products of the grasslands and the manufactured goods that are imported from the coast and the interior. The [centaurs](/content/centaurs) of the surrounding plains are regular visitors to the market, and the trade between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is the lifeblood of the city's commerce.

## Significance

Avaros is the agricultural heart of Aethelar, and the grain that is produced, stored, and traded in the city feeds a significant portion of the continent. The city is the largest and most prosperous settlement of the central grasslands, and it is the primary member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), the alliance of the grassland cities and the pastoral peoples. The prosperity of Avaros is a direct consequence of the fertility of the land and the industry of its people, and the city is a symbol of the agricultural wealth that is the foundation of the economy of Aethelar.
