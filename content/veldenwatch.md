## Overview

Veldenwatch is the Morvind-side town of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), situated on the northern shore of the strait where it connects the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) to the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The town is one of the two principal settlements that govern the strait, and its position on the Morvind shore gives it a strategic and a commercial significance that is disproportionate to its modest size. Veldenwatch is the primary point of Morvind-side pilotage and toll collection, and its harbour is the first or the last port of call for vessels navigating the strait.

## The Strait and the Pilotage

The strait that Veldenwatch commands is the only passage between the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and the waters that flow through it are among the most treacherous in the known world. The currents are strong and unpredictable, the channel is narrow, and the tidal range is considerable, and the pilots of Veldenwatch are specialists who are trained from youth in the particular conditions of the strait. The pilotage service is the primary function of the town, and the pilots of Veldenwatch are organised into a guild that regulates training, sets fees, and maintains the standards of the profession. The guild maintains a school where the young pilots learn the currents, the rocks, and the eddies of the strait, and the training period lasts several years before a pilot is permitted to guide a vessel through the passage alone.

The harbour of Veldenwatch is a deep, sheltered cove that is cut into the rocky shore of the strait, and the approach is marked by lighthouses that are among the oldest on the strait. The quays line the inner shore of the cove, and the warehouses, the pilotage offices, and the toll houses that serve the strait trade are situated along the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels that are waiting for a pilot, for the tide to turn, or for the weather to clear, and the town is a gathering point for the mariners and the merchants who travel the strait. The anchorage outside the cove can accommodate a considerable number of vessels, and during the busiest periods of the trading season the roadstead is filled with ships that lie at anchor while they await their turn for pilotage.

The strait is at its most dangerous during the winter months, when the storms that sweep across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) drive strong winds and heavy seas through the channel. The pilots of Veldenwatch are reluctant to take a vessel through the strait during the worst weather, and the harbour of the town serves as a refuge for vessels that are unwilling to risk the passage until conditions improve. The winter months are a quieter period for the town, but the income that is accumulated during the trading season is sufficient to sustain the community through the leaner months.

## Trade and the Compact

The trade of Veldenwatch is dominated by the tolls and the pilotage fees that are collected from the vessels that pass through the strait, and the town is a principal beneficiary of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), the joint governance of the strait towns that regulates passage and divides the revenue between the two shores. The compact is the foundation of the town's prosperity, and the governance of the strait is the primary occupation of the officials and the merchants of Veldenwatch.

The town is also a centre for the repair and the supply of vessels that have navigated the strait, and the shipyards and the chandleries that line the harbour are a significant part of the local economy. The vessels that pass through the strait are often in need of repair, and the shipwrights of Veldenwatch are skilled in the rapid and the reliable maintenance that the strait trade demands. The [dwarves](/content/dwarves) of the Morvind interior maintain agents in Veldenwatch to manage the flow of metalwork through the strait, and their counting houses are among the most substantial buildings on the waterfront.

## The Town

Veldenwatch is a compact, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the strait trade has brought, with substantial buildings of stone, well-paved streets, and a commercial district that is oriented toward the harbour. The buildings rise three and four storeys high, their upper floors projecting over the narrow streets to maximise the use of space within the confined walls. The population of Veldenwatch is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people identify more closely with the strait and its trade than with the interior of Morvind, and their loyalties are shaped by the sea and the commerce it brings. The town is walled, and the fortifications are maintained by the compact, for the strait is a point of strategic importance and the defence of the passage is a responsibility that the member towns share.

The social life of Veldenwatch is dominated by the pilotage guild and the merchant community, and the two groups are the most influential elements of the town's society. The pilots are organised into families that have practised the profession for generations, and the guild is governed by a council of senior pilots who oversee the training, the discipline, and the conduct of the members. The merchants of Veldenwatch are chiefly agents of the trading houses of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and their counting houses are the points of contact between the town and the commercial networks of the wider world.

## Significance

Veldenwatch is the primary Morvind-side settlement of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), and its role in the pilotage and the toll collection of the strait trade is a significant component of the economy of the strait region. The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), and its position on the northern shore of the strait gives it a strategic and a commercial importance that is disproportionate to its modest size. The prosperity of Veldenwatch is built upon the trade that flows through the strait, and the town is a key point in the trade network that connects Morvind to the wider world. The pilots of Veldenwatch are among the most skilled mariners in the known world, and their expertise is a resource that cannot be replaced, for the strait is a passage that no vessel can navigate safely without their guidance.
