## Overview

The Southern Reach is a mysterious and poorly charted political entity of far southern [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), encompassing the lands that lie beyond the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh) and the southernmost reaches of the continent. The Southern Reach is the least known of any political entity in the known world, and its existence is attested chiefly through the accounts of the nomadic peoples of the deep desert and the rare expeditions that have ventured into the far south and returned. The Southern Reach is a land of rumour and legend, and its people, its governance, and its way of life are the subject of speculation rather than record.

## The Far South

The far south of Sahrana is a region that lies beyond the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh), and its geography is almost entirely unknown to the peoples of the northern coast and the continents beyond. The accounts of the nomadic peoples who have travelled to the edge of the known desert speak of a land that changes as one travels south, where the dunes give way to rocky plateaus and the climate becomes cooler and more temperate. Rumours persist of fertile highlands and even of inland seas in the deep south, but no expedition has returned with confirmation, and the peoples of the northern coast regard the far south as a land of legend.

The Southern Reach, if it exists as the accounts suggest, is situated in these poorly charted lands, and its territory is said to encompass the fertile highlands and the inland seas that are rumoured to lie beyond the desert. The nature of the political entity is unknown, and the accounts of the nomads and the explorers describe it variously as a kingdom, a confederation, or a loose association of settled communities, and the reliability of these accounts is difficult to assess.

## The Accounts and the Rumours

The accounts of the Southern Reach that have reached the northern coast are fragmentary and often contradictory, and they are drawn from the oral traditions of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/kharesh-tribes) and the reports of the few expeditions that have ventured into the far south. The nomads speak of a people who inhabit the fertile highlands of the deep south, a people who are settled and agricultural, and who have established communities that are larger and more permanent than the oases and the upland settlements of the Kharesh. The expeditions that have returned from the far south report a land of surprising abundance, where the climate is milder than the desert and the water is more plentiful, but the details of these accounts are inconsistent and the expeditions have not been sufficient to establish a reliable picture of the region.

The [salawa](/content/salawa) of the deep desert are the most frequent visitors to the far south, and their oral traditions contain the most detailed accounts of the Southern Reach, though these accounts are known to the northern peoples only through the reports of the salawa who have travelled to the coast. The salawa speak of a people who are distinct from themselves and from the humans of the northern coast, and who have established a political entity that governs the fertile highlands and the inland seas of the deep south. The reliability of these accounts is debated by the scholars of the northern coast, and the question of whether the Southern Reach is a real political entity or a legend that has grown from the rumour of fertile lands beyond the desert remains unresolved.

## Significance

The Southern Reach is the most mysterious political entity in the known world, and its significance lies chiefly in the fact of its obscurity, for the unknown lands of the far south are a source of speculation and wonder for the peoples of the northern continents. The Southern Reach represents the limit of the known world, the boundary beyond which the knowledge of the chroniclers and the naturalists does not extend, and its existence, whether real or legendary, is a reminder that the world is larger and more various than the records of its settled peoples can convey. The significance of the Southern Reach lies in its mystery, and the entity is a symbol of the unknown and the uncharted that lies beyond the boundaries of the known world.
