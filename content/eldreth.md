## Overview

Eldreth is the wealthiest city of the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and the principal hub of trade across the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). It stands on a broad, sheltered harbour at the mouth of a river that connects the coast to the interior, and its position at the meeting of sea and land has made it the most prosperous and most cosmopolitan settlement in the known world. The city is the eastern terminus of the overland routes that cross the continent and the northern terminus of the maritime routes that connect [Sahrana](/content/sahrana) to the northern continents, and the goods that pass through its harbours and warehouses are the foundation of its wealth and its influence.

## The Harbour and Trade

The harbour of Eldreth is one of the finest natural anchorages on the eastern coast, a deep, sheltered bay that can accommodate the largest vessels and the busiest fleets. The quays extend for nearly a mile along the waterfront, and they are lined with warehouses, counting houses, and the offices of the merchants who manage the flow of goods through the port. The volume of trade that passes through Eldreth is greater than that of any other city, and the harbour is seldom quiet, for the ships of Aethelar, Sahrana, and the islands arrive and depart at all hours and in all seasons.

The trade of Eldreth is dominated by the exchange of northern and southern goods. Grain, cloth, iron, and the crafted metalwork of [Morvind](/content/morvind) arrive overland and by sea, and they are exchanged for the spices, dyes, precious stones, and rare fabrics of Sahrana. The merchants of Eldreth are among the most skilled and the most wealthy in the known world, and their operations extend from the harbour to the farthest reaches of the trade network, funding voyages, caravans, and the construction of the roads and warehouses that sustain the commerce of the continent.

## The City

Eldreth is a large and well-built city, and its architecture reflects the wealth that has flowed through it for centuries. The public buildings are constructed of light-coloured stone, with tiled roofs and shaded courtyards that provide relief from the summer sun. The streets are broad and paved, and they are laid out in a pattern that reflects the growth of the city from its original harbour-side core to the extensive suburbs that now surround it. The walls of the city are high and well-maintained, for the wealth of Eldreth has attracted the attention of hostile powers in the past, and the city has been besieged more than once in its long history.

The population of Eldreth is the most diverse of any city in the known world. [Humans](/content/humans) are the majority, but [elves](/content/elves), [dwarves](/content/dwarves), and [gnomes](/content/gnomes) are present in significant numbers, drawn by the opportunities for trade and the relative tolerance of the city. The streets and markets of Eldreth are a meeting point of peoples and traditions from every continent, and the exchange of goods is matched by an exchange of ideas and knowledge that has made the city a centre of learning as well as of commerce.

## Significance

Eldreth is the commercial heart of the known world, and its prosperity is the engine of the economy of Aethelar and beyond. The trade that flows through its harbour connects the three continents and the islands, and the wealth that accumulates in the city funds the construction of roads, the building of ships, and the maintenance of the maritime and overland routes that sustain the commerce of the world. The city is the capital of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/kingdom-of-aldreth), and its influence extends far beyond its walls, shaping the politics and the economy of the eastern coast and the continent as a whole.
