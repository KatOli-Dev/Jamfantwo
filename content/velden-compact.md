## Overview

The Velden Compact is the joint governance of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden) towns, encompassing the settlements on both the Morvind and the Aethelaran shores of the strait. The compact is not a kingdom but a commercial and a regulatory agreement, and its purpose is to ensure the safe and the efficient passage of vessels through the strait, the fair collection of tolls and pilotage fees, and the maintenance of the navigation aids and the pilotage services that are essential to the strait trade. The Velden Compact is one of the most effective and the most enduring political institutions in the known world, and its governance of the strait has sustained the trade between the two oceans for many centuries.

## The Strait and the Member Towns

The [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden) is the only passage between the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and all vessels that travel between the two must pass through these waters. The compact governs the towns on both shores of the strait, and its members include [Veldenwatch](/content/veldenwatch) on the Morvind side and the Aethelaran towns on the opposite shore. [Hafnir](/content/hafnir) and [Torshavn](/content/torshavn) are associated members, situated near the strait on the Morvind coast, and their trade is connected to the strait through the compact.

The compact is governed by a joint council of representatives from each member town, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the pilotage, the toll collection, the navigation aids, and the defence of the strait. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the compact has the power to regulate the passage of vessels, to set the tolls and the pilotage fees, and to dispatch a collective force in times of need. The governance of the compact is a model of co-operation between the settlements of two different continents, and the council is among the most effective and the most respected political institutions in the known world.

## Trade and the Pilotage

The trade that flows through the strait is the commerce of two oceans, and the goods that pass through the channel include the metalwork of [Morvind](/content/morvind), the grain and the cloth of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and the spices and the dyes of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). The tolls and the pilotage fees that are collected by the compact are the primary source of revenue for the member towns, and the fair and the efficient collection of these tolls is the foundation of the compact's prosperity. The compact ensures that the tolls are collected uniformly and that the passage of vessels is not delayed or obstructed, for the trade that flows through the strait is vital to the economies of all three continents.

The pilotage service is the other great function of the compact, and the pilots of the strait are trained and regulated by the compact's guild. The pilots are drawn from both shores and from the families that have navigated the strait for generations, and their knowledge is a closely guarded inheritance, passed from parent to child and refined by each generation's experience. The compact ensures that the pilotage service is available to all vessels that require it, and that the fees are fair and the standards are maintained.

## The People and the Significance

The population of the strait towns is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the strait identify more closely with the channel and its trade than with the inland nations that claim their allegiance. The communities on both shores share a common identity as the people of the strait, and their co-operation in the governance and the defence of the channel has created a bond that transcends the political division between the two continents. The [dwarves](/content/dwarves) of Morvind maintain agents in the strait towns to manage the flow of metalwork through the channel, and a few [gnomes](/content/gnomes) have established themselves as craftsmen and merchants of particular skill.

The Velden Compact is the primary governance of the most important waterway in the known world, and its fair and efficient regulation of the strait trade is a critical component of the economy of all three continents. The compact's co-operation between the settlements of two different continents is a model of the kind of accommodation that has sustained the strait trade for many centuries, and its endurance and its effectiveness are a testament to the power of shared interest and mutual respect. The significance of the Velden Compact lies in its role as the guardian of the strait and the regulator of the trade that flows through it, and the compact is a symbol of the co-operation and the commerce that connect the continents through the waters of the world.
