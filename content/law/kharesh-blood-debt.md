## The Obligation of Compensation and Honour

The Kharesh Blood Debt is the customary law of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) that governs the payment of compensation for killings, injuries, and other serious offences against the person. The blood debt is the most important institution of the customary law of the desert, for it is the means by which the social order is maintained and the cycle of revenge is prevented in a society that has no formal courts or enforcement institutions.

The blood debt is not a punishment in the sense that the settled kingdoms understand punishment. It is an obligation that arises from the commission of a serious offence, requiring the offender or the offender's kindred to pay compensation to the victim or the victim's kindred. The payment of the blood debt restores the balance that has been disturbed by the offence, and it allows the victim's kindred to accept the compensation in place of seeking revenge.

## The Origins of the Blood Debt

The blood debt has its origins in the earliest traditions of the nomadic peoples of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) desert, before the formation of the confederation. In those times, the killing of a member of one kindred by a member of another kindred would typically lead to a cycle of revenge killings that could continue for generations. The blood debt was developed as a means of breaking this cycle, allowing the victim's kindred to accept compensation in place of blood vengeance.

The elders of the kindreds, recognising that the cycle of revenge was destructive to the community as a whole, established the principle that the killing of a person created an obligation on the part of the killer's kindred to pay compensation to the victim's kindred. The amount of the compensation was determined by the elders, taking into account the status of the victim, the circumstances of the killing, and the means of the killer's kindred.

The principle of the blood debt was extended over time to cover other serious offences, including injuries that caused permanent disability, the violation of the code of hospitality, and the theft of livestock or other valuable property. The blood debt became the foundation of the customary law of the desert, the means by which the social order was maintained and the rights of the members of the tribes were protected.

## The Payment of the Blood Debt

The payment of the blood debt is the responsibility of the offender's kindred, not merely the offender alone. The entire kindred shares the obligation to pay the compensation, reflecting the principle that the kindred is collectively responsible for the actions of its members. The kindred that fails to pay the blood debt is regarded as having failed in its collective responsibility, and the consequences of the failure may extend to all members of the kindred.

The payment is made to the victim's kindred, and the compensation is distributed among the members of the kindred according to the decisions of the elders. The compensation is typically paid in livestock, but it may also be paid in goods, in services, or in the transfer of rights to water sources or grazing grounds.

The payment of the blood debt is accompanied by a ceremony at which the elders of the affected kindreds gather to witness the payment and to declare the obligation fulfilled.
