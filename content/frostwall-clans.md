## Overview

The Frostwall Clans are the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) clans of the deep interior of [Morvind](/content/morvind), encompassing the remote mountain settlements and the fortress-cities that are situated in the most rugged and inaccessible region of the continent. The clans are not a unified kingdom but a confederation of independent dwarf clans that are united by their common interest in the defence of the deep interior and the maintenance of the routes that connect the interior ranges to the coast. The Frostwall Clans are the guardians of the most remote and the most demanding region of Morvind, and their hardihood and their martial tradition are the foundation of their significance.

## The Deep Interior and the Clans

The deep interior of Morvind is the most remote and the most rugged region of the continent, and the Frostwall Clans are the dwarven communities that inhabit this region. The territory of the clans encompasses the highest peaks, the deepest valleys, and the most isolated plateaus of the interior ranges, and the settlements of the clans are situated in locations that are too remote or too difficult for any other people to claim. [Grimhold](/content/grimhold) is the principal fortress-city of the clans, and [Ravenscrag](/content/ravenscrag) is the most remote of the clan settlements, situated in a far valley of the interior where the approaches are the most difficult.

The clans are organised by family and by territory, and each clan is a self-governing entity that manages its own affairs while contributing to the collective defence of the deep interior. The [Frostwall Clans](/content/frostwall-clans) are the most martial of the dwarven peoples of Morvind, for the deep interior is a region where the threat from the wild peoples of the mountains and the steppes is a constant concern, and the defence of the interior is the primary function of the clans.

## The Fortress and the Defence

The defence of the deep interior is the primary concern of the Frostwall Clans, and the fortress of [Grimhold](/content/grimhold) is the principal instrument of this defence. The fortress is carved into the rock of a great peak that dominates the surrounding ranges, and its walls and towers command every approach to the deep interior. The garrison of Grimhold is among the largest and the most professional in the dwarven world, and the soldiers of the fortress are the guardians of the routes that connect the interior to the coast. The wild peoples of the deep interior, including the [orcs](/content/orcs) and the [lupines](/content/lupines) of the cold steppes, are a constant presence in the region, and the relationship between the dwarves and the wild peoples is one of mutual wariness.

The routes that pass through the deep interior are the only overland connections between the eastern and western portions of the Morvind ranges, and their maintenance is a vital function of the Frostwall Clans. The routes are navigable only during the summer months, when the snow retreats and the passes become passable, and the clans are responsible for the clearing and the maintenance of the routes during this period.

## Governance and the Council

The Frostwall Clans are governed by a council of clan chiefs that meets at [Grimhold](/content/grimhold), and the council is the primary political institution of the deep interior. The decisions of the council are made by consensus, and the governance of the clans is based on the accumulated wisdom of the chiefs and the martial tradition that is the heritage of the dwarven people of the interior. The council co-ordinates the defence of the interior, the maintenance of the routes, and the management of the mining operations that are the economic foundation of the clans.

## Significance

The Frostwall Clans are the guardians of the deep interior of Morvind, and their defence of the routes and the passes that connect the interior to the coast is a critical function that has sustained the dwarven presence in the most remote region of the continent for centuries. The clans' martial tradition and their hardihood are the foundation of their significance, and their fortress at [Grimhold](/content/grimhold) is a symbol of the dwarven commitment to the defence of the interior. The significance of the Frostwall Clans lies in their endurance and their resilience in the most demanding environment in Morvind, and the clans are a testament to the capacity of the dwarven people to establish and to maintain secure settlements in the most remote and the most difficult regions of the known world.
