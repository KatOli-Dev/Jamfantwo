## Overview

The Frostwall Clans are the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) clans of the deep interior of [Morvind](/content/morvind), encompassing the remote mountain settlements and the fortress-cities that are situated in the most rugged and inaccessible region of the continent. The clans are not a unified kingdom but a confederation of independent dwarf clans that are united by their common interest in the defence of the deep interior and the maintenance of the routes that connect the interior ranges to the coast. The Frostwall Clans are the guardians of the most remote and the most demanding region of Morvind, and their hardihood and their martial tradition are the foundation of their significance.

## The Deep Interior and the Clans

The deep interior of Morvind is the most remote and the most rugged region of the continent, and the Frostwall Clans are the dwarven communities that inhabit this region. The territory of the clans encompasses the highest peaks, the deepest valleys, and the most isolated plateaus of the interior ranges, and the settlements of the clans are situated in locations that are too remote or too difficult for any other people to claim. [Grimhold](/content/grimhold) is the principal fortress-city of the clans, and [Ravenscrag](/content/ravenscrag) is the most remote of the clan settlements, situated in a far valley of the interior where the approaches are the most difficult.

The clans are organised by family and by territory, and each clan is a self-governing entity that manages its own affairs while contributing to the collective defence of the deep interior. The [Frostwall Clans](/content/frostwall-clans) are the most martial of the dwarven peoples of Morvind, for the deep interior is a region where the threat from the wild peoples of the mountains and the steppes is a constant concern, and the defence of the interior is the primary function of the clans.

The individual clans are identified by the names of their founding families and the territories that they inhabit, and the members of each clan are bound by the ties of kinship and the shared responsibility for the defence of the clan's territory. The clans maintain their own fortifications, their own mines, and their own forges, and the self-sufficiency of each clan is a principle that is valued as highly as the collective defence. The clans are known by the distinctive patterns and the devices that they bear on their shields and their banners, and the identification of a dwarf by clan is the first and the most important fact of dwarven social life in the interior.

## The Fortress and the Defence

The defence of the deep interior is the primary concern of the Frostwall Clans, and the fortress of [Grimhold](/content/grimhold) is the principal instrument of this defence. The fortress is carved into the rock of a great peak that dominates the surrounding ranges, and its walls and towers command every approach to the deep interior. The garrison of Grimhold is among the largest and the most professional in the dwarven world, and the soldiers of the fortress are the guardians of the routes that connect the interior to the coast. The wild peoples of the deep interior, including the [orcs](/content/orcs) and the [lupines](/content/lupines) of the cold steppes, are a constant presence in the region, and the relationship between the dwarves and the wild peoples is one of mutual wariness.

The routes that pass through the deep interior are the only overland connections between the eastern and western portions of the Morvind ranges, and their maintenance is a vital function of the Frostwall Clans. The routes are navigable only during the summer months, when the snow retreats and the passes become passable, and the clans are responsible for the clearing and the maintenance of the routes during this period. The winter snows close the passes for many months, and the deep interior is effectively isolated from the coast during this period, and the clans must rely on their own resources and their own stores to sustain themselves through the winter.

The martial tradition of the Frostwall Clans is the most developed of any dwarven people, for the demands of the deep interior require a level of military readiness that is not necessary in the more settled regions of Morvind. Every dwarf of the clans is trained in the use of arms from an early age, and the militia of each clan is a standing force that can be mobilised at short notice. The soldiers of the clans are equipped with the weapons and the armour that are produced in the forges of the interior, and the quality of the dwarven metalwork is a significant advantage in the defence of the passes and the valleys.

## Governance and the Council

The Frostwall Clans are governed by a council of clan chiefs that meets at [Grimhold](/content/grimhold), and the council is the primary political institution of the deep interior. The decisions of the council are made by consensus, and the governance of the clans is based on the accumulated wisdom of the chiefs and the martial tradition that is the heritage of the dwarven people of the interior. The council co-ordinates the defence of the interior, the maintenance of the routes, and the management of the mining operations that are the economic foundation of the clans.

The council convenes at regular intervals, and the chiefs of the clans travel to Grimhold for the council sessions, which are occasions of ceremony and social gathering as well as governance. The council sessions are attended by the elders and the warriors of the clans, and the proceedings include the recitation of the histories and the traditions of the clans, the resolution of disputes between the clans, and the planning of the collective defence. The council is the most important institution of the deep interior, and the decisions that are made at the council sessions are binding on all the clans.

## Economy and the Mining

The economy of the Frostwall Clans is based on the mining of the minerals that are found in the interior ranges, and the ores that are extracted from the deep veins of the mountains include iron, copper, silver, and the rarer metals that are valued by the smiths of the dwarven world. The mining operations are conducted by the individual clans on their own territories, and the ores that are extracted are transported to the forges of the interior, where they are worked into the weapons, the tools, and the other products that are used by the clans and traded to the coast.

The trade between the Frostwall Clans and the coast is managed through the routes that connect the interior to the coastal settlements, and the metalwork and the stone of the interior are exchanged for the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the clans cannot produce for themselves. The trade is conducted during the summer months, when the passes are open, and the caravans that travel the routes are guarded by the soldiers of the clans, for the wild peoples of the mountains are a constant threat to the traffic.

## Significance

The Frostwall Clans are the guardians of the deep interior of Morvind, and their defence of the routes and the passes that connect the interior to the coast is a critical function that has sustained the dwarven presence in the most remote region of the continent for centuries. The clans' martial tradition and their hardihood are the foundation of their significance, and their fortress at [Grimhold](/content/grimhold) is a symbol of the dwarven commitment to the defence of the interior. The significance of the Frostwall Clans lies in their endurance and their resilience in the most demanding environment in Morvind, and the clans are a testament to the capacity of the dwarven people to establish and to maintain secure settlements in the most remote and the most difficult regions of the known world.

The Frostwall Clans are a critical component of the dwarven civilisation of Morvind, for their defence of the interior ranges protects the flanks of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim) and the other dwarven realms of the continent, and their mining operations contribute to the mineral wealth that is the foundation of the dwarven economy. The clans are respected and valued by the other dwarven peoples of Morvind, and their martial tradition and their hardihood are recognised as a vital element of the collective strength of the dwarven nation.
