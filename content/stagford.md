## Overview

Stagford is a central city of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at a major crossing of one of the principal rivers of the central grasslands. The city stands where the river is at its narrowest, and the bridge that spans the water at this point is the finest and the most heavily trafficked in the central plains. Stagford is a prosperous, well-positioned city, and its control of the river crossing has made it a significant node in the trade network of the grasslands, for the roads that cross the river at this point connect the northern and southern portions of the plain.

## The River and the Bridge

The river that flows past Stagford is one of the great waterways of the central grasslands, and the crossing at Stagford is the most important point of passage on the river, for the bridge that spans the water is the only stone bridge on the lower reaches, and the ford that preceded it has been a crossing point since the earliest days of human settlement in the region. The bridge is a substantial structure, wide enough for two wagons to pass, and it is supported by piers that are sunk deep into the riverbed, a feat of engineering that was accomplished with the assistance of [dwarven](/content/dwarves) stonemasons.

The tolls on the bridge are a significant source of the city's revenue, and the traffic that crosses the river at Stagford includes the grain wagons of the agricultural regions, the trading caravans that connect the northern and southern plains, and the livestock that is driven to the markets of the south. The river is also navigable, and the barges that carry goods up and down the waterway call at the wharves of Stagford, where the river trade and the road trade meet and exchange.

## Trade and the City

The trade of Stagford is a mixture of the agricultural products of the surrounding plain and the manufactured goods that are imported from the coast and the interior. The city is a centre for the livestock trade, and the stockyards of Stagford are among the largest in the grasslands, for the cattle and the sheep that are raised on the pasture of the plain are gathered at the city for sale and for transport to the markets of the coast. The name of the city is derived from the stags that were once driven across the ford in great numbers, and the deer of the grasslands are still a valued commodity in the markets of Stagford.

The city itself is well-built and prosperous, with broad streets and substantial buildings that reflect the wealth of the surrounding agricultural region. The market square is the centre of the city, and the market that is held there is a weekly affair that draws farmers, traders, and craftsmen from the surrounding country. The population of Stagford is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a community of [centaurs](/content/centaurs) who are regular visitors to the market and the stockyards.

## Significance

Stagford is the primary river crossing of the central grasslands, and its bridge and its roads connect the northern and southern portions of the plain. The city is a significant member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), and its trade in grain, livestock, and manufactured goods is a valuable component of the economy of the grasslands. The prosperity of Stagford is built upon its position at the crossing, and the city has grown and thrived on the traffic that flows over its bridge and along its roads.
