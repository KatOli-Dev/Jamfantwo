## Overview

Torshavn is a southeastern port of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated on the coast near the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden) where the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) meets the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea). The town is the southernmost of the Morvind coastal settlements, and its position near the strait gives it a role in the strait trade that is secondary to [Veldenwatch](/content/veldenwatch) but nonetheless significant. Torshavn is a fishing and trading port, and its harbour is a valued stopping point for vessels that are approaching or leaving the strait from the south.

## The Harbour and the Fishing

The harbour of Torshavn is a broad, open bay that is sheltered by a headland that extends from the southern end of the town, and the approach is straightforward in most weather, though the currents that run near the strait can affect the passage. The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the fishing boats and the trading vessels that are moored there are the primary assets of the town. The fishing grounds off Torshavn are productive, for the mixing of the warm waters of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) and the cold waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) near the strait creates a rich and diverse marine environment that sustains a great variety of fish.

The fishing fleet of Torshavn is the largest on the southeastern coast, and the boats venture into the waters near the strait where the fish are most abundant. The catch is landed at the harbour, salted and dried in the curing houses that line the waterfront, and exported to the inland markets and the ports of the strait. The fish of Torshavn is a valued commodity in the strait towns, where the demand for provisions is constant and the supply from the local fishing grounds is insufficient.

## Trade and the Strait

The trade of Torshavn is a mixture of fishing and the commerce that flows through the strait, and the town is a natural stopping point for vessels that are approaching the strait from the south or leaving it for the open ocean. The harbour is a staging point for the vessels that are preparing to enter the strait, and the inns, the taverns, and the chandleries that serve the mariners are a significant part of the local economy. The town is also a centre for the repair and the supply of the fishing boats and the smaller vessels that work the southeastern coast, and the boatbuilders of Torshavn are noted for their skill in the construction of sturdy, seaworthy craft.

The population of Torshavn is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people are a mixture of fishermen and traders, with a small community of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) who are involved in the metal trade and the management of the overland routes that connect the port to the interior. The [merfolk](/content/merfolk) of the strait waters are occasionally encountered by the fishermen of Torshavn, and a cautious but peaceful coexistence has developed between the two peoples.

## The Town

Torshavn is a small, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the coastal tradition of Morvind, with sturdy buildings of dark stone, steep roofs, and narrow streets that are sheltered from the wind. The town is oriented toward the harbour, and the market square is situated at the head of the main quay, where the fishing trade and the strait commerce meet. The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), and its role in the strait trade is a valued supplement to its fishing economy.

## Significance

Torshavn is the southernmost port of Morvind and a valued stopping point for vessels navigating the waters near the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden). The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), and its fishing and trading economy is a modest but valuable contribution to the commerce of the strait region. The prosperity of Torshavn is built upon the richness of the waters that surround it and the traffic that passes through the nearby strait, and the community is a model of the maritime tradition that characterises the Morvind coast.
