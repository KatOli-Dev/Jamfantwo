## A Factory and Watchpost on the Southern Kharesh Coast

On the southern coast of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), within the sheltered waters of a broad bay called the Western Reach, the consul of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) maintains a small factory and watchpost known as the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) Anchorage. The anchorage was established in the years following [Kaelen's Voyage](/content/history/kaelens-voyage) and has been the principal point of contact between the people of the three continents and the Kharesh Spirits ever since. A small community of merchants and craftsmen lives here, governed by the consul in consultation with the elders of the Kharesh Spirits, a body of representatives chosen by the Spirit communities to speak for their interests in matters of trade and diplomacy.

The anchorage is one of the more distinctive outposts of the warm coast, held in particular esteem by the merchants and pilots who trade with the Kharesh. It has been the foundation of trade with the region for several centuries. The bay that shelters the anchorage is one of the few reliable harbours on the southern coast of the Kharesh, a deep and well-protected inlet where vessels can lie at anchor in safety while their crews conduct business with the Spirit communities of the interior. The position of the anchorage at the meeting point of the ocean routes and the interior trails has made it a natural centre for the exchange of goods between the three continents and the peoples who dwell beyond the coastal waters, and the trade that passes through its warehouses is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

## The Stone and Timber Compound of the Merchants

The factory is a compound of stone and timber erected by the merchants of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in the early years of trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). The buildings are arranged around a central courtyard paved with flat stones, with covered walkways connecting the various structures so that work can continue even during the heat of the day. The factory contains warehouses where the spices and hardwoods of the Kharesh are stored before shipment, workshops where the merchants prepare cargoes for the return voyage, and dwellings for the merchants and craftsmen who reside at the anchorage. The warehouses are built with thick walls and raised floors to protect the valuable spices from damp and pests, and the workshops are equipped with the tools and materials needed for the sorting, weighing, and packing of goods. The factory is governed by the consul in consultation with the elders of the Kharesh Spirits, and the consul has the power to adjudicate disputes that arise between the merchants and the Spirits. This authority is exercised with care, for the consul must balance the interests of the [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) merchants against the customs and the rights of the Spirit communities, and a decision that favours one party too heavily may disrupt the trade that is the foundation of the anchorage's prosperity.

## The Stone Building and the Sergeant's Register

The watchpost is a small stone building erected at the same time as the factory, maintained by the garrison of the Watch. It stands upon a rise of ground that commands a wide view of the bay and the approaches to the anchorage, serving as the principal point of defence. The garrison consists of a sergeant and four men, each tour lasting two months before a fresh company arrives from Khalisar to take their place. The sergeant is responsible for maintaining the watchpost, inspecting the bay for any sign of hazard or hostile approach, and keeping the register of vessels that call at the anchorage. The register records the name of each vessel, its port of origin, the nature and quantity of its cargo, and the dates of its arrival and departure. The consul relies upon this register to judge the state of trade and the volume of commerce passing through the anchorage, and a careful register is essential to the fair collection of the harbour fees that fund the maintenance of the factory and the watchpost.

## Spices, Hardwoods, and the Consul's Prices

Trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) is the principal business of the anchorage. The goods that merchants purchase from the Spirit communities include the spices that grow upon the island, notably the aromatic barks and seeds that are ground into the seasonings valued in the kitchens of the three continents, and the hardwoods produced by the interior forests, whose dense grain and rich colour make them prized by the furniture makers and the shipwrights of the coast. The goods that the merchants sell in exchange include the cloth woven in the workshops of Khalisar, the metalwork forged by the smiths of the city, and the salt harvested from the coastal pans. The trade has been the foundation of contact between the people of the three continents and the Kharesh Spirits, and the relationship that has grown from this exchange is one of mutual benefit, for the Spirits receive the manufactured goods they cannot produce for themselves, while the merchants gain access to the rare and valuable products of the Kharesh interior.

The trade is regulated by the consul in consultation with the elders of the Kharesh Spirits, and the consul has the power to set the prices at which goods are exchanged. The prices are reviewed at the beginning of each trading season and adjusted according to the supply of spices and hardwoods, the demand in the markets of Khalisar, and the cost of the manufactured goods that the merchants bring. The elders of the Spirits are consulted on every change, and the consul may not alter the schedule of prices without their consent.

## The Stone and Timber Buildings of the Residents

The quarters were erected at the same time as the factory and are maintained by the consul. These small stone and timber buildings provide shelter for the merchants and craftsmen who live at the anchorage and house the garrison that maintains the watchpost. The quarters are simple but well-built, with thick walls that keep out the heat of the day and shuttered windows that can be closed against the dust storms that occasionally sweep across the bay. Each building contains a common room with a hearth, sleeping chambers for the residents, and a small storeroom for personal belongings. The quarters are furnished with supplies delivered by the vessels that call at the anchorage, including bedding, cooking utensils, and the staple foods that the residents require.

## The Foundation of Trade with the Kharesh Spirits

The Kharesh Anchorage is one of the more distinctive outposts of the warm coast and the foundation of trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). The factory, watchpost, quarters, and the trade they support together form the inheritance that the anchorage has bequeathed to the present age. The anchorage is held in particular memory by the merchants and pilots who rely upon it, and they mark the anniversaries of the founding with ceremonies attended by the consul and the elders of the Kharesh Spirits.
