## Overview

Upon the southern coast of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), within the sheltered waters of a broad bay that the chroniclers of the academies have named the Western Reach, the consul of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) maintains a small factory and a watchpost that the chroniclers have named the Kharesh Anchorage. The anchorage was established in the years following the [Discovery of the Kharesh](/content/history/discovery-of-the-kharesh), and the anchorage has been the principal point of contact between the people of the three continents and the [Spirits of the Kharesh](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-spirits-of-the-kharesh) ever since. The anchorage is the home of a small community of merchants and craftsmen, and the community is governed by the consul in consultation with the elders of the [Spirits of the Kharesh](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-spirits-of-the-kharesh).

The anchorage is one of the more distinctive of the outposts of the warm coast, and the anchorage is held in particular esteem by the merchants and the pilots who trade with the island. The chronicler notes that the anchorage has been the foundation of the trade with the Kharesh for several centuries, and the anchorage is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

## The Factory

The factory is a compound of stone and timber that the merchants of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) erected in the early years of the trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and the factory has been maintained by the consul ever since. The factory contains the warehouses in which the spices and the woods of the Kharesh are stored, the workshops in which the merchants prepare the cargoes for the return voyage, and the dwellings in which the merchants and the craftsmen live. The chronicler notes that the factory is one of the more substantial of the outposts of the warm coast, and the factory is held in particular esteem by the merchants and the craftsmen who live within it.

The factory is governed by the consul in consultation with the elders of the [Spirits of the Kharesh](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-spirits-of-the-kharesh), and the consul has the power to adjudicate the disputes that arise between the merchants and the Spirits. The chronicler notes that the adjudication has been the foundation of the careful management of the trade, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of both parties.

## The Watchpost

The watchpost is a small stone building that the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) erected at the same time as the factory, and the watchpost has been maintained by the garrison of the Watch ever since. The watchpost stands upon a rise of ground that commands a wide view of the bay and the approaches to the anchorage, and the watchpost is the principal point of defence of the anchorage. The chronicler notes that the watchpost is one of the more substantial of the outposts of the warm coast, and the watchpost is held in particular esteem by the garrison that maintains it.

The garrison of the watchpost consists of a sergeant and four men, and the garrison holds the watchpost for a tour of two months. The sergeant is responsible for the maintenance of the watchpost, the inspection of the bay, and the register of the vessels that call at the anchorage. The chronicler notes that the sergeant has a duty to keep the register carefully, for the register is the evidence by which the consul judges the state of the trade and the navigation of the bay.

## The Trade

The trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) is the principal business of the anchorage, and the trade has been the foundation of the prosperity of the merchants of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) since the [Discovery of the Kharesh](/content/history/discovery-of-the-kharesh). The principal goods that the merchants of the three continents purchase are the spices that grow upon the island and the hardwoods that the forests of the interior produce. The principal goods that the merchants sell are the cloth, the metalwork, and the salt that the warm coast produces. The chronicler notes that the trade has been the foundation of the contact between the people of the three continents and the [Spirits of the Kharesh](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-spirits-of-the-kharesh), and the contact is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of both parties.

The trade is regulated by the consul in consultation with the elders of the [Spirits of the Kharesh](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-spirits-of-the-kharesh), and the consul has the power to set the prices at which the goods are exchanged. The chronicler notes that the regulation has been the foundation of the careful management of the trade, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of both parties.

## The Quarters

The quarters of the anchorage were erected by the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) at the same time as the factory, and the quarters are maintained by the consul. The quarters are small buildings of stone and timber that provide shelter for the merchants and the craftsmen who live at the anchorage, and the quarters are the home of the garrison that maintains the watchpost. The chronicler notes that the quarters are the foundation of the safety of the anchorage, and the quarters are held in high esteem by the merchants and the craftsmen who live within them.

The quarters are furnished with the supplies that the merchants and the garrison require, and the supplies are delivered by the vessels that call at the anchorage. The chronicler notes that the delivery has been the foundation of the careful management of the anchorage, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of both parties.

## Significance

The Kharesh Anchorage is one of the more distinctive of the outposts of the warm coast, and the anchorage is the foundation of the trade with the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). The factory, the watchpost, the quarters, the trade, and the consul are the inheritance that the anchorage has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that the anchorage is held in particular memory by the merchants and the pilots who rely upon it, and the merchants and the pilots mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies. The ceremonies are among the most important of the public observances of the warm coast, and the ceremonies are attended by the consul and the elders of the [Spirits of the Kharesh](/content/belief/religion/polytheist/the-spirits-of-the-kharesh).
