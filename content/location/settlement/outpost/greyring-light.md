## Overview

At the western entrance to the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden), where the strait opens into the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean), the council of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) maintains a small lighthouse and a watchpost that the chroniclers of the academies have named Greyring Light. The lighthouse was erected in the early generations of the [Long Peace of the Twin Cities](/content/history/long-peace-of-the-twin-cities), and the lighthouse has been maintained by the [Standing Watch of the Northern Roads](/content/government/local/standing-watch-of-the-northern-roads) ever since. The lighthouse is the home of a small garrison of the Watch, and the garrison maintains the light and oversees the navigation of the strait.

The lighthouse is one of the more important of the outposts of the cold north, and the lighthouse is held in particular esteem by the pilots and the merchants who rely upon the light for the safe navigation of the strait. The chronicler notes that the lighthouse has been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the strait for several centuries, and the lighthouse is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden).

## The Lighthouse

The lighthouse is a stone tower of four stories, with a stout door and a stout roof, and the lighthouse is built upon a rocky islet that lies just within the western entrance to the strait. The lighthouse contains a small hearth, a small store of provisions, and the arms of the garrison. The chronicler notes that the lighthouse is one of the more substantial of the outposts of the cold north, and the lighthouse is held in particular esteem by the garrison that maintains it.

The lantern room occupies the upper story, and the lantern is fitted with the great oil lamp that the lighthouse keepers have maintained since the founding of the tower. The keeper of the lighthouse maintains the lamp, trims the wicks at dusk, and signals the state of the tide by the colour of the light that he shows. The chronicler notes that the lamp has been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the strait, and the lamp is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden).

## The Garrison

The garrison of the lighthouse consists of a sergeant and four men, and the garrison holds the lighthouse for a tour of two months. The sergeant is responsible for the maintenance of the lamp, the inspection of the beacons, and the register of the vessels that pass through the strait. The chronicler notes that the sergeant has a duty to keep the register carefully, for the register is the evidence by which the magistrates of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) judge the state of the navigation of the strait.

The garrison also maintains the small boats that ferry supplies and relief crews to the lighthouse from the mainland, and the boats are the principal means of communication between the lighthouse and the town. The chronicler notes that the boats have been the foundation of the careful management of the lighthouse, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the coast.

## The Beacons

The beacons of the strait were erected by the council of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) at the same time as the lighthouse, and the beacons have been maintained by the Standing Watch ever since. The beacons stand upon the rocks and the shoals of the strait, and the beacons are the marks that the pilots use to set their course through the dangerous waters. The chronicler notes that the beacons have been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the strait, and the beacons are held in high esteem by the pilots and the merchants of every city of the coast.

The beacons are connected by a chain of signals that the garrison exchanges with the neighbouring towers upon the mainland, and the signals inform the garrison of the state of the weather and the approach of any large vessel. The chronicler notes that the signals have been the foundation of the careful management of the strait, and the signals are the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates and the merchants of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden).

## The Navigation

The waters of the strait are treacherous, and the navigation of the strait has been the subject of careful study by the pilots of the [Surveying of Heights and Distances](/content/belief/science/physical/the-surveying-of-heights-and-distances) since the founding of the tower. The pilots maintain the charts of the strait, and the pilots report the state of the channels to the harbormaster of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden). The chronicler notes that the charts have been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the strait, and the navigation is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the coast.

The pilots also oversee the pilots of the larger vessels that pass through the strait, and the pilots direct the vessels through the dangerous waters with care. The chronicler notes that the direction has been the foundation of the safety of the strait, and the direction is the measure of the careful management that the Standing Watch maintains.

## The Records

The records of the lighthouse are maintained by the sergeant of the garrison, and the records are sent to the harbormaster of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) at the end of every month. The records include the register of the vessels that pass through the strait, the readings of the barometer and the wind, and any unusual event that the garrison observed during the tour. The chronicler notes that the records are the most complete of the records of the navigation of the strait, and the records are consulted by every pilot who must judge a question of the navigation of the coast.

The records are preserved in the archives of the magistrates of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden), and the magistrates have maintained the records with care since the founding of the lighthouse. The chronicler notes that the records are the foundation of the careful management of the navigation of the strait, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the coast.

## Significance

Greyring Light is one of the more important of the outposts of the cold north, and the lighthouse is the foundation of the navigation of the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). The lighthouse, the beacons, the garrison, the charts, and the pilots are the inheritance that the lighthouse has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that the lighthouse is held in particular memory by the pilots and the merchants who rely upon it, and the pilots and the merchants mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies. The ceremonies are among the most important of the public observances of the cold north, and the ceremonies are attended by the magistrates and the principal citizens of every town of the coast.
