## Overview

Windward is a settlement on a remote island of the eastern [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), situated far from the trade routes that connect the continents and the larger islands. The island is one of the most isolated inhabited places in the known world, and its distance from the trade routes has given the settlement a character that is self-sufficient and distinct from the other island communities. Windward is small and remote, and its people are among the most self-reliant in the known world, for contact with the wider world is infrequent and the community must sustain itself on what the island and the surrounding waters provide.

## The Island and the Wind

The island on which Windward stands is a small, rugged landmass rising from the deep waters of the eastern [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). Its shores are steep and rocky, with few beaches and fewer natural harbours. The settlement is situated on the leeward side of the island, where the cliffs provide shelter from the prevailing winds that give the island its name; the harbour is a small, sheltered cove accessible only through a narrow entrance between the rocks. The wind is the dominant feature of the island's climate; the eastern Auric is a region of steady and sometimes fierce winds blowing from the east across the open ocean. The vegetation of the island is shaped by the wind; trees grow low and bent in the direction of the prevailing gusts.

The island is too small and remote to support agriculture on any significant scale. The community sustains itself through fishing and the gathering of resources the island and surrounding waters provide. The fishing grounds around the island are productive, for the deep waters of the eastern Auric sustain a variety of fish and marine life; the catch is the primary food source of the settlement. The island also provides a modest quantity of fresh water, drawn from springs emerging from the volcanic rock; the community manages the water carefully, for it is the most precious resource on the island.

## Trade and the Outside World

The trade of Windward is minimal, for the island is far from the trade routes and contact with the wider world is infrequent. The vessels calling at the harbour are few, chiefly explorers and occasional traders who venture into the remote waters of the eastern Auric. The goods exchanged at Windward include fish and marine products of the island, traded for grain, cloth, and manufactured goods the community cannot produce for itself; the trade is conducted on a small scale and at irregular intervals.

The [cyclopes](/content/species/sapient/cyclopes) of the remote islands and coastlines are the nearest neighbours of the Windward community. Occasional encounters between the islanders and the cyclopes are rare and generally distant, for the cyclopes are solitary and the Windward islanders are few. The [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) of the warm coasts and islands are occasional visitors to the harbour; trade between the two peoples is a minor but valued connection to the wider world.

The islanders have developed a tradition of self-sufficiency born of necessity. The community produces the tools, clothing, and other necessities of life from the materials the island and surrounding waters provide. The boats used for fishing are built and maintained by the islanders; the construction of boats is a skill passed from generation to generation. The cloth worn by the islanders is woven from fibres of plants growing on the island; the garments are plain and durable, suited to the wind and salt that are constant features of the island environment.

## The Settlement

Windward is a small, compact settlement whose buildings are constructed of the dark stone of the cliffs, with low profiles and thick walls that withstand the wind and salt. The buildings are clustered on the leeward slope above the harbour; the streets are narrow and sheltered, for the wind is a constant presence and the settlement is built to minimise its exposure. The population is small and entirely [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people are known for their extraordinary self-reliance and deep attachment to the remote island that is their home.

The community is governed by a council of elders drawn from the families that have inhabited the island for generations. The council oversees the management of water, the fishing grounds, and relations with the occasional visitors who call at the harbour. The governance is informal and practical, for the community is too small to require an elaborate administration; the decisions of the council are made by consensus among the elders.

## History

Windward was established by mariners blown off course during a storm who found shelter in the cove on the leeward side of the island. The initial settlement was a small camp intended as a temporary refuge, but the discovery of springs and the productivity of the fishing grounds convinced the mariners to remain; the settlement grew as the families of the founders were joined by occasional castaways and wanderers finding their way to the island. The history of Windward is a history of endurance; the community has survived the storms, droughts, and isolation that are the constant challenges of life on the remote island.

The admission of Windward to the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) was a formality arranged by the councillors of the confederation who wished to include the settlement in the records of the island communities. The participation of Windward in the confederation is nominal, for the distance between the island and the other members of the confederation is too great for regular attendance at the council; contact between Windward and the other islands is limited to the occasional vessel calling at the harbour.

## Significance

Windward is the most remote permanent settlement in the known world, and its people are the guardians of one of the most isolated inhabited places in the eastern [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The settlement is a member of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), the confederation of island communities, though its distance from the other islands means its participation is nominal. The significance of Windward lies in its survival; the settlement is a testament to the capacity of humans to endure and thrive in the most remote and demanding environments the known world can offer.

The islanders of Windward are a symbol of human capacity for endurance and self-reliance. The community that has sustained itself on the remote island for generations is a testament to the values of hardihood, independence, and attachment to the land that are the hallmark of the people of the eastern Auric. The settlement is a reminder that the known world extends to the most remote and isolated corners of the ocean, and that the human presence is not confined to the trade routes and the continental shores.
