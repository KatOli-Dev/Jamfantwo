## A Settlement on a Volcanic Island in the Auric Ocean

Tidesmeet is a settlement on a volcanic island in the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), situated at the meeting point of two oceanic currents that converge near the island and give the settlement its name. The island is one of the larger of the volcanic chain rising from the deep waters of the central Auric; its position astride the trade routes between [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) has made it a natural stopping point for vessels crossing the ocean. Tidesmeet is the largest and most important of the island settlements, and its harbour is the primary refuge and resupply point for vessels travelling the Auric trade routes.

## The Volcanic Peak, the Caldera Harbour, and the Convergent Currents

The island on which Tidesmeet stands is a volcanic peak rising from the deep water of the central [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). Its slopes are steep and rugged, covered in a thick growth of tropical vegetation sustained by warm climate and abundant rainfall. The harbour of Tidesmeet is a natural anchorage sheltered by the curve of the island's caldera; the approach is marked by a lighthouse visible for many miles across the open ocean. The harbour is deep and well-protected, able to accommodate the largest vessels travelling the Auric trade routes, though the entrance is narrow and requires careful navigation.

The convergence of two currents near the island is the source of the name Tidesmeet. The meeting of warm and cooler waters creates a zone of turbulent and unpredictable conditions that can be dangerous for vessels unfamiliar with local waters. The pilots of Tidesmeet are skilled in the navigation of the currents; their services are sought by every captain who must approach or leave the harbour, for passage through the convergent zone requires local knowledge and experience.

## The Auric Trade Routes and the Tropical Produce

Tidesmeet's trade is built upon its position astride the Auric trade routes. The settlement is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling between [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). Vessels calling at the harbour are provisioned with water, food, and materials needed for continuation of the voyage; the services the settlement provides are the foundation of its economy. Goods passing through the harbour include spices and dyes of Sahrana, grain and metalwork of Aethelar, and products of the other islands of the chain, gathered at Tidesmeet for transhipment to continental markets.

The island itself produces tropical fruits, fish, and other products of the volcanic soil and surrounding waters; these are exported to continental markets as minor but valued commodities. The fishing grounds around the island are rich, for the convergent currents sustain a diverse and abundant marine life; the catch is a significant supplement to the settlement's economy. The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the Auric waters are occasional visitors to the harbour; a cautious but peaceful coexistence has developed between the islanders and the merfolk.

The pilots of Tidesmeet are among the most skilled navigators in the Auric. Their knowledge of the convergent currents and the approaches to the harbour is a valuable asset passed from generation to generation. The pilotage service is managed by the settlement; the fees charged for pilotage are a significant source of income for the community. The pilots are held in high regard by the captains calling at the harbour; the reputation of the Tidesmeet pilots is a factor in the decision of many vessels to stop at the island rather than attempt the crossing of the convergent zone without local guidance.

## Dark Stone, Timber, and the Waterfront Warehouses

Tidesmeet is a small, well-built settlement whose architecture reflects the character of the volcanic island: buildings of dark stone and timber, broad streets, and open squares taking advantage of the sea breezes. The buildings are constructed to withstand occasional storms sweeping across the island, with thick walls and steep roofs. The settlement is oriented toward the harbour, with the market square and warehouses situated along the waterfront. The population is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a small community of traders and sailors from the continental ports.

The market square of Tidesmeet is the centre of the settlement. Goods bought and sold in the market include provisions and supplies needed by vessels calling at the harbour, fish and tropical fruits produced on the island, and goods transhipped between the continents and the other islands of the chain. The warehouses lining the waterfront are the storage points for goods awaiting transhipment, managed by the merchants and agents of continental trading houses who maintain a permanent presence in the settlement.

## From Provisioning Station to Capital of the Auric Isles

Tidesmeet was established by the first mariners who recognised the value of the natural harbour and the island's position astride the trade routes. The initial settlement was a small provisioning station serving vessels beginning to cross the Auric in the early centuries of continental trade; the community grew as the volume of trade increased and the services it provided became more elaborate. The lighthouse marking the approach to the harbour was one of the first substantial structures built; it remains one of the most important, for the convergent currents and open ocean make the approach to the island a demanding passage.

The admission of Tidesmeet to the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) was a natural development, for the settlement was the largest and most important of the island communities, and its harbour was the primary refuge for vessels travelling the ocean. The leadership of Tidesmeet in the confederation has been a factor in the coordination of island trade and the maintenance of navigation aids essential to the safety of the ocean routes. The council of the confederation meets at Tidesmeet; the settlement is the effective capital of the island communities.

## The Largest and Most Important of the Island Settlements

Tidesmeet is the largest and most important of the island settlements of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and its harbour is the primary refuge and resupply point for vessels travelling the Auric trade routes. The settlement is a member of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), the confederation of island communities, and its role in the ocean trade is a vital component of the economy of the Auric. The significance of Tidesmeet lies in its position astride the trade routes and its harbour, which is the finest and most accessible of any island in the central Auric; the settlement is a symbol of the maritime connections that link the continents through the ocean.

The role of Tidesmeet as the meeting point of continental and island trade gives it a cultural character distinct from the other settlements of the Auric. The population is a mixture of islanders and continental traders who have settled in the community; the traditions and customs of the settlement reflect the diversity of its people. The settlement is a testament to the capacity of the island peoples to sustain a prosperous and significant community in the most remote and demanding environment of the open ocean.
