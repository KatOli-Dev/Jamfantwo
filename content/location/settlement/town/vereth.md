## The Largest Settlement of the Southern Reach

Vereth is the largest settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), situated on the northern shore of the [Greater Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea) in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), beyond the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). The town is the seat of the council of the federation, and its position on the shore of the largest of the inland seas gives it a commercial and political significance disproportionate to its modest size. Vereth is the most populous and prosperous of the Southern Reach settlements, and its harbour is the centre of trade flowing across the inland seas and through the caravan routes to the north.

## The Broad Bay and the Rich Fishing Grounds

The Greater Inland Sea is the largest body of fresh water in the far south, and its shores are the most fertile and densely settled lands in the Southern Reach. The sea is navigable throughout its extent, and the boats travelling its waters are the primary means of transport and trade between the lakeside communities. The harbour of Vereth is a broad, sheltered bay cut into the northern shore of the sea; quays line the inner shore of the bay, where fishing boats and trading vessels of the inland seas are moored.

The fishing grounds of the Greater Inland Sea are rich, for the fresh waters sustain a great variety of fish that are the staple food of the lakeside communities. The fishing fleet of Vereth is the largest on the inland seas; the catch is landed at the harbour, preserved by salting and drying, and exported to the other settlements of the Southern Reach and to the northern world through the caravan routes. The preserved fish of the inland seas are among the most valued products of the south and are a significant component of the trade flowing between the Southern Reach and the north.

## The Hall of the Federation and the Local Council

Vereth is the seat of the council of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), the federation of settled communities inhabiting the shores of the inland seas and the surrounding highlands. The council meets in a hall situated in the centre of the town; the representatives of the communities gather there twice each year, in the spring and autumn, to conduct the business of the federation. The hall is the most substantial public building in Vereth, its architecture reflecting the civic traditions of the Southern Reach: thick walls of pale stone, a broad interior lit by high windows, and a council chamber arranged in a circle to reflect the equality of the communities.

The governance of Vereth itself is administered by a local council of elders drawn from the most experienced and respected members of the community; the town's internal affairs are managed independently of the federation. The local council oversees the administration of the harbour, the regulation of fishing and trade, the maintenance of the town's walls and public buildings, and the administration of justice. The governance of Vereth is a model of the civic tradition that characterises the Southern Reach, and the town's reputation for competence and fairness is recognised throughout the federation.

## The Overlap of Inland Sea and Caravan Commerce

Vereth's trade combines the commerce of the inland seas with the caravan trade connecting the Southern Reach to the northern world. Goods flowing through the harbour include the preserved fish of the inland seas, the wool and leather of the highland herds, and the medicinal herbs growing in the temperate uplands, all bound for northern markets through the caravan routes of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). Goods arriving from the north include grain, cloth, metalwork, and manufactured goods the south cannot produce for itself; the warehouses of Vereth are filled with the commerce flowing between the two worlds.

The caravans travelling the southern routes pass through [Torem](/content/location/settlement/town/torem), the gateway settlement in the highlands, before descending to the inland seas. Vereth is the primary destination for the goods the caravans carry. The merchants of Vereth are the intermediaries between the caravan trade and the lakeside commerce; the town's position at the meeting of the two routes gives it a commercial importance that is the foundation of its prosperity.

## Pale Stone, Broad Streets, and the Harbour Architecture

Vereth is a well-built, prosperous town whose architecture reflects the wealth the inland sea and caravan trade have brought: substantial buildings of pale stone, broad streets, and a commercial district oriented toward the harbour. The buildings are constructed in the southern tradition, with thick walls providing insulation against the heat of the day and the cool of the night, shaded courtyards, and high windows that channel breezes from the sea. The town is surrounded by walls of stone maintained in good order, and the gates are guarded by a modest force funded by the local council.

The population is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), a mixture of fishermen, farmers, merchants, and craftsmen. A small community of [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) is found on the shore of the inland sea; their knowledge of the waters and their long perspective on the history of the basin are valued by the human community. The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) of the deep desert are occasional visitors, arriving with the caravans or on their own travels; their presence is a reminder of the connection between the Southern Reach and the northern world.

The architecture of Vereth reflects the prosperity that the inland sea trade has brought. The buildings of the commercial district are substantial structures of pale stone, their thick walls providing insulation against the heat of the day and the cool of the night. The houses of the wealthier merchants are situated on the rising ground above the harbour, where they catch the breezes that blow across the water. The market square is a broad, paved space surrounded by the warehouses and trading houses that are the centre of the town's commerce. The harbour itself is lined with quays of dressed stone, and the fishing boats and trading vessels that are moored there are a constant presence, their masts forming a forest of spars that is visible from the surrounding hills. The fishermen of Vereth work the waters of the Greater Inland Sea, and their catch is landed daily at the harbour, where the women of the town process the fish in the salting houses that line the shore.

## The Capital of the Southern Reach

Vereth is the largest and most prosperous settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), and its role as the seat of the council and the centre of the inland sea trade is a significant component of the economy and governance of the federation. The town's position on the shore of the Greater Inland Sea gives it a commercial and political importance disproportionate to its modest size, and its prosperity is built upon the fish of the inland seas and the commerce flowing through the caravan routes. The significance of Vereth lies in its role as the capital of the Southern Reach and the centre of the civilisation of the far south.
