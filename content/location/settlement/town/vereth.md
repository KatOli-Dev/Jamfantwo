## Overview

Vereth is the largest settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), situated on the northern shore of the Greater Inland Sea in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), beyond the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). The town is the seat of the council of the federation, and its position on the shore of the largest of the inland seas gives it a commercial and a political significance that is disproportionate to its modest size. Vereth is the most populous and the most prosperous of the Southern Reach settlements, and its harbour is the centre of the trade that flows across the inland seas and through the caravan routes to the north.

## The Inland Sea and the Harbour

The Greater Inland Sea is the largest body of fresh water in the far south, and its shores are the most fertile and the most densely settled lands in the Southern Reach. The sea is navigable throughout its extent, and the boats that travel its waters are the primary means of transport and trade between the lakeside communities. The harbour of Vereth is a broad, sheltered bay that is cut into the northern shore of the sea, and the quays line the inner shore of the bay, where the fishing boats and the trading vessels of the inland seas are moored.

The fishing grounds of the Greater Inland Sea are rich, for the fresh waters sustain a great variety of fish that are the staple food of the lakeside communities. The fishing fleet of Vereth is the largest on the inland seas, and the catch is landed at the harbour, preserved by salting and drying, and exported to the other settlements of the Southern Reach and to the northern world through the caravan routes. The preserved fish of the inland seas are among the most valued products of the south, and they are a significant component of the trade that flows between the Southern Reach and the north.

## The Council and the Governance

Vereth is the seat of the council of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), the federation of the settled communities that inhabit the shores of the inland seas and the surrounding highlands. The council meets in a hall that is situated in the centre of the town, and the representatives of the communities gather there twice each year, in the spring and in the autumn, to conduct the business of the federation. The hall is the most substantial public building in Vereth, and its architecture reflects the civic traditions of the Southern Reach, with thick walls of pale stone, a broad interior that is lit by high windows, and a council chamber that is arranged in a circle to reflect the equality of the communities.

The governance of Vereth itself is administered by a local council of elders, drawn from the most experienced and the most respected members of the community, and the town's internal affairs are managed independently of the federation. The local council oversees the administration of the harbour, the regulation of the fishing and the trade, the maintenance of the town's walls and public buildings, and the administration of justice. The governance of Vereth is a model of the civic tradition that characterises the Southern Reach, and the town's reputation for competence and fairness is recognised throughout the federation.

## Trade and the Caravans

The trade of Vereth is a mixture of the commerce of the inland seas and the caravan trade that connects the Southern Reach to the northern world. The goods that flow through the harbour include the preserved fish of the inland seas, the wool and the leather of the highland herds, and the medicinal herbs that grow in the temperate uplands, all of which are bound for the northern markets through the caravan routes of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). The goods that arrive from the north include the grain, the cloth, the metalwork, and the manufactured goods that the south cannot produce for itself, and the warehouses of Vereth are filled with the commerce that flows between the two worlds.

The caravans that travel the southern routes pass through [Torem](/content/location/settlement/town/torem), the gateway settlement in the highlands, before descending to the inland seas, and Vereth is the primary destination for the goods that the caravans carry. The merchants of Vereth are the intermediaries between the caravan trade and the lakeside commerce, and the town's position at the meeting of the two routes gives it a commercial importance that is the foundation of its prosperity.

## The Town

Vereth is a well-built, prosperous town, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the inland sea and the caravan trade have brought, with substantial buildings of pale stone, broad streets, and a commercial district that is oriented toward the harbour. The buildings are constructed in the southern tradition, with thick walls that provide insulation against the heat of the day and the cool of the night, shaded courtyards, and high windows that channel the breezes from the sea. The town is surrounded by walls of stone that are maintained in good order, and the gates are guarded by a modest force that is funded by the local council.

The population of Vereth is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people are a mixture of fishermen, farmers, merchants, and craftsmen. A small community of [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) is found on the shore of the inland sea, and their knowledge of the waters and their long perspective on the history of the basin are valued by the human community. The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) of the deep desert are occasional visitors, arriving with the caravans or on their own travels, and their presence is a reminder of the connection between the Southern Reach and the northern world.

## Significance

Vereth is the largest and the most prosperous settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), and its role as the seat of the council and the centre of the inland sea trade is a significant component of the economy and the governance of the federation. The town's position on the shore of the Greater Inland Sea gives it a commercial and a political importance that is disproportionate to its modest size, and its prosperity is built upon the fish of the inland seas and the commerce that flows through the caravan routes. The significance of Vereth lies in its role as the capital of the Southern Reach and the centre of the civilisation of the far south.
