## Overview

Redhollow is a southern settlement of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated at the frontier of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) where the cultivated grasslands meet the [Emberlands](/content/location/settlement/region/emberlands). The town stands in a shallow valley where the red soil of the dry country meets the darker earth of the grasslands, and the name of the settlement is derived from the reddish hue of the ground that surrounds it. Redhollow is the smallest and the most remote of the settlements of the southern plains, and its people are hardy and self-reliant, accustomed to the demands of a life at the edge of the cultivated land.

## The Frontier

The land around Redhollow is a transition zone, where the fertility of the southern plains gives way to the aridity of the dry country, and the agriculture of the region is limited to the hardy crops that can tolerate the poor soil and the scarce water. The farms that surround the town are smaller and less productive than those of the central grasslands, but they are sufficient to sustain the community, and the people of Redhollow supplement their agriculture with the hunting and the foraging that the dry country provides. The hunters of Redhollow are skilled in the pursuit of the game that inhabits the marginal lands, and the hides and the meat of the wild animals are a supplement to the diet and the economy of the town.

The wells of Redhollow are the most valuable resource of the settlement, for the water table is deep and the supply is limited. The wells are maintained by the community, and the water is rationed during the dry season, when the level falls and the pressure drops. The construction of the wells was a feat of determination and labour, for the rock is hard and the shafts are deep, and the wells are the most significant works of the community.

The frontier that Redhollow marks is the southern limit of the settled land of Aethelar, and the town is the last outpost of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) before the dry country and the warm regions beyond. The traffic that passes through the town is light, for the routes that cross the dry country are few and the trade is modest, but the town serves as a staging point for the caravans that venture into the warm regions, and the inns and the stables of Redhollow are a valued refuge for those who travel the southern roads.

## Trade and the Town

The trade of Redhollow is limited to the products of the surrounding farms and the goods that are imported from the central grasslands. The town is a centre for the trade in the hardy livestock that is raised on the frontier, and the cattle and the goats that are bred in the region are valued for their resilience and their adaptability to the dry conditions. The wool of the frontier sheep is coarser than that of the central grasslands, but it is durable and warm, and it is a minor export that finds a market among the traders who pass through the town. The hides of the goats and the cattle are also traded, and the leather that is tanned from them is a valued product of the frontier, known for its toughness and its resistance to the wear of the dry conditions.

The inns of Redhollow are a central institution of the town, for the caravans that venture into the dry country rely on the lodging and the provisioning that the inns provide. The innkeepers of Redhollow are experienced in the needs of the caravans, and the inns are equipped with the storerooms, the water cisterns, and the stables that are required to serve the traffic of the frontier. The innkeepers are also the keepers of the news, for the caravans that pass through bring the tidings of the grasslands and the dry country, and the innkeepers of Redhollow are among the best-informed people on the southern frontier.

Redhollow is a small, rugged town, and its buildings are constructed of the red stone and the sun-baked clay that are the materials of the region, with thick walls and small windows that provide insulation against the heat and the wind. The streets are broad and dusty, and the town is oriented toward the road that connects it to the central grasslands, with the market square and the inns situated along the main street. The population of Redhollow is small and entirely [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people are known for their hardihood and their independence.

## Significance

Redhollow is the southernmost settlement of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and its position at the frontier of the plains gives it a strategic significance that belies its modest size. The town is a staging point for the trade and the travel that crosses the dry country, and its people are the guardians of the southern frontier, maintaining the roads, the wells, and the shelters that sustain the traffic between the grasslands and the warm regions beyond. The prosperity of Redhollow is modest, but the community is proud of its role as the frontier settlement of the southern plains.

## History

Redhollow was founded by a group of settlers who ventured to the edge of the grasslands in search of grazing land and the freedom of the frontier. The earliest settlement was a collection of farmsteads clustered around the wells, and the town grew slowly as the trade between the grasslands and the dry country developed. The red soil that gave the town its name was the most distinctive feature of the site, and the settlers who chose the location were drawn by the water and the shelter that the shallow valley provided.

The history of Redhollow is a record of endurance, for the frontier is a demanding environment, and the community has faced drought, hardship, and the occasional threat from the wild lands beyond. The people of Redhollow have met these challenges with a determination that is the hallmark of the frontier character, and the town has endured through the generations. The wells have been deepened and maintained, the roads have been kept in repair, and the inns have served the caravans that pass through the town on their way to and from the dry country. The membership of the Plains Confederation has been a source of support, and the confederation has provided assistance in times of need, a relationship that has sustained the community through the most difficult years.
