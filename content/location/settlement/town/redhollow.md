## A Southern Frontier Settlement at the Edge of the Grasslands

Redhollow is a southern settlement of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated at the frontier of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) where the cultivated grasslands meet the [Emberlands](/content/location/settlement/region/emberlands). The town stands in a shallow valley where the red soil of the dry country meets the darker earth of the grasslands; the name of the settlement derives from the reddish hue of the ground surrounding it. Redhollow is the smallest and most remote of the settlements of the southern plains, and its people are hardy and self-reliant, accustomed to the demands of life at the edge of cultivated land.

## The Transition Zone and the Deep Wells

The land around Redhollow is a transition zone where the fertility of the southern plains gives way to the aridity of the dry country. [Agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) is limited to hardy crops that can tolerate poor soil and scarce water. The farms surrounding the town are smaller and less productive than those of the central grasslands, but they are sufficient to sustain the community. The people supplement their agriculture with hunting and foraging the dry country provides. The hunters of Redhollow are skilled in the pursuit of game inhabiting the marginal lands; the hides and meat of wild animals supplement the diet and economy of the town.

The wells of Redhollow are the most valuable resource of the settlement, for the water table is deep and the supply is limited. The wells are maintained by the community, and water is rationed during the dry season when the level falls and pressure drops. The construction of the wells was a feat of determination and labour, for the rock is hard and the shafts are deep; the wells are the most significant works of the community.

The frontier Redhollow marks is the southern limit of settled land of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The town is the last outpost of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) before the dry country and the warm regions beyond. Traffic passing through the town is light, for the routes crossing the dry country are few and the trade is modest, but the town serves as a staging point for caravans venturing into the warm regions. The inns and stables of Redhollow are a valued refuge for those who travel the southern roads.

## Hardy Livestock, Wool, and the Frontier Inns

Redhollow's trade is limited to the products of surrounding farms and goods imported from the central grasslands. The town is a centre for trade in the hardy livestock raised on the frontier; cattle and goats bred in the region are valued for their resilience and adaptability to dry conditions. The wool of the frontier sheep is coarser than that of the central grasslands but is durable and warm, a minor export finding a market among traders passing through. The hides of goats and cattle are also traded; the leather tanned from them is a valued product of the frontier, known for its toughness and resistance to wear in dry conditions.

The inns of Redhollow are a central institution of the town, for caravans venturing into the dry country rely on the lodging and provisioning the inns provide. The innkeepers are experienced in the needs of the caravans, and the inns are equipped with storerooms, water cisterns, and stables required to serve the frontier traffic. The innkeepers are also the keepers of news, for the caravans bring tidings of the grasslands and the dry country, making the innkeepers among the best-informed people on the southern frontier.

Redhollow is a small, rugged town whose buildings are constructed of red stone and sun-baked clay, the materials of the region, with thick walls and small windows providing insulation against heat and wind. The streets are broad and dusty, and the town is oriented toward the road connecting it to the central grasslands, with the market square and inns situated along the main street. The population is small and entirely [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people are known for their hardihood and independence.

The life of Redhollow is shaped by the scarcity of resources that is the constant condition of the frontier. Every drop of water is measured and conserved; the wells are the focus of the community, and the rationing of water during the dry season is accepted as a necessary discipline. The buildings are constructed with cisterns that collect the rare rainfall, and the roofs are sloped to direct water into the storage tanks. The diet of the people is simple, based on the grains and vegetables that can be grown in the poor soil, supplemented by the game that the hunters bring from the marginal lands. The inhabitants are lean and weathered, their faces marked by the sun and the wind that are the constant companions of life at the frontier.

## The Southernmost Outpost of the Plains Confederation

Redhollow is the southernmost settlement of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and its position at the frontier of the plains gives it a strategic significance that belies its modest size. The town is a staging point for trade and travel crossing the dry country, and its people are the guardians of the southern frontier, maintaining the roads, wells, and shelters that sustain traffic between the grasslands and the warm regions beyond. The prosperity of Redhollow is modest, but the community is proud of its role as the frontier settlement of the southern plains.

## A Record of Endurance on the Frontier

Redhollow was founded by a group of settlers who ventured to the edge of the grasslands in search of grazing land and the freedom of the frontier. The earliest settlement was a collection of farmsteads clustered around the wells; the town grew slowly as trade between the grasslands and the dry country developed. The red soil that gave the town its name was the most distinctive feature of the site, and the settlers who chose the location were drawn by the water and the shelter the shallow valley provided.

The history of Redhollow is a record of endurance. The frontier is a demanding environment, and the community has faced drought, hardship, and occasional threats from the wild lands beyond. The people have met these challenges with a determination that is the hallmark of the frontier character, and the town has endured through the generations. The wells have been deepened and maintained, the roads kept in repair, and the inns have served the caravans passing through on their way to and from the dry country. The membership of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) has been a source of support, providing assistance in times of need, a relationship that has sustained the community through the most difficult years.
