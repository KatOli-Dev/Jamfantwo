## Overview

Ironhollow is a [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) industrial settlement in the eastern ranges of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), situated in a region where the mountains are rich in iron ore and the conditions are well suited to the large-scale smelting and working of metal. The settlement is the primary centre of iron production in Morvind, and its forges and smelteries are among the largest and the most active in the known world. Ironhollow is a dependency of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and its iron is the raw material that feeds the forges of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) and [Khazgar](/content/location/settlement/city/khazgar), where it is worked into the finished products that are exported to every continent.

## The Mines and the Smelteries

The mines of Ironhollow are the most productive iron workings in Morvind, and the ore that is extracted from the depths of the eastern ranges is among the richest in the known world. The mines are worked on a large scale, for the demand for iron is great and the deposits are extensive, and the miners of Ironhollow are among the most skilled and the most numerous in the dwarven world. The ore that is extracted is smelted in the great smelteries that are situated close to the mine workings, and the smelteries are the dominant feature of the settlement, their chimneys and their furnaces producing a constant output of pig iron and crude steel that is the raw material of the dwarven metalworking industry.

The smelteries of Ironhollow are among the largest industrial structures in the known world, and the furnaces that are used to smelt the ore are fuelled by the charcoal that is produced from the forests of the lower slopes and the coal that is mined from the seams that run through the eastern ranges. The smelting operations are conducted continuously, for the demand for iron is constant and the furnaces must be kept burning, and the smelteries are a hive of activity at all hours, the ore arriving from the mines, the fuel arriving from the forests and the coal seams, and the pig iron and the crude steel departing for the forges of the interior.

The mining operations are organised in shifts that operate around the clock, and the miners who work the deepest veins are among the most experienced and the most skilled in the dwarven world. The veins of iron ore that are found in the eastern ranges are thick and extensive, and the mining of them is conducted according to a system of galleries and shafts that has been developed and refined over the centuries of the settlement's history. The maintenance of the mines is a constant concern, for the stability of the galleries and the ventilation of the shafts are essential for the safety of the miners, and the engineers of Ironhollow are among the most accomplished in the design and the maintenance of the underground workings.

## The Forges and the Trade

The forges of Ironhollow are devoted chiefly to the preliminary working of the iron, and the pig iron and the crude steel that are produced in the smelteries are worked into the bars, the ingots, and the rough castings that are the raw material of the finished metalwork that is produced in Stoneheim and Khazgar. The forges of Ironhollow are large and efficient, and the iron that is produced here is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry, for the quantity and the quality of the Ironhollow output is unmatched by any other source in Morvind.

The trade that connects Ironhollow to the rest of the dwarven world is managed through the overland routes that connect the eastern ranges to the interior, and the iron that is produced in Ironhollow is transported overland to Stoneheim and Khazgar, where it is worked into the finished products that are exported to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond. The return cargo includes the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the settlement cannot produce for itself, and the trade that flows through the routes is the lifeline of the community.

The caravans that transport the iron from Ironhollow to the interior are large and heavily laden, for the weight of the iron is great and the number of wagons that are required is substantial. The routes that connect the eastern ranges to the interior are maintained by the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and the security of the routes is a constant concern of the administration, for the wild peoples of the mountains are a recurring threat to the caravans. The iron that is transported to the interior is worked in the forges of Stoneheim and Khazgar into the weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects that are exported to every continent, and the quality of the Ironhollow iron is a significant factor in the reputation of the dwarven metalwork.

## The Settlement

The population of Ironhollow is entirely [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), and the community is devoted to the iron industry that is the reason for the settlement's existence. The dwellings of Ironhollow are carved into the rock above the smelteries, and the living quarters are warmer than those of most dwarven cities, for the heat of the furnaces permeates the surrounding stone. The settlement is a grim, industrial place, and the air within the halls is thick with the smoke and the heat of the smelteries, but the community is prosperous and the dwarves of Ironhollow are proud of their role as the primary producers of the iron that feeds the dwarven metalworking industry.

The community of Ironhollow is organised around the shifts and the operations of the mines and the smelteries, and the rhythm of life in the settlement is determined by the demands of the iron industry. The dwarves of Ironhollow are known for their endurance and their skill in the demanding conditions of the mines and the smelteries, and the traditions of the settlement are rooted in the craft and the labour that are the foundation of the community. The social life of the settlement is centred on the halls and the common rooms that are carved into the rock, and the gatherings that are held in these spaces are the occasions for the renewal of the ties that bind the community together.

## History

The settlement of Ironhollow was established in the early centuries of the dwarven expansion into the eastern ranges, when the rich deposits of iron ore were discovered by the prospectors who were sent from [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) to explore the mineral wealth of the region. The initial settlement was a mining camp that was established at the site of the richest deposits, and the camp grew as the scale of the mining operations expanded and the smelteries were built to process the ore that was being extracted. The settlement was admitted to the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) as a dependency of the realm, and the iron that was produced at Ironhollow became the primary source of the raw material that fed the forges of the interior.

The history of Ironhollow has been one of steady growth and increasing productivity, for the demand for iron has been constant throughout the history of the settlement, and the deposits that are found in the eastern ranges have proved to be among the most extensive in the known world. The settlement has been expanded on several occasions to accommodate the growing workforce and the increasing scale of the operations, and the smelteries and the forges have been enlarged and improved to meet the growing demand for the iron that is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry.

## Significance

Ironhollow is the primary centre of iron production in Morvind, and its output is the raw material that sustains the dwarven metalworking industry. The settlement is a dependency of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and its iron is a critical component of the economy of the dwarven realm and the continent as a whole. The significance of Ironhollow lies in the scale and the efficiency of its iron production, and the settlement is a testament to the dwarven mastery of the industrial processes that are the foundation of the dwarven civilisation.

The iron that is produced at Ironhollow is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry, and the weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects that are produced in the forges of Stoneheim and Khazgar are made from the iron that is extracted and smelted in the eastern ranges. The settlement is a critical component of the economy of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and the prosperity of the realm is built in part upon the iron that flows from the mines and the smelteries of Ironhollow. The dwarves of Ironhollow are proud of their role as the primary producers of the iron that feeds the forges of the dwarven world, and their craft and their labour are a valued and a recognised part of the dwarven tradition.
