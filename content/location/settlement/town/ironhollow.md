## Overview

Ironhollow is a [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) industrial settlement in the eastern ranges of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), situated in a region where the mountains are rich in iron ore and the conditions are well suited to large-scale smelting and working of metal. The settlement is the primary centre of iron production in Morvind, and its forges and smelteries are among the largest and most active in the known world. Ironhollow is a dependency of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and its iron is the raw material that feeds the forges of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) and [Khazgar](/content/location/settlement/city/khazgar), where it is worked into finished products exported to every continent.

## The Mines and the Smelteries

The mines of Ironhollow are the most productive iron workings in Morvind, and the ore extracted from the depths of the eastern ranges is among the richest in the known world. The mines are worked on a large scale, for the demand for iron is great and the deposits are extensive. The miners are among the most skilled and numerous in the dwarven world. The ore is smelted in great smelteries situated close to the mine workings; the smelteries are the dominant feature of the settlement, their chimneys and furnaces producing a constant output of pig iron and crude steel that is the raw material of the dwarven metalworking industry.

The smelteries of Ironhollow are among the largest industrial structures in the known world. The furnaces used to smelt the ore are fuelled by charcoal produced from the forests of the lower slopes and coal mined from seams running through the eastern ranges. Smelting operations are conducted continuously, for the demand for iron is constant and the furnaces must be kept burning. The smelteries are a hive of activity at all hours: ore arriving from the mines, fuel arriving from the forests and coal seams, and pig iron and crude steel departing for the forges of the interior.

Mining operations are organised in shifts operating around the clock. The miners who work the deepest veins are among the most experienced and skilled in the dwarven world. The veins of iron ore found in the eastern ranges are thick and extensive, and mining is conducted according to a system of galleries and shafts developed and refined over centuries. Maintenance of the mines is a constant concern; the stability of the galleries and the ventilation of the shafts are essential for the safety of the miners, and the engineers of Ironhollow are among the most accomplished in the design and maintenance of underground workings.

## The Forges and the Trade

The forges of Ironhollow are devoted chiefly to the preliminary working of iron. The pig iron and crude steel produced in the smelteries are worked into bars, ingots, and rough castings that are the raw material of finished metalwork produced in Stoneheim and Khazgar. The forges are large and efficient, and the iron produced here is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry, for the quantity and quality of Ironhollow's output is unmatched by any other source in Morvind.

The trade connecting Ironhollow to the rest of the dwarven world is managed through overland routes connecting the eastern ranges to the interior. The iron produced at Ironhollow is transported overland to Stoneheim and Khazgar, where it is worked into finished products exported to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond. The return cargo includes grain, cloth, and manufactured goods the settlement cannot produce for itself; this trade is the lifeline of the community.

The caravans transporting iron from Ironhollow to the interior are large and heavily laden. The routes connecting the eastern ranges to the interior are maintained by the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and the security of the routes is a constant concern, as the wild peoples of the mountains are a recurring threat to the caravans. The iron transported to the interior is worked in the forges of Stoneheim and Khazgar into weapons, tools, and decorative objects exported to every continent; the quality of Ironhollow iron is a significant factor in the reputation of dwarven metalwork.

## The Settlement

The population of Ironhollow is entirely [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), and the community is devoted to the iron industry that is the reason for the settlement's existence. The dwellings are carved into the rock above the smelteries, and the living quarters are warmer than those of most dwarven cities, for the heat of the furnaces permeates the surrounding stone. The settlement is a grim, industrial place; the air within the halls is thick with the smoke and heat of the smelteries, but the community is prosperous and the dwarves of Ironhollow are proud of their role as the primary producers of the iron that feeds the dwarven metalworking industry.

The community is organised around the shifts and operations of the mines and smelteries; the rhythm of life is determined by the demands of the iron industry. The dwarves of Ironhollow are known for their endurance and skill in the demanding conditions of the mines and smelteries, and the traditions of the settlement are rooted in the craft and labour that are the foundation of the community. Social life is centred on the halls and common rooms carved into the rock; gatherings in these spaces are occasions for renewing the ties that bind the community together.

## History

The settlement of Ironhollow was established in the early centuries of dwarven expansion into the eastern ranges, when rich deposits of iron ore were discovered by prospectors sent from [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) to explore the mineral wealth of the region. The initial settlement was a mining camp at the site of the richest deposits; the camp grew as the scale of mining operations expanded and smelteries were built to process the ore. The settlement was admitted to the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) as a dependency of the realm, and the iron produced at Ironhollow became the primary source of raw material feeding the forges of the interior.

The history of Ironhollow has been one of steady growth and increasing productivity. The demand for iron has been constant throughout the settlement's history, and the deposits found in the eastern ranges have proved among the most extensive in the known world. The settlement has been expanded on several occasions to accommodate the growing workforce and the increasing scale of operations; the smelteries and forges have been enlarged and improved to meet the growing demand for the iron that is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry.

## Significance

Ironhollow is the primary centre of iron production in Morvind, and its output is the raw material that sustains the dwarven metalworking industry. The settlement is a dependency of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and its iron is a critical component of the economy of the dwarven realm and the continent as a whole. The significance of Ironhollow lies in the scale and efficiency of its iron production, and the settlement is a testament to the dwarven mastery of the industrial processes that are the foundation of dwarven civilisation.

The iron produced at Ironhollow is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry. The weapons, tools, and decorative objects produced in the forges of Stoneheim and Khazgar are made from the iron extracted and smelted in the eastern ranges. The settlement is a critical component of the economy of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and the prosperity of the realm is built in part upon the iron flowing from the mines and smelteries of Ironhollow. The dwarves of Ironhollow are proud of their role as the primary producers of the iron that feeds the forges of the dwarven world.
