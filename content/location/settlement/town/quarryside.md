## Overview

Quarryside is a town of the cold north, situated at the meeting of the southern quarries of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). It is the seat of the [Bench of the Stone Quarries](/content/government/local/bench-of-the-stone-quarries), and the town takes both its name and its principal reason for existing from the quarries that have been worked in the surrounding hills for as many generations as the chronicles record. The masonic hall at the centre of the town is the meeting place of the Bench, the office of its clerk, and the archive in which the standards of the stone, the judgements of the disputes, and the registers of the apprentices are preserved. The chronicler records that the records of the Bench are among the most complete of the records of any trade body of the known world, and the records are the foundation of the reputation of the stone of the region.

The town is small in comparison with the great cities of the coast and the river, but the population swells during the quarterly meetings of the Bench, when the masters of the quarries, the heads of the masonic lodges, and the sworn deputies gather from the surrounding villages. The inns of the town are filled to overflowing on these occasions, and the market square becomes a forum in which the orders of dressed stone are discussed, the price of the next quarter's yield is debated, and the disputes of the past season are settled in the council chamber of the masonic hall. The chronicler notes that the town is at its liveliest during the spring and the autumn meetings, and the liveliness is a measure of the importance of the trade to the life of the region.

## The Quarries and the Stone

The quarries that give the town its name are worked into the hills to the south and the east, where the stone is of a colour and a grain that the masons of the region have long prized. The principal quarries are open cuts in the flank of the hill, and the stone is taken in blocks that are squared at the face and trimmed at the edges before they are loaded onto the carts that bring them to the dressing yards of the town. The Bench of the Stone Quarries maintains the standards of the stone by colour, by grain, by hardness, and by the sound that a block gives when it is struck with a hammer, and the standards are enforced at the autumn meeting of the Bench by a public testing that the sworn members and the town alike attend.

The stone of Quarryside is carried outward by the cart roads that descend from the town to the plains of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). The principal roads run west toward the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/government/national/deep-realm-of-karhold), north toward the passes of the [Stonespire](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire), and south toward the warm coast. The carts are loaded in the yards of the masonic lodges, and the loading is overseen by the sworn members of the lodges, who certify by the seal of the Bench that the stone meets the standard. The chronicler records that the seal of the Bench has been the foundation of the reputation of the stone of Quarryside for more than a century, and the stone of the region is held in high esteem by the builders of the cold north.

## The Masonic Hall and the Bench

The masonic hall of Quarryside is the most substantial building of the town, and the building was erected more than a century ago by the subscriptions of the sworn members of the Bench. The hall is built of the local stone, and the walls are squared and dressed to a standard that the masons of the Bench have used as their own measure of quality. The hall contains the council chamber, the offices of the clerk, the small yard in which the standard stones are kept, and the archives in which the records of the Bench are preserved. The council chamber is the meeting place of the master, the two wardens, and the sworn members when the Bench is in session, and the chamber is arranged to permit the orderly conduct of the business of the trade.

The archives of the Bench are the principal repository of the records of the trade, and the archives are open to the sworn members and to the magistrates of the principal towns. The registers of the Bench include the register of the sworn members, the register of the judgements, the register of the standards, and the register of the apprentices. The chronicler records that the registers have been consulted by the magistrates and by the historians in many a dispute, and the registers are the foundation of the reputation of the Bench for fair dealing and careful administration. The clerk of the Bench is responsible for the accuracy of the entries, and the clerk is assisted by the sworn deputies who represent the quarry villages.

The Bench meets in the masonic hall four times in each year, in the spring, the summer, the autumn, and the winter. The meetings are the occasion for the testing of the standards of the stone, the inspection of the dressing of the masons, the adjudication of the disputes that have arisen in the interval since the previous meeting, and the election of the master and the wardens when the term of their office expires. The meetings are also the occasion for the agreement of the proportion in which the quarryman is paid in coin and in dressed stone, and the proportion is revised from time to time to reflect the harvest of the quarry and the demand of the builders.

## The Town and the Surrounding Country

Quarryside is a town of masons, of quarrymen, of carters, and of the merchants who buy the dressed stone for the building trade. The principal streets of the town are paved with the local stone, and the houses are built of the same stone, though the dwelling houses are of a humbler finish than the masonic hall. The town is surrounded by a wall of stone that was erected by the subscriptions of the sworn members in the generation before the masonic hall was built, and the wall is maintained by the town council. The gates of the wall are kept shut at night, and the watch is maintained by the sworn members in rotation. The chronicler records that the wall has been sufficient to deter the disorder that has afflicted some of the smaller quarry towns, and the wall is a measure of the prudence of the Bench.

The country around Quarryside is hill country, with the high moorlands of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) to the south and the lower vales of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) to the north. The hills are worked for stone, the vales are grazed by the sheep and the cattle of the hill farmers, and the streams that descend from the hills are turned to the dressing yards and the mills. The cart roads that converge upon the town are the principal means of transport, and the road to the deep realm is the most heavily travelled, for the stone of Quarryside is the foundation of much of the building of the great underground cities of the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves). The chronicler records that the road to the deep realm is maintained by the Bench and the deep realm in common, and the maintenance is the foundation of the steady flow of stone between the two polities.

The population of the town is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a substantial [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) presence among the masons of the lodges and the master cutters of the quarries. The dwarven masons came to Quarryside several generations ago at the invitation of the Bench, and the dwarven masons have brought with them techniques of dressing and of cutting that have been adopted by the human masons of the lodges. The chronicler notes that the relationship between the human and the dwarven masons has been one of quiet co-operation, and the cooperation is a measure of the mutual respect that the two peoples have developed through their common work in the stone.

## Significance

Quarryside is the seat of the [Bench of the Stone Quarries](/content/government/local/bench-of-the-stone-quarries) and the principal market of the dressed stone of the southern flank of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). The town's significance lies in the trade that gives it its name, and the trade is the foundation of the prosperity of the surrounding region. The masonic hall, the archives, and the standards of the stone are the inheritance of the town, and the inheritance is preserved by the Bench with a care that the chronicler finds exemplary. Quarryside is a town of stone, and the stone is the measure of the town's place in the life of the cold north.
