## A Town of the Cold North at the Meeting of Quarries and Foothills

Quarryside is a town of the cold north, situated at the meeting of the southern quarries of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). It is the seat of the [Stone Quarries' Bench](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-bench), and the town takes both its name and its principal reason for existing from the quarries that have been worked in the surrounding hills for as many generations as records exist. The [Stone Quarries' Hall](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-hall) at the centre of the town is the meeting place of the Bench, the office of its clerk, and the archive in which the standards of stone, the judgements of disputes, and the registers of apprentices are preserved. The records of the Bench are among the most complete of any trade body in the known world, and they are the foundation of the reputation of the stone of the region.

The town is small in comparison with the great cities of the coast and the river, but the population swells during the quarterly meetings of the Bench, when the masters of the quarries, the heads of the masonic lodges, and the sworn deputies gather from the surrounding villages. The inns of the town are filled to overflowing on these occasions, and the market square becomes a forum in which orders of dressed stone are discussed, the price of the next quarter's yield is debated, and disputes of the past season are settled in the council chamber of the [Stone Quarries' Hall](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-hall). The town is at its liveliest during the spring and autumn meetings, a measure of the importance of the trade to the life of the region.

## The Open Cuts and the Standard of the Stone

The quarries that give the town its name are worked into the hills to the south and east, where the stone is of a colour and grain that the masons of the region have long prized. The principal quarries are open cuts in the flank of the hill; stone is taken in blocks that are squared at the face and trimmed at the edges before they are loaded onto carts bringing them to the dressing yards of the town. The [Stone Quarries' Bench](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-bench) maintains the standards of the stone by colour, grain, hardness, and the sound a block gives when struck with a hammer. The standards are enforced at the autumn meeting of the Bench by public testing that the sworn members and the town alike attend.

The stone of Quarryside is carried outward by cart roads descending from the town to the plains of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). The principal roads run west toward the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), north toward the passes of the [Stonespire](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire), and south toward the warm coast. The carts are loaded in the yards of the masonic lodges, overseen by sworn members of the lodges who certify by the seal of the Bench that the stone meets the standard. The seal of the Bench has been the foundation of the reputation of the stone of Quarryside for more than a century, and the stone of the region is held in high esteem by the builders of the cold north.

## The Building of the Hall and the Archives of the Bench

The [Stone Quarries' Hall](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-hall) of Quarryside is the most substantial building of the town, erected more than a century ago by subscriptions of the sworn members of the Bench. The hall is built of local stone, the walls squared and dressed to a standard that the masons have used as their own measure of quality. The hall contains the council chamber, the offices of the clerk, a small yard in which the standard stones are kept, and the archives in which the records of the Bench are preserved. The council chamber is the meeting place of the master, the two wardens, and the sworn members when the Bench is in session, arranged to permit the orderly conduct of the business of the trade.

The archives of the Bench are the principal repository of the records of the trade, open to the sworn members and to the magistrates of the principal towns. The registers include the register of sworn members, the register of judgements, the register of standards, and the register of apprentices. The registers have been consulted by magistrates and historians in many a dispute, and they are the foundation of the reputation of the Bench for fair dealing and careful administration. The clerk of the Bench is responsible for the accuracy of the entries, assisted by the sworn deputies who represent the quarry villages.

The Bench meets in the [Stone Quarries' Hall](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-hall) four times each year: in spring, summer, autumn, and winter. The meetings are the occasion for testing the standards of stone, inspecting the dressing of the masons, adjudicating disputes that have arisen since the previous meeting, and electing the master and wardens when their terms expire. The meetings are also the occasion for agreeing the proportion in which the quarryman is paid in coin and in dressed stone, revised from time to time to reflect the harvest of the quarry and the demand of the builders.

## The Walled Town and the Hill Country

Quarryside is a town of masons, quarrymen, carters, and merchants who buy dressed stone for the building trade. The principal streets are paved with local stone, and the houses are built of the same stone, though the dwelling houses are of a humbler finish than the [Stone Quarries' Hall](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-hall). The town is surrounded by a wall of stone erected by subscriptions of the sworn members in the generation before the masonic hall was built, maintained by the town council. The gates are kept shut at night, and the watch is maintained by the sworn members in rotation. The wall has been sufficient to deter the disorder that has afflicted some smaller quarry towns, a measure of the prudence of the Bench.

The country around Quarryside is hill country, with the high moorlands of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) to the south and the lower vales of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) to the north. The hills are worked for stone; the vales are grazed by sheep and cattle of hill farmers; the streams descending from the hills are turned to dressing yards and mills. The cart roads converging on the town are the principal means of transport, and the road to the deep realm is the most heavily travelled, for the stone of Quarryside is the foundation of much of the building of the great underground cities of the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves). The road to the deep realm is maintained by the Bench and the deep realm in common, the foundation of the steady flow of stone between the two polities.

The population of the town is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a substantial [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) presence among the masons of the lodges and the master cutters of the quarries. The dwarven masons came to Quarryside several generations ago at the invitation of the Bench, bringing techniques of dressing and cutting that have been adopted by the human masons of the lodges. The relationship between human and dwarven masons has been one of quiet cooperation, a measure of the mutual respect the two peoples have developed through their common work in stone.

## The Principal Market of Dressed Stone of the Southern Flank

Quarryside is the seat of the [Stone Quarries' Bench](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-bench) and the principal market of dressed stone of the southern flank of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands). The town's significance lies in the trade that gives it its name, the foundation of the prosperity of the surrounding region. The [Stone Quarries' Hall](/content/government/local/stone-quarries-hall), the archives, and the standards of the stone are the inheritance of the town, preserved by the Bench with exemplary care. Quarryside is a town of stone, and the stone is the measure of the town's place in the life of the cold north.
