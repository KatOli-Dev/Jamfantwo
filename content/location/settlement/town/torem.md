## Overview

Torem is the gateway settlement of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), situated in the highlands at the head of the pass that descends from the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) into the basin of the inland seas in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The town is the first settlement that the caravans from the north encounter upon entering the Southern Reach, and its position at the head of the pass gives it a role in the trade and the communication between the south and the north that is disproportionate to its modest size. Torem is the smallest of the three principal settlements of the Southern Reach, but it is the most cosmopolitan, for the caravans that pass through it bring a steady flow of goods and travellers from the northern world.

## The Pass and the Caravans

The pass that Torem commands is the only viable route from the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) into the basin of the inland seas, for the highlands that surround the basin are steep and broken, and the other approaches are impassable to anything but a lone traveller on foot. The pass is a narrow valley that descends from the desert plateau through a series of switchbacks and terraces, and the town is situated at the head of the valley, where the pass opens into the gentler terrain of the basin. The position of Torem at the head of the pass gives it control over the entry and the exit of the caravan trade, and the town's prosperity is built upon the tolls, the provisions, and the services that it provides to the caravans.

The caravans that travel the southern routes are guided by the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), whose knowledge of the desert extends to the edges of the southern highlands, and the journey from the northern coast to Torem is the longest and the most demanding of any caravan route in Sahrana. The caravans arrive at Torem weary and depleted, and the town is a place of rest and resupply before the final descent to the inland seas and the settlements of the basin. The provisions that Torem provides include water, food, fodder, and the repairs that the caravan animals and equipment require after the long crossing of the desert.

## Trade and the Southern Reach

Torem is the primary point of exchange between the caravan trade of the north and the inland sea commerce of the Southern Reach. The goods that arrive from the north include the grain, the cloth, the metalwork, and the manufactured goods that the south cannot produce for itself, and these are transferred at Torem to the boats and the pack animals that carry them to [Vereth](/content/location/settlement/town/vereth) and the other settlements of the basin. The goods that are sent north through Torem include the wool and the leather of the highland herds, the preserved fish of the inland seas, and the medicinal herbs that grow in the temperate uplands, all of which are gathered from the southern communities and brought to Torem for shipment to the north.

The merchants of Torem are the intermediaries between the caravan traders and the southern producers, and the town's market is the principal point of exchange for the goods that flow between the two worlds. The market is held in a broad square that is situated at the centre of the town, and the caravans and the southern traders gather there to conduct their business. The tolls that are collected at Torem are a significant source of revenue for the town, and a portion is contributed to the council of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) for the maintenance of the pass and the caravan routes.

## The Town

Torem is a compact, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the dual character of a frontier settlement and a trading post, with substantial buildings of stone that are constructed to withstand the winds and the cold of the highland climate. The buildings are lower and more heavily walled than those of the basin settlements, for the highlands are cooler than the inland shores, and the winters bring frost and occasional snow to the upper reaches of the pass. The town is surrounded by walls that are maintained in good order, and the gates are guarded by a force that is funded jointly by the local council and the federation, for the defence of the pass is a matter of concern to the whole Southern Reach.

The population of Torem is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people are a mixture of merchants, pack handlers, provisioners, and the craftsmen who serve the caravan trade. The community is the most transient of any in the Southern Reach, for the caravans bring a constant flow of travellers and traders who pass through the town on their way to and from the basin, and the inns and the taverns of Torem are a significant part of the local economy. The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) of the deep desert are frequent visitors, for they are the guides of the southern caravans, and their presence in Torem is a regular feature of the town's life. A small community of [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) is found in the town, and their knowledge of the highland passes and the slow, careful temperament that suits the demands of the terrain are valued by the caravan traders.

## Significance

Torem is the gateway to the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), and its position at the head of the only pass from the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) into the basin of the inland seas gives it a role in the trade and the communication between the south and the north that is disproportionate to its modest size. The town's prosperity is built upon the caravans that pass through it, and its function as the point of exchange between the northern trade and the southern economy is a critical component of the commerce of the Southern Reach. The significance of Torem lies in its role as the bridge between the desert and the inland seas, and the town is a symbol of the connection between the far south and the wider world.
