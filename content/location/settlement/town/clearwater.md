## A Farming Town in the Vael Tributaries

Clearwater is a farming town of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) situated on the banks of the Clearwater, a tributary of the [River Vael](/content/location/natural/feature/river-vael), at the point where the stream emerges from the foothills of the western highlands and enters the broad valley of the Vael. The town takes its name from the stream, whose waters are notably clear and pure, and it serves as a centre of trade and administration for the farming communities of the tributary valley. Clearwater is a settlement of modest size but considerable prosperity, built upon the agricultural wealth of the surrounding country.

The position of Clearwater at the point where the Clearwater stream enters the Vael valley has made it a natural centre for the trade of the region. The roads that follow the tributary valley converge on the town, and the weekly market attracts farmers and traders from the surrounding country. The prosperity of Clearwater is founded upon the grain that is grown in the fields of the valley and the livestock that is raised on the pastures of the foothills.

## The Clearwater Stream and the Vael Tributaries

The Clearwater stream rises in the western highlands, where the snowmelt and the springs of the mountains feed a network of small streams that unite to form the Clearwater. The stream flows eastward through a narrow valley that broadens as it approaches the Vael, and the waters of the Clearwater are noted for their clarity and purity, a quality that has given the stream and the town their name.

The Vael tributaries are the streams and rivers that feed the River Vael from the western highlands and the southern hills. The tributaries are numerous, and the valleys that they have carved are among the most fertile agricultural regions in central Aethelar. The Clearwater valley is typical of the tributary valleys, with fields of grain and pastures of grass covering the valley floor and the lower slopes of the hills.

## From Farming Hamlet to Market Town

The earliest settlement at the site of Clearwater was a farming hamlet established by the first farmers to cultivate the fertile soils of the Clearwater valley. The hamlet consisted of a few houses built around a spring, with fields of grain and vegetables spread across the valley floor. The farmers of the hamlet prospered, and the hamlet grew as the population of the valley increased.

The grant of a market charter to Clearwater was the turning point in the growth of the settlement. The charter permitted the settlement to hold a weekly market, and the market quickly became the principal commercial event of the Clearwater valley. Farmers, traders, and craftsmen from the surrounding country gathered at the market to buy and sell the products of the region, and the prosperity generated by the market transformed the hamlet into a town.

## The Fields of Grain and the Pastures of the Valley

The fields of grain that surround Clearwater are the foundation of the towns prosperity. The soils of the Clearwater valley are deep and fertile, and the climate is well suited to the cultivation of wheat and barley, the principal crops of the region. The fields are divided among the farming families of the valley, and each family works its own land, bringing the harvest to the market at Clearwater for sale.

The pastures of the valley provide grazing for the cattle and sheep that are the livestock of the region. The cattle are raised for meat and milk, and the dairy products of the Clearwater valley are sold at the market and exported to the cities of the Vael. The sheep are raised for wool and meat, and the cloth of the Clearwater valley is valued for its quality and durability.

## The Mills and the Breweries of Clearwater

The Clearwater stream provides the power for the mills that process the grain of the valley. Several watermills line the stream, their wheels driven by the flow of the clear water, grinding the grain of the farmers into flour for the market. The millers of Clearwater are among the most skilled in the Vael valley, and the flour produced by the mills is prized for its fineness and purity.

The breweries of Clearwater are another important component of the local economy. The barley of the valley and the clear water of the stream are the raw materials for the production of ale, and the breweries of Clearwater produce a range of ales that are sold at the market and exported to the surrounding region. The ale of Clearwater is noted for its quality and flavour, and it is a valued commodity in the markets of the Vael.

## The Weekly Market and the Autumn Harvest Fair

The weekly market of Clearwater is held in the market square, an open space at the centre of the town. The square is surrounded by the principal buildings of the settlement, including the market hall, the guild hall, and the houses of the principal merchants. The stalls and booths erected on market day offer the grain, meat, dairy products, and ale produced in the valley, along with the goods of the craftsmen of the town.

The autumn harvest fair of Clearwater is the principal event of the commercial calendar, marking the conclusion of the harvest and the beginning of the winter season. The fair lasts for several days, and the farmers of the valley bring their surplus grain and livestock to the fair for sale. The fair is also the occasion for the celebration of the harvest, with feasting and entertainment that draw visitors from the surrounding country.

## The Farmers, the Millers, and the Council of Clearwater

The governance of Clearwater is in the hands of a council elected by the citizens of the town, with the principal farmers and millers holding the positions of greatest authority. The council is responsible for the administration of the town, the regulation of the market, and the maintenance of the roads and bridges that connect Clearwater to the surrounding region.

The farmers of the Clearwater valley are among the most skilled and prosperous in the Vael tributaries. The knowledge they possess of the soils, the climate, and the crops of the valley is the foundation of the agricultural prosperity of the region. The millers of Clearwater are also among the most respected figures in the community, for the mills that they operate are essential to the processing of the grain that is the principal product of the valley.

## Clearwater and the Trade of the Vael Valley

Clearwater is the principal market and commercial centre of the Clearwater valley, and its trade connects the farming communities of the tributary to the wider markets of the Vael. The grain, meat, dairy products, and ale of the Clearwater valley are traded at the market of the town and exported to the cities of the Vael and beyond. The town is a settlement of modest size but considerable prosperity, built upon the agricultural wealth of the surrounding country and the enterprise of its people.
