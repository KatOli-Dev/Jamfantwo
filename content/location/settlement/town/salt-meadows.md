## A Salt-Producing Coastal Town

[Salt](/content/economy/salt) Meadows is a coastal town of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) situated on the low-lying shores of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), at the mouth of a small river that flows through a broad expanse of salt marsh. The town takes its name from the salt meadows that surround it, where the salt-tolerant grasses that grow on the margins of the marsh provide grazing for sheep and the pans in which the sea water is evaporated produce the salt that is the foundation of the town's prosperity. Salt Meadows is a settlement of modest size but considerable importance in the local economy, serving as the principal source of salt for the surrounding region.

The position of Salt Meadows at the mouth of the river and the edge of the salt marsh has determined the character and the economy of the town. The salt marsh provides the conditions necessary for the production of salt by solar evaporation, and the meadows provide grazing for the sheep whose wool and meat are the secondary products of the local economy. The town is built on the higher ground at the edge of the marsh, protected from the highest tides by a low embankment that has been maintained since the earliest period of settlement.

## The Salt Marsh and the Evaporation Pans

The salt marsh that surrounds Salt Meadows is a broad expanse of low-lying ground that is flooded by the highest tides and drained by a network of channels and creeks. The marsh is covered with salt-tolerant grasses and other plants that provide grazing for the sheep of the town and habitat for the birds and fish that are the other resources of the marsh. The marsh is also the source of the saltwater that is the raw material of the salt industry.

The evaporation pans of Salt Meadows are constructed on the higher ground at the edge of the marsh, where the clay soil provides a surface that can be made impermeable to water. The pans are shallow basins, carefully levelled and packed, into which the saltwater from the marsh is admitted during the summer months. The heat of the sun evaporates the water, leaving the salt behind, and the salt is then harvested and taken to the warehouses for storage and sale.

## From Salt Camp to Chartered Town

The earliest settlement at the site of Salt Meadows was a salt camp established by the first salt-makers who recognised the potential of the marsh for the production of salt. The camp consisted of a few shelters and a single evaporation pan, but the quality of the salt produced was recognised by the merchants of the coastal cities, and the demand for the salt of Salt Meadows grew rapidly.

The growth of the salt camp into a permanent settlement followed the expansion of the salt industry. More evaporation pans were constructed, and the work of harvesting the salt attracted workers and their families to the settlement. The settlement became a village, and the village was granted a market charter that allowed it to hold a weekly market and an annual fair.

The chartering of Salt Meadows as a market town was the turning point in its growth. The market attracted merchants from the inland cities, who came to purchase the salt of the meadows for distribution to the interior. The prosperity generated by the salt trade and the market transformed the village into a town, and Salt Meadows was recognised as the principal salt-producing settlement of the coast.

## The Salt Harvest and the Processing of the Salt

The salt harvest of Salt Meadows is the principal event of the commercial year, and the work of harvesting the salt is the most important activity of the town. The salt harvest takes place in the late summer, when the heat of the sun has evaporated the water from the pans and the salt is ready for collection. The salt is raked from the pans by the salt-makers, working in teams to gather the crystals and transport them to the warehouses.

The salt harvested from the pans is not ready for immediate use. The salt must be processed to remove the impurities that are present in the seawater, and the processing of the salt is the second stage of the industry. The salt is washed in fresh water to remove the bitter compounds, and it is then dried in the sun or in ovens before being ground to the desired consistency. The processed salt of Salt Meadows is prized for its purity and whiteness, and it commands a premium in the markets of the interior.

## The Sheep of the Salt Meadows

The sheep that graze on the salt meadows are the secondary foundation of the town's economy. The salt-tolerant grasses of the marsh provide grazing that is rich in the minerals that sheep require, and the meat and wool of the sheep of Salt Meadows are prized for their quality. The flocks of the town are owned by the families that have held the grazing rights to the meadows for generations, and the management of the flocks is a seasonal activity that occupies the farmers of the town during the months when the salt pans are idle.

The wool of the Salt Meadows sheep is used for the production of cloth and garments that are sold at the market and exported to the cities of the interior. The meat of the sheep is consumed locally and traded at the market, and the salted mutton produced in the town is a valued commodity in the diet of the coastal region.

## The Weekly Market and the Salt Fair

The weekly market of Salt Meadows is held in the market square, an open space at the centre of the town. The square is surrounded by the principal buildings of the settlement, including the market hall, the chapel, and the warehouses of the salt merchants. The stalls and booths erected on market day offer the salt of the meadows, the wool and meat of the sheep, and the fish and shellfish of the coastal waters.

The annual salt fair of Salt Meadows is the principal event of the commercial calendar, drawing salt merchants and buyers from across the coastal region to the town. The fair is held at the conclusion of the salt harvest, and the best of the years salt is offered for sale. The fair lasts for several days, and the salt-makers and merchants of the town negotiate the prices and quantities for the coming year.

## The Salt-Makers, the Merchants, and the Council of Salt Meadows

The governance of Salt Meadows is in the hands of a council elected by the citizens of the town, with the master salt-makers and the principal merchants holding the positions of greatest authority. The council is responsible for the administration of the town, the regulation of the salt industry, and the maintenance of the embankment and the channels that protect the town from the sea.

The master salt-makers of Salt Meadows are among the most skilled and experienced producers of salt on the coast. The master salt-makers are responsible for the management of the evaporation pans and the direction of the salt harvest. The knowledge they possess of the conditions necessary for the production of high-quality salt is the foundation of the prosperity of the town.

## Salt Meadows and the Trade of the Coast

Salt Meadows is the principal source of salt for the surrounding region, and the salt produced at the town is traded across the coastal lowlands and into the interior of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The salt of the meadows is used for the preservation of food, for the seasoning of meals, and for the industrial processes that require salt as an input. The town is a settlement of modest size but considerable importance in the economy of the coast, and the salt-makers and sheep farmers of Salt Meadows are known for the quality of their products and the reliability of their trade.
