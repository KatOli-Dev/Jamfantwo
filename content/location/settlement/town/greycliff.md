## Overview

Greycliff is the largest fishing and whaling town on the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and it is the principal settlement of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings). The town stands on a high cliff of grey stone that overlooks the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean), and its harbour is tucked into a narrow inlet that cuts deep into the cliff, sheltered from the wind and the swell that batter the open coast. The town is smaller and more rugged than the cities of the eastern coast, but it is the wealthiest and most important settlement on the western shore, and its prosperity is built upon the cold, rich waters of the Greyring.

## The Harbour and the Fisheries

The harbour of Greycliff is a natural inlet that penetrates the cliff for several hundred yards, forming a deep, sheltered basin that is accessible at all states of the tide. The inlet is narrow, and the entrance is marked by lighthouses that are visible for many miles across the open ocean, for the fog that blankets the western coast can reduce visibility to almost nothing, and the harbour is one of the few safe refuges on the entire coastline. The quays line both sides of the inlet, and they are occupied by the fishing boats, the whaling vessels, and the small craft that serve the town. The harbour master maintains a chain that can be drawn across the mouth of the inlet during the worst storms, preventing the surge from damaging the moored vessels, and this simple device has preserved many a craft that would otherwise have been lost.

The fisheries of Greycliff are the most productive on the western coast, and the cold, nutrient-rich waters of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) yield a bounty of fish that is the foundation of the town's economy. The fishing fleet of Greycliff is the largest on the western coast, and the boats venture far from land in pursuit of the shoals that move through the deep water beyond the continental shelf. The catch is landed at the harbour, processed in the salting houses and the smokehouses that line the inlet, and exported to the inland markets and the eastern coast, where the dried and salted fish of Greycliff is a valued commodity. The chief species that are taken from the Greyring include the silver hake, the deep-water cod, and the great flatfish that are found on the sandy bottoms of the continental shelf, and the seasonal runs of the oily pelagic fish that pass through the coastal waters in the autumn months are the occasion for the largest expeditions of the fishing year.

Whaling is the other great industry of Greycliff, and the whaling vessels of the town are the most seaworthy and the most daring on the western coast. The great whales of the Greyring are hunted for their oil, bone, and ambergris, and the products of the hunt are exported far inland, where they command high prices. The whalers of Greycliff are among the most skilled and the most hardy mariners in the known world, and their voyages into the open ocean are the closest thing to deep-water exploration that the Greyring has seen. The whaling season runs from the early spring to the late autumn, and the vessels that put out at the beginning of the season are often at sea for weeks at a time, for the great whales are found far beyond the continental shelf, in the deep waters that few other vessels ever reach. The try-works that render the blubber into oil are situated at the head of the inlet, and the smoke and the smell of the rendering are a constant presence in the harbour quarter.

## The Town

Greycliff is built of the same grey stone that forms the cliffs, and the buildings are sturdy and low, with thick walls, small windows, and steep roofs weighted with stone to resist the gales that sweep across the coast. The streets are narrow and winding, sheltered from the wind, and the buildings huddle together as if for warmth. The town is dominated by the harbour and the industries that serve it, and the salting houses, the smokehouses, and the warehouses are the largest and most substantial buildings in the settlement. The council hall of Greycliff stands on the rise above the harbour, a solid building of dressed stone that serves as the meeting place of the town's governing body and the court of the harbour master, and from its tower the watch can see the approach of vessels and weather alike.

The population of Greycliff is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the town are known for their hardiness and their independence. The [cancrids](/content/species/sapient/cancrids) of the rocky intertidal zone are occasionally encountered at the margins of the settlement, and a small community of [goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins) is present in the ruins and the caves along the cliff, where they scavenge and trade as opportunity allows. The goblins of Greycliff have inhabited the cliff caves for longer than the human settlement has existed, and the relationship between the two communities is one of wary tolerance, with the goblins performing small services for the townsfolk in exchange for goods that they cannot obtain for themselves.

## The Fog and the Coast

The western coast of Aethelar is notorious for the fog that blankets the shore for much of the year, and Greycliff is no exception to this rule. The cold current that flows south along the coast meets the warmer air of the land, and the result is a dense, persistent fog that can last for days at a time. The fog is a danger to the vessels that approach the coast, and the lighthouses of Greycliff are kept burning through the thickest weather, for the light is the only guide that a vessel has when the visibility is reduced to a few yards. The fog is also a feature of daily life in the town, and the streets are often damp and grey, and the buildings are coated with a fine salt mist that corrodes the metal and the paint and gives the town its characteristic weathered appearance.

The coast to the north and the south of Greycliff is a wild and rocky shore, with few safe landings and no settlements of any size, and the town is the only refuge for vessels that are caught in the storms that sweep across the Greyring. The [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings) maintain a chain of signal stations along the coast, and the stations are manned by the members of the holdings to provide warning of approaching weather and to assist vessels that are in distress.

## Significance

Greycliff is the principal settlement of the western coast of Aethelar, and its fisheries and whaling operations are the foundation of the economy of the entire western shore. The town is the largest and most prosperous member of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings), and its harbour is the primary safe refuge for vessels on the western coast. The trade that flows through Greycliff, though small compared to that of the eastern ports, is the sole channel through which the products of the Greyring reach the markets of the interior, and the dried fish, the whale oil, and the ambergris of Greycliff are among the most valued commodities of the western trade. The town stands as a testament to the capacity of the human people to establish and to sustain a prosperous community in the most demanding conditions that the coast of Aethelar can offer.
