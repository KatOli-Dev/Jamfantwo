## Overview

Skaldshavn is a fishing and trading port on the southern coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), situated on a stretch of coast where the mountains descend almost to the water's edge and the harbours are few and difficult of access. The town stands on a narrow shelf of land between the cliffs and the sea, and its harbour is a deep, sheltered inlet carved into the rock of the coast by the same geological forces that built the mountains above. Skaldshavn is smaller than [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) but is a thriving and self-sufficient community, and its fishing fleet and trading vessels are among the most seaworthy in Morvind.

## The Harbour and the Fishing

The harbour of Skaldshavn is a narrow inlet penetrating the coast for several hundred yards, its entrance so constricted that only one vessel may pass at a time. The harbour is deep and well-sheltered; the water within is calm even when storms rage outside, for the cliffs enclosing the inlet absorb the force of the wind and swell. Quays line both sides of the inlet, and the fishing boats and trading vessels moored there are hauled out of the water during the winter months, when ice can form within the harbour itself.

The fishing grounds off Skaldshavn are among the most productive in Morvind. The cold, nutrient-rich waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) sustain vast shoals of fish that are the foundation of the town's economy. The fishing fleet is small but hardy; boats venture far from land in pursuit of shoals, braving storms and cold that would deter less experienced mariners. The catch is landed at the harbour, salted and dried in curing houses lining the inlet, and exported to inland markets and the ports of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar).

The fishermen of Skaldshavn are among the most skilled in Morvind; the techniques of their trade have been refined over the centuries the town has been inhabited. The boats are small, open vessels that are rowed and sailed, and the fishermen work with nets and lines set in deeper waters offshore. The catch includes cod, herring, and other fish that are staples of the northern diet. The fish are processed in curing houses lining the inlet, where they are gutted, split, salted, and hung on racks to dry in the cold, clean wind blowing from the sea. The dried fish of Skaldshavn are a valued export, and the trade in dried fish is the primary source of the town's prosperity.

## Trade and the Town

The trade of Skaldshavn is secondary to its fishing but is nonetheless significant. The town is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling along the southern coast, and goods passing through its harbour include the metalwork of the interior and the manufactured goods of Aethelar. The town is also a centre for building the small, sturdy fishing boats used along the Morvind coast; the boatbuilders of Skaldshavn are noted for their skill in constructing vessels suited to the demanding conditions of the Stormfall waters.

Skaldshavn is a small, rugged town whose buildings are constructed of dark stone of the cliffs, with low profiles and thick walls that withstand the wind and salt. The streets are steep and narrow, climbing the cliff from the harbour to the plateau above, where the newer districts are situated. The population is small and entirely [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people are known for their hardihood, their skill in the difficult waters they work, and their deep attachment to the coast that sustains them. The families of Skaldshavn have fished the same grounds for generations; knowledge of the shoals, currents, and weather is passed from father to son, a tradition that is the foundation of the town's fishing culture.

The curing houses that line the inlet are among the most important buildings in Skaldshavn. The fish are processed within hours of landing, gutted and split by workers who have performed the task since childhood. The salt used in the curing is imported from the southern ports, for the local waters are too cold for the production of salt by evaporation. The dried fish are packed in barrels and stored in the warehouses that are built into the cliff, where the constant temperature preserves them through the winter. The export of the dried fish is managed by the merchants who maintain offices in the town; the trade is conducted primarily with the ports of Aethelar, where the dried fish of Skaldshavn is a valued commodity in the markets.

## Significance

Skaldshavn is one of the most important fishing ports on the southern coast of Morvind, and its catch is a valued contribution to the economy of the continent. The town is a member of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven), and its position on the remote southern coast gives it a strategic significance as a refuge and stopping point for vessels navigating the difficult waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The prosperity of Skaldshavn is modest compared to that of the larger ports, but the community is proud of its independence and its mastery of one of the most demanding environments in the known world.

## History

Skaldshavn was founded by fishermen who sought the shelter of the inlet; the natural harbour was the attraction that drew the first settlers to the site. The earliest settlement was a collection of huts on the shore of the inlet; the fishermen worked the grounds offshore and traded their catch with inland communities. The town grew slowly, for the site is remote and the access is difficult, but the productivity of the fishing grounds sustained the community, and the town developed a character and tradition distinct from the larger settlements of the coast.

The boatbuilders of Skaldshavn have been building the small, sturdy fishing vessels of the Morvind coast for centuries; the techniques of construction have been refined over the generations. The boats are built of timber brought from inland forests, and the hulls are shaped and sealed with tar distilled from pine resin of the coastal woods. The boats of Skaldshavn are regarded as among the most seaworthy in Morvind, and the fishermen who work them are among the most respected mariners on the southern coast. Membership in the Nordhaven has provided a measure of security and a market for the catch, and the town has prospered within the framework of the kingdom, a relationship that has endured through the centuries.
