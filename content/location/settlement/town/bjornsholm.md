## Overview

Bjornsholm is a coastal trading settlement on the southern coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), situated on a broad, sheltered bay that is one of the few natural harbours on a coast where the mountains leave little room for settlement. The town is smaller than [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) or [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn), but it is a prosperous and well-situated community, and its harbour is a valued stopping point for vessels trading along the coast and across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). Bjornsholm is known for the [ursine](/content/species/sapient/ursines) community that inhabits the forests behind the town, and the trade between the coastal humans and the forest ursines is a distinctive feature of the local economy.

## The Harbour and the Coast

The harbour of Bjornsholm is a broad, shallow bay that is sheltered by a pair of headlands that enclose the water on three sides, and the approach is straightforward in fair weather, though the shallows at the entrance require careful navigation. The quays are built along the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses that line the waterfront are the centre of the town's commerce. The fishing grounds off Bjornsholm are productive, and the catch is a valued supplement to the trade that flows through the harbour.

The coast around Bjornsholm is one of the few stretches of the Morvind shore where the mountains leave room for a settlement, and the shelf of land between the cliffs and the sea is broad enough to support the town and the farms that surround it. The forests that cover the slopes behind the town are among the most extensive on the southern coast, and the timber and the game that they provide are a significant part of the local economy.

The farms that surround Bjornsholm are situated on the shelf of land between the town and the cliffs, and the soil is a mixture of the alluvial deposits of the river that enters the bay and the weathered rock of the mountain slopes. The farms produce grain, vegetables, and the fodder that sustains the livestock of the region, and the pasture on the higher ground supports the sheep and the cattle that are a secondary resource of the community. The agriculture of Bjornsholm is sufficient to feed the town and to provide a modest surplus for export, and the farms are well-maintained and productive.

## Trade and the Ursine Connection

The trade of Bjornsholm is a mixture of coastal commerce and the exchange between the coast and the forest. The fishing and the coastal trade connect the town to the other ports of Morvind and the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the goods that pass through the harbour include the metalwork of the interior and the manufactured goods of the south. The trade between the coastal humans and the [ursine](/content/species/sapient/ursines) community of the inland forests is the distinctive feature of the local economy, and the fur, the meat, and the forest products that the ursines provide are exchanged for the fish, the grain, and the manufactured goods that the coast offers.

The ursines of the forests behind Bjornsholm are the most accessible community of their kind in Morvind, and the trade between the two peoples is among the most sustained and the most peaceful of any contact between the coastal humans and the forest peoples. The ursines are occasional visitors to the market square of Bjornsholm, and their presence in the town is a familiar and a welcome sight.

The ursine community of the inland forests is organised in family groups, and the groups are governed by the elders who manage the hunting and the gathering that sustain the forest economy. The ursines are a careful and a deliberate people, and their approach to the trade with the coastal humans is cautious and measured, for they value their independence and their way of life. The trade between the two peoples is conducted on terms that are mutually agreed, and the exchanges are fair and equitable, for both sides recognise that the relationship is too valuable to be exploited for short-term gain.

## History and the Forest

The founding of Bjornsholm is dated to the early centuries of the Morvind coastal settlement, when the broad bay was first recognised as a valuable harbour on a coast where natural harbours are scarce. The early settlement was a fishing community, and the first permanent buildings were constructed on the shore of the bay. The growth of the town was steady, and the discovery of the ursine community in the inland forests was a significant event in the history of Bjornsholm, for the trade that developed between the two peoples became a distinctive and a valuable feature of the local economy.

The relationship between the coastal humans and the forest ursines has been peaceful throughout the history of the town, for the two peoples have always recognised the value of co-operation and the importance of maintaining the trade that connects them. The forests behind Bjornsholm are the territory of the ursines, and the humans have never sought to settle or to exploit the woodland, for the ursines are the stewards of the forest and the providers of the forest products that the coastal community values.

## The Town

Bjornsholm is a small, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the coastal tradition of Morvind, with sturdy buildings of dark stone, steep roofs, and narrow streets that are sheltered from the wind. The town is oriented toward the harbour, and the market square is situated at the head of the main quay, where the coastal trade and the forest trade meet. The population of Bjornsholm is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a small but visible community of [ursines](/content/species/sapient/ursines) who are involved in the forest trade.

## Culture and the Coexistence

The culture of Bjornsholm is shaped by the coexistence of the coastal and the forest peoples, and the community is noted for its openness and its respect for the ways of the ursines. The market square of the town is the meeting point of the two cultures, and the exchanges that take place there are conducted with a courtesy and a formality that reflect the long history of the relationship. The humans of Bjornsholm have adopted some of the customs of the ursines, including the practice of the formal greeting that is exchanged between the traders of the two peoples before the beginning of the business, and the ursines have adopted some of the tools and the techniques of the coastal humans.

The festivals of Bjornsholm are oriented toward the sea and the forest, and the most important is the autumn fair, which coincides with the gathering of the forest products and the preparation of the town for the winter. The fair is held in the market square, and the ursines are the guests of honour, for the forest products that they provide are the most valuable commodities of the season. The fair includes the contests of strength and skill that are a tradition of both the coastal and the forest peoples, and the wrestling and the log-carrying are the most fiercely contested events.

## The Coastal Trade

The trade of Bjornsholm is connected to the wider network of the Morvind coastal trade, and the vessels that call at the harbour carry the goods of the town to the other ports of the southern coast and across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) to the markets of Aethelar. The timber and the furs of the forests are the most valuable exports, and the demand for the straight-grained wood of the northern slopes is constant in the markets of the south. The metalwork of the Morvind interior is the primary import, and the tools and the weapons that are produced in the dwarven halls are valued by the farmers and the fishermen of the coast.

The town is a member of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven), and the governance of Bjornsholm is conducted in accordance with the laws and the customs of the kingdom. The town is represented in the council of the kingdom by a delegate who is elected by the merchants and the householders of the community, and the delegate is responsible for representing the interests of Bjornsholm in the affairs of the kingdom.

## Significance

Bjornsholm is a valued port of the southern coast of Morvind, and its harbour is an important stopping point for vessels trading along the coast and across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The town is a member of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven), and its trade with the ursine community of the inland forests is a distinctive and a valuable component of the local economy. The prosperity of Bjornsholm is modest but secure, and the community is a model of the coexistence between the coastal and the forest peoples that is characteristic of the Morvind coast.
