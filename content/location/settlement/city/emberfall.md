## Overview

Emberfall is a southern trade city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated at the junction of the overland routes near the northern threshold of the [Emberlands](/content/location/settlement/region/emberlands). The city stands at the point where the roads from the eastern coast, the western coast, and the southern plains converge, and its position at the meeting of these routes has made it the primary hub of southern inland trade. Emberfall is a bustling, well-connected city of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and its prosperity is built upon the traffic that flows through its streets and the commerce that accumulates at the junction.

## The Roads and the Junction

The roads that converge on Emberfall are the principal overland routes of southern Aethelar, and the city stands at the point where they meet, a junction that has been a natural gathering point for travellers and traders since the earliest days of human settlement in the region. The east road runs to [Sallowmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sallowmere) and the eastern coast, the west road runs to [Brackenford](/content/location/settlement/town/brackenford) and the western interior, and the north road runs to [Sunmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sunmere) and the southern plains. The traffic that passes through Emberfall includes the goods of the eastern coast, the western interior, and the southern plains, and the city's position at the junction of these routes makes it a natural point of exchange.

The roads that enter the city are broad and well-maintained, for the traffic that uses them is heavy and constant, and the tolls on the roads are a significant source of the city's revenue. The caravans that travel the roads are a common sight in the streets of Emberfall, and the inns, the taverns, and the stables that serve the travellers are a significant part of the city's economy.

The junction has been a gathering point since before the founding of the city, for the natural geography of the southern interior funnels the overland routes toward this single point. The earliest settlements at the junction were seasonal camps, established by the traders and the drovers who travelled the roads and who needed a place to rest, to exchange goods, and to wait for companions before continuing into the less-settled country beyond. The permanent settlement that became Emberfall was founded in the early centuries of human expansion into the southern interior, and the city grew steadily as the roads were improved and the traffic upon them increased.

## History and Foundation

The founding of Emberfall is dated to the early centuries of the human settlement of southern Aethelar, when the junction of the roads was formalised by the construction of the first permanent buildings. The early settlement was a modest cluster of inns, stables, and storehouses that served the traders who passed through the junction, but the volume of trade that flowed along the roads was sufficient to support a growing community. The city was granted its charter by the council of the southern plains in the year 312, and the charter established the rights of the city to collect tolls on the roads and to regulate the trade that passed through its streets.

The growth of Emberfall was steady rather than dramatic, for the city expanded in proportion to the trade that flowed through its streets, and the prosperity of the city was a reflection of the prosperity of the southern interior as a whole. The city weathered the periods of disturbance that occasionally disrupted the trade of the south, for the roads that converged on Emberfall were too valuable to be abandoned, and the trade that flowed along them resumed as soon as the disturbances were resolved. The construction of the stone bridge over the river that runs through the southern quarter of the city, completed in the year 489, was a significant milestone in the development of Emberfall, for it replaced the older wooden bridge that had been destroyed by floods on more than one occasion and provided a reliable crossing for the heavy traffic of the west road.

## Trade and the Market

The trade of Emberfall is the most diverse of any southern inland city, for the goods that pass through the city come from every region of the southern continent. The agricultural products of the southern plains, the manufactured goods of the eastern coast, the timber and the metalwork of the western interior, and the exotic products of the warm regions beyond the frontier are all traded at Emberfall, and the market square of the city is one of the busiest in the south. The market that is held there is a daily affair, for the constant traffic of the roads ensures a steady flow of goods and customers.

The trading houses of Emberfall are among the oldest in the southern interior, and the most established of them have operated in the city for generations. The houses that deal in the metal trade, the grain trade, and the cloth trade are the most prominent, and their agents are stationed in every city that lies along the roads that converge on the junction. The competition between the trading houses is vigorous but orderly, for the merchants of Emberfall have long understood that the reputation of the city as a fair and reliable market is the foundation of its prosperity.

The city is also a centre for the financial services that support the trade of the southern routes, and the bankers and the money-changers of Emberfall are among the most influential in the southern economy. The warehouses and the counting houses that line the main streets are substantial buildings, and the trading houses that occupy them are among the most prosperous in the southern inland.

## The City

Emberfall is a large, bustling city, and its architecture reflects its function as a trade hub, with broad streets, substantial buildings, and a commercial district that dominates the city centre. The city is built around the junction of the roads, and the main streets radiate outward from the central market square in the pattern of the routes that converge on the city. The central market square of Emberfall is the heart of the city, and it is a broad, paved space that is surrounded by the warehouses, the counting houses, and the inns that serve the trade of the junction. The square is the point where the main streets converge, and the daily market that is held there is the busiest in the southern interior. The buildings that line the square are the most substantial in the city, and their facades reflect the wealth and the confidence of the trading houses that occupy them. The residential districts of Emberfall are situated beyond the commercial centre, and the houses are well-built and comfortable, arranged along the broad streets that radiate from the square.

The population of Emberfall is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) who are involved in the metal trade and the maintenance of the roads.

## Culture and Daily Life

The culture of Emberfall is shaped by its function as a crossroads city, for the constant flow of travellers and traders through the streets has created a community that is open, diverse, and accustomed to the ways of distant places. The inns and taverns of the city are famed for the variety of their cuisine, for the cooks of Emberfall draw upon the ingredients and the recipes of every region that trades with the city. The festivals of the city are also shaped by the crossroads, for the calendar of Emberfall includes the celebration of the harvest festivals of the plains, the spring festivals of the eastern coast, and the autumn fairs of the western interior.

The city is governed by a council of merchants and guildmasters, and the governance is oriented toward the maintenance of the roads, the regulation of the market, and the collection of the tolls that fund the operations of the city. The council meets in the council hall that stands on the central market square, and the hall is a substantial building of stone that is the most prominent public building in the city. The dwarven community of Emberfall is represented on the council, for the dwarves are involved in the metal trade and the maintenance of the roads, and their expertise in engineering and construction is a valued contribution to the governance of the city.

## Governance and the Council

Emberfall is governed by a council of merchants and guildmasters, and the governance of the city reflects its character as a commercial community where the trading houses hold the greatest influence. The council is composed of representatives of the principal guilds, the trading companies, and the financial institutions, and it convenes in the council hall that stands on the western side of the market square. The council is responsible for the maintenance of the roads, the collection of the tolls, the regulation of the market, and the adjudication of the commercial disputes that arise from the constant traffic of goods and money that flows through the city.

The council also manages the relations between Emberfall and the surrounding communities, and the ambassadors of the city are a familiar presence at the courts of the southern cities and the councils of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation). The governance of Emberfall is noted for its efficiency and its pragmatism, for the merchants who dominate the council understand that the prosperity of the city depends upon the free flow of trade and the maintenance of the roads that carry it.

## The Seasons and the Festivals

The rhythm of life in Emberfall is shaped by the seasons of trade, for the traffic on the roads varies with the time of year, and the city is busiest during the spring and the autumn, when the weather is mild and the roads are passable. The spring fair of Emberfall is the largest commercial gathering in the southern interior, and the merchants and the traders who attend it come from every region of the south and from the cities of the north and the coast. The fair is held in the market square and the surrounding streets, and it lasts for a full week, during which the normal business of the city is suspended and the entire population turns its attention to the festivities and the commerce of the occasion.

The autumn fair is a smaller affair, but it is nonetheless a significant event, for it coincides with the harvest of the southern plains and the gathering of the agricultural products that form the bulk of the winter trade. The festivals of Emberfall are occasions of celebration as well as commerce, and the feasts, the music, and the entertainments that accompany the fairs are a valued part of the cultural life of the city. The inns and the taverns of Emberfall are filled to capacity during the fair days, and the stable yards and the warehouse floors are turned over to the accommodation of the visitors who crowd the city.

## Significance

Emberfall is the primary hub of southern inland trade, and its position at the junction of the overland routes makes it the most connected settlement in the southern interior. The city is a significant member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and its trade and financial services are a valuable component of the economy of the southern region. The prosperity of Emberfall is a direct consequence of its position, and the city has grown and thrived on the commerce that flows through its streets. The reputation of Emberfall as a fair, well-ordered, and hospitable market has sustained the confidence of the traders who pass through its gates, and the city's commitment to the maintenance of the roads and the regulation of the trade has ensured that the junction remains the primary hub of southern inland commerce for generation after generation.
