## Overview

Hafnir is a port on the southeastern coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), situated near the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) where the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) meets the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The city stands on a natural harbour that is one of the best on the southeastern coast, and its position near the strait has made it a significant point in the trade network that connects Morvind to the markets of Aethelar and Sahrana. Hafnir is smaller than [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven), but its proximity to the strait gives it a commercial importance that is disproportionate to its size.

## The Harbour and the Strait

The harbour of Hafnir is a deep, sheltered bay that is protected from the open sea by an island that lies across the entrance, and the approach is straightforward in most weather, though the currents that run through the nearby strait can affect the passage. The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses that serve the strait trade are situated along the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels that are preparing to enter the strait or that have just passed through it, and the port is a natural staging point for the trade that flows between the two seas.

The proximity of the strait is the defining feature of Hafnir's economy, for the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) is the only passage between the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and all vessels that travel between the two must pass through these waters. The pilots of Hafnir are among the most experienced in the strait trade, and the town is a base for the pilotage services that guide vessels through the narrow and treacherous channel.

The pilotage service of Hafnir is a regulated profession, and the pilots are trained and licensed by the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact), the joint governance of the strait towns. The training of a pilot is a long and demanding process, for the strait is a complex and treacherous channel, and the knowledge that is required to navigate it safely is gained only through years of experience on the water. The pilots of Hafnir are among the best-paid and the most respected figures in the town, for the safety of the vessels and the cargo that passes through the strait depends upon their skill and their judgement.

## Trade and the Strait Connection

The trade of Hafnir is dominated by the strait traffic, and the goods that pass through the harbour include the metalwork of the Morvind interior, the grain and cloth of Aethelar, and the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The town is a centre for the transhipment of goods between the vessels that trade on the Stormfall and those that trade on the Auric, and the warehouses of Hafnir are filled with the goods that are transferred between the two routes. The tolls and the pilotage fees that are collected at Hafnir are a significant source of the town's revenue, and the management of the strait trade is the primary occupation of the merchants and the officials of the town.

The town is also a centre for the repair and the supply of vessels that have passed through the strait, and the shipyards and the chandleries of Hafnir are a significant part of the local economy. The vessels that navigate the strait are often in need of repair after the difficult passage, and the shipwrights of Hafnir are skilled in the rapid and the reliable maintenance that the strait trade demands.

## History and the Strait

The founding of Hafnir is dated to the early centuries of the Morvind coastal settlement, when the natural harbour was first recognised as a valuable staging point for the trade that flowed through the strait. The early settlement was a modest community of fishermen and pilots, but the growth of the strait trade in the centuries that followed transformed Hafnir into a prosperous and well-built town. The construction of the stone quays and the warehouses that line the waterfront was undertaken in the year 445, and the construction marked the transformation of Hafnir from a fishing village into a significant commercial port.

The history of Hafnir is closely tied to the history of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact), for the town was a founding member of the compact, and the governance of the strait trade has been a central concern of the town's merchants and officials since the compact was established. The compact has ensured that the pilotage and the tolls of the strait are managed co-operatively by the towns that border the channel, and the prosperity of Hafnir has been sustained by the orderly management of the trade that the compact provides.

## The City

Hafnir is a well-built, prosperous town, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the strait trade has brought, with substantial buildings of stone, well-paved streets, and a commercial district that is oriented toward the harbour. The population of Hafnir is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) who are involved in the metal trade and the management of the overland routes that connect the port to the interior.

## The Waterfront and the Warehouses

The waterfront of Hafnir is the commercial heart of the town, and the quays and the warehouses that line the inner shore of the bay are the most substantial buildings in the settlement. The warehouses are divided between the transit stores, where the goods that are transferred between the two routes are held pending transhipment, and the bonded stores, where the high-value goods such as the spices and the precious metals are held under the supervision of the customs officials. The chandleries and the provisioners that serve the vessels are situated along the quays, and the shipyards and the repair yards are located at the southern end of the bay, where the shore is broad enough to accommodate the ways and the workshops.

The commercial district of Hafnir is situated behind the waterfront, and the streets are well-paved and well-maintained, for the wealth that the strait trade has brought is reflected in the quality of the public buildings and the private houses. The merchant houses of Hafnir are substantial buildings of stone, and their facades are ornamented in the manner of the Morvind coastal tradition, with carved doorways and window frames that reflect the prosperity of the community. The residential districts are situated on the slopes above the harbour, and the houses are well-built and comfortable, with views across the bay and the strait.

## Culture and the Strait

The culture of Hafnir is shaped by the strait, for the town is oriented toward the channel and the trade that flows through it, and the rhythms of the community are the rhythms of the shipping and the tides. The people of Hafnir are a mixture of sailors, merchants, and artisans, and the community is diverse and cosmopolitan, for the constant traffic of vessels through the strait brings visitors from every coast and every continent. The taverns and the inns of the town are famed for the variety of their company, for the mariners and the merchants who gather there are drawn from every port that trades on the strait.

The festivals of Hafnir are oriented toward the sea and the strait, and the most important is the annual feast of the pilotage, which celebrates the founding of the pilotage service and the skill of the pilots who guide the vessels through the channel. The feast is held on the waterfront, and the pilots are the guests of honour, for the safety of the trade and the prosperity of the town depend upon their expertise. The festival includes the regatta, in which the fishing boats and the pilot vessels race across the bay, and the competition is a valued and a fiercely contested tradition.

## Significance

Hafnir is the primary Morvind-side port of the strait trade, and its position near the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) gives it a commercial importance that is disproportionate to its size. The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact), the joint governance of the strait towns, and its role in the pilotage and the transhipment of the strait trade is a significant component of the economy of the strait region. The prosperity of Hafnir is built upon the trade that flows through the strait, and the town is a key point in the trade network that connects Morvind to the wider world.
