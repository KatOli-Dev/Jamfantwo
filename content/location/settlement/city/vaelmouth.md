## A City at the Mouth of the Great River

Vaelmouth stands at the mouth of the [River Vael](/content/location/natural/feature/river-vael), the greatest waterway of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the river empties into the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) on the eastern coast. The city is the principal port of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael), and its harbour is the gateway through which the goods of the Vael valley reach the wider world. The River Vael is navigable for much of its length, and the trade flowing downriver from the interior passes through Vaelmouth on its way to the markets of the coast and the oceans beyond, making the city a natural point of exchange between the river and the sea.

## The River Mouth Harbour and the Sandbanks

The harbour of Vaelmouth is formed by the broad mouth of the River Vael, where the current slows and the water spreads wide before meeting the sea. The harbour is sheltered by a pair of headlands that enclose the bay, and the depth of the water is sufficient for the largest vessels, though the sandbanks that shift with the current require the services of experienced pilots. The quays line both banks of the river, and the waterfront is a continuous stretch of warehouses, granaries, and the offices of merchants who manage the trade between the river and the sea.

The River Vael is the lifeblood of Vaelmouth, and the city's prosperity is built upon the goods that travel its waters. Grain, timber, and the produce of the Vael valley flow downriver to the harbour, where they are loaded onto seagoing vessels for transport to the markets of the eastern coast and the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The return cargo includes goods of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), carried upriver to the interior, and the exchange of upriver and downriver goods is the foundation of the city's commerce.

The pilots of Vaelmouth are among the most skilled on the eastern coast, for the sandbanks at the river mouth shift with every season, and the channels navigable in one year may be impassable in the next. The pilotage service is regulated by the harbour authority, and pilots are licensed after a long apprenticeship that includes memorisation of the riverbed and tides. The fees for pilotage are a significant source of revenue for the city, and the service is regarded as essential, for the loss of a vessel on the sandbanks would disrupt trade and damage the reputation of the port.

## The Twin Banks, Bridges, and the Royal Seat

Vaelmouth is a well-built and prosperous city, smaller than [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) but no less proud of its heritage. The city is laid out along both banks of the river, connected by a series of bridges that are among the finest in Aethelar. The oldest portions stand on the higher ground above the floodplain, where the first settlements were established. The streets of the older quarters are narrow and winding, while the newer districts are broader and more regular, reflecting the planned expansion of the city during its period of greatest growth.

The population of Vaelmouth is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with smaller numbers of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) and [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) drawn by the trade and opportunities of the port. The city is the seat of the court of the Vael, and the administrative buildings, barracks, and residence of the king are situated in the oldest quarter, overlooking the harbour and the river. The court of Vael is a centre of political influence on the eastern coast, and the presence of the king and his household is a source of patronage and employment sustaining a large population of servants, craftsmen, and suppliers.

The bridges of Vaelmouth are among the finest in Aethelar. The oldest is a stone arch spanning the river in three elegant spans, built by dwarven engineers in the golden age of the kingdom. The bridge is wide enough for two wagons to pass and is lined with stalls of merchants selling their goods to the traffic crossing between the two halves of the city. The newer bridges are built of timber and iron and are raised to allow the passage of masted vessels moving up and down the river. The harbour authority regulates the opening and closing of the bridges with a careful eye to the flow of commerce.

## Commercial and Political Centre of the Vael

Vaelmouth is the commercial and political centre of the Vael, and its harbour is the primary outlet for the produce of the Vael valley, the most fertile region of Aethelar. The trade flowing through the city connects the interior of the continent to the markets of the coast and the oceans, and the wealth accumulating in Vaelmouth funds the maintenance of the river, roads, and defences of the kingdom. The city is a rival of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) in the trade of the eastern coast, and the competition between the two ports is a driving force in the economy of the continent.

## From Ferry Crossing to Royal Capital

The site of Vaelmouth was occupied first by a fishing village and a ferry crossing at the mouth of the great river. [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) chose the high ground above that settlement for her palace and chartered the harbour city that bears the river's name, making it the capital of the newly united [Vael](/content/government/national/vael). The royal court and the growth of the river trade transformed the old village into a city, while the harbour below the palace became the principal outlet of the realm.

The rivalry between Vaelmouth and Eldreth has shaped the history of the eastern coast, and the two cities have competed for the trade of the interior and the ocean for centuries. The competition has been conducted chiefly through commerce, but there have been periods of tension when the interests of the two ports have clashed, and the relationship between the Vael and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) has been coloured by the rivalry of their capitals. The maintenance of the river has been a constant concern of the kings of Vael, for the sandbanks and silting of the channel threaten the trade that is the foundation of the kingdom's prosperity, and the dredging of the harbour is a recurring expense borne by the royal treasury.
