## Overview

Vaelmouth stands at the mouth of the [River Vael](/content/location/natural/feature/river-vael), the greatest waterway of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the river empties into the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) on the eastern coast. The city is the principal port of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael), and its harbour is the gateway through which the goods of the Vael valley reach the wider world. The River Vael is navigable for much of its length, and the trade that flows downriver from the interior passes through Vaelmouth on its way to the markets of the coast and the oceans beyond, making the city a natural point of exchange between the river and the sea.

## The River and the Harbour

The harbour of Vaelmouth is formed by the broad mouth of the River Vael, where the current slows and the water spreads wide before meeting the sea. The harbour is sheltered by a pair of headlands that enclose the bay, and the depth of the water is sufficient for the largest vessels, though the sandbanks that shift with the current require the services of experienced pilots. The quays line both banks of the river, and the waterfront is a continuous stretch of warehouses, granaries, and the offices of the merchants who manage the trade between the river and the sea.

The River Vael is the lifeblood of Vaelmouth, and the city's prosperity is built upon the goods that travel its waters. Grain, timber, and the produce of the Vael valley flow downriver to the harbour, where they are loaded onto seagoing vessels for transport to the markets of the eastern coast and the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The return cargo includes the goods of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), which are carried upriver to the interior, and the exchange of upriver and downriver goods is the foundation of the city's commerce.

The pilots of Vaelmouth are among the most skilled on the eastern coast, for the sandbanks at the river mouth shift with every season, and the channels that are navigable in one year may be impassable in the next. The pilotage service is regulated by the harbour authority, and the pilots are licensed after a long apprenticeship that includes the memorisation of the riverbed and the tides. The fees for pilotage are a significant source of revenue for the city, and the service is regarded as essential, for the loss of a vessel on the sandbanks would disrupt the trade and damage the reputation of the port.

## The City

Vaelmouth is a well-built and prosperous city, smaller than [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) but no less proud of its heritage. The city is laid out along both banks of the river, connected by a series of bridges that are among the finest in Aethelar, and the oldest portions of the city stand on the higher ground above the floodplain, where the first settlements were established. The streets of the older quarters are narrow and winding, while the newer districts are broader and more regular, reflecting the planned expansion of the city during its period of greatest growth.

The population of Vaelmouth is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with smaller numbers of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) and [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) who are drawn by the trade and the opportunities of the port. The city is the seat of the court of the Kingdom of Vael, and the administrative buildings, the barracks, and the residence of the king are situated in the oldest quarter, overlooking the harbour and the river. The court of Vael is a centre of political influence on the eastern coast, and the presence of the king and his household is a source of patronage and employment that sustains a large population of servants, craftsmen, and suppliers.

The bridges of Vaelmouth are among the finest in Aethelar, and the oldest of them is a stone arch that spans the river in three elegant spans, built by dwarven engineers in the golden age of the kingdom. The bridge is wide enough for two wagons to pass, and it is lined with the stalls of the merchants who sell their goods to the traffic that crosses between the two halves of the city. The newer bridges are built of timber and iron, and they are raised to allow the passage of the masted vessels that move up and down the river, for the traffic of the river and the traffic of the bridges must coexist, and the harbour authority regulates the opening and the closing of the bridges with a careful eye to the flow of commerce.

## Significance

Vaelmouth is the commercial and political centre of the Kingdom of Vael, and its harbour is the primary outlet for the produce of the Vael valley, the most fertile region of Aethelar. The trade that flows through the city connects the interior of the continent to the markets of the coast and the oceans, and the wealth that accumulates in Vaelmouth funds the maintenance of the river, the roads, and the defences of the kingdom. The city is a rival of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) in the trade of the eastern coast, and the competition between the two ports is a driving force in the economy of the continent.

## History

The site of Vaelmouth was occupied first by a fishing village and a ferry crossing at the mouth of the great river. [Queen Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) chose the high ground above that settlement for her palace and chartered the harbour city that bears the river's name, making it the capital of the newly united [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael). The royal court and the growth of the river trade transformed the old village into a city, while the harbour below the palace became the principal outlet of the realm.

The rivalry between Vaelmouth and Eldreth has shaped the history of the eastern coast, and the two cities have competed for the trade of the interior and the ocean for centuries. The competition has been conducted chiefly through commerce, but there have been periods of tension when the interests of the two ports have clashed, and the relationship between the Kingdom of Vael and the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) has been coloured by the rivalry of their capitals. The maintenance of the river has been a constant concern of the kings of Vael, for the sandbanks and the silting of the channel are a threat to the trade that is the foundation of the kingdom's prosperity, and the dredging of the harbour is a recurring expense that is borne by the royal treasury.
