## A Port City on the Auric Coast

Qassir is a port city-state on the Auric coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), situated on the eastern shore where the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) meets the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast. The city stands on a natural harbour sheltered by a headland of rock, and its position on the eastern coast gives it a role in the Auric trade distinct from the cities of the northern coast. Qassir is a prosperous city whose wealth is built upon the trade that flows through its harbour and the maritime connections linking the eastern Sahrani coast to the islands and the northern continents.

## The Sheltered Bay and the Eastern Routes

The harbour of Qassir is a deep sheltered bay protected by the headland extending from the northern end of the city, and the approach is marked by a lighthouse visible for many miles across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses serving the Auric trade line the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels travelling between Sahrana and the islands of the Auric, and the trade flowing through the port includes the spices and dyes of the Sahrani coast and the products of the islands: exotic fruits, shells, and other materials found only in the island communities.

The trade of Qassir combines Sahrani exports with goods imported from the islands and northern continents, and the city is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling between the Sahrani coast and the islands. The merchants of Qassir are skilled in managing the island trade, organising voyages, provisioning, and exchange of goods between the coast and the islands. The island routes connecting Qassir to the island communities are among the most valued trade routes in the Auric.

## The Auric Isles and the Tortolan Connection

The islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) are the primary trading partners of Qassir, and the relationship between the city and the island communities is one of mutual benefit and long-standing co-operation. The [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) are the closest of the island communities to the Sahrani coast, and the trade between Qassir and the isles is the most valuable component of the city's commerce. Goods imported from the islands include exotic fruits, shells, and other materials found only in the island communities, and these are exported to the markets of the northern continents, where they command high prices.

The [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) of the warm coasts and islands are regular visitors to the harbour of Qassir, and the trade between the coastal humans and the tortolan communities is a valued part of the city's commerce. The tortolans provide the products of the islands and warm coasts, and the city provides the manufactured goods and grain that the tortolan communities need. The tortolan traders are a familiar sight in the markets of the city, and the relationship between the two peoples is among the most enduring and harmonious in the Auric.

The merchants of Qassir have developed a network of contacts and agents in the island communities, and the information they gather about conditions in the islands and the availability of goods is a valuable asset in managing the trade. Merchants organise voyages to the islands, and the provisioning and crews required are assembled at the harbour of Qassir. The island routes maintained by the merchants of Qassir are among the most valued in the Auric, and the knowledge of the routes and conditions is a closely guarded asset passed from generation to generation within the merchant families of the city.

## Light-Coloured Stone and the Public Buildings

Qassir is a well-built, prosperous city whose architecture reflects the character of the eastern coast, with buildings of light-coloured stone, shaded courtyards, and the gardens and fountains that are the hallmark of the Sahrani architectural tradition. The streets are broad and well-paved, and the public buildings are substantial and well-maintained. The harbour is the heart of the city, and the waterfront is the busiest and most prosperous district. The population of Qassir is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a diverse community of traders and merchants from the islands and the northern continents.

The public buildings of Qassir include the council hall, the treasury, and the warehouses maintained by the city for the storage of goods passing through the harbour. The council hall is the centre of governance, and the officials who administer the city are chosen from the merchant and civic families. The treasury is the repository of revenue collected from tolls and taxes on trade, and the funds are used to maintain the harbour, fortifications, and public works that serve the city and the trade.

## From Trading Post to League Member

The founding of Qassir is attributed to early Sahrani settlers who established the first trading posts on the eastern coast, and the natural harbour and the city's position on the Auric trade routes provided the foundation for growth. The city developed in parallel with the growth of the island trade, and the relationship between Qassir and the island communities was established in the earliest period of the city's history. Qassir was admitted to the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) in the early centuries of the league's existence, and membership has been a significant factor in the prosperity and influence of the city.

The history of Qassir has been one of steady growth and relative stability, for the eastern coast has been less exposed to the conflicts that have affected the northern coast, and the city has been able to develop its trade and infrastructure without the disruption of war. The city has maintained good relations with the island communities and the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) throughout its history, and the co-operation between the city and the confederation has been a factor in the security and efficiency of the island trade.

## The Primary Eastern Port of Sahrana

Qassir is the primary port of the eastern Sahrani coast and the main point of contact between Sahrana and the islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and its trade is a valued component of the economy of the Sahrani coast. The city is a member of the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league), and its position on the eastern coast gives it a commercial significance distinct from the cities of the northern coast. The significance of Qassir lies in its control of the island trade, a valuable and distinctive component of Sahrani commerce, and the city is a symbol of the maritime connections that link Sahrana to the wider world.

The cultural influence of Qassir is felt in the island communities and the eastern coast, for the architecture and traditions of the city have been adopted and adapted by settlements that look to Qassir as their primary contact with the Sahrani mainland. The city is a meeting point of Sahrani and island cultures, and the diversity of the population and trade is a valued feature of the character of the city.
