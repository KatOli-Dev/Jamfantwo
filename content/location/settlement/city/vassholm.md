## Overview

Vassholm is a city of the cold north, founded upon a small island at the mouth of a sheltered harbour on the southern flank of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands). The island is connected to the mainland by a stone causeway that can be raised in time of danger, and the harbour has been a useful refuge for the vessels that work the coast and the seas to the north since the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The city takes its name from an older northern word meaning "harbour of refuge," and the name has been preserved in the city's chronicles since its founding.

Vassholm is the principal northern port for the trade in fish, seal-oil, and the wool of the inland valleys, and the city is the seat of the magistrates who govern the region of the southern [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands). The city has grown steadily since its founding, and the city is today among the larger of the towns of the cold north. The chronicler notes that Vassholm is held in particular esteem by the fishermen and the merchants of the cold north, and the magistrates mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies.

## Founding

The chronicles of the city attribute the founding of Vassholm to the fishermen and the seal-hunters who settled upon the natural harbour in the generation before the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The settlement was founded upon the small island that commands the mouth of the harbour, and the settlers built the first causeway to connect the island to the mainland. The chroniclers of the academies note that the founding was a deliberate choice, made by the elders of the fishing clans in council, to seek a site that would be defensible against the reavers who troubled the coast.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed in the chronicles to the accord that the fishermen made with the merchants who traded with the inland valleys and with the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds). The accord established the standards of the fish and the seal-oil, the rules of the trade, and the procedures by which the disputes were adjudicated. The accord has been revised from time to time but never replaced, and the accord is preserved in the archives of the magistrates of Vassholm.

## The Harbour

The harbour of Vassholm is one of the better natural harbours of the cold north. The harbour is sheltered from the prevailing winds by the cliffs that rise to the east, and the harbour is deep enough for the larger vessels that carry the cargoes of the trade. The harbour is divided into an inner basin for the fishing boats and an outer basin for the merchant vessels, and the basins are separated by a stone mole that the magistrates erected in the early generations of the city's growth.

The chronicler notes that the harbour has been the foundation of the prosperity of Vassholm since the founding of the city, and the harbour has been carefully maintained by the magistrates ever since. The mole has been repaired several times after the storms that the chroniclers have recorded in the city's chronicles, and the basins have been dredged at intervals to keep the channels navigable. The chronicler observes that the maintenance has been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the harbour, and the safety is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the city.

## The Quays

The quays of Vassholm line the inner basin and the outer basin, and the quays are the principal points of contact between the city and the vessels that call at the harbour. The quays were built by the magistrates of the city in the early generations of the city's growth, and the quays have been maintained and extended over the centuries. The chronicler notes that the quays are the foundation of the commerce of the city, and the quays are held in particular esteem by the merchants and the fishermen of the cold north.

The quays are served by the warehouses that the merchants have erected upon the land behind them, and the warehouses are the principal storage of the goods that the merchants handle. The chronicler notes that the warehouses have been built up over the centuries, and the warehouses are the most substantial of the buildings of the city. The chronicler observes that the warehouses are the foundation of the storage of the goods that the merchants handle, and the storage is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the city.

## The Magistrates

The city is governed by the magistrates of Vassholm, who are appointed by the council of the city from among the principal merchants and the elders of the fishing clans. The magistrates hold office for a term of three years, and the magistrates govern the city in concert with the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) and the [Heartwood Fellowship](/content/government/local/heartwood-fellowship). The chronicler notes that the magistrates are held in particular esteem by the citizens of the city, and the magistrates mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies.

The magistrates maintain the harbour, the quays, the warehouses, the roads, and the public order of the city. The magistrates also oversee the collection of the small dues that pay for the maintenance of the harbour and the roads, and the magistrates audit the accounts of the dues twice in each year. The chronicler notes that the audit has been the foundation of the careful management of the city's finances, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants and the fishermen of the city.

## The Trade

The trade of Vassholm is the principal business of the city, and the trade has been the foundation of the prosperity of the city since the founding. The merchants of the city trade in fish, seal-oil, the wool of the inland valleys, the cloth of the inland towns, and the iron and the steel of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds). The chronicler notes that the trade has been the foundation of the contact between the city and the towns of the inland valleys, and the contact is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of both parties.

The trade is regulated by the magistrates in consultation with the [Vintners and Cellarers](/content/government/local/vintners-and-cellarers) and the merchants of the principal towns of the inland valleys. The chronicler notes that the regulation has been the foundation of the careful management of the trade, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the city. The trade is conducted through the harbour and the quays, and the merchants of the city travel the roads of the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) to conduct the trade.

## Significance

Vassholm is among the larger of the cities of the cold north, and the city is the foundation of the trade in fish and seal-oil of the region of the southern [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands). The harbour, the quays, the warehouses, the magistrates, and the trade are the inheritance that the city has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that Vassholm is held in particular memory by the magistrates and the merchants of the cold north, and the magistrates and the merchants mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies. The ceremonies are among the most important of the public observances of the cold north, and the ceremonies are attended by the magistrates and the principal citizens of every city of the region.
