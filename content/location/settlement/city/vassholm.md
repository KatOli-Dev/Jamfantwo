## A Harbour of Refuge on the Morvind Uplands

Vassholm is a city of the cold north, founded upon a small island at the mouth of a sheltered harbour on the southern flank of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands). The island is connected to the mainland by a stone causeway that can be raised in time of danger, and the harbour has served as a refuge for vessels working the coast and the northern seas since the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The city takes its name from an older northern word meaning "harbour of refuge." Vassholm is the principal northern port for the trade in fish, seal-oil, and the wool of the inland valleys and is the seat of the magistrates who govern the region of the southern [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands). The public hospital of the city is overseen by [Joren Vassholm](/content/people/notable/joren-vassholm), the chief healer whose catalogue of remedies is the foundation of present-day healing in the cold north. It ranks among the larger towns of the cold north.

## The Fishermen and Seal-Hunters' Settlement

The founding of Vassholm is attributed to the fishermen and seal-hunters who settled upon the natural harbour in the generation before the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The settlement was established on the small island that commands the mouth of the harbour, and the settlers built the first causeway to connect the island to the mainland. The elders of the fishing clans chose the site deliberately for its defensibility against the reavers who troubled the coast.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed to an accord the fishermen made with the merchants who traded with the inland valleys and the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds). The accord established the standards for fish and seal-oil, the rules of trade, and the procedures for adjudicating disputes. It has been revised from time to time but never replaced, and it is preserved in the archives of the magistrates of Vassholm.

## The Inner and Outer Basins of the Northern Harbour

The harbour of Vassholm is one of the better natural harbours of the cold north. It is sheltered from the prevailing winds by the cliffs that rise to the east and deep enough for the larger vessels that carry the cargoes of the trade. The harbour is divided into an inner basin for fishing boats and an outer basin for merchant vessels, separated by a stone mole erected by the magistrates in the early generations of the city's growth.

The harbour has been fundamental to Vassholm's prosperity since its founding. The mole has been repaired several times after storm damage, and the basins have been dredged at intervals to keep the channels navigable. The safety of the harbour has encouraged the growth of the merchant community and sustained the trade that passes through the city. The inner basin shelters a substantial fishing fleet that supplies the city and the surrounding region, while the outer basin accommodates the merchant vessels that carry the goods of the cold north to the markets of the coast.

## The Quays and the Warehouse District

The quays of Vassholm line both the inner and outer basins and are the principal points of contact between the city and the vessels that call at the harbour. They were built by the magistrates in the early generations of the city's growth and have been maintained and extended over the centuries. Behind the quays stand the warehouses erected by the merchants for the storage of goods. These warehouses are the most substantial buildings in the city, their size and solid construction reflecting the volume of trade that passes through the port.

The warehouses of Vassholm serve a vital function in the trade of the cold north. Fish and seal-oil are processed and stored here before shipment to the inland valleys, while wool and cloth from the interior are held until they can be loaded onto vessels for the coastal trade. The largest warehouses are operated by the merchant families who have dominated the trade of Vassholm for generations, and their agents oversee the sorting, grading, and packing of goods according to the standards established by the original accord.

## Governance by the Appointed Magistrates

Vassholm is governed by magistrates appointed by the city council from among the principal merchants and elders of the fishing clans. They hold office for a term of three years and govern in concert with the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) and the [Heartwood Fellowship](/content/government/local/heartwood-fellowship). The magistrates are responsible for maintaining the harbour, quays, warehouses, roads, and public order. They oversee the collection of dues that fund the maintenance of the harbour and roads and audit the accounts twice each year.

## Fish, Seal-Oil, and the Northern Wool

The trade of Vassholm is the principal business of the city. Merchants trade in fish, seal-oil, the wool of the inland valleys, the cloth of the inland towns, and the iron and steel of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds). This trade connects the city to the towns of the inland valleys, generating wealth for merchants on both sides. The trade is regulated by the magistrates in consultation with the [Vintners and Cellarers](/content/government/local/vintners-and-cellarers) and the merchants of the principal towns of the inland valleys. Commerce is conducted through the harbour and quays, and merchants travel the roads of the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) to conduct their business.

The seasonal rhythm of the trade is shaped by the fishing calendar and the weather of the northern seas. The fishing fleet puts to sea in the spring and summer, returning with catches that are processed and stored in the warehouses for shipment during the autumn and winter. The seal-hunters operate in the late winter and early spring, when the seals gather on the ice floes that form along the coast. The wool trade follows the agricultural calendar of the inland valleys, with the fleeces arriving at Vassholm in the summer after the shearing.

## The Foundation of Northern Fish and Oil Trade

Vassholm is among the larger cities of the cold north and is the foundation of the trade in fish and seal-oil in the region of the southern [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands). The harbour, quays, warehouses, and governance structures are the inheritance the city has bequeathed to the present age. The anniversary ceremonies of the founding are among the most important public observances of the cold north, attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every city in the region. The city's position on the defensible island and its management of the harbour trade have sustained its prosperity through centuries of changing conditions in the northern seas.
