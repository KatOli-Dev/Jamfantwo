## Overview

Khalisar is the greatest of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coastal city-states and the wealthiest and most influential settlement on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The city stands on a broad, natural harbour on the coast where the warm currents of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) meet the fertile strip of the northern coast. Its position at the centre of the most productive region of the Sahrani coast has made it the primary hub of trade between Sahrana and the northern continents. Khalisar is a large, sophisticated city whose wealth, learning, and political influence are unmatched by any other settlement in Sahrana.

## The Harbour and the Trade

The harbour of Khalisar is one of the finest on the Sahrani coast, a deep, sheltered bay protected by a natural breakwater of rock and coral, with lighthouses that are among the tallest in the known world marking the approach. The quays extend for nearly a mile along the waterfront and are lined with the warehouses, counting houses, and offices of the merchants who manage the trade flowing through the port. The volume of trade passing through Khalisar is the greatest of any Sahrani city, and the harbour is the busiest on the northern coast, as the ships of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the islands arrive and depart at all hours.

The trade of Khalisar is dominated by the exchange of Sahrani and northern goods. The spices, dyes, precious stones, and rare fabrics of Sahrana are the primary exports and command the highest prices in the markets of the northern continents. The return cargo includes grain, iron, and the crafted metalwork of Aethelar and Morvind, and the exchange of southern and northern goods is the foundation of the city's wealth. The merchants of Khalisar are among the most skilled and influential in the known world, and their operations extend from the harbour to the farthest reaches of the trade network. The harbour handles vessels of every size, from small coastal traders to the great ocean-going carracks that make the voyage to Aethelar and back in a single season.

## The City

Khalisar is a large and beautiful city whose architecture is noted for its elegance and its adaptation to the warm climate. The buildings are constructed of light-coloured stone that reflects the sun, with shaded courtyards, high windows, and ventilated passages that channel the breezes from the sea. Water is channelled through the city by means of aqueducts and underground channels, and the presence of running water in fountains and gardens is both a practical necessity and a symbol of the city's prosperity. The aqueduct system draws water from springs in the foothills several miles inland and delivers it to public fountains distributed throughout the residential districts, ensuring that every neighbourhood has access to clean water.

The libraries of Khalisar are among the most extensive in the known world. The collections housed in the library quarter include the works of Sahrani scholars, the histories of the city-states, and the treatises on the sciences and arts that have been produced by the learned men and women of the continent. The libraries are maintained by the council and are open to the scholars and citizens of the city, and the reputation of the libraries is a significant factor in the cultural prestige of Khalisar. The library quarter occupies an entire district near the centre of the city, and the largest of its buildings, the Great Library of Khalisar, is said to hold more than fifteen thousand volumes covering subjects from astronomy to zoology.

The temples of the city are likewise noted for their beauty and refinement. The religious traditions of Sahrana are represented in the architecture and rituals of the temples situated throughout the city. The Temple of the Sun, the largest religious building in Khalisar, stands on a raised platform at the eastern end of the main square, its dome covered in gilded tiles that catch the first light of dawn.

The population of Khalisar is the most diverse of any Sahrani city, and the streets and markets are a meeting point of peoples from every continent. [Humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) are the majority, but [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves), and the peoples of the southern regions are present in significant numbers, drawn by the trade and the relative tolerance of the city. The [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga) of the warm rivers are occasional visitors to the harbour, and the trade between the coastal humans and the riverine naga is a valued part of the city's commerce. The markets of the city are the most vibrant in Sahrana, and the goods bought and sold in the bazaars and trading houses include the products of every continent and the craftsmanship of every people.

## History

The history of Khalisar is the history of the Sahrani coast, for the city has been the primary hub of continental trade for as long as the records of the region have been kept. The city was founded by the human settlers who established the first trading posts on the northern coast, and the natural harbour and fertile hinterland surrounding the site provided the foundation for the growth of the settlement. The city expanded rapidly as trade between Sahrana and the northern continents developed, and the wealth that accumulated funded the construction of the public buildings, fortifications, and harbour works that are the visible legacy of the city's prosperity.

The [Khalisar Council](/content/government/local/khalisar-council) was established in the early centuries of the city's history, and the council's governance has been a significant factor in the stability and prosperity of the city ever since. The city's leadership in the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) was established in the early years of the league, and Khalisar has been the leading member of the alliance throughout its history. The city has been besieged on several occasions, but its fortifications and naval force have been sufficient to deter or repel threats, and the city has never fallen to an external force.

## Governance and Significance

Khalisar is governed by the [Khalisar Council](/content/government/local/khalisar-council), a body of elected officials and appointed elders that oversees the administration, defence, and trade of the city. The council is the primary political institution of the city, and its decisions shape the economy and culture of the Sahrani coast. The city is the leading member of the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league), the alliance of Sahrani coastal city-states, and its influence extends throughout the league and beyond.

Khalisar is the commercial and cultural heart of Sahrana, and its prosperity is the engine of the economy of the northern coast and the continent as a whole. The trade flowing through its harbour connects Sahrana to the northern continents and the islands, and the wealth accumulating in the city funds the construction of public works, the maintenance of trade routes, and the support of the learning and arts that are the pride of the city. Khalisar is the greatest of the Sahrani city-states, and its significance is unmatched by any other settlement on the continent.

The cultural influence of Khalisar extends throughout Sahrana and beyond. The architecture, scholarship, and civic traditions of the city have been adopted and adapted by the other cities of the coast and the continent. The city is a centre of learning and the arts, and the scholars and artisans drawn to Khalisar by its libraries, temples, and workshops contribute to the cultural vitality that is one of the most valued features of the city. The significance of Khalisar lies in its wealth, its culture, and its governance, and the city stands as a symbol of Sahrani civilisation at its most accomplished and refined.
