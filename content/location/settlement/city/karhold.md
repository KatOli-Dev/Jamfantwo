## Overview

Karhold is the greatest [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) city of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and it is the capital of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/government/national/deep-realm-of-karhold). The city is carved into the living rock of the mountains, its halls and chambers extending for miles into the interior of the range, and its wealth is built upon the mineral deposits that are worked in the depths beneath the city. Karhold is one of the oldest dwarven settlements in the known world, and its halls are among the most extensive and the most magnificent of any dwarven city, rivalled only by the great halls of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) in [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind).

## The Halls

The halls of Karhold are carved into the granite of the mountain spine, and they are arranged in a pattern that reflects the geology of the range, following the veins of ore and the natural cave systems that the dwarves have enlarged and shaped over centuries. The great hall of Karhold is the largest single chamber in the city, a cavernous space that is supported by pillars of living rock and lit by the glow of the forges that line its walls. The hall is the heart of the city, the gathering place for the community, and the site of the forges, the council chambers, and the oldest dwellings.

The dwellings of Karhold are carved into the walls of the halls and the passages that branch from them, and each dwelling is a self-contained apartment with its own hearth, its own water supply, and its own ventilation shaft. The deeper dwellings are warmer, for the rock of the mountain retains a constant temperature throughout the year, and the deepest chambers are those of the oldest families, who have inhabited the city for the longest and who hold the most prestigious positions in the community.

The ventilation of Karhold is among the most impressive engineering achievements of the dwarven people, for the air that circulates through the halls and the passages is drawn from the surface through the shafts that are driven through the rock, and the flow is regulated by the vents that are cut into the walls of the chambers. The ventilation is essential for the health of the community and the operation of the forges, for the smoke and the heat that the forges produce must be carried away, and the fresh air that the shafts provide is the lifeblood of the city. The engineers who maintain the ventilation are among the most skilled and the most respected figures in the community, for the safety of the city depends upon their expertise.

## Mining and Metalwork

The mining operations of Karhold are the foundation of its wealth, and the ore that is extracted from the depths beneath the city includes iron, copper, tin, and the precious metals that are among the most valued products of the mountain spine. The mines extend for miles below the city, following the veins of ore deeper and further as the older workings are exhausted, and the miners of Karhold are among the most skilled and the most experienced in the known world. The ore that is extracted is smelted in the forges of the great hall, and the metalwork that is produced in Karhold is noted for its quality and its durability, and it is exported to the markets of the lowlands and the coast.

The metalwork of Karhold includes tools, weapons, armour, and the decorative objects for which the dwarven craftsmen are renowned, and the reputation of Karhold's smiths extends throughout Aethelar and beyond. The city is also a centre for the working of stone, and the stonemasons of Karhold are responsible for the construction and the maintenance of the city's halls, the fortifications, and the roads and bridges that connect the mountain spine to the lowlands.

## History and the Foundation

The founding of Karhold is lost in the depths of dwarven history, for the city was carved from the mountain in an age before the human chronicles began, and the dwarven records of the founding are preserved in the inscriptions that are cut into the walls of the deepest halls. The inscriptions record the names of the first kings and the first master smiths, and the dates of the carving of the great hall and the opening of the first mines. The city has been inhabited continuously since its founding, and the dwarven families that hold the deepest dwellings are the descendants of the original settlers who carved the first chambers from the rock.

The history of Karhold is a history of expansion and endurance, for the city has grown steadily over the centuries as the dwarves have carved new halls and opened new mines, and the community has weathered the disturbances that have occasionally affected the mountain spine. The construction of the great road that connects Karhold to the lowland markets was the most significant public work in the history of the city, for the road provided a reliable route for the trade that is the foundation of the economy of the realm. The road was built in the year 378, and the construction was a joint effort of the dwarven engineers of Karhold and the human builders of the lowland settlements.

## The City and the Realm

Karhold is the capital of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/government/national/deep-realm-of-karhold), the dwarven kingdom that encompasses the mountain spine of Aethelar, and the city is the seat of the king and the council that govern the realm. The population of Karhold is almost entirely [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), with a small community of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) who are involved in the trade between the city and the lowlands, and a few [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) who are drawn by the metalwork and the craftsmanship for which the city is known.

## Trade and the Lowlands

The trade of Karhold is the most valuable of any dwarven city in Aethelar, for the metalwork that is produced in its forges is prized in every market of the continent, and the demand for the weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects of the city is constant. The trade is conducted through the great road that connects the mountain spine to the lowland markets, and the caravans that carry the metalwork of Karhold to the cities of the lowlands and the coast are a familiar sight on the mountain routes. The trade is managed by the merchant guilds of the city, and the guilds are among the most influential institutions in the community, for the wealth that flows through the trade is the lifeblood of the economy of the realm.

The humans who reside in Karhold are the agents of the trading houses of the lowlands, and their function is to facilitate the exchange between the dwarven smiths and the human markets. The humans are a small but valued community, and their presence in the city is a sign of the importance of the trade that connects the mountain spine to the lowlands. The gnomes of Karhold are drawn by the craftsmanship of the smiths, and the gnomish artisans are among the most skilled workers of the finer metals and the decorative objects that are a valued part of the output of the city.

## Governance and the King

Karhold is governed by the king and the council, and the council is composed of the representatives of the great families, the master smiths, and the merchant guilds. The king is the hereditary ruler of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/government/national/deep-realm-of-karhold), and his authority is unquestioned in matters of defence and foreign policy. The council is responsible for the governance of the city and the management of the mines, the forges, and the trade that is the foundation of the economy of the realm. The council meets in the council chamber that is situated in the great hall, and the chamber is the most ornate of the public spaces in the city, its walls carved with the inscriptions that record the history and the laws of the dwarven people.

The governance of Karhold is noted for its stability and its continuity, for the king and the council have governed the city for centuries without significant disruption, and the traditions and the laws that guide the governance are among the oldest and the most respected in the known world. The king is advised by the council on all matters of consequence, and the relationship between the king and the council is one of mutual respect and co-operation, for the king relies upon the expertise of the councillors and the councillors respect the authority of the king.

## Culture and the Craft

The culture of Karhold is shaped by the craft and the mountain, for the community is devoted to the working of metal and stone, and the rhythms of the city are the rhythms of the forges and the mines. The master smiths of Karhold are the most respected figures in the community, and their workshops are the training grounds for the apprentices who will carry the craft forward into the next generation. The craft is a matter of pride and of identity, for the metalwork of Karhold is the most valued product of the dwarven people, and the skill of the smiths is the foundation of the wealth and the influence of the city.

The festivals of Karhold are oriented toward the craft and the mountain, and the most important is the annual feast of the forge, which celebrates the founding of the great forges and the skill of the smiths who work them. The feast is held in the great hall, and the master smiths are the guests of honour, for the prosperity of the city depends upon their expertise. The festival includes the contests of craft that are a tradition of the dwarven people, and the forging and the engraving are the most fiercely contested events, for the master smiths of Karhold are the most skilled in the known world, and the competition among them is a matter of pride and of reputation.

## Significance

Karhold is the greatest dwarven city of Aethelar and the capital of the Deep Realm, and its mineral wealth and its craftsmanship are the foundation of the economy of the mountain spine. The city is the most important dwarven settlement on the continent, and its influence extends throughout the range and into the lowlands, where the metalwork and the stone of Karhold are valued commodities. The city is a symbol of the endurance and the skill of the dwarven people, and its halls are among the most impressive achievements of subterranean architecture in the known world.
