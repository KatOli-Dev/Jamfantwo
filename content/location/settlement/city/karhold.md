## Greatest Dwarven City of Aethelar

Karhold is the greatest [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) city of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the capital of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm). The city is carved into the living rock of the mountains, its halls and chambers extending for miles into the interior of the range, and its wealth is built upon the mineral deposits worked in the depths beneath the city. Karhold is one of the oldest dwarven settlements in the known world, and its halls are among the most extensive and magnificent of any dwarven city, rivalled only by the great halls of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) in [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind).

## Granite Chambers, the Great Hall, and the Ventilation Shafts

The halls of Karhold are carved into the granite of the mountain spine and arranged in a pattern that reflects the rock of the range, following the veins of ore and the natural cave systems that the dwarves have enlarged and shaped over centuries. The great hall of Karhold is the largest single chamber in the city, a cavernous space supported by pillars of living rock and lit by the glow of the forges lining its walls. The hall is the heart of the city, the gathering place for the community, and the site of the forges, the council chambers, and the oldest dwellings.

The dwellings of Karhold are carved into the walls of the halls and the passages that branch from them. Each dwelling is a self-contained apartment with its own hearth, water supply, and ventilation shaft. The deeper dwellings are warmer, for the rock of the mountain retains a constant temperature throughout the year, and the deepest chambers are those of the oldest families, who have inhabited the city the longest and who hold the most prestigious positions in the community.

The ventilation of Karhold is among the most impressive engineering achievements of the dwarven people. The air circulating through the halls and passages is drawn from the surface through shafts driven through the rock, and the flow is regulated by vents cut into the walls of the chambers. Ventilation is essential for the health of the community and the operation of the forges, for the smoke and heat the forges produce must be carried away, and the fresh air the shafts provide is the lifeblood of the city. The engineers who maintain the ventilation are among the most skilled and respected figures in the community, for the safety of the city depends upon their expertise. The main ventilation shaft rises more than three hundred paces from the deepest hall to the mountainside above, and its construction required decades of continuous labour.

## Ore, Forges, and the Stonemasons' Craft

The mining operations of Karhold are the foundation of its wealth. The ore extracted from the depths beneath the city includes iron, copper, tin, and the precious metals that are among the most valued products of the mountain spine. The mines extend for miles below the city, following the veins of ore deeper and further as older workings are exhausted, and the miners of Karhold are among the most skilled and experienced in the known world. The ore extracted is smelted in the forges of the great hall, and the metalwork produced in Karhold is noted for its quality and durability, exported to the markets of the lowlands and the coast.

The metalwork of Karhold includes tools, weapons, armour, and the decorative objects for which dwarven craftsmen are renowned, and the reputation of Karhold's smiths extends throughout [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond. The city is also a centre for the working of stone, and the stonemasons of Karhold are responsible for the construction and maintenance of the city's halls, the fortifications, and the roads and bridges connecting the mountain spine to the lowlands.

## Lost in the Depths of Dwarven History

The founding of Karhold is lost in the depths of dwarven history. The city was carved from the mountain in an age before the human chronicles began, and the dwarven records of the founding are preserved in the inscriptions cut into the walls of the deepest halls. The inscriptions record the names of the first kings and the first master smiths and the dates of the carving of the great hall and the opening of the first mines. The city has been inhabited continuously since its founding, and the dwarven families holding the deepest dwellings are the descendants of the original settlers who carved the first chambers from the rock.

The history of Karhold is a history of expansion and endurance. The city has grown steadily over the centuries as the dwarves have carved new halls and opened new mines, and the community has weathered the disturbances that have occasionally affected the mountain spine. The construction of the great road connecting Karhold to the lowland markets was the most significant public work in the history of the city, providing a reliable route for the trade that is the foundation of the economy of the realm. The road was built in the year 378, a joint effort of the dwarven engineers of Karhold and the human builders of the lowland settlements.

## The King and the Realm

Karhold is the capital of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), the dwarven kingdom encompassing the mountain spine of Aethelar, and the city is the seat of the king and the council that govern the realm. The population is almost entirely [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), with a small community of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) involved in the trade between the city and the lowlands and a few [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) drawn by the metalwork and craftsmanship for which the city is known.

## The Great Road and the Merchant Guilds

The trade of Karhold is the most valuable of any dwarven city in Aethelar. The metalwork produced in its forges is prized in every market of the continent, and the demand for the weapons, tools, and decorative objects of the city is constant. The trade is conducted through the great road connecting the mountain spine to the lowland markets, and the caravans carrying the metalwork of Karhold to the cities of the lowlands and the coast are a familiar sight on the mountain routes. The trade is managed by the merchant guilds of the city, and the guilds are among the most influential institutions in the community, for the wealth flowing through the trade is the lifeblood of the economy of the realm.

The humans residing in Karhold are the agents of the trading houses of the lowlands, and their function is to aid exchange between the dwarven smiths and the human markets. The humans are a small but valued community, and their presence in the city is a sign of the importance of the trade connecting the mountain spine to the lowlands. The gnomes of Karhold are drawn by the craftsmanship of the smiths, and the gnomish artisans are among the most skilled workers of the finer metals and decorative objects that are a valued part of the output of the city.

## King, Council, and the Stability of Continuity

Karhold is governed by the king and the council. The council is composed of representatives of the great families, the master smiths, and the merchant guilds. The king is the hereditary ruler of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), and his authority is unquestioned in matters of defence and foreign policy. The council is responsible for the governance of the city and the management of the mines, the forges, and the trade that is the foundation of the economy of the realm. The council meets in the council chamber in the great hall, the most ornate of the public spaces in the city, its walls carved with the inscriptions that record the history and laws of the dwarven people.

The governance of Karhold is noted for its stability and continuity. The king and council have governed the city for centuries without significant disruption, and the traditions and laws guiding governance are among the oldest and most respected in the known world. The king is advised by the council on all matters of consequence, and the relationship between the king and the council is one of mutual respect and co-operation.

## Feast of the Forge and the Master Smiths

The culture of Karhold is shaped by the craft and the mountain. The community is devoted to the working of metal and stone, and the rhythms of the city follow the rhythms of the forges and mines. The master smiths of Karhold are the most respected figures in the community, and their workshops are the training grounds for the apprentices who will carry the craft forward into the next generation. The craft is a matter of pride and identity, for the metalwork of Karhold is the most valued product of the dwarven people, and the skill of the smiths is the foundation of the wealth and influence of the city.

The festivals of Karhold are oriented toward the craft and the mountain. The most important is the annual feast of the forge, which celebrates the founding of the great forges and the skill of the smiths who work them. The feast is held in the great hall, and the master smiths are the guests of honour, for the prosperity of the city depends upon their expertise. The festival includes contests of craft that are a tradition of the dwarven people, and the forging and engraving competitions are the most fiercely contested events, for the master smiths of Karhold are the most skilled in the known world, and the competition among them is a matter of pride and reputation.

## Greatest Dwarven City and Capital of the Deep Realm

Karhold is the greatest dwarven city of Aethelar and the capital of the Deep Realm, and its mineral wealth and craftsmanship are the foundation of the economy of the mountain spine. The city is the most important dwarven settlement on the continent, and its influence extends throughout the range and into the lowlands, where the metalwork and stone of Karhold are valued commodities. The city is a symbol of the endurance and skill of the dwarven people, and its halls are among the most impressive achievements of subterranean architecture in the known world.
