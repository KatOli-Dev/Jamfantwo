## A City on the Greywash River

Silver Ford is a city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) situated on the middle course of the [Greywash River](/content/location/natural/feature/greywash-river), at the point where the river narrows and the current runs swift over a bed of pale stone. The city takes its name from the ford that crosses the river at this point, where the water is shallow enough for wading and the stone of the riverbed catches the light and gleams like silver in the sun. Silver Ford is the principal settlement of the middle Greywash valley and serves as a centre of trade and administration for the surrounding country.

The position of Silver Ford at the crossing of the Greywash has been the foundation of its prosperity since the earliest period of settlement. The ford is the most reliable crossing of the Greywash for many miles in either direction, and the roads that converge on the city from the east and west carry the traffic of the interior to the ford and across the river. The roads have been improved over the centuries, and the ford itself has been paved with stone to provide a firm surface for the passage of wagons and livestock.

## The Greywash River and the Pale Stone Bed

The [Greywash River](/content/location/natural/feature/greywash-river) at Silver Ford flows over a bed of pale grey stone that gives the ford its distinctive appearance. The stone is a fine-grained granite worn smooth by centuries of water, and it extends across the full width of the river at the ford, forming a natural pavement that provides a firm and even surface for crossing. The stone floor of the ford is exposed at low water, and the surface gleams with the metallic lustre that has given the crossing its name.

The river at Silver Ford is wider than at many points along its course, but the current is swifter, for the river narrows again immediately below the ford, and the water is funnelled through a rocky channel that increases the flow. The crossing at the ford is safe for most of the year, but during the spring floods the water rises above the level of the stone bed and the ford becomes impassable for several weeks. The city maintains a ferry service that operates during the flood season, carrying travellers and goods across the river by boat.

## From Crossing Place to Chartered City

The earliest settlement at Silver Ford was a small hamlet established at the crossing to serve the needs of travellers using the ford. The hamlet consisted of a few houses, a smithy, and a tavern, providing shelter and provisions for the merchants, pilgrims, and other travellers who passed through the crossing on their journeys. The hamlet grew slowly over the centuries, as the volume of traffic using the ford increased and the settlement expanded to meet the needs of the travellers and traders.

The grant of a market charter in a later period was the turning point in the growth of Silver Ford. The charter permitted the settlement to hold a weekly market, and the market quickly became the principal commercial event of the middle Greywash valley. Farmers, traders, and craftsmen from the surrounding country gathered at the market to buy and sell the products of the region, and the prosperity generated by the market transformed the hamlet into a town.

The elevation of Silver Ford to the status of a city followed the growth of the settlement and the development of its trade and administration. The grant of city status was made by the authority that governed the Greywash valley at the time, and the new city was given the rights and privileges appropriate to its rank. The grant recognised the importance of Silver Ford as the principal settlement of the middle Greywash and confirmed its position as the commercial and administrative centre of the region.

## The Paved Ford, the Stone Bridge, and the River Trade

The ford that gives the city its name remains the principal crossing of the river at Silver Ford, despite the construction of a stone bridge in a later century. The bridge was built to provide a crossing during the flood season, when the ford is impassable, and to reduce the congestion that occurred at the ford during the busiest periods of the year. The bridge is a sturdy structure of grey stone, with arches that span the full width of the river, and it remains the principal crossing for wheeled traffic and for travellers who prefer the convenience of a dry crossing.

The ford is still used by travellers on foot and by those who prefer to cross by the old way. The ford is maintained by the city, and the stone bed is cleared of debris and repaired as needed to keep the crossing safe and passable. The ford is also used for the watering of livestock and for the washing of cloth and hides.

The river trade of Silver Ford is a secondary but significant component of the city's economy. The Greywash is navigable by small boats and barges for much of its length, and the goods that pass through Silver Ford by river include the agricultural products of the valley and the manufactured goods of the cities to the north and south.

## The Weekly Market and the Autumn Fair

The weekly market of Silver Ford is held in the market square, a broad open space at the centre of the city. The square is surrounded by the principal buildings of the settlement, including the guild hall, the market hall, and the houses of the principal merchants. The stalls and booths erected on market day offer a wide range of goods, including the food and drink of the valley, the cloth and leather of the city's craftsmen, and the tools and implements of the smiths and carpenters.

The autumn fair of Silver Ford is the principal event of the commercial calendar, drawing merchants and buyers from across the Greywash valley to the city. The fair lasts for several days and marks the end of the harvest season, and the farmers of the valley bring their surplus grain and livestock to the fair for sale. The fair is also the occasion for the settlement of debts and the making of contracts for the coming year, and the legal officials of Silver Ford attend the fair to witness and record the transactions.

## The Guilds and the Council of the Ford

The trade of Silver Ford is regulated by the guilds of the city, each guild representing the craftsmen and merchants of a particular trade. The guilds set the standards for the quality of goods and the conduct of trade, and they adjudicate disputes between their members and between members and outsiders. The guilds of Silver Ford are among the oldest and most respected in the Greywash valley, and their regulations are followed throughout the region.

The council of the ford is the governing body of Silver Ford, composed of representatives of the guilds and the principal landowners of the surrounding country. The council is responsible for the administration of the city, the maintenance of the ford and the bridge, and the regulation of the market and the fair. The council meets in the guild hall, a substantial building of stone and timber that stands at the head of the market square.

## Silver Ford as the Gateway to the Middle Greywash

Silver Ford is the gateway to the middle Greywash and the principal settlement of the river valley. The city's position at the crossing of the river and its role as the commercial and administrative centre of the surrounding country have made it a settlement of considerable importance in the interior of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The ford that gives the city its name is the most recognisable landmark of the region, and the stone bridge that spans the river at the city is one of the principal works of engineering in the Greywash valley. The merchants and craftsmen of Silver Ford are known for their skill and reliability, and the goods produced in the city are traded across Aethelar.
