## The Largest City of the Southern Aethelaran Plains

Sunmere is the largest city of the southern plains of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated on the banks of a broad, slow river that winds through the warm grasslands of the south. The city is the primary settlement of the southern plains and the principal market for the agricultural and pastoral products of the region, and its prosperity is built upon the fertility of the warm soil and the industry of the farmers and herders who work it. Sunmere's climate and way of life are distinctly different from those of the cities of the temperate north.

## Warm Grasslands and Irrigation Channels

The southern plains of Aethelar are a region of warm, open grassland stretching from the foothills of the mountain spine to the southern coast. The land is among the most productive in the continent for warm-weather crops that are staples of the southern diet. The soil is dark and rich, and the river that flows past Sunmere provides the water that sustains the agriculture of the surrounding country. The farms along the river produce grain, fruits, and vegetables that are the primary exports of the city, and the pasture beyond the cultivated land supports herds of cattle and sheep.

The pastoral peoples of the southern plains include the [centaur](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) kindreds that range across the open grass. The relationship between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is one of cautious co-operation, with each people pursuing its own way of life and meeting at the markets of Sunmere to exchange the products of their different economies.

The climate of the southern plains is warm and dry for much of the year, and the growing season is longer than that of the temperate north, allowing for two harvests of the principal grain crops in a single year. The river is the lifeline of the agriculture, for rainfall alone is insufficient to sustain the warm-weather crops, and the irrigation channels dug from the river to the fields are among the oldest and most important works of engineering in the region. The management of the water is a responsibility of the city, and the overseers of irrigation are among the most important officials of the community.

## Grain, Livestock, and the River Barges

The trade of Sunmere is dominated by the agricultural products of the surrounding plains. Grain, fruit, and livestock produced in the region are gathered at the city for sale and transport to the markets of the north. The river is the primary route of trade, and the barges carrying goods downriver to the southern coast and upriver to the interior are a common sight on the waterway. The city is also a centre for processing agricultural products, and the mills, presses, and workshops lining the riverbank are a significant part of the city's economy.

The market square of Sunmere is one of the largest in the southern plains, and the weekly market draws farmers, herders, and traders from the surrounding country. The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) of the plains are regular visitors to the market, and the trade between the settled and nomadic peoples is the lifeblood of the city's commerce. The barges that carry the city's goods are broad, flat-bottomed vessels suited to the shallow waters of the river, poled and towed by their crews. The river is the most economical route for bulk goods, and the volume of traffic on the waterway is a measure of the city's prosperity.

## From Farming Community to Chartered City

The founding of Sunmere dates to the early centuries of human settlement of the southern plains, when the site was chosen for its position on the river and the fertility of the surrounding soil. The early settlement was a farming community, and the first permanent buildings were constructed on the banks of the river. The city grew rapidly, for the productivity of the land and the mildness of the climate attracted settlers from the temperate north, and the population swelled within a few generations. The city was granted its charter by the council of the southern plains in the year 298, establishing the rights of the city to manage the irrigation and collect tolls on the river trade.

The history of Sunmere is a history of agricultural prosperity. The city has never been threatened by war or disturbance, and the community has devoted its energies to the improvement of the land and the expansion of trade. The construction of the great irrigation channel running from the river to the eastern fields was the most significant public work in the city's history, completed in the year 412 after a decade of labour by the engineers and workmen of the community.

## Broad Streets and Wind-Catchers

Sunmere is a large, open city whose architecture reflects the warm climate of the southern plains, with broad streets, shaded courtyards, and buildings of light-coloured stone that reflect the sun. The buildings have wide windows and high ceilings that allow air to circulate, and the roofs are tiled and fitted with the wind-catchers that are a distinctive feature of the southern architectural tradition. The population of Sunmere is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a growing community of [halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings) drawn by the fertile land and mild climate.

## Thick Walls and Shaded Courtyards

The architecture of Sunmere is the most distinctive of any city on the southern plains, designed to provide relief from the summer heat. The walls are thick, the windows are screened with lattice shutters that allow air to circulate while keeping out the sun, and the courtyards are planted with shade trees and fountains that cool the air. The wind-catchers fitted to the roofs channel the breeze into the interiors and are the most distinctive feature of the skyline of Sunmere, their tapered silhouettes visible from many miles across the plain.

The public buildings of Sunmere are situated around the market square. The council hall, the granary, and the exchange are the most substantial buildings in the city. The granary is the largest building, for the storage of grain is a critical function, and it is built of stone with walls thick enough to keep the interior cool and dry. The exchange is the centre of the financial services that support the trade of the southern plains, and the merchants and money-lenders of Sunmere conduct their business in the cool of its shaded arcade.

## Hospitality and the Nomadic Kindreds

The culture of Sunmere is shaped by the warmth of the climate and the diversity of the peoples who inhabit the southern plains, and the community is noted for its hospitality and openness to strangers. The cuisine of Sunmere is the most varied in the south, for the warm-weather crops provide a greater range of ingredients than the staples of the temperate north, and the cooks of the city are skilled in the preparation of spiced dishes and fruit preserves that are the specialities of the southern tradition.

The relationship between the settled humans and the [centaur](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) kindreds of the plains is a central feature of the cultural life of Sunmere. The market is the meeting point where the two peoples interact. The centaurs are nomadic herders who range across the open grass in patterns governed by the seasons and the availability of grazing, and their visits to the market are occasions of commerce and social exchange. The humans of Sunmere have learned to respect the ways of the centaurs, and the trade between the two peoples is conducted with a courtesy and formality reflecting the long history of the relationship. The centaurs provide wool, leather, and livestock, and the humans provide grain, manufactured goods, and tools required for the nomadic life.

## Agricultural Wealth of the Southern Plains

Sunmere is the largest and most prosperous city of the southern plains, and its agricultural products are a significant component of the economy of Aethelar. The city is the primary settlement of the southern region and a key member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), the alliance of grassland cities and pastoral peoples. The prosperity of Sunmere is a direct consequence of the fertility of the warm soil and the industry of its people, and the city is a symbol of the agricultural wealth of the southern plains.
