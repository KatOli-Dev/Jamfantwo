## Overview

Thornhaven is an eastern trade city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated at the mouth of a great river that descends from the mountain spine and crosses the eastern lowlands before reaching the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The city is a major node in the trade network of the eastern coast, for its river connects the coast to the interior highlands and the mountain passes, and the goods that travel its waters pass through Thornhaven on their way to and from the sea. The city is smaller than [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) or [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), but its position at the junction of river and mountain gives it a strategic importance that belies its size.

## The River and the Passes

The river that serves Thornhaven is one of the principal waterways of eastern Aethelar, navigable for a considerable distance inland, and its valley forms the most direct route from the coast to the mountain passes that connect the eastern and western halves of the continent. The trade that flows through the river valley is diverse, including the metalwork and stone of the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) settlements in the mountains, the wool and leather of the upland pastures, and the grain and timber of the lowland valleys. These goods are gathered at Thornhaven, where they are loaded onto seagoing vessels for transport to the markets of the coast and the wider world.

The mountain passes that lie at the head of the river valley are among the most important routes of communication in Aethelar, and the control of these passes has long been a source of contention between the powers that border them. Thornhaven serves as the eastern terminus of the overland trade that crosses the passes, and the city's merchants are skilled in the management of the caravans that make the journey, organising the transport, the provisioning, and the exchange of goods between the two coasts. The caravans that depart from Thornhaven are large and well-organised, for the crossing of the mountain spine is a journey of many weeks, and the provisions, the pack animals, and the guards that are required for the passage are assembled in the city before the departure.

The passes are open for only a few months of the year, for the snows of winter close the high routes, and the caravans must time their departure to reach the passes before the first snows or after the spring thaw. The merchants of Thornhaven maintain a network of contacts on the western side of the mountains, and the co-ordination of the trade between the two coasts is a skilled and complex business that requires trust, planning, and a thorough knowledge of the routes and the conditions that prevail along them.

## The Harbour and the City

The harbour of Thornhaven is a broad, open roadstead that is sheltered by a chain of small islands that lie offshore, and the approach to the harbour is marked by lighthouses that are among the oldest on the eastern coast. The waterfront is lined with the warehouses and offices of the trading houses that manage the commerce of the city, and the quays are busy with the loading and unloading of the vessels that call at the port. The provisioning of the caravans that depart from Thornhaven is a significant business, for the crossing of the mountain spine requires supplies of food, fodder, and equipment that are assembled in the warehouses and the supply yards of the waterfront. The pack animals that carry the caravans are stabled in the yards that lie behind the waterfront, and the muleteers and the drivers who manage them are a significant part of the workforce of the city.

The city itself is built on a series of low hills that rise above the river mouth, and the older quarters are fortified by a wall that dates from an earlier age of conflict. The streets are steep and winding in the older parts, and broader and more regular in the newer districts that have grown up along the river and the harbour. The population of Thornhaven is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) who are involved in the trade of metalwork and the management of the mountain routes. The dwarven community of Thornhaven is the link between the city and the dwarven settlements of the mountains, and the dwarven merchants who reside in the city are among the most influential figures in the trade that crosses the passes.

## Significance

Thornhaven is the primary gateway between the eastern coast and the interior of Aethelar, and its trade connects the coast to the mountain passes and the western shore beyond. The goods that flow through the city are the foundation of its prosperity, and the merchants of Thornhaven are among the most influential in the trade network of the continent. The city is a member of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league), and its position in the league is strengthened by its control of the river and the passes, which are the most valuable assets of the city.

## History

Thornhaven was founded as a river crossing and a fishing village, and the earliest settlers were drawn by the natural advantages of the harbour and the proximity to the mouth of the river. The village grew into a town as the trade between the coast and the interior developed, and the discovery of the mountain passes that connect the eastern and western halves of the continent transformed the town into a city. The old wall that fortifies the upper quarters was built in the age of conflict that followed the opening of the passes, when the powers that bordered the mountains contested the control of the routes, and the wall is a reminder of the strategic significance that Thornhaven has held for centuries. The lighthouses that mark the approach to the harbour are among the oldest on the eastern coast, and their construction is attributed to the early merchants of the city, who recognised the need to guide the vessels that brought the goods of the interior to the port. The membership of the Thessaran League has been a source of prosperity and protection, and the league's fleet has defended the harbour of Thornhaven from the raiders who have threatened the eastern coast in ages past.
