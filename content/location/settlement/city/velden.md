## Overview

Velden is a city of the cold north, founded upon the northern bank of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) at the point where the strait opens into the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean). The city commands the principal crossing of the strait, and the city has been the principal port for the trade between the cold north and the warm coast since the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The city takes its name from the strait that it commands, and the name has been preserved in the city's chronicles since its founding.

Velden is the seat of the magistrates who govern the region of the northern strait, and the city is the home of the pilots and the merchants who conduct the trade of the strait. The city has grown steadily since its founding, and the city is today among the larger of the towns of the cold north. The chronicler notes that Velden is held in particular esteem by the pilots and the merchants of the cold north, and the magistrates mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies.

## Founding

The chronicles of the city attribute the founding of Velden to the merchants who settled upon the natural harbour at the mouth of the strait in the generation before the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The settlement was small at first, consisting of a few warehouses, a small temple, and a modest mole. The settlement grew rapidly, for the harbour was the best at the northern end of the strait, and the merchants who traded with the warm coast found it convenient to keep their goods in the warehouses of the settlement rather than to carry them further.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed in the chronicles to the accord that the merchants made with the fishermen of the coast and with the pilots of the strait. The accord established the standards of the navigation, the rules of the trade, and the procedures by which the disputes were adjudicated. The accord has been revised from time to time but never replaced, and the accord is preserved in the archives of the magistrates of Velden.

## The Harbour

The harbour of Velden is one of the more reliable natural harbours of the cold north. The harbour is sheltered from the prevailing winds by the cliffs that rise to the north, and the harbour is deep enough for the larger vessels that carry the cargoes of the trade. The harbour is divided into an inner basin for the fishing boats and an outer basin for the merchant vessels, and the basins are separated by a stone mole that the magistrates erected in the early generations of the city's growth.

The chronicler notes that the harbour has been the foundation of the prosperity of Velden since the founding of the city, and the harbour has been carefully maintained by the magistrates ever since. The mole has been repaired several times after the storms that the chroniclers have recorded in the city's chronicles, and the basins have been dredged at intervals to keep the channels navigable. The chronicler observes that the maintenance has been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the strait, and the safety is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the city.

## The Lighthouses

The lighthouses of Velden are among the more important of the lighthouses of the cold north. The principal lighthouse stands upon the cliff that commands the northern entrance to the strait, and the lighthouse is visible far out to sea in clear weather. The lighthouse was erected by the magistrates of the city in the early generations of the city's growth, and the lighthouse has been maintained by the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) ever since. The chronicler notes that the lighthouse has been the foundation of the safety of the navigation of the strait for several centuries, and the lighthouse has been the refuge of many a pilot who has been caught upon the coast in time of storm.

The lighthouses are connected by a chain of signals that the keepers exchange with the [Greyring Light](/content/location/settlement/outpost/greyring-light) and the other towers upon the coast, and the signals inform the pilots of the state of the weather and the approach of any large vessel. The chronicler notes that the signals have been the foundation of the careful management of the navigation of the strait, and the management is the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates and the pilots of Velden.

## The Magistrates

The city is governed by the magistrates of Velden, who are appointed by the council of the city from among the principal merchants and the pilots of the strait. The magistrates hold office for a term of three years, and the magistrates govern the city in concert with the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) and the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings). The chronicler notes that the magistrates are held in particular esteem by the citizens of the city, and the magistrates mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies.

The magistrates maintain the harbour, the lighthouses, the quays, the warehouses, the roads, and the public order of the city. The magistrates also oversee the collection of the small dues that pay for the maintenance of the harbour and the lighthouses, and the magistrates audit the accounts of the dues twice in each year. The chronicler notes that the audit has been the foundation of the careful management of the city's finances, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants and the pilots of the city.

## The Trade

The trade of Velden is the principal business of the city, and the trade has been the foundation of the prosperity of the city since the founding. The merchants of the city trade in the fish and the wool of the cold north, the grain and the cloth of the inland valleys, and the salt and the spices of the warm coast. The chronicler notes that the trade has been the foundation of the contact between the city and the towns of the warm coast, and the contact is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of both parties.

The trade is regulated by the magistrates in consultation with the pilots of the strait and the merchants of the principal towns of the warm coast. The chronicler notes that the regulation has been the foundation of the careful management of the trade, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the merchants of the city. The trade is conducted through the harbour and the quays, and the merchants of the city travel the roads of the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) to conduct the trade.

## Significance

Velden is among the larger of the cities of the cold north, and the city is the foundation of the trade in fish and wool of the region of the northern strait. The harbour, the lighthouses, the quays, the warehouses, the magistrates, and the trade are the inheritance that the city has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that Velden is held in particular memory by the pilots and the merchants of the cold north, and the pilots and the merchants mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies. The ceremonies are among the most important of the public observances of the cold north, and the ceremonies are attended by the magistrates and the principal citizens of every city of the region.
