## A City Commanding the Northern Strait

Velden is a city of the cold north, founded upon the northern bank of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) at the point where the strait opens into the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean). The city commands the principal crossing of the strait and has been the primary port for the trade between the cold north and the warm coast since the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). Velden takes its name from the strait it commands. The city is the seat of the magistrates who govern the region of the northern strait and is home to the pilots and merchants who conduct the trade of the strait. It ranks among the larger towns of the cold north.

## The Merchants' Settlement on the Strait

The founding of Velden is attributed to the merchants who settled upon the natural harbour at the mouth of the strait in the generation before the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era). The settlement began with a few warehouses, a small temple, and a modest mole, but it grew rapidly because the harbour was the finest at the northern end of the strait, and the merchants who traded with the warm coast preferred to keep their goods in the settlement's warehouses rather than carry them further.

The growth of the settlement into a city is attributed to an accord the merchants made with the fishermen of the coast and the pilots of the strait. The accord established the standards of navigation, the rules of trade, and the procedures for adjudicating disputes. It has been revised from time to time but never replaced, and it is preserved in the archives of the magistrates of Velden.

## The Harbour and the Sheltered Basins

The harbour of Velden is one of the more reliable natural harbours of the cold north. It is sheltered from the prevailing winds by the cliffs that rise to the north and deep enough for the larger vessels that carry the cargoes of the trade. The harbour is divided into an inner basin for fishing boats and an outer basin for merchant vessels, separated by a stone mole erected by the magistrates in the early generations of the city's growth.

The harbour has been fundamental to Velden's prosperity since its founding. The mole has been repaired several times after storm damage, and the basins have been dredged at intervals to keep the channels navigable. The safety of the harbour has been a key factor in the growth of the merchant community and the volume of trade that passes through the city.

## The Lighthouses and the Signal Chain

The lighthouses of Velden are among the more important navigational aids of the cold north. The principal lighthouse stands upon the cliff that commands the northern entrance to the strait and is visible far out to sea in clear weather. It was erected by the magistrates in the early generations of the city's growth and has been maintained by the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) ever since. The lighthouse has guided countless vessels through the dangerous waters of the strait and has served as a refuge for pilots caught upon the coast in time of storm.

The lighthouses are connected by a chain of signals exchanged with the [Greyring Light](/content/location/settlement/outpost/greyring-light) and other towers along the coast. These signals inform pilots of the state of the weather and the approach of vessels, allowing the navigation of the strait to be managed with care and precision. The signal system is maintained by a dedicated corps of keepers who are among the most respected members of the community.

## Governance by the Magistrates and Pilots

Velden is governed by magistrates appointed by the city council from among the principal merchants and pilots of the strait. They hold office for a term of three years and govern in concert with the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) and the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings). The magistrates are responsible for maintaining the harbour, lighthouses, quays, warehouses, roads, and public order. They oversee the collection of dues that fund the maintenance of the harbour and lighthouses and audit the accounts twice each year.

## Fish, Wool, and the Strait Traffic

The trade of Velden is the principal business of the city. Merchants trade in the fish and wool of the cold north, the grain and cloth of the inland valleys, and the salt and spices of the warm coast. This trade connects the city to the towns of the warm coast, benefiting merchants on both sides. The trade is regulated by the magistrates in consultation with the pilots of the strait and the merchants of the principal towns of the warm coast. Commerce is conducted through the harbour and quays, and merchants travel the roads of the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) to conduct their business.

The strait is the key to Velden's commercial significance. Vessels passing between the Greyring Ocean and the sheltered waters of the inner coast must navigate the narrow passage, and Velden's position at the northern entrance allows the city to control and service this traffic. The pilots of Velden board vessels at the mouth of the strait to guide them through the channels, and the fees for pilotage are a significant source of revenue for the city. The warehouses of Velden hold goods waiting to be transhipped between the ocean-going vessels and the smaller craft that serve the coastal communities. The trade passing through the strait connects the cold north to the markets of the warm coast, and the merchants of Velden have established networks of agents in the principal ports of both regions to manage the flow of goods.

## The Foundation of Strait Navigation

Velden is among the larger cities of the cold north and is the foundation of the trade in fish and wool in the region of the northern strait. The harbour, lighthouses, quays, warehouses, and governance structures are the inheritance the city has bequeathed to the present age. The anniversary ceremonies of the founding are among the most important public observances of the cold north, attended by the magistrates and principal citizens of every city in the region. The city's control of the strait crossing and its skilled pilots have made it an indispensable link in the trade routes connecting the cold north to the warm coast.
