## A Northern City of the Ancient River Valleys

Thornwick is a northern city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated in one of the broad river valleys that thread between the ancient forests of the north. The city is among the oldest [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) settlements in Aethelar, its foundations dating to the earliest period of human expansion into the temperate regions of the continent. The river that flows past its walls is one of the principal waterways of the north, and the valley that surrounds it is among the most fertile regions in Aethelar, with rich alluvial soils that have sustained agriculture for centuries. Thornwick is a quiet, prosperous city, proud of its age and heritage, and its long history is visible in every street and wall.

## The Broad Valley and Its Alluvial Soils

The river valley surrounding Thornwick is broad and well-watered, and the alluvial soils lining its banks are among the most productive in the continent. The agriculture of the valley is the foundation of the city's prosperity, and the fields stretching out from the city walls produce grain, vegetables, and the fodder that sustains the livestock of the region. The river itself is navigable for much of its length, and the trade flowing along its waters connects Thornwick to the other settlements of the valley and the markets of the coast.

The age of the settlement is reflected in the landscape. The land around Thornwick has been cultivated for so long that the pattern of fields, hedgerows, and lanes is indistinguishable from the natural contours of the valley. The oldest farms are those closest to the city, their boundaries fixed for centuries, while the newer clearings are further out where the forest once stood and has been gradually pushed back by the expansion of cultivated land.

The river is also a source of fish, and the eels caught in the weirs below the city are a valued delicacy exported to the markets of the south. The watermills lining the riverbank are among the oldest in Aethelar, and the flour ground in the mills of Thornwick is noted for its quality and consistency.

## The Old Walls, Narrow Streets, and the Garrison

Thornwick is a well-fortified city, and its walls are among the oldest and thickest in Aethelar, built of stone quarried from the hills above the valley and reinforced many times over the centuries. The streets within the walls are narrow and winding, for the city was built when space was limited and defence was the primary concern, and the buildings are close-set and ancient, their foundations dating to the earliest period of settlement. The newer districts, built outside the walls during long periods of peace, are broader and more regular, and the contrast between the old city and the new reflects the changing fortunes of Thornwick over its long history.

The city is the seat of the administration of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), and the public buildings, barracks, and courthouse are situated in the oldest quarter, where the streets are widest and the buildings most substantial. The market square of Thornwick is one of the largest in the north, and the weekly market is a gathering point for the farmers, traders, and craftsmen of the valley.

The walls are a notable feature of the city, among the oldest and thickest fortifications in Aethelar. They have been reinforced and extended many times, and the layers of construction are visible in the different colours and styles of stonework. The four gates, one for each cardinal direction, are surmounted by guard towers that are the most prominent features of the walls. The northern gate is the oldest, its arch decorated with carved figures that are the work of the earliest stonemasons of the city.

## From Fortified Camp to Administrative Centre

The founding of Thornwick dates to the earliest period of human expansion into the temperate regions of Aethelar, and the city is regarded as the oldest human settlement in the northern river valleys. The early settlement was a fortified camp established on the banks of the river, and it grew into a permanent settlement as the fertility of the valley became apparent and the population increased. The construction of the first walls was undertaken in the year 142, and they have been the defining feature of the city ever since.

The role of Thornwick as the administrative centre of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) was established in the year 287, when the council of the marches was formed and the courthouse was built in the oldest quarter. The council is responsible for governing the northern region, and the administration of the marches is conducted from the public buildings around the courthouse. The barracks of the garrison are the most substantial of the public buildings, and the garrison of Thornwick is the largest military force in the northern interior.

## Agricultural Produce and River Barges

The trade of Thornwick is dominated by the agricultural produce of the valley. Grain, livestock, and manufactured goods are exported to the markets of the south and the coast. The river is the primary route of trade, and the barges carrying goods downriver are a common sight on the waterway. The city is also a centre for crafts serving the agricultural economy, and the smiths, wheelwrights, and carpenters of Thornwick are noted for their sturdy products. The leather workers are also noted for their skill, and the harness and saddles produced in the workshops of Thornwick are valued by farmers and herders of the valley.

## Festivals, Guilds, and the Masonic Tradition

The culture of Thornwick is shaped by the age of the city and the pride the inhabitants take in their heritage. The community is noted for its respect for tradition and attachment to the old ways. The festivals of Thornwick are the oldest in the north, and the most important is the harvest festival, held in the market square at the end of the growing season. It includes the procession of the guilds, feasting, and contests of skill. The procession is the most distinctive feature of the festival, with each guild marching in the order of its founding, carrying banners and regalia that are among the oldest and most valued possessions of the community.

The stonemasons of Thornwick are the most respected craftsmen in the city, for the maintenance of the walls and public buildings is a continuous and demanding task, and the skills of the masons are passed down from generation to generation. The masons' guild is the oldest guild in the city, and its members are responsible for the construction and repair of the fortifications, public buildings, and bridges crossing the river. The guild hall of the masons is situated in the oldest quarter and is a notable building of stone decorated with carved figures, the hallmark of the Thornwick tradition.

Thornwick is significant as the oldest human settlement in the northern river valleys and the administrative centre of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches). Its long history and position in the most fertile valley of the north give it an importance that is cultural as well as economic, and the city is regarded throughout Aethelar as a symbol of the endurance and continuity of human settlement on the continent.
