## Overview

Caerwyn is an eastern port city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), renowned throughout the known world for its shipyards and the vessels they produce. The city stands on a rocky promontory that juts into the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and its harbour is sheltered by a natural breakwater of rock that forms a deep, calm basin within sight of the open sea. The combination of a fine harbour, a ready supply of timber from the inland forests, and a long tradition of shipbuilding has made Caerwyn the foremost centre of maritime construction on the eastern coast, and its ships are the backbone of the merchant fleets and the naval forces of Aethelar.

## The Shipyards

The shipyards of Caerwyn are the largest and most extensive on the eastern coast, and they line the inner shore of the harbour in a continuous stretch of slipways, dry docks, and workshops. The construction of vessels is the dominant activity of the city, and the sound of sawing, hammering, and the caulking of seams fills the air from dawn to dusk. The ships built in Caerwyn are noted for their strength, their speed, and their seaworthiness, and the techniques of their construction have been refined over centuries of experience with the conditions of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean).

The timber for the shipyards is brought downriver from the inland forests, where the great trees of the northern woodland are felled and floated to Caerwyn in rafts that are a common sight on the river. The selection and seasoning of timber is a skilled practice, and the shipwrights of Caerwyn are knowledgeable in the properties of the different woods, matching the timber to the function of each part of the vessel. The shipyards also produce the masts, spars, oars, and the smaller craft that are essential to the maritime trade of the coast, and the output of the yards is a significant export in its own right.

The construction of a large vessel is a complex undertaking that requires the labour of many hands and the co-ordination of many trades. The master shipwrights of Caerwyn oversee the work, and the carpenters, the caulkers, the riggers, and the sailmakers each contribute their skills to the completion of the vessel. The ironwork, including the anchors, the nails, and the fittings, is supplied by the smiths of the city, and the sails are woven from the cloth that is imported from the interior. The construction of a single large vessel can take the better part of a year, and the shipyards of Caerwyn employ hundreds of workers in the construction and the fitting out of the fleet that is built each year.

The population of Caerwyn is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) who are drawn by the demand for metalwork, anchors, and the iron fittings that the ships require. The [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) of Caerwyn are noted for their skill in the making of navigational instruments and the fine mechanisms that are used aboard the larger vessels, and their workshops are a valued part of the shipbuilding complex. The gnomish instrument-makers are renowned for the precision of their astrolabes, compasses, and the sounding leads that are essential to the navigation of the open ocean, and the instruments of Caerwyn are sought after by the navigators of every fleet.

## The Harbour and Trade

The harbour of Caerwyn is a deep, sheltered basin that accommodates both the ships under construction and the vessels that call at the port for trade. The trade of Caerwyn is secondary to its shipbuilding, but it is nonetheless substantial, for the city is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling along the eastern coast, and the goods that pass through its harbour include the produce of the interior, the materials for the shipyards, and the provisions for the vessels that are fitted out and supplied at the port.

The harbour guild assigns berths so that arriving traders do not obstruct launches from the slips, and its pilots move unfinished hulls between the yards and fitting quays. Timber, pitch, sailcloth, and iron receive priority in the crowded basin because a delay in one material can idle several trades at once. This ordering of the waterfront is as important to Caerwyn's output as the skill of any single craft, and neighbouring ports send observers to study the records and signals by which the guild coordinates the work.

## History

Caerwyn was founded as a fishing village on the rocky promontory, where the natural harbour and ready supply of inland timber encouraged the earliest inhabitants to build and repair their own boats. The settlement grew as demand for larger vessels increased, and the workshops beside the harbour developed the techniques that made the city the foremost centre of maritime construction on the eastern coast. The oldest surviving yard dates from the early centuries of settlement, and several shipwright families trace their service to those first workshops.

The shipyards assumed their present scale during the great expansion of eastern trade, when the smaller yards of the coastal villages could no longer meet the demand for seagoing vessels. New slips and dry docks were cut into the inner harbour, while timber roads and river landings were established to supply them from the northern forests. The concentration of shipwrights, metalworkers, sailmakers, and instrument-makers gave Caerwyn an advantage that no isolated yard could readily match.

Caerwyn entered the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) during the league's early centuries, securing steady commissions and a protected market for its ships. The city has since built vessels for league fleets, independent merchants, and neighbouring kingdoms, while preserving its reputation through guild inspection and long apprenticeships. Its pre-eminence rests not upon a single invention but upon generations of careful craftsmanship sustained across every maritime trade.

## Significance

Caerwyn is the primary centre of shipbuilding on the eastern coast of Aethelar, and its ships are the foundation of the maritime power of the continent. The vessels built in its shipyards carry the trade of Aethelar across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) to [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), patrol the coast against raiders, and explore the waters beyond the known trade routes. The reputation of Caerwyn's ships extends to every port in the known world, and the city is a valued ally of the trading cities and a respected rival of the other shipbuilding centres of the coast.
