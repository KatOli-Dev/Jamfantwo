## Foremost Shipbuilding Centre of the Eastern Coast

Caerwyn is an eastern port city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) renowned throughout the known world for its shipyards and the vessels they produce. The city stands on a rocky promontory that juts into the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and its harbour is sheltered by a natural breakwater of rock that forms a deep, calm basin within sight of the open sea. The combination of a fine harbour, a ready supply of timber from the inland forests, and a long tradition of shipbuilding has made Caerwyn the foremost centre of maritime construction on the eastern coast. Its ships are the backbone of the merchant fleets and naval forces of Aethelar.

## Slipways, Timber Rafts, and the Master Shipwrights

The shipyards of Caerwyn are the largest and most extensive on the eastern coast, lining the inner shore of the harbour in a continuous stretch of slipways, dry docks, and workshops. The construction of vessels is the dominant activity of the city, and the sound of sawing, hammering, and the caulking of seams fills the air from dawn to dusk. The ships built in Caerwyn are noted for their strength, speed, and seaworthiness, and the techniques of their construction have been refined over centuries of experience with the conditions of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean).

The timber for the shipyards is brought downriver from the inland forests, where great trees of the northern woodland are felled and floated to Caerwyn in rafts that are a common sight on the river. The selection and seasoning of timber is a skilled practice, and the shipwrights of Caerwyn understand the properties of different woods, matching timber to the function of each part of the vessel. Oak is used for frames and planking, pine for masts and spars, and elm for underwater components that must resist rot. The shipyards also produce masts, spars, oars, and the smaller craft essential to the maritime trade of the coast, and the output of the yards is a significant export in its own right.

The construction of a large vessel is a complex undertaking requiring many labourers and the co-ordination of many trades. Master shipwrights oversee the work, and carpenters, caulkers, riggers, and sailmakers each contribute their skills. Ironwork, including anchors, nails, and fittings, is supplied by the city's smiths, and the sails are woven from cloth imported from the interior. The construction of a single large vessel can take the better part of a year, and the shipyards of Caerwyn employ hundreds of workers in the construction and fitting out of the fleet built each year. The yards maintain a stock of prefabricated components, including standardised frame sections and deck planks, that allow for the rapid repair of vessels damaged at sea.

The population of Caerwyn is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) drawn by the demand for metalwork, anchors, and iron fittings. The [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) of Caerwyn are noted for their skill in making navigational instruments and the fine mechanisms used aboard larger vessels. Gnomish instrument-makers are renowned for the precision of their astrolabes, compasses, and sounding leads essential to open-ocean navigation, and the instruments of Caerwyn are sought after by navigators of every fleet.

## The Basin, the Berths, and the Guild

The harbour of Caerwyn is a deep, sheltered basin that accommodates both ships under construction and vessels calling at the port for trade. The trade of Caerwyn is secondary to its shipbuilding but is nonetheless substantial, as the city is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling along the eastern coast. Goods passing through the harbour include the produce of the interior, materials for the shipyards, and provisions for the vessels fitted out and supplied at the port.

The harbour guild assigns berths so that arriving traders do not obstruct launches from the slips, and its pilots move unfinished hulls between the yards and fitting quays. Timber, pitch, sailcloth, and iron receive priority in the crowded basin because a delay in one material can idle several trades at once. This ordering of the waterfront is as important to Caerwyn's output as the skill of any single craft, and neighbouring ports send observers to study the records and signals by which the guild co-ordinates the work.

## From Fishing Village to the League's Shipyard

Caerwyn was founded as a fishing village on the rocky promontory, where the natural harbour and ready supply of inland timber encouraged the earliest inhabitants to build and repair their own boats. The settlement grew as demand for larger vessels increased, and the workshops beside the harbour developed the techniques that made the city the foremost centre of maritime construction on the eastern coast. The oldest surviving yard dates from the early centuries of settlement, and several shipwright families trace their service to those first workshops.

The shipyards assumed their present scale during the great expansion of eastern trade, when the smaller yards of the coastal villages could no longer meet the demand for seagoing vessels. New slips and dry docks were cut into the inner harbour, while timber roads and river landings were established to supply them from the northern forests. The concentration of shipwrights, metalworkers, sailmakers, and instrument-makers gave Caerwyn an advantage that no isolated yard could readily match.

Caerwyn entered the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) during the league's early centuries, securing steady commissions and a protected market for its ships. The city has since built vessels for league fleets, independent merchants, and neighbouring kingdoms while preserving its reputation through guild inspection and long apprenticeships. Its pre-eminence rests not upon a single invention but upon generations of careful craftsmanship sustained across every maritime trade.

## Foundation of Aethelar's Maritime Power

Caerwyn is the primary centre of shipbuilding on the eastern coast of Aethelar, and its ships are the foundation of the maritime power of the continent. The vessels built in its shipyards carry the trade of Aethelar across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) to [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), patrol the coast against raiders, and explore the waters beyond the known trade routes. The reputation of Caerwyn's ships extends to every port in the known world, and the city is a valued ally of the trading cities and a respected rival of the other shipbuilding centres of the coast.
