## A City in the Ashen Heath

Ash Vale is a city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) situated in the Ashen Heath, a region of the eastern interior characterised by its pale, volcanic soils and its sparse vegetation. The city stands in a shallow valley where a tributary of the Greywash River provides water for the settlement and the surrounding fields. Ash Vale is the principal settlement of the Ashen Heath and serves as a centre of trade and administration for the region.

The Ashen Heath is a landscape shaped by the volcanic activity that occurred in the region in a distant age. The soils of the heath are composed of ash and pumice, pale grey in colour and light in texture, and the vegetation that grows upon them is sparse and stunted. The heath is a harsh and demanding environment, but the soils are fertile when watered, and the fields of Ash Vale produce crops of grain and vegetables that sustain the city and the surrounding settlements.

## The Ashen Heath and the Volcanic Soils

The Ashen Heath extends across a broad area of the eastern interior, covering the lower slopes of the Crown Range and the plains that lie at their foot. The heath takes its name from the volcanic ash that covers the ground, a fine, pale grey material that gives the landscape an otherworldly appearance. The ash was deposited by the eruption of a chain of volcanoes in the Crown Range in a distant age, and the deposits are deep enough to cover the underlying rock in many places.

The volcanic soils of the Ashen Heath are light and porous, draining rapidly after rain and warming quickly in the sun. The soils are rich in the minerals that plants require for growth, and the fields that have been cleared and cultivated in the Ashen Heath are among the most productive in the eastern interior. The cultivation of the volcanic soils requires careful management, for the soils are easily eroded by wind and water, and the farmers of Ash Vale have developed techniques of terracing and drainage that protect the fields from loss.

## From Frontier Outpost to Regional Centre

The earliest settlement at the site of Ash Vale was a frontier outpost established during the expansion of the settled areas into the Ashen Heath. The outpost was a small fortified settlement built to protect the farmers and herders who were beginning to cultivate the volcanic soils of the heath. The outpost was garrisoned by a small force responsible for the defence of the settlers against the raiders who inhabited the eastern margins.

The growth of the outpost into a permanent settlement followed the successful cultivation of the Ashen Heath. The fertility of the volcanic soils was discovered by the early settlers, and the fields that they planted produced bountiful harvests that attracted more settlers to the region. The outpost became a village, the village became a town, and the town was eventually raised to the status of a city.

The granting of city status was a recognition of the importance of Ash Vale as the principal settlement of the Ashen Heath and a centre of trade and administration for the eastern interior. The city was given the rights and privileges appropriate to its rank, and the officials responsible for the governance of the heath established their seat at Ash Vale.

## The Terrace Fields and the Irrigation Channels

The fields that surround Ash Vale are among the most distinctive features of the landscape. The volcanic soils of the heath are easily eroded, and the farmers of the region have developed a system of terraced fields that follow the contours of the valley, each terrace supported by a retaining wall of stone that prevents the soil from washing away. The terraces are irrigated by a system of channels that divert water from the tributary stream and distribute it across the fields.

The irrigation system of Ash Vale is among the most extensive in the eastern interior, and the management of the channels and the distribution of water are regulated by the city. The irrigation channels are maintained by the farming communities of the valley, and the work of cleaning and repairing the channels is a regular obligation of the landholders. The system of water rights that governs the distribution of irrigation water is based on the area of land under cultivation and the needs of the crops.

## The Grain, the Wool, and the Trade of the Heath

The economy of Ash Vale is built on the agricultural products of the Ashen Heath and the trade that connects the heath to the wider world. The grain of the heath is the principal crop, and the flour produced by the mills of Ash Vale is exported to the cities of the eastern lowlands. The wool of the sheep that graze on the coarse vegetation of the heath is prized for its strength and durability, and the cloth woven from the wool is used for the clothing of working people across the eastern districts.

The trade of Ash Vale is conducted through the weekly market held in the market square of the city and through the seasonal fairs that mark the principal events of the agricultural calendar. The merchants of Ash Vale trade with the cities of the Greywash valley and the eastern lowlands, exchanging the grain and wool of the heath for the manufactured goods and luxuries that the heath cannot produce.

## The Stone Houses and the Streets of Ash Vale

The architecture of Ash Vale is adapted to the conditions of the Ashen Heath. The buildings of the city are constructed of stone from the Crown Range, the same grey volcanic stone that underlies the soils of the heath. The houses are built with thick walls and small windows, providing shelter from the wind and the sun. The roofs are covered with stone tiles, heavy enough to resist the strong winds that sweep across the heath.

The streets of Ash Vale are broad and straight, laid out in a grid pattern that reflects the planned character of the settlement. The city was rebuilt after a destructive fire in a century past, and the opportunity was taken to lay out the streets in a regular pattern that would ease the movement of traffic and the access of fire crews. The market square occupies the centre of the grid, with the principal public buildings of the city arranged around it.

## The Council of the Heath and the Governance of Ash Vale

Ash Vale is governed by a council of elected representatives, with the principal landholders and merchants of the city holding the positions of greatest authority. The council is responsible for the administration of the city and the regulation of the agriculture and trade of the Ashen Heath. The council meets in the guild hall, a substantial building of stone that stands at one end of the market square.

The governance of the Ashen Heath beyond the city is the responsibility of the wardens of the heath, officials appointed by the council to oversee the farming communities of the region. The wardens are responsible for the maintenance of the irrigation system, the regulation of the grazing rights, and the resolution of disputes between the landholders of the heath.

## Ash Vale as the Heart of the Ashen Heath

Ash Vale is the heart of the Ashen Heath and the principal settlement of the eastern interior. The city's position in the fertile valley of the heath and its role as the centre of trade and administration for the surrounding region have made it a settlement of considerable importance in the eastern districts of Aethelar. The farmers and merchants of Ash Vale have adapted to the demanding conditions of the Ashen Heath and have built a prosperous settlement in a landscape that many would consider inhospitable. The city stands as a testament to the capacity of determined people to transform a harsh environment into a productive and sustaining home.
