## Overview

Avaros is a great agricultural city of the central grasslands of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated in the heart of the most productive farming region of the continent. The city stands on a low rise in the middle of the open plain, where the grassland stretches in every direction to the horizon, and its position at the centre of the grasslands has made it the primary market and the principal depot for the grain, the livestock, and the other products of the region. Avaros is a large, prosperous city, and its wealth is built upon the fertility of the soil and the industry of the farmers who work it.

## The Grasslands and the Agriculture

The central grasslands of Aethelar are among the most productive agricultural regions in the known world, and the land around Avaros is the richest portion of the grasslands. The soil is deep and dark, formed over centuries from the decay of the prairie grasses that cover the plain, and it produces grain in abundance, along with the other crops that are grown in the region. The agriculture of the grasslands is a mixture of large-scale grain production and the pastoralism of the [centaur](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) kindreds, and the two systems coexist in a relationship that is generally beneficial to both.

The grain that is produced on the farms around Avaros is the primary export of the city, and the granaries of Avaros are among the largest in the known world, capable of storing enough grain to feed the continent for many months. The grain is purchased by the merchants of the city from the farmers of the surrounding country, stored in the granaries, and sold to the traders who transport it to the markets of the coast and the interior. The grain trade is the foundation of the economy of Avaros, and the city's prosperity is directly tied to the fertility of the land and the success of the harvest.

The pastoralism of the centaur kindreds is the other pillar of the agricultural economy, and the herds of horses and cattle that are tended on the open plain are a significant part of the output of the region. The centaurs are nomadic, and their movements across the grasslands are governed by the seasons and the availability of pasture. The relationship between the settled farmers and the nomadic herders is governed by a body of custom that has developed over the centuries, and the rights of pasture, water, and passage are regulated by agreements that are renegotiated each year at the market of Avaros. The system is not without its tensions, but the mutual benefit of the trade has sustained a working relationship that is among the most successful examples of co-operation between the settled and the nomadic peoples of the continent.

## The City

Avaros is a large, open city, and its architecture reflects the character of the grasslands, with broad streets, low buildings, and wide-open spaces that accommodate the market, the granaries, and the stockyards that are the primary institutions of the settlement. The buildings are constructed of the light-coloured stone that is quarried from the occasional outcrops that rise from the plain, and the roofs are pitched to shed the rain that comes in sudden, heavy storms during the summer months. The granaries are the most prominent structures in the city, and their great silos rise above the rooftops, visible for miles across the flat expanse of the plain. The walls of the granaries are thick and sealed against the damp, and the interiors are ventilated by a system of channels that allow the air to circulate and keep the grain dry.

The stockyards of Avaros are the other great institution of the city, and the pens and the pastures that surround the town are the gathering point for the livestock that is traded at the market. The cattle and the horses that are bred on the grasslands are brought to the stockyards for sale, and the centaur herders who tend them camp in the open ground beside the yards, their tents and their herds a familiar sight during the market days.

The market square of Avaros is one of the largest in the known world, and the market that is held there is the primary point of exchange for the agricultural products of the grasslands and the manufactured goods that are imported from the coast and the interior. The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) of the surrounding plains are regular visitors to the market, and the trade between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is the lifeblood of the city's commerce. The market is held each week, and the crowds that gather there are a mixture of farmers, traders, craftsmen, and centaur herders who come to buy and sell, to exchange news, and to settle the business of the plains.

## Significance

Avaros is the agricultural heart of Aethelar, and the grain that is produced, stored, and traded in the city feeds a significant portion of the continent. The city is the largest and most prosperous settlement of the central grasslands, and it is the primary member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), the alliance of the grassland cities and the pastoral peoples. The prosperity of Avaros is a direct consequence of the fertility of the land and the industry of its people, and the city is a symbol of the agricultural wealth that is the foundation of the economy of Aethelar.

## History

Avaros was founded in the early centuries of human settlement in the central grasslands, and the site was chosen for the low rise that provided a defensible position in the open plain and the proximity to the river that watered the surrounding fields. The earliest settlement was a farming village, and the fertility of the soil encouraged the growth of the community. The granaries were built as the surplus of the harvest grew beyond the needs of the local population, and the grain trade that developed around the granaries transformed the village into a town and then a city.

The Plains Confederation was founded at Avaros, and the city has been the seat of the confederation's council since its inception. The council of the confederation meets in the great hall that stands at the centre of the market square, and the representatives of the grassland cities and the centaur kindreds gather there to settle the business of the plains. The confederation has been a force for stability and co-operation in the grasslands, and the leadership of Avaros has been a constant in the politics of the region, a steadiness that is a reflection of the enduring prosperity of the city and the wisdom of its people.
