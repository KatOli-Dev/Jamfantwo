## A City at the Edge of the Brackenshield Wood

Thornwood is a city of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) built at the edge of Brackenshield Wood, an extensive forest that covers the eastern slopes of the southern highlands. The city stands where the forest gives way to the open farmlands of the interior, and its position at the boundary of woodland and cultivated land has shaped its character and its economy. Thornwood is not among the oldest or the largest cities of Aethelar, but it is a settlement of considerable importance in the eastern districts, serving as a centre of trade, administration, and the management of the great forest that surrounds it.

The Brackenshield Wood is a forest of oak, ash, and beech, with thickets of thorn and briar at its margins from which the city derives its name. The forest is of ancient origin, its trees standing where they have stood for centuries, and its undergrowth is dense and tangled, making passage through the woodland difficult except by the roads and tracks that have been maintained since the earliest period of settlement. The forest is home to deer, boar, and smaller game, and the hunters of Thornwood pursue these animals for meat and hide.

## The Brackenshield Wood and the Southern Highland Slopes

The Brackenshield Wood covers the eastern slopes of the southern highlands, extending from the foothills in the east to the higher ground of the interior. The forest is the largest continuous woodland in the eastern districts of Aethelar, and its trees are among the finest in the continent, producing timber of exceptional quality for the construction and carpentry trades of the southern cities. The forest has been managed since the earliest period of settlement, and the rights to fell timber and to hunt game within its boundaries are regulated by the authorities of Thornwood.

The wood takes its name from the dense thickets of thorn that grow at its margins, forming a natural barrier between the forest and the surrounding farmland. The thorn thickets are composed of several species of hawthorn and blackthorn, their branches intertwined to form a barrier that is difficult to penetrate without the use of tools and axes. The thickets have been cleared in places to create access to the forest, but they remain a defining feature of the landscape, marking the boundary between the woodland and the open country.

## From Timber Camp to Chartered Market Town

The earliest settlement at the site of Thornwood was a timber camp established by loggers who came to the Brackenshield Wood to fell trees for the construction of the southern cities. The camp grew over time as the loggers were joined by hunters, charcoal burners, and the craftsmen who worked the timber into boards and beams. The settlement became a permanent village, and the village became a town when the lords of the eastern districts granted it a charter to hold a weekly market.

The chartering of Thornwood as a market town was the turning point in its growth. The weekly market drew farmers, traders, and craftsmen from the surrounding country, and the town became the commercial centre of the eastern districts. The timber of the Brackenshield Wood was the principal commodity traded at the market, and the merchants of Thornwood grew wealthy on the trade in wood and wood products that passed through the town.

Thornwood was raised to the status of a city in a later century, when the growth of the settlement and the increase in its population and trade justified the elevation of its status. The grant of city status was made by the authority that governed the eastern districts at the time, and the new city was endowed with the rights and privileges that pertained to urban settlements of its rank. The grant recognised the importance of Thornwood as the principal settlement of the eastern forest districts and confirmed its position as the centre of the timber trade of the region.

## The Timber Yards, the Sawpits, and the Carpenters Quarter

The economy of Thornwood is dominated by the timber trade, and the industries that process the timber are the largest employers in the city. The timber yards lie at the eastern edge of the city, where the logs brought from the forest are sorted, measured, and prepared for processing. The yards are extensive, covering many acres, and the stacks of logs and planks are a familiar sight in the eastern districts.

The sawpits of Thornwood are the principal sites where logs are cut into planks and beams. The pits are staffed by skilled sawyers who work in pairs, one standing above the log and one in the pit below, using a long saw to cut the timber to the required dimensions. The sawpits of Thornwood are among the most productive in the eastern districts, and the timber processed there is exported to markets across Aethelar.

The carpenters quarter of Thornwood is the district where the finished timber is worked into the products of the woodworking trade. The quarter is lined with the workshops of carpenters, joiners, cabinet-makers, and carvers, each plying the craft for which they are known. The furniture of Thornwood is noted for its quality and durability, and the carved objects produced in the quarter are exported to the southern cities and beyond.

## The Weekly Market and the Annual Timber Fair

The weekly market of Thornwood is held in the market square at the centre of the city, a broad open space surrounded by the principal buildings of the settlement. The square is occupied by stalls and booths on market day, and the farmers, traders, and craftsmen of the surrounding country gather there to buy and sell the goods of the region. The market is supervised by the market authority of Thornwood, which regulates the trade and collects the dues that fund the maintenance of the square and the city's public works.

The annual timber fair of Thornwood is the most important event of the commercial calendar, drawing timber merchants and buyers from across Aethelar to the city. The fair is held in the autumn, after the logging season has ended and the timber has been brought from the forest to the yards. The fair lasts for several days, and the merchants of Thornwood exhibit the best of the year's timber, competing for the attention of the buyers who attend from the cities of the coast and the interior.

## The Masters of the Wood and the Council of Thornwood

The governance of Thornwood is in the hands of a council elected by the citizens of the city, with the timber merchants and master craftsmen holding the principal positions of authority. The council is responsible for the administration of the city, the regulation of the timber trade, and the maintenance of the public works. The council meets in the guild hall, a substantial building of timber and stone that stands at one side of the market square.

The masters of the wood are the principal figures of authority in the timber trade of Thornwood. The masters are experienced loggers and foresters appointed by the council to oversee the management of the Brackenshield Wood and to regulate the felling and removal of timber. The masters of the wood are among the most respected figures in Thornwood, for the knowledge and experience they bring to the management of the forest are the foundation of the prosperity of the city.

## Thornwood as the Centre of the Eastern Timber Trade

Thornwood is the principal centre of the timber trade in the eastern districts of Aethelar and the largest settlement of the Brackenshield Wood region. The city's position at the edge of the great forest and its long history of managing and processing the timber of the woodland have made it the natural centre of the trade that is the foundation of the economy of the eastern districts. The citizens of Thornwood are proud of the crafts of the wood that have sustained the city for centuries, and the skill of the woodsmen, sawyers, and carpenters of Thornwood is recognised across Aethelar. The city stands at the boundary of forest and field, a settlement that has grown in harmony with the woodland that surrounds it.
