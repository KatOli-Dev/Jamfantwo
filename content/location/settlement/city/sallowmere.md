## Overview

Sallowmere is a southern port city on the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), situated where the coastline begins to curve toward the warm plains of the south. The city is the gateway to the warm plains and the southern trade routes, and its harbour is the last major port of call for vessels heading south along the eastern coast before the land turns toward the open [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The trade of Sallowmere is shaped by its position at the boundary of the temperate and the warm regions, and the goods that pass through its harbour reflect the transition from the products of the temperate north to the exotic goods of the southern seas.

## The Harbour and Trade

The harbour of Sallowmere is a broad, shallow bay that is sheltered by a long sandbar that runs parallel to the shore, forming a natural breakwater that calms the waters within. The approach to the harbour requires careful navigation, for the sandbar shifts with the seasonal storms, and the pilots of Sallowmere are skilled in the reading of the channels and the tides. The quays are built along the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses of the city line the waterfront.

The trade of Sallowmere is a mixture of coastal and oceanic commerce. The coastal trade connects the city to the ports of the eastern shore, carrying grain, timber, and the manufactured goods of the north. The oceanic trade connects the city to [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and the goods that arrive from the south include spices, dyes, and the rare products of the warm regions that are prized in the markets of the north. Sallowmere is also the terminus of the overland routes that cross the warm plains, and the goods of the interior, including the wool and leather of the pastoral peoples, are gathered at the city for shipment to the northern markets.

The seasonal rhythm of the trade is shaped by the winds and the currents of the Auric Ocean, for the voyage to Sahrana and the islands is governed by the monsoon pattern that prevails in the southern waters. The southbound vessels depart in the autumn, when the winds turn favourable, and the northbound vessels arrive in the spring, bringing the goods of the south to the markets of the eastern coast. The merchants of Sallowmere are skilled in the timing of the voyages, and the harbour is busiest in the weeks before the departure of the southern fleet, when the warehouses are filled and the quays are crowded with the loading of the ships.

The population of Sallowmere is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a growing community of [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) from the warm plains who are drawn to the city by the trade and the opportunities of the coast. The relationship between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is one of cautious co-operation, and the markets of Sallowmere are a meeting point where the two peoples exchange goods and news. The centaurs of the plains bring wool, leather, and the livestock that they raise on the open grassland, and they take in return the grain, the cloth, and the metal tools that the city imports from the north.

## The City

Sallowmere is a warm, sunlit city, and its architecture reflects the mild climate of the southern coast. The buildings are constructed of light-coloured stone, with wide windows and shaded verandas that take advantage of the sea breezes, and the roofs are tiled in the manner of the southern tradition. The streets are broad and lined with trees that provide shade, and the public squares are furnished with fountains and wells that are a central feature of the city's life.

The population of Sallowmere is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a growing community of [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) from the warm plains who are drawn to the city by the trade and the opportunities of the coast. ## Significance

Sallowmere is the primary gateway between the temperate eastern coast and the warm southern regions, and its trade connects the markets of the north to the goods of the south. The city is the southernmost major port of the eastern coast, and its position at the boundary of two climatic zones gives it a commercial significance that is disproportionate to its size. The city is a member of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league), and its trade with the centaurs of the warm plains is a valuable asset of the league's southern commerce.

## History

Sallowmere was founded as a fishing village on the sheltered bay, and the earliest settlers were drawn by the abundance of the coastal waters and the fertility of the land nearby. The village grew into a town as the trade between the northern coast and the southern regions developed, and the natural advantages of the harbour attracted merchants and shipowners who established the trading houses that still line the waterfront. The city was admitted to the Thessaran League in the early centuries of the league's existence, and the membership has been a source of prosperity and protection, for the league's fleet patrols the southern waters and the league's council has arbitrated the disputes that have arisen between Sallowmere and its neighbours.

The growth of the centaur trade has been the most significant development in the recent history of Sallowmere, and the relationship between the city and the plains kindreds has deepened over the generations. The centaurs were once regarded with suspicion by the settled population, but the mutual benefit of the trade has fostered a degree of trust and co-operation that is unusual between the settled and the nomadic peoples. The centaur quarter of the city, a district of broad yards and open shelters that lies outside the walls, is a testament to the growing permanence of the relationship, and the centaur elders who reside there during the trading season are a recognised part of the city's commercial life.
