## Overview

The Amber Vale is a fertile river valley in central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), renowned for its agricultural wealth and the quiet prosperity of its settled communities. The vale takes its name from the warm, golden light that falls upon the ripening grain in the late summer, and the name has been used by the inhabitants of the region for as many generations as the chronicles record. The valley is the heartland of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth), and the city of [Stagford](/content/location/settlement/city/stagford) is its principal settlement, though numerous towns and villages are scattered along the length of the river that waters the vale.

## Geography and Climate

The Amber Vale lies along the course of the Ald, the principal river of central Aethelar, which rises in the highlands to the east and flows westward through a broad, gentle valley before joining the larger river systems that drain toward the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean). The river meanders across the valley floor, its course shifting over the centuries and leaving behind the rich alluvial soils that make the vale one of the most productive agricultural regions in the known world. The valley is sheltered on its northern and southern sides by low, wooded hills that moderate the climate, giving the vale warm summers and mild winters with reliable rainfall throughout the growing season.

The soil of the Amber Vale is the foundation of its prosperity, a deep, dark loam that is replenished by the annual flooding of the Ald. The floodplain is broad and flat, and the fields that cover it are divided by hedgerows and drainage ditches that have been maintained and improved over generations of careful husbandry. The hills that bound the vale are clothed in mixed woodland, chiefly oak, ash, and beech, with stands of pine on the drier slopes, and these woodlands provide timber, fuel, and shelter for the wildlife that is hunted by the inhabitants of the valley.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the Amber Vale are numerous and well-established, ranging from the prosperous city of Stagford to the modest farming villages that dot the valley floor. The city of Stagford is the seat of the Aldreth monarchy and the administrative centre of the kingdom, and its market is among the largest in Aethelar, drawing merchants from across the continent and beyond. The towns of the vale, including [Brackenford](/content/location/settlement/town/brackenford) and [Fernmoor](/content/location/settlement/town/fernmoor), are centres of local trade and craftsmanship, each with its own market and its own specialisation in the production of goods from the resources of the valley.

The population of the Amber Vale is predominantly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), but the region has long been a meeting point of different peoples. [Halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings) are present in significant numbers, particularly in the farming communities, where their skill in agriculture and their love of good food and drink are valued by their human neighbours. [Gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) are found in the towns, where they work as craftsmen and engineers, and their workshops are a familiar feature of the market squares and the artisan quarters. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the neighbouring woodlands maintain a presence in the vale through trade, and the relations between the settled peoples of the valley and the elven communities of the forest are generally cordial.

## Economy

The economy of the Amber Vale is built upon agriculture, and the fields of the valley produce an abundance of grain, chiefly wheat and barley, that is exported to regions less favoured by climate or soil. The grain of the Amber Vale is noted for its quality, and the flour that is milled from it is prized by bakers throughout central Aethelar. The orchards that cover the lower slopes of the hills produce apples, pears, and plums, and the cider and the preserved fruits of the vale are valued exports that are traded as far as the markets of the Sahrani coast.

The livestock of the Amber Vale is another significant element of the economy, and the cattle and sheep that graze on the hillsides and the stubble fields after the harvest provide meat, milk, wool, and leather for the local population and for export. The wool of the Amber Vale is particularly noted for its softness and its warmth, and the cloth that is woven from it is the material of choice for the garments of the northern kingdoms. The leather that is produced by the tanneries of Stagford is used for harnesses, saddles, and boots, and the quality of the workmanship is such that the goods of the Aldreth leatherworkers are sought after by travellers from the distant continents.

## Culture and Governance

The culture of the Amber Vale is shaped by the agricultural cycle and by the traditions of the kingdom that governs it. The festivals of the planting and the harvest are the most important events of the year, and the people of the vale gather in their towns and villages to celebrate with feasting, music, and the exchange of gifts. The monarchy of Aldreth is the unifying institution of the region, and the king or queen who sits at Stagford is the focus of the loyalty of the people of the vale, though the administration of the valley is managed by the local lords and the councils of the towns.

## Significance

The Amber Vale is the agricultural heartland of the Kingdom of Aldreth and one of the most productive regions in Aethelar. Its grain, its wool, and its livestock are the foundation of the kingdom's prosperity, and its markets are the meeting point of the trade that flows through central Aethelar. The vale is a region of settled, ordered prosperity, and its stability is the foundation upon which the influence of the Kingdom of Aldreth is built. The Amber Vale is a reminder that the wealth of nations is built not upon the conquest of distant lands but upon the patient cultivation of the land that lies beneath the feet of the people.
