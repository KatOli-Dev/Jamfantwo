## Overview

The Thessaran Basin is the broad lowland and valley country of the eastern interior of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the region of fertile plains, slow rivers, and gentle hills that is drained by the great rivers flowing east to the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The basin takes its name from the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league), the confederation of eastern coast port cities that has governed the maritime terminus of these rivers for many centuries, and the name is applied by chroniclers to the entire inland country whose commerce finds its outlet through the league's harbours. The Thessaran Basin is the agricultural heartland of the eastern coast, and the grain, the wool, the timber, and the other products of its fields and forests sustain the cities of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) and the trade of the wider world.

The basin is not a single realm but a region of overlapping jurisdictions, including the territories of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) in the centre and the south, the holdings of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) at the coast, and the more distant territories of the interior kingdoms that look to the eastern rivers for their outlet to the sea. The political complexity of the basin is matched by the economic unity of the region, for the rivers that flow through the basin carry the goods of the interior to the ports of the league and bring the goods of the ocean to the markets of the interior. The result is a region of shared prosperity and frequent rivalry, where the cities of the league and the inland towns depend upon one another for their wealth.

## Geography and Climate

The basin occupies the eastern interior of the continent, stretching from the foothills of the mountain spine in the west to the coastal lowlands and the harbours of the eastern shore. The land is gently rolling, with broad valleys and low, rounded hills that break the horizon without ever obscuring it entirely. The rivers that drain the basin are slow and meandering in their lower courses, fed by the tributaries that descend from the foothills, and their floodplains are among the most fertile in Aethelar. The soil of the basin is a deep, dark loam that has been renewed by centuries of cultivation and by the annual flooding of the rivers, and the fertility of the region is such that the grain crops grow as tall as a man in the summer.

The climate of the basin is mild and temperate, with warm summers and cool winters that are moderated by the influence of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The prevailing winds from the east bring moisture from the ocean, and the rainfall is reliable throughout the growing season, supporting the agriculture that is the foundation of the regional economy. The fogs that rise from the rivers in the autumn are a familiar feature of the basin, and the morning mists that cling to the valleys in the late summer are a sign of the coming harvest. The winters are not severe, and the snow that falls upon the basin is light and rarely lingers beyond a few days.

The rivers of the basin are the principal feature of the regional geography, and their courses have shaped the distribution of the population and the pattern of the trade. The largest of these rivers is navigable for much of its length, and the barges that carry the goods of the interior to the coast and the goods of the coast to the interior are a common sight on the waterways during the navigable season. The smaller rivers are too shallow for barge traffic but support the local commerce of the towns and villages that line their banks, and the mills that grind the grain of the basin are powered by the waters that flow from the foothills to the sea.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the basin are of two principal kinds, reflecting the dual character of the regional economy. The agricultural communities are concentrated along the rivers and in the areas where the soil is deepest and the rainfall most reliable, and these communities are organised into towns and villages that are connected by a network of roads and tracks. The market towns of the interior serve as the gathering points for the agricultural produce of the surrounding country, and the markets that are held at regular intervals are the centre of the social and the commercial life of the region.

The cities of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) are the maritime outlets of the basin, and their harbours are the points through which the goods of the interior reach the ocean. [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) is the largest and the most influential of the league's members, a wealthy port whose harbour is among the finest on the eastern coast. [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) is the principal shipyard of the league, renowned for the vessels that are built in its yards for the Auric trade. [Thornhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/thornhaven) commands the upper reaches of the principal river and the mountain passes that connect the basin to the interior highlands. [Sallowmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sallowmere) is the southernmost member, and its trade with the warm plains and the southern regions is a valued component of the league's commerce.

The population of the basin is the most diverse and the most numerous of any region of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). [Humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) are the majority, and their communities range from small farming villages to the great port cities of the coast. [Elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) are present in the more wooded portions of the basin, particularly in the river valleys where the ancient forests have been preserved. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) are found in the foothills and the smaller mining settlements, where their skill in metalwork and stone is applied to the resources of the region. [Gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) inhabit the transitional country between the fields and the forests, and their workshops produce the finer instruments and the mechanisms that are valued by the cities and the farms alike. [Halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings) are present in the agricultural communities, where their skill in farming and their attachment to the settled life make them valued members of the towns and villages.

## Agriculture and the Trade

The agriculture of the Thessaran Basin is the foundation of the regional economy, and the grain, the wool, the flax, and the other crops that are produced in the fertile fields of the interior are the primary exports of the region. The wheat and the barley of the basin are among the finest in Aethelar, and the flour that is milled from them is exported to the cities of the coast and the other regions of the continent. The wool of the basin sheep is woven into cloth that is valued in the markets of the eastern ports, and the linen that is produced from the flax of the river valleys is exported to the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) and the ports beyond. The orchards and the vineyards of the basin produce fruit and wine in abundance, and the surplus is traded to the markets of the coast.

The rivers are the arteries of the basin's commerce, and the barges that travel them carry the agricultural produce of the interior to the harbours of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league). The journey from the upper river to the coast takes several days, and the barges are a familiar sight on the waterways, their loads marked by the standards of the merchant houses that own them. The tolls that are collected at the locks and the weirs along the rivers are a significant source of revenue for the inland communities, and the maintenance of the navigation channels is a constant expense of the regional administration.

The return trade is equally important, for the cities of the coast provide the manufactured goods, the spices, and the exotic products that the inland communities cannot produce for themselves. The cloth, the metalwork, and the finer goods of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the eastern ports are carried upriver to the markets of the interior, and the spices and the dyes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) reach the inland towns through the same routes. The volume of this two-way trade is the foundation of the prosperity of the basin, and the merchants who manage it are among the most influential members of the regional society.

## Cities and the League

The cities of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) are the commercial heart of the basin, and their collective power in the maritime trade of the eastern coast is the foundation of the regional prosperity. The league is governed by a council of representatives from each member city, and the council meets regularly to coordinate the trade, the defence, and the navigation of the eastern coast. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the league has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the league is a model of cooperation among independent cities, and the council is among the most effective political institutions in Aethelar.

The collective fleet of the league is the principal instrument of its power, and the vessels that patrol the trade routes of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) are a deterrent to the pirates and the raiders who would otherwise prey upon the commerce of the basin. The admiral of the fleet is appointed by the council, and the ships are funded by the contributions of the member cities. The fleet is divided between the coast patrol, which protects the harbours and the coastal trade, and the ocean squadron, which escorts the merchant convoys on the longer voyages to the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) and the ports of the southern seas.

The relationship between the league and the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) is one of cooperation and occasional tension, for the capital of the kingdom is also the largest member of the league, and the dual role of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) as a member of the league and the seat of the crown is a source of complexity in the politics of the basin. The interests of the league and the kingdom are not always identical, for the league represents the collective interest of the port cities while the kingdom represents the broader interests of the realm. The balance between the two is maintained through negotiation and compromise, and the resulting relationship has been a factor in the stability of the eastern coast.

## Culture and Governance

The culture of the Thessaran Basin is shaped by the fertility of the land and the prosperity that flows from it. The agricultural communities value the stability and the continuity that the basin provides, and the traditions of the farming year are the foundation of the social order. The harvest festivals that are observed in every village are the most important occasions of the year, and the gatherings that accompany them are the principal expressions of the regional culture. The songs and the dances of the harvest are distinctive to the basin, and the traditions of the regional cuisine are the subject of pride among the inhabitants.

The governance of the basin is fragmented, and the various jurisdictions overlap in ways that can be confusing to the outsider. The [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) administers the central and the southern portions of the basin through a network of officials and local councils. The [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) governs the coastal cities and the maritime trade. The smaller polities of the interior, including the market towns and the mining communities, manage their own affairs through their own councils and their own traditions. The result is a region of complex governance in which the authority of the king, the council of the league, and the local assemblies all play a part, and the negotiation among these authorities is a constant feature of the regional politics.

## Significance

The Thessaran Basin is the agricultural and commercial heartland of the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the prosperity of the region is the foundation of the wealth of the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) and the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league). The grain and the wool that are produced in the basin feed and clothe the cities of the coast and sustain the populations of the wider continent, and the trade that flows through the ports of the league is a critical component of the commerce of the known world. The significance of the basin lies in its productivity and its commercial power, and the region is a symbol of the wealth and the cosmopolitanism of the eastern coast of Aethelar.

The rivers that drain the basin are the arteries of its commerce, and the harbours of the league are the outlet through which the goods of the interior reach the markets of the world. The cooperation between the cities of the league and the agricultural communities of the interior is a model of the regional integration that sustains the prosperity of the eastern coast, and the balance between the kingdom and the league is a testament to the capacity of the peoples of the basin to manage their differences for the common good. The Thessaran Basin is thus both a region and an institution, a fertile country whose wealth is matched only by the sophistication of the politics that governs it.
