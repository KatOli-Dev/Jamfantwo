## Overview

The Plains of the Confederation are the vast grasslands that cover the central interior of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), a region of open horizons, deep skies, and the constant movement of the wind across the tall grasses. The plains are the territory of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), a union of settled agricultural communities and nomadic pastoralist kindreds that have found a common purpose in the governance of the grasslands. The plains are among the most productive regions of Aethelar in terms of livestock and grain, and the character of the land has shaped the peoples who inhabit it in ways that are visible in every aspect of their culture and their society.

## Geography and Climate

The Plains of the Confederation occupy a broad belt across the centre of Aethelar, stretching from the foothills of the eastern highlands to the river systems that drain toward the western ocean. The land is gently rolling, with broad, shallow valleys and low, rounded hills that break the horizon without ever obscuring it entirely. The soil is a deep, rich loam that is the product of thousands of years of grass growth and decay, and the fertility of the plains is such that the grasses grow as tall as a man in the summer, providing abundant grazing for the herds.

The climate of the plains is continental, with hot summers and cold winters, and the rainfall is moderate but reliable, falling chiefly in the spring and the autumn. The wind is a constant presence on the plains, and the grasses move in waves that are visible from a great distance, a sight that is familiar to every inhabitant of the region. The rivers that cross the plains are slow and meandering, their courses shifting over the centuries as the land is shaped by the movement of water and the growth of vegetation, and the valleys that they have carved are the locations of the most productive farmland and the most substantial settlements.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the Plains of the Confederation are of two types, reflecting the dual nature of the society that inhabits the region. The settled agricultural communities are concentrated along the rivers and in the areas where the soil is deepest and the rainfall most reliable, and these communities are organised into towns and villages that are connected by a network of roads and tracks. The city of [Sunmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sunmere) is the largest settlement of the plains and the seat of the council that governs the confederation, and its market is the focal point of the trade of the grasslands.

The nomadic communities of the plains are the centaur kindreds and the human pastoralists who follow the herds across the grasslands, moving their camps with the seasons and the availability of grazing. The nomadic population is as numerous as the settled population, and the two groups are connected by ties of trade, marriage, and mutual obligation that are the foundation of the confederation. The centaur kindreds are the most prominent of the nomadic peoples, and their knowledge of the grasslands, the herds, and the seasonal patterns of the land is respected by the settled communities.

The population of the Plains of the Confederation includes [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans), [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs), and [halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings), together with smaller communities of [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) and [lupines](/content/species/sapient/lupines). The halflings are found chiefly in the agricultural communities, where their skill in farming and their love of the settled life make them valued members of the towns and villages. The lupines are present in the more remote parts of the plains, where they maintain their own kindreds and their own traditions, coexisting with the other peoples of the confederation.

## Economy

The economy of the Plains of the Confederation is based on livestock and grain, the two products that the grasslands provide in abundance. The cattle and the sheep of the plains are among the finest in Aethelar, and the herds that graze across the grasslands are the source of the meat, the milk, the wool, and the leather that are the principal exports of the region. The livestock trade is the most significant element of the economy, and the cattle drives that move the herds from the grasslands to the markets of the surrounding regions are a feature of the life of the plains that has continued unchanged for centuries.

The grain of the plains is grown in the river valleys, where the soil is deepest and the water is most reliable, and the wheat, the barley, and the oats that are harvested from these fields are the foundation of the diet of the settled communities. The surplus grain is exported to the regions where the climate is less favourable for agriculture, and the trade in grain connects the plains to the Amber Vale, the Grey Coast, and the markets of the other regions of Aethelar.

## Culture and Governance

The culture of the Plains of the Confederation is shaped by the openness of the land and by the dual character of the society that inhabits it. The settled communities value the stability and the continuity that the land provides, while the nomadic kindreds value the freedom and the movement that the grasslands permit. The Plains Confederation is the institution that reconciles these two ways of life, and the council that governs the confederation includes representatives of both the settled and the nomadic communities, ensuring that the interests of both are respected in the decisions that affect the region.

## Significance

The Plains of the Confederation are the heartland of central Aethelar, a region of abundant natural wealth and a distinctive culture that has grown from the interaction of settled and nomadic peoples. The grasslands are among the most productive regions of the continent, and the livestock and the grain that they produce are the foundation of the prosperity of the confederation. The significance of the plains lies in the balance that they represent, the balance between the settled and the mobile, the cultivated and the wild, the enduring and the changing, a balance that is the defining feature of the region and of the people who call it home.
