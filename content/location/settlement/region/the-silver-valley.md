## A Mining Region in the Mountain Spine

The Silver Valley is a mining region of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) situated in the mountain spine of the continent, the great chain of peaks and ridges that divides the eastern and western halves of the land. The valley is a deep, narrow gorge that penetrates the heart of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range), and it takes its name from the silver deposits that are found in the rock of the surrounding peaks. The Silver Valley is among the most productive mining regions in Aethelar, and the silver that is extracted from its mines has been the foundation of the wealth of the region for centuries.

The valley is not a single political unit but a collection of mining settlements, each associated with a particular vein or deposit of ore. The settlements are connected by roads and tracks that follow the course of the valley, and the mining operations are regulated by a council of representatives from the principal mines and settlements. The region is governed under the authority of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), though the valley lies within the territory of Aethelar, a circumstance that has given rise to complex arrangements of jurisdiction and ownership.

## The Mountain Spine and the Geological Formations

The mountain spine of Aethelar is a chain of peaks and ridges that runs from the northern coast to the southern highlands, forming the watershed that divides the continent. The spine is composed of ancient rock that has been folded and faulted over ancient ages, and the formations that it contains are rich in mineral deposits. The silver deposits of the Silver Valley are among the richest in the spine, and the veins of ore that run through the rock have attracted miners and prospectors to the region for centuries.

The formations of rock of the Silver Valley are complex, with multiple layers of rock that have been exposed by the erosion of the valley walls. The silver ore is found in veins that run through the darker rock, and the veins can be traced for considerable distances through the peaks. The ore is of high quality, and the silver that is extracted from it is pure and malleable, prized by the silversmiths of the cities.

## The Mines and the Settlements of the Valley

The mines of the Silver Valley are the most extensive and productive in the mountain spine. The mines are driven into the rock of the valley walls, following the veins of ore as they descend into the depths of the peaks. The mines are ventilated by shafts that connect the workings to the surface, and the tunnels are supported by timber and stone to prevent collapse. The miners of the Silver Valley are among the most skilled in Aethelar, and the techniques they have developed for working the silver ore are the foundation of the prosperity of the region.

The settlements of the Silver Valley are small, compact communities that have grown around the mines. Each settlement is associated with a particular mine or group of mines, and the inhabitants are the miners and their families, together with the merchants and craftsmen who serve the mining population. The largest of the settlements is the town of Silverford, which serves as the administrative and commercial centre of the valley.

## The Extraction and Processing of the Silver Ore

The extraction of the silver ore is the first stage of the mining operation. The ore is removed from the veins by blasting and drilling, and it is transported to the surface through the tunnels of the mine. The ore is then crushed and ground to a fine powder, and the silver is separated from the rock by a process of smelting and refining that has been practised in the valley for centuries.

The smelters of the Silver Valley are situated in the lower part of the valley, where the furnaces are fuelled by charcoal brought from the forests of the lower slopes. The smelters operate throughout the year, and the silver that they produce is cast into ingots for transport to the cities. The refining of the silver is a skilled craft, and the refiners of the Silver Valley are among the most respected figures in the mining community.

## The Transport of the Silver and the Trade Routes

The silver that is produced in the Silver Valley is transported to the cities of Aethelar and beyond by a network of roads and tracks that connect the valley to the lowlands. The principal route is the Silver Road, a paved highway that descends from the valley to the plains of the interior, following the course of the river that flows through the valley. The silver is carried by pack trains of mules and horses, each animal laden with ingots of silver that are the product of the mines.

The trade routes that connect the Silver Valley to the wider world are the foundation of the commercial life of the region. The silver that passes along these routes is traded for the goods that the valley cannot produce for itself, including food, cloth, tools, and the luxuries that the mining communities require. The merchants of the Silver Valley are among the wealthiest merchants of the mountain spine, and the trade in silver is the foundation of their prosperity.

## The Governance of the Silver Valley

The governance of the Silver Valley is a complex matter, for the valley lies within the territory of Aethelar but the mining operations are conducted under the authority of the Stoneheim Holds, who have maintained a presence in the region since the earliest period of settlement. The arrangements that govern the mining operations and the administration of the valley are based on treaties and agreements that have been negotiated between the two powers over the centuries.

The council of the Silver Valley is the principal governing body of the region, composed of representatives of the principal mines and settlements. The council is responsible for the regulation of the mining operations, the maintenance of the roads and the infrastructure of the valley, and the resolution of disputes between the mining communities. The council meets at Silverford, and its decisions are binding on all the settlements of the valley.

## The People of the Silver Valley

The population of the Silver Valley is diverse, including [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans), [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), and a smaller number of [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) who are drawn by the mining and metalworking trades. The humans are the majority population, and they are engaged in the mining, processing, and transport of the silver ore. The dwarves are valued for their skill in mining and metalworking, and they hold positions of authority in the management of the mines. The gnomes are skilled craftsmen who work in the smelters and the workshops of the valley.

The culture of the Silver Valley is shaped by the mining industry that is the foundation of the economy of the region. The people of the valley are known for their hardiness and their skill, and the traditions of the mining communities are passed down through the generations. The festivals and celebrations of the valley are centred on the mining calendar, and the feast of the first silver of the season is the most important event of the year.

## The Silver Valley and the Economy of the Mountain Spine

The Silver Valley is the most productive mining region of the mountain spine of Aethelar, and the silver that is extracted from its mines is the foundation of the wealth of the region and a valued commodity in the markets of the known world. The valley is a region of considerable economic importance, and the trade in silver that passes through it connects the mountain spine to the cities of the lowlands and the ports of the coast. The mines and settlements of the Silver Valley are the heart of the mining industry of the Crown Range, and the people of the valley are the guardians of the traditions and the knowledge that have sustained the extraction of silver from the rock of the peaks for centuries.
