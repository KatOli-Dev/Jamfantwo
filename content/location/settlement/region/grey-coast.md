## The Western Seaboard of Aethelar

The Grey Coast is the western seaboard of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), a long stretch of shoreline facing the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean), defined by mist, cool climate, and the steady rhythms of the sea. The coast takes its name from the grey skies that overhang it for much of the year and from the pale stone of the cliffs rising from the ocean, and the name is used both by the inhabitants and by the chroniclers of the eastern kingdoms. The Grey Coast is the territory of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings), a loose confederation of port towns and fishing villages that have maintained independence through maritime skill and mutual co-operation.

## Pale Cliffs, Shingle Beaches, and Maritime Fog

The Grey Coast runs from the northern reaches of the continent, where the land meets the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), to the warmer latitudes of the south, where the climate grows milder and the influence of the Greyring Ocean diminishes. The coastline is varied, with high cliffs of pale limestone and sandstone alternating with long stretches of shingle beach and sheltered estuaries where the rivers of western Aethelar meet the sea. The offshore waters are shallow and rich with marine life, and the continental shelf extends for a considerable distance from the shore before dropping into the deeper waters of the Greyring Ocean.

The climate is cool and maritime, with mild winters and summers rarely hot, for prevailing winds from the ocean bring moisture and cool air throughout the year. Fog and mist, the defining features of the Grey Coast, are most common in spring and autumn, and the mariners have learned to navigate by the feel of currents and the sound of waves when visibility is poor. The rainfall is frequent but moderate, supporting hardy grasses and wind-shaped trees covering the coastal hills.

## Greycliff, Misthaven, and the Harbour Towns

The settlements are concentrated along the shoreline and in sheltered river valleys providing access to the interior. The largest is [Greycliff](/content/location/settlement/town/greycliff), serving as the principal port of the Grey Coast Holdings and the centre of maritime trade. Its harbour, protected by a natural breakwater of rock, can accommodate vessels of moderate size. Other towns, including [Misthaven](/content/location/settlement/town/misthaven) and [Saltreach](/content/location/settlement/town/saltreach), are smaller but no less established, each sustaining the fishing fleets and coastal trade of its own stretch of shoreline.

The population is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), hardy and accustomed to the demands of the sea. The mariners are known throughout Aethelar for their skill in handling the small, sturdy vessels suited to the Greyring Ocean. The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the offshore waters are occasional visitors to the harbours of the coast, and the relationship between the coastal humans and merfolk communities is one of mutual respect, for the merfolk know the waters as well as any mariner.

## Fishing, Whaling, and the Maritime Trade

The economy is built upon the sea. The fishing fleets harvest abundant stocks of cod, herring, and mackerel from the shallow waters of the continental shelf, and the fish are salted, dried, or smoked in processing houses lining the harbours of every town and village. The dried fish of the Grey Coast are an important export, traded inland to the markets of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) and the central kingdoms, and the quality of the preservation is such that the fish remain edible for months.

The maritime trade is a mixture of local coastal shipping and longer voyages to northern and southern ports of Aethelar. Merchants at Greycliff exchange dried fish, timber, whale products, and woollen cloth for the grain, wine, and manufactured goods the western shore does not produce for itself. Misthaven handles smaller cargoes in its fogbound harbour, while Saltreach supplies salt and preserved fish to coasting vessels. The region's boatyards chiefly build and repair the sturdy fishing craft required by the Greyring, leaving construction of great seagoing ships to the eastern yards.

The Grey Coast Holdings include among their members the principal ports and harbours of the western shore, stretching from the fishing villages of the north to the larger markets of the south. Each member is bound by the terms of the confederation to contribute to the common defence, the maintenance of navigation aids, and the relief of vessels in distress. The council of the Holdings meets at Greycliff twice a year, in the spring and the autumn, to review the state of the coast and to agree upon the contributions that each member shall make.

## Watch Stations, Signal Fires, and Shared Salvage

The Holdings maintain more than a political compact. Each member keeps a watch station, signal fire, and crew capable of putting out when another harbour reports a vessel in distress. Bells and horn patterns identify the direction of a wreck, allowing neighbouring crews to search adjoining stretches without waiting for orders from Greycliff. The cost is shared according to the size of each fleet, and disputes over salvage are judged only after survivors have been brought ashore. This obligation has done much to preserve the independence of the coast. No town could maintain lights, pilots, and rescue craft along the whole western seaboard, but together the settlements provide a chain of refuge from Saltreach to the northern headlands. Mariners recognise membership in the Holdings not by a common banner alone, but by the assurance that a signal seen through fog will be answered from the next harbour. The system of shared responsibility has been tested by storms and wreaks over many generations, and it has proven its worth in the number of lives saved and the continuity of coastal trade that it has made possible.

## Maritime Independence and Quiet Prosperity

The culture is shaped by the sea and the independence of living at the edge of the ocean. The people are reserved and self-reliant, respectful of the power of the sea and mindful of the dangers it presents. The Grey Coast Holdings are a loose association, each community governed by its own council or portmaster, functioning through negotiation and agreement rather than central authority. The Grey Coast is a region of quiet prosperity and steady purpose, its maritime traditions among the oldest in Aethelar, reaching back to the earliest days of human settlement on the continent.
