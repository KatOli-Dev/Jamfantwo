## Overview

The Southern Reach is the term given to the warm, dry territories that lie at the southern extremity of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the climate grows hotter and the land becomes more arid as the continent narrows toward its southernmost point. The region is a frontier of human and other settlement, a place where the cultivated landscapes of the central continent give way to the scrublands, the dry grasslands, and the rocky badlands that stretch to the southern coast. The Southern Reach is governed as a single administrative unit by the [Kingdom of the Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), a realm that has grown from a scattering of frontier settlements into a stable and self-sufficient nation.

## Geography and Climate

The Southern Reach occupies the southern third of the continent of Aethelar, a region that broadens from the narrow southern peninsula into the wider expanses of the dry interior. The landscape is varied, with broad plains of dry grassland, rolling hills covered in scrub and thorny vegetation, and extensive badlands where the soft sedimentary rock has been carved by the occasional rains into dramatic gorges and mesas. The rivers of the Southern Reach are seasonal, flowing only after the winter rains and drying to a chain of pools during the long, hot summer, and the settlements of the region are located along the courses of these watercourses or near the springs that emerge from the bedrock.

The climate of the Southern Reach is the driest in Aethelar, with hot summers and mild winters, and the rainfall is both scarce and unpredictable. The winter rains, when they come, can be intense, causing the dry riverbeds to flood with a sudden and dangerous ferocity that has caught many unwary travellers by surprise. The vegetation of the region is adapted to the aridity, with deep-rooted grasses, drought-resistant shrubs, and the hardy trees that are found only in the sheltered valleys and along the watercourses. The wildlife of the Southern Reach includes the herds of antelope, the lizards, the snakes, and the birds of prey that are characteristic of dry climates, and the ecosystem is finely balanced between the demands of the heat and the scarcity of water.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the Southern Reach are concentrated in the regions where water is reliable, and the towns and villages are small and widely scattered. The principal settlement of the region is the city of [Goldmeadow](/content/location/settlement/city/goldmeadow), which lies in the broad valley of the most reliable river in the Southern Reach, and the city is the seat of government for the kingdom and the centre of the trade that flows through the region. The town of [Dustford](/content/location/settlement/town/dustford) is the frontier settlement where the grasslands of the south meet the country beyond, and the outpost of [Dunesend](/content/location/settlement/outpost/dunesend) marks the southern limit of settled habitation.

The population of the Southern Reach is predominantly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), but the region is also home to significant communities of other peoples who have adapted to the conditions of the dry south. The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) of the southern grasslands are the most numerous of these, and the relationship between the settled human communities and the nomadic centaur kindreds is one of the defining features of the region. The [saurians](/content/species/sapient/saurians) are found in the warmest and most remote parts of the Southern Reach, where the climate suits their cold-blooded physiology, and the [goliaths](/content/species/sapient/goliaths) are present in the rocky highlands, where their strength and endurance are suited to the demanding conditions.

## Economy

The economy of the Southern Reach is based on pastoralism, mining, and the trade that flows between the central continent and the southern regions. The livestock of the Southern Reach, chiefly cattle, sheep, and goats, are the principal source of wealth, and the herds are moved between the seasonal grazing lands in patterns that have been established over many generations. The wool and the leather of the Southern Reach are traded northward to the markets of the central continent, and the dried meat and the rendered tallow are exported to regions where livestock is less abundant.

The mineral wealth of the Southern Reach is another significant element of the economy, and the copper, the tin, and the precious stones that are mined from the rocky uplands are a valued contribution to the trade of the kingdom. The mines of the Southern Reach are worked by the settled communities and by the goliath kindreds of the highlands, and the ores are smelted in the furnaces that are built near the mines, using the charcoal that is produced from the woodlands of the river valleys. The quality of the metals from the Southern Reach is recognised throughout Aethelar, and the copper and the tin are traded as far north as the Amber Vale and the Grey Coast.

## Significance

The Southern Reach is the frontier of the settled world in Aethelar, a region where the conditions of life are demanding and the margin between sufficiency and hardship is narrow. The people of the Southern Reach have adapted to the aridity and the isolation with a resilience that is characteristic of frontier communities, and the kingdom that they have built is a testament to the capacity of settled peoples to thrive in conditions that would defeat those who are less resourceful. The Southern Reach is a region of stark beauty and quiet purpose, and its significance lies in its role as the southern boundary of the known world of Aethelar, a land where the old certainties of the central continent give way to the different truths of the dry country.
