## A Hilly Region Rich in Iron Ore

The Iron Hills are a region of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) situated in the eastern foothills of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range), where the mountain spine gives way to the rolling lowlands of the interior. The hills take their name from the iron ore that is found in abundance in the rock beneath the soil, and the region has been a source of iron for the forges and smithies of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) for centuries. The Iron Hills are among the most productive iron-mining regions of the continent, and the metal that is extracted from their mines is the foundation of the industries of the eastern districts.

The Iron Hills are not a single range but a series of low ridges and rounded hills that extend across a broad area of the eastern foothills. The hills are covered with grass and heath, with stands of oak and birch in the valleys, and the landscape is one of open, rolling country punctuated by the workings of the mines and the settlements of the miners.

## The Rock of the Iron Hills

The rock of the Iron Hills is the foundation of the region's prosperity. The hills are composed of sedimentary rock that has been folded and faulted over ancient ages, and the iron ore is found in seams and nodules that run through the rock at accessible depths. The ore is of high quality, with a high iron content and low levels of the impurities that can make smelting difficult.

The iron ore of the hills is found in several different formations, each with its own characteristics. The most important of these formations is the vein of hematite that runs through the central ridge of the hills, a deposit of exceptional richness that has been worked for many centuries. The hematite of the Iron Hills is prized for the quality of the iron that can be smelted from it, and the metal produced from the ore is used for the forging of tools, weapons, and the components of machinery.

## The Mines and the Mining Settlements

The mines of the Iron Hills are the most extensive in the eastern foothills of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range). The mines are driven into the hillsides, following the seams of ore as they descend into the rock. The mines are ventilated by shafts that connect the workings to the surface, and the tunnels are supported by timber and stone to prevent collapse. The miners of the Iron Hills are among the most skilled in Aethelar, and the techniques they have developed for the extraction of the ore are the foundation of the prosperity of the region.

The mining settlements of the Iron Hills are small towns and villages that have grown around the mines. The largest of the settlements is the town of [Copper Gate](/content/location/settlement/city/copper-gate), which serves as the administrative and commercial centre of the region. The other settlements are smaller, each associated with a particular mine or group of mines. The settlements are connected by roads and tracks that follow the valleys and ridges of the hills.

## The Smelters and the Forges

The smelters of the Iron Hills are the sites where the ore extracted from the mines is processed to produce iron. The smelters are situated in the lower parts of the hills, where the furnaces are fuelled by charcoal brought from the forests of the lower slopes. The smelters operate throughout the year, and the iron that they produce is cast into ingots for transport to the forges and the markets of the lowlands.

The forges of the Iron Hills are the establishments where the iron is worked into the products that are the foundation of the region's trade. The forges are staffed by skilled smiths who produce tools, weapons, and the components of machinery that are required by the industries of the eastern districts. The ironwork of the Iron Hills is known for its strength and durability, and the products of the forges are in demand across Aethelar.

## The Transport of the Iron and the Trade Routes

The iron that is produced in the Iron Hills is transported to the markets of Aethelar by a network of roads and tracks that connect the region to the lowlands. The principal route is the Iron Road, a paved highway that descends from the hills to the plains of the interior, following the course of the river that flows through the region. The iron is carried by wagons drawn by teams of oxen, each wagon laden with the ingots that are the product of the smelters.

The trade routes that connect the Iron Hills to the wider world are the foundation of the commercial life of the region. The iron that passes along these routes is traded for the goods that the region cannot produce for itself, including food, cloth, and the luxuries that the mining communities require. The merchants of the Iron Hills are among the wealthiest merchants of the eastern foothills, and the trade in iron is the foundation of their prosperity.

## The People of the Iron Hills

The population of the Iron Hills is predominantly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a smaller number of [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) who are drawn by the mining and metalworking trades. The humans are the majority population, and they are engaged in the mining, smelting, and forging of the iron that is the principal product of the region. The dwarves are valued for their skill in mining and metalworking, and the dwarven smiths of the Iron Hills are among the most skilled in the eastern foothills.

The culture of the Iron Hills is shaped by the mining industry that is the foundation of the economy of the region. The people of the hills are known for their hardiness and their skill, and the traditions of the mining communities are passed down through the generations. The festivals and celebrations of the region are centred on the mining calendar, and the feast of the first iron of the season is the most important event of the year.

## The Governance of the Iron Hills

The governance of the Iron Hills is divided among the settlements and the mine owners of the region, with the council of Copper Gate holding the principal authority over the affairs of the hills. The council is responsible for the regulation of the mining operations, the maintenance of the roads and the infrastructure of the region, and the resolution of disputes between the mining communities. The council meets in Copper Gate, and its decisions are binding on the settlements of the hills.

The laws and customs of the Iron Hills are based on the traditions of the mining communities, and they reflect the conditions of life in the hills. The rights to the ore, the water, and the timber are regulated by the customs of the region, and the disputes that arise over these rights are resolved by the council and the elders of the settlements.

## The Iron Hills and the Industry of the Eastern Districts

The Iron Hills are the principal source of iron for the eastern districts of Aethelar, and the metal that is extracted from the hills and smelted in the furnaces of the region is the foundation of the industries of the lowlands. The mines, smelters, and forges of the Iron Hills are among the most productive in the continent, and the iron that they produce is used for the forging of the tools, weapons, and machinery that sustain the economy of the eastern districts. The region is a landscape of industry and enterprise, where the people have learned to extract the wealth of the rock and to shape it into the products that the world requires.
