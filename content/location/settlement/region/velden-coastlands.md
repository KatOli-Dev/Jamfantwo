## Overview

The [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) Coastlands are the coastal territories on both shores of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden), the narrow channel that connects the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) to the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) between the southeastern coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the northeastern tip of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The coastlands take their name from the strait, whose own name is drawn from the oldest charts of the region and is thought to derive from a word in an ancient coastal tongue meaning narrows or gate. The region is a meeting point of two continents and two oceans, and its people have built their livelihoods upon the steady flow of trade that passes through the channel and the difficult pilotage that the passage demands.

The coastlands are not a kingdom but a community of interest, defined by the geography of the strait and the commerce that it carries. The settlements on both shores are joined by the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact), the joint governance that regulates the pilotage, the tolls, and the maintenance of the navigation aids that keep the strait open to the trade of the world. The coastlands extend from the sheltered coves of the Aethelaran side to the steep and rocky Morvind shore, encompassing a stretch of shoreline that is varied in character but united in its dependence upon the strait.

## Geography and Climate

The coastlands are defined by the waters of the strait and the land that descends to meet them. On the Morvind side, the mountains come close to the shore, and the cliffs rise steeply from the water's edge, broken only by the occasional gully through which a stream descends from the heights above. The harbours on this shore are few and difficult of access, sheltered in narrow coves that are carved into the rock by centuries of wave and weather. On the Aethelaran side, the coast is gentler, a line of low cliffs and sheltered coves that provide anchorage for vessels waiting for the tide to turn, and the land rises gradually inland toward the forests and river valleys of the northeastern continent.

The climate of the coastlands is shaped by the meeting of two oceans, and the air above the strait is laden with moisture that rises from the warm waters of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and condenses against the cooler air from the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). Fog and mist are common, particularly in the spring and the autumn, and the mariners of the coastlands have learned to navigate by the feel of the current and the sound of the waves when visibility fails. The prevailing winds blow along the axis of the strait, funnelling between the two shores and rising to a gale during the storms of the winter months, when the passage becomes too dangerous for all but the most determined captains.

The tidal range within the strait is considerable, and the direction and strength of the current reverses with each tide, so that a vessel entering the channel at the wrong time may find itself unable to make headway against the flow. The waters of the Auric, warm and swift, press against the cooler waters of the Stormfall, producing eddies and tide-rips that test the skill of any mariner. The pilots who navigate these conditions are specialists, trained from youth in the particular requirements of the strait, and their services are sought by every captain who must make the passage with a cargo of value.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the coastlands are situated at the points where the shore offers shelter and where the trade concentrates. [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch) is the principal Morvind-side town, situated on the northern shore where the cliffs fall back to permit a deep, sheltered cove that serves as the harbour. [Southport](/content/location/settlement/town/southport) is the counterpart of Veldenwatch on the Aethelaran side, occupying a raised shelf of rock that overlooks a broad cove at the narrowest reach of the strait. [Caerhaven](/content/location/settlement/town/caerhaven) lies to the east of Southport, where the strait opens into the Auric Ocean, and its harbour is the staging point for vessels preparing to enter or leave the passage from the southern sea.

Beyond the three principal towns of the compact, the coastlands include a number of smaller settlements and outposts. [Hafnir](/content/location/settlement/city/hafnir) is a port to the north on the Morvind side, associated with the compact through its trade with the strait. [Torshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/torshavn) is a smaller settlement on the same coast, its harbour connected to the strait by the coastal roads and the overland routes that descend from the mountains. These settlements supply the strait towns with timber, fish, and the produce of the Morvind interior, and their prosperity is bound to the fortunes of the trade.

The population of the coastlands is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the strait identify more closely with the channel and its trade than with the inland nations that claim their allegiance. The communities on both shores share a common identity as the people of the strait, and their cooperation in the governance and defence of the passage has created a bond that transcends the political division between the two continents. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the Morvind interior maintain agents in the strait towns to manage the flow of metalwork through the channel, and their counting houses are among the most substantial buildings on the waterfront. A small community of [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) has established itself in the towns, and their workshops produce fine instruments and mechanisms that are valued by the mariners who pass through.

## Trade and the Compact

The trade that flows through the coastlands is the commerce of two oceans, and the goods that pass through the channel include the metalwork of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), the grain and the cloth of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the spices and the dyes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The tolls and the pilotage fees that are collected by the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) are the primary source of revenue for the member towns, and the fair and efficient collection of these tolls is the foundation of the coastlands' prosperity. The compact ensures that the tolls are levied uniformly and that the passage of vessels is not delayed or obstructed, for the trade that flows through the strait is vital to the economies of all three continents.

The pilotage service is the other great function of the coastlands, and the pilots of the strait are trained and regulated by the guilds of Veldenwatch and Southport. The pilots are drawn from both shores and from the families that have navigated the strait for generations, and their knowledge is a closely guarded inheritance, passed from parent to child and refined by each generation's experience. The training of a pilot begins in childhood, and the sons and daughters of the pilot families are taught the currents, the tides, and the hazards of the strait from the earliest age. The qualification of a pilot is determined by an examination that is conducted by the guild, and the examination is among the most rigorous in the known world, for the safety of the vessels and the commerce that passes through the strait depends upon the skill and the knowledge of the pilots.

The shipyards and chandleries of the coastlands are a significant part of the regional economy, and the towns are known for the rapid and reliable maintenance that the strait trade demands. The vessels that pass through the strait are often in need of repair after the difficult passage, and the shipwrights of Veldenwatch and Southport are skilled in the work that the commerce requires. The provisioners of Caerhaven supply fresh water, salted provisions, and replacement cordage and sailcloth to vessels that are continuing their voyage across the Auric, and the harbour district of the town is a centre of activity at all hours.

## The Life of the Strait

The rhythm of life in the coastlands is set by the tides and the seasons of trade. During the summer months, when the weather is most favourable and the passage is least hazardous, the harbours are filled with vessels awaiting their turn for pilotage, and the streets of the towns are crowded with the mariners and merchants who work the strait. During the winter, when the storms drive strong winds through the channel, the harbours serve as refuges for vessels that are unwilling to risk the passage, and the towns enter a quieter period in which the crews repair their ships and the merchants settle the accounts of the trading season.

The pilots of the strait maintain the navigation aids that line the channel, including the lighthouses on both shores and the buoys that mark the navigable passages through the most treacherous reaches. The lighthouses are among the oldest structures in the coastlands, and their keepers are a respected community within the strait towns. The buoys are reset each spring after the winter storms, and the work of positioning them is conducted by the pilotage guilds with the assistance of small, sturdy vessels built for the purpose. The cost of the navigation aids is shared between the member towns of the compact according to the revenue formula established by the founding treaty.

The defence of the coastlands is the shared responsibility of the compact, and the walls of Veldenwatch and Southport are maintained in good order against the possibility of attack by sea. The strait has been a point of strategic importance throughout its history, and the memory of past sieges is preserved in the traditions of the towns. The collective force that can be raised by the compact is modest in numbers but well-trained in the particular conditions of the strait, and the presence of the pilots on the walls is a deterrent that no invader can afford to ignore, for no enemy fleet can navigate the passage without their guidance.

## Significance

The Velden Coastlands are the human community of the most important waterway in the known world, and their prosperity and their skill are the foundation of the trade that connects the continents. The coastlands are not a single realm but a confederation of interests, bound together by the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact) and the shared dependence upon the strait. The settlements on both shores have developed a character that is distinct from the surrounding coast, shaped by the constant passage of vessels and the wealth that the trade brings, and the people of the coastlands are known throughout the known world for their seamanship, their instruments, and their tolerance of strangers.

The significance of the coastlands lies in their role as the guardians of the strait and the regulators of the trade that flows through it. The passage is the only route between two oceans, and the prosperity of all three continents depends upon the fair and efficient regulation that the compact provides. The coastlands are a symbol of the cooperation and the commerce that connect the peoples of the world through the waters they share, and their endurance is a testament to the power of shared interest to overcome the divisions of continent and kingdom.
