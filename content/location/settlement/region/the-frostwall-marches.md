## Overview

The Frostwall Marches are the northern borderlands of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the southern coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), a cold, rugged region that straddles the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and extends into the high latitudes of the north. The region is named for the Frostwall, the great escarpment that marks the boundary between the lowlands of the coast and the frozen interior of northern Aethelar, and for the marches, the border territories that have been contested and defended for as long as the chronicles record. The Frostwall Marches are governed by the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans), a confederation of human and dwarven communities that have learned to thrive in the demanding conditions of the far north.

## Geography and Climate

The Frostwall Marches encompass a wide variety of landscapes, from the northern lowlands of Aethelar, where the forests of pine and birch give way to the open tundra, to the southern coast of Morvind, where the mountains meet the Stormfall Sea. The Frostwall itself is a dramatic geological feature, a cliff of dark stone that rises several hundred feet above the lowlands, forming a natural barrier between the relatively habitable coastal regions and the frozen interior that lies beyond. The escarpment runs for several hundred miles across the northern part of Aethelar, and it is punctuated by the passes and the valleys that provide the only routes through the wall.

The climate of the Frostwall Marches is the coldest in the known world, with long, severe winters and short, cool summers that are barely sufficient for the cultivation of the hardy crops that sustain the population. The snow lies deep upon the ground for the majority of the year, and the rivers and the lakes are frozen for months at a time, providing a surface for travel that is used by the inhabitants as readily as the roads in the summer months. The Stormfall Sea that separates the two continents is a vigorous and dangerous body of water throughout the year, and the crossing between Aethelar and Morvind is attempted only by the most experienced mariners and only during the brief window of the summer.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the Frostwall Marches are small, hardy, and widely scattered, located in the sheltered valleys, along the coast, and at the passes that cross the Frostwall. The principal settlement on the Aethelar side of the marches is the town of [Torshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/torshavn), a port that lies at the foot of the Frostwall and serves as the primary link between the coastal communities and the interior. On the Morvind side, the town of [Vargvik](/content/location/settlement/town/vargvik) is the main settlement of the southern coast, a fishing and trading port that is connected to the dwarven holds of the interior by the mountain passes.

The population of the Frostwall Marches is a mixture of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) and [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), the two peoples having learned to coexist and to co-operate in the demanding conditions of the north. The human communities are concentrated on the coast and in the lowlands, where the fishing and the limited agriculture provide the foundation of their economy. The dwarven communities are found in the mountains and the highlands, where their forges and their mines are the source of the metal goods and the minerals that are the principal wealth of the marches. [Ursines](/content/species/sapient/ursines) are present in the remote valleys and the high passes, and the [giants](/content/species/sapient/giants) of the northern mountains are a rare but significant presence in the region.

## Economy

The economy of the Frostwall Marches is based on fishing, mining, and the limited agriculture that the short summers permit. The fishing fleets of Torshavn and Vargvik harvest the cod and the herring that are abundant in the cold waters of the Stormfall Sea, and the fish are dried and salted for export to the southern regions where fresh fish is less readily available. The whale and the seal are also hunted by the coastal communities, and the oil, the bone, and the hide that are obtained from these animals are valued products that are traded throughout the known world.

The mining of the Frostwall Marches is the most significant element of the economy, and the iron, the copper, and the silver that are extracted from the mountains of Morvind and the escarpment of the Frostwall are the foundation of the prosperity of the region. The dwarven smiths of the interior are among the finest metalworkers in the known world, and the weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects that are produced in their forges are sought after by the nobles and the merchants of the southern kingdoms. The trade that flows through the passes of the Frostwall and across the Stormfall Sea connects the northern economy to the markets of the central continent.

## Significance

The Frostwall Marches are the northern frontier of the known world, a region where the conditions of life are among the most demanding that any settled people have encountered and overcome. The human and dwarven communities of the marches have built a society that is adapted to the cold, the isolation, and the scarcity of the northern latitudes, and the confederation of the Frostwall Clans is a testament to the capacity of different peoples to co-operate in the face of shared challenges. The significance of the Frostwall Marches lies in their role as the gateway to the northern continent of Morvind and as a reminder that the known world extends into regions that demand resilience, resourcefulness, and the willingness to endure hardship.
