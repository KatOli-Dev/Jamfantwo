## Overview

The Grey Coast is the western seaboard of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), a long stretch of shoreline that faces the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) and is defined by the mist, the cool climate, and the steady rhythms of the sea. The coast takes its name from the grey skies that overhang it for much of the year and from the pale stone of the cliffs that rise from the ocean, and the name is used both by the inhabitants and by the chroniclers of the eastern kingdoms. The Grey Coast is the territory of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/government/local/grey-coast-holdings), a loose confederation of port towns and fishing villages that have maintained their independence through a combination of maritime skill and mutual co-operation.

## Geography and Climate

The Grey Coast runs from the northern reaches of the continent, where the land meets the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), to the warmer latitudes of the south, where the climate grows milder and the influence of the Greyring Ocean diminishes. The coastline is varied, with high cliffs of pale limestone and sandstone alternating with long stretches of shingle beach and the sheltered estuaries where the rivers of western Aethelar meet the sea. The offshore waters are shallow and rich with marine life, and the continental shelf extends for a considerable distance from the shore before dropping into the deeper waters of the Greyring Ocean.

The climate of the Grey Coast is cool and maritime, with mild winters and summers that are rarely hot, for the prevailing winds from the ocean bring moisture and cool air to the land throughout the year. The fog and the mist that are the defining features of the Grey Coast are most common in the spring and the autumn, and the mariners of the coast have learned to navigate by the feel of the currents and the sound of the waves when the visibility is poor. The rainfall is frequent but moderate, supporting the growth of the hardy grasses and the wind-shaped trees that cover the coastal hills, and the rivers that flow into the ocean are fed by the same reliable precipitation that waters the western slopes of Aethelar.

## Settlements and Population

The settlements of the Grey Coast are concentrated along the shoreline and in the sheltered river valleys that provide access to the interior. The largest of these settlements is the town of [Greycliff](/content/location/settlement/town/greycliff), which serves as the principal port of the Grey Coast Holdings and the centre of the maritime trade of the region. The other towns of the coast, including [Caerhaven](/content/location/settlement/town/caerhaven) and [Misthaven](/content/location/settlement/town/misthaven), are smaller but no less prosperous, each serving as a centre for the fishing fleets and the coastal trade of its own stretch of shoreline.

The population of the Grey Coast is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), for the conditions of the coast have shaped a people who are hardy, resourceful, and accustomed to the demands of the sea. The mariners of the Grey Coast are known throughout Aethelar for their skill in handling the small, sturdy vessels that are suited to the conditions of the Greyring Ocean, and the fishing communities are among the most knowledgeable of the ways of the sea. The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the offshore waters are occasional visitors to the harbours of the coast, and the relationship between the coastal humans and the merfolk communities is one of mutual respect, for the merfolk know the waters of the coast as well as any mariner.

## Economy

The economy of the Grey Coast is built upon the sea, and the fishing and the maritime trade are the foundations of the prosperity of the region. The fishing fleets of the Grey Coast harvest the abundant stocks of cod, herring, and mackerel that are found in the shallow waters of the continental shelf, and the fish are salted, dried, or smoked in the processing houses that line the harbours of every town and village. The dried fish of the Grey Coast are an important export, traded inland to the markets of the Amber Vale and the central kingdoms, and the quality of the preservation is such that the fish remain edible for months, a vital consideration for the long journeys to the interior.

The maritime trade of the Grey Coast is a mixture of local coastal shipping and the longer voyages to the northern and southern ports of Aethelar. The merchants of Greycliff and Caerhaven trade with the ports of the Stormfall Sea to the north and with the warmer harbours of the south, carrying the dried fish, the timber, and the woollen cloth of the coast to other regions and returning with the grain, the wine, and the manufactured goods that the Grey Coast does not produce for itself. The shipyards of the Grey Coast are noted for the quality of their work, and the vessels that are built in the yards of Caerhaven are respected throughout the known world for their seaworthiness and their durability.

## Culture and Significance

The culture of the Grey Coast is shaped by the sea and by the independence that comes from living at the edge of the ocean. The people of the coast are reserved and self-reliant, respectful of the power of the sea and mindful of the dangers that it presents. The Grey Coast Holdings are a loose association of communities, each governed by its own council or portmaster, and the confederation functions through negotiation and agreement rather than through the imposition of central authority. The Grey Coast is a region of quiet prosperity and steady purpose, and its maritime traditions are among the oldest in Aethelar, reaching back to the earliest days of human settlement on the continent.
