## The Fertile Lowlands of the Eastern Aethelaran Interior

The Thessaran Basin is the broad lowland and valley country of the eastern interior of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the region of fertile plains, slow rivers, and gentle hills drained by the great rivers flowing east to the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The basin takes its name from the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league), the confederation of eastern coast port cities that has governed the maritime terminus of these rivers for many centuries, and the name is applied to the entire inland country whose commerce finds its outlet through the league's harbours. The Thessaran Basin is the agricultural heartland of the eastern coast, and the grain, wool, timber, and other products of its fields and forests sustain the cities of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and the trade of the wider world.

The basin is not a single realm but a region of overlapping jurisdictions, including the territories of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) in the centre and south, the holdings of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) at the coast, and the interior kingdoms that look to the eastern rivers for their outlet to the sea. The political complexity is matched by the economic unity of the region, for the rivers carry the goods of the interior to the league's ports and bring ocean goods to the interior markets.

## Gentle Hills, Slow Rivers, and the Reliable Rainfall

The basin occupies the eastern interior of the continent, stretching from the foothills of the mountain spine in the west to the coastal lowlands and harbours of the eastern shore. The land is gently rolling, with broad valleys and low, rounded hills. The rivers draining the basin are slow and meandering in their lower courses, fed by tributaries descending from the foothills, and their floodplains are among the most fertile in [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The soil is a deep, dark loam renewed by centuries of cultivation and annual flooding.

The climate is mild and temperate, with warm summers and cool winters moderated by the influence of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The prevailing winds from the east bring moisture, and rainfall is reliable throughout the growing season. The fogs rising from the rivers in autumn are a familiar feature, and the morning mists clinging to the valleys in late summer signal the coming harvest. The rivers are the principal feature of the regional geography, their courses having shaped the distribution of population and the pattern of trade. The largest is navigable for much of its length, and the barges carrying goods between interior and coast are a common sight during the navigable season.

## Eldreth, Caerwyn, and the Market Towns of the Interior

The agricultural communities are concentrated along rivers and in areas where the soil is deepest and rainfall most reliable, organised into towns and villages connected by a network of roads. The market towns of the interior serve as gathering points for agricultural produce. The cities of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) are the maritime outlets of the basin. [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) is the largest and most influential, a wealthy port whose harbour is among the finest on the eastern coast. [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn) is the principal shipyard of the league, renowned for the vessels built in its yards for the Auric trade. [Thornhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/thornhaven) commands the upper reaches of the principal river and the mountain passes connecting the basin to the interior highlands. [Sallowmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sallowmere) is the southernmost member, its trade with the warm plains a valued component of the league's commerce.

The population is the most diverse and numerous of any region of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). [Humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) are the majority. [Elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) are present in the more wooded portions, particularly in river valleys where ancient forests have been preserved. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) are found in the foothills and mining settlements. [Gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) inhabit the transitional country between fields and forests, their workshops producing finer instruments and mechanisms. [Halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings) are present in the agricultural communities.

## Wheat, Wool, and the Barges of the River Routes

[Agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) is the foundation of the regional economy. Wheat and barley of the basin are among the finest in Aethelar, and the flour milled from them is exported to coastal cities and other regions. The wool of the basin sheep is woven into cloth valued in eastern ports, and the linen produced from flax is exported beyond the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). Orchards and vineyards produce fruit and wine in abundance.

The rivers are the arteries of commerce. Barges travel them carrying agricultural produce to the harbours of the league. The return trade brings manufactured goods, spices from [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), and metalwork from [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) to the inland towns. Tolls collected at locks and weirs along the rivers are a significant source of revenue for inland communities.

## The League Council and the Maritime Outlets

The cities of the league are the commercial heart of the basin. The league is governed by a council of representatives from each member city, meeting regularly to coordinate trade, defence, and navigation. The council has the power to levy contributions, negotiate treaties, and dispatch a collective fleet divided between coast patrol and ocean squadron. The relationship between the league and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is one of cooperation and occasional tension, for the capital of the kingdom is also the largest member of the league.

## Harvest Festivals and Fragmented Governance

The culture is shaped by the fertility of the land and the prosperity flowing from it. The harvest festivals observed in every village are the most important occasions of the year, and the songs and dances of the harvest are distinctive to the basin. The governance of the basin is fragmented, with the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) administering central and southern portions, the league governing the coastal cities and maritime trade, and smaller polities managing their own affairs through their own councils.

## The Agricultural and Commercial Heartland of the Eastern Coast

The Thessaran Basin is the agricultural and commercial heartland of the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The grain and wool produced in the basin feed and clothe the coastal cities and sustain populations of the wider continent. The rivers draining the basin are the arteries of its commerce, and the harbours of the league are the outlet through which the goods of the interior reach the markets of the world. The cooperation between the league cities and the agricultural communities of the interior is a model of regional integration that sustains the prosperity of the eastern coast.
