## Overview

The Vintner's Vale lies between the southern flank of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the northern reach of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands), where the Greywash and its tributaries descend through a broad, sheltered trough that opens gradually toward the warm coast. The vale takes its name from the vineyards and the orchards that the valley floor and the lower slopes support, and the vale has been the principal source of the wines of the cold north since the [Era of the Founding Cities](/content/history/era-of-the-founding-cities). The vale is bounded on the north by the forests of the uplands, on the south by the foothills of the Stonespire, on the east by the headwaters of the Greywash, and on the west by the gorge through which the river descends to the plains.

The vale covers several days of travel from end to end, and the vale is the home of a string of small towns and villages that have grown up along the river and the principal road. The towns and villages are connected by the paved road that the [Building of the Stone Roads](/content/history/building-of-the-stone-roads) constructed, and the road is one of the most reliable of the roads of the cold north. The chronicler notes that the vale is held in particular esteem by the merchants of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and the merchants travel the road in every season to purchase the wines and the fruits that the vale produces.

## Climate

The vale enjoys a milder climate than the surrounding uplands, for the southern flank of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) shelters the vale from the worst of the northern winds, and the foothills of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) shelter the vale from the southern storms. The winters are cold but rarely severe, and the summers are long and warm. The growing season is sufficient for the grape and for the stone fruits that the orchards produce, and the vale has been famous for the quality of its wines and its fruits since the founding of the towns.

The vale is subject to the occasional flood when the snow of the Stonespire melts rapidly in the spring, and the floods have been the source of much of the hardship of the vale's history. The chronicler notes that the floods are recorded in the chronicles of the towns as exceptional events, and the chronicles describe the damage that the floods inflict and the labour that the towns must undertake to repair the damage. The chronicler observes that the floods have been the foundation of the careful management of the river that the [Treaty of the Greywash](/content/history/treaty-of-the-greywash) established.

## The Vineyards

The vineyards of the vale are planted upon the lower slopes of the hills that flank the valley floor, where the soil is well-drained and the sun reaches the vines for the greater part of the day. The principal grape is a dark-skinned variety that the founders of the towns brought from the warm coast in the generations of the founding, and the grape has been adapted to the cooler climate by generations of careful cultivation. The wine that the grape produces is deep-coloured, full-bodied, and well-suited to the long winters of the cold north.

The vineyards are tended by the vintner-houses of the towns, and the vintner-houses are organised under the [Council of Vintners and Cellarers](/content/government/local/council-of-vintners-and-cellarers). The Council maintains the standards of the wine, and the standards are the foundation of the reputation of the wines of the vale. The chronicler notes that the vineyards have been the source of much of the prosperity of the towns, and the prosperity is the measure of the public spirit of the vintner-houses.

## The Orchards

The orchards of the vale are planted upon the valley floor and the lower slopes, where the soil is deep and the water is plentiful. The principal fruits are the apple, the pear, the plum, and the cherry, and the fruits are the staple of the diet of the towns and the villages of the vale. The orchards are tended by the farming households of the towns, and the households have cultivated the fruits for several generations. The chronicler notes that the orchards are the foundation of the food supply of the cold north, and the orchards are held in high esteem by the magistrates and the merchants of every city of the cold north.

The orchards are subject to the occasional failure of the blossom when a late frost kills the flowers before they can set, and the failures are recorded in the chronicles of the towns as exceptional events. The chronicler notes that the failures are rare and that the orchards recover quickly, and the recovery is the measure of the resilience of the farming households of the vale.

## The Towns

The towns of the vale are strung along the river and the principal road, and each town is the centre of a small district of vineyards and orchards. The principal towns are Greenwell at the head of the vale, Vintner's Bridge at the centre, and Orchardmouth at the western end. Each town has a small magistracy, a small temple, a small market, and a small garrison. The chronicler notes that the towns are quiet and functional, and the towns are the foundation of the public order of the vale.

The towns are governed by the magistrates in consultation with the vintner-houses and the farming households, and the magistrates maintain the roads, the bridges, the irrigation works, and the public order of the vale. The magistrates are appointed by the council of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and the magistrates hold office at the pleasure of the council. The chronicler notes that the magistrates are held in high esteem by the towns and the villages, and the magistrates mark the anniversaries of the founding with appropriate ceremonies.

## The Wildlife

The vale is the home of a variety of wildlife that is adapted to the milder climate of the sheltered trough. The hills support deer and wild pig, and the woods support smaller game that the hunters of the towns pursue in the season. The river supports trout and grayling, and the fishermen of the towns are licensed by the [Forest Fellowship of the Heartwood](/content/government/local/forest-fellowship-of-the-heartwood) to fish the waters of the river and its tributaries. The chronicler notes that the wildlife is the source of much of the food of the towns, and the wildlife is the foundation of the careful management of the chase that the Fellowship maintains.

## Significance

The Vintner's Vale is one of the most productive and most esteemed of the regions of the cold north, and the vale is the foundation of the wine trade and the fruit supply of the [Council of Vintners and Cellarers](/content/government/local/council-of-vintners-and-cellarers) for several centuries. The vineyards, the orchards, the towns, and the road are the inheritance that the vale has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that the vale is held in particular memory by the merchants of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and the merchants travel the road of the vale in every season to purchase the wines and the fruits that the vale produces. The vale is one of the quiet foundations of the prosperity of the cold north, and the vale is held in high esteem by every magistrate and every merchant of the cold north.
