## Overview

The River Vael is the greatest waterway of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), a long and generous river that rises among the western foothills of the central mountain spine and flows eastward across the temperate heartland before emptying into the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) at the port of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth). The river drains a substantial portion of the continent's interior, carries the silt that has built the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) into one of the most fertile regions in the known world, and serves as the principal highway of the kingdom that takes its name from it. The Vael is at once a feature of geography and a foundation of polity, for the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) has grown up along its banks over many generations, and the laws, the customs, and the daily labour of the river communities remain bound to its waters.

The chronicler observes that the Vael is unusual among the great rivers of the known world for the steadiness of its flow and the breadth of its navigable reach. Few rivers of comparable length offer so long a stretch of calm water to laden vessels, and the kingdom that has grown up along the Vael has been able to extend its commerce and its influence further into the interior than the kingdoms founded upon shorter or swifter streams. The river is also a source of fish, of irrigation, and of the boundary lines that have shaped the territorial arrangement of the valley, and the Vael is woven into the identity of the kingdom in a manner that no other feature of the landscape quite matches.

## Course and Character

The Vael rises in the western foothills of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) mountain spine, where the snowmelt and the rains of the high country gather into a number of streams that converge in the upper valleys. The upper course is swift and narrow, passing through wooded country where the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the foothills have long maintained fords and small ferries for the metal trade that descends from the mountain workshops. The river gathers volume as it enters the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale), and its character changes from a mountain stream to a broad, slow river that meanders across the floodplain in long, gentle bends.

Within the vale, the river is flanked by low wooded hills and by the fields that have been cultivated along its banks for as many generations as the chronicles record. The Vael is at its most generous in the middle course, where the water is deep enough for barges and the current is slow enough for laden vessels to make their way upstream under sail or by haulage from the bank. The trade of the kingdom depends upon this reach, and the customs of the riverside communities have evolved around the long-distance haulage of grain, timber, and the other goods of the valley to the harbour at [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth). The chronicler notes that the Vael is at its most picturesque in this middle reach, where the broad water, the willows, and the distant white bridges give the journey a quality that the river poets have celebrated for centuries.

The lower course of the Vael passes through the wider lowlands east of the Amber Vale, and the river broadens as it approaches the coast. The current slows further, and the channels divide around low islands and sandbanks that have accumulated over the centuries. The mouth of the river forms the harbour of Vaelmouth, where the water spreads wide and meets the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) in a broad estuary. The estuary is shallow and the sandbanks shift with the season, and the pilots of Vaelmouth maintain a careful watch upon the channels that lead from the open water to the quays. The river is tidal in its lowest reaches, and the salt water pushes upstream for several miles, marking the meeting of the fresh and the salt in a manner familiar to all of the river communities.

## The Annual Flood and the Fertility of the Vale

The annual flood is the most important natural event in the life of the Vael valley, and the rhythm of the agricultural year is set by the rise and the fall of the river. The flood begins in the late spring, when the snowmelt from the high country swells the upper tributaries, and the rise of the water moves downstream over a period of several weeks. The floodwaters spill over the low banks of the river and spread across the fields of the floodplain, depositing a layer of fine silt that renews the fertility of the soil. The farmers of the valley have learned to manage the flood through a system of dykes and channels that control the distribution of the water, and the irrigated fields that line the river produce some of the highest yields in [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar).

The chronicler records that the flood is not regarded by the valley communities as a danger but as a gift, and the customary law of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale) gives the maintenance of the dykes, the channels, and the bridges precedence over every private claim. Each village maintains an assigned reach of embankment, and the market towns keep stores of timber, stone, and grain against a season in which the river may rise beyond expectation. The Crown of [Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) sends inspectors before the winter rains, but the inspectors work beside wardens chosen locally, for the knowledge of the old channels and the weak banks is held by the families that have tended them for generations. The water law of the valley is among the oldest of the kingdom's institutions, and it remains the foundation of the agricultural prosperity of the realm.

The flood is also a hazard when it is excessive, and the chronicles of the valley record several occasions upon which the river has burst its banks and destroyed entire villages. The flood of the third year of the reign of [Queen Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) was the most severe of recorded history, and the queen is remembered for her personal supervision of the repair of the embankments in the year that followed. The chronicler notes that the memory of this flood is preserved in the customary law of the valley, which requires that each household set aside a portion of the harvest in a common store against the recurrence of such a calamity, and that the store is administered by the village elder under the oversight of the royal governor.

## Trade and Navigation

The trade of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) is built upon the navigation of the river, and the barges that travel its waters are the foundation of the kingdom's commercial power. The vessels of the Vael are of several kinds, suited to the different reaches of the river: the small boats of the upper course that are hauled by trackers along the bank, the broad barges of the middle course that carry grain and timber downstream under sail or by current, and the sea-going vessels of the lower course that transfer cargo between the river and the ocean at Vaelmouth. The boatbuilders of the river communities are the heirs of a long tradition, and the boats of the Vael are noted throughout [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) for their seaworthiness and their capacity.

The customs of the river trade are governed by the water law of the valley, and the regulations that govern the passage of vessels, the loading of cargo, and the use of the quays and the bridges are enforced by officers of the Crown. The pilotage of the harbour at Vaelmouth is a separate and more specialised service, for the shifting sandbanks of the estuary require a knowledge that is acquired only through long apprenticeship. The pilots of Vaelmouth are licensed by the harbour authority, and their fees are a significant source of revenue for the city. The chronicler records that the pilots of Vaelmouth are among the most skilled on the eastern coast, and that their services are sought by the captains of vessels bound for the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) alike.

The bridges of the Vael are among the most substantial works of the kingdom, and the oldest of them is a stone arch that spans the river in three elegant spans at Vaelmouth, built by dwarven engineers in the golden age of the kingdom. The bridges are raised at Vaelmouth to allow the passage of masted vessels, and the opening and the closing of the bridges is regulated by the harbour authority with a careful eye to the flow of commerce. The bridges of the upper river are simpler, and the fords of the mountain reaches are maintained by the riverside villages under the customary law of the water. The chronicler notes that the bridges of the Vael are the symbols of the unity of the kingdom, and that their construction and maintenance is regarded by the river communities as a duty that is owed to the river itself.

## Fish, Waterfowl, and River Life

The Vael is a rich fishery, and the eel traps, the salmon nets, and the other devices of the riverside communities provide a supplementary food supply that is valued in the local markets. The salmon run is the most celebrated event of the river year, and the fish are taken in the late spring and early summer as they ascend the river to spawn. The eel traps are maintained in the lower reaches, where the eels are caught in the autumn as they descend toward the sea. The fish of the Vael are exported to the other settlements of the kingdom and to the markets of the eastern coast, and the riverside villages that depend upon the fishery have developed a body of customary law that governs the timing and the methods of the catch.

The waterfowl of the river are abundant, and the ponds and the back-channels of the floodplain support herons, ducks, and the long-legged waders that feed upon the shallows. The fishers of the river are familiar with the waterfowl, and the migration patterns of the birds are used as a calendar of the seasons. The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the lower river and the estuary are a familiar presence in the harbour of Vaelmouth, and the chronicler records that the merfolk of the Vael have a particular attachment to the river and a willingness to assist the pilots in difficult conditions that is not always found among the merfolk of other waterways.

The riparian woodland of the Vael is among the most beautiful in [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), with willows, alders, and poplars lining the banks and providing a habitat for a variety of small mammals and nesting birds. The woodland is protected by the customary law of the valley, and the cutting of timber from the riverside is regulated to ensure that the banks are not destabilised and the shade that the trees provide is preserved. The chronicler records that the riverside woodland is a refuge for the traveller and a source of small timber for the boatbuilders, and that the customary law that governs it is among the most carefully observed of the kingdom's institutions.

## Significance

The River Vael is the foundation of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) and the principal feature of the geography of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). Its waters have shaped the agriculture, the trade, the law, and the identity of the kingdoms and the communities that have grown up along its banks, and the river is the central fact of the daily life of the valley. The grain that is exported from the Vael valley feeds the cities of the eastern coast and the islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and the prosperity of the kingdom is built upon the fertility that the river's annual flood renews. The chronicler finds in the Vael a river that has been a faithful partner to the peoples who have settled upon its banks, and whose generous waters have made possible a kingdom that endures.
