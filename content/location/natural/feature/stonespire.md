## The Peak that Gives the Pass Its Name

The higher Stonespire is the great peak of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands) that gives its name to the [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass) at whose head it stands, and the peak is the most prominent of the summits that flank the principal overland route between the cold north and the warm coast. The Stonespire rises from a saddle in the range where the highlands narrow between two of the highest crests, and its snow-streaked flanks are visible from the foothills of the warm coast on a clear day and from the upper valleys of the cold north throughout the brightest weeks of the summer. The chronicler of the highland cartographers has set down that the higher Stonespire is the single dominant peak of the central Stoneheim, and that the lesser summits that surround it are known to the highland shepherds and the dwarven road-wardens by their own names but are not regarded as rivals to the great peak itself.

The name of the peak is older than the present kingdoms of the cold north, and the name is preserved in the chronicles of the academies of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) and in the older inscriptions that the highland road-wardens maintain at the principal crossings. The chronicler of the highland records has recorded that the name was applied to the peak by the earliest of the dwarven chroniclers, and that the name has been retained in every subsequent record. The chronicler notes that the highland communities apply the name to the single dominant peak and not to the surrounding summits, and that the lesser peaks are known by the names of the local features - the Sentinel to the east and the Watcher to the west - rather than by the great name of the Stonespire.

## The Shape of the Peak

The higher Stonespire is a single, sharp summit of dark stone, with a southern face that drops in a long and unbroken cliff toward the foothills of the warm coast, and a northern face that is more broken but equally steep. The peak rises above the line of perpetual snow, and the snowfields that lie upon its flanks feed the streams that descend in two directions - southward to the warm coast and northward to the headwaters of the rivers of the cold north. The chronicler of the highland cartographers has set down that the shape of the peak is unlike any of the other summits of the Stoneheim, and that a traveller who has seen the Stonespire once will recognise it from any of the lower ridges that look upon it.

The flanks of the peak support the hardy vegetation that the climate permits. The lower slopes are covered by the conifers that the [Reading the Ground](/content/belief/science/physical/reading-the-ground) has catalogued with care, and the higher slopes support only the lichens and the mosses that the cold allows. The peak is the home of the smaller mammals that the birds of prey feed upon, and the peak is the home of the wild goats that the hunters of the holds pursue in the season. The chronicler of the highland records has recorded that the peak has been the subject of careful survey by the pilots of the [Distance-Craft](/content/belief/science/physical/distance-craft), and the surveys have established the height of the Stonespire with a precision that the chronicler has noted as one of the more remarkable achievements of the highland road-builders.

## The Pass and the Peak

The [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass) lies at the head of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands), in the saddle between the higher Stonespire to the north and the lesser peak that the locals call the Sentinel to the south. The pass is broad enough to accommodate the road and the small streams that run off the slopes to either side, and the pass has been the site of the road that the [Laying the Stone Roads](/content/history/laying-the-stone-roads) constructed. The chronicler of the highland road-builders has recorded that the road is paved with the hard stone of the surrounding peaks, and that the road is one of the most reliable of the roads of the cold north.

The road descends from the pass to the foothills of the warm coast in a long, winding course that the engineers of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) cut into the rock with care. The engineer who oversaw the design and the construction of the road was [Bron, Engineer of Stoneheim](/content/people/historical/engineer-bron-of-stoneheim), the master engineer of the road-building programme whose work is the foundation of the land trade between the cold north and the warm coast. The chronicler of the dwarven records has set down that Bron's road across the pass is the most remarkable of the works of the road-building programme, and that the road has been the foundation of the land trade for several generations.

## The Engineering of the Stone Roads

The Stonespire was the principal challenge of the [Laying the Stone Roads](/content/history/laying-the-stone-roads), and the road that crosses the pass is among the most admired of the works of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace). The road was built upon a foundation of large stones that were set into a trench, and the foundation was covered with a layer of smaller stones that were packed tight. The surface was finished with a layer of gravel that was rolled smooth, and the surface was crowned slightly so that the water would run off to the ditches that were cut along the sides. The chronicler of the road-builders has recorded that the engineering was adapted to the terrain, and the engineers of the Stoneheim faced challenges that the engineers of the warm coast did not.

The engineers of the Stoneheim cut the road into the rock of the steepest sections, and the engineers built the retaining walls that hold the road in place upon the steeper slopes. The cuttings on the southern face of the pass are the most remarkable of the works, and the cuttings are the sections of the road that the chronicler of the highland road-builders has cited as the foundation of the dwarven reputation for engineering. The cuttings were cut by the dwarven engineers of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) over the course of more than twenty years, and the cuttings are maintained to the present day by the road wardens of the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch).

## The Watchtowers and the Quarters

The watchtowers of the pass were erected by the magistrates of the towns of the cold north in the early generations of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace), and the watchtowers have been maintained by the [Northern Roads Watch](/content/government/local/northern-roads-watch) ever since. The watchtowers stand at the northern and southern ends of the pass, and the watchtowers are the refuge of the travellers who are caught upon the road in time of storm. The garrisons of the watchtowers consist of a sergeant and four men, and the garrisons hold the towers for a tour of two months. The chronicler of the Standing Watch has recorded that the watchtowers are the foundation of the safety of the pass, and the watchtowers are held in high esteem by the merchants and the travellers of the road.

The quarters of the pass are small buildings of stone that provide shelter for the travellers who are caught upon the road in time of storm, and the quarters are the home of the small garrisons that maintain the watchtowers. The quarters are furnished with the supplies that the garrisons require for the tour, and the supplies are delivered by the magistrates of the towns of both regions at the beginning of every season. The chronicler has set down that the quarters are the foundation of the safety of the pass, and the quarters are held in esteem by the merchants and the travellers of the road.

## The Stoneheim Holds and the Engineers

The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) is the great dwarven realm of the interior, and the realm is the principal political entity of the cold north. The realm encompasses the underground cities, the mining settlements, and the mountain territories that are inhabited and governed by the dwarven people of the continent, and the engineers of the realm are the most skilled of any of the engineers of the cold north. The chronicler of the dwarven records has set down that the engineers of the holds have been the foundation of the road-building programme since the [Founding Era](/content/history/founding-era), and the engineers of the holds are held in particular esteem for the quality of their work upon the Stonespire Pass.

The [Bron, Engineer of Stoneheim](/content/people/historical/engineer-bron-of-stoneheim) was born into a household of miners and engineers who had served the holds for several generations, and Bron was trained as an engineer in the workshops of the senior holds before being appointed to the position of chief engineer of the road-building programme. The road that Bron designed and oversaw was the work of more than twenty years, and the road was opened to traffic in the middle of Bron's service to the programme. The chronicler of the highland records has set down that Bron's road across the Stonespire is the most remarkable of the works of the road-building programme, and the road is the foundation of the land trade of the cold north for several generations.

## The Wildlife and the Streams

The Stonespire is the home of a rich variety of wildlife, and the wildlife is the foundation of the ecology of the peak. The slopes support the smaller mammals that the birds of prey feed upon, and the slopes support the wild goats that the hunters of the holds pursue in the season. The streams that run off the slopes support the trout and the grayling that the fishermen of the holds pursue, and the streams are the source of the water that the quarters and the watchtowers require. The chronicler of the highland records has set down that the wildlife has been the subject of careful study by the scholars of the [Bestiaries and Herbals](/content/belief/science/physical/bestiaries-and-herbals), and the scholars have recorded the patterns of the mammals and the fish in their catalogues.

The streams that descend from the Stonespire are the source of several of the principal rivers of the cold north, and the streams are the foundation of the water supply of the towns of the upper valleys. The streams are carefully managed by the highland communities, and the management is a communal obligation that the customary law of the highland enforces. The chronicler of the highland records has set down that the streams are the source of the water that the towns require, and the towns mark the anniversaries of the founding of the customary law with appropriate ceremonies.

## Significance

The Stonespire is one of the most distinctive of the natural features of the [Stoneheim Highlands](/content/location/settlement/region/stoneheim-highlands), and the peak is the foundation of the engineering and the land trade of the cold north. The shape of the peak, the pass that bears its name, the road that crosses the pass, the watchtowers and the quarters of the Standing Watch, the engineers of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and the streams that descend from the flanks are the inheritance that the Stonespire has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler of the highland records has set down that the Stonespire is held in particular memory by the dwarves of the holds and by the merchants of the inland valleys, and the dwarves and the merchants mark the anniversaries of the founding of the road with appropriate ceremonies. The Stonespire endures, in the snow upon its flanks and in the road that crosses the pass at its head, as the living inheritance of those whose work and whose life are bound up with the highland road between the cold north and the warm coast.
