## The Southernmost Continent

Sahrana is the southernmost continent, lying across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) from [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). Much of its interior is covered by vast desert, punctuated by rocky uplands and seasonal river systems. The northern coast, washed by warm currents, supports several fertile regions that have given rise to prosperous and cultured city-states. Beyond the Kharesh, the remote southern region called the [Southern Lands](/content/location/natural/continent/southern-lands) contains temperate highlands and inland seas governed by the Southern Reach. Sahrana's distance from the northern continents has allowed distinct traditions to flourish, and its trade routes for rare [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) are among the most valuable in the known world. The continent is a land of contrasts, where great wealth and refinement coexist with some of the harshest conditions found anywhere.

## Fertile Coast, Foothills, and the Great Desert

The northern coast of Sahrana benefits from warm currents that flow northward along the continental shelf, granting the coastal regions a mild and agreeable climate. Fertile valleys and well-watered plains behind the coast support agriculture of great variety, and it is here that the wealthiest and most populous settlements are found. The coastal climate is warm and humid, with rains that come reliably in their season and sustain crops of rice, cotton, spices, and fruits prized throughout the known world. The rivers that water the coast descend from the interior highlands, and their floodplains are among the most productive lands on any continent.

Behind the coastal strip, the land rises in a series of steps toward the interior. The foothills are dry but fertile where water is available, supporting orchards and vineyards that produce some of the finest wines and oils known. As the land climbs, the vegetation thins and the soil becomes stony and arid, until the foothills give way to the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), the great desert that covers the bulk of the continent's interior. The transition from fertile coast to barren desert is gradual but unmistakable, and the caravans that travel between the two must prepare for a journey through some of the most demanding terrain in the world.

The [southern reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach)es of Sahrana are remote and difficult to access, lying beyond the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), but expeditions and the oral traditions of the desert peoples have confirmed the existence of fertile highlands and inland seas in the deep south. The far south is home to the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), a federation of settled communities that inhabit the shores of the inland seas and the surrounding temperate uplands. The Southern Reach is the most remote of the known political entities, and its trade with the northern world flows through the caravan routes of the Kharesh. Much of the Kharesh itself remains unmapped, and the nomadic peoples who traverse its dunes possess a knowledge of its paths and water sources that no outsider has been able to replicate.

## City-States of the Warm Shore

The city-states of the northern coast are among the oldest and most cultured settlements in the known world. Each is independent, governed by its own council or ruler, and each has developed a distinct character shaped by its particular geography and history. Some are ports oriented toward maritime trade across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), while others are inland cities that serve as termini for the caravan routes of the Kharesh. All are united by their dependence on trade, and the wealth that flows through them has funded [public works](/content/economy/public-works), libraries, temples, and gardens that are the envy of the northern continents.

The architecture of the coastal cities is noted for its elegance and its adaptation to the warm climate. Buildings are constructed of light-coloured stone that reflects the sun, with shaded courtyards, high windows, and ventilated passages that channel the breezes from the sea. Water is channelled through the cities by means of aqueducts and underground channels, and the presence of running water in fountains and gardens is both a practical necessity and a symbol of prosperity.

## Humans, Salawa, and the Desert Tribes

[Humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) are concentrated along the fertile northern coast, with smaller numbers in the desert interior where they trade and travel alongside the nomadic peoples of the Kharesh. The humans of Sahrana are distinct in appearance from their northern kin, being taller and more slender on average, with darker skin adapted to the strong sun. Their civilisation is ancient, and their traditions of scholarship, commerce, and art are among the most developed of any human population.

Small communities of [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) are present in the warm and humid northern coastal regions, where they have settled in wooded areas near the coast. These elves are the most distinct of all elven populations, adapted to a warmer climate than any of their kin, and their appearance and customs reflect the influence of their surroundings. They are few in number and seldom encountered by outsiders.

[Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) are rarely encountered in Sahrana, as the hot and low-lying terrain ill suits their constitution, though a few may be found as temporary visitors in the trading cities of the north. [Orcs](/content/species/sapient/orcs) are present in the arid interior beyond the Kharesh. [Goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins) are found in scattered pockets along the desert fringes and in the ruins of abandoned settlements.

The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) are the other major people of Sahrana. Their jackal-headed form and keen senses are well suited to the demands of the desert. The salawa are the primary nomadic people of the Kharesh, and their knowledge of the desert and its resources is the foundation of the caravan trade that connects the coast to the interior. The salawa are organised into the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), the confederation of nomadic peoples that governs the desert and its routes, and their cooperation with the settled cities of the coast is the foundation of the inland economy.

## Across the Erg to the Oases

The caravan routes of Sahrana are among the most valuable and the most demanding trade routes in the known world. The caravans that cross the Kharesh follow paths established over centuries and are navigated by experienced guides who know the desert and its water sources. The caravans carry spices, dyes, and precious stones of the interior to the coastal cities, returning with grain, cloth, and manufactured goods from the coast and the northern continents.

[Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari), the largest and most prosperous of the inland cities, is the primary hub of caravan commerce. The [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of inland caravan cities, coordinates the caravan routes and water rights. Cooperation between the pact and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) is the foundation of the inland economy. The oasis settlements of the deep desert, including [Shurra](/content/location/settlement/village/shurra), serve as waypoints that sustain the caravans, providing water and provisions vital for desert traffic.

## The Federation of the Deep South

The far south of Sahrana is home to the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), a federation of settled communities that inhabit the shores of the inland seas and the surrounding temperate uplands. The settlements of the Southern Reach, including [Ashaar](/content/location/settlement/town/ashaar) on the [Lesser Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/lesser-inland-sea) and [Vereth](/content/location/settlement/town/vereth) on the [Greater Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea), are centres of agriculture and fishing. Their products are traded across the inland seas and through the caravan routes to the north.

The Southern Reach is a self-sufficient region. The land around the inland seas is fertile and the waters are rich with fish, and the communities of the south have developed a way of life distinct from that of the northern world. Trade between the Southern Reach and the north is modest but valuable, with preserved fish, citrus fruits, and highland wool exchanged for manufactured goods and northern products.

## Spices, Caravans, and the Desert Crossings

Sahrana's trade routes are the source of many of the rarest and most valued goods in the known world, including spices, dyes, and precious stones found nowhere else. The city-states of the northern coast have grown wealthy on this trade, and their markets are among the most cosmopolitan anywhere, where merchants from every continent gather to exchange goods. The desert interior, though forbidding, is crossed by ancient caravan routes that connect the coast to scattered oases and upland settlements within the Kharesh.
