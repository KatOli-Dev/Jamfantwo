## Overview

The Auric Passage is the principal maritime trade route across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), connecting the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) to the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) in a crossing that has been the foundation of intercontinental commerce for longer than any written record can reliably attest. The passage runs from the great port of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) in the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), sweeping south-eastward across the open ocean before making landfall at the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast between [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra), a voyage of some three weeks in favourable conditions and considerably longer when the winds and the currents are less obliging. The passage is the busiest and the most valuable single trade route in the known world, carrying the spices, the dyes, and the precious stones of Sahrana northward and returning with the grain, the cloth, the iron, and the manufactured goods of Aethelar, and the volume of traffic that moves along it has shaped the economies, the cultures, and the political arrangements of every nation that borders the ocean.

## Geography and Navigation

The waters of the Auric Passage cross the central basin of the Auric Ocean, a deep and open expanse of sea that offers few landmarks and fewer havens for vessels in distress. The voyage is divided into three distinct legs, each with its own character and its own demands upon the mariner. The first leg follows the coast of Aethelar southward from Eldreth, keeping within sight of the shore for several days before the course turns eastward into the open ocean. This coastal leg is the most sheltered portion of the journey, and the villages and the watchtowers that line the Aethelaran shore provide a measure of security that gives way to something more solitary once the land falls below the horizon.

The middle leg of the passage is the longest and the most exposed, crossing the open ocean at a latitude where the trade winds blow with a reliable steadiness that has made the route possible since the earliest days of sail. The winds in this region blow from the north-east for most of the year, driving the vessels of the passage south-westward toward the Sahrani coast with a force and a consistency that reduces the crossing to a matter of weeks rather than months. The current beneath the surface runs in the same direction, and the combination of the wind and the current has been the engine of the Auric Passage since the first mariners discovered that a vessel could be carried from one continent to the other without the need for constant tacking and adjustment. The reverse journey, from Sahrana to Aethelar, is more demanding, for the vessel must sail against the prevailing wind and the current, and the captains who make the northward voyage must have a thorough knowledge of the counter-currents and the seasonal shifts in the wind that can ease the passage.

The third leg of the passage approaches the Sahrani coast through a chain of islands that serve as the landmarks for vessels making landfall from the open ocean. These islands, which are members of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), rise from the deep water like the steps of a submerged staircase, and their presence on the approach to Sahrana is the first sign of land after the long crossing of the open sea. The channel between the islands is deep and clear, and the passage through this archipelago is the safest approach to the Sahrani coast, sheltered from the worst of the ocean weather by the islands themselves.

## The Ports and the Staging Points

The Aethelaran terminus of the Auric Passage is [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), the wealthiest port on the continent and the primary hub of the eastern Aethelaran trade. The harbour of Eldreth is the largest and the busiest in the known world, its quays stretching for miles along the shore and its warehouses capable of holding the cargoes of an entire trading season. The vessels that sail the Auric Passage gather at Eldreth for the southward voyage, assembling into the convoys that are the standard practice for the crossing, and the merchants who trade at Eldreth are the wealthiest and the most influential in Aethelar. The northern terminus on the Sahrani side is divided among several ports, of which [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are the most significant, each serving as the primary point of entry for the goods that cross the ocean. Khalisar is the larger and the more cosmopolitan of the two, its harbour accommodating the largest vessels and its markets offering the widest range of goods, while Amurra is the centre of the spice trade, its merchants and its warehouses dedicated to the single commodity that is the most valuable cargo on the passage.

The intermediate islands of the Auric Isles serve as staging points for the passage, offering shelter and resupply for the vessels that cross the ocean. The principal of these is the island of [Tidesmeet](/content/location/settlement/village/tidesmeet), which lies roughly at the midpoint of the passage and provides a harbour where vessels can take on fresh water, repair damage, and wait out the weather that may threaten the crossing. The island communities of the Auric Isles are experienced in the service of the passage, and their inhabitants are skilled in the trades that support the ocean traffic, including boatbuilding, sailmaking, and the provisioning of vessels for the longer voyages that lie ahead.

## The Goods of the Passage

The cargoes that move along the Auric Passage are the most diverse and the most valuable of any trade route in the known world. The southward voyage from Aethelar carries the products of the northern continent, including the grain that feeds the cities of the Sahrani coast, the cloth that clothes their inhabitants, and the iron and the metal goods that supply their industries. The merchants of the north also send southward the crafted goods that are the product of the Aethelaran workshops, including the fine furniture, the glassware, and the tools that are valued in the Sahrani markets for their quality and their durability. The northward voyage from Sahrana carries the cargoes that have made the fortune of the passage, including the spices that are the most valuable commodity in the world, the dyes that colour the finest cloth of every continent, and the precious stones that are the foundation of the jewellery trade from Eldreth to [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven). The spices of Sahrana, including the saffron, the cardamom, the black pepper, and the cinnamon that are cultivated in the coastal strip and the interior foothills, are the cargo that every merchant seeks, for their value is consistent and their market is inexhaustible. A single shipload of Sahrani spices can make the fortune of a merchant, and the competition for the space on the vessels that sail the northward passage is among the most intense in the commercial world.

## The Peoples of the Passage

The Auric Passage is travelled by a diverse and cosmopolitan population of mariners, merchants, and travellers from every continent and every nation. The crews of the vessels that sail the passage are drawn chiefly from the coastal populations of Aethelar and Sahrana, and the traditions of seamanship that have developed along the route are a blend of the practices of both continents. The captains who command the vessels of the passage are among the most experienced mariners in the known world, and their knowledge of the winds, the currents, and the weather of the Auric Ocean is the product of a lifetime of study and practice. The merchants who travel the passage are a similarly diverse group, representing the trading houses of every nation that has an interest in the intercontinental trade, and the competition between them for the best cargoes and the most favourable terms is a constant feature of the passage.

The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the Auric Ocean are a presence along the entire length of the passage, their settlements and their territories overlapping with the shipping lanes in ways that require constant negotiation and accommodation. The relationship between the mariners of the passage and the merfolk is one of cautious but generally peaceful coexistence, for the merfolk are known to warn sailors of approaching storms and to guide vessels away from the submerged hazards that would threaten their safety, and the sailors of the passage have learned to read the signs that the merfolk give and to return the courtesy with gifts of metal and crafted goods. The [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) of the Sahrani coast are occasional passengers on the vessels of the passage, their slow and deliberate nature making them patient travellers who are valued for the wisdom and the long perspective that they bring to the affairs of the shorter-lived peoples.

## The Hazards of the Crossing

The Auric Passage is not without its dangers, and the mariners who sail it must contend with hazards that range from the seasonal storms of the ocean to the pirates who prey on the shipping of the passage. The storms of the Auric Ocean are the most formidable natural hazard, building in the summer months when the heat of the sun and the warmth of the water combine to produce the towering clouds and the spirited winds that are the hallmark of the Auric storm season. The vessels that are caught in the open ocean during a summer storm are at the mercy of the elements, and the loss of a ship to the weather is a tragedy that the communities of the passage know all too well. The captains who sail the passage plan their voyages around the storm season, scheduling their crossings for the calmer months of the spring and the autumn and taking shelter at the intermediate islands when the weather threatens.

The pirates of the Auric Ocean are the other great hazard of the passage, and the history of the route is marked by the recurring challenge of the raiders who prey on the shipping that crosses the ocean. The pirates operate from hidden anchorages among the islands of the Auric, striking at the slower and the less well-defended vessels and retreating to their refuges before the naval patrols can respond. The [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) and the Aldreth maintain a permanent naval presence on the passage, their warships escorting the convoys and patrolling the approaches to the island anchorages, but the pirates are persistent and resourceful, and the threat has never been entirely suppressed.

## Economic and Cultural Significance

The Auric Passage is the most significant commercial artery in the known world, and its importance to the economies of the continents that it connects cannot be overstated. The trade that flows along the passage is the foundation of the prosperity of the ports at either end, and the revenue that is generated by the tolls, the taxes, and the commercial activity along the route sustains the governments of both Aethelar and Sahrana. The passage has also been a channel for the exchange of ideas, of technologies, and of cultural practices between the northern and the southern continents, and the influence of this exchange can be seen in the architecture, the cuisine, and the customs of every nation that borders the Auric Ocean. The ships that sail the passage carry not only goods but also the travellers, the pilgrims, and the scholars who move between the continents, and the intellectual and the cultural traffic that flows along the route is as valuable as the commercial cargoes that fill the holds. The Auric Passage is a highway of civilisation, a connection between two worlds that have shaped each other through the centuries of trade and contact that the route has made possible, and its significance to the history and the future of the known world is beyond measure.

## The Future of the Passage

The continued prosperity of the Auric Passage depends upon the maintenance of the peace and the co-operation that have sustained the intercontinental trade for so many centuries. The political arrangements that govern the passage, including the treaties that regulate the tolls, the pilotage, and the naval patrols, are the product of long negotiation and careful compromise, and the nations that share the ocean have generally found it more profitable to co-operate than to quarrel over the division of the revenue that the trade generates. The future of the passage will be shaped by the same forces that have shaped its past, including the growth of the populations at either end of the route, the expansion of the trade into new commodities, and the technological developments that may change the nature of the crossing itself. Whatever changes may come, the Auric Passage will remain the principal link between the northern and the southern continents, a route that has carried the commerce and the culture of the known world for as long as there have been vessels to sail it and ports to receive its cargoes.
