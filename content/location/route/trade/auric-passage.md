## The Busiest Maritime Route in the Known World

The Auric Passage is the principal maritime trade route across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), connecting the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) to the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) in a crossing that has been the foundation of intercontinental commerce for longer than any written record can reliably attest. The passage runs from the great port of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) in the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), sweeping south-eastward across the open ocean before making landfall at the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast between [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra), a voyage of some three weeks in favourable conditions and considerably longer when winds and currents are less obliging. The passage is the busiest and most valuable single trade route in the known world, carrying spices, dyes, and precious stones of Sahrana northward and returning with grain, cloth, iron, and manufactured goods of Aethelar.

## Three Legs Across the Central Basin

The waters of the Auric Passage cross the central basin of the Auric Ocean, a deep and open expanse offering few landmarks and fewer havens for vessels in distress. The voyage is divided into three distinct legs. The first leg follows the coast of Aethelar southward from Eldreth, keeping within sight of the shore for several days before the course turns eastward into the open ocean. This coastal leg is the most sheltered portion of the journey.

The middle leg is the longest and most exposed, crossing the open ocean at a latitude where the trade winds blow with reliable steadiness. The winds in this region blow from the north-east for most of the year, driving vessels south-westward toward the Sahrani coast. The current beneath the surface runs in the same direction. The reverse journey, from Sahrana to Aethelar, is more demanding, for the vessel must sail against the prevailing wind and current, requiring thorough knowledge of counter-currents and seasonal shifts in the wind.

The third leg approaches the Sahrani coast through a chain of islands that serve as landmarks for vessels making landfall from the open ocean. These islands, members of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), rise from the deep water like the steps of a submerged staircase. The channel between the islands is deep and clear, and the passage through this archipelago is the safest approach to the Sahrani coast.

## Eldreth, Khalisar, and the Islands Between

The Aethelaran terminus of the Auric Passage is [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), the wealthiest port on the continent and the primary hub of eastern Aethelaran trade. The harbour of Eldreth is the largest and busiest in the known world, its quays stretching for miles along the shore and its warehouses capable of holding the cargoes of an entire trading season. The northern terminus on the Sahrani side is divided among several ports, of which [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are the most significant. Khalisar is the larger and more cosmopolitan, its harbour accommodating the largest vessels, while Amurra is the centre of the spice trade.

The intermediate islands of the Auric Isles serve as staging points, offering shelter and resupply. The principal of these is [Tidesmeet](/content/location/settlement/village/tidesmeet), which lies roughly at the midpoint of the passage and provides a harbour where vessels can take on fresh water, repair damage, and wait out threatening weather.

## Spices North, Grain South

The cargoes moving along the Auric Passage are the most diverse and valuable of any trade route. The southward voyage from Aethelar carries grain that feeds the cities of the Sahrani coast, cloth, iron, metal goods, and the crafted products of Aethelaran workshops including fine furniture, glassware, and tools. The northward voyage from Sahrana carries the cargoes that have made the fortune of the passage: spices including saffron, cardamom, black pepper, and cinnamon; the dyes that colour the finest cloth; and precious stones that are the foundation of the jewellery trade from Eldreth to [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven).

## Mariners, Captains, and the Merfolk Presence

The Auric Passage is travelled by a diverse and cosmopolitan population of mariners, merchants, and travellers from every continent and nation. The crews are drawn chiefly from the coastal populations of Aethelar and Sahrana, and the traditions of seamanship along the route are a blend of the practices of both continents. The captains who command the vessels are among the most experienced mariners in the known world.

The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the Auric Ocean are a presence along the entire length of the passage, their settlements overlapping with the shipping lanes in ways requiring constant negotiation and accommodation. The relationship is one of cautious but generally peaceful coexistence, for the merfolk warn sailors of approaching storms and guide vessels away from submerged hazards, and the sailors return the courtesy with gifts of metal and crafted goods. The [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) of the Sahrani coast are occasional passengers, valued for their wisdom and long perspective.

## Storms and Pirates

The Auric Passage is not without dangers. The storms of the Auric Ocean are the most formidable natural hazard, building in the summer months when heat and warm water combine to produce towering clouds and spirited winds. Vessels caught in the open ocean during a summer storm are at the mercy of the elements. Captains plan voyages around the storm season, scheduling crossings for the calmer months of spring and autumn.

The pirates of the Auric Ocean are the other great hazard, operating from hidden anchorages among the islands. They strike at slower and less well-defended vessels, retreating to their refuges before naval patrols can respond. The [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) and the Aldreth maintain a permanent naval presence on the passage, escorting convoys and patrolling the approaches, but the pirates are persistent and resourceful.

## Most Significant Commercial Artery

The Auric Passage is the most significant commercial artery in the known world. The trade flowing along the passage is the foundation of the prosperity of the ports at either end, and the revenue from tolls, taxes, and commercial activity sustains governments of both Aethelar and Sahrana. The passage has also been a channel for the exchange of ideas, technologies, and cultural practices between the northern and southern continents. The ships carry not only goods but also travellers, pilgrims, and scholars moving between continents. The intellectual and cultural traffic along the route is as valuable as the commercial cargoes.

## The Future of the Passage

The continued prosperity of the Auric Passage depends upon the maintenance of peace and co-operation that have sustained intercontinental trade for centuries. The political arrangements governing the passage, including treaties regulating tolls, pilotage, and naval patrols, are the product of long negotiation and careful compromise. The future will be shaped by the same forces that have shaped its past: growth of populations at either end, expansion into new commodities, and technological developments that may change the nature of the crossing itself.
