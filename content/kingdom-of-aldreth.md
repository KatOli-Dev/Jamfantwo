## Overview

The Kingdom of Aldreth is a human kingdom of the eastern coast of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), centred on the city of [Eldreth](/content/eldreth), the wealthiest port on the continent. The kingdom occupies the eastern coast from the northern reaches of the temperate zone to the margins of the warm plains in the south, and its territory includes some of the most productive and the most densely settled land in Aethelar. The Kingdom of Aldreth is the rival of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/kingdom-of-vael) in the trade of the eastern coast, and its commercial power and its political influence are among the greatest of any realm on the continent.

## The Coast and the Trade

The eastern coast of Aethelar is the most commercially productive region of the continent, and the Kingdom of Aldreth controls the largest and the most valuable portion of the coast. The trade that flows through the harbour of [Eldreth](/content/eldreth) is the greatest of any port in the known world, and the goods that pass through the city include the products of every continent and the islands. The kingdom's revenue is drawn chiefly from the tolls and the taxes on this trade, and the wealth that accumulates in the treasury funds the maintenance of the fleet, the construction of public works, and the defence of the realm.

The kingdom is a major maritime power, and the fleet of Aldreth is among the largest and the most capable in Aethelar. The fleet protects the trade routes of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) and the coast, and it is the primary instrument of the kingdom's power and influence. The shipyards of [Caerwyn](/content/caerwyn), a member of the [Thessaran League](/content/thessaran-league), are a major source of the vessels that serve the kingdom's fleet, and the relationship between Aldreth and the league is one of mutual benefit and occasional tension.

## The City and the Court

The capital of the kingdom is [Eldreth](/content/eldreth), and the city is the seat of the king and the court. The king of Aldreth is the supreme authority of the realm, and the court is the centre of the political and the social life of the kingdom. The nobility of Aldreth are the merchants and the landowners of the coast, and their wealth and their influence are drawn from the trade that flows through the harbours of the realm. The administration of the kingdom is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the collection of taxes, the maintenance of the fleet and the fortifications, and the administration of justice.

## The People and the Defence

The population of the Kingdom of Aldreth is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the coast are among the most cosmopolitan and the most prosperous in Aethelar. The [elves](/content/elves), [dwarves](/content/dwarves), and [gnomes](/content/gnomes) are present in significant numbers, drawn by the trade and the relative tolerance of the kingdom, and the streets and the markets of the realm are a meeting point of peoples from every continent. The [centaurs](/content/centaurs) of the warm plains are occasional visitors to the southern portions of the kingdom, and the trade between the settled humans and the nomadic centaurs is a valued part of the kingdom's commerce.

The defence of the kingdom is maintained by the fleet and by a standing army that is funded by the treasury, and the fortifications of Eldreth and the other coastal cities are maintained in good order. The kingdom has been at peace with its neighbours for many generations, but the rivalry with the Kingdom of Vael and the occasional tensions with the Thessaran League require the maintenance of a credible military force.

## Significance

The Kingdom of Aldreth is the major human kingdom of the eastern coast and one of the most powerful and the most influential political entities in Aethelar. Its commercial power and its maritime strength are the foundation of its significance, and its trade connects the continent to the markets of the wider world. The kingdom is a symbol of the wealth and the cosmopolitanism of the eastern coast, and its long history and its stable governance are a model of the political order that has sustained the continent for centuries.
