## Overview

Khalisar is the greatest of the Sahrani coastal city-states, and it is the wealthiest and most influential settlement on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana). The city stands on a broad, natural harbour on the coast where the warm currents of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) meet the fertile strip of the northern coast, and its position at the centre of the most productive region of the Sahrani coast has made it the primary hub of trade between Sahrana and the northern continents. Khalisar is a large, sophisticated city, and its wealth, its learning, and its political influence are unmatched by any other settlement in Sahrana.

## The Harbour and the Trade

The harbour of Khalisar is one of the finest on the Sahrani coast, a deep, sheltered bay that is protected by a natural breakwater of rock and coral, and the approach is marked by lighthouses that are among the tallest in the known world. The quays extend for nearly a mile along the waterfront, and they are lined with the warehouses, the counting houses, and the offices of the merchants who manage the trade that flows through the port. The volume of trade that passes through Khalisar is the greatest of any Sahrani city, and the harbour is the busiest on the northern coast, for the ships of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), [Morvind](/content/morvind), and the islands arrive and depart at all hours.

The trade of Khalisar is dominated by the exchange of Sahrani and northern goods. The spices, the dyes, the precious stones, and the rare fabrics of Sahrana are the primary exports, and they command the highest prices in the markets of the northern continents. The return cargo includes the grain, the iron, and the crafted metalwork of Aethelar and Morvind, and the exchange of southern and northern goods is the foundation of the city's wealth. The merchants of Khalisar are among the most skilled and the most influential in the known world, and their operations extend from the harbour to the farthest reaches of the trade network.

## The City

Khalisar is a large and beautiful city, and its architecture is noted for its elegance and its adaptation to the warm climate. The buildings are constructed of light-coloured stone that reflects the sun, with shaded courtyards, high windows, and ventilated passages that channel the breezes from the sea. Water is channelled through the city by means of aqueducts and underground channels, and the presence of running water in fountains and gardens is both a practical necessity and a symbol of the city's prosperity. The public buildings of Khalisar are among the most impressive in Sahrana, and the temples, the libraries, and the council halls are works of architectural refinement that reflect the wealth and the culture of the city.

The libraries of Khalisar are among the most extensive in the known world, and the collections that are housed in the library quarter include the works of the Sahrani scholars, the histories of the city-states, and the treatises on the sciences and the arts that have been produced by the learned men and women of the continent. The libraries are maintained by the council, and they are open to the scholars and the citizens of the city, and the reputation of the libraries is a significant factor in the cultural prestige of Khalisar. The temples of the city are likewise noted for their beauty and their refinement, and the religious traditions of Sahrana are represented in the architecture and the rituals of the temples that are situated throughout the city.

The population of Khalisar is the most diverse of any Sahrani city, and the streets and markets are a meeting point of peoples from every continent. [Humans](/content/humans) are the majority, but [dwarves](/content/dwarves), [elves](/content/elves), and the peoples of the southern regions are present in significant numbers, drawn by the trade and the relative tolerance of the city. The [naga](/content/naga) of the warm rivers are occasional visitors to the harbour, and the trade between the coastal humans and the riverine naga is a valued part of the city's commerce. The markets of the city are the most vibrant in Sahrana, and the goods that are bought and sold in the bazaars and the trading houses include the products of every continent and the craftsmanship of every people.

## History

The history of Khalisar is the history of the Sahrani coast, for the city has been the primary hub of the continental trade for as long as the records of the region have been kept. The city was founded by the human settlers who established the first trading posts on the northern coast, and the natural harbour and the fertile hinterland that surrounded the site provided the foundation for the growth of the settlement. The city expanded rapidly as the trade between Sahrana and the northern continents developed, and the wealth that accumulated in the city funded the construction of the public buildings, the fortifications, and the harbour works that are the visible legacy of the city's prosperity.

The [Council of Khalisar](/content/council-of-khalisar) was established in the early centuries of the city's history, and the council's governance has been a significant factor in the stability and the prosperity of the city ever since. The city's leadership in the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast) was established in the early years of the league, and Khalisar has been the leading member of the alliance throughout its history. The city has been besieged on several occasions, but the fortifications and the naval force have been sufficient to deter or to repel the threats, and the city has never fallen to an external force.

## Governance and Significance

Khalisar is governed by the [Council of Khalisar](/content/council-of-khalisar), a body of elected officials and appointed elders that oversees the administration, the defence, and the trade of the city. The council is the primary political institution of the city, and its decisions shape the economy and the culture of the Sahrani coast. The city is the leading member of the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast), the alliance of the Sahrani coastal city-states, and its influence extends throughout the league and beyond.

Khalisar is the commercial and the cultural heart of Sahrana, and its prosperity is the engine of the economy of the northern coast and the continent as a whole. The trade that flows through its harbour connects Sahrana to the northern continents and the islands, and the wealth that accumulates in the city funds the construction of public works, the maintenance of the trade routes, and the support of the learning and the arts that are the pride of the city. Khalisar is the greatest of the Sahrani city-states, and its significance is unmatched by any other settlement on the continent.

The cultural influence of Khalisar extends throughout Sahrana and beyond, for the architecture, the scholarship, and the civic traditions of the city have been adopted and adapted by the other cities of the coast and the continent. The city is a centre of learning and the arts, and the scholars and the artisans who are drawn to Khalisar by its libraries, its temples, and its workshops contribute to the cultural vitality that is one of the most valued features of the city. The significance of Khalisar lies in its wealth, its culture, and its governance, and the city is a symbol of the Sahrani civilisation at its most accomplished and its most refined.
