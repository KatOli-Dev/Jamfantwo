## Overview

Ironhollow is a [dwarven](/content/dwarves) industrial settlement in the eastern ranges of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated in a region where the mountains are rich in iron ore and the conditions are well suited to the large-scale smelting and working of metal. The settlement is the primary centre of iron production in Morvind, and its forges and smelteries are among the largest and the most active in the known world. Ironhollow is a dependency of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim), and its iron is the raw material that feeds the forges of [Stoneheim](/content/stoneheim) and [Khazgar](/content/khazgar), where it is worked into the finished products that are exported to every continent.

## The Mines and the Smelteries

The mines of Ironhollow are the most productive iron workings in Morvind, and the ore that is extracted from the depths of the eastern ranges is among the richest in the known world. The mines are worked on a large scale, for the demand for iron is great and the deposits are extensive, and the miners of Ironhollow are among the most skilled and the most numerous in the dwarven world. The ore that is extracted is smelted in the great smelteries that are situated close to the mine workings, and the smelteries are the dominant feature of the settlement, their chimneys and their furnaces producing a constant output of pig iron and crude steel that is the raw material of the dwarven metalworking industry.

The smelteries of Ironhollow are among the largest industrial structures in the known world, and the furnaces that are used to smelt the ore are fuelled by the charcoal that is produced from the forests of the lower slopes and the coal that is mined from the seams that run through the eastern ranges. The smelting operations are conducted continuously, for the demand for iron is constant and the furnaces must be kept burning, and the smelteries are a hive of activity at all hours, the ore arriving from the mines, the fuel arriving from the forests and the coal seams, and the pig iron and the crude steel departing for the forges of the interior.

## The Forges and the Trade

The forges of Ironhollow are devoted chiefly to the preliminary working of the iron, and the pig iron and the crude steel that are produced in the smelteries are worked into the bars, the ingots, and the rough castings that are the raw material of the finished metalwork that is produced in Stoneheim and Khazgar. The forges of Ironhollow are large and efficient, and the iron that is produced here is the foundation of the dwarven metalworking industry, for the quantity and the quality of the Ironhollow output is unmatched by any other source in Morvind.

The trade that connects Ironhollow to the rest of the dwarven world is managed through the overland routes that connect the eastern ranges to the interior, and the iron that is produced in Ironhollow is transported overland to Stoneheim and Khazgar, where it is worked into the finished products that are exported to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and beyond. The return cargo includes the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the settlement cannot produce for itself, and the trade that flows through the routes is the lifeline of the community.

## The Settlement

The population of Ironhollow is entirely [dwarven](/content/dwarves), and the community is devoted to the iron industry that is the reason for the settlement's existence. The dwellings of Ironhollow are carved into the rock above the smelteries, and the living quarters are warmer than those of most dwarven cities, for the heat of the furnaces permeates the surrounding stone. The settlement is a grim, industrial place, and the air within the halls is thick with the smoke and the heat of the smelteries, but the community is prosperous and the dwarves of Ironhollow are proud of their role as the primary producers of the iron that feeds the dwarven metalworking industry.

## Significance

Ironhollow is the primary centre of iron production in Morvind, and its output is the raw material that sustains the dwarven metalworking industry. The settlement is a dependency of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim), and its iron is a critical component of the economy of the dwarven realm and the continent as a whole. The significance of Ironhollow lies in the scale and the efficiency of its iron production, and the settlement is a testament to the dwarven mastery of the industrial processes that are the foundation of the dwarven civilisation.
