## Overview

Goldmeadow is a central trade hub of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the meeting of river and road in the heart of the central grasslands. The city stands at the confluence of two of the principal rivers of the plain, where the roads that cross the grasslands from north, south, east, and west converge on a single point. The position of Goldmeadow at the intersection of the major routes of the central grasslands has made it the primary hub of inland trade in the region, and the goods that pass through its markets come from every corner of the continent.

## The Rivers and the Roads

The two rivers that meet at Goldmeadow are navigable for much of their length, and the confluence is a natural point for the transfer of goods between the river traffic and the road traffic that cross the grasslands. The rivers connect Goldmeadow to the agricultural regions of the plain, to the mountain passes of the spine, and to the coast, and the roads connect the city to the overland routes that run in every direction. The combination of river and road access makes Goldmeadow the most connected settlement in the central grasslands, and the trade that flows through the city is the most diverse of any inland settlement in Aethelar.

The wharves and quays of Goldmeadow line both banks of the two rivers above the confluence, and the river traffic that calls at the city includes the grain barges of the agricultural regions, the timber rafts of the mountain forests, and the trading vessels that carry manufactured goods from the coast. The roads that enter the city are broad and well-maintained, for the traffic that uses them is heavy and constant, and the tolls on the roads are a significant source of the city's revenue.

## Trade and the Market

The trade of Goldmeadow is the most diverse of any inland city, for the city stands at the meeting point of the river and road networks of the central grasslands, and the goods that pass through its markets include the agricultural products of the plain, the timber and the metalwork of the mountains, the manufactured goods of the coast, and the exotic products of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) trade. The market square of Goldmeadow is one of the busiest in the known world, and the market that is held there is a daily affair, for the constant traffic of the rivers and the roads ensures a steady flow of goods and customers.

The city is also a centre for the financial services that support the trade of the grasslands, and the bankers and the money-changers of Goldmeadow are among the most influential in the inland economy. The city's position at the hub of the trade network makes it a natural centre for the exchange of currencies, the extension of credit, and the settlement of debts, and the financial institutions of Goldmeadow are a significant part of its commerce.

## The City

Goldmeadow is a large, bustling city, and its architecture reflects its function as a trade hub, with broad streets, substantial buildings, and a commercial district that dominates the city centre. The city is built on both banks of the two rivers, connected by bridges that are among the finest in the grasslands, and the waterfront is the busiest part of the city, with wharves, warehouses, and the offices of the trading houses that line the rivers. The population of Goldmeadow is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) who are involved in the metal trade and the maintenance of the bridges and the roads.

## Significance

Goldmeadow is the primary hub of inland trade in the central grasslands, and its position at the meeting of river and road makes it the most connected settlement in the region. The city is a key member of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), and its trade and financial services are a significant component of the economy of the grasslands and the continent as a whole. The prosperity of Goldmeadow is a direct consequence of its position, and the city has grown and thrived on the trade that flows through its streets and over its bridges.
