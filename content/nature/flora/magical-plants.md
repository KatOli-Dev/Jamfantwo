## The Flora of the Art

The plants that possess properties relevant to the Art of magic are among the most closely studied and the most carefully guarded of all the natural resources of the known world. The magical properties of the plants are inherent in the substances that the plants produce, and the knowledge of the plants that are useful in the practice of the Art is the preserve of the mages and the alchemists who devote their lives to the study of the magical world.

The plants that are used in the Art are of two principal categories: the plants that are used in the preparation of the potions and the elixirs that the mages consume to enhance the magical abilities, and the plants that are used in the construction of the foci and the implements through which the Art is channeled. The cultivation and the harvest of the magical plants are the subjects of the elaborate rituals that ensure the potency of the plants and the safety of the practitioners.

## Moonflower and the Night of the Full Moon

The moonflower is the most celebrated of the magical plants of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), a rare and beautiful plant that blooms only on the night of the full moon in the late summer. The moonflower is a climbing vine that grows in the clearings of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood), and the flowers that the vine produces are large and white, opening in the hours after the sunset and closing before the dawn.

The petals of the moonflower are used in the preparation of the potion that enhances the vision of the mage, allowing the mage to perceive the flows of the magical energy that are invisible to the ordinary eye. The petals are harvested at the moment when the moon is at its zenith, and the petals are dried in the dark and ground into the powder that is the base of the potion.

The moonflower is protected by the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Heartwood, who regard the plant as a sacred gift of the forest and who restrict the harvest of the flowers to the members of their own community. The moonflower is not traded in the markets of the settlements, and the mages who seek the petals of the moonflower must negotiate with the elven communities for the access to the harvest.

## Sunvine and the Concentrated Light

The sunvine is a plant that grows in the [Sunfire Steppe](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sunfire-steppe) of Sahrana, a trailing vine that produces the small and golden flowers that open in the full heat of the day. The sunvine absorbs the light of the sun and concentrates the energy in the leaves and the stems, and the plant that is harvested at the right moment yields a substance that is used in the preparation of the light-based spells of the Art.

The sap of the sunvine is collected by the alchemists of the Sahrani coast, who cut the stems of the vine in the early morning and collect the sap that drips from the cut. The sap is distilled and concentrated into a viscous liquid that glows with a faint internal light, and the liquid is used in the construction of the foci that channel the energy of the sun.

The trade in the sunvine sap is regulated by the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), which controls the access to the regions where the sunvine grows and the routes by which the sap is transported to the markets of the coast.

## The Shadow Moss of the Deep Caves

The shadow moss that grows in the deepest caves of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) is a plant that has adapted to the conditions of the complete darkness. The shadow moss does not draw energy from the light, for there is no light in the depths where the moss grows, and the moss derives its energy from the minerals that are dissolved in the water that seeps through the rock.

The shadow moss is used in the preparation of the darkness-based spells of the Art, and the moss is also used in the construction of the foci that conceal the presence of the mage from the perception of the enemies. The moss is harvested by the dwarven miners who work in the deep levels of the holds, and the moss is traded to the mages of the northern cities.

The harvest of the shadow moss is a dangerous undertaking, for the moss grows in the unstable and the inaccessible parts of the caves, and the gatherers who collect the moss must be experienced in the navigation of the underground passages. The dwarven communities that control the access to the deep caves regulate the harvest of the shadow moss, and the trade in the moss is a minor but significant part of the economy of the underground holds.

## The Dream Leaf of the Kharesh Oases

The dream leaf is a plant that grows in the shaded gardens of the oases of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), a small and unremarkable plant that produces a leaf that, when chewed or steeped in the water, induces a state of the dreaming that the mages of the Sahrani tradition use for the divination and the communication with the spirits.

The dream leaf is harvested in the autumn, when the leaves have reached the full size and the alkaloids that produce the dreaming effect have accumulated in the tissue of the leaf. The leaves are dried in the shade, and the dried leaves are stored in the sealed containers that preserve the potency of the leaf.

The use of the dream leaf in the practice of the Art is regulated by the mages of the Sahrani coast, and the trade in the dream leaf is restricted to the members of the mages' guilds. The misuse of the dream leaf is a serious offense in the legal codes of the Sahrani states, for the dreaming that the leaf induces can be dangerous if the user is not prepared for the experience.

## The Starlight Cactus of the Southern Desert

The starlight cactus that grows in the remote regions of the southern desert produces a flower that opens only on the clearest nights of the winter, when the stars are visible in the full brilliance. The flower of the starlight cactus is a cluster of the white petals that surround the centre of the flower that glows with a faint luminescence that mirrors the light of the stars.

The flower of the starlight cactus is used in the preparation of the potions that enhance the abilities of the mage in the practices of the divination and the astronomy. The flower is harvested at the moment when the glow of the centre is at its brightest, and the flower is dried and ground into the powder that is the base of the potion.

The starlight cactus is rare and difficult to find, and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) who know the locations of the cacti are the only source of the flowers. The trade in the starlight cactus flowers is conducted through the personal connections between the mages and the tribal leaders, and the prices that the flowers command are among the highest of any magical plant.

## The Ironbark Tree of the Dwarven Halls

The ironbark tree is a tree that grows only in the underground halls of the dwarven holds, a species that has been cultivated by the dwarves for the centuries for the wood that is used in the construction of the magical foci and the implements of the Art. The ironbark is a slow-growing tree that thrives in the conditions of the underground environment, and the wood of the ironbark is the hardest and the most resistant to the magical energies of any material in the known world.

The wood of the ironbark is used in the construction of the staves and the wands that the mages use to channel the Art, and the wood is also used in the construction of the circles and the barriers that contain the magical energies within the designated area. The dwarven woodworkers who craft the foci from the ironbark are the most skilled of the artisans of the underground halls, and the tools that they produce are valued by the mages of the three continents.

## The Regulation of the Magical Plant Trade

The trade in the magical plants is regulated by the guilds of the mages and the alchemists, who maintain the standards of the quality and the purity of the plants that are used in the practice of the Art. The guilds of the mages in [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) are the principal regulators of the magical plant trade, and the guilds maintain the lists of the approved suppliers and the standards of the processing that the plants must undergo.

The trade in the most dangerous of the magical plants, the plants that can be used to harm as well as to heal, is restricted by the laws of the kingdoms and the city-states. The mages who deal in the restricted plants must hold the licences that are granted by the authorities, and the penalties for the unlicensed possession of the restricted plants are severe.
