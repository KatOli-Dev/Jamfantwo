## The Aromatic Treasures of Sahrana

The spice plants of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) are the most valuable category of agricultural products traded in the known world, and the cultivation of the spice plants in the irrigated gardens of the interior and the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is the foundation of the prosperity of the Sahrani city-states. The spices that are produced in Sahrana include saffron, cardamom, black pepper, and the rare sunroot, and the trade in these spices is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the spice merchants, whose wealth and influence exceed that of any other commercial institution on the continent.

The conditions that the spice plants require are particular and demanding, and the cultivation of the spices is limited to the regions where the climate and the soil provide the conditions that the plants need. The spice gardens are concentrated in the oases and the river valleys of the Sahrani interior, where the water is abundant and the heat of the desert is moderated by the shade of the trees and the moisture of the irrigated soil. The knowledge of the cultivation and the processing of the spices is passed from generation to generation within the families of the gardeners, and the techniques that are used in the production of the finest spices are the secrets of the art.

## Saffron and the Crocus Gardens of the Interior

Saffron is the most valuable spice by weight that is traded in the known world, produced from the dried stigmas of the crocus flower that is cultivated in the irrigated gardens of the Sahrani interior. The crocus is a small and delicate plant that flowers in the autumn, and the flowers must be harvested in the early morning of the days when they open, for the stigmas lose their potency and their colour if they are left on the plant for more than a few hours after the flower has opened.

The harvest of the saffron is the most labour-intensive of all the agricultural operations in the Sahrani interior, and the labour of the harvest is performed by the women and the children of the families that cultivate the crocus beds. The three red stigmas of each flower are carefully plucked from the flower and placed in baskets that are carried to the drying sheds. The stigmas are dried over a low fire, a process that reduces the weight of the stigmas by a factor of five and concentrates the colour and the flavour of the spice.

The saffron of Sahrana is recognised as the finest in the known world, and the colour of the Sahrani saffron is deep red and the aroma is strong and distinctive. The saffron is used in the preparation of the most elaborate dishes of the Sahrani cuisine and in the dyeing of the ceremonial garments that are worn by the priests and the sovereigns of the region. The price of saffron in the markets of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) is measured in gold pieces per ounce, and the trade in saffron is the most profitable of all the spice trades.

## Cardamom and the Shaded Gardens of the Oases

Cardamom is the second most valuable spice of the Sahrani interior, grown in the shaded gardens of the oases where the ground is moist and the air is warm. The cardamom plant is a member of the ginger family that produces pods containing the small black seeds that are the spice. The plant requires the shade of the taller trees and the constant moisture that is provided by the irrigation of the oasis gardens.

The cardamom pods are harvested in the dry season, when the pods have reached their full size but have not yet split open and released the seeds. The pods are dried in the sun, and the dried pods are packed into the sealed containers that preserve the aroma and the flavour of the spice. The cardamom of Sahrana has a distinctive flavour that is sweeter and more aromatic than the cardamom of the other regions where it is grown, and the Sahrani cardamom commands a premium in the markets of Aethelar.

The cardamom is used in the preparation of the sweet dishes and the pastries of the Sahrani and the Aethelaran cuisines, and the cardamom is also used in the brewing of the spiced tea that is the common beverage of the southern continent. The seeds of the cardamom are ground and added to the coffee that is served in the houses of the wealthy, and the aroma of the cardamom is one of the characteristic scents of the Sahrani market.

## Black Pepper and the Trellises of the Coast

Black pepper is the most widely traded spice of Sahrana, consumed in every region of the known world where the people can afford it. The pepper vine is grown on trellises in the gardens of the coast and the interior, producing the clusters of the berries that are harvested when the berries begin to turn red.

The pepper berries are dried in the sun, turning black and wrinkled as the moisture leaves them, and the dried peppercorns are packed into the sacks for the transport to the markets of the coast. The pepper that is produced in the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is the finest of the black pepper, and the gardens of the coast produce the peppercorns that are the largest and the most aromatic of the pepper of the known world.

The demand for the black pepper in the cities of Aethelar and Morvind is enormous, and the volume of the pepper that passes through the ports of the Sahrani coast is measured in thousands of tons each year. The pepper is used in the cooking of every region of the known world, and the spice is the most common of all the seasonings that are used in the preparation of the food.

## Sunroot and the Hidden Groves of the Kharesh

The sunroot is the rarest and the most expensive of the Sahrani spices, harvested from the deepest parts of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) desert by the [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) guides who know the locations of the hidden groves where the sunroot plant grows. The sunroot is a tuber that grows beneath the sand, sending up a single leaf to the surface that is visible only to those who know what to look for.

The sunroot is harvested in the spring, when the plant has stored its energy for the coming season, and the tuber is dried and ground into a powder that has a flavour unlike any other spice in the known world. The sunroot is used in the preparation of the most elaborate dishes of the Sahrani cuisine, and the sunroot is also valued for the medicinal properties that the healers of the interior attribute to it.

The harvest of the sunroot is a ritual that is conducted by the salawa guides in accordance with the traditions of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), and the locations of the groves are passed from generation to generation within the families of the guides. The sunroot is never cultivated, for the plant does not grow in the gardens and the plantations, and the supply of the spice is limited to what the desert yields naturally.

## The Spice Gardens of the Green Crescent

The spice gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) are the most productive and the most valuable of all the agricultural lands of Sahrana. The gardens are irrigated by the channels that carry the water from the rivers of the interior, and the soil of the gardens is enriched by the silt that the rivers deposit during the flood season.

The organisation of the spice gardens is the work of the families of the gardeners who have cultivated the spices for the generations, and the knowledge of the cultivation of the spice plants is the heritage of these families. The gardens are divided into the plots that are planted with the different spice plants, and the rotation of the crops and the management of the irrigation are the responsibilities of the master gardeners who supervise the work.

## The Regulation of the Spice Trade

The trade in the spice plants and the prepared spices is regulated by the guilds of the spice merchants, who maintain the standards of the quality and the purity of the spices that are sold in the markets of the coast. The guilds of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are the most powerful of the merchant guilds of Sahrana, and the regulation of the spice trade by these guilds has maintained the value of the Sahrani spices at a level that sustains the prosperity of the cities and the interior communities that produce them.

The [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of the interior cities that controls the caravan routes and the oases of the desert, is the principal regulator of the spice trade from the production side. The pact sets the duties that are levied on the spices that pass through the territory of the member cities, and the pact maintains the wells and the fortified stations that make the crossing of the desert possible.
