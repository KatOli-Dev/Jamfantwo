## The Problem Plants of the Known World

The weeds and the invasive plants that infest the fields and the gardens of the known world are a constant challenge for the farmers and the gardeners who must contend with the competition that these plants offer to the crops and the useful plants that they cultivate. The weeds are the plants that grow where they are not wanted, and the invasive plants are the species that spread beyond their natural ranges and displace the native plants of the regions that they invade.

The management of the weeds and the invasive plants is a significant part of the labour of the agricultural year, and the methods that the farmers and the gardeners use to control the problem plants range from the hand-pulling of the individual weeds to the application of the preparations that suppress the growth of the unwanted species. The knowledge of the weeds and the methods of the control is passed from generation to generation within the families of the farmers, and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the gardeners and the farmers maintain the records of the methods that are effective against the most troublesome species.

## The Thistle of the Fields of Aethelar

The thistle that grows in the fields of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the most widespread of the weeds of the cultivated land, and the thistle is a constant presence in the fields of the wheat and the barley that the farmers of the region cultivate. The thistle is a prickly plant that produces a deep root system that competes with the crops for the water and the nutrients of the soil, and the thistle also produces the seeds that are spread by the wind and that germinate in the disturbed soil of the cultivated fields.

The control of the thistle is achieved by the repeated cutting of the plant before the flowers produce the seeds, and the farmers of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) have developed the technique of the summer fallow that involves the ploughing of the field in the summer to destroy the thistles and the other weeds that have grown in the field. The fallow is left without a crop for a season, and the repeated ploughing of the fallow eliminates the thistles and the other perennial weeds that the cultivation of the crop cannot control.

The thistle of the Aethelaran fields is not without its uses, for the young shoots of the thistle are edible and the leaves of the thistle are used in the preparation of the rennet that is used in the making of the cheese. The thistle is also valued by the beekeepers, for the flowers of the thistle produce the nectar that the bees use in the production of the honey.

## The Bindweed of the Gardens and the Hedges

The bindweed that grows in the gardens and the hedges of the Aethelaran settlements is a climbing plant that twines around the stems of the cultivated plants and chokes the growth of the vegetables and the flowers. The bindweed is a perennial plant that reproduces from the fragments of the root as well as from the seeds, and the bindweed is one of the most difficult of the weeds to control in the garden.

The control of the bindweed requires the careful digging of the roots and the removal of every fragment of the root from the soil, for the plant can regenerate from a piece of the root that is no longer than a finger. The gardeners of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) have developed the method of the smothering of the bindweed by the covering of the infested area with the layers of the straw and the manure that exclude the light and suppress the growth of the plant.

## The Nettle of the Waste Places

The nettle that grows in the waste places and the disturbed soils of every region of Aethelar is a weed that is both a nuisance and a resource. The nettle is a stinging plant that causes a painful rash when the skin comes into contact with the hairs of the leaves, and the nettle is a common problem in the gardens and the pastures where the plant competes with the crops and the grasses.

The nettle is difficult to control in the gardens and the pastures, for the plant reproduces from the seeds and from the fragments of the root, and the nettle is tolerant of the poor soil and the dry conditions that would kill the other plants. The farmers of Aethelar have developed the method of the repeated cutting of the nettle in the early summer, when the plant is at the peak of the growth and the cutting weakens the root system and reduces the vigour of the plant.

The nettle is valued by the healers and the weavers of the northern settlements, for the leaves of the nettle are used in the preparation of the medicinal teas and the stems of the nettle are used in the production of the fibre that is woven into the cloth. The nettle is also a host plant for the caterpillars of the butterflies that are valued for the beauty and the diversity that they bring to the gardens.

## The Gorse of the Heathlands

The gorse that grows in the heathlands of the [Ashen Heath](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/ashen-heath) and the uplands of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is a shrub that is invasive in the regions where the land has been cleared of the forest and the grazing of the animals is not sufficient to control the growth. The gorse is a spiny and dense shrub that forms the thickets that are impenetrable to the animals and the people, and the gorse is a serious problem in the regions where the land has been abandoned and the gorse has taken over.

The control of the gorse is achieved by the burning of the thickets in the dry season, when the gorse is dry and the fire removes the above-ground growth and kills the seeds that are lying in the soil. The burning of the gorse is a dangerous operation that must be conducted under the supervision of the experienced fire managers, and the burning of the heathlands is regulated by the authorities of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael).

## The Water Hyacinth of the Reed Marshes

The water hyacinth that has been introduced to the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) in the recent decades is an invasive aquatic plant that has spread rapidly through the waterways of the marsh, forming the dense mats that choke the channels and the open water and that block the passage of the boats and the flow of the water.

The water hyacinth is a floating plant that reproduces rapidly, and the mats of the hyacinth can double in size in a matter of weeks under the favourable conditions. The control of the water hyacinth in the Reed Marshes is a constant struggle for the marsh-dwelling communities, who must clear the channels of the hyacinth to maintain the navigation and the drainage of the marsh.

The methods of the control of the water hyacinth include the mechanical removal of the mats by the boats that drag the nets through the infested channels, and the application of the plant preparations that suppress the growth of the hyacinth without harming the other species of the marsh. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the reed cutters and the marsh farmers cooperate with the authorities of the region in the management of the hyacinth infestation.

## The Prickly Pear of the Sahrani Margins

The prickly pear cactus that was introduced to the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) margins in the centuries past has become an invasive species in the regions where the cactus has spread beyond the areas of the cultivation. The prickly pear is a cactus that grows in the dense thickets that crowd out the native plants of the [Sunfire Steppe](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sunfire-steppe) and the transition zone between the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh).

The prickly pear is difficult to control, for the cactus reproduces from the fragments of the stem as well as from the seeds, and the removal of the established thickets is a labour-intensive process that requires the cutting and the removal of every piece of the cactus from the infested area. The spread of the prickly pear in the Sahrani margins is a matter of the concern for the herders and the farmers of the region, for the cactus reduces the area of the pasture and the arable land that is available for the use.

## The Management of the Weeds and the Invasives

The management of the weeds and the invasive plants is the responsibility of the farmers and the gardeners who cultivate the land, and the methods that are used in the management of the problem plants are the subject of the continuous development and the refinement. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the farmers and the gardeners maintain the records of the methods that are effective against the most troublesome weeds, and the guilds also maintain the registers of the invasive species that are a threat to the agricultural productivity of the region.

The prevention of the introduction of the invasive plants to the new regions is the most effective method of the management, and the authorities of the ports and the markets inspect the goods that are arriving from the other regions to prevent the accidental introduction of the seeds and the fragments of the invasive species. The rules that govern the movement of the plant materials between the regions are enforced by the officers of the guilds and the customs authorities of the ports.
