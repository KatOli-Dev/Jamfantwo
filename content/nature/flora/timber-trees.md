## Oak and the Shipbuilding of Aethelar

The oak is the most valuable timber tree in the known world, its wood being the preferred material for the construction of ships, buildings, and furniture that must endure for generations. The oak forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), particularly the groves of the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) and the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood), produce timber that is recognised as the finest available for the purposes of the shipwright and the master carpenter.

The oak is a slow-growing tree that requires a hundred years or more to reach the size at which its timber is suitable for the largest construction projects. The oak of Brackenshield is particularly valued for the curved branches and forked trunks that provide the natural shapes required for the frames and the keels of vessels. The shipwrights of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other shipbuilding cities pay premium prices for Brackenshield oak, and the [timber and logging](/content/economy/timber-and-logging) trade of the region is organised around the harvest of this specialised resource.

The wood of the oak is dense, strong, and resistant to decay, properties that make it the ideal material for the structural elements of buildings and ships. The heartwood of the oak is a rich brown colour that darkens with age, and the grain is pronounced and attractive, making it a favourite of the furniture makers who produce the finest pieces for the houses of the wealthy. The oak is also valued for its bark, which is used in the tanning of leather, and for its acorns, which are a food source for the pigs that are pastured in the oak forests during the autumn season.

## Ash for the Shafts of Spears and the Handles of Tools

The ash tree grows throughout the temperate regions of Aethelar, preferring the deeper soils of the river valleys and the lower slopes of the central highlands. The wood of the ash is elastic and strong, properties that make it the material of choice for the shafts of spears, the handles of tools, and the frames of wagons and carts that must withstand the shocks and stresses of heavy use.

The ash is a faster-growing tree than the oak, reaching timber size in forty to fifty years, and the ash forests of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) produce a steady supply of wood for the manufacturing industries of the settlements. The wood of the ash is pale in colour, with a straight grain that makes it easy to work, and it is used in the production of the bows that are the primary ranged weapon of the armies of Aethelar. The bowyers of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) prefer the ash of the river valleys for its combination of strength and flexibility, and the longbows that they produce are among the most effective weapons in the hands of the northern archers.

The ash also produces a timber that is used in the construction of furniture, particularly the chairs and the tables that must withstand the daily use of a busy household. The wood of the ash is not as decorative as the oak or the walnut, but its strength and durability make it a practical choice for the pieces that must serve their purpose without fail.

## Beech for the Workbenches and the Floorboards

The beech is a tree of the deeper soils of the Aethelaran lowlands, growing in pure stands or in mixture with oak and ash in the forests that cover the central and eastern regions of the continent. The wood of the beech is hard and close-grained, with a uniform texture that makes it ideal for the workbenches of the craftsmen and the floorboards of the buildings where the traffic is heavy.

The beech is not as resistant to decay as the oak, and the beech timber that is used in outdoor construction must be treated with preservatives or protected from the weather. The principal use of beech is in the production of the furniture that equips the households of the cities and the towns, where the pale colour of the wood and the smooth finish that it accepts make it a popular choice for the pieces that are painted or stained to imitate more expensive timbers.

The beech nuts that the tree produces in autumn are a food source for the wild boar and the deer that inhabit the forests, and the pigs that are pastured in the beech woods in the autumn months grow fat on the fallen nuts. The relationship between the beech forests and the pig herds of the Aethelaran settlements is an ancient one, and the right to pasture pigs in the beech woods is a valuable privilege that is granted by the owners of the forests.

## Pine and the Softwood Timber of the North

The pine is the dominant timber tree of the northern regions, growing in the extensive forests of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) of Aethelar. The pine is a coniferous tree that grows more quickly than the hardwoods of the southern forests, reaching timber size in thirty to forty years, and the volume of pine timber that is harvested each year exceeds the volume of all the hardwood timbers combined.

The wood of the pine is light and strong, easy to work with hand tools or with the saws of the timber mills, and it is used in the construction of the houses, the barns, and the workshops of the northern settlements. The pine is also the principal source of the timber that is used for the masts and the spars of ships, for the lightness of the wood and the straightness of the grain make it ideal for these purposes.

The pine forests of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are managed by the dwarven communities who have developed techniques for harvesting the timber without damaging the fragile soils of the mountain slopes. The pine of the uplands is slower-growing than the pine of the lowlands, but the wood is denser and more resinous, and the timber that it produces is more resistant to decay. The dwarven foresters mark the trees that are to be felled and supervise the harvest to ensure that the forest regenerates successfully.

## Spruce for the Soundboards of Instruments

The spruce is a coniferous tree of the higher elevations and the colder regions, growing in the mountain forests of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the highlands of western [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The wood of the spruce is light and resonant, properties that make it the preferred material for the soundboards of the musical instruments that are produced by the instrument makers of the cities.

The spruce of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is particularly valued for its even grain and its lack of knots, and the timber from this region is used in the production of the finest lutes, harps, and dulcimers that are crafted in the workshops of Aethelar. The instrument makers travel to the ports of the Stormfall Coast to select their timber personally, and the pieces of spruce that they choose are aged for years before they are worked into instruments.

## The Ironwood of the Heartwood

The ironwood is the hardest and densest of the timber trees of the known world, growing only in the deepest parts of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood). The tree is slow-growing and long-lived, and the wood that it produces is so dense that it sinks in water and resists the sharpest tools. The ironwood is not harvested in large quantities, for the difficulty of working it and the slowness of its growth make it a specialty timber that is used only for the purposes where its unique properties are necessary.

The principal use of ironwood is in the construction of the bearings and the gears of the water mills and the windmills that power the industries of the settlements. The ironwood is also used for the mallets and the hammers of the stonecutters and the masons, for the wood does not splinter or crack under the repeated impacts that would destroy tools made of softer timbers.

## The Management of the Timber Forests

The forests that supply the timber for the industries of the known world are managed by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the timber merchants and the foresters who serve the sovereigns of the regions. The management of the timber forests is a matter of long-term planning, for the trees that are planted today will not be harvested for a generation or more, and the decisions that the foresters make about the composition and the density of the forest will affect the supply of timber for decades to come.

The principle of sustainable harvest that guides the management of the timber forests is based on the concept of the allowable cut, the volume of timber that can be harvested each year without reducing the capacity of the forest to regenerate. The foresters survey the forest, measure the growth of the trees, and calculate the allowable cut that will maintain the forest in a condition of permanent productivity. The harvest that is permitted under the allowable cut is allocated to the timber merchants by the guilds, who distribute the rights to the timber through auctions and lotteries.

The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Aethelar Heartwood have their own traditions of forest management that are based on a different understanding of the relationship between the people and the trees. The elves do not harvest timber in the manner of the human or the dwarven communities, for they regard the trees as sentient beings with their own purposes and rights. The elves take only the fallen wood and the branches that break in storms, and they shape the growth of the forest through selective planting and careful pruning that enhances the diversity and the beauty of the woodland without diminishing its vitality.
