## The Plants of the Cloth and the Cordage

The fibre plants that provide the material for the cloth, the cordage, the thatching, and the weaving of the known world are the foundation of the textile and the construction industries that are the basis of the economy of every region. The fibres that are produced from the plants are of two principal types: the bast fibres that are extracted from the stems of the plants, and the leaf fibres that are extracted from the leaves of the plants. Each type of the fibre has its own properties and its own uses, and the knowledge of the extraction and the processing of the fibres is the preserve of the skilled workers who carry out these operations.

The principal fibre plants of the known world are the flax, the hemp, the jute, and the reed, each of which is grown in the regions where the climate and the soil provide the conditions that the plants require. The trade in the fibres and the products that are made from the fibres is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the weavers, the ropemakers, and the thatchers, who maintain the standards of the quality and the purity of the materials.

## Flax and the Linen of the North

Flax is the most important fibre plant of the temperate regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the linen that is produced from the flax is the most common of the textiles that are worn by the people of the northern settlements. The flax is an annual plant that is sown in the spring and harvested in the late summer, when the stems have reached the full height and the seeds have begun to ripen.

The process of the extraction of the fibre from the flax is called the retting, and the retting involves the soaking of the flax stems in the water for a period of several weeks, during which the natural processes of decay break down the substances that bind the fibres to the woody core of the stem. The retted stems are dried and then broken and scutched to separate the fibre from the woody waste, and the cleaned fibre is combed and spun into the yarn that is woven into the linen cloth.

The linen of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is recognised as the finest in the known world, and the flax that is grown in the river valleys of these regions produces a fibre that is long and strong and fine, the ideal material for the production of the cloth that is used in the garments of the wealthy and the sailcloth of the ships.

## Hemp and the Cordage of the Ships

Hemp is the second fibre plant of the northern regions, and the hemp is grown for the fibre that is used in the production of the cordage, the rope, and the canvas that the ships of the coastal cities require. The hemp is a tall and robust plant that grows to a height of ten feet or more, and the fibre that is extracted from the stems of the hemp is stronger and more durable than the fibre of the flax.

The processing of the hemp is similar to the processing of the flax, but the hemp stems are thicker and the retting takes longer. The fibre that is extracted from the hemp is coarser than the flax fibre, and the hemp fibre is used in the production of the ropes that must withstand the stress of the heavy loads and the canvas that must endure the weather of the open sea.

The hemp that is grown in the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is the principal source of the cordage for the ships of the [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) fleet, and the ropemakers of these regions are among the most skilled in the known world. The ropes that are produced in the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) are used in the rigging of the largest vessels, and the quality of the Northern Marches rope is recognised throughout the maritime world.

## The Jute of the Green Crescent

The jute that is grown in the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) is a fibre plant that produces a soft and strong fibre that is used in the production of the sacking, the carpeting, and the coarse cloth of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) settlements. The jute is a tall and fast-growing plant that is harvested in the summer, and the fibre is extracted from the stems by the retting process that is similar to the retting of the flax and the hemp.

The jute fibre is not as strong or as durable as the hemp fibre, but the jute is much cheaper to produce and the jute products are the standard materials for the packaging and the transport of the goods. The sacks that are made from the jute are used in the transport of the grains, the spices, and the other products of the Sahrani interior, and the trade in the jute sacks is a significant part of the economy of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent).

## The Reed of the Greywash Marshes

The reed that grows in the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) is the most important of the thatching materials in the known world, and the reed is also used in the production of the mats, the baskets, and the paper that are produced in the marsh settlements. The reed is a tall and hollow-stemmed grass that grows to a height of twelve feet or more, and the stems of the reed are harvested in the winter when the plant is dead and dry.

The thatching reed of the Greywash is the finest in the known world, and the roofs that are thatched with the Greywash reed are durable and watertight, lasting for thirty years or more before they must be replaced. The reed is also used in the production of the mats that cover the floors of the houses and the baskets that are used in the transport and the storage of the goods.

The cutting of the reed in the marshes is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the thatchers and the reed merchants, who manage the harvest of the reed to ensure that the reed beds are not depleted and that the wildlife of the marsh is not harmed.

## The Papyrus of the Sahrani Pools

The papyrus that grows in the shallow pools and the slow-moving waterways of the Sahrani interior is a plant that has been used for the production of the writing material since the earliest times. The papyrus plant is a tall and triangular-stemmed sedge that grows to a height of fifteen feet or more, and the stems of the papyrus are used in the production of the paper that is used by the scribes of the [Reading Cloisters](/content/religion/cloisters-of-reading) for the production of the manuscripts.

The production of the papyrus paper is a skilled process that involves the cutting of the stems of the papyrus into thin strips, the laying of the strips in the overlapping layers, and the pressing and the drying of the strips into a sheet that is smooth and durable. The papyrus paper that is produced in the Sahrani interior is the finest in the known world, and the manuscripts that are written on the Sahrani papyrus are valued for the quality of the writing surface.

## The Yucca of the Desert Margins

The yucca that grows in the desert margins of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the [Sunfire Steppe](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sunfire-steppe) is a plant that produces a fibre from the leaves that is used in the production of the cordage and the coarse cloth of the desert peoples. The yucca is a drought-tolerant plant that grows in the arid regions where the other fibre plants cannot survive, and the fibre that is produced from the yucca leaves is strong and durable.

The processing of the yucca fibre involves the beating of the leaves to separate the fibre from the fleshy tissue, and the cleaning and the drying of the fibre before it is spun into the yarn. The yucca fibre is used in the production of the ropes and the nets that the desert peoples use in the daily life, and the fibre is also used in the production of the sandals and the mats that are the common products of the desert settlements.

## The Nettle of the Northern Woods

The nettle that grows in the waste places and the disturbed soils of the northern woods is a plant that has been used for the production of the fibre since the earliest times. The nettle produces a bast fibre that is similar to the flax fibre, and the nettle fibre is used in the production of the cloth and the cordage of the northern settlements.

The processing of the nettle fibre is similar to the processing of the flax, but the nettle stems are harvested in the autumn when the plant is dead and the fibre is easier to extract. The nettle fibre is not as strong as the hemp fibre, but the nettle cloth is soft and comfortable and the nettle is used in the production of the undergarments and the household linens of the northern settlements.

## The Regulation of the Fibre Trade

The trade in the fibres and the products that are made from the fibres is regulated by the guilds of the weavers, the ropemakers, and the thatchers, who maintain the standards of the quality and the purity of the materials. The guilds of the weavers in [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other cities of the coast are the principal regulators of the fibre trade, and the guilds set the prices and the standards for the raw fibres and the finished products.

The export of the fibres from the regions of the production to the markets of the other continents is a significant part of the intercontinental trade, and the merchants who deal in the fibres are among the most active of the trading community. The fibres of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and Sahrana are traded in the markets of all three continents, and the quality of the fibres from each region is the subject of the continuous assessment and comparison.
