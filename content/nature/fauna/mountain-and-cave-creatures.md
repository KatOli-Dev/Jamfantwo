## The Animals of the High and the Dark Places

The mountain and the cave creatures of the known world are the animals that have adapted to the conditions of the high elevations and the underground environment, and these animals are among the most specialised and the most remarkable of the fauna of the three continents. The mountains of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) and the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the caves that lie beneath the surface of the earth support the communities of the animals that are found nowhere else in the known world.

The animals of the mountains are the creatures of the steep and the rocky terrain, and the animals have developed the adaptations that allow the animals to survive in the conditions of the low temperatures, the high winds, and the thin air. The animals of the caves are the creatures of the darkness and the silence, and the caves are the habitats of the animals that have lost the sight and the colour over the generations of the life in the dark.

## The Mountain Goats of the Crown Range

The mountain goats of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) are the most typical of the mountain animals, and the mountain goats are the herbivores of the high elevations that graze on the sparse vegetation of the alpine zone. The mountain goat is a sure-footed and agile animal that can climb the steepest and the most treacherous of the slopes with the ease that is remarkable for the size of the animal.

The mountain goat is a stocky and powerful animal with the thick coat of the white hair that protects the animal from the cold and the wind of the high elevations. The mountain goat lives in the herds that are led by the older females, and the herds move through the range in the patterns that follow the growth of the vegetation and the availability of the shelter.

## The Morvind Snow Cat of the Alpine Zone

The [Morvind snow cat](/content/species/beasts/morvind-snow-cat) of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is the apex predator of the alpine zone, a large and powerful feline that hunts the mountain goat and the other animals of the high elevations. The snow cat is a solitary and elusive animal that is adapted to the conditions of the snow and the cold, and the white coat of the snow cat provides the camouflage that conceals the cat from the prey and the observer.

The snow cat is the most dangerous of the predators of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands), and the snow cat is the animal that the herders of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) and the dwarven communities of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) must protect the herds from.

## The Marmots and the Pikas of the Talus Slopes

The marmots and the pikas of the talus slopes of the mountains are the small herbivores that are the prey of the eagles and the snow cats and the other predators of the high elevations. The marmot is a large rodent that lives in the colonies in the burrows that the marmot excavates in the talus and the meadows of the alpine zone.

The marmot is a social animal that lives in the family groups, and the marmot is the animal whose whistle is the characteristic sound of the alpine summer. The marmot hibernates through the winter in the burrow that is insulated by the snow, and the marmot emerges in the spring when the snow has melted and the vegetation has begun to grow.

## The Cave Fish of the Dwarven Depths

The cave fish of the deep passages of the dwarven holds are the animals that have adapted to the conditions of the complete darkness and the constant temperature of the underground environment. The cave fish are the small and the pale fish that live in the underground streams and the pools that flow through the caves, and the cave fish have lost the eyes and the pigment over the generations of the life in the dark.

The cave fish are the source of the protein that supplements the diet of the dwarven communities that live in the deep holds. The fish are caught in the nets and the traps that are set in the underground streams, and the fish are eaten fresh or preserved for the winter.

## The Bats of the Cave Passages

The bats of the cave passages of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) are the mammals of the dark, and the bats are the animals that navigate the caves by the echo that the bats emit. The bats are the small and the agile flyers that hunt the insects of the cave passages and the forest edges, and the bats are the essential components of the cave ecosystems.

The bats are also the animals that are valued by the dwarven communities for the guano that the bats produce. The guano of the bats is used as the fertiliser that enriches the soils of the underground gardens where the dwarven fungi are cultivated.

## The Mountain Lions of the Low Elevations

The mountain lions of the lower elevations of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) and the [Whitewall Range](/content/location/natural/feature/whitewall-range) are the large predators that hunt the deer and the other herbivores of the mountain forests. The mountain lion is a powerful and stealthy animal that stalks the prey through the forest and the rocky terrain, and the mountain lion is the animal that the farmers and the herders of the mountain regions must protect the livestock from.

## The Underground Creatures of the Deep Caves

The deepest caves of the known world are the habitats of the animals that are not found anywhere else, and the creatures of the deep caves are the subjects of the curiosity and the study for the naturalists and the scholars who seek to understand the diversity of the life.

The [Kharesh Glass Spider](/content/species/beasts/kharesh-glass-spider) of the caves of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) is the most famous of the cave creatures, a large and venomous spider that weaves the webs of the glass-like threads that are the most beautiful and the most dangerous of the products of the cave.

## The Regulation of the Mountain and the Cave Hunting

The hunting of the mountain and the cave creatures is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the hunters and the trappers and the authorities of the dwarven holds. The hunting of the mountain goat and the snow cat is restricted to the holders of the licences and the permits that are granted by the authorities.

The trade in the pelts and the other products of the mountain and the cave creatures is regulated by the guilds, and the guilds maintain the standards of the quality of the products that are sold in the markets.
