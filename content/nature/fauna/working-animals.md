## The Beasts of the Labour and the Burden

The working animals that serve the peoples of the known world are the beasts that carry the loads, pull the ploughs, and power the mills that are the foundation of the economy of every region. The working animals are the oxen, the donkeys, the mules, and the other beasts of burden that are used in the agriculture, the transport, and the industry of the settlements.

The working animals are the most valued of the domesticated animals after the horse, and the care and the management of the working animals is the principal concern of the farmers and the merchants who depend upon the animals for the livelihood. The working animals are fed and housed and worked according to the schedules and the methods that have been developed over the centuries of the experience.

## The Oxen of the Fields and the Forests

The ox is the most important of the working animals in the agriculture of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the ox is used for the pulling of the ploughs and the harrows and the wagons that are the implements of the farming and the transport. The ox is a castrated male of the cattle that has been trained for the work, and the ox is stronger and more patient than the horse and less expensive to maintain.

The oxen that are used in the farming of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are the largest and the strongest of the oxen in the known world, and the teams of the oxen that pull the heavy ploughs through the deep soils of the river valleys are the foundation of the productivity of the region.

The oxen are also used in the logging industry of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood), where the oxen pull the felled trees from the forest to the roads and the rivers. The ox is the ideal animal for this work, for the ox can move the heavy loads through the rough and the muddy terrain where the horses would struggle.

The training of the ox begins in the calfhood, when the young animal is handled and taught to accept the yoke. The training of the ox to respond to the commands of the driver takes months, and the ox that is well trained is a valuable asset that serves the farmer for many years.

## The Donkeys of the Sahrani Interior

The donkeys that are used in the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) are the beasts of burden that carry the goods of the caravan trade across the desert and the coastal plains. The donkey is a small and hardy animal that can survive on the sparse vegetation of the desert, and the donkey can carry the loads for the long distances without the water.

The donkeys are used in the caravans that carry the [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) from the interior to the coast, and the trains of the donkeys are the common sight on the [Kharesh Caravan Way](/content/location/route/trade/kharesh-caravan-way) and the [Sahrani Desert Trail](/content/location/route/trade/sahrani-desert-trail). The donkey is also used in the farming of the coastal settlements, where the donkey pulls the carts and the ploughs that are used in the cultivation of the fields.

## The Mules of the Mountain Passes

The mule is the hybrid of the horse and the donkey, and the mule combines the strength and the size of the horse with the hardiness and the endurance of the donkey. The mule is the ideal animal for the transport of the goods through the mountain passes and the rough terrain of the uplands.

The mules that are used in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) of Aethelar are the principal means of the transport in the regions where the roads are steep and the wagons cannot pass. The mule trains carry the ores and the metals from the mines in the mountains to the forges and the markets in the valleys, and the mules also carry the goods that are traded between the settlements of the uplands.

The breeding of the mules is the work of the specialists who maintain the herds of the mares and the jacks that produce the mules for the trade. The mule is sterile and cannot reproduce, and every mule that is used in the work must be produced from the breeding of the horse and the donkey.

## The Water Buffalo of the Green Crescent

The water buffalo that are used in the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of Sahrana are the animals of the rice paddies and the irrigated fields, and the water buffalo are used for the pulling of the ploughs through the flooded fields where the oxen and the horses cannot work.

The water buffalo is a large and powerful animal that is adapted to the conditions of the wet and the warm environment. The buffalo is covered in the sparse hair and the skin is thick, and the buffalo is able to tolerate the heat and the humidity of the Sahrani coast.

The water buffalo is also valued for the milk that is produced, and the milk of the water buffalo is richer and creamier than the milk of the cow. The cheese that is produced from the milk of the water buffalo in the Sahrani settlements is the soft cheese that is the staple of the diet of the coastal peoples.

## The Dogs of the Sledge and the Herd

The dogs that are used as the working animals in the northern regions of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) are the animals of the sledge and the herd. The sledge dogs are the large and powerful breeds that pull the sledges through the snow and the ice of the winter, and the sledge dogs are the principal means of the transport in the northern settlements when the snow covers the ground.

The herding dogs of the Aethelaran plains are the breeds that work with the flocks of the sheep and the herds of the cattle, and the dogs are trained to move the animals and to protect the herds from the predators. The herding dog is the companion of the shepherd and the cowherd, and the bond between the dog and the human is one of the oldest of the relationships between the species.

## The Management of the Working Animals

The management of the working animals is the principal concern of the farmers and the merchants who depend upon the animals for the livelihood. The working animals must be fed and housed and rested according to the schedules that maintain the health and the strength of the animals.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the teamsters and the caravaneers regulate the use of the working animals in the transport industry, and the guilds set the standards for the treatment of the animals and the loads that the animals may be required to carry. The guilds also regulate the trade in the working animals, and the guilds maintain the standards of the quality of the animals that are sold in the markets.
