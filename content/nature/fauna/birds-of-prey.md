## The Raptors of the Skies

The birds of prey that inhabit the skies of the known world are the hunters of the air, and the hawks, the falcons, and the eagles that are used in the pursuit of the game are the partners of the falconers who practice the ancient art of the hunting with the raptors. The birds of prey are also the wild predators that play the essential role in the control of the populations of the smaller animals, and the presence of the raptors in the landscape is the mark of the healthy ecosystem.

The raptors that are used in the falconry are the peregrine falcon, the goshawk, and the golden eagle, each of which has the qualities and the characteristics that make the bird suitable for the different types of the hunting.

## The Peregrine Falcon of the Stormfall Coast

The peregrine falcon of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is the most valued of the birds of prey for the falconry, and the peregrine is the bird that is used in the hunting of the game birds of the coastal marshes and the open fields. The peregrine is a medium-sized falcon that is distinguished by the speed and the agility of the flight, and the peregrine is the fastest of the birds of the known world.

The peregrine falcon is taken from the nests on the cliffs of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) and the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast), and the young falcons are trained by the falconers who are the members of the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the falconers. The training of the falcon is the process that takes the months of the patient work, and the falcon that is well trained is the most valued of the possessions of the falconer.

The peregrine is used in the hunting of the heron and the duck and the pheasant, and the falcon is flown from the fist of the falconer to pursue the game that is flushed from the cover. The falcon strikes the prey in the air and returns to the falconer when the prey is taken.

## The Goshawk of the Aethelar Heartwood

The goshawk of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is the bird of the forest, and the goshawk is used in the hunting of the game that lives in the woodland and the thickets. The goshawk is a large and powerful hawk that can fly through the dense forest with the speed and the precision that are remarkable for the size of the bird.

The goshawk is taken from the nests of the Heartwood by the elven falconers who have the knowledge of the locations of the nests and the habits of the bird. The goshawk is the bird that is used in the hunting of the hare and the pheasant and the woodcock, and the goshawk is the most valued of the forest raptors for the falconry.

## The Golden Eagle of the Morvind Uplands

The golden eagle of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is the largest of the birds of prey that are used in the falconry, and the golden eagle is the bird that is used in the hunting of the larger game of the mountains and the uplands. The golden eagle is a massive and powerful bird with the wingspan that can reach the eight feet or more, and the golden eagle can take the prey as large as the mountain goat and the young reindeer.

The golden eagle is the bird of the dwarven falconers of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds), and the dwarven falconers have developed the traditions of the eagle hunting that are among the most distinctive of the cultural practices of the northern peoples. The golden eagle is taken from the eyrie on the cliffs of the high mountains, and the eagle is trained to hunt the wolves and the foxes and the other predators that threaten the herds of the northern settlements.

## The Wild Raptors of the Plains and the Coasts

The wild raptors that are not used in the falconry are the essential components of the ecosystems of the three continents. The kestrel and the sparrowhawk are the small raptors that hunt the insects and the small birds of the fields and the gardens, and the buzzard and the kite are the scavengers that consume the carrion of the dead animals.

The sea eagles of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) coast are the large raptors that feed on the fish and the seabirds of the coastal waters, and the sea eagles are the birds that are the most impressive of the raptors of the known world for the size and the power.

## The Training and the Keeping of the Raptors

The training of the raptors for the falconry is the skill that is passed from the master to the apprentice in the guilds of the falconers, and the training is the process that requires the patience and the understanding of the nature of the bird. The raptor is carried on the fist of the falconer, and the raptor is hooded to keep the bird calm in the presence of the distractions.

The feeding of the raptor is the careful matter, for the bird must be kept in the condition that is suitable for the hunting. The raptor is fed on the meat of the game that the bird catches, and the diet of the raptor is supplemented with the nutrients that the bird requires for the health.

## The Regulation of the Falconry

The falconry is regulated by the guilds of the falconers, who maintain the standards of the practice and the registers of the birds and the falconers. The guilds also regulate the trade in the birds of prey, and the falcons and the hawks and the eagles that are traded between the regions are the subjects of the permits and the inspections.

The trade in the birds of prey is the commerce that connects the regions where the raptors are taken from the wild to the markets where the falconers purchase the birds for the hunting. The price of a well-trained peregrine falcon is among the highest of any animal that is traded in the markets of the known world.
