## The Wild Prey of the Three Continents

The game animals that are hunted for the food and the sport in the known world are the principal source of the fresh meat that supplements the diet of the populations of the settlements and the camps. The hunting of the game is the pursuit of the hunters who know the habits and the habitats of the animals that they seek, and the traditions of the hunting are among the oldest of the cultural practices of the peoples of the three continents.

The game animals of the known world are of several types. The large game includes the deer, the boar, and the bear, the pursuit of which is the subject of the elaborate rituals and the regulations that govern the hunting of the noble game. The small game includes the hare, the rabbit, and the birds of the field and the forest, the hunting of which is the common practice of the villagers and the farmers who supplement the diet of the household.

## The Red Deer of the Aethelar Heartwood

The red deer of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is the most important of the game animals of the northern continent, and the hunting of the red deer is the principal pursuit of the hunters of the forest settlements. The red deer is a large and graceful animal that lives in the herds in the forest and the woodland margins, and the deer is valued for the meat and the hide and the antlers that the animal provides.

The hunting of the red deer is conducted in the autumn and the winter, when the deer are in the season and the meat is at the best. The hunters track the deer through the forest, using the knowledge of the signs and the habits of the animal that the experienced hunters possess. The deer is killed with the arrow or the spear, and the meat of the deer is carried to the settlement for the distribution among the households.

The hunting of the red deer is regulated by the authorities of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), who grant the licences and the permits to the hunters who may take the deer from the forest. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Heartwood have their own traditions of the hunting that are based on the principle of the taking of only what is needed and the respect for the spirit of the animal.

## The Wild Boar of the Brackenshield Wood

The wild boar of the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) and the forests of the northern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the most dangerous of the game animals, and the hunting of the boar is the sport of the brave and the skilled hunter. The boar is a large and powerful animal with the tusks that can inflict the fatal wounds, and the boar is not a prey that the hunter can take without the risk.

The hunting of the boar is conducted with the spears and the dogs, and the dogs are the essential partners of the hunters in the pursuit of the boar. The dogs track the boar and hold the animal at the bay, and the hunter dispatches the boar with the spear when the animal is occupied with the dogs.

The meat of the boar is dark and rich, and the boar is valued for the flavour that the meat has. The boar is hunted in the autumn, when the animal has fattened on the acorns and the beech nuts of the forest, and the meat of the autumn boar is the finest of the meats of the forest.

## The Plains Thunder Boar of the Great Grass Plains

The [Plains Thunder Boar](/content/species/beasts/plains-thunder-boar) of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) is the largest of the game animals of Aethelar, a massive and powerful creature that roams the plains in the herds that number in the hundreds. The thunder boar is a relative of the wild boar of the forests, but the [plains thunder boar](/content/species/beasts/plains-thunder-boar) is larger and faster and more dangerous than the forest boar.

The hunting of the plains thunder boar is the principal pursuit of the [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) and the human herders of the plains region. The hunt is conducted on the horseback, and the hunters surround the herd and drive the animals toward the waiting archers who dispatch the animals with the arrows.

## The Mountain Goat of the Morvind Uplands

The mountain goat of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is the game animal of the high elevations, and the hunting of the mountain goat is the pursuit of the hunters of the dwarven and the human settlements of the uplands. The mountain goat is a cautious and elusive animal that lives in the steep and the rocky terrain, and the hunter must be skilled in the climbing and the stalking to take the goat.

The meat of the mountain goat is lean and gamey, and the meat is valued by the northern peoples for the flavour and the nutrition that the meat provides. The hide of the mountain goat is used in the production of the warm garments that the northern peoples wear, and the horns of the goat are used in the production of the tools and the ornaments.

## The Hares and the Rabbits of the Fields

The hares and the rabbits that are hunted in the fields and the meadows of Aethelar are the small game that supplements the diet of the villagers and the farmers who live in the rural settlements. The hare is a fast and elusive animal that is hunted with the dogs and the falcons, and the hare is the prey of the hunters who pursue the animal for the sport as well as the food.

The rabbit is a more sedentary animal that lives in the burrows in the banks and the slopes of the fields. The rabbit is hunted with the ferrets that drive the animal from the burrow, and the rabbit is caught in the nets that are placed at the entrances of the burrow.

## The Regulation of the Hunting

The hunting of the game animals is regulated by the authorities of the kingdoms and the settlements, and the regulations that govern the hunting are designed to maintain the populations of the game animals and to prevent the extinction of the species that are valued for the food and the sport.

The hunting of the noble game, the deer and the boar and the bear, is restricted to the holders of the licences and the permits that are granted by the authorities. The hunting of the small game is the common right of the villagers who live in the rural settlements.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the hunters and the gamekeepers maintain the standards of the hunting practice and the registers of the game that is taken in the territories that the guilds administer. The guilds also regulate the trade in the meat and the hides of the game animals, and the guilds ensure that the products of the hunt are sold in the markets at the fair prices.
