## The Animals of the Winter Coat

The fur-bearing creatures of the known world are the animals that are hunted and trapped for the pelts and the skins that the peoples of the three continents use in the production of the warm garments and the decorative objects. The fur trade is one of the oldest of the extractive industries, and the pelts of the northern animals are the most valued for the thickness and the warmth of the fur.

The fur-bearing animals of the known world are of several types. The animals of the northern regions, the sable, the marten, the fox, and the beaver, are the animals that produce the finest furs for the garments of the wealthy. The animals of the temperate regions, the rabbit and the hare, are the sources of the cheaper furs that are used in the garments of the common people.

## The Sable of the Northern Forests

The sable of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the forests of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) is the most valued of the fur-bearing animals in the known world, and the pelt of the sable is the material that is used in the production of the finest garments that are worn by the sovereigns and the high priests.

The sable is a small and agile member of the weasel family that lives in the dense forests of the northern region. The sable is a solitary and elusive animal that is active in the night, and the sable is hunted by the trappers who set the traps in the forest and who check the traps in the regular rounds.

The pelt of the sable is a dark and glossy fur that is valued for the softness and the warmth. The sable pelts are sorted by the quality, and the finest sable pelts are the ones that are the darkest and the most uniform in the colour.

## The Marten of the Aethelar Heartwood

The marten of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is the relative of the sable that is found in the forests of the southern continent. The marten is a small and agile animal that lives in the trees of the Heartwood, and the marten is hunted by the trappers of the forest settlements for the pelt that is used in the production of the garments and the trimmings.

The pelt of the marten is a lighter and a less valuable fur than the pelt of the sable, but the marten pelt is still valued for the quality that the fur has. The marten pelt is used in the production of the collars and the cuffs of the garments that are worn by the people of the moderate means.

## The Fox of the Fields and the Uplands

The fox of the fields and the uplands of Aethelar and Morvind is the most widely distributed of the fur-bearing animals, and the fox is hunted for the pelt that is used in the production of the warm garments and the decorative objects. The fox is a cunning and adaptable animal that lives in the range of the habitats from the farmlands to the alpine zone.

The pelt of the fox varies in the colour according to the region and the season. The red fox of the lowlands produces the pelt of the warm reddish colour, and the white fox of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) produces the pelt of the pure white colour that is the most valued of the fox pelts.

## The Beaver of the Rivers and the Lakes

The beaver of the rivers and the lakes of Aethelar and Morvind is the largest of the fur-bearing animals that are hunted and trapped in the northern regions. The beaver is a large rodent that lives in the colonies on the rivers and the lakes, and the beaver is valued for the thick and the waterproof pelt that the animal produces.

The beaver is trapped by the trappers who set the traps in the waterways and the ponds that the beaver inhabits. The pelt of the beaver is used in the production of the hats and the collars of the garments, and the beaver fur is also used in the production of the felt that is used in the hat-making industry.

## The Rabbit and the Hare of the Fields

The rabbit and the hare of the fields of the known world are the sources of the cheaper furs that are used in the garments of the common people. The rabbit is the animal that is kept in the warrens and the hutches by the farmers who raise the rabbit for the meat and the fur.

The pelt of the rabbit is a soft and a warm fur that is used in the production of the lining of the garments and the gloves and the hats that the people of the moderate means wear. The rabbit fur is also used in the production of the felt that is used in the manufacture of the hats.

## The Trappers and the Furriers

The trappers of the northern forests and the uplands are the hunters who set the traps and the snares that catch the fur-bearing animals. The trappers are the independent operators who work the territories that are allocated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the furriers, and the trappers sell the pelts to the merchants who deal in the fur.

The furriers of the cities are the craftsmen who process the pelts and who produce the garments and the accessories that are made from the fur. The furriers are the members of the guilds that regulate the trade in the fur, and the furriers maintain the standards of the quality of the pelts and the products that are sold in the markets.

## The Regulation of the Fur Trade

The fur trade is regulated by the guilds of the furriers and the merchants who deal in the pelts. The guilds set the standards for the quality of the pelts that are traded in the markets, and the guilds also regulate the seasons of the trapping and the methods that the trappers use to take the animals.

The trade in the fur is a significant part of the economy of the northern regions, and the pelts that are exported from the north to the markets of the south are the valued commodities that contribute to the prosperity of the northern settlements.
