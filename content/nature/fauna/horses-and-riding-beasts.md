## The Horse of the Three Continents

The horse is the most valued of the domesticated animals in the known world, serving the purposes of the transport, the warfare, the agriculture, and the sport in the societies of all three continents. The breeds of the horses are as various as the regions where they are raised, and the traditions of the horse breeding and the riding are among the most highly developed of the cultural practices of the horse peoples.

The horse was domesticated in the ancient times, and the relationship between the horse and the peoples of the known world is one of the longest and the deepest of the bonds between the humans and the animals. The horse is not merely a means of the transport and a tool of the war but a companion and a partner in the life of the peoples who depend upon the horse for the survival and the prosperity.

## The Warhorses of the Aldreth

The warhorses of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are the most celebrated of the Aethelaran horse breeds, the animals that carry the knights and the men-at-arms of the Aldreth armies into the battle. The warhorse of the Aldreth is a large and powerful animal, bred for the strength and the courage and the endurance that the warhorse must have to carry the armoured rider through the chaos of the conflict.

The breeding of the warhorses is the preserve of the noble houses and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the horse breeders, who maintain the studs of the stallions and the mares that produce the foals that are trained for the war. The training of the warhorse is a process that takes years, and the bond between the rider and the horse is developed through the countless hours of the practice and the mutual trust that is the foundation of the partnership.

## The Ponies of the Morvind Uplands

The ponies of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are the horses of the northern peoples, the animals that carry the riders and the goods of the dwarven and the human communities through the steep and the rocky terrain of the mountains. The Morvind pony is a small and sturdy animal with a thick coat and a steady temperament, the ideal mount for the conditions of the upland trails.

The Morvind pony is not as fast as the horses of the lowlands, but the pony is sure-footed and hardy, capable of the carrying of the heavy loads for the long distances over the rough terrain. The dwarven communities of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) use the ponies for the transport of the ores and the metals from the mines to the forges and the markets, and the pony is the principal means of the transport in the upland settlements.

## The Desert Horses of the Sahrani Coast

The horses of the Sahrani coast are the animals of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip), the breeds that have been developed for the speed and the endurance that are required for the travel in the warm and the arid conditions. The Sahrani horse is a lean and elegant animal with a fine coat and a spirited temperament, the mount of the merchants and the messengers and the raiders who travel the routes of the desert margins.

The breeding of the Sahrani horses is the preserve of the wealthy families and the merchant houses of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra), who maintain the studs of the finest animals and who compete in the races and the displays of the horsemanship that are the entertainment of the coastal cities.

## The Wild Horses of the Great Grass Plains

The wild horses of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) are the ancestors of the domesticated breeds of Aethelar, and the herds of the wild horses that still roam the plains are the source of the new bloodlines that the breeders of the region use to refresh the stock of the domestic herds.

The wild horses are the animals of the [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs), who regard the wild horses as the kin and who do not hunt or capture the wild horses for the domestication. The centaurs observe the wild herds and they know the movements and the habits of the horses, and the centaurs are the source of the knowledge of the wild horse that the human and the elven peoples of the plains respect.

## The Riding Beasts of the Desert

The camels that are used as the riding beasts in the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) desert are the subjects of a separate entry, but the [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) guides of the desert also ride the horses that have been adapted to the conditions of the desert margins. The desert horse is a breed that can travel for the long distances without the water, and the horse can carry the rider across the dry terrain where the other horses would perish.

## The Husbandry of the Horse

The husbandry of the horse is the most refined of the practices of the animal husbandry in the known world, and the knowledge of the breeding, the training, and the care of the horse is the preserve of the skilled horsemen who have devoted their lives to the study of the animal.

The breeding of the horse is the most important of the practices of the husbandry, and the selection of the stallion and the mare that will produce the foal is the subject of the careful consideration of the qualities that the breeder values. The horse is not bred in the large herds like the cattle or the sheep, and the horse breeding is the individual undertaking of the breeder who manages the stud of the mares and the stallions.

The training of the horse begins in the yearling stage, when the foal is handled and taught to accept the halter and the lead. The training of the riding horse takes years, and the horse is not ready for the saddle until the third or the fourth year of the life. The training of the warhorse is the longest and the most demanding of the training processes, and the horse that is trained for the war is the most valuable of the animals that the horse breeders produce.

## The Horse in the Economy and the Culture

The horse is used in the economy of the three continents for the purposes of the transport, the agriculture, the warfare, and the sport. The horse is the principal means of the personal transport for the people who can afford the keeping of the horse, and the horse is the companion of the messengers, the merchants, the soldiers, and the travellers who must cover the long distances.

The horse is also the subject of the cultural practices that are the expression of the values and the traditions of the horse peoples. The horse races that are held in the settlements of every region are the occasions of the celebration and the competition, and the prizes that are awarded to the winners of the races are the marks of the honour and the prestige.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the horse breeders and the farriers and the saddlers regulate the trade in the horses and the equipment of the riding. The guilds set the standards for the quality of the horses and the equipment, and the guilds also regulate the competitions and the fairs that are the venues of the trade in the horses.
