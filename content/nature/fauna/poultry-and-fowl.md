## The Birds of the Farmyard and the Village

The poultry and the fowl that are kept in the settlements of the known world are the most widespread of the domesticated animals, providing the eggs, the meat, and the feathers that are used in every household and every industry. The poultry are the animals of the smallholder and the village farmer, and the birds are kept in the yards and the gardens of every settlement from the coasts of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) to the highlands of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind).

The domesticated birds of the known world are of several species, each of which has been developed over the centuries for the purposes that the bird serves. The chicken is the most common of the poultry, and the chicken is kept in every region where the climate permits the survival of the bird. The duck and the goose are the birds of the wetter regions, and the turkey is the bird of the forest margins of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar).

## The Chickens of the Aethelaran Settlements

The chickens that are kept in the settlements of Aethelar are the most numerous of the domesticated birds in the known world, and the eggs and the meat of the chicken are the staple products of the poultry industry of the northern continent. The chicken is a hardy and adaptable bird that can survive in the range of the climates, and the chicken is kept in the yards of the houses and the farms of every settlement.

The keeping of the chickens is the work of the women and the children of the household, who feed the birds and collect the eggs and who protect the chickens from the predators that would take the birds. The chickens are fed on the scraps from the household and the grain that is grown for the purpose, and the chickens that are allowed to range freely in the yards and the fields produce the eggs that are the most valued for the flavour and the quality.

The breeds of the chickens in Aethelar are various, and the breeds that are kept in the different regions are adapted to the conditions of the region. The hardy chickens of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) are the breeds that can tolerate the cold and the wet conditions of the north, while the chickens of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) are the larger and more productive breeds that are kept for the meat and the eggs.

## The Ducks and the Geese of the Wetlands

The ducks and the geese that are kept in the settlements of the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) and the other wetland regions are the birds that are adapted to the conditions of the watery environment. The ducks and the geese are kept for the eggs, the meat, and the feathers, and the birds are also valued for the down that is used in the production of the bedding and the garments that keep the people warm in the winter.

The ducks are the more common of the waterfowl in the domestic flocks, and the ducks are kept in the yards and the gardens that have access to the water. The ducks are fed on the grain and the scraps, and the ducks also forage in the ponds and the streams for the insects and the plants that supplement the diet.

The geese are the larger of the waterfowl, and the geese are kept for the meat and the feathers and the down that are the most valued of the products of the waterfowl. The geese are also used as the guardians of the farms, for the geese are alert and noisy and will raise the alarm when the strangers approach the property.

## The Turkeys of the Aethelar Heartwood Margins

The turkeys that are kept in the settlements of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) margins are the larger birds that are raised for the meat that is served at the feasts and the celebrations of the northern settlements. The turkey is a bird of the forest margins, and the turkeys are kept in the yards and the gardens that are close to the woodland where the birds can forage for the insects and the seeds.

The turkey is a large and impressive bird, and the male turkey is a colourful and dramatic animal that is kept for the display as well as for the meat. The roast turkey is the centrepiece of the feasts that are held in the winter and the autumn, and the turkey is the most valued of the poultry for the special occasions.

## The Pigeons and the Doves of the Cities

The pigeons and the doves that are kept in the cities of the known world are the birds that are used for the messenger service and for the meat. The pigeons are the most important of the messenger birds, and the pigeons that are trained to carry the messages between the cities are the most reliable means of the communication over the long distances.

The keeping of the messenger pigeons is the preserve of the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the pigeon keepers, who breed and train the birds that carry the messages of the merchants and the authorities. The pigeons are kept in the lofts that are built on the roofs of the buildings, and the pigeons are trained to return to the loft from the distant locations where the messages are sent.

The squabs of the pigeons and the doves are the young birds that are taken from the nests and slaughtered for the meat. The squab is a delicacy that is valued by the cooks of the wealthy households, and the squab is the most expensive of the poultry meats in the markets of the cities.

## The Eggs and the Feathers

The eggs of the poultry are the most important of the products of the birds, and the eggs are consumed in every household of the known world. The eggs are used in the preparation of the countless dishes of the cuisine of the three continents, and the eggs are also used in the production of the paints and the medicines and the other products that the craftsmen and the healers produce.

The feathers of the poultry are used in the production of the bedding and the garments and the decorations that the people of the settlements use. The down of the geese is the finest of the fillings for the pillows and the quilts, and the feathers of the turkeys and the peacocks are used in the decorations that the craftsmen produce.

## The Husbandry of the Poultry

The husbandry of the poultry is the simplest of the practices of the animal husbandry, and the poultry are the animals that are kept by the households that do not have the resources or the space for the larger livestock. The poultry are fed on the scraps and the grain that are available in the household, and the poultry are housed in the simple coops and the sheds that protect the birds from the weather and the predators.

The breeding of the poultry is the matter of the selection of the hens and the cockerels that will produce the eggs and the meat for the household. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the poultry keepers in the cities maintain the standards of the quality of the eggs and the meat that are sold in the markets, and the guilds also regulate the trade in the breeding stock that the poultry keepers use.
