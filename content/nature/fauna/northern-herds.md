## The Animals of the Cold Regions

The northern herds of the cold regions of the known world are the animals that are adapted to the conditions of the low temperatures and the short growing seasons, and the herds of the reindeer, the musk oxen, and the mountain sheep are the foundation of the survival of the peoples of the north. The northern herds are the animals that provide the meat, the milk, the wool, the hides, and the transport that the northern peoples require.

The northern herds are managed by the human and the dwarven communities of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the traditions of the herding of the northern animals are among the oldest of the cultural practices of the northern peoples.

## The Reindeer of the Morvind Uplands

The reindeer of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are the most important of the northern herd animals, and the reindeer are the animals that sustain the peoples of the high latitudes. The reindeer is a large and hardy deer that is adapted to the conditions of the cold and the snow, and the reindeer is the animal that can survive in the regions where the cattle and the sheep cannot live.

The reindeer are herded by the human communities of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) and the upland settlements, and the herds of the reindeer are moved in the seasonal migrations that follow the growth of the vegetation. The reindeer feed on the lichens and the mosses that grow in the uplands, and the reindeer can dig through the snow to reach the food that lies beneath.

The reindeer provide the meat, the milk, the hides, and the antlers that are the foundation of the material culture of the northern peoples. The meat of the reindeer is the principal food of the winter, and the hides of the reindeer are used in the production of the warm garments and the bedding that the northern peoples require.

## The Reindeer of the Sledge

The reindeer are also used for the transport in the northern regions, and the reindeer are the animals that pull the sledges across the snow and the ice of the winter. The reindeer are faster and more agile than the dogs in the deep snow, and the reindeer are the animals that the northern travellers use for the long journeys across the uplands.

The sledge reindeer are the larger and the stronger of the herd, and the sledge reindeer are trained to respond to the commands of the driver. The sledge reindeer are the most valued of the animals of the northern herds, and the owner of the sledge reindeer is the person of the status and the prosperity in the northern communities.

## The Musk Oxen of the Northern Tundra

The musk oxen of the northern tundra of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) are the largest of the northern herd animals, and the musk oxen are the animals of the most extreme conditions of the cold and the wind. The musk ox is a large and shaggy animal that is covered in the thick coat of the hair that protects the animal from the cold, and the musk ox is the animal that can survive in the regions where the reindeer cannot find the food.

The musk oxen are not as numerous as the reindeer, and the hunting of the musk oxen is the pursuit of the hunters of the northern settlements. The meat of the musk ox is rich and gamey, and the hide of the musk ox is the warmest of the hides that the northern peoples use.

## The Mountain Sheep of the Valdmark

The mountain sheep of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) and the uplands of the Morvind massif are the wild and the domesticated animals that provide the wool and the meat for the northern settlements. The mountain sheep are the ancestors of the domesticated breeds that are kept by the northern farmers, and the mountain sheep are the animals that are herded in the summer pastures of the uplands.

The wool of the mountain sheep is thick and warm, and the wool is the principal material for the garments that the northern peoples wear in the winter. The mountain sheep are also valued for the meat, and the mutton of the mountain sheep is the meat of the autumn and the winter.

## The Herding Practices of the North

The herding of the northern herds is the principal occupation of the northern communities, and the practices of the herding are adapted to the conditions of the cold and the snow. The herds are moved in the seasonal migrations that follow the growth of the vegetation, and the herders live in the temporary camps that are established in the grazing grounds.

The protection of the herds from the predators is the constant concern of the herders, and the [Morvind snow cat](/content/species/beasts/morvind-snow-cat) and the wolves of the uplands are the principal predators that threaten the herds. The herders keep the dogs that guard the herds, and the herders also use the weapons and the traps to protect the animals.

## The Trade in the Products of the Northern Herds

The products of the northern herds are the meat, the hides, the wool, and the antlers that are traded in the markets of the northern settlements and the coastal cities. The trade in the reindeer hides is the significant part of the commerce of the north, and the hides that are exported to the markets of Aethelar are valued for the warmth and the durability.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the furriers and the leatherworkers regulate the trade in the products of the northern herds, and the guilds maintain the standards of the quality of the hides and the wool that are sold in the markets. The trade in the antlers of the reindeer is also significant, for the antlers are used in the production of the tools and the ornaments that the northern craftsmen produce, and the antlers that are shed by the reindeer in the spring are gathered and sold to the merchants who deal in the bone and the horn.
