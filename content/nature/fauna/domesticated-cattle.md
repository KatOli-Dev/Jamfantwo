## The Bovine Herds of Aethelar

The domesticated cattle that are raised in the fields and the pastures of Aethelar are the foundation of the livestock economy of the northern continent, providing the meat, the milk, the leather, and the draft power that sustain the population of the settlements. The cattle of Aethelar are of several distinct breeds, each of which has been developed over centuries for the purposes that the breed serves and for the conditions of the region where the cattle are raised.

The management of the cattle is the principal occupation of the farming communities in the regions where the pasture is abundant and the climate is mild. The cattle are pastured in the fields and the meadows during the summer months, and the cattle are brought into the barns and the byres in the winter, where the animals are fed on the hay and the fodder that have been harvested in the summer.

## The Red Cow of the Aldreth

The red cow of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is the most common of the cattle breeds of Aethelar, a medium-sized animal with a coat of rich red colour that is the basis of the dairy industry of the northern settlements. The red cow is a productive milker, and the milk of the red cow is rich in the butterfat that is used in the production of the butter and the cheese that are the staple products of the dairy.

The red cow is also valued for the meat, and the calves and the older animals that are culled from the dairy herd are slaughtered for the beef that is sold in the markets of the settlements. The leather of the red cow is used in the production of the shoes and the harnesses and the other goods that the craftsmen of the settlements produce.

## The Grey Ox of the Northern Marches

The grey ox of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) is a large and powerful breed of cattle that is used for the draft work on the farms and in the logging camps of the northern region. The grey ox is a heavy and muscular animal with a coat of grey colour that is the characteristic of the breed, and the ox is capable of pulling the heavy loads that the horses cannot manage.

The grey ox is not a productive milker, and the ox is not raised for the beef, for the meat of the older working ox is tough and the flavour is strong. The principal use of the grey ox is the pulling of the ploughs and the wagons and the sledges that carry the timber and the other goods of the northern region. The ox is also used in the logging camps for the hauling of the felled trees from the forest to the roads and the rivers.

## The Hornless Cattle of the Vintners Vale

The hornless cattle of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) are a breed that has been developed for the ease of the handling and the safety of the herders who work with the animals. The hornless cattle are the descendants of the breeds that have been selected for the absence of the horns, and the hornless cattle are more docile and easier to manage than the horned breeds.

The hornless cattle of the Vintners Vale are raised for the beef, and the meat of the hornless cattle is the finest beef that is produced in Aethelar. The cattle are grass-fed on the pastures of the vale, and the meat of the grass-fed cattle is lean and flavourful, valued by the cooks of the wealthy households and the inns of the cities.

## The Long-Horned Cattle of the Great Grass Plains

The long-horned cattle of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) are the breed that is raised by the [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) and the human herders of the plains region. The long-horned cattle are a hardy and independent breed that can survive on the coarse grasses of the plains and that can defend themselves against the predators of the region.

The long-horned cattle are raised for the meat and the leather, and the herds of the long-horned cattle are driven across the plains in the seasonal migrations that follow the growth of the grasses. The herders of the plains live with the herds, moving the camps as the cattle move across the range, and the life of the plains herders is one of the constant movement and the adaptation to the conditions of the environment.

The milk cattle of the Sahrani coast are not as numerous as the cattle of Aethelar, for the climate of Sahrana is not ideal for the cattle, but the herds that are maintained by the dairy farmers of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) provide the milk and the cheese that are consumed in the coastal settlements.

## The Husbandry of the Cattle

The husbandry of the cattle is the principal concern of the farmers and the herders who raise the animals, and the practices of the husbandry are the subject of the continuous refinement and the adaptation to the conditions of the region. The breeding of the cattle is the most important of the practices of the husbandry, for the quality of the herd depends upon the selection of the bulls and the cows that are the parents of the next generation.

The breeding of the cattle in Aethelar is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the farmers and the herders, who maintain the registers of the bulls that are approved for the breeding and who set the standards for the conformation and the productivity of the animals. The guilds also organise the competitions and the fairs where the best animals of the region are exhibited and where the prizes are awarded to the owners of the finest cattle.

The feeding of the cattle is the second of the principal concerns of the husbandry, and the farmers of Aethelar have developed the systems of the pasture management and the fodder production that ensure that the cattle have adequate nutrition through the year. The pastures are rotated to allow the grasses to recover from the grazing, and the hay and the fodder that are harvested in the summer are stored in the barns for the feeding of the cattle in the winter.

## The Use of the Cattle in the Economy

The cattle are used in the economy of the three continents for the multiple purposes that the animals serve. The milk of the cattle is the foundation of the dairy industry, and the milk is processed into the butter, the cheese, and the yoghurt that are the staple products of the diet of the northern settlements. The meat of the cattle is the most valued of the meats that are consumed in the settlements, and the beef that is produced in the regions of the prime pasture is the most expensive of the meats in the markets of the cities.

The leather of the cattle is the most important of the materials that are used in the production of the goods that the craftsmen of the settlements produce. The leather is used in the production of the shoes, the boots, the belts, the harnesses, the saddles, and the bags that are the common products of the leatherworking trade. The horns and the bones of the cattle are used in the production of the handles, the buttons, and the other small goods that the craftsmen produce.
