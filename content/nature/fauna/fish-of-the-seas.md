## The Bounty of the Oceans and the Coasts

The fish and the seafood that are taken from the seas and the coasts of the known world are the foundation of the diet of the coastal populations, and the fishing of the seas is the industry that sustains the communities of the shores and the ports. The seas of the known world, the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean), and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), support a diversity of the fish and the shellfish that are the principal source of the protein for the peoples of the coasts.

The sea fishing is conducted by the fleets of the boats that range from the small inshore vessels of the village fishermen to the large oceangoing ships of the whaling and the deep-sea fishing industries. The methods of the sea fishing are the nets, the lines, the traps, and the harpoons that are used for the different species and the different conditions of the sea.

## The Herring of the Greyring Ocean

The herring of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) is the most abundant of the sea fish of the northern waters, and the herring is the fish that is caught in the greatest numbers by the fishing fleets of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) and the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The herring is a small and oily fish that travels in the vast schools that move through the coastal waters in the spring and the summer.

The herring is caught in the nets that are set across the path of the schools, and the boats that fish for the herring are the small and the agile vessels that can move quickly to the location of the school. The herring that are caught are preserved by the salting and the smoking that are the standard methods of the preservation, and the salted herring of the Greyring coast is the staple food of the inland settlements and the ships of the trade.

## The Cod of the Northern Banks

The cod of the northern banks of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the deeper waters of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) is the fish that is valued for the white and flaky flesh and for the oil that is extracted from the liver. The cod is a large and bottom-dwelling fish that is caught on the lines that are set from the fishing boats that work the banks.

The cod is caught by the longlines that carry the hundreds of the hooks, and the boats that fish for the cod are the larger vessels that can stay at the sea for the weeks at a time. The cod that are caught are cleaned and salted on the boat, and the dried and the salted cod is the product that is traded in the markets of the three continents.

## The Mackerel of the Auric Ocean

The mackerel of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) is the fish of the warm waters of the southern coast, and the mackerel is the fish that is caught by the fishing fleets of the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip) and the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent). The mackerel is a fast and streamlined fish that travels in the schools through the coastal waters.

The mackerel is caught in the nets that are set around the schools, and the fish that are caught are eaten fresh in the markets of the coastal cities. The mackerel does not preserve well, for the oily flesh of the mackerel becomes rancid quickly, and the mackerel is consumed in the season when the fish are running.

## The Shellfish of the Tidal Shores

The shellfish that are gathered from the tidal shores of the three continents are the clams, the mussels, the oysters, and the cockles that are the common food of the coastal populations. The shellfish are gathered by the shore gatherers who work the tidal flats and the rocky shores at the low tide.

The oysters of the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast) are the most valued of the shellfish in the known world, and the oysters are cultivated in the beds that are managed by the oyster farmers of the coast. The oysters are eaten raw or cooked, and the pearls that are produced by the oysters of the Glasslight Coast are the finest in the known world.

## The Crabs and the Lobsters of the Rocky Shores

The crabs and the lobsters that are caught in the traps that are set on the rocky shores of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) and the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) are the most valued of the shellfish for the meat that the animals provide. The crab and the lobster are the scavengers of the sea floor, and the animals are caught in the baited traps that are set from the small boats.

The crab and the lobster are the luxury foods that are served in the inns and the houses of the wealthy, and the prices that the animals command in the markets of the cities are the highest of any of the seafood. The crab and the lobster are boiled alive and served with the butter and the herbs that complement the flavour of the meat.

## The Greyring Whale and the Whaling Industry

The [greyring-whale](/content/species/beasts/greyring-whale) of the Greyring Ocean is the largest of the animals that are hunted by the peoples of the known world, and the whaling industry of the Greyring coast is one of the most dangerous and the most profitable of the sea industries.

The whaling is conducted by the specialised ships and the crews that are trained in the hunting of the whale. The whale is harpooned from the small boats that approach the animal, and the whale is towed to the shore where the blubber and the bone are processed. The oil of the whale is used in the lamps and the lubricants of the settlements, and the bone of the whale is used in the production of the tools and the ornaments.

## The Regulation of the Sea Fishing

The sea fishing is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the fishermen and the merchants who deal in the fish and the seafood. The guilds set the limits on the catches and the seasons of the fishing, and the guilds enforce the regulations that protect the spawning grounds and the habitats of the fish.

The trade in the sea fish and the seafood is the commerce that connects the coastal settlements to the inland markets. The fish that are caught in the seas are transported to the inland markets in the barrels of the salt and the ice that preserve the freshness of the fish, and the dried and the salted fish are the staple provisions of the ships and the armies.
