## The Animals of the Tidal and the Shore Zones

The coastal and the shore creatures of the known world are the animals that inhabit the zones of the meeting of the land and the sea, and the animals of the shores are among the most diverse and the most specialised of the fauna of the three continents. The coastal zone is the region of the transitions and the extremes, and the animals that live in the shores are adapted to the conditions of the tides, the waves, and the salt spray.

The coastal and the shore creatures are of several types. The birds of the shore are the waders and the seabirds that feed on the fish and the invertebrates of the tidal zone. The mammals of the shore are the seals and the sea lions that breed on the beaches and the rocks. The invertebrates of the shore are the crabs, the molluscs, and the worms that live in the sand and the rocks of the tidal zone.

## The Seals of the Glasslight Coast

The seals of the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast) and the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) are the most common of the marine mammals that breed on the shores of the known world. The seals are the large and the sleek animals that are adapted to the life in the sea, and the seals come to the shore to breed and to give birth to the pups.

The seals of the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast) are the grey seals that are the common species of the northern waters, and the seals breed in the colonies that are established on the remote beaches and the rocky islands that are not disturbed by the human settlements. The seals are hunted by the coastal peoples for the oil and the pelts, and the seal oil is used in the lamps and the seal pelts are used in the production of the garments and the footwear.

## The Seabirds of the Coastal Cliffs

The seabirds of the coastal cliffs of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) are the birds that nest in the vast colonies that cover the faces of the cliffs and the stacks of the coast. The seabirds include the gulls, the auks, and the gannets, and the birds are the most numerous of the animals of the coastal zone.

The seabirds are the source of the eggs and the meat that the coastal peoples harvest in the breeding season. The eggs of the seabirds are gathered from the cliffs by the collectors who descend the cliffs on the ropes that are anchored at the top of the cliff, and the eggs are the valued food that supplements the diet of the coastal settlements.

## The Sea Turtles of the Sahrani Coast

The sea turtles of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast are the reptiles of the warm waters of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and the turtles come to the beaches of the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip) and the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) to lay the eggs in the sand.

The sea turtles are the large and the ancient animals that have nested on the Sahrani beaches for the millennia, and the turtles are the subjects of the protection and the regulation by the authorities of the coastal settlements. The eggs of the turtles are collected by the coastal peoples, and the eggs are the valued food that is eaten fresh or preserved in the oil.

## The Crabs of the Tidal Flats

The crabs of the tidal flats of the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) and the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast) are the invertebrates that are the most common of the animals of the tidal zone. The crabs are the scavengers that feed on the dead and the decaying matter that washes up on the shore, and the crabs are the prey of the birds and the fish and the other animals of the coastal zone.

The crabs are gathered by the shore gatherers who walk the tidal flats at the low tide, and the crabs that are gathered are eaten fresh or boiled for the preservation. The crabs of the Greywash are the blue crabs that are valued for the sweetness of the meat, and the blue crabs of the Greywash are exported to the markets of the coastal cities.

## The Shellfish of the Rocky Shores

The shellfish of the rocky shores of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) and the [Glasslight Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/glasslight-coast) are the mussels, the limpets, and the barnacles that cover the rocks of the tidal zone. The shellfish are the filter feeders that strain the plankton from the water, and the shellfish are the prey of the birds and the starfish and the other predators of the shore.

The mussels of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) are the most common of the shellfish that are gathered by the shore gatherers, and the mussels are eaten fresh or smoked for the preservation.

## The Regulation of the Shore Harvesting

The harvesting of the coastal and the shore creatures is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the fishermen and the shore gatherers, who maintain the standards of the practice and the seasons of the harvest. The guilds also regulate the trade in the products of the shore, including the eggs, the meat, and the shells of the coastal animals.

The protection of the breeding colonies of the seabirds and the sea turtles is the matter of the concern for the authorities of the coastal settlements, and the regulations that protect the colonies are enforced by the officers of the guilds and the authorities of the coast.
