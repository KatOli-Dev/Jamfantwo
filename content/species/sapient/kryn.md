## Origins

The origins of the kryn are among the most alien of any thinking people, for the kryn body is unlike that of any other species and their way of life is fundamentally different from that of the vertebrate peoples. Kryn are an insectoid people, found in the deep underground, and their bodies are adapted to a colonial existence in the lightless spaces beneath the mountains of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). Some naturalists have proposed a distant kinship between kryn and [arachne](/content/species/sapient/arachne), citing the subterranean habitat and the segmented form shared by both peoples, but the differences are too great for the comparison to be sustained, for the kryn are six-limbed, social, and organised in a manner that bears no resemblance to the solitary arachne. The kryn themselves do not preserve oral traditions in the manner of other peoples, for their communication is chemical and tactile rather than spoken, and they have no concept of origin that can be conveyed to outsiders in words.

What is certain is that kryn have inhabited the deep underground of Morvind for as long as any people can account for, and their colonies are among the oldest structures in the known world. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of Morvind have long been aware of the kryn, and the relationship between the two peoples, though distant and largely incomprehensible to both, is among the oldest interspecies contacts in the subterranean world.

## Anatomy and Physiology

Kryn are a medium-sized people, and their form is immediately recognisable as insectoid. The body is divided into three segments: a head, a thorax, and an abdomen. The head bears two large, compound eyes that are adapted to the complete darkness of the deep underground, two antennae that are the primary organs of communication and sensation, and a pair of mandibles that are used for cutting, shaping, and the manipulation of materials. The thorax bears three pairs of limbs, the first pair modified for grasping and fine work, the second and third pairs for walking and climbing. The abdomen houses the digestive and reproductive organs, and the whole body is covered in a hard, dark chitin that ranges from deep brown to black.

The kryn body is superbly adapted to the deep underground. Their chitin provides excellent protection against abrasion and the pressures of the rock, and their limbs are strong and flexible, capable of carrying them across walls and ceilings as easily as floors. Their compound eyes detect motion with extraordinary sensitivity, though their resolution is lower than that of the single-lensed eyes of the vertebrate peoples, and they are adapted to the infrared, allowing them to detect the body heat of other creatures in the total darkness of the deep caves. Their antennae are their most remarkable feature, for they are the primary organs of both sensation and communication, detecting the faintest chemical traces in the air and producing the pheromones that convey information between individuals.

Kryn do not speak in the manner of other peoples, for they have no vocal apparatus. Their communication is entirely chemical and tactile, conveyed through the release of pheromones and the touching of antennae, and the complexity and subtlety of this system is comparable to spoken language in its capacity to convey information. The kryn pheromone language is unintelligible to other peoples, and the spoken languages of the vertebrate peoples are equally inaccessible to the kryn, though limited communication has been achieved through gesture and the exchange of objects.

## Life Cycle

Kryn are short-lived as individuals, with a typical lifespan of fifteen to twenty-five years, but their colonies persist for centuries, and the kryn people as a whole are far older than any individual. They mature with extraordinary speed, reaching physical adulthood within two or three years of hatching from an egg. Kryn queens lay eggs in batches of hundreds, and the eggs hatch into larvae that are fed and tended by the workers of the colony. The larvae grow quickly, passing through several moults before reaching their adult form, and the adults are fully functional within weeks of their final moult.

Kryn populations are organised into colonies, and each colony is a single social organism rather than a community of individuals. The colony is ruled by a queen, who is the sole reproductive member, and the workers, soldiers, and other castes are sterile, their lives devoted to the maintenance and defence of the colony. The colony grows steadily when food is abundant, and a large colony may contain thousands of individuals, all working in a co-ordinated manner that is directed by the pheromone signals of the queen and the workers. The death of an individual kryn is of no consequence to the colony, for the individual is replaced within weeks, but the death of a queen is a catastrophe, for the colony cannot long survive without her reproductive capacity and her pheromone regulation.

## Habitat and Distribution

Kryn are found chiefly in the deep underground of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), where the vast cave systems of the interior ranges provide the space and the stable conditions that their colonial way of life requires. They are the dominant people of the deepest levels of the underground, and their colonies are found at depths that no other sapient people can reach. The [geography](/content//geography) of the underground determines the distribution of kryn colonies, and each colony is situated where the cave system provides access to food, water, and the materials that the kryn use in their construction.

Smaller colonies are reported in the deepest reaches of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the cave systems descend to levels that the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) have not explored. Kryn are entirely absent from the surface world, for their chitin and their compound eyes are adapted to the darkness and the stable conditions of the deep, and exposure to sunlight is harmful to them. The kryn are the most subterranean of all the underground peoples, and their range is defined by the depth and the extent of the cave systems rather than by the surface geography.

## Diet

The kryn diet is dominated by the fungi and organic materials of the deep underground, and the kryn are the primary cultivators of the subterranean fungal gardens that sustain their colonies. The fungi are grown in tended beds, fed by the organic matter that the kryn gather from the cave system and the waste of the colony itself, and the harvest provides a steady and reliable food source that can support a large population. The kryn are skilled in the management of these gardens, and their cultivation techniques have been refined over centuries of colony life.

Animal protein is obtained from the creatures of the deep underground, including the pale, eyeless organisms that share the kryn habitat, and the hunting of these creatures is conducted by the soldier caste. The catch is returned to the colony and distributed according to the pheromone-regulated system that governs all aspects of kryn life. The kryn do not cook, for fire is impractical in the deep caves, and they consume their food raw, digesting it with a system that is well adapted to the fungal and animal materials of the underground.

## Ecology

Kryn occupy the niche of a colonial insectoid cultivator and predator in the deep subterranean ecosystems they inhabit. Their impact on the ecology of the deep caves is enormous, for the kryn are the dominant organisms of the deepest levels, and their fungal gardens and their hunting parties shape the distribution and abundance of all other subterranean life. The kryn management of the underground environment is more thorough and more deliberate than that of any other people, for the colony operates as a single, co-ordinated entity, and the alteration of the environment to suit the colony's needs is the fundamental activity of kryn life.

Kryn have little contact with other sapient peoples, owing to the depth and inaccessibility of their colonies. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) and kryn share the underground world, and the two peoples maintain a distant and largely incomprehensible coexistence, each avoiding the territory of the other. The dwarves regard the kryn with a mixture of wariness and respect, for the kryn colonies are formidable structures, and the two peoples have no means of meaningful communication. [Arachne](/content/species/sapient/arachne) and kryn occasionally share the same deep cave systems, and the relationship between the two is one of mutual avoidance, for the solitary arachne and the colonial kryn are too different in nature for either to benefit from contact.
