## Insectoid Colonists of the Deep Underground

The kryn are an insectoid people found in the deep underground, adapted to a colonial existence in the lightless spaces beneath the mountains of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). Some naturalists have proposed a distant kinship between kryn and [arachne](/content/species/sapient/arachne), citing the subterranean habitat and segmented form shared by both peoples, but the differences are too great for the comparison to be sustained: kryn are six-limbed, social, and organised in a manner that bears no resemblance to the solitary arachne. Kryn do not preserve oral traditions in the manner of other peoples, as their communication is chemical and tactile rather than spoken. Their colonies are among the oldest structures in the known world. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) have long been aware of the kryn, and the relationship between the two peoples, though distant and largely incomprehensible to both, is among the oldest interspecies contacts in the subterranean world.

## Hard Shell, Mandibles, and Pheromone Speech

Kryn are medium-sized, with a body divided into three segments: head, thorax, and abdomen. The head bears two large compound eyes adapted to the complete darkness of the deep underground, two antennae that serve as primary organs of communication and sensation, and a pair of mandibles used for cutting, shaping, and manipulating materials. The thorax bears three pairs of limbs: the first modified for grasping and fine work, the second and third for walking and climbing. The abdomen houses digestive and reproductive organs, and the whole body is covered in a hard dark shell ranging from deep brown to black.

The kryn body is superbly adapted to the deep underground. Their hard shell provides excellent protection against abrasion and rock pressures, and their limbs are strong and flexible, capable of carrying them across walls and ceilings as easily as floors. Their compound eyes detect motion with extraordinary sensitivity, though resolution is lower than that of single-lensed eyes, and they detect body heat in total darkness. Their antennae are the most remarkable feature, serving as primary organs of both sensation and communication, detecting the faintest chemical traces in the air and producing the pheromones that convey information between individuals.

Kryn do not speak, having no vocal apparatus. Their communication is entirely chemical and tactile, conveyed through the release of pheromones and the touching of antennae. The complexity and subtlety of this system is comparable to spoken language in its capacity to convey information. The kryn pheromone language is unintelligible to other peoples, and the spoken languages of vertebrate peoples are equally inaccessible to the kryn, though limited communication has been achieved through gesture and the exchange of objects.

## Queen, Workers, and Colonial Organisation

Kryn live fifteen to twenty-five years as individuals, but their colonies persist for centuries. They mature with extraordinary speed, reaching physical adulthood within two or three years of hatching. Kryn queens lay eggs in batches of hundreds, which hatch into larvae fed and tended by colony workers. The larvae grow quickly, passing through several moults before reaching adult form, and adults are fully functional within weeks of their final moult.

Kryn populations are organised into colonies, each functioning as a single social body rather than a community of individuals. The colony is ruled by a queen, the sole reproductive member, while workers, soldiers, and other castes are sterile, their lives devoted to the maintenance and defence of the colony. The colony grows steadily when food is abundant, and a large colony may contain thousands of individuals all working in a co-ordinated manner directed by the pheromone signals of the queen and workers. The death of an individual kryn is of no consequence to the colony, as the individual is replaced within weeks, but the death of a queen is catastrophic, as the colony cannot long survive without her reproductive capacity and pheromone regulation.

## Deepest Levels of Morvind's Caves

Kryn are found chiefly in the deep underground of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), where vast cave systems of the interior ranges provide the space and stable conditions their colonial way of life requires. They are the dominant people of the deepest underground levels, and their colonies are found at depths no other sapient people can reach. The [geography](/content/geography) of the underground determines the distribution of kryn colonies, each situated where the cave system provides access to food, water, and materials for construction.

Smaller colonies are reported in the deepest reaches of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where cave systems descend to levels the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) have not explored. Kryn are entirely absent from the surface world, as their hard shell and compound eyes are adapted to darkness and stable conditions of the deep, and exposure to sunlight is harmful. They are the most subterranean of all underground peoples, their range defined by the depth and extent of cave systems rather than by surface geography.

## Fungal Gardens and Subterranean Protein

The kryn diet is dominated by the fungi and organic materials of the deep underground. Kryn are the primary cultivators of subterranean fungal gardens that sustain their colonies. The fungi are grown in tended beds, fed by organic matter gathered from the cave system and colony waste, and the harvest provides a steady reliable food source that can support a large population. Kryn cultivation techniques have been refined over centuries of colony life.

Animal protein is obtained from creatures of the deep underground, including the pale eyeless creatures that share the kryn habitat, hunted by the soldier caste. The catch is returned to the colony and distributed according to the pheromone-regulated system that governs all aspects of kryn life. Kryn do not cook, as fire is impractical in the deep caves, and they consume food raw, digesting it with a system well adapted to the fungal and animal materials of the underground.

## Dominant Engineers of the Deep Caves

Kryn occupy the niche of a colonial insectoid cultivator and predator in deep subterranean ecosystems. Their impact on the ecology of the deep caves is enormous: they are the dominant creatures of the deepest levels, and their fungal gardens and hunting parties shape the distribution and abundance of all other subterranean life. The kryn management of the underground environment is more thorough and deliberate than that of any other people, as the colony operates as a single co-ordinated entity, and altering the environment to suit the colony's needs is the fundamental activity of kryn life.

Kryn have little contact with other sapient peoples owing to the depth and inaccessibility of their colonies. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) and kryn share the underground world, maintaining a distant and largely incomprehensible coexistence, each avoiding the territory of the other. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) regard the kryn with a mixture of wariness and respect, as kryn colonies are formidable structures, and the two peoples have no means of meaningful communication. [Arachne](/content/species/sapient/arachne) and kryn occasionally share the same deep cave systems, and the relationship is one of mutual avoidance, as the solitary arachne and the colonial kryn are too different in nature for either to benefit from contact.
