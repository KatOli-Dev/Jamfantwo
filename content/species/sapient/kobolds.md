## Scaled Tunnel-Dwellers of Ancient Lineage

Kobolds are a small reptilian people found in the caves and shallow tunnels beneath the hills and mountains of the known world. Their scaled bodies and sharp senses suggest a lineage adapted to darkness and confined spaces. Some naturalists propose a distant kinship between kobolds and [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga), citing the reptilian form shared by both peoples, though the differences in size, habitat, and habits are considerable. Others argue that kobolds are entirely unrelated to any other sapient species, representing a lineage that diverged in the deep past and has remained separate ever since. Kobold presence in the known world predates written records of the surface peoples, and their oral traditions preserve extensive accounts of the cave systems and tunnel networks they have inhabited for countless generations.

## Fine Scales, Infrared Vision, and Nimble Fingers

Kobolds stand no taller than two-thirds the height of an average [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a compact and wiry frame that is both lightweight and resilient. Their bodies are covered in fine scales ranging from reddish-brown to dark copper and greenish-black, providing natural protection against abrasion and the elements. Their limbs are proportioned for agility rather than strength, and they move through narrow tunnels and rough terrain with a speed and sureness that larger peoples cannot match.

Kobold senses are specialised for the underground environment. Their eyes are large and adapted to very low light, capable of seeing in conditions that would leave a human or an [elf](/content/species/sapient/elves) in total darkness, and their vision extends into the infrared spectrum, allowing them to detect the body heat of other creatures. Their hearing is acute, particularly in the higher registers that carry well through stone and earth, and their elongated pointed ears are mobile and sensitive. Their sense of smell is moderate, less developed than that of dwarves, but adequate for detecting food, water, and approaching creatures through the tunnels.

Kobold hands are small but capable, with long slender fingers suited to fine work and manipulation of small objects. Their feet are similarly dexterous, and kobolds grip with their toes, aiding in climbing and navigating irregular surfaces. Kobold teeth are sharp and numerous, with enlarged canines reflecting carnivorous leanings, and their jaws are strong for their size.

## Eggs, Clutches, and Rapid Maturation

Kobolds live forty to sixty years under favourable conditions, though many do not reach old age owing to the harshness of subterranean existence. They mature quickly, reaching full stature and physical adulthood by their early teens, and their decline in later years is rapid. Kobold women carry their young for approximately five months before laying a clutch of two to four eggs, incubated in a communal nest for a further two months before hatching. The hatchlings are small but well-developed, capable of walking and feeding within hours of emerging, and they grow quickly, reaching adolescence within five or six years.

Kobold populations increase rapidly when conditions are favourable, as the frequency of egg-laying and size of clutches ensure each generation is larger than the last. This capacity for growth is balanced by the high mortality rate of the young and the constant dangers of the underground environment. Kobold populations tend to fluctuate, growing quickly when a tunnel system is secure and declining sharply when disrupted. The rapid turnover of generations has produced a people who are practical, adaptable, and oriented toward the immediate needs of survival.

## Shallow Tunnels Beneath Hills

Kobolds are found in caves and shallow tunnels beneath hills and mountains of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), with smaller populations in the rocky uplands of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). They favour terrain where natural cave systems provide shelter and the rock is soft enough to excavate. Their tunnel networks are typically close to the surface, where air and water are accessible. Kobold settlements are excavated rather than built, with nesting areas, storage chambers, and guard posts arranged in patterns reflecting kobold instincts for defence and order.

In Aethelar, kobolds are found in the foothills of the mountain spine and the hills of the central grasslands, where their tunnel networks are often discovered by [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) or [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) miners. In Morvind, they inhabit the lower slopes of the interior ranges, where volcanic rock provides natural cave systems ideal for kobold settlement. The [geography](/content/geography) of each region shapes kobold communities, but in all cases they seek the same qualities of shelter, seclusion, and access to underground resources.

## Subterranean Fungi and Nighttime Foraging

The kobold diet is dominated by subterranean and surface-foraged foods. Fungi, insects, and small subterranean creatures form the staple, gathered from tunnel systems and supplemented by hunting parties that venture to the surface at night. Kobolds are capable hunters of small game, and their knowledge of the underground ecosystem is extensive, encompassing not only edible organisms but also those useful for tools, medicine, and maintenance of tunnel networks.

Surface foods are gathered cautiously, as kobolds are wary of open air and daylight, preferring to forage at night or in the dim hours of dawn and dusk. Berries, roots, and small animals are taken from the vicinity of tunnel entrances and carried underground for preparation. Kobolds cook their food, maintaining fire in their settlements using gathered wood and dried fungi, and meal preparation is a communal activity that reinforces community bonds.

## Excavators of the Shallow Underground

Kobolds occupy the niche of small-bodied forager and excavator in subterranean and hill-country environments. Their practice of excavation alters the underground landscape, affecting the flow of air and water and the distribution of subterranean life. Their tunnel networks provide habitat for a variety of cave-dwelling organisms, and their management of these systems sustains a complex underground ecosystem.

Kobolds share their subterranean habitat with [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), and the relationship between the two peoples is shaped by competition for the same resources. Dwarves regard kobolds as a nuisance, as their shallow tunnels can undermine dwarven workings, while kobolds regard dwarves with a mixture of wariness and opportunism, trading when they can and retreating when they must. [Goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins) and kobolds occasionally share the same hill country, maintaining a wary coexistence as each occupies a different niche in the same environment.
