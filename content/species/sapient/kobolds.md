## Origins

The origins of the kobolds are a subject of some debate among naturalists, for the kobold body bears the marks of a people shaped by the underground, yet their form is distinct from that of the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) who share their subterranean habitat. Kobolds are a small, reptilian people, found in the caves and shallow tunnels beneath the hills and mountains of the known world, and their scaled bodies and sharp senses suggest a lineage adapted to darkness and confined spaces. Some naturalists have proposed a distant kinship between kobolds and [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga), citing the reptilian form shared by both peoples, though the differences in size, habitat, and habits are considerable. Others have argued that kobolds are entirely unrelated to any other sapient species, representing a lineage that diverged in the deep past and has remained separate ever since.

The kobolds themselves preserve oral traditions that speak of their having been born from the stone and the dark, and they regard the underground as their rightful home. These traditions are extensive and detailed, passed down through countless generations, and they preserve accounts of the cave systems and tunnel networks that the kobolds have inhabited for longer than any other people can account for.

## Anatomy and Physiology

Kobolds are a small people, standing no taller than two thirds the height of an average [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a compact and wiry frame that is both lightweight and resilient. Their bodies are covered in fine scales that range from reddish-brown to dark copper and greenish-black, providing a measure of natural protection against abrasion and the elements. Their limbs are proportioned for agility rather than strength, and they are capable of moving through narrow tunnels and over rough terrain with a speed and sureness that larger peoples cannot match.

Kobold senses are specialised for the underground environment. Their eyes are large and adapted to very low light, capable of seeing in conditions that would leave a human or an [elf](/content/species/sapient/elves) in total darkness, and their vision extends into the infrared spectrum, allowing them to detect the body heat of other creatures. Their hearing is acute, particularly in the higher registers that carry well through stone and earth, and their elongated, pointed ears are mobile and sensitive. Their sense of smell is moderate, less developed than that of the dwarves, but adequate for the detection of food, water, and the approach of other creatures through the tunnels.

Kobold hands are small but capable, with long, slender fingers that are well suited to fine work and the manipulation of small objects. Their feet are similarly dexterous, and kobolds are capable of gripping with their toes, a trait that aids them in climbing and in navigating the irregular surfaces of their underground homes. Kobold teeth are sharp and numerous, with enlarged canines that reflect their carnivorous leanings, and their jaws are strong for their size.

## Life Cycle

Kobolds are short-lived, with a typical lifespan of forty to sixty years under favourable conditions, though many do not reach old age owing to the harshness of their subterranean existence. They mature quickly, reaching full stature and physical adulthood by their early teens, and their decline in later years is rapid. Kobold women carry their young for approximately five months before laying a clutch of two to four eggs, which are incubated in a communal nest for a further two months before hatching. The hatchlings are small but well-developed, capable of walking and feeding within hours of emerging, and they grow quickly, reaching adolescence within five or six years.

Kobold populations can increase rapidly when conditions are favourable, for the frequency of egg-laying and the size of the clutches ensure that each generation is larger than the last. This capacity for growth is balanced by the high mortality rate of the young and by the constant dangers of the underground environment, and kobold populations tend to fluctuate, growing quickly when a tunnel system is secure and declining sharply when it is disrupted. The rapid turnover of generations has produced a people who are practical, adaptable, and oriented toward the immediate needs of survival.

## Habitat and Distribution

Kobolds are found in the caves and shallow tunnels beneath the hills and mountains of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), with smaller populations reported in the rocky uplands of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). They favour terrain where natural cave systems provide shelter and where the rock is soft enough to excavate, and their tunnel networks are typically situated close to the surface, where air and water are accessible. Kobold settlements are excavated rather than built, and the tunnels and chambers are shaped to the needs of the community, with nesting areas, storage chambers, and guard posts arranged in a pattern that reflects the kobold instinct for defence and order.

In Aethelar, kobolds are found in the foothills of the mountain spine and in the hills of the central grasslands, where their tunnel networks are often discovered by [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) or [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) miners. In Morvind, they inhabit the lower slopes of the interior ranges, where the volcanic rock provides natural cave systems that are ideal for kobold settlement. The [geography](/content//geography) of each region shapes the character of the kobold communities, but in all cases the kobolds seek the same qualities of shelter, seclusion, and access to the resources of the underground.

## Diet

The kobold diet is dominated by subterranean and surface-foraged foods, reflecting the mixed habitat they occupy. Fungi, insects, and small subterranean creatures form the staple, gathered from the tunnel systems and supplemented by hunting parties that venture to the surface at night. Kobolds are capable hunters of small game, and their knowledge of the underground ecosystem is extensive, encompassing not only what may be eaten but also what may be used for tools, medicine, and the maintenance of the tunnel networks.

Surface foods are gathered cautiously, for kobolds are wary of the open air and the daylight, and they prefer to forage at night or in the dim hours of dawn and dusk. Berries, roots, and small animals are taken from the vicinity of the tunnel entrances and carried underground for preparation. Kobolds cook their food, for they maintain fire in their settlements using gathered wood and dried fungi, and the preparation of meals is a communal activity that reinforces the bonds of the community.

## Ecology

Kobolds occupy the niche of a small-bodied forager and excavator in the subterranean and hill-country environments they inhabit. Their impact on their surroundings is considerable, for the kobold practice of excavation alters the underground landscape in ways that affect the flow of air and water and the distribution of subterranean life. Their tunnel networks provide habitat for a variety of cave-dwelling organisms, and the kobold management of these systems sustains a complex underground ecosystem.

Kobolds share their subterranean habitat with [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves), and the relationship between the two peoples is shaped by competition for the same resources. The dwarves regard the kobolds as a nuisance, for their shallow tunnels can undermine dwarven workings, and the kobolds regard the dwarves with a mixture of wariness and opportunism, trading when they can and retreating when they must. [Goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins) and kobolds occasionally share the same hill country, and the two peoples maintain a wary coexistence, each occupying a different niche in the same environment.
