## Reptilian People of Warm Southern Rivers

Saurians are a large reptilian people found in the warm southern rivers of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), combining a humanoid upper form with the lower body and tail of a large reptile. Their body suggests a lineage of great age. Some naturalists propose a kinship between saurians and [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga), citing the reptilian form, warm climate, and aquatic habits shared by both peoples, though saurians are larger, more broadly built, and possess legs rather than a serpentine trunk. Others argue for a connection to [lacertians](/content/species/sapient/lacertians), but the saurians are too different in scale and in habit for the comparison to be sustained. Saurians have inhabited the warm southern rivers of Sahrana for as long as any people can account for, and their presence is attested in the oldest records of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) city-states. The saurians and the [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) of the northern coast have coexisted for centuries, their relationship shaped by the difference in their preferred terrain.

## Thick Scales and Powerful Tail

Saurians stand taller than [orcs](/content/species/sapient/orcs) and are comparable in height to [trolls](/content/species/sapient/trolls), with a powerful and heavily built frame. The upper body is humanoid in outline, broad and muscular, with a head, shoulders, and arms. The head is reptilian, with a long powerful snout, rows of sharp teeth, and eyes set high and forward, granting excellent vision both above and below the water. The lower body is supported by two thick muscular legs and a long heavy tail that provides balance and propulsion in the water. The whole form is covered in thick scales ranging from dark green to grey, brown, and mottled combinations, thicker and more robust than those of smaller reptilian peoples, providing substantial natural protection.

The saurian body is superbly adapted to warm river environments. Their legs are strong and capable of sustained movement on land, and their webbed feet and powerful tail make them excellent swimmers, moving through water with speed and force no other people can match. Their lungs are large and efficient, allowing extended submersion, and their metabolism is adapted to warm climates, functioning efficiently in the heat but becoming sluggish when the temperature drops. Saurians are warm-blooded despite their reptilian appearance, insulated by thick scales and a layer of fat beneath.

Saurian senses are well developed and specialised for the river environment. Their eyes are adapted to both above-water and underwater vision, with a nictitating membrane protecting them during submersion. Vision is keen in low light, for the rivers they inhabit are often turbid and shadowed by overhanging vegetation. Hearing is competent in air and excellent in water, and sense of smell is acute, particularly in water, where they detect chemical signatures of prey and seasonal changes in the river.

## Eggs in Sandy Banks, Slow Growth

Saurians live one hundred to one hundred and fifty years under favourable conditions. They mature slowly, reaching full stature and physical adulthood by their late twenties. Saurian women lay clutches of six to ten eggs in nests dug into sandy banks of the rivers, incubated by the warmth of the sun and decomposing vegetation for three to four months before hatching. The hatchlings are large and well-developed, capable of swimming and walking within hours of emerging from the egg, and they grow steadily over many years.

Saurian populations grow slowly, as births are infrequent and the young require many years of care before becoming fully independent. Mortality among the young is moderate, as the rivers contain predators that take their toll, but the size and strength of saurian adults provide effective protection, and the community is attentive to defence of the nesting sites. The saurian community is small and closely knit, bonds between kindred maintained through shared territory and collective defence of nesting grounds.

## Warm Rivers of Sahrana

Saurians are found chiefly in the warm southern rivers of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), where water is warm and banks are thick with vegetation. They favour the broad slow-moving reaches of great rivers, lagoons and backwaters beside the main channel, and flooded forests that line riverbanks during seasonal floods. Their settlements are typically built on the banks, combining structures above and below the waterline, situated where water is deep enough for swimming and banks stable enough for construction.

Smaller populations are found in warm rivers of southern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the climate is mild enough to support their way of life, and in river systems of the Sahrani interior, where seasonal rivers provide habitat shared with [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga) and [lacertians](/content/species/sapient/lacertians). Saurians are absent from cold regions, mountains, and the sea, as their bodies are adapted to warm fresh water and fare poorly in cold or salt. The [geography](/content/geography) of each region determines the distribution of saurian populations, and their range is restricted to warm wet lowlands where conditions suit them.

## Apex Predator of River Systems

The saurian diet is dominated by fish and aquatic creatures. Saurians are formidable predators in the rivers, hunting the large fish of warm waters including species too large or too powerful for smaller predators to take. Their strength and speed in the water make them the dominant hunters of the riverine environment, conducting the hunt by ambush and pursuit, overtaking the swiftest fish with a burst of speed from their powerful tail.

Meat from land animals is a secondary part of the diet, taken from creatures that come to the river to drink, and saurians are capable of overwhelming large prey through sheer strength. Plant foods are a minor part of the diet, though saurians eat fruits and tender shoots growing along riverbanks. They consume food raw, as fire is impractical in their semi-aquatic way of life, and their robust digestion is well adapted to raw fish and meat. Their metabolism is efficient, sustaining long periods between meals: a saurian gorges after a successful hunt and then fasts for many days while the food is slowly digested.

## Riverine Hunters of the Warm South

Saurians occupy the niche of a large-bodied aquatic predator in warm river ecosystems. They are the apex predators of warm waters, and their hunting regulates populations of large fish and aquatic mammals throughout their range. The saurian presence in a river system is generally associated with a healthy balanced ecosystem, as they take what they need and no more, and their hunting is governed by the slow metabolism and infrequent needs of a large long-lived predator.

Saurians share warm rivers with [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga) and [lacertians](/content/species/sapient/lacertians) in portions of their range, and the three peoples maintain a wary coexistence, as all are aquatic predators with overlapping needs. Saurians, being the largest and most powerful, favour the deepest waters and largest prey, while naga prefer the middle reaches and lacertians the shallows and vegetation, and the three separate by depth and by the size of prey they pursue.
