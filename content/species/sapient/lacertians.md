## Origins

Lacertians are a reptilian people found in the swamps and warm wetlands of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the southern reaches of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). Their scaled bodies and aquatic habits suggest a lineage adapted to warmth and water. Some naturalists propose a kinship between lacertians and [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga), citing the reptilian form and warm watery habitat shared by both peoples, though lacertians are smaller, more broadly built, and lack the serpentine lower body of the naga. Others argue for a connection to [kobolds](/content/species/sapient/kobolds), but the differences in size, habitat, and habit are too great for the comparison to be sustained. Lacertians have inhabited the swamps and warm wetlands for as long as any people can account for, and their presence is attested in the oldest records of the peoples who border the marshes. The lacertians and the [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) of the southern coasts have coexisted for centuries, their relationship shaped by the difference in their preferred terrain.

## Anatomy and Physiology

Lacertians stand shorter than [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) but taller than [kobolds](/content/species/sapient/kobolds), with a compact muscular frame suited to both swimming and navigation of dense vegetation. Their bodies are covered in smooth overlapping scales ranging from dark green to olive, brown, and mottled combinations, providing camouflage in the shadowed waters and vegetation of the swamp. The scales are water-resistant and provide natural protection against abrasion and the bites of aquatic creatures.

The lacertian head is broad and flat, with a long rounded snout and rows of sharp teeth suited to a carnivorous diet. The eyes are set high on the head, allowing the lacertian to see above the surface while the body remains submerged, and vision is keen both above and below the water. A nictitating membrane protects the eyes during submersion, and colour vision is good, adapted to the variable light of the swamp. Hearing is competent though less acute than that of surface-dwelling peoples, and sense of smell is well developed, particularly in water.

Lacertian limbs are strong and proportioned for a life alternating between water and land, with webbed fingers and toes that aid in swimming and powerful legs capable of sustained movement through mud and dense vegetation. Their tails are long and muscular, used for swimming and balance, and the whole body is built for the sustained patient effort that the swamp environment demands. Lacertians are warm-blooded despite their reptilian appearance, and their bodies are well suited to warm climates, though they become sluggish when the temperature drops.

## Life Cycle

Lacertians live sixty to ninety years under favourable conditions, maturing at a pace comparable to humans and reaching full stature by their late teens. Lacertian women lay clutches of six to twelve eggs in a nest dug into the muddy bank of a swamp or waterway, and the eggs are incubated by the warmth of decomposing vegetation for two to three months before hatching. The hatchlings are small and numerous, capable of swimming and feeding within hours of emerging from the egg.

Lacertian populations can grow quickly when conditions are favourable, as the size of clutches ensures each generation is larger than the last, and the warm sheltered environment of the swamp provides a relatively safe setting for the young. The mortality rate among hatchlings is moderate, as the swamp contains predators that take their toll, but survivors grow quickly and reach maturity within a decade. The rapid growth and high reproductive rate give lacertian communities a resilience that allows them to recover quickly from losses, and their populations tend to be stable or growing when the wetlands are healthy.

## Habitat and Distribution

Lacertians are found chiefly in the swamps and warm wetlands of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), where the climate is hot and humid, water is warm, and vegetation is dense and abundant. They favour slow-moving rivers, still lagoons, and flooded forests of the coastal lowlands, where water provides both food and shelter and the land is too wet for other peoples to settle. Their settlements are typically built on raised platforms and mounds within the swamp, constructed from materials the wetland provides, situated where water is shallow and fishing is good.

Smaller populations are found in the warm wetlands of southern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the rivers of the southern plains spread into marshes and lagoons, and in the river deltas of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast. Lacertians are absent from cold regions, mountains, and dry interior, as their bodies are adapted to warmth and water and fare poorly without both. The [geography](/content/geography) of each region determines the distribution of lacertian populations, and their range is restricted to warm wet lowlands where conditions suit them.

## Diet

The lacertian diet is dominated by aquatic and semi-aquatic prey. Fish, crustaceans, amphibians, and water birds form the staple, hunted in the water and along the margins of the swamp. Lacertians are patient methodical hunters, capable of lying submerged for long periods while waiting for prey to come within range, and their speed and power in the water make them formidable predators in their element. They are also skilled foragers, collecting edible plants, roots, and fruits that grow in and around the wetlands, and their knowledge of the swamp's resources is extensive.

Lacertians do not practise agriculture, as the swamp provides abundantly without cultivation. They consume food raw or lightly cooked over driftwood fires, and their robust digestion is well adapted to a diet of raw aquatic foods. The lacertian tolerance for spoiled and partially decomposed food is notable, as their digestive system can process materials that would make a human ill, a gift of the warm organic environment in which they live.

## Ecology

Lacertians occupy the niche of a medium-bodied aquatic predator in swamp and wetland ecosystems. They are the dominant predators of warm wetlands, and their hunting regulates the populations of fish, amphibians, and water birds throughout their range. The lacertian management of the swamp is subtle but pervasive: their movements through the water and construction of platforms and mounds alter the flow and vegetation of the wetland in ways that support a diverse and abundant ecosystem.

Lacertians share warm wetlands with [naga](/content/species/sapient/naga) in portions of their range, maintaining a wary coexistence as both are aquatic predators with overlapping needs. Naga, being larger and more mobile, favour deeper waters, while lacertians prefer shallows and vegetation, and the two peoples separate by depth and terrain. [Anurans](/content/species/sapient/anurans) share the swamp environment with lacertians, and the relationship between the two is shaped by difference in size and habit, with the smaller anurans occupying the margins and lacertians the deeper waters.
