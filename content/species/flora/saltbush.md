## The Saltbush of the Kharesh Margins

The saltbush that grows in the alkaline plains of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) margins is a plant that has adapted to the soils that are too salty for the other species of the desert. The saltbush accumulates the salt from the soil in the leaves, and the leaves are shed when the salt concentration becomes too high for the plant to tolerate. The salt that accumulates in the leaves is harvested by the desert peoples, who use the salt in the preservation of the meat and the fish that are the staple provisions of the caravan trade.
