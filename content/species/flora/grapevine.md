The grapevine of the Vintners Vale is the most celebrated of the cultivated plants of the known world, its grapes producing wines of exceptional quality that are traded to every corner of Aethelar and beyond, while distinct regional traditions flourish in the Green Crescent of Sahrana and the vineyards of the Aldreth.

## The Vineyards of the Vintners Vale

The cultivation of the grapevine for the production of wine is among the most celebrated of the agricultural arts in the known world, and the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the region where this art has reached its highest development. The vale is a sheltered valley that lies between the eastern slopes of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) and the foothills of the central highlands, a region of moderate climate and well-drained soils that provides the conditions that the grapevine requires to produce fruit of exceptional quality.

The vineyards of the Vintners Vale cover the slopes of the valley on both sides, the vines planted in rows that follow the contours of the land and that are trained on trellises of chestnut wood that allow the sun to reach the fruit on every side. The soils of the vale are a mixture of limestone and clay that retains enough moisture to sustain the vines through the dry summer months but drains well enough to prevent the waterlogging that would damage the roots.

The wine that is produced in the Vintners Vale is divided into two principal categories: the red wine that is produced from the dark-skinned grapes that grow on the lower slopes, and the white wine that is produced from the pale-skinned grapes that grow on the higher slopes where the nights are cooler and the grapes develop a more delicate flavour. The red wine of the vale is a deep and complex beverage that improves with age in the cellars of the producers, and the white wine is a fresh and lively drink that is consumed in the year of its production.

## The Grape Varieties of the Vale

The grapes that are grown in the Vintners Vale are the product of centuries of selection and breeding, and the varieties that are cultivated in the vale are not found elsewhere in the known world. The principal red grape is the velden noir, a dark-skinned variety that produces a wine of deep colour and firm structure. The velden noir ripens late in the autumn, and the harvest of the dark grapes is the climax of the growing season, a period of intense activity that involves the whole population of the valley.

The principal white grape is the golden muscat, a pale-skinned variety that produces a wine of floral aroma and sweet flavour. The golden muscat is harvested earlier than the velden noir, and the wine that is produced from the golden muscat is consumed in the year of its production, for the wine does not improve with age and must be drunk while it is fresh and lively.

The lesser grape varieties of the vale include the [aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) red and the greywash white, both of which are grown in smaller quantities and used in the blending of the wines that are produced in the valley. The aethelar red is a hardy variety that grows on the higher slopes, producing a wine that is lighter in colour and body than the velden noir. The greywash white is a variety that is grown on the lower slopes, producing a wine that is used as the base for the fortified wines and the spirits that are produced in the valley.

## The Wine-Making Process

The transformation of the grapes into wine is a process that combines the knowledge of the grower with the art of the vintner, and the methods that are used in the Vintners Vale are the result of generations of experience and refinement. The grapes are harvested by hand in the cool of the early morning, when the fruit is at its coolest and the flavours are most concentrated. The harvested grapes are carried to the presses in baskets that are designed to prevent the crushing of the fruit before it reaches the press.

The pressing of the grapes is the first stage of the wine-making process, and the presses that are used in the Vintners Vale are of two types. The traditional press is a wooden beam that is weighted with stones, and the weight of the beam crushes the grapes and releases the juice that flows into the collecting trough below. The screw press applies a more controlled pressure, and the juice that is extracted by the screw press is clearer and less contaminated by the stems and the seeds of the grapes.

The juice that is extracted from the grapes is transferred to the fermentation vessels, where the natural yeasts that are present on the skins of the grapes convert the sugar of the juice into alcohol and carbon dioxide. The fermentation of the red wine takes place in oak barrels that are stored in the cellars of the vintners, where the temperature remains steady through the year. The fermentation of the white wine takes place in ceramic vessels that are kept cool by the circulation of water around them. The duration of the fermentation varies with the type of wine and the conditions of the season, and the vintner must judge the moment at which the fermentation has reached the desired point.

## The Cellars of the Vintners

The cellars in which the wine is aged and stored are among the most important structures in the Vintners Vale, and the cellars that have been dug into the slopes of the valley are extensive and elaborate. The cellars maintain a constant temperature and a high humidity that are ideal for the ageing of the wine, and the barrels that are stored in the cellars are turned and monitored by the cellar masters who are responsible for the quality of the wine.

The ageing of the wine in the oak barrels adds flavour and complexity to the wine, and the vintners of the Vintners Vale select the oak for their barrels with care. The barrels that are used for the ageing of the velden noir are made from the oak of the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood), for the oak of that region imparts a distinctive flavour that complements the character of the wine. The barrels are toasted on the inside before they are filled, the toasting adding a note of vanilla and smoke to the wine.

## The Wines of the Green Crescent

The vineyards of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) produce wines that are different in character from the wines of Aethelar, the warm climate of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast producing grapes that are higher in sugar and lower in acidity than the grapes of the northern continent. The wines of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) are sweet and full-bodied, and they are consumed in the region and exported to the northern markets.

The principal wine of the Green Crescent is the amber wine of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), a sweet wine that is produced from grapes that are dried in the sun before pressing to concentrate the sugars. The amber wine is aged in ceramic vessels that are sealed with wax and stored in the cellars of the merchants, and the wine that emerges after years of ageing is a deep golden colour with a flavour of dried fruit and honey.

## The Table Wines of the Aldreth

The vineyards of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) produce the table wines that are consumed in the daily life of the northern settlements, the wine that accompanies the meals of the working people and the travellers who pass through the region. The wines of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are simple and unpretentious, and they are consumed in the year of their production.

The grape varieties that are grown in the Aldreth are the hardy varieties that tolerate the cooler climate of the northern regions, and the wine that is produced from these grapes is lighter in body and lower in alcohol than the wines of the Vintners Vale. The table wine of the Aldreth is served in the taverns and the inns of the northern settlements, and the price of the wine is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the vintners and the tavern keepers.
