## Nettle Fibre

The nettle that grows in the waste places and the disturbed soils of the northern woods is a plant that has been used for the production of the fibre since the earliest times.

The nettle produces a bast fibre that is similar to the flax fibre, and the nettle fibre is used in the production of the cloth and the cordage of the northern settlements. The processing of the nettle fibre is similar to the processing of the flax, but the nettle stems are harvested in the autumn when the plant is dead and the fibre is easier to extract. The nettle fibre is not as strong as the hemp fibre, but the nettle cloth is soft and comfortable and the nettle is used in the production of the undergarments and the household linens of the northern settlements.

## Physical Characteristics of the Fibre

The nettle fibre is a bast fibre that is found in the inner bark of the stem, the fibre being arranged in the bundles that run the length of the stem and that give the plant its structural strength. The individual nettle fibres are long and fine, the fibres being comparable to the [flax](/content/species/flora/flax) fibres in the length and the fineness, and the nettle fibre being one of the softest of the bast fibres that are available in the northern regions. The colour of the nettle fibre is a pale grey green that is the natural colour of the fibre, the colour being different from the golden colour of the flax and the dark colour of the [hemp](/content/species/flora/hemp).

The nettle fibre is weaker than the flax and the hemp fibres, the breaking strength of the nettle being about two thirds the strength of the flax, but the nettle fibre is sufficient for the purposes for which it is used, the softness and the comfort of the nettle cloth being the qualities that compensate for the lesser strength. The nettle fibre is also more absorbent than the flax and the hemp, the fibre being able to absorb the moisture from the skin and the air, and the nettle cloth being the cloth that is comfortable to wear in the humid and the variable conditions of the northern climate. The nettle fibre becomes softer and more supple with the repeated washing, the cloth improving with the use that the domestic laundering provides.

## The Harvesting of the Nettle Stems

The harvesting of the nettle stems for the fibre is the operation that is performed in the autumn, the stems being cut when the plant has died back and the fibre is at its most accessible. The nettle that is harvested for the fibre is the nettle that grows in the dense stands that are found in the waste places and the disturbed soils of the northern woods, the stands being the natural colonies that the nettle forms when the conditions are favourable. The stems are cut with the sickles and tied in the bundles that are called the beets, the beets being carried to the retting ponds where the fibre is separated from the woody core of the stem.

The harvesting of the nettle in the autumn is the practice that avoids the stinging that the living plant inflicts, the nettle being safe to handle when the plant has died and the stinging hairs have lost their potency. The nettle that is harvested in the autumn is the nettle that produces the fibre that is the easiest to extract, the dead stems being more susceptible to the retting and the processing than the green stems that are harvested in the summer. The [nettle weed](/content/species/flora/nettle-weed) that is the nuisance to the farmer and the gardener is the nettle that is the resource for the fibre-maker, and the nettle that is harvested for the fibre is the nettle that is gathered from the waste places where the plant grows in the dense and the productive stands.

## Retting and the Processing

The retting of the nettle is the process that separates the fibre from the woody core of the stem, the process being the same in the principle as the retting of the [flax](/content/species/flora/flax) and the [hemp](/content/species/flora/hemp) but being completed more quickly due to the softer texture of the nettle stems. The retting ponds are the shallow pools that are dug in the ground and filled with the water from the streams and the rivers, and the beets of nettle are submerged in the ponds and weighted with the stones. The retting of the nettle takes from one to two weeks, the period being shorter than the retting of the flax and the hemp due to the finer and the softer texture of the nettle stems.

The retted nettle is removed from the ponds and dried in the sun, the dried stems being broken and scutched to separate the fibre from the woody waste that is called the shives. The breaking of the nettle is done by the wooden breaks that crush the stems, the breaking being an easier task than the breaking of the flax and the hemp due to the softer texture of the nettle. The scutched fibre is hackled by drawing it through the combs that separate the long fibre from the short tow and that align the fibres for the spinning. The hackled nettle fibre is spun into the yarn on the spinning wheels that are the equipment of the northern households, and the yarn is woven into the cloth on the looms that are the standard equipment of the [textiles and cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) industry.

## The Nettle Cloth and the Uses

The nettle cloth is the cloth that is produced from the nettle fibre, the cloth being soft and comfortable and suitable for the uses that the northern settlements require. The nettle cloth is the cloth that is used in the production of the undergarments and the household linens that are the everyday textiles of the northern people, the nettle being the material that is worn against the skin and used in the bedding and the table linens that the domestic life requires. The nettle cloth is the cloth that is valued for the softness and the absorbency that the flax and the hemp cloths do not provide, and the nettle is the fibre that is the preferred material for the undergarments and the linens that are the personal and the intimate textiles.

The nettle cloth is also used in the production of the sacking and the coarse cloth that are the materials for the packaging and the transport of the goods, the nettle being the fibre that is the alternative to the hemp and the [jute](/content/species/flora/jute) for the purposes that do not require the strength of the stronger fibres. The nettle sacking is the sacking that is used in the transport of the wool and the other soft goods that do not require the strength of the hemp sacking, and the nettle cloth is the cloth that is the economical alternative to the flax and the hemp for the uses that the softer fibre can serve. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the weavers regulate the production of the nettle cloth, the guilds setting the standards of the quality and the prices that are paid for the different grades of the cloth.

## Economic Significance and the Tradition

The production of the nettle fibre is a tradition that has been maintained in the northern settlements since the earliest times, the nettle being the plant that was the source of the fibre before the flax and the hemp were introduced to the northern regions. The nettle fibre is the fibre that is the oldest of the textile fibres in the northern regions, the archaeological evidence and the traditions of the people indicating that the nettle was the plant that was used for the cloth and the cordage in the times before the cultivation of the flax and the hemp became the practice. The nettle fibre is the fibre that is the continuation of the ancient tradition, the nettle being the plant that is still used for the fibre by the people who maintain the old ways.

The economic significance of the nettle fibre is the significance of a fibre that is produced from a plant that is not cultivated but gathered, the nettle being the plant that is harvested from the wild stands that grow in the waste places and the disturbed soils. The nettle fibre is the fibre that is the cheapest of the bast fibres, the cost of the nettle being the cost of the harvesting and the processing rather than the cost of the cultivation, and the nettle cloth is the cloth that is the most affordable of the textiles that are available in the northern settlements. The [agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) and the [textiles and cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) industries of the northern regions include the nettle fibre as a minor but the significant component, and the nettle is the fibre that is the resource of the people who live in the regions where the plant grows in the abundance that the waste places provide.
