## Citrus

The citrus trees that grow in the gardens of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and the other coastal cities of Sahrana are the most celebrated fruit trees of the southern continent.

The orange, the lemon, and the lime are grown in walled gardens where the trees are protected from the desert winds and irrigated by channels that carry water from the rivers of the interior. The citrus gardens of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) are planted in a pattern of concentric squares, with channels between the rows and the trees spaced to allow the sun to reach the fruit on every side.

The orange of Khalisar is a sweet variety that ripens in the winter months, providing fresh fruit in the season when the markets of the north have little else to offer. The oranges are exported to Aethelar, where they are sold at prices that make them a luxury of the wealthy households. The peels of the orange are candied and used in the baking of the elaborate confections that are served at the tables of the rich, and the blossoms of the orange tree are distilled into a fragrant water that is used in perfumery and in the preparation of the finest pastries.

The lemon and the lime are grown for their juice, which is used in the cooking of the Sahrani coast and in the preparation of the spiced dishes that are the hallmark of the region. The juice of the lemon is also used as a preservative, and the ships that carry the trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) are provisioned with lemons that prevent the scurvy that afflicts crews who subsist on salted provisions for months at a time.
