## The Grain of the Known World

Wheat is the most widely cultivated grain in the known world and the foundation of the diet of the majority of its peoples. The wheat grown in the river valleys of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), particularly the fields of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the terraced slopes of the central highlands, is of a quality that commands a premium in the markets of the coastal cities. The two principal varieties cultivated are common wheat, which is the staple of everyday bread, and durum wheat, a harder variety grown in the warmer southern districts of the continent from which the finest pasta flour is milled.

## The Cycle of Cultivation

The cultivation of wheat follows a rhythm that has changed little over centuries. The fields are ploughed in autumn, the seed broadcast by hand, and the young shoots emerge before the onset of winter. The crop lies dormant through the cold months, then resumes growth in spring when the rains return. The harvest comes in late summer, when the stalks have turned golden and the heads have begun to droop under the weight of the grain. The reapers move through the fields with sickles, cutting the stalks at the base and binding them into sheaves that are left to dry before the threshing begins. The grain is separated from the chaff by flailing or by the trampling of [oxen](/content/species/fauna/oxen), and the winnowed grain is carried to the granaries and the mills.

## The Wheat Regions

The regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) that produce the greatest volumes of wheat are the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) and the broad river valleys of eastern Aethelar, where the soils are deep and the rainfall is reliable. The wheat of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) region is less abundant but of a quality that bakers prize for its strong gluten and its ability to rise in the oven. The [prices and livelihood](/content/economy/prices-and-livelihood) of the baking guilds are closely tied to the wheat harvest, and the price of bread in the cities is a matter of constant attention for the authorities who regulate the grain trade.

## Physical Characteristics

The wheat is an annual grass that produces the stalks reaching a height of two to four feet in the mature growth, the stalks being hollow and jointed and bearing the leaves that are the long and the narrow blades that the grasses produce. The head of the wheat is the spike that carries the grain, the spike being composed of the numerous spikelets that are arranged along the central axis of the head and each spikelet containing the two to the three grains that are the seed of the plant. The grain of the wheat is the oval and the hard structure that is enclosed in the chaff that protects the grain from the elements and the predators, the chaff being the papery covering that is removed in the threshing and the winnowing that the harvest requires.

The common wheat that is the staple of the bread production is a soft wheat that produces the flour that is easily milled and that produces the dough that rises well in the fermentation that the baking requires. The durum wheat that is the variety of the warmer districts is a hard wheat that produces the flour that is granular and the dough that is firm, the durum being the wheat that the pasta makers use for the production of the noodles and the other pasta products that the Sahrani and the southern Aethelari cuisines include. The distinction between the common and the durum wheat is the distinction that the [agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) of the known world maintains, the two varieties being the crops that the different regions and the climates produce.

## Varieties and Selection

The varieties of the wheat that are cultivated in the known world are the product of the centuries of the selection that the farmers and the breeders have practised, the selection being the process by which the varieties that are best suited to the conditions of the region and the purposes of the use have been identified and the improved. The farmers of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) have developed the varieties that are adapted to the soils and the climate of the vale, the varieties being the strains that produce the grain of the quality that the markets of the coastal cities demand. The farmers of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) have developed the varieties that are adapted to the warmer and the drier conditions of the southern continent, the varieties being the strains that produce the grain that the Sahrani cuisine uses.

The selection of the wheat varieties is the practice that the farmers and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the seed merchants maintain, the practice being the method by which the quality and the productivity of the wheat are improved over the generations of the cultivation. The seed that is saved from the best of the harvest is the seed that is sown in the following season, the selection being the process that the farmer applies in the choice of the seed that is retained for the sowing. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the seed merchants regulate the trade in the wheat seed and maintain the standards of the purity and the germination that the quality of the seed requires.

## Milling and Baking

The milling of the wheat is the process that converts the grain into the flour that the bakers use, the milling being done in the mills that the [milling and baking](/content/economy/milling-and-baking) industry operates. The mills of the Aethelari regions are the water mills and the wind mills that the technology of the continent provides, the water mills being the mills that are powered by the streams and the rivers that flow through the wheat-growing regions, and the wind mills being the mills that are powered by the winds that blow across the open plains where the wheat is grown. The grinding of the grain is done by the stones that the millwrights dress and maintain, the stones being the circular and the grooved surfaces that crush the grain and produce the flour that the milling yields.

The baking of the bread is the process that converts the flour into the staple food that the diet of the known world is based on, the baking being done in the ovens that the bakers of the cities and the villages operate. The ovens of the Aethelari bakers are the wood-fired ovens that the bakers heat to the temperatures that the bread requires, the bread being the product that is baked in the batches that the ovens accommodate. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the bakers regulate the production and the sale of the bread, the guilds maintaining the standards of the quality and the weight that the [weights and measures](/content/economy/weights-and-measures) of the trade require. The price of the bread is the matter that the authorities of the cities monitor, the bread being the food that the population depends on and the price of the bread being the economic indicator that the stability of the region is measured by.

## The Grain Trade

The trade in the wheat is the commerce that connects the wheat-growing regions to the cities and the regions that cannot produce the wheat that the population requires, the trade being the network of the [cartage and transport](/content/economy/cartage-and-transport) and the [trade and tariffs](/content/economy/trade-and-tariffs) that the movement of the grain from the farms to the markets involves. The wheat is transported in the sacks and the bulk that the carts and the ships carry, the grain being the commodity that the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the cities trade in the quantities that the population demands. The [Grain Fleet Wars](/content/history/the-grain-fleet-wars) are the conflicts that the control of the grain trade has provoked, the wars being the historical events that demonstrate the importance of the wheat to the stability and the prosperity of the known world.

The grain trade is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the grain merchants and the authorities who oversee the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) where the grain is traded, the regulation being the means of ensuring the supply and the fair pricing that the population requires. The [banking and credit](/content/economy/banking-and-credit) systems of the Aethelari cities provide the financing that the grain trade depends on, the credit being the instrument that the merchants use to purchase the grain before the harvest is sold and the payment for the grain is received. The grain trade is the economic activity that the [Aethelari](/content/language/spoken/aethelari) cities and the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) centres conduct, the trade being the commerce that the wheat sustains.

## Wheat in the Sahrani Economy

The wheat of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) is the grain that the Sahrani cuisine uses for the bread and the pasta that the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) diet includes, the wheat being the crop that the agriculture of the Green Crescent produces in the quantities that the population of the southern continent requires. The durum wheat that the Green Crescent produces is the grain that the pasta makers of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and the other Sahrani cities use for the pasta that is the staple of the Sahrani cuisine, the durum being the wheat that the warm climate of the Green Crescent is suited to.

The [sahrani city life](/content/culture/sahrani-city-life) of the coastal cities is dependent on the wheat that the Green Crescent produces, the bread and the pasta that the cities consume being the foods that the wheat provides. The [Sahrani Desert Trail](/content/location/route/trade/sahrani-desert-trail) and the other trade routes of the southern continent carry the wheat from the Green Crescent to the cities and the regions that the wheat does not reach, the trade routes being the network that the distribution of the grain depends on. The [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) trade of the Sahrani coast is partly dependent on the wheat, the grain being the commodity that the caravans carry alongside the more valuable spices and the dyes that the Sahrani trade is known for.

## Cultural Significance

The wheat is the grain that the culture and the mythology of the Aethelari and the Sahrani peoples place at the centre of the agricultural life, the wheat being the crop that the [First-Grain Feast](/content/culture/first-grain-feast) and the other festivals of the agricultural year celebrate. The [halfling harvest tales](/content/mythology/halfling-harvest-tales) and the [human wandering peoples](/content/mythology/human-wandering-peoples) mythology include the wheat as the symbol of the settled life and the civilisation that the cultivation of the grain represents, the wheat being the crop that the transition from the nomadic to the agricultural existence is based on. The [seasonal calendar](/content/culture/seasonal-calendar) of the Aethelari peoples is organised around the cycle of the wheat, the sowing and the harvest being the events that the calendar marks as the milestones of the agricultural year.

The [feasting and dining](/content/culture/feasting-and-dining) traditions of the known world are built on the bread that the wheat provides, the bread being the food that the meals of the rich and the poor alike include, the bread being the staple that the cuisine of the three continents is founded on. The [milling and baking](/content/economy/milling-and-baking) industry that the wheat supports is the industry that the urban life of the cities depends on, the bread being the product that the daily life of the population requires and the industry that produces the bread being the economic activity that the cities maintain.
