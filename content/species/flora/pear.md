## Pear

The pear is a more delicate fruit than the apple, requiring warmer conditions and more careful handling, and its cultivation is concentrated in the sheltered valleys of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the coastal districts of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth).

The pear tree is long-lived, capable of bearing fruit for a century or more, and the oldest pear trees in the known world are found in the walled orchards of the central Aethelaran settlements. The pear is consumed fresh when ripe, its flesh soft and sweet, and it is also used in the production of perry, a fermented beverage similar to cider but with a more delicate flavour. The perry of the Vintners Vale is exported to the cities of the coast, where it is served at the tables of the wealthy as an accompaniment to the rich dishes of the northern cuisine.

The wood of the pear is prized by the woodworkers for the carving of the decorative pieces and the making of the printing blocks that are used in the production of the textiles.

The leaves of the pear are oval and glossy, the surface being smooth and the edges being finely toothed, and the leaves emerge in the spring before the flowers, the young foliage being a bright and tender green that contrasts with the dark foliage of the mature tree. The flowers of the pear are white and borne in clusters, the petals being rounder and more open than the petals of the apple flower, and the flowering of the pear is a brief and beautiful spectacle that lasts for a week or two in the early spring.

The fruit of the pear is a pome, a fleshy fruit with a core of seeds, and the shape of the fruit varies from the round to the elongated according to the variety. The skin of the pear may be green, yellow, or red, and the flesh is white or cream, the texture being soft and buttery when the fruit is ripe. The pear ripens from the inside out, and the fruit that is firm to the touch may be ripe within, a characteristic that makes the harvest and the storage of the pear more demanding than the harvest of the apple.

## Cultivation and Growing Conditions

The pear requires warmer conditions than the apple and more shelter from the wind, and the cultivation of the pear is concentrated in the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the coastal districts of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), where the climate is moderated by the proximity of the sea and the soils are deep and well-drained. The pear is propagated by grafting, the varieties being grafted onto the rootstocks of the wild pear or the quince, the quince rootstock being used to produce the dwarf trees that are planted in the smaller orchards of the coastal settlements.

The pear tree begins to bear fruit in the fourth or fifth year after planting, and the full production of the tree is reached at the age of ten or twelve years. The pear is a long-lived tree, capable of bearing fruit for a century or more, and the oldest pear trees in the known world are found in the walled orchards of the central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) settlements, where the trees that were planted by the founders of the settlements still produce fruit for the descendants of the families that have tended them.

The pruning of the pear is a skilled task that is performed in the winter, the branches being thinned to allow the light and the air to reach the fruit, and the suckers that emerge from the rootstock being removed to prevent them from diverting the energy of the tree from the production of the fruit. The pear is also trained against the walls of the gardens in the espalier form, the branches being tied to the trellises that are attached to the south-facing walls, where the warmth of the stone ripens the fruit earlier than in the open orchard.

## Perry and the Fermentation of the Pear

The perry is the fermented beverage that is produced from the pear, and the production of the perry is similar to the production of the cider from the [Apple](/content/species/flora/apple). The pears are harvested in the autumn and allowed to ripen in the straw, the ripening being necessary for the development of the sugars that the fermentation requires. The ripened pears are crushed in the mills, and the juice that is extracted is fermented in the barrels that are stored in the cellars of the perry makers.

The perry of the Vintners Vale is a clear and golden beverage with a delicate flavour that is more subtle than the cider, and the perry is considered the finer of the two drinks by the connoisseurs of the coast. The perry is served cold in the glasses that are produced in the glassworks of the cities, and the perry that is aged for a year or more in the cellars develops a complexity of flavour that is prized by the merchants and the nobles who can afford the price of the aged product.

The pear is cooked into the preserves and the jams that are the pride of the households that prepare them, and the pear that is poached in the red wine is a dessert that is served at the tables of the wealthy.
