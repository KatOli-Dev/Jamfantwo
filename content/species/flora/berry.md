## Berry

The berries that grow in the wilds of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the uplands of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) are not cultivated in orchards but gathered from the wild, and their harvest is an important part of the diet of the northern peoples.

The cloudberry, the bilberry, and the lingonberry grow on the open slopes above the treeline, where they survive the harsh conditions of the alpine zone and produce their fruit in the brief intensity of the northern summer. The gathering of berries is the work of the women and children of the northern settlements, who travel to the upland pastures in the late summer with baskets and buckets to harvest the fruit that will sustain the household through the winter. The berries are preserved as jams and jellies, or stored in barrels of water that keep them fresh for months. The juice of the berries is fermented into a wine that is consumed in the feasts of the northern strongholds, and the dried berries are added to the porridges and the breads that are the staple food of the cold season.

The berry harvest of the north is not regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) or the authorities of the settlements, for the berries grow on land that is owned by no one and the right to gather them is a customary freedom that the northern peoples regard as a birthright. The berries of the north are a gift of the land that supplements the diet of the settlements and provides a source of sweetness and variety in the monotony of the winter fare.

## The Cloudberry

The cloudberry is the most prized of the northern berries, a large and amber-coloured fruit that grows in the boggy areas of the [alpine pastures](/content/species/flora/alpine-pasture) and the wet meadows of the uplands. The cloudberry ripens in the late summer, and the fruit turns from the red to the golden amber as it ripens, and the ripe fruit is soft and juicy with a flavour that is described as a blend of the apple and the raspberry. The cloudberry is the first of the berries to be gathered, and the harvest of the cloudberry is a race against the birds and the foxes that also feed on the fruit.

The cloudberry is preserved as the jam that is the luxury of the northern households, and the cloudberry jam is served with the cheese and the bread that are the staples of the northern diet. The cloudberry is also used in the preparation of the liqueur that is produced in the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark), and the cloudberry liqueur is a beverage that is consumed in the feasts and the celebrations of the northern peoples. The cloudberry is the most valuable of the wild berries in the trade of the northern regions, and the cloudberry jam and the liqueur are exported to the markets of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the other northern settlements of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar).

## The Bilberry

The bilberry is the small and dark blue berry that grows on the shrubs of the heath and the open woodland of the uplands, and the bilberry is the most abundant of the northern berries. The bilberry ripens in the August, and the fruit is gathered in the quantities that are sufficient for the jam and the wine that are the products of the harvest. The bilberry is a relative of the [berry](/content/species/flora/berry) that is cultivated in the southern regions, and the flavour of the bilberry is richer and more tart than the flavour of the cultivated berry.

The bilberry is used in the preparation of the jam and the jelly that are the preserves of the northern households, and the bilberry is also used in the baking of the pies and the tarts that are the desserts of the northern settlements. The juice of the bilberry is fermented into the wine that is the beverage of the feasts, and the bilberry wine is a dark and the fruity drink that is consumed in the autumn and the winter. The bilberry is also valued for the medicinal properties that are attributed to the fruit, and the bilberry is used in the preparation of the remedies that are believed to strengthen the eyes and the digestion.

## The Lingonberry

The lingonberry is the small and bright red berry that grows on the evergreen shrubs of the heath and the open forest of the uplands. The lingonberry ripens in the September, and the fruit is the last of the berries to be gathered in the season of the harvest. The lingonberry is a tart and the firm berry that keeps well in the storage, and the lingonberry is preserved in the water and the sugar that maintain the fruit through the winter.

The lingonberry is used in the preparation of the jam and the sauce that accompany the meat dishes of the northern settlements, and the lingonberry sauce is the traditional accompaniment to the roasted meat that is the feast dish of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) peoples. The lingonberry is also used in the baking of the breads and the cakes that are the winter foods of the northern households, and the lingonberry is a berry that is valued for the long keeping and the versatility that the fruit provides.

## Preservation and the Winter Diet

The preservation of the berries is a practice that is essential to the survival of the northern households, for the berries are the source of the vitamins and the flavour that are scarce in the winter diet. The berries are preserved as the jams and the jellies that are made by boiling the fruit with the sugar or the honey, and the preserves are stored in the jars and the pots that are sealed with the wax or the fat that keeps the air from the fruit. The berries are also stored in the barrels of the water that keep the fruit fresh for the months, and the berries that are stored in the water are used in the preparation of the dishes that are cooked through the winter.

The dried berries are a provision that is used in the porridges and the breads of the cold season, and the drying of the berries is carried out in the sun or in the ovens that are used for the drying of the grain. The dried berries are light and the durable, and the dried fruit is a provision that is carried by the travellers and the hunters who need the food that is light and the sustaining. The wine that is made from the berries is a beverage that is consumed in the feasts and the gatherings of the winter, and the berry wine is a drink that is the product of the harvest and the labour of the northern peoples.

## Ecological Role

The berry shrubs of the northern uplands are a component of the [alpine pastures](/content/species/flora/alpine-pasture) that provide the food and the habitat for the animals of the alpine zone. The berries are a food source for the birds and the mammals that inhabit the uplands, and the [reindeer](/content/species/fauna/reindeer) and the other grazing animals feed on the leaves and the shoots of the berry shrubs. The berry shrubs are also a component of the vegetation that stabilises the soil of the uplands, and the roots of the shrubs hold the soil that would otherwise be eroded by the wind and the water of the alpine environment.
