## Madder

The madder plant is the most widely used source of red dye in the known world, its roots containing a pigment that produces a range of colours from the palest pink to the deepest crimson.

The madder is cultivated in the fields of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the river valleys of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), where the soil is deep and the rainfall is reliable. The plant is a perennial that produces its roots in the second and the third years of its growth, and the roots are harvested in the autumn of the third year for the best results.

The harvested roots are washed, dried, and ground to a powder that is used in the dye bath. The cloth that is to be dyed is mordanted with alum to fix the colour, and the cloth is immersed in the dye bath for a period that varies from hours to days depending on the depth of the colour that is required. The red that the madder produces is a warm and earthy colour, and the best madder reds are achieved by the dyers who have mastered the art of managing the temperature and the timing of the dye bath.

The madder of the Vintners Vale is considered to be the finest in the known world, and the dyers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other cities of the coast pay premium prices for the madder that is produced in the region. The trade in madder is a significant component of the agricultural economy of the vale, and the farmers who grow the madder are organised into a cooperative that negotiates with the dyers' guilds on the terms of the trade.

## Physical Characteristics

The madder is a perennial herb that grows to a height of two to four feet, the stems being slender and angular and the leaves being arranged in the whorls that are characteristic of the species. The leaves of the madder are narrow and lance-shaped, the surfaces being rough to the touch due to the small hooks that cover the leaf margins and the stems. The flowers of the madder are small and star-shaped, the petals being a pale yellow or a greenish colour that is inconspicuous in the field, and the flowers give way to the small black berries that are the fruit of the plant.

The roots of the madder are the part of the plant that contains the pigment, the roots being long and slender and spreading, the root system extending to the depth of two feet or more in the well-drained soils that the madder prefers. The roots are a reddish brown colour on the exterior and a rich orange red on the interior, the pigment being concentrated in the inner bark of the root and the rootlets that are the finest of the roots being the parts that contain the highest concentration of the dye. The roots of the madder that is harvested in the third year are the roots that produce the deepest and the most intense colour, the younger roots being paler and the older roots being coarser and less rich in the pigment.

## Cultivation and the Growing Cycle

The cultivation of the madder is a long-term investment that requires the farmer to commit the land to the crop for the three years that the madder needs to produce the roots of the best quality. The madder is propagated by the seed or by the division of the root, the seed being sown in the spring in the prepared beds and the divisions being planted in the rows that are spaced to allow the cultivation and the weeding. The madder requires a deep and well-drained soil and a supply of the moisture that is sufficient to sustain the growth through the summer, and the madder is grown in the regions where the soil is deep and the rainfall is reliable.

The madder field is kept clean and the weeds are controlled by the regular cultivation that the farmer performs in the first year of the growth, the madder being a slow-growing plant that is vulnerable to the competition from the weeds in the early stages of the establishment. The madder that is grown in the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) benefits from the limestone soils and the moderate climate that the vale provides, the conditions being the ideal for the production of the roots that contain the highest concentration of the pigment. The [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) madder is grown in the river valleys where the soil is alluvial and the moisture is constant, the madder of the Aldreth being the madder that is the second finest in the known world.

## Harvesting and the Preparation of the Roots

The harvesting of the madder is the operation that is performed in the autumn of the third year, the roots being dug from the ground with the forks and the spades that lift the root system without the damage that would reduce the quality of the dye. The dug roots are washed in the water to remove the soil and the debris, the washing being done in the streams and the troughs that are available in the field, and the cleaned roots are dried in the sun or in the kilns that are built for the purpose. The drying of the roots is the critical stage of the preparation, the roots being dried to the moisture content that prevents the mould and the spoilage that would destroy the pigment.

The dried roots are ground to a powder in the mills that are operated by the madder farmers or the specialist grinders who process the crop for the farmers, the powder being the form in which the madder is traded and used. The grinding of the madder is a dusty and the arduous operation that is performed in the controlled conditions that protect the workers from the fine powder that can cause the respiratory problems that the madder dust is known for. The ground madder is sieved to remove the coarse fragments and the impurities, the fine powder that passes the sieve being the madder that is sold to the dyers, and the madder that is sold in the markets is graded by the colour and the fineness that the powder exhibits.

## The Dyeing Process and the Red Cloth

The dyeing of the cloth with the madder is a process that requires the skill and the experience that the master dyers have acquired through the years of the practice, the quality of the colour being determined by the management of the dye bath and the mordant that fixes the colour to the cloth. The cloth that is to be dyed is mordanted with the alum that is the mordant that is most commonly used for the madder, the alum being dissolved in the water and the cloth being soaked in the solution and then dried before the dyeing. The mordanted cloth is immersed in the dye bath that contains the madder powder and the water, the bath being heated slowly to the temperature that releases the pigment and that allows the colour to penetrate the fibres.

The depth of the colour that the madder produces is determined by the concentration of the dye in the bath and the duration of the immersion, the pale pinks being achieved by the short immersion in the dilute bath and the deep crimsons being achieved by the long immersion in the concentrated bath. The madder red is a colour that is valued for the warmth and the earthiness that it exhibits, the colour being different from the bright and the intense red of the [kermes](/content/species/flora/kermes) and the other insect dyes. The madder red is the red that is used in the [textiles and cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) that are the everyday wear of the people of the northern settlements, and the madder is the dye that is the foundation of the red cloth industry of the known world.

## Trade and the Cooperative

The trade in madder is a significant component of the [agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) and the [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) economy of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), the madder that is produced in these regions being traded to the dyers of every city in the known world. The madder that is produced in the Vintners Vale is the madder that is considered the finest, and the dyers of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other cities of the coast pay the premium prices that the Vale madder commands. The madder that is produced in the Aldreth is the madder that is used by the dyers of the northern settlements, the Aldreth madder being the dye that is the standard for the quality and the reliability that the trade demands.

The farmers who grow the madder in the Vintners Vale are organised into the cooperative that negotiates with the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the dyers on the terms of the trade, the cooperative being the organisation that represents the interests of the madder growers and that ensures the fair prices and the stable markets that the farmers need to justify the long-term investment that the madder cultivation requires. The cooperative also maintains the standards of the quality that the madder must meet, the cooperative inspecting the madder that is sold under its mark and guaranteeing the quality and the origin of the dye. The cooperative is one of the most successful of the agricultural organisations of the known world, the madder growers having achieved the level of the cooperation and the coordination that the other agricultural sectors aspire to.

The madder red is the colour that is used in the garments that are worn in the festivals and the celebrations that mark the seasons of the year, and the red cloth that is dyed with the madder is the cloth that is the sign of the prosperity and the well-being that the people of the north strive for. The madder red is also the colour that is used in the banners and the flags of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), the red being the colour that is associated with the identity and the pride of the northern peoples.

The madder is also associated with the traditions of the [tanning and leather](/content/economy/tanning-and-leather) industry, the madder being used in the dyeing of the leather that is the material of the shoes and the harness and the other leather goods that the northern settlements produce. The madder-dyed leather is the leather that is the standard for the quality and the colour that the leather trade demands, and the madder is the dye that is the foundation of the red leather industry that is a significant component of the [construction and masonry](/content/economy/construction-and-masonry) and the other industries of the north. The madder is a plant that is essential to the economy and the culture of the northern settlements, and the cultivation and the trade of the madder is the activity that supports the communities that produce the dye and the cloth that the known world depends upon.
