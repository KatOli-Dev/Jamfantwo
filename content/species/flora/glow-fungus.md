## The Glow Fungus of the Dwarven Halls

The second fungus of the dwarven halls is the glow fungus, a species that produces a faint inner light that illuminates the passages with a pale green glow. The glow fungus is not eaten, for the flavour is bitter and the texture is tough, but the fungus is cultivated for the light that it provides and for the medicinal properties that the healers of the dwarven holds attribute to it. The glow fungus is dried and ground into a powder that is used in the treatment of the wounds that are slow to heal.

## Habitat and the Underground Environment

The glow fungus is a species that is found only in the underground environment of the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) halls, the fungus thriving in the cool and the damp conditions of the tunnels and the chambers that the dwarves have carved from the rock over the centuries. The glow fungus grows on the decaying organic matter that accumulates in the underground passages, the fungus being found on the rotting wood and the damp stone where the moisture and the temperature are stable and the darkness is complete. The fungus does not grow in the open air or in the presence of the direct sunlight, and the attempts to cultivate the glow fungus in the surface settlements have consistently failed.

The natural habitat of the glow fungus is the deep passages and the abandoned chambers of the dwarven holds, where the fungus colonises the surfaces that are damp and rich in the organic matter. The dwarves who have studied the fungus have identified the conditions that the fungus requires for the optimal growth, and the cultivation of the glow fungus in the controlled environments of the dwarven halls is a practice that has been refined over the centuries. The fungus is grown in the chambers that are maintained at a constant temperature and humidity, the chambers being supplied with the substrate of the rotting wood and the organic waste that the fungus consumes, and the cultivation of the glow fungus is one of the specialised crafts of the dwarven halls.

## Physical Characteristics and the Nature of the Glow

The glow fungus is a small and delicate species, the fruiting body being a thin and spreading mat of the white filaments that covers the surface of the substrate and produces the pale green light that is the distinguishing characteristic of the species. The light is produced by a chemical reaction within the cells of the fungus, the reaction being sustained by the nutrients that the fungus absorbs from the substrate, and the glow is continuous and steady, not flickering or pulsing like the light of a flame. The intensity of the glow varies with the health and the vigour of the fungus, the well-fed and the actively growing fungus producing a brighter light than the fungus that is starved or dormant.

The glow fungus does not produce the large and conspicuous fruiting bodies that are characteristic of the [crimson cap](/content/species/flora/crimson-cap) and the other fungi of the surface world, and the fungus is identified by the light that it produces rather than by the form of the fruiting body. The colour of the glow is a pale green that is soft and diffused, the light being sufficient to illuminate the immediate vicinity of the fungus but not strong enough to cast the shadows or the beams that the torches and the lanterns produce. The dwarves who work in the deep passages value the glow fungus for the gentle and the continuous light that does not consume the air or produce the smoke that the open flames generate, and the chambers where the glow fungus is cultivated are the chambers where the dwarves prefer to work and to rest.

## Cultivation in the Dwarven Halls

The cultivation of the glow fungus is a practice that is unique to the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) halls, the dwarves being the only people who have developed the methods for the deliberate and the controlled growth of the fungus. The cultivation chambers are carved from the rock in the sections of the halls that are near the water sources, the chambers being lined with the wood and the organic matter that the fungus consumes, and the chambers being maintained at the temperature and the humidity that the fungus requires. The dwarves who tend the glow fungus are the specialists who have studied the habits of the fungus and the conditions that promote the growth and the vigour of the species, and the cultivation of the glow fungus is a hereditary craft that is passed from the parent to the child in the families that are responsible for the illumination of the halls.

The glow fungus is propagated by the transfer of the fragments of the living fungus from the established cultures to the new substrates, the fragments being placed on the prepared surfaces and covered with the thin layer of the organic matter that the fungus consumes. The new cultures begin to glow within a few weeks of the planting, the light increasing in intensity as the fungus spreads across the substrate and establishes the colony. The cultivation chambers are arranged in the networks that are connected by the passages, the chambers being located at the intervals that provide the illumination of the routes that the dwarves travel in the course of their work and their daily life.

## Medicinal Uses and the Healing of Wounds

The medicinal properties of the glow fungus are valued by the healers of the dwarven halls, the powder that is ground from the dried fungus being used in the treatment of the wounds that are slow to heal. The wounds that are sustained in the underground environment, the cuts and the crushes that result from the rockfalls and the accidents of the mining and the tunneling, are prone to the infections that are difficult to treat in the damp and the dirty conditions of the underground. The powder of the glow fungus is applied to the cleaned wounds as a dressing, the powder promoting the healing and the regeneration of the tissue in the manner that the other remedies do not.

The healers of the dwarven halls have developed the methods for the preparation of the glow fungus powder, the fungus being dried in the warm and the dry chambers and ground in the mortars that are reserved for the medicinal preparations. The powder is mixed with the fats and the oils that are available in the underground, the mixture being applied to the wounds as a salve that adheres to the tissue and provides the sustained release of the active compounds. The glow fungus salve is a remedy that is specific to the dwarven halls, and the remedy is not traded to the surface settlements, for the dwarves regard the medicinal preparations of the glow fungus as a secret of their people.

## Cultural Significance and the Light of the Halls

The glow fungus is a symbol of the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) mastery of the underground environment, the cultivation of the fungus being one of the practices that distinguishes the dwarven halls from the crude and the temporary mines of the other peoples. The pale green light of the glow fungus is the light that the dwarves associate with the home and the safety of the halls, and the dwarves who travel to the surface speak with the nostalgia of the soft and the steady glow that illuminated the passages and the chambers of their youth. The glow fungus is also a symbol of the patience and the skill of the dwarven cultivators, the growth of the fungus being a slow and the gradual process that rewards the careful and the consistent tending that the dwarves are renowned for.

The glow fungus is also associated with the religious traditions of the dwarves, the light of the fungus being regarded as a manifestation of the deep fire that the dwarves believe burns in the heart of the rock. The chambers where the glow fungus is cultivated are the chambers where the dwarves gather for the ceremonies and the rituals that mark the passages of the life, and the pale green light of the fungus is the light that illuminates the vows and the oaths that the dwarves swear in the presence of their community. The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) are the principal centres of the glow fungus cultivation, and the glow fungus chambers of Stoneheim are the largest and the most elaborate of the underground gardens that the dwarves have created in the centuries of their habitation of the deep places of the world.
