## The Dream Leaf of the Kharesh Oases

The dream leaf is a plant that grows in the shaded gardens of the oases of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), a small and unremarkable plant that produces a leaf that, when chewed or steeped in the water, induces a state of the dreaming that the mages of the Sahrani tradition use for the divination and the communication with the spirits.

The dream leaf is harvested in the autumn, when the leaves have reached the full size and the alkaloids that produce the dreaming effect have accumulated in the tissue of the leaf. The leaves are dried in the shade, and the dried leaves are stored in the sealed containers that preserve the potency of the leaf.

The use of the dream leaf in the practice of magic is regulated by the mages of the Sahrani coast, and the trade in the dream leaf is restricted to the members of the mages' guilds. The misuse of the dream leaf is a serious offense in the legal codes of the Sahrani states, for the dreaming that the leaf induces can be dangerous if the user is not prepared for the experience.
