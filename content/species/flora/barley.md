## Barley

Barley is the hardiest of the grain crops capable of sustained cultivation in the cold climates of the northern regions. It is grown throughout [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and in the upland districts of Aethelar where the summers are too short or too cool for wheat to ripen reliably. The barley of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is a six-row variety that has been selected over generations for its tolerance of cold and its short growing season, which requires only seventy days from planting to harvest.

The principal use of barley in all three continents is the brewing of beer and ale, a beverage consumed by peoples of every station from the dwarven halls of the deep mountains to the fishing villages of the coastal strips. The malting of barley is a skilled trade that involves steeping the grain in water, allowing it to germinate, and then drying it in a kiln to develop the sugars that fermentation will convert into alcohol. The maltsters of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the brewmasters of the dwarven holds are renowned throughout the known world for the quality of their product, and the trade in malted barley is a significant component of the agricultural economy of the northern regions.

Barley is also used as a food grain, ground into flour for flatbreads and porridges that are the staple diet of the poorer households of the northern settlements. The flour of barley is darker and heavier than wheat flour, and the bread made from it is dense and filling, sustaining the labourers and the miners who require a diet that provides lasting energy through the long working day. In the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark), barley porridge is a common breakfast dish, served with milk or buttermilk and occasionally sweetened with honey.

## Physical Characteristics

The barley is a cereal grass that reaches a height of two to three feet in the conditions of the northern fields. The stem of the barley is hollow and jointed, and the leaves are flat and narrow, with the sheaths that wrap the stem at the joints. The head of the barley is a spike that bears the grain in the rows along the central stalk, and the six-row variety that is grown in the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) Uplands produces the grain on the six rows of the spike. The grain of the barley is enclosed in the hull that adheres to the grain in the varieties that are grown in the north, and the hull must be removed by the abrasion before the grain can be used for the food.

The barley is a plant that is adapted to the cold and the short growing season of the northern regions, and the barley can survive the frosts that would destroy the [wheat](/content/species/flora/wheat) and the other grains. The root system of the barley is deep and extensive, allowing the plant to access the moisture that is stored in the soil during the dry periods that occur even in the cooler regions. The barley ripens early, and the harvest of the barley is completed before the autumn rains that would damage the standing grain.

## Cultivation and the Agricultural Calendar

The cultivation of the barley follows the cycle of the seasons in the northern regions, and the barley is planted in the spring as soon as the soil can be worked. The fields are ploughed and harrowed to prepare the seedbed, and the seed is broadcast by hand or sown in the rows by the drill that is drawn by the oxen. The barley germinates quickly in the cool soil, and the young plants emerge within a week or two of the planting.

The barley grows through the short summer, and the heads of the grain develop and fill as the season progresses. The harvest of the barley is carried out in the late summer, and the grain is cut with the sickle or the scythe and bound into the sheaves that are left to dry in the field. The threshing of the barley is done by the flailing or by the trampling of the oxen, and the grain is separated from the chaff by the winnowing that is carried out in the breeze.

## Brewing and the Malt Trade

The brewing of the beer and the ale is the most important use of the barley in the known world, and the beer is the beverage that is consumed in the greatest quantities by the peoples of the northern regions. The malting of the barley is a process that converts the starch of the grain into the sugars that the yeast ferments into the alcohol, and the maltsters are the craftsmen who carry out the malting in the kilns and the malting houses of the settlements.

The maltsters of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) are renowned for the quality of the malt that they produce, and the malt of Eldreth is exported to the breweries of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the other northern regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The brewmasters of the dwarven holds produce the ales that are consumed in the deep halls of the mountains, and the dwarven ale is a thick and the potent beverage that is valued by the peoples of every race. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the brewers and the maltsters regulate the trade in the malt and the beer, and the guilds maintain the standards of the quality that protect the reputation of the regional products.

## Food Uses and the Northern Diet

The barley is a food grain that is ground into the flour for the flatbreads and the porridges that are the staple diet of the poorer households of the northern settlements. The flour of the barley is darker and heavier than the [wheat](/content/species/flora/wheat) flour, and the bread that is made from the barley flour is dense and filling. The flatbreads of the barley are baked on the griddles and the hearthstones of the northern kitchens, and the breads are eaten with the butter and the cheese that are the products of the northern pastures.

The barley porridge is a dish that is consumed throughout the northern regions, and the porridge is prepared by boiling the barley grits in the water or the milk until the grain is soft and the porridge is thick. The porridge is served with the milk or the buttermilk, and the porridge is occasionally sweetened with the honey or the sugar that is a luxury in the northern settlements. The barley is also used in the soups and the stews of the northern regions, and the barley is added to the broth to thicken the liquid and to provide the substance that sustains the labourers through the working day.

## Geographical Distribution

The barley is grown throughout the northern regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and across the entire continent of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the barley is the grain that is most reliable in the cold and the short-season climates of the north. The barley is also grown in the higher elevations of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) and the central highlands of Aethelar, where the [wheat](/content/species/flora/wheat) does not ripen reliably. The barley is not grown in the warmer regions of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), where the [wheat](/content/species/flora/wheat) is the preferred grain, and the barley is a crop of the north that is not competitive with the wheat in the regions where the wheat can be grown.

## Cultural Significance

The barley is a grain of the great antiquity in the northern regions, and the cultivation of the barley is a practice that predates the written records of the continent. The barley is mentioned in the oldest of the songs and the legends of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) peoples, and the grain is associated with the rituals and the customs of the harvest. The harvest of the barley is a time of the celebration in the northern settlements, and the last sheaf of the barley is the object of the ceremonies that mark the end of the growing season and the beginning of the autumn.

The beer that is brewed from the barley is a beverage of the central importance in the social life of the northern settlements, and the beer is consumed in the feasts and the gatherings that are the occasions of the community. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the brewers are among the most influential of the organisations of the northern cities, and the breweries are the establishments that are central to the economy and the culture of the settlements.
