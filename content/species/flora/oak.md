## Oak and the Shipbuilding of Aethelar

The oak is the most valuable timber tree in the known world, its wood being the preferred material for the construction of ships, buildings, and furniture that must endure for generations. The oak forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), particularly the groves of the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) and the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood), produce timber that is recognised as the finest available for the purposes of the shipwright and the master carpenter.

The oak is a slow-growing tree that requires a hundred years or more to reach the size at which its timber is suitable for the largest construction projects. The oak of Brackenshield is particularly valued for the curved branches and forked trunks that provide the natural shapes required for the frames and the keels of vessels. The shipwrights of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other shipbuilding cities pay premium prices for Brackenshield oak, and the [timber and logging](/content/economy/timber-and-logging) trade of the region is organised around the harvest of this specialised resource.

The wood of the oak is dense, strong, and resistant to decay, properties that make it the ideal material for the structural elements of buildings and ships. The heartwood of the oak is a rich brown colour that darkens with age, and the grain is pronounced and attractive, making it a favourite of the furniture makers who produce the finest pieces for the houses of the wealthy. The oak is also valued for its bark, which is used in the tanning of leather, and for its acorns, which are a food source for the pigs that are pastured in the oak forests during the autumn season.

## Physical Characteristics

The oak is a large deciduous tree that grows to a height of one hundred feet or more, the trunk being thick and straight and the canopy being broad and spreading. The bark of the oak is dark grey and deeply furrowed, the bark being thick and rough and providing the protection that the tree requires against the fire and the pests that would damage the thinner-barked species. The leaves of the oak are lobed and dark green, the leaves being arranged alternately along the stem and the surfaces being smooth and glossy on the upper side and paler on the underside. The leaves turn to the yellow and the brown in the autumn and fall from the tree in the winter, the oak being one of the last of the trees to lose its leaves in the season of the decline.

The wood of the oak is the most remarkable feature of the tree, the timber being a dense and strong wood that is the product of the slow growth and the close grain that the oak produces. The heartwood of the oak is a rich brown colour that darkens with the age, the wood being prized for the beauty and the durability that the heartwood provides, and the sapwood being a paler colour that is less valued and less durable than the heartwood. The grain of the oak is pronounced and attractive, the growth rings being visible and the medullary rays being the features that give the oak wood its characteristic appearance, and the oak being the wood that is the favourite of the furniture makers and the carpenters who produce the finest work.

## Habitat and the Oak Forests

The oak is a tree of the temperate regions, the species being found in the forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) where the soil is deep and the rainfall is reliable and the climate is moderate. The oak forests of Aethelar are the most extensive of the timber forests of the known world, the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) and the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) being the two principal regions where the oak is the dominant tree. The oak requires the deep and the well-drained soil that the lowland and the foothill regions provide, the tree being intolerant of the waterlogged and the shallow soils that the upland and the wetland regions offer.

The oak forests of Aethelar are the forests that have been managed for the centuries, the trees being harvested and the replanted in the cycles that the [timber and logging](/content/economy/timber-and-logging) industry has developed to ensure the continuous supply of the timber that the economy requires. The oak forests of Brackenshield are the forests that are managed by the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), the management being the system of the selective harvesting and the replanting that maintains the age and the species composition of the forest. The Aethelar Heartwood is the forest that is managed by the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves), the elven management being the system that is based on the minimal intervention and the natural regeneration that the elven philosophy of the forest stewardship prescribes.

## The Shipbuilding and the Naval Timber

The oak is the timber that is most valued for the construction of the ships that are the foundation of the maritime trade and the naval power of Aethelar, the wood being the material that is strong and durable and resistant to the rot that the salt water and the shipworm would cause in the lesser timbers. The [shipbuilding](/content/economy/shipbuilding) industry of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other coastal cities is the industry that is the principal consumer of the oak timber, the shipwrights requiring the oak for the keels and the frames and the planking that are the structural elements of the vessels. The oak of Brackenshield is the oak that is preferred for the shipbuilding, the curved branches and the forked trunks of the Brackenshield oak providing the natural shapes that the shipwrights need for the frames and the knees that the construction requires.

The oak that is used in the shipbuilding is the oak that has been seasoned for the years that the drying and the stabilising of the wood require, the oak being cut and stacked in the yards where the air and the sun dry the timber to the moisture content that the shipwright requires. The seasoned oak is the oak that is worked by the shipwrights and the caulkers who construct the ships, the oak being cut and shaped and fastened with the iron and the copper that the shipbuilding uses. The oak ships that are built in the yards of Eldreth are the ships that carry the trade of Aethelar across the [Lesser Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/lesser-inland-sea) and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), and the oak ships are the vessels that are the foundation of the [trade and tariffs](/content/economy/trade-and-tariffs) and the naval power that the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) project across the known world.

## The Furniture and the Interior Woodwork

The oak is the timber that is used in the production of the furniture and the interior woodwork that are the finest of the furnishings that are produced in the known world, the wood being the material that is strong and beautiful and capable of the fine work that the furniture maker and the joiner require. The furniture that is made from the oak is the furniture that is the standard of the quality and the durability that the [construction and masonry](/content/economy/construction-and-masonry) and the other industries that use the wood products recognise. The oak furniture that is produced in the workshops of the cities is the furniture that is used in the houses and the public buildings of the wealthy and the powerful, the oak being the wood that is the sign of the status and the permanence that the oak represents.

The oak is also used in the construction of the buildings that are the structures that are intended to endure, the oak being the material that is used for the beams and the posts and the frames that support the buildings. The oak-framed building is the building that is the characteristic of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) architecture, the oak frame being the skeleton that supports the walls and the roof, and the oak frame being the structure that gives the building the strength and the longevity that the oak provides. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the carpenters and the joiners regulate the production of the oak furniture and the oak-framed buildings, the guilds setting the standards of the quality and the training that the crafts require.

## The Bark and the Tanning

The bark of the oak is the material that is used in the tanning of the leather that is the essential product of the [tanning and leather](/content/economy/tanning-and-leather) industry, the bark containing the tannins that are the compounds that convert the raw hide into the leather that is durable and resistant to the decay. The oak bark is stripped from the trees in the spring when the sap is rising and the bark is loose, the stripping being done by the barkers who work in the oak forests and who sell the bark to the tanners who operate the tanyards. The oak bark is the tannin source that is most valued by the tanners, the bark producing the leather that is firm and durable and the colour that is the characteristic of the oak-tanned leather.

The acorns of the oak are the fruit that is the food of the [Wild Boar](/content/species/fauna/wild-boar) that are pastured in the oak forests in the autumn, the pigs being the animals that are turned into the forest to consume the acorns that the oak trees produce. The acorn-feeding is the practice that is called the pannage, the pannage being the right that the farmers of the oak-forest regions have to graze their pigs in the forest, and the pannage being the practice that produces the pork that is the characteristic of the autumn season in the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth). The acorn-fed pork is the meat that is valued for the flavour and the quality that the acorn diet provides, and the acorn-fed pork is the product that is the sign of the relationship between the oak forest and the [livestock and herding](/content/economy/livestock-and-herding) economy of the northern regions.
