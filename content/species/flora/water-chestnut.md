## The Water Chestnut of the Aethelaran Lakes

The water chestnut is a plant that grows in the lakes and the slow-moving rivers of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), producing a nut that is valued for the flavour and the texture. The water chestnut is an aquatic plant that floats on the surface of the water, and the leaves of the plant are arranged in a rosette that floats on the surface.

The nut of the water chestnut is harvested in the autumn, when the nuts have fallen from the plant and are floating on the surface of the water. The nuts are collected by the boats that sweep the surface of the lake, and the collected nuts are dried and stored for the use in the winter months. The water chestnut is eaten fresh, boiled, or roasted, and the flavour of the water chestnut is sweet and nutty.

## Physical Characteristics

The water chestnut is an aquatic plant that produces the floating rosette of the leaves that lie flat on the surface of the water, the leaves being diamond-shaped and toothed at the margins and being connected to the submerged stem by the long and the slender petioles. The rosette of the leaves may reach a diameter of two feet in the mature specimens, the leaves overlapping and covering the surface of the water in the dense mats that the plant produces in the favourable conditions. The submerged portions of the plant include the stem and the roots that anchor the plant to the mud at the bottom of the lake, the roots being the fine and the thread-like structures that extract the nutrients from the sediment.

The flowers of the water chestnut are small and white, the flowers being produced in the axils of the leaves and being held above the surface of the water on the short stalks that elevate the flowers for the pollination that the insects perform. The fruit of the water chestnut is the nut that is the valued product of the plant, the nut being a hard and the woody structure that is equipped with the four sharp spines that project from the body of the nut and that give the nut the distinctive and the formidable appearance. The spines of the nut are the means by which the nut is dispersed, the spines catching on the feathers of the [Duck and Goose](/content/species/fauna/duck-and-goose) and the fur of the [beavers](/content/species/fauna/beaver) that carry the nut to the new locations.

## Habitat and Distribution

The water chestnut grows in the lakes and the slow-moving rivers of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) continent, the plant being found in the shallow water where the depth is no more than four to six feet and the bottom is the soft mud that the roots of the plant require. The water chestnut is most abundant in the lakes of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the other regions of Aethelar where the still water and the rich sediment provide the conditions that the plant favours. The water chestnut is also found in the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) where the slow current and the shallow depth create the habitat that the plant occupies.

The water chestnut is an annual plant that germinates in the spring from the nuts that have survived the winter in the mud at the bottom of the lake, the plant growing through the summer and producing the nuts that are harvested in the autumn. The plant is dependent on the still water and the soft sediment, the water chestnut being unable to establish in the fast-flowing rivers or the rocky bottoms that the other aquatic plants tolerate. The water chestnut can become a nuisance in the waterways where the conditions are favourable, the dense mats of the floating rosettes blocking the channels and the navigation that the boats require.

## Harvest and Gathering

The harvest of the water chestnut is conducted in the autumn when the nuts have matured and have fallen from the plant, the nuts floating on the surface of the water or sinking to the bottom depending on the condition of the nut. The gathering is done by the boats that the lake communities use for the harvesting of the aquatic crops, the boats being maneuvered through the mats of the plants and the nuts being collected by the nets and the scoops that the gatherers use. The [fishing and whaling](/content/economy/fishing-and-whaling) communities of the lake districts are the principal gatherers of the water chestnut, the harvest being a component of the seasonal round that the lake economy follows.

The gathering of the water chestnut is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the lake communities and the authorities who manage the waterways, the regulation being the means of preventing the over-harvesting that would deplete the population and the conflicts that the competition for the resource would produce. The [Vael Water Law](/content/law/vael-water-law) governs the rights to the harvest of the water chestnut in the regions where the law applies, the rights being allocated to the communities and the families that have traditionally harvested the crop.

## Culinary Uses

The water chestnut is a valued food that is consumed in the lake districts of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the regions where the plant is harvested, the nut being prepared by the methods that the lake communities have developed to exploit the flavour and the texture of the species. The fresh nut is eaten raw after the hard shell has been removed, the flesh being a white and the crisp material that has a sweet and the nutty flavour. The fresh nut is also used in the salads and the other dishes that the raw preparation suits, the texture of the water chestnut being the element that the dishes feature.

The boiled water chestnut is the preparation that the lake communities use for the nuts that are not eaten fresh, the boiling being the method that softens the flesh and makes the nut suitable for the soups and the stews that the winter diet comprises. The roasted water chestnut is the preparation that the street vendors of the lake district towns sell in the winter months, the roasting being done on the coals that heat the nuts and the aroma of the roasting drawing the customers to the vendors. The [feasting and dining](/content/culture/feasting-and-dining) traditions of the lake districts include the water chestnut as the ingredient that is featured in the dishes that are served at the harvest festivals that celebrate the gathering of the autumn crops.

## Ecological Role

The water chestnut plays a role in the ecology of the lakes and the rivers where the plant grows, the floating rosettes providing the shade and the shelter that the [Carp](/content/species/fauna/carp) and the other aquatic animals use. The [carp](/content/species/fauna/carp) and the [pike](/content/species/fauna/pike) that inhabit the lakes of Aethelar are among the fish that find the shelter beneath the leaves of the water chestnut, the shade that the rosettes provide being the feature that the fish seek in the heat of the summer. The [Duck and Goose](/content/species/fauna/duck-and-goose) that live on the lakes also use the water chestnut, the birds consuming the nuts and the leaves that the plant produces.

The water chestnut can become the invasive plant in the waterways where the conditions are favourable and the natural controls are absent, the dense mats of the rosettes blocking the light from the submerged plants and the navigation of the boats. The management of the water chestnut in the waterways where the plant is a nuisance is the responsibility of the authorities who maintain the channels and the drainage, the management being the removal of the excess growth and the maintenance of the open water that the navigation and the ecology require. The [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) are among the regions where the management of the water chestnut is a concern, the plant being one of the species that the marsh authorities must control.

## Economic Significance

The water chestnut is a component of the economy of the lake districts of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the harvest and the sale of the nuts being the source of the income that the lake communities derive from the aquatic resources. The [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the lake district towns trade the water chestnut in the autumn when the harvest is done, the nuts being sold fresh and the dried nuts being available through the winter months. The [prices and livelihood](/content/economy/prices-and-livelihood) of the lake communities are partly dependent on the water chestnut, the crop being one of the resources that the lake economy utilises.

The water chestnut is also traded to the inland towns and the cities of Aethelar where the nut is a specialty food that the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the food merchants distribute. The dried water chestnut is the form in which the nut is transported over the longer distances, the dried nut being the product that the [cartage and transport](/content/economy/cartage-and-transport) networks carry from the lake districts to the markets of the interior. The trade in the water chestnut is a modest but the steady component of the commerce of the lake regions, the nut being among the products that the Aethelari lake districts contribute to the economy of the continent.
