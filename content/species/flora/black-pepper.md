## Black Pepper

Black pepper is the most widely traded spice of Sahrana, consumed in every region of the known world where the people can afford it.

## Black Pepper and the Trellises of the Coast

The pepper vine is grown on trellises in the gardens of the coast and the interior, producing the clusters of the berries that are harvested when the berries begin to turn red.

The pepper berries are dried in the sun, turning black and wrinkled as the moisture leaves them, and the dried peppercorns are packed into the sacks for the transport to the markets of the coast. The pepper that is produced in the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is the finest of the black pepper, and the gardens of the coast produce the peppercorns that are the largest and the most aromatic of the pepper of the known world.

The demand for the black pepper in the cities of Aethelar and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) is enormous, and the volume of the pepper that passes through the ports of the Sahrani coast is measured in thousands of tons each year. The pepper is used in the cooking of every region of the known world, and the spice is the most common of all the seasonings that are used in the preparation of the food.

## Physical Characteristics

The pepper vine is a climbing plant that reaches a length of ten to fifteen feet on the trellises that are built in the gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent). The stem of the pepper vine is thin and flexible, and the vine produces the roots at the nodes that allow the plant to cling to the support. The leaves of the pepper vine are oval and alternate, with the glossy surface that reflects the light of the sun. The leaves have a spicy aroma when they are crushed, and the leaves are sometimes used in the cooking of the regions where the pepper is grown.

The pepper vine produces the small and white flowers that are arranged in the spikes that form at the nodes of the stem. The flowers develop into the berries that are green when they are young and red when they are ripe, and the berries are harvested before the ripening is complete, when the berries have begun to turn from the green to the red. The berries are dried in the sun, and the drying turns the berries from the green and the red to the black and the wrinkled, and the dried peppercorns are the spice that is known as the black pepper.

## Cultivation and Harvest

The pepper vine is grown in the gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the other coastal regions of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), and the vine is planted in the shade of the taller trees that protect the pepper from the direct sun. The trellises that support the pepper vine are built of the wood of the [acacia](/content/species/flora/acacia) and the other durable timbers, and the trellises are spaced to allow the air to circulate between the vines and the sun to reach the fruit on every side.

The cultivation of the pepper vine requires the constant attention of the gardeners, and the vines are watered and fertilised throughout the year. The pepper vine is propagated by the cuttings that are taken from the productive vines, and the cuttings are planted at the base of the trellises and trained to climb the support. The pepper vine begins to produce the berries in the third year after the planting, and the vine reaches the full production in the fifth or the sixth year. The harvest of the pepper is carried out in the dry season, and the berries are picked by the hand from the spikes that are cut from the vines.

## Processing and Trade

The processing of the pepper begins with the drying of the berries in the sun, and the drying is carried out on the mats that are laid in the open areas of the gardens. The berries are turned regularly to ensure the even drying, and the drying is complete when the berries have turned black and the moisture has been reduced to the level that preserves the spice. The dried peppercorns are cleaned and sorted by the size and the quality, and the finest peppercorns are packed into the sacks that are lined with the linen that protects the spice from the contamination.

The trade in the black pepper is one of the most valuable of the trades that connect the continents of the known world, and the pepper is carried in the ships that sail the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) from the ports of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the spice merchants regulate the trade in the pepper, and the guilds maintain the standards of the quality and the purity that protect the reputation of the pepper of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) in the markets of the world.

## Culinary Uses

The black pepper is the spice that is used in the cooking of every region of the known world, and the pepper is the seasoning that is added to the dishes of the meat and the fish and the vegetables. The pepper is ground in the mills that are found in the kitchens of the wealthy and the poor, and the ground pepper is used to season the dishes at the table and in the cooking. The pepper is also used in the preparation of the spice mixtures that are the characteristic seasonings of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) cuisine, and the pepper is combined with the [cardamom](/content/species/flora/cardamom) and the other spices in the mixtures that are used in the cooking of the region.

The pepper is also used in the preservation of the meat and the fish, and the pepper is added to the salt that is used in the curing of the provisions that are stored for the winter. The pepper is a spice that is valued for the flavour and the preservative properties that the spice provides, and the pepper is the spice that is most widely used in the preparation of the food that is stored and the food that is eaten fresh.
