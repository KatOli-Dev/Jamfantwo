## Yucca

The yucca that grows in the desert margins of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the [Sunfire Steppe](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sunfire-steppe) is a plant that produces a fibre from the leaves that is used in the production of the cordage and the coarse cloth of the desert peoples.

The yucca is a drought-tolerant plant that grows in the arid regions where the other fibre plants cannot survive, and the fibre that is produced from the yucca leaves is strong and durable. The processing of the yucca fibre involves the beating of the leaves to separate the fibre from the fleshy tissue, and the cleaning and the drying of the fibre before it is spun into the yarn. The yucca fibre is used in the production of the ropes and the nets that the desert peoples use in the daily life, and the fibre is also used in the production of the sandals and the mats that are the common products of the desert settlements.

## Physical Characteristics

The yucca is a perennial plant that produces the rosette of the stiff and the sword-shaped leaves that radiate from the central crown of the plant, the leaves being a grey-green in the colour and being armed with the sharp tip that the leaf terminates in. The leaves of the yucca are a foot to two feet in the length in the mature specimens, the leaves being narrow and the edges being the fibrous margins that produce the threads that the desert peoples extract for the fibre that the plant yields. The root system of the yucca is the deep and the extensive network that the plant uses to extract the moisture from the arid soil, the roots being the adaptation that enables the yucca to survive in the conditions that the desert and the steppe margins provide.

The flower stalk of the yucca rises from the centre of the rosette to a height of three to five feet, the stalk bearing the numerous white and the waxy flowers that are the bell-shaped blooms that the yucca produces in the late spring and the early summer. The flowers of the yucca are the flowers that are pollinated by the [Honey Bee](/content/species/fauna/honey-bee) that the plant is specifically associated with, the pollination being the relationship that the yucca and the [Honey Bee](/content/species/fauna/honey-bee) have evolved in the manner that the biology of the species describes. The fruit of the yucca is the capsule that contains the seeds that the plant produces, the capsule being the structure that splits open when the seeds are mature and that releases the seeds to the wind and the water that disperse them.

## Habitat and Distribution

The yucca grows in the desert margins of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the [Sunfire Steppe](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sunfire-steppe) where the soil is the sandy and the gravelly substrate that the arid regions provide, the yucca being the plant that the conditions of the desert margin suit. The yucca is distributed across the [Sahrani Wastes](/content/location/settlement/region/sahrani-wastes) and the [Kharesh Badlands](/content/location/settlement/region/kharesh-badlands) where the plant is the common sight that the landscape of the desert margin displays, the yucca being the plant that the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) peoples associate with the transition from the desert to the steppe.

The yucca is also found in the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip) where the plant is cultivated in the gardens of the coastal cities for the ornamental value that the flower stalk and the rosette provide, the yucca being the plant that the Sahrani gardeners use for the decoration of the gardens and the courtyards that the coastal architecture includes. The yucca that is cultivated in the gardens is the same species that grows in the desert margins, the plant being the species that the Sahrani coast and the interior share.

## Fibre Production

The fibre that the yucca produces is the strong and the durable material that the desert peoples extract from the leaves of the plant, the extraction being the process that the beating and the scraping of the leaves involves. The leaves are harvested from the plant by the cutting of the leaves at the base, the leaves being collected in the bundles that the processing requires. The leaves are beaten with the wooden mallets that the [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) and the [Vashari](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) peoples use for the extraction of the fibre, the beating being the process that separates the fibrous tissue from the fleshy tissue that the leaf contains.

The fibre that is extracted from the yucca leaves is cleaned and dried before the spinning into the yarn that the production of the cordage and the cloth requires, the cleaning being the process that removes the residue of the fleshy tissue and the impurities that the beating leaves in the fibre. The [textiles and cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) that the yucca fibre produces are the coarse and the durable products that the desert peoples use in the daily life, the yucca being the plant that the [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) trade and the other commerce of the desert regions incorporate as the source of the fibre that the desert economy uses.

## Uses in Cordage and Nets

The yucca fibre is the material that the desert peoples use for the production of the ropes and the cordage that the daily life and the [livestock and herding](/content/economy/livestock-and-herding) economy of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) interior require, the yucca being the source of the fibre that the ropes and the halters and the other equipment that the [camels](/content/species/fauna/camel) and the [goats](/content/species/fauna/goats) are managed with are made from. The yucca rope is the strong and the rot-resistant cordage that the desert environment demands, the fibre being the material that the dry conditions and the heat of the desert do not degrade.

The yucca fibre is also used in the production of the nets that the [fishing and whaling](/content/economy/fishing-and-whaling) communities of the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip) and the other coastal regions use, the nets being the equipment that the coastal and the desert peoples share. The nets that are made from the yucca fibre are the nets that the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) and the other [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) peoples use for the fishing that the coastal waters provide and for the hunting that the desert and the steppe support. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the net makers and the rope makers regulate the production and the trade in the yucca cordage, the guilds maintaining the standards of the quality that the maritime and the pastoral use demands.

## Sandals and Mats

The yucca fibre is the material that the desert peoples use for the production of the sandals that the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) and the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) peoples wear, the sandals being the footwear that the hot sand and the rocky ground of the desert require. The sandals that are made from the yucca fibre are the durable and the comfortable footwear that the desert peoples have worn for the centuries, the sandals being the products that the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the Sahrani cities trade. The sandals are the products that the [cartage and transport](/content/economy/cartage-and-transport) networks distribute from the desert communities where the sandals are made to the coastal cities where the sandals are sold.

The mats that are woven from the yucca fibre are the products that the desert peoples use for the flooring and the bedding that the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) dwelling requires, the mats being the furnishings that the desert household employs. The yucca mats are also used in the [sahrani caravan feast](/content/culture/sahrani-caravan-feast) and the other cultural occasions that the Sahrani peoples observe, the mats being the seating and the table surfaces that the [feasting and dining](/content/culture/feasting-and-dining) traditions use. The [trade and tariffs](/content/economy/trade-and-tariffs) of the Sahrani regions include the yucca mats as the commodities that the commerce of the desert and the coast involves.
