## Plum and Cherry

The stone fruits of Aethelar include the plum, the cherry, and the damson, each of which has its own season and its own uses.

### Plum

The plum is the most widely grown of the stone fruits, its varieties ranging from the small and tart damsons of the northern orchards to the large and sweet plums that ripen in the late summer sun of the southern valleys. The plum is eaten fresh, dried into prunes that keep through the winter, or cooked into preserves and jams that are the pride of the households that prepare them.

### Cherry

The cherry is a less common fruit, the cherry tree being more demanding in its requirements for soil and aspect, and its cultivation is limited to the most favoured districts of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the lower slopes of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood). The cherry harvest in early summer is a brief and intense period, for the fruit does not keep for more than a few days after picking, and the cherries that are not consumed immediately are preserved in syrup or distilled into a spirit that is highly valued by the connoisseurs of the coastal cities.

## Physical Characteristics of the Stone Fruits

The stone fruits are the fruits of the genus that includes the plum, the cherry, the damson, and the apricot, the fruits being characterised by the single hard stone that encloses the seed. The trees of the stone fruits are deciduous, the leaves being shed in the autumn and the new growth emerging in the spring with the flowers that appear before the leaves. The bark of the stone fruit trees is dark and smooth, the wood being hard and dense, with a grain that is prized by the woodworkers for the turning of the small objects and the carving of the decorative pieces.

The flowers of the stone fruits are white or pink, the petals being round and open, and the flowering of the cherry is among the most beautiful of the spectacles of the spring, the trees being covered in the blossom that stands out against the dark branches and the pale sky. The flowering of the stone fruits is brief, the petals being shed within a week or two of the opening, and the fruit that develops from the flowers ripens over the summer months, the plum in the late summer and the cherry in the early summer.

## The Damson and the Northern Orchards

The damson is the plum of the northern orchards, the small and dark fruit being the product of the trees that have been selected for their hardiness and their resistance to the frosts. The damson trees are grown in the orchards of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the upland districts of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range), and the damsons are harvested in the late autumn, the fruit being too tart to be eaten fresh but being made into the preserves and the jams that are the staple of the winter diet.

The damson is also used in the production of the damson cheese, a thick and sweet paste that is made by the boiling of the damsons with the sugar until the mixture sets to a firm consistency. The damson cheese is served as an accompaniment to the cold meats and the cheeses that are the dishes of the winter table, and the damson cheese is also exported to the markets of the south, where the sweet and tart flavour is appreciated by the consumers of the coastal cities.

## The Cherry Spirit and the Distillation of the Stone Fruits

The cherry spirit, called the kirsch, is a clear and strong liqueur that is distilled from the fermented cherries and the stones, the stones giving the spirit a distinctive flavour of the almond that is the characteristic of the kirsch. The distillation of the kirsch is a specialised craft that is practised by the distillers of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael), and the kirsch that is produced in the distilleries of the region is exported to the markets of the coast, where it is served as a digestif at the end of the meals of the wealthy.

The plum is also distilled into a spirit that is called the slivovitz, the spirit being made from the fermented plums that are crushed and allowed to ferment in the vats. The slivovitz is a strong and fiery spirit that is drunk by the peoples of the northern regions during the winter months, and the slivovitz is also used in the preparation of the remedies that the healers of the north prescribe for the colds and the infections of the winter season.

## The Cherry Tree and Its Wood

The cherry tree is also valued for its wood, the wood of the cherry being a reddish brown with a fine and even grain that is prized by the furniture makers for the inlay and the veneer that are used in the decoration of the finest pieces. The cherry wood is also used in the making of the pipes that are smoked by the peoples of the northern regions, the wood being resistant to the heat and the moisture of the tobacco.

The cherry wood is a scarce material, for the cherry tree is not grown in the quantities that would supply the demand for the wood, and the cherry wood that is available in the markets is the product of the trees that have been felled because of the age or the disease. The wood of the cherry is also used in the turning of the bowls and the plates that are the tableware of the wealthy households, the warm colour and the smooth texture of the wood making it a favoured material for the objects that are used in the daily life.

## Cultural Significance of the Stone Fruits

The stone fruits have a cultural significance in the traditions of the peoples of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the flowering of the cherry being the occasion of the festivals that celebrate the arrival of the spring. The cherry blossom is the symbol of the renewal and the hope that the spring brings, and the cherry trees that are planted in the gardens of the wealthy are valued as much for their blossom as for their fruit.

The plum and the damson are the fruits of the harvest festivals that are held in the autumn, the fruit being the centrepiece of the feasts that celebrate the gathering of the crops. The preserves and the jams that are made from the stone fruits are the gifts that are exchanged between the households at the winter solstice, and the jars of the preserved fruit are the measure of the skill and the industry of the women who have prepared them.

## Trade and the Export of the Stone Fruits

The trade in the stone fruits is a component of the agricultural economy of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the dried plums and the preserved cherries being exported to the markets of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) coast. The dried plums are a provision of the ships that carry the trade of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean), and the preserved cherries are a luxury of the tables of the wealthy in the cities of the south. The trade in the kirsch and the slivovitz is regulated by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the distillers, who maintain the standards of the quality and the prices of the spirits that are produced in the distilleries of the north.
