## The Shadow Moss of the Deep Caves

The shadow moss does not draw energy from the light, for there is no light in the depths where the moss grows, and the moss derives its energy from the minerals that are dissolved in the water that seeps through the rock.

The shadow moss is used in the preparation of the darkness-based spells of magic, and the moss is also used in the construction of the foci that conceal the presence of the mage from the perception of the enemies. The moss is harvested by the dwarven miners who work in the deep levels of the holds, and the moss is traded to the mages of the northern cities.

The harvest of the shadow moss is a dangerous undertaking, for the moss grows in the unstable and the inaccessible parts of the caves, and the gatherers who collect the moss must be experienced in the navigation of the underground passages. The dwarven communities that control the access to the deep caves regulate the harvest of the shadow moss, and the trade in the moss is a minor but significant part of the economy of the underground holds.
