## Yarrow and the Wound Healing of the Battlefield

Yarrow is the most important of the wound-healing herbs, its leaves being used in the treatment of the cuts and the abrasions that are the common injuries of the working life and the battlefield. The plant grows in the meadows and the pastures of Aethelar, and it is cultivated in the gardens of the healers for the supply that is required in the times of conflict and the periods of intense labour.

The leaves of the yarrow are harvested in the summer, when the plant is in flower and the medicinal compounds are at their highest concentration. The leaves are dried and ground to a powder that is applied directly to the wound, or the fresh leaves are crushed and pressed onto the injury to staunch the bleeding and to prevent the infection that would otherwise complicate the healing. The healers of the armies of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) carry yarrow in their satchels as a standard part of their equipment, and the use of yarrow in the treatment of wounds is taught to every soldier as a basic skill of self-care.

## Physical Characteristics

The yarrow is a perennial herb that grows to a height of one to three feet in the mature specimens, the stem being erect and the angular and the furrowed stem that bears the leaves and the flower heads that the plant produces. The leaves of the yarrow are the feathery and the finely divided structures that give the plant the delicate and the lacy appearance that the meadows and the pastures display when the yarrow is abundant, the leaves being divided into the numerous narrow segments that are arranged along the rachis in the pinnate pattern that the species displays.

The flower heads of the yarrow are the flat-topped clusters that are produced at the apex of the stem and the branches, the clusters being composed of the numerous small flowers that are white or the pale pink in the most common varieties. The flowers of the yarrow are the composite flowers that the daisy family produces, each flower head being the collection of the disc and the ray florets that the composite structure comprises. The flowers are pollinated by the [Honey Bee](/content/species/fauna/honey-bee) and the [Seabird](/content/species/fauna/seabird) and the other insects that visit the flowers for the nectar and the pollen that the flowers provide.

## Habitat and Distribution

The yarrow grows in the meadows and the pastures and the waste places of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the plant being found in the regions where the soil is well-drained and the sunlight is abundant. The yarrow is most common in the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the other northern regions of Aethelar where the conditions are suitable for the growth of the species, the yarrow being a plant that is tolerant of the cooler climate and the shorter growing season that the north provides. The yarrow is also found in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) and the other central regions of the continent where the meadows and the pastures that the plant favours are present.

The yarrow is a plant that is both gathered from the wild and cultivated in the gardens of the healers and the apothecaries, the wild population being the source that the casual use exploits and the cultivated supply being the source that the systematic practice of the [healing and restoration](/content/magic/healing-and-restoration) requires. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the apothecaries and the healers regulate the cultivation and the harvest of the yarrow, the guilds maintaining the standards of the quality and the supply that the medicinal use demands.

## Medicinal Properties

The yarrow is the herb that the medicinal practice of the known world values most for the treatment of the wounds that the injury and the conflict produce, the leaves of the plant containing the compounds that promote the clotting of the blood and the compounds that inhibit the growth of the bacteria that cause the infection. The powder of the dried leaves is the preparation that the healers apply to the wounds that the bleeding and the infection threaten, the powder being the form that the direct application to the wound requires. The fresh leaves are also used in the treatment of the wounds, the fresh leaves being crushed and the juice being expressed onto the injury for the effect that the fresh compounds provide.

The yarrow is also used in the treatment of the [fever](/content/species/flora/feverfew) and the digestive disorders that the [healing and restoration](/content/magic/healing-and-restoration) practitioners address, the infusion of the dried yarrow being the remedy that the healers prescribe for the conditions that the internal use of the herb suits. The infusion is prepared by the steeping of the dried leaves and the flowers in the hot water, the liquid being the preparation that the patient drinks for the relief that the remedy provides. The [arnica](/content/species/flora/arnica) and the [feverfew](/content/species/flora/feverfew) and the other herbs that the healers use are the herbs that the yarrow is combined with in the preparations that the medicinal practice employs.

## Military Use

The yarrow is the standard issue of the [Warfare of the Known World](/content/military/warfare-overview) of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the other regions of the known world where the armies maintain the supplies that the treatment of the battlefield wounds requires, the yarrow being the herb that every soldier carries in the pouch that the personal kit includes. The [weaponry and armour](/content/military/weaponry-and-armour) that the soldier bears is accompanied by the yarrow that the soldier uses for the treatment of the wounds that the combat produces, the yarrow being the remedy that the soldier applies to the injury that the comrade or the self suffers.

The [northern marches rangers](/content/military/northern-marches-rangers) and the other [Warfare of the Known World](/content/military/warfare-overview) units of the known world train the soldiers in the use of the yarrow, the training being the instruction that the basic care of the wounds requires, the yarrow being the remedy that the soldier is expected to use without the assistance of the healer. The [signal and scout network](/content/military/signal-and-scout-network) and the other [Warfare of the Known World](/content/military/warfare-overview) systems that the armies maintain include the distribution of the yarrow as the component of the supplies that the [fortifications and siegecraft](/content/military/fortifications-and-siegecraft) and the other military infrastructure requires.

## Cultivation and Trade

The yarrow is cultivated in the gardens of the healers and the apothecaries of the Aethelari and the [Morvindi](/content/language/spoken/morvindi) regions, the cultivated yarrow being the supply that the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the apothecaries and the [healing and restoration](/content/magic/healing-and-restoration) practitioners maintain for the demand that the medicinal use produces. The cultivation of the yarrow is the [agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) that the apothecaries conduct in the gardens that are adjacent to the workshops and the dispensaries that the healers operate, the cultivation being the means of ensuring the supply that the trade and the practice depend on.

The trade in the yarrow is the commerce that the [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) and the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the Aethelari regions conduct, the dried yarrow being the commodity that the [cartage and transport](/content/economy/cartage-and-transport) networks distribute from the regions where the herb is grown to the centres where the herb is used. The [prices and livelihood](/content/economy/prices-and-livelihood) of the apothecaries and the healers are partly dependent on the yarrow, the herb being one of the remedies that the medicinal trade is based on.

## Cultural Significance

The yarrow has a place in the [folk omens and superstitions](/content/mythology/folk-omens-and-superstitions) and the [halfling harvest tales](/content/mythology/halfling-harvest-tales) of the Aethelari peoples that reflects the association of the herb with the healing and the protection that the plant provides, the yarrow being the herb that the folklore and the mythology of the meadow and the battlefield connect to the qualities of the courage and the endurance that the plant represents. The yarrow is the herb that the [halfling hearth traditions](/content/religion/halfling-hearth-traditions) and the other cultural practices of the Aethelari peoples include in the rituals of the [seasonal calendar](/content/culture/seasonal-calendar) that the agricultural year marks, the yarrow being the plant that the midsummer and the harvest festivals use for the decoration and the symbolism that the occasions employ.

The yarrow is also associated with the [divination and foresight](/content/magic/divination-and-foresight) practices that the mages and the folk practitioners of the known world conduct, the stalks of the yarrow being the instrument that the [Divination and Foresight](/content/magic/divination-and-foresight)-like systems of the casting of the lots use for the generation of the patterns that the diviner interprets. The [chroniclers](/content/government/local/chroniclers-college) of the Aethelari cities record the use of the yarrow in the [pattern reading](/content/magic/pattern-reading) and the other divination practices that the [magic](/content/magic/magic-overview) of the known world includes, the yarrow being the herb that the divination and the healing traditions share.
