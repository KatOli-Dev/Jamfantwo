The ironwood is the hardest and densest wood in the known world, growing only in the deepest parts of the Aethelar Heartwood where its extreme durability is prized for the bearings and the gears of mills and for the mallets of stonecutters.

## The Ironwood of the Heartwood

The ironwood is the hardest and densest of the timber trees of the known world, growing only in the deepest parts of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood). The tree is slow-growing and long-lived, and the wood that it produces is so dense that it sinks in water and resists the sharpest tools. The ironwood is not harvested in large quantities, for the difficulty of working it and the slowness of its growth make it a specialty timber that is used only for the purposes where its unique properties are necessary.

The principal use of ironwood is in the construction of the bearings and the gears of the water mills and the windmills that power the industries of the settlements. The ironwood is also used for the mallets and the hammers of the stonecutters and the masons, for the wood does not splinter or crack under the repeated impacts that would destroy tools made of softer timbers.

## Physical Characteristics

The ironwood is a large tree that grows to a height of eighty to one hundred feet, the trunk being straight and cylindrical and the bark being dark and deeply furrowed. The leaves of the ironwood are oval and leathery, the leaves being a dark green colour that is almost black in the shade of the forest canopy, and the leaves persist on the tree through the winter, the ironwood being one of the few evergreen trees of the Aethelar Heartwood. The flowers of the ironwood are small and inconspicuous, the flowers being produced in the early spring and the fruit being a small nut that is enclosed in a hard shell.

The wood of the ironwood is the most remarkable feature of the tree, the timber being a dark brown colour that is almost black, the grain being fine and straight and the texture being uniform and smooth. The ironwood is so dense that it sinks in the water, the specific gravity of the wood being greater than that of the water, and the wood is so hard that it resists the sharpest metal tools and can be worked only with the greatest difficulty. The ironwood is also resistant to the decay and the rot that would destroy the other timbers, the wood being impervious to the fungi and the insects that attack the softer woods, and the ironwood that is used in the construction will last for the centuries without the signs of the deterioration.

## Habitat and Distribution

The ironwood is a tree of the deep forest, the species being found only in the oldest and the most mature parts of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) where the canopy is closed and the soil is deep and the moisture is constant. The ironwood requires the shade and the shelter of the surrounding forest, the tree being intolerant of the open conditions and the exposure to the wind and the sun that would damage the tree and the stunt the growth. The ironwood is not found in the younger forests or the disturbed areas, the tree being one of the species that defines the climax forest that has developed over the centuries without the disturbance of the fire or the cutting.

The ironwood is a rare tree, the species being represented by the scattered individuals that are found in the deep forest rather than the dense stands that are characteristic of the [oak](/content/species/flora/oak) and the other timber trees. The ironwood trees are separated by the distances that reflect the slow growth and the poor reproduction of the species, the tree producing the seeds only at the long intervals and the seedlings being rare in the forest floor. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Aethelar Heartwood regard the ironwood as one of the ancient trees of the forest, the species being a survivor of the earlier forests that once covered the continent, and the ironwood trees that grow in the deepest parts of the Heartwood are believed by the elves to be the oldest of the living things in the forest.

## The Harvesting of the Ironwood

The harvesting of the ironwood is a difficult and the specialised operation that is undertaken only by the most skilled of the [timber and logging](/content/economy/timber-and-logging) crews. The ironwood is so hard that the felling of the tree requires the axes and the saws that are made of the best steel and that are sharpened to the finest edge, and the felling of a single ironwood tree can occupy the crew for a day or more. The cutting of the ironwood is the work that is performed only in the winter, when the sap is down and the wood is at its hardest and the most stable, and the felled timber is extracted from the forest by the teams of the [oxen](/content/species/fauna/oxen) that are the only animals strong enough to drag the heavy logs through the difficult terrain.

The harvesting of the ironwood is restricted by the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Aethelar Heartwood, the felling of the ironwood being permitted only in the areas that the elves have designated and only in the quantities that the elves have approved. The ironwood is a tree that the elves regard as sacred, and the felling of the ironwood is accompanied by the rituals and the ceremonies that the elves observe to honour the tree and the forest. The timber that is harvested under the elven supervision is the timber that is sold to the craftsmen who work the ironwood, and the trade in the ironwood is a trade that is conducted with the respect for the tree and the forest that the ironwood comes from.

## Uses in the Mills and the Industry

The ironwood is the timber that is most valued for the construction of the bearings and the gears of the mills that power the industries of the settlements, the wood being the material that can withstand the friction and the wear that the continuous operation of the machinery produces. The bearings that are made from the ironwood are the bearings that are used in the water mills and the windmills of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth) and the other industrial cities, the ironwood bearings lasting for the decades without the replacement that the bearings made of the softer woods would require. The [milling and baking](/content/economy/milling-and-baking) industry is one of the principal consumers of the ironwood bearings, the flour mills that grind the grain for the bread being the mills that require the most reliable and the most durable of the bearings.

The ironwood is also used in the construction of the tools that the [construction and masonry](/content/economy/construction-and-masonry) industry requires, the mallets and the hammers that the stonecutters and the masons use being the tools that are made from the ironwood. The ironwood mallet is the tool that can strike the stone and the chisel without the splintering or the cracking that would destroy the mallets made of the softer woods, and the ironwood mallet is the tool that is preferred by the stonecutters who work the hardest of the stones. The ironwood is also used in the manufacture of the planes and the other tools that the woodworkers use, the ironwood being the material that is used for the soles of the planes and the handles of the tools that require the durability and the stability that the ironwood provides.

## Trade and Economic Value

The ironwood is one of the most expensive of the timbers that are traded in the known world, the price of the ironwood being many times the price of the [oak](/content/species/flora/oak) and the other timbers that are used in the construction. The ironwood is sold by the weight rather than by the volume, the density of the wood making the weight the more convenient measure, and the ironwood that is sold in the markets of the cities is the ironwood that has been harvested and worked under the supervision of the elves and the dwarven woodworkers who have the skills to work the material.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the millwrights and the toolmakers regulate the trade in the ironwood products, the guilds setting the standards of the quality and the prices that are paid for the ironwood bearings and the tools. The ironwood that is used in the mills is the ironwood that is purchased by the millwrights who maintain the mills, and the ironwood that is used in the tools is the ironwood that is purchased by the toolmakers who supply the stonecutters and the masons. The ironwood is a material that is essential to the industry of the northern settlements, and the supply of the ironwood is a concern of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) who depend on the mills and the tools that the ironwood makes possible.
