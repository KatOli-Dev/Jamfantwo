## The Water Lily of the Aethelaran Lakes

The water lilies that grow in the lakes and the ponds of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) are floating aquatic plants, their large and fragrant flowers resting on the surface of the water and their broad leaves providing shade for the fish that gather beneath them. The water lilies are not cultivated but are gathered from the wild in the summer months when the flowers are open and the leaves are fully expanded.

The root of the water lily is a thick and fleshy rhizome that grows in the mud at the bottom of the pond, and the rhizome is harvested by the gatherers who wade into the water and pull the rhizome from the mud. The rhizome of the water lily is edible, and the rhizome is sliced and dried for the use in the soups and the stews of the households of the lake districts.

The flowers of the water lily are used in the preparation of the infusions that are drunk for the calming of the nerves and the relief of the anxiety that afflicts the people of the cities. The petals of the water lily are dried and added to the potpourris that are placed in the rooms of the houses to provide a pleasant scent.

## Physical Characteristics

The water lily is a perennial aquatic plant that produces the large and the floating leaves that lie flat on the surface of the water, the leaves being circular or oval in the shape and reaching a diameter of eight to twelve inches in the mature specimens. The leaves of the water lily are a deep green on the upper surface and a paler green on the underside, the underside being the surface that is in the contact with the water. The leaf is attached to the rhizome by the long and the flexible petiole that extends from the mud at the bottom of the pond to the surface of the water, the length of the petiole being the measure of the depth of the water in which the plant grows.

The flower of the water lily is the most conspicuous and the celebrated feature of the plant, the flower being a large and the fragrant bloom that floats on the surface of the water or is held slightly above the surface by the stout peduncle that supports the flower. The flowers of the Aethelaran water lily are a white to the pale pink in the colour, the petals being numerous and being arranged in the concentric rings that surround the centre of the flower where the reproductive structures are located. The flower opens in the day and closes in the evening, the opening and the closing being the rhythm that the flower follows through the days of the flowering period that extends over the summer months.

## Habitat and Distribution

The water lily grows in the still and the shallow water of the lakes and the ponds of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the plant being found in the water that is no deeper than five to six feet and where the bottom is the soft mud that the rhizome of the plant requires. The water lily is distributed across the lakes of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the other regions of Aethelar where the still water and the rich sediment provide the conditions that the plant favours. The water lily is also found in the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) where the slow current and the shallow depth create the habitat that the plant occupies, the water lily being among the plants that the marsh supports.

The water lily requires the quiet water for the growth, the plant being unable to establish in the fast-flowing rivers or the streams where the current would dislodge the floating leaves and the flowers. The water lily is most abundant in the sheltered bays and the protected coves of the lakes where the wind and the waves are reduced by the surrounding land, the shelter being the condition that the plant requires for the display of the flowers and the production of the seeds.

## Harvest and Gathering

The gathering of the water lily is the activity that the lake communities of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) conduct in the summer months when the flowers are open and the leaves are fully expanded, the gatherers wading into the shallow water to harvest the rhizomes and the flowers that the plant produces. The rhizome is the principal product of the harvest, the gatherers pulling the rhizome from the mud by the hand or with the hooks that the gatherers use to grip the slippery and the heavy root. The harvested rhizomes are carried to the shore in the baskets that the gatherers use, the rhizomes being cleaned and prepared for the use or the storage that the harvest is destined for.

The flowers of the water lily are gathered with the care that the fragility of the petals requires, the flowers being cut at the surface of the water and being placed in the containers that protect the petals from the bruising and the damage that the handling would cause. The flowers are gathered in the morning when the bloom is at the fullest and the fragrance is at the most intense, the timing being the factor that the gatherers follow to ensure the quality of the harvest. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the gatherers regulate the harvest of the water lily and the access to the gathering grounds, the regulation being the means of preventing the over-harvesting that would deplete the wild populations.

## Culinary Uses

The rhizome of the water lily is the edible portion of the plant that the lake communities of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) use in the preparation of the dishes that the lake diet includes. The rhizome is sliced and dried for the storage, the dried slices being the form in which the rhizome is preserved for the use in the winter months when the fresh produce is not available. The dried rhizome is reconstituted by the soaking and the boiling that the preparation requires, the reconstituted rhizome being added to the soups and the stews that the lake households produce, the rhizome contributing the texture and the nutrition that the dishes benefit from.

The fresh rhizome is also consumed in the regions where the water lily is harvested, the fresh root being boiled or roasted and served as the vegetable that the lake diet includes. The flavour of the water lily rhizome is a mild and the slightly sweet flavour that resembles the flavour of the [water chestnut](/content/species/flora/water-chestnut), the two plants being the aquatic species that the lake communities of Aethelar harvest for the food that the water provides. The [feasting and dining](/content/culture/feasting-and-dining) traditions of the lake districts include the water lily rhizome as the ingredient that is featured in the dishes that are served at the summer festivals that celebrate the gathering of the aquatic crops.

## Medicinal and Calming Properties

The flowers of the water lily are used in the preparation of the infusions that are drunk for the calming of the nerves and the relief of the anxiety that afflicts the people of the cities, the infusion being the remedy that the [healing and restoration](/content/magic/healing-and-restoration) practitioners and the herbalists of the Aethelari regions recommend. The petals of the water lily are dried and steeped in the hot water to produce the infusion that is consumed in the evening for the relaxation that the infusion promotes, the calming effect being the property that the flower is valued for.

The dried petals of the water lily are also added to potpourris placed in the rooms of houses to provide a pleasant scent, valued by the households of the Aethelari cities for its delicate sweetness. The potpourris are the products that the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the apothecaries and the herbalists prepare, the potpourris being among the goods that the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the cities trade. The water lily petals are the ingredient that the apothecaries select for the quality of the scent and the calming property that the petals provide.

## Ecological Role

The water lily plays a role in the ecology of the lakes and the ponds where the plant grows, the floating leaves providing the shade that the [Carp](/content/species/fauna/carp) and the other aquatic animals use, the shade being the feature that the fish seek in the heat of the summer. The [carp](/content/species/fauna/carp) and the [pike](/content/species/fauna/pike) that inhabit the lakes of Aethelar are among the fish that find the shelter beneath the leaves of the water lily, the fish being the predators and the prey that the ecology of the lake supports. The [Duck and Goose](/content/species/fauna/duck-and-goose) that live on the lakes also use the water lily, the birds consuming the seeds and the rhizomes that the plant produces.

The water lily is also the host for the [Honey Bee](/content/species/fauna/honey-bee) that pollinate the flowers, the flowers being the source of the nectar and the pollen that the insects collect. The [Honey Bee](/content/species/fauna/honey-bee) that visit the water lily flowers are the pollinators that the plant depends on for the production of the seeds, the pollination being the service that the insects provide in the exchange for the nectar that the flowers yield. The ecological role of the water lily is the role that the plant plays in the web of the relationships that the lake ecosystem comprises, the water lily being the component that the ecology of the Aethelari lakes requires.

## Cultural Significance

The water lily has a place in the culture and the symbolism of the [Aethelari](/content/language/spoken/aethelari) peoples that is based on the beauty and the serenity of the flower, the water lily being associated with the qualities of the tranquillity and the purity that the plant represents. The water lily is the motif that appears in the [Textiles and Cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) and the decoration of the Aethelari households, the image of the water lily being the symbol that the designers use to evoke the peace and the calm that the flower embodies. The [folk omens and superstitions](/content/mythology/folk-omens-and-superstitions) of the lake communities include the belief that the water lily flowering in the abundance is the sign of the good fortune that the year will bring, the flowering being the indicator of the health of the lake that the community depends on.
