## The Poppy of the Sahrani Gardens

The poppy that is cultivated in the gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the oases of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) produces a sap that is the most powerful painkiller known to the healers of the three continents. The sap is collected by cutting the unripe seed pods of the poppy in the early morning, when the sap flows most freely, and the collected sap is dried into a dark resin that is stored in sealed containers.

The resin of the poppy is administered in tiny quantities, for the effect of the resin is powerful and the difference between a healing dose and a lethal dose is small. The healers who use the poppy resin are trained in its administration, and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the apothecaries regulate the trade in the resin with the strictness that the danger of the substance demands. The resin is used in the treatment of the most severe pain, the pain of the major injuries and the terminal illnesses that the other remedies cannot relieve.

The cultivation of the poppy in the gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is regulated by the authorities of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) and the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), and the trade in the resin is restricted to the apothecaries who hold the licences that are granted by the guilds. The misuse of the poppy resin is a problem that the authorities of the cities are aware of, and the penalties for the possession of the resin without a licence are among the most severe in the legal codes of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) states.

## Physical Characteristics of the Poppy

The poppy is an annual plant that grows to a height of two to four feet, the stem being erect and covered with the coarse hairs that give the plant a greyish-green appearance. The leaves of the poppy are lobed and toothed, the lower leaves being broader and the upper leaves being narrower, and the leaves clasp the stem with the bases that extend around the circumference of the stem. The flowers of the poppy are large and showy, the petals being white, pink, or purple, and the flowers are borne on the tall stems that rise above the leaves.

The fruit of the poppy is a rounded capsule that contains the seeds and the sap, the capsule being the source of the resin that is the valuable product of the plant. The capsule is formed after the petals have fallen, and the sap that is collected from the capsule is the latex that exudes from the cuts that are made in the wall of the unripe capsule. The seeds of the poppy are small and white or grey, and the seeds are used in the baking of the breads and the pastries that are the products of the Sahrani kitchens.

## Cultivation and the Harvest of the Resin

The poppy is cultivated in the irrigated gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the oases of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), the plant requiring the warm climate and the abundant water that are the conditions of the Sahrani interior. The poppy is sown in the autumn and the plants grow through the winter, the flowering occurring in the spring and the capsules forming in the late spring and the early summer. The poppy is a labour-intensive crop, the harvest of the resin requiring the daily attention of the cultivators during the weeks that the capsules are producing the sap.

The resin is collected by the cutting of the unripe capsules in the early morning, when the sap flows most freely, the cuts being made with the small knives that are specially designed for the purpose. The sap that exudes from the cuts is white at first, but it darkens on exposure to the air, and the resin that is collected is the darkened sap that is scraped from the surface of the capsule with the blunt blades. The collected resin is dried in the sun and stored in the sealed containers that protect it from the moisture and the light that would degrade the quality of the product.

## The Medicinal Use of the Poppy Resin

The resin of the poppy is administered in tiny quantities, for the effect of the resin is powerful and the difference between a healing dose and a lethal dose is small. The healers who use the poppy resin are trained in its administration, and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the apothecaries regulate the trade in the resin with the strictness that the danger of the substance demands. The resin is used in the treatment of the most severe pain, the pain of the major injuries and the terminal illnesses that the other remedies cannot relieve.

The resin is also used in the preparation of the tinctures and the elixirs that are the remedies of the apothecaries, the resin being dissolved in the alcohol that extracts the active principles of the sap. The tincture of the poppy is a standard remedy of the apothecaries, and the tincture is used in the treatment of the coughs and the diarrhoea that afflict the patients of the cities and the countryside. The tincture is also used as a sedative, the small doses that are prescribed by the healers producing a sleep that is deep and dreamless.

## The Regulation of the Poppy Trade

The cultivation of the poppy in the gardens of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is regulated by the authorities of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) and the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), and the trade in the resin is restricted to the apothecaries who hold the licences that are granted by the guilds. The fields where the poppy is grown are registered with the authorities, and the quantity of the resin that is produced is recorded and accounted for at every stage of the trade, from the harvest to the sale of the final product.

The misuse of the poppy resin is a problem that the authorities of the cities are aware of, and the penalties for the possession of the resin without a licence are among the most severe in the legal codes of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) states. The resin is smoked by the addicts who have acquired the habit, and the smoking of the resin produces a euphoria that is followed by a lethargy that incapacitates the user and leads to the dependence that is the ruin of the addicts. The authorities of the cities prosecute the dealers who supply the resin to the unlicensed users, and the penalties for the dealing in the resin include the forfeiture of the property and the exile of the convicted.

## The Poppy Seeds and Their Uses

The seeds of the poppy are not subject to the regulation that applies to the resin, for the seeds do not contain the active principles of the sap, and the seeds are used in the baking of the breads and the pastries that are the products of the Sahrani kitchens. The seeds are small and nutty in flavour, and the bread that is sprinkled with the poppy seeds is a common sight in the bakeries of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra). The seeds are also pressed to produce an oil that is used in the cooking and in the preparation of the paints that are used by the artists of the coast.

The oil of the poppy seeds is a light and clear oil that dries to a thin and hard film, and the oil is used as a medium for the pigments that are used in the painting of the panels and the canvases. The oil is also used in the preparation of the varnishes that protect the paintings, and the oil of the poppy seed is valued by the artists for the clarity and the lack of the yellowing that is the characteristic of the other drying oils. The poppy seed oil is also used in the lamps that burn in the temples of the coast, the oil being considered the purest of the oils that are available for the lighting.
