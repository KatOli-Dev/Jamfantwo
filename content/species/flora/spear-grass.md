## The Spear Grass of the Great Grass Plains

The spear grass is the dominant grass of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), a tall and robust species that grows to the height of a man and that produces the seed heads that are the principal food of the herds of the [Plains Thunder Boar](/content/species/fauna/plains-thunder-boar) and the other grazing animals of the region.

## The Perennial Grasses of the Great Grass Plains

The spear grass is a warm-season grass that begins its growth in the spring after the last of the frosts and that reaches its peak growth in the summer when the temperatures are high and the rainfall is abundant. The grass cures on the stem in the autumn, and the cured grass provides the forage that sustains the herds through the winter months when the fresh growth is not available.

## Habitat and Distribution

The spear grass is the dominant species of the tallgrass prairie that covers the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the spear grass is the species that gives the plains the character and the ecology that define the region. The spear grass grows in the deep and the fertile soils that the plains possess, the soils being the loams and the clays that the glaciers of the ancient age deposited and that the centuries of the grass growth have enriched with the organic matter of the roots and the leaves. The distribution of the spear grass extends from the eastern margins of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) where the prairie gives way to the woodlands of the eastern Aethelar to the western edge where the rainfall diminishes and the prairie transitions to the shortgrass steppe that is dominated by the [buffalo grass](/content/species/flora/buffalo-grass) and the other species of the drier conditions. The spear grass is the dominant species in the central plains where the rainfall and the soil depth are the greatest, and the spear grass is the tallest and the most productive in the central region of the plains that is the heart of the tallgrass prairie ecosystem.

The spear grass is also found in the smaller areas of the tallgrass prairie that occur in the other regions of Aethelar where the conditions of the climate and the soil favour the grass over the trees, and the spear grass grows in the openings and the clearings of the forested regions and in the river valleys where the flooding in the spring and the fire in the summer maintain the grassland against the advance of the woodland.

The spear grass is the dominant of the several species of the tallgrasses that compose the tallgrass prairie of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains), and the spear grass is the species that grows the tallest and that produces the most of the biomass of the prairie community.
