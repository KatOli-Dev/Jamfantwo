## Oats as the Grain of the Cool Regions

Oats are the third grain of the northern regions, grown primarily as feed for the [Horse](/content/species/fauna/horse) and other working animals but also consumed by human populations in the cooler climates of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The oat plant thrives in conditions that are too wet or too acidic for barley or wheat, and it is often grown in the upland pastures and the reclaimed moorlands of the western districts where the rainfall is heavy and the soil is poor.

The grain of the oat is harvested and processed in the same manner as wheat and barley, but the hard husk that encloses the grain requires a more vigorous threshing to separate it from the edible kernel. The husked grain is ground into oatmeal or rolled into flakes that are cooked into porridge or baked into oatcakes that are a staple of the western regions. The horses of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the cavalry troops of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are fed on oats that give them the energy for the long marches and the hard campaigns that the armies of Aethelar undertake.

The cultivation of oats serves a secondary purpose in the rotation of crops, for the oat plant produces a dense root system that improves the structure of the soil and suppresses the growth of weeds. Farmers who include oats in their rotation find that the following crop of wheat or barley yields more heavily than it would if oats had not preceded it, and the practice of including oats in the rotation is recommended by the agricultural advisors of the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) who study such matters.

## Physical Characteristics

The oat is an annual grass that grows to a height of two to four feet, the stems being slender and erect and the leaves being broad and flat and arranged alternately along the stem. The seed head of the oat is a panicle that is open and spreading, the individual spikelets being borne on the fine branches that hang from the main stem, and the spikelets containing the grains that are enclosed in the husks that are the chaff of the oat. The oat is a plant that is adapted to the cool and the wet conditions of the northern regions, the root system being fibrous and dense and the leaves being tolerant of the cold and the damp that would damage the wheat and the barley.

The grain of the oat is the kernel that is enclosed in the hard husk that is called the hull, the hull being the structure that protects the grain from the moisture and the pests that would damage the seed. The hull of the oat is the structure that makes the threshing of the oat more difficult than the threshing of the wheat and the barley, the hull being firmly attached to the grain and requiring the vigorous milling that separates the grain from the husk. The grain of the oat is rich in the protein and the fat that make the oat a valuable food for the humans and the animals, the oat being the grain that contains the highest proportion of the fat of any of the cereal grains that are grown in the known world.

## Cultivation and the Growing Season

The cultivation of the oat is the practice that is suited to the cool and the wet regions of the north, the oat being the grain that is the most tolerant of the conditions that the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the western [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) provide. The oat is sown in the spring, the seed being broadcast in the prepared fields and covered with a light layer of the soil, and the oat is also sown in the autumn in the regions where the winter is mild enough to allow the survival of the crop. The oat that is sown in the autumn is the oat that is called the winter oat, the winter oat being the crop that produces the earlier harvest and the higher yield than the spring oat that is sown in the regions where the winter is too severe for the autumn sowing.

The oat requires a moist and the cool climate for the best growth, the plant being intolerant of the heat and the drought that would cause the wheat and the barley to thrive. The oat is grown in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the western coast of Aethelar where the rainfall is heavy and the summers are cool, the oat being the grain that is the most reliable crop in the conditions that the upland and the coastal regions provide. The [agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) of the northern regions is based on the oat, the oat being the grain that is the staple of the diet and the fodder that is the foundation of the [livestock and herding](/content/economy/livestock-and-herding) economy of the north.

## The Harvesting and the Processing

The harvesting of the oat is the operation that is performed in the late summer when the grain has ripened and the stems have turned from the green to the gold, the stage of the maturity that the farmer recognises by the colour and the hardness of the grain. The oat is harvested by the cutting of the stems with the sickles or the reaping machines that are the equipment of the grain harvest, the cut stems being tied in the bundles that are called the sheaves and the sheaves being stood in the stooks that allow the grain to dry in the field. The dried sheaves are carried to the barns where the grain is threshed by the flails or the threshing machines that separate the grain from the straw.

The threshed oat is the grain that is enclosed in the hull, the hull being the structure that must be removed before the grain is usable as the food. The hulling of the oat is the operation that is performed by the mill that is equipped with the stones that crack the hull and the sieves that separate the grain from the chaff, the hulling being the process that is the special feature of the oat milling. The [milling and baking](/content/economy/milling-and-baking) industry includes the oat milling as one of the operations that the mills perform, the oatmeal that is produced being the flour that is the base of the porridge and the oatcakes that are the foods of the northern regions.

## The Oat as the Food and the Fodder

The oat that is consumed by the human populations of the northern regions is the oat that is prepared as the porridge and the oatcakes that are the staple foods of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The porridge is the food that is made by the boiling of the oatmeal in the water or the milk, the porridge being the dish that is the breakfast of the northern people and the food that is the foundation of the daily diet. The oatcakes are the bread that is made by the baking of the oatmeal on the griddle or in the oven, the oatcakes being the bread that is the characteristic of the western regions and the bread that is the alternative to the wheaten bread that is the staple of the lowland regions.

The oat that is used as the fodder is the oat that is fed to the [Horse](/content/species/fauna/horse) and the [oxen](/content/species/fauna/oxen) that are the working animals of the northern settlements, the oat being the grain that provides the concentrated energy that the working animals need for the labour that the farm and the road demand. The [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the cavalry of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are the forces that depend on the oat for the feeding of the horses that are the mounts of the soldiers and the scouts, the oat being the grain that gives the horses the stamina and the strength that the military operations require. The oat straw that remains after the threshing is the fodder that is fed to the [cattle](/content/species/fauna/cattle) and the [sheep](/content/species/fauna/sheep) in the winter, the straw being the feed that is the supplement to the [hay crop](/content/species/flora/hay-crop) that is the foundation of the winter feeding.

## The Crop Rotation and the Soil

The oat is the crop that is valued in the rotation of the crops for the improvement of the soil that the oat provides, the dense root system of the oat breaking up the soil and the organic matter that the oat returns to the soil adding the fertility that the following crop benefits from. The oat is the crop that is grown in the rotation that the northern farmers practice, the oat being the crop that follows the [fodder crop](/content/species/flora/fodder-crop) and that precedes the wheat or the barley in the cycle that the rotation prescribes. The farmers who include the oat in the rotation are the farmers who achieve the higher yields and the better quality in the following crops, the oat being the crop that is the means by which the soil is maintained and the fertility is improved.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the agricultural advisors recommend the inclusion of the oat in the rotation as the practice that is the most beneficial for the soil and the most productive for the farmer, the recommendation being based on the studies and the observations that the advisors have conducted in the farming regions of the north. The [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) and the other northern peoples who practice the rotation are the peoples who have maintained the tradition of the oat in the rotation for the centuries, the tradition being the knowledge that has been passed from the father to the son in the farming families of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The oat is the crop that is the foundation of the sustainable [agriculture](/content/economy/agriculture) of the northern regions, and the oat is the grain that is the basis of the food and the fodder that the northern settlements depend upon.
