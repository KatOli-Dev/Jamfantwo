The nettle weed is the stinging plant of the waste places and the disturbed soils across Aethelar, a persistent nuisance to the farmer and the gardener but a valued resource for the healers who brew its leaves into medicinal teas and the weavers who spin its stems into fibre.

## The Nettle of the Waste Places

The nettle that grows in the waste places and the disturbed soils of every region of Aethelar is a weed that is both a nuisance and a resource. The nettle is a stinging plant that causes a painful rash when the skin comes into contact with the hairs of the leaves, and the nettle is a common problem in the gardens and the pastures where the plant competes with the crops and the grasses.

The nettle is difficult to control in the gardens and the pastures, for the plant reproduces from the seeds and from the fragments of the root, and the nettle is tolerant of the poor soil and the dry conditions that would kill the other plants. The farmers of Aethelar have developed the method of the repeated cutting of the nettle in the early summer, when the plant is at the peak of the growth and the cutting weakens the root system and reduces the vigour of the plant.

The nettle is valued by the healers and the weavers of the northern settlements, for the leaves of the nettle are used in the preparation of the medicinal teas and the stems of the nettle are used in the production of the fibre that is woven into the cloth. The nettle is also a host plant for the caterpillars of the butterflies that are valued for the beauty and the diversity that they bring to the gardens.

## Physical Characteristics

The nettle weed is a perennial herb that grows to a height of three to six feet, the stems being erect and square and the leaves being arranged in the opposite pairs that are characteristic of the [nettle](/content/species/flora/nettle) family. The leaves of the nettle weed are oval and toothed, the surfaces being dark green and the undersides being paler, and the leaves and the stems being covered with the stiff stinging hairs that are the defensive apparatus of the plant. The stinging hairs are the hollow structures that contain the formic acid and the histamine that are released when the hairs are broken, the chemicals causing the painful rash and the inflammation that the contact with the nettle weed produces.

The root system of the nettle weed is extensive and creeping, the roots spreading by the underground stems that are called the rhizomes, and the nettle weed forming the dense colonies that are the characteristic of the waste places where the plant is established. The rhizomes of the nettle weed are the structures that make the plant so difficult to control, the fragments of the rhizome that are left in the soil after the digging or the cultivating being capable of producing the new plants that re-establish the colony. The nettle weed is a plant that is adapted to the disturbed soils and the competition that the waste places provide, the plant being one of the most resilient and the most persistent of the weeds that the farmer and the gardener must contend with.

## Habitat and the Invasive Nature

The nettle weed is a plant of the waste places and the disturbed soils, the species being found in every region of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) where the soil has been disturbed and the vegetation has been removed. The nettle weed is a plant that is associated with the human habitation, the plant being found in the gardens and the pastures and the roadsides and the rubbish tips that are the features of the settled landscape. The nettle weed is a plant that thrives in the rich and the disturbed soils that the human activity creates, the plant being one of the species that has followed the human settlement and the cultivation across the continent.

The invasive nature of the nettle weed is the characteristic that makes the plant a nuisance to the farmer and the gardener, the nettle weed being a plant that colonises the disturbed soils and the waste places with the speed and the density that overwhelm the other plants. The nettle weed is a plant that competes with the crops and the garden plants for the light and the water and the nutrients, the nettle weed being a plant that is taller and more vigorous than the most of the plants that it grows among, and the nettle weed being a plant that shades and suppresses the plants that are beneath it. The [nettle weed](/content/species/flora/nettle-weed) is a plant that is also a problem in the pastures, the nettle being unpalatable to the [cattle](/content/species/fauna/cattle) and the [sheep](/content/species/fauna/sheep) that graze the fields, and the nettle weed being a plant that reduces the productivity of the pasture by the competition and the shading that the dense colonies produce.

## Control and the Management

The control of the nettle weed is a perennial problem for the farmers and the gardeners of Aethelar, the plant being too resilient and too persistent to be eliminated by the ordinary methods of the weed control. The digging of the nettle weed is the method that is most effective, the digging being done in the early spring when the soil is moist and the rhizomes are near the surface, the rhizomes being lifted and removed from the soil with the forks and the spades that the farmer uses. The digging of the nettle weed is a laborious and the time-consuming operation, the thorough removal of the rhizomes being the only method that prevents the re-growth of the colony, and the farmer who does not remove all the rhizomes will find the nettle weed re-establishing in the following season.

The repeated cutting of the nettle weed is the method that is used by the farmers who cannot dig the entire colony, the cutting being done in the early summer when the plant is at the peak of the growth and the cutting weakens the root system and reduces the vigour of the plant. The cutting of the nettle weed is the method that requires the persistence and the regularity, the cutting being done three or four times in the season to deplete the reserves of the root system and to prevent the plant from producing the seeds that would spread the colony. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the agricultural advisors recommend the combination of the digging and the cutting as the most effective method of the nettle weed control, and the farmers who follow the recommendation are the farmers who achieve the best results in the management of the weed.

## The Nettle as a Resource

The nettle weed is a plant that is not only a nuisance but also a resource, the nettle being a plant that is valued by the healers and the weavers of the northern settlements for the properties that the leaves and the stems provide. The leaves of the nettle weed are used in the preparation of the medicinal teas that are the spring tonic of the northern settlements, the leaves being gathered in the spring when the young growth is at its most potent, and the dried leaves being stored for the use through the year. The [nettle](/content/species/flora/nettle) tea is the remedy that is used for the purification of the blood and the treatment of the rheumatic pains, and the nettle weed is the plant that is the source of the leaves that the healers use.

The stems of the nettle weed are used in the production of the [nettle fibre](/content/species/flora/nettle-fibre) that is woven into the cloth of the northern settlements, the stems being harvested in the autumn when the plant is dead and the fibre is easier to extract. The nettle fibre is the fibre that is used in the production of the undergarments and the household linens that are the everyday textiles of the northern people, the nettle being the material that is soft and comfortable and suitable for the uses that the [flax](/content/species/flora/flax) and the [hemp](/content/species/flora/hemp) do not serve as well. The [textiles and cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) industry of the northern regions includes the nettle fibre as a minor but the significant component, and the nettle weed is the plant that is the resource for the fibre-makers who produce the cloth.

## The Butterfly Host Plant

The nettle weed is a plant that is valued by the naturalists and the gardeners of the northern settlements for the role that the plant plays as the host for the caterpillars of the butterflies that are the most beautiful of the insects of the northern regions. The nettle weed is the plant on which the caterpillars of the peacock and the small tortoiseshell and the red admiral butterflies feed, the caterpillars being the larvae that consume the leaves of the nettle and that develop into the adult butterflies that are the ornaments of the gardens and the meadows. The nettle weed is the plant that is essential for the survival of these butterflies, the caterpillars being unable to feed on the other plants and the butterflies being dependent on the nettle for the reproduction.

The gardeners who value the butterflies in their gardens are the gardeners who tolerate the nettle weed in the corners and the margins of the garden, the nettle being left for the caterpillars and the butterflies that the nettle supports. The naturalists of the northern settlements have studied the relationship between the nettle weed and the butterflies, the studies revealing the importance of the nettle for the diversity and the beauty of the butterfly populations that the northern regions support. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) regard the nettle weed as a plant that is part of the balance of the forest and the meadow, the nettle being the plant that supports the insects that are the food of the birds and the other creatures that are the inhabitants of the natural world.
