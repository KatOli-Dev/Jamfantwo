## The Ironbark Tree of the Dwarven Halls

The ironbark tree is a tree that grows only in the underground halls of the dwarven holds, a species that has been cultivated by the dwarves for the centuries for the wood that is used in the construction of the magical foci and the implements of magic. The ironbark is a slow-growing tree that thrives in the conditions of the underground environment, and the wood of the ironbark is the hardest and the most resistant to the magical energies of any material in the known world.

The wood of the ironbark is used in the construction of the staves and the wands that the mages use to channel magic, and the wood is also used in the construction of the circles and the barriers that contain the magical energies within the designated area. The dwarven woodworkers who craft the foci from the ironbark are the most skilled of the artisans of the underground halls, and the tools that they produce are valued by the mages of the three continents.

## Physical Characteristics

The ironbark tree is a slow-growing evergreen that reaches a height of fifteen to twenty feet in the conditions of the underground halls, the trunk being short and thick and the branches being gnarled and spreading. The bark of the ironbark is dark grey and deeply furrowed, the bark being so dense and hard that it can be worked only with the tools of the metal and the stone that the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) have developed for the purpose. The leaves of the ironbark are small and leathery, the leaves being a dark green colour that is almost black in the dim light of the underground environment, and the leaves persist on the tree for many years before they are shed.

The wood of the ironbark is the most remarkable feature of the tree, the timber being a dark reddish brown colour that is so dense that it sinks in the water and so hard that it resists the sharpest metal tools. The grain of the ironbark is fine and interlocked, the wood being resistant to the splitting and the fracture that would destroy the tools made of the softer timbers, and the wood has a natural resistance to the fire and the heat that is greater than the resistance of the other timbers. The ironbark wood also has the property that is unique among the timbers of the known world, the wood being resistant to the magical energies that would damage or destroy the other materials, and it is this property that makes the ironbark so valuable to the mages who practice magic.

## Habitat and the Underground Environment

The ironbark tree is a species that is found only in the underground environment of the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) halls, the tree being adapted to the conditions of the cool and the damp and the dark that prevail in the deep places where the dwarves have made their homes. The ironbark grows in the chambers that are lit by the [glow fungus](/content/species/flora/glow-fungus) and the other sources of the underground light, the tree being able to photosynthesise in the dim and the spectral light that the underground provides. The ironbark requires a supply of the water that is provided by the underground streams and the springs, and the tree is grown in the chambers where the water is available and the drainage is sufficient to prevent the waterlogging that would damage the roots.

The natural habitat of the ironbark is the deep caverns and the fissures that are found in the regions of the igneous rock that underlie the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) and the other dwarven territories. The ironbark is not found in the surface world, and the attempts to grow the tree in the open air or in the conditions of the surface have consistently failed, the tree requiring the stable temperature and the humidity and the spectral light of the underground to thrive. The dwarves who have studied the ironbark believe that the tree is a relic of the ancient forests that once grew in the deep places of the world, the ironbark being one of the species that has survived from the age when the underground was connected to the surface and the trees could grow in the light of the sun.

## Cultivation and the Dwarven Woodworkers

The cultivation of the ironbark is a practice that is unique to the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) halls, the dwarves being the only people who have developed the methods for the deliberate and the controlled growth of the tree. The ironbark is propagated by the seeds that are produced by the mature trees and by the grafting that is used to reproduce the selected varieties that have the superior qualities of the wood. The seeds of the ironbark are slow to germinate, the seeds requiring the period of the cold and the damp that the underground provides, and the seedlings grow slowly, the tree reaching the size at which the wood is suitable for the working only after fifty or more years of the growth.

The dwarven woodworkers who craft the foci from the ironbark are the artisans who have spent the decades in the mastery of the techniques that the working of the ironbark requires. The ironbark wood is so hard that it can be worked only with the tools of the tungsten and the diamond that the dwarves have developed, and the shaping of the staves and the wands is a slow and the painstaking process that can occupy the woodworker for months or years. The woodworkers who are entrusted with the ironbark are the masters of the craft, the position being one of the most respected in the dwarven halls, and the foci that they produce are the treasures that are valued by the mages of the known world.

## The Magical Properties and the Uses

The ironbark wood is the material that is most valued by the mages for the construction of the foci that channel and direct the magical energies, the staves and the wands that are made from the ironbark being the instruments that the most powerful mages prefer. The ironbark has the property of the resistance to the magical energies that would damage or destroy the other materials, the wood being able to contain and to direct the flows of magic without the degradation or the failure that would occur in the instruments made of the other woods. The staves that are made from the ironbark are the staves that are used in the working of the most powerful spells, and the wands that are crafted from the ironbark are the wands that are used in the delicate and the precise work that magic requires.

The ironbark is also used in the construction of the circles and the barriers that contain the magical energies within the designated area, the wood being set into the floors and the walls of the chambers where the magical work is performed. The circles that are made from the ironbark are the circles that are used in the summoning and the binding that the most powerful mages perform, the wood being the material that can withstand the energies that are released in these operations. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) have their own traditions of the magical wood, but the ironbark is the material that is preferred by the mages who work with the most powerful and the most dangerous energies, and the demand for the ironbark foci is the demand that the dwarven woodworkers struggle to meet.

## Trade and the Value of the Ironbark

The ironbark is a material that is traded only in the small quantities that the dwarven woodworkers produce, the foci that are made from the ironbark being among the most expensive of the items that are bought and sold in the known world. The staves and the wands that are crafted from the ironbark are the items that are commissioned by the mages who can afford the prices that the dwarven woodworkers charge, and the waiting list for the ironbark foci is measured in the years and the decades. The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) are the principal source of the ironbark foci, and the trade in the ironbark is a significant component of the wealth of the Stoneheim dwarves.

The ironbark is also used in the manufacture of the amulets and the talismans that the dwarves and the other peoples wear for the protection against the magical energies, the small pieces of the ironbark being shaped and inscribed with the runes that are the protective symbols of the dwarven tradition. The amulets that are made from the ironbark are the amulets that are worn by the dwarven warriors and the miners who work in the conditions where the magical energies are present, and the amulets are also traded to the mages and the other peoples who value the protection that the ironbark provides. The trade in the ironbark amulets is a minor but the significant part of the commerce between the dwarven halls and the surface world, and the amulets that are made from the ironbark are among the most sought of the dwarven products.
