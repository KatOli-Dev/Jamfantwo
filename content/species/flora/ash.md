## Ash

The ash is the elastic and strong wood of the river valleys of Aethelar, its timber preferred for the shafts of spears, the bows of archers, the handles of tools, and the frames of wagons and carts.

## Ash for the Shafts of Spears and the Handles of Tools

The ash tree grows throughout the temperate regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), preferring the deeper soils of the river valleys and the lower slopes of the central highlands. The wood of the ash is elastic and strong, properties that make it the material of choice for the shafts of spears, the handles of tools, and the frames of wagons and carts that must withstand the shocks and stresses of heavy use.

The ash is a faster-growing tree than the oak, reaching timber size in forty to fifty years, and the ash forests of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) produce a steady supply of wood for the manufacturing industries of the settlements. The wood of the ash is pale in colour, with a straight grain that makes it easy to work, and it is used in the production of the bows that are the primary ranged weapon of the armies of Aethelar. The bowyers of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) prefer the ash of the river valleys for its combination of strength and flexibility, and the longbows that they produce are among the most effective weapons in the hands of the northern archers.

The ash also produces a timber that is used in the construction of furniture, particularly the chairs and the tables that must withstand the daily use of a busy household. The wood of the ash is not as decorative as the oak or the walnut, but its strength and durability make it a practical choice for the pieces that must serve their purpose without fail.

## Physical Characteristics

The ash is a large deciduous tree that reaches a height of sixty to eighty feet in the favourable conditions of the river valleys, and the trunk of the ash is straight and cylindrical, free of the branches for the lower half of the tree. The bark of the ash is grey and smooth in the young trees, developing the fissures and the ridges that characterise the older trees. The crown of the ash is open and spreading, and the branches are arranged in the opposite pairs that are the distinctive feature of the species.

The leaves of the ash are compound and opposite, composed of the leaflets that are arranged in the pairs along the central stalk. The leaflets are lance-shaped and toothed, and the leaves appear late in the spring, later than the leaves of the oak and the beech. The ash produces the clusters of the winged seeds that are called the keys, and the seeds are dispersed by the wind that carries them from the branches to the ground. The seeds of the ash are produced in the years that are favourable to the flowering, and the ash does not produce the heavy seed crops every year.

## Habitat and Distribution

The ash is a tree of the temperate regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the ash is found in the river valleys and the lower slopes of the central highlands where the soils are deep and the moisture is reliable. The ash is a tree of the mixed forests, and the ash grows in the company of the [oak](/content/species/flora/oak) and the [beech](/content/species/flora/beech) in the forests that cover the central and the eastern regions of the continent. The ash is not found in the cold regions of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) uplands or the arid regions of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), and the distribution of the ash is limited by the requirements of the tree for the moisture and the warmth.

The ash is most abundant in the river valleys of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), where the alluvial soils of the floodplains provide the conditions that the tree prefers. The ash is also found on the lower slopes of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range), where the soils are deeper and the rainfall is more reliable than on the higher slopes. The ash forests of these regions are managed by the [timber and logging](/content/economy/timber-and-logging) trade, and the ash is harvested in the rotation that ensures the sustainable supply of the wood.

## Military Uses

The ash is the timber of war, and the wood of the ash is the material that is preferred for the shafts of the spears and the bows that are the weapons of the soldiers and the hunters of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The elasticity of the ash wood allows the spear shaft to flex without breaking when the spear is thrust into the shield or the body of the enemy, and the strength of the ash wood allows the bow to store the energy that is released when the arrow is loosed. The bowyers of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) select the ash that grows in the river valleys for the bows, and the bows that are made from the ash of the river valleys are the standard weapon of the archers of the northern armies.

The ash is also used in the construction of the shields that the soldiers carry, and the lightness and the strength of the ash wood make it the ideal material for the shields that must be carried for the long marches and the battles. The shields of the ash are covered with the leather and the metal that provide the protection against the arrows and the swords, and the core of the ash wood provides the structure that holds the shield together under the blows of the combat.

## Domestic and Agricultural Uses

The ash is a tree that provides a variety of the products that are used in the daily life of the settlements of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The handles of the axes and the hammers and the other tools are made from the ash wood, and the handles of the ash are strong and resilient, absorbing the shock of the blow without transmitting it to the hand of the user. The frames of the wagons and the carts are made from the ash wood, and the ash is the material that is preferred for the parts of the wagons that must flex and spring under the load.

The ash is also used in the production of the furniture that equips the households of the cities and the towns. The chairs and the tables that are made from the ash are simple and sturdy, and the furniture of the ash is the practical choice for the households that value the durability over the decoration. The ash wood is also used in the production of the tool handles and the implements that are sold in the markets of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) settlements, and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the carpenters and the joiners regulate the trade in the ash products.

## Cultural Significance

The ash is a tree of the great significance in the mythology and the folklore of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) peoples, and the ash is associated with the powers of the protection and the strength. The ash is mentioned in the oldest of the songs and the legends of the continent, and the tree is a symbol of the endurance and the resilience that the peoples of the continent value. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) consider the ash a sacred tree, and the ash groves of the Heartwood are the sites of the rituals and the ceremonies of the elven communities.

The ash is also a tree of the practical wisdom in the folklore of the northern peoples, and the sayings and the proverbs of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) are rich with the references to the ash. The ash is a symbol of the work and the craftsmanship, and the wood of the ash is the material that the craftsman selects when the work demands the strength and the flexibility that the other woods do not provide. The ash is a tree that is woven into the fabric of the daily life of the peoples of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the tree is valued for the products that it provides and the symbolism that it carries.
