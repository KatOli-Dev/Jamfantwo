## Bindweed

The bindweed is the twining perennial weed of the Aethelaran gardens and hedges, a climbing plant that chokes the stems of cultivated vegetables and flowers and requires the careful removal of every fragment of its root from the soil.

## The Bindweed of the Gardens and the Hedges

The bindweed that grows in the gardens and the hedges of the Aethelaran settlements is a climbing plant that twines around the stems of the cultivated plants and chokes the growth of the vegetables and the flowers. The bindweed is a perennial plant that reproduces from the fragments of the root as well as from the seeds, and the bindweed is one of the most difficult of the weeds to control in the garden.

The control of the bindweed requires the careful digging of the roots and the removal of every fragment of the root from the soil, for the plant can regenerate from a piece of the root that is no longer than a finger. The gardeners of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) have developed the method of the smothering of the bindweed by the covering of the infested area with the layers of the straw and the manure that exclude the light and suppress the growth of the plant.

## Methods of Control

The control of the bindweed is a challenge that tests the skill and the patience of the gardeners of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) settlements. The most effective method of the control is the digging of the roots and the removal of the every fragment of the root from the soil, a process that requires the careful excavation of the bed and the sifting of the soil to separate the fragments of the root from the earth. The roots of the bindweed are deep and extensive, and the roots can extend to the depth of several feet in the soil that is suitable for the growth.

The smothering of the bindweed is a method that is used by the gardeners of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), and the method involves the covering of the infested area with the layers of the straw and the manure that exclude the light and prevent the growth of the plant. The smothering must be maintained for the period of a full growing season or more, for the bindweed can survive beneath the covering for the months, and the plant will emerge when the covering is removed if the smothering has not been complete.

The [clover](/content/species/flora/clover) and the other ground-covering plants are used to suppress the bindweed in the pastures and the meadows, for the bindweed cannot compete with the dense growth of the ground-cover that shades the soil and prevents the emergence of the bindweed seedlings. The bindweed is a plant that is controlled by the management of the soil and the competition, and the gardeners who maintain the health of the soil and the density of the planting are the gardeners who are most successful in the control of the bindweed.
