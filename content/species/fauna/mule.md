The mule is the hybrid of the horse and the donkey, combining the strength and the size of the horse with the hardiness and the endurance of the donkey, and the mule is the ideal animal for the transport of the goods through the mountain passes and the rough terrain of the uplands.

## The Mules of the Mountain Passes

The mule is the hybrid of the horse and the donkey, and the mule combines the strength and the size of the horse with the hardiness and the endurance of the donkey. The mule is the ideal animal for the transport of the goods through the mountain passes and the rough terrain of the uplands.

The mules that are used in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) are the principal means of the transport in the regions where the roads are steep and the wagons cannot pass. The mule trains carry the ores and the metals from the mines in the mountains to the forges and the markets in the valleys, and the mules also carry the goods that are traded between the settlements of the uplands.

The breeding of the mules is the work of the specialists who maintain the herds of the mares and the jacks that produce the mules for the trade. The mule is sterile and cannot reproduce, and every mule that is used in the work must be produced from the breeding of the horse and the donkey.

## The Characteristics of the Mule

The mule inherits the qualities of both the parents, and the combination of the traits makes the mule the animal that is suited to the work that neither the horse nor the donkey can perform as well. The mule inherits the size and the strength of the [horse](/content/species/fauna/horse) and the hardiness and the endurance and the sure-footedness of the [donkey](/content/species/fauna/donkey), and the mule is the animal that can carry the heavy loads through the rough terrain and the steep passes where the horse would stumble and the donkey would lack the strength.

The mule is also the animal that inherits the patience and the intelligence that the donkey possesses, and the mule is the animal that can be trusted to navigate the narrow and the dangerous paths without the constant direction that the horse requires. The mule is the animal that will stop at the edge of the precipice and refuse to proceed when the path is unsafe, and the stubbornness that the mule displays is the quality that the experienced muleteer recognises as the sign of the caution that preserves the animal and the load.

## The Mule Trains of the Mountain Routes

The mule trains that carry the goods through the mountain passes are the caravans of the mules that are led by the muleteers who know the routes and the conditions of the trails. The mule trains of the [Stoneheim Highland Road](/content/location/route/trade/stoneheim-highland-road) and the [Frostwall Pass](/content/location/route/trade/frostwall-pass) are the principal means of the transport in the regions where the roads are too steep and the too narrow for the wagons, and the mule trains carry the [Mining and Metals](/content/economy/mining-and-metals) from the mines of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) to the forges and the markets of the valleys.

The mule trains of the [Amber Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/amber-pass) and the [Stonespire Pass](/content/location/natural/feature/stonespire-pass) carry the goods that are traded between [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the mules that are used on these routes are the animals that are bred for the size and the strength that the heavy loads require. The muleteers who lead the trains are the men and the women who have spent the years on the trails, and the muleteers are the people who know the dangers and the shelters of the passes that the trains must cross.

## The Care and the Keeping of the Mule

The care of the mule is the concern of the muleteer who knows the needs of the animal and the conditions that the mule requires for the health and the work. The mule is the animal that can survive on the coarse feed and the minimal water, but the mule that is overworked or underfed will suffer from the ailments that reduce the capacity of the animal for the labour. The mule requires the regular rest and the adequate feed, and the muleteer who cares for the mule will have the animal that serves for the years of the work.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the muleteers and the packmen in the cities of the mountain regions regulate the trade in the mules and the use of the mules in the transport of the goods. The guilds set the standards for the loads that the mule may carry and the distances that the mule may travel in the day, and the guilds also maintain the registers of the mules that are approved for the work and the muleteers who are licensed to lead the trains.

## The Mule in the Economy of the Uplands

The mule is the animal that enables the economy of the upland regions, for the mule is the means by which the goods of the mountains reach the markets of the valleys and the goods of the valleys reach the settlements of the heights. The [mining and metals](/content/economy/mining-and-metals) industry of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) depends on the mule trains that carry the ore from the mines to the forges, and the [timber and logging](/content/economy/timber-and-logging) industry of the uplands uses the mules to carry the timber from the forests to the roads and the rivers.

The mule is also the animal that carries the [textiles and cloth](/content/economy/textiles-and-cloth) and the other goods that are traded between the settlements of the uplands, and the mule is the animal that the merchants of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) and the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) use for the transport of the goods that the markets of the remote settlements require. The mule that is used in the trade is the animal that is valued for the reliability and the endurance that the mountain routes demand.
