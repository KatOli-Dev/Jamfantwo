## The Pigeons and the Doves of the Cities

The pigeons and the doves that are kept in the cities of the known world are the birds that are used for the messenger service and for the meat. The pigeons are the most important of the messenger birds, and the pigeons that are trained to carry the messages between the cities are the most reliable means of the communication over the long distances.

The keeping of the messenger pigeons is the preserve of the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the pigeon keepers, who breed and train the birds that carry the messages of the merchants and the authorities. The pigeons are kept in the lofts that are built on the roofs of the buildings, and the pigeons are trained to return to the loft from the distant locations where the messages are sent.

The squabs of the pigeons and the doves are the young birds that are taken from the nests and slaughtered for the meat. The squab is a delicacy that is valued by the cooks of the wealthy households, and the squab is the most expensive of the poultry meats in the markets of the cities.

## The Messenger Pigeons of the Guilds

The messenger pigeons that are kept by the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the pigeon keepers are the birds that have been bred and trained for the carrying of the messages across the long distances. The training of the messenger pigeon begins when the young bird is taken from the loft to the short distance and released to return to the home, and the distance is gradually increased until the bird can return from the hundreds of the miles to the loft where the bird was raised.

The messenger pigeons are the birds that carry the messages of the merchants who need the information about the prices and the supplies in the distant markets, and the messages are the small scrolls of the parchment that are attached to the leg of the bird in the tiny capsules that the pigeon carries. The messages that the pigeons carry are the information that the merchants use to make the decisions about the buying and the selling that the trade requires, and the speed of the pigeon that crosses the hundreds of the miles in the day gives the merchant who uses the pigeon the advantage over the competitor who waits for the messenger on the horse.

The authorities of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) also use the messenger pigeons for the communication between the cities and the garrisons, and the pigeons that carry the official messages are the birds that are kept in the lofts of the government buildings. The messenger pigeons of the authorities are the birds that are bred from the finest of the stock, and the pigeons that serve the authorities are the birds that are protected by the law from the interference of the persons who would shoot or capture the birds.

## The Doves of the Temples and the Gardens

The doves that are kept in the temples and the gardens of the known world are the birds that are associated with the traditions and the ceremonies of the peoples. The dove is the bird that is released at the weddings and the festivals as the symbol of the peace and the prosperity that the peoples wish for the future, and the dove that returns to the temple is the bird that is regarded as the sign of the favour of the spirits.

The doves of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) are the birds that are kept by the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) in the dovecotes that are built in the trees and the gardens of the elven settlements. The elven doves are the birds that are valued for the beauty and the gentleness that the elves associate with the bird, and the elven doves are the birds that are released at the ceremonies of the [Coming of Age Rites](/content/culture/coming-of-age-rites) that mark the passage of the young elf to the adulthood.

## The Breeding and the Keeping of the Pigeons

The breeding of the pigeons is the practice that the pigeon keepers have refined over the generations, and the breeds that are kept for the messenger service are the birds that are selected for the homing ability and the speed and the endurance that the long-distance carrying requires. The breeds that are kept for the squab production are the birds that are selected for the size and the growth and the frequency of the breeding that the production of the meat demands.

The keeping of the pigeons requires the loft that is built on the roof or the upper floor of the building, and the loft must be dry and the ventilated and the protected from the predators that would take the birds. The loft is fitted with the nesting boxes and the perches and the trap that allows the birds to enter and to leave while preventing the entry of the other birds and the animals. The pigeon keeper who maintains the loft must clean the boxes and the perches and the feed the birds on the grain and the water that the health of the birds requires.

## The Pigeons and the Doves in the Markets

The pigeons and the doves are the birds that are traded in the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the cities, and the live birds and the squabs are the products that the poultry sellers offer. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the pigeon keepers regulate the trade in the birds and the squabs, and the guilds set the standards for the quality and the freshness of the birds that are sold in the markets.

The squab that is sold in the markets of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) and the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) is the meat that is served at the feasts of the wealthy households, and the squab that is prepared by the cooks of the [inns and hospices](/content/economy/inns-and-hospices) is the dish that the travellers of the means order at the table. The squab is the meat that is roasted and the braised and the served with the [plum and cherry](/content/species/flora/plum-and-cherry) and the other fruits that the cooks use in the preparation of the dishes that accompany the bird.
