The keeping of the bees is the practice of the beekeepers who manage the hives that produce the honey and the wax that are used in the households and the industries of the settlements, and the beekeeping is the tradition that is practiced in every region of the known world where the flowers are abundant and the climate is mild.

## The Honey Bees of Aethelar

The honey bees of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) are the most common of the social bees, and the hives of the honey bee are kept in the gardens and the orchards of the northern settlements. The honey bee is a small and industrious insect that lives in the colonies of the tens of thousands of the individuals, and the colony is organised into the castes of the queen, the workers, and the drones.

The honey bee is valued for the honey that the bee produces from the nectar of the flowers, and the honey of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the principal sweetener that is used in the cooking of the northern settlements. The honey is harvested in the late summer, when the honeycomb is full and the bees have stored the honey for the winter.

The gathering of the honey from the hives is the work of the beekeeper, who must calm the bees with the smoke and remove the honeycomb from the hive. The honey is extracted from the comb by the pressing or the draining, and the honey is stored in the sealed vessels that keep the honey fresh for the years.

## The Hives and the Apiaries

The hives that the beekeepers use in Aethelar are the woven baskets of the straw and the twigs that are called the skeps, and the skeps are placed in the apiaries that are sited in the sunny and the sheltered locations where the flowers are abundant. The apiary is the collection of the hives that the beekeeper manages, and the apiary is the place where the beekeeper works through the summer months.

The hives are protected from the weather and the predators by the covers and the stands that raise the hive from the ground. The hives are also protected from the mice and the other animals that would enter the hive and consume the honey and the brood.

## The Honey of the Aethelar Heartwood

The honey that is produced in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is the most valued of the honeys in the known world, for the bees of the Heartwood feed on the flowers of the lime trees and the wildflowers that grow in the forest clearings. The honey of the Heartwood is a pale and aromatic honey that is valued for the flavour and the medicinal properties that the healers of the region attribute to it.

The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Heartwood are the beekeepers who manage the hives of the forest, and the elven beekeepers have developed the methods of the beekeeping that are different from the methods of the human beekeepers. The elven hives are placed in the hollow trees and the caves, and the bees are allowed to swarm and to establish the new colonies in the natural sites.

## The Bees of the Green Crescent

The bees of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana) are the bees that produce the honey from the flowers of the [saffron](/content/species/flora/saffron) and the citrus trees that grow in the irrigated gardens of the coast. The honey of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is a dark and rich honey that is flavoured by the blossoms of the orange and the lemon and the spice plants that the bees visit.

The beekeeping of the Green Crescent is the practice of the gardeners and the farmers who keep the hives in the orchards and the gardens where the bees can pollinate the crops and produce the honey.

## The Wax and the Other Products of the Hive

The wax that the bees produce is the second of the products of the hive, and the wax is used in the production of the candles, the polishes, and the sealants that the craftsmen and the households use. The wax of the honey bee is the finest of the waxes, and the candles that are made from the beeswax are the candles that are used in the ceremonies and the rituals of the temples and the guilds.

The beeswax is also used in the production of the modelling materials and the casting materials that the metalworkers and the sculptors use in the production of the fine metalwork.

## The Life of the Colony

The colony of the honey bee is one of the most complex social structures among the insects of the known world. The queen of the colony is the sole reproductive female, and the queen can lay the eggs at the rate of the thousands per day during the height of the season. The workers are the sterile females that perform all of the labour of the colony, from the cleaning of the cells to the nursing of the brood, the building of the comb, the guarding of the entrance, and the foraging for the nectar and the pollen. The drones are the males whose sole function is the mating with the virgin queens of the other colonies, and the drones are expelled from the hive at the end of the season when they are no longer needed.

The swarming of the colony is the process by which the honey bee propagates the new colonies. The swarm occurs in the late spring or the early summer, when the colony has grown too large for the hive and the old queen departs with the portion of the workers to seek the new home. The swarm settles in the cluster on the branch or the wall while the scouts search for the suitable cavity, and the beekeeper who catches the swarm can establish the new hive from the captured colony.

## The Honey in Medicine and Trade

The honey that is produced by the bees is valued not only as the sweetener but also as the medicine that the healers of the known world prescribe for the ailments of the body. The honey of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is used in the treatment of the wounds and the burns, for the honey has the properties that prevent the growth of the corruption in the injured flesh. The healers of the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) and the human settlements alike apply the honey to the dressings that are placed on the wounds, and the honey is also mixed with the herbs and the other substances that the apothecaries use in the preparation of the remedies.

The honey that is flavoured by the [feverfew](/content/species/flora/feverfew) and the other medicinal plants is the most valued of the medicinal honeys, and the beekeepers who site their hives near the stands of the medicinal herbs produce the honey that commands the highest prices in the markets of the apothecaries. The honey that is produced in the regions where the [poppy](/content/species/flora/poppy) and the other powerful plants grow is handled with the care, for the honey can acquire the properties of the plants that the bees visit.

The trade in the honey is the commerce that connects the beekeepers of the distant regions to the markets of the cities. The honey of the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) is exported to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the dark and the rich honey of the south is valued for the flavour that differs from the pale honey of the north. The honey of the Heartwood is traded through the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), and the jars of the Heartwood honey are among the most expensive of the goods that the merchants offer.

## The Pollination of the Crops

The honey bee is also valued for the service that the bee provides to the agriculture of the known world, for the bees that forage among the flowers of the crops transfer the pollen that enables the fruit and the seed to form. The beekeepers who keep the hives in the [apple](/content/species/flora/apple) orchards and the [plum and cherry](/content/species/flora/plum-and-cherry) groves of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) provide the pollination that is essential to the production of the fruit, and the orchard owners pay the beekeepers for the placement of the hives among the trees during the blossom.

The pollination that the bees provide is the service that the farmers of the [clover](/content/species/flora/clover) and the [vetch](/content/species/flora/vetch) fields also rely upon, for the seed of the fodder crops depends upon the visitation of the bees to the flowers. The farmers who recognise the value of the pollination encourage the beekeepers to site the hives near the fields, and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the beekeepers and the farmers have established the arrangements that govern the placement and the payment for the hives.
