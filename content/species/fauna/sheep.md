## The Flocks of the Uplands and the Plains

The sheep that are raised in the fields and the pastures of the three continents are among the most important of the domesticated animals, providing the wool, the milk, the meat, and the leather that sustain the population of the settlements. The sheep are the animals of the marginal lands where the cattle cannot thrive, and the flocks of the sheep are the principal form of the livestock in the uplands and the dry regions of the known world.

The management of the sheep is the principal occupation of the herding communities in the regions where the pasture is poor and the climate is harsh. The flocks are moved across the range in the patterns of the seasonal migration that follow the growth of the grasses and the availability of the water, and the herders who accompany the flocks live in the temporary camps that are established in the grazing grounds.

## The Wool Sheep of the Morvind Uplands

The wool sheep of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are the most important of the sheep breeds of the northern continent, producing the fleece that is the principal source of the wool for the textile industry of the northern settlements. The wool sheep of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) are a hardy breed that can survive the harsh conditions of the upland winter, and the fleece of the Morvind sheep is thick and long, providing the wool that is used in the production of the warm garments that the northern peoples require.

The sheep are pastured in the alpine meadows of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) and the upland slopes of the Morvind massif, and the flocks are moved to the higher elevations in the summer when the snow has retreated and the grasses are growing. The wool is sheared in the late spring, when the fleece has reached the full length and the sheep are ready for the summer pasture.

The wool of the Morvind sheep is the finest that is produced in the known world for the quality of the fibre, and the wool that is exported from the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is valued for the warmth and the durability of the garments that are made from it.

## The Meat Sheep of the Aethelaran Plains

The meat sheep of the Aethelaran plains are the breeds that are raised for the mutton and the lamb that are the principal meats of the northern settlements. The meat sheep are larger and faster-growing than the wool sheep, and the meat sheep are raised on the lush pastures of the river valleys and the lowland plains.

The meat sheep of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) and the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) are the most productive of the breeds, and the lamb that is produced in these regions is the finest in the known world. The lamb is slaughtered in the spring and the early summer, when the meat is tender and the flavour is mild, and the lamb is served at the feasts of the wealthy households and the inns of the cities.

## The Sheep of the Sahrani Coastal Strip

The sheep that are raised in the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip) and the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) are a breed that has been adapted to the warm climate of the southern continent. The [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) sheep are smaller than the sheep of the north, and the fleece of the Sahrani sheep is shorter and lighter, providing the wool that is used in the production of the lighter garments that the Sahrani peoples wear.

The Sahrani sheep are raised for the meat and the milk, and the milk of the Sahrani sheep is used in the production of the cheese that is a staple of the Sahrani diet. The cheese that is produced from the sheep milk in the Sahrani coastal settlements is a soft and salty cheese that is consumed fresh or preserved in the oil.

## The Husbandry of the Sheep

The husbandry of the sheep is the principal concern of the herding communities, and the herders have developed the practices and the traditions that are adapted to the conditions of the regions where they live. The seasonal movement of the flocks is the most important of the practices of the husbandry, and the patterns of the movement are determined by the availability of the pasture and the water in the different seasons.

The breeding of the sheep is the second of the principal concerns of the husbandry, and the herders select the rams that will be the sires of the next generation of the flock. The selection of the breeding animals is based on the qualities that the herders value, including the quality of the wool, the size and the conformation of the animal, and the hardiness and the resistance to the disease.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the herders and the wool merchants regulate the trade in the sheep and the products that are produced from the animals. The guilds set the standards for the quality of the wool and the meat and the cheese that are sold in the markets, and the guilds also maintain the registers of the breeding animals that are approved for the use.

## The Use of the Sheep in the Economy

The sheep are used in the economy of the three continents for the multiple purposes that the animals serve. The wool of the sheep is the principal textile fibre of the northern settlements, and the wool is processed into the yarn that is woven into the cloth and the garments that the people wear. The milk of the sheep is the foundation of the dairy industry of the marginal regions, and the cheese that is produced from the sheep milk is valued for the distinctive flavours that the cheeses have.

The meat of the sheep is the principal meat of the herding communities, and the meat is traded in the markets of the settlements where the animals are raised. The leather of the sheep is used in the production of the goods that the craftsmen of the settlements produce.

## The Trade in the Wool

The trade in the wool of the Morvind sheep is a significant part of the intercontinental commerce. The wool of the north is valued in the markets of Sahrana, where the wool is used in the production of the fine garments that the wealthy wear in the cooler months of the year.

The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the wool merchants in the ports of the [Stormfall Coast](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/stormfall-coast) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) regulate the trade in the wool, and the merchants who deal in the wool are among the most active of the trading community.
