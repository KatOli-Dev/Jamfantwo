## The Oxen of the Fields and the Forests

The ox is the most important of the working animals in the agriculture of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the ox is used for the pulling of the ploughs and the harrows and the wagons that are the implements of the farming and the transport. The ox is a castrated male of the cattle that has been trained for the work, and the ox is stronger and more patient than the horse and less expensive to maintain.

The oxen that are used in the farming of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are the largest and the strongest of the oxen in the known world, and the teams of the oxen that pull the heavy ploughs through the deep soils of the river valleys are the foundation of the productivity of the region.

The oxen are also used in the logging industry of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood), where the oxen pull the felled trees from the forest to the roads and the rivers. The ox is the ideal animal for this work, for the ox can move the heavy loads through the rough and the muddy terrain where the horses would struggle.

The training of the ox begins in the calfhood, when the young animal is handled and taught to accept the yoke. The training of the ox to respond to the commands of the driver takes months, and the ox that is well trained is a valuable asset that serves the farmer for many years.

## The Breeds of the Working Oxen

The oxen that are used in the agriculture of the known world are drawn from the various breeds of [cattle](/content/species/fauna/cattle) that have been developed in the different regions. The grey ox of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) is the largest and the most powerful of the working oxen, and the grey ox is the animal that can pull the heaviest of the ploughs through the stiffest of the soils. The red ox of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is the breed that is used for the general farm work, and the red ox is the animal that combines the strength with the docility that makes the animal easy to handle.

The oxen of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) are the breed that is used for the pulling of the carts and the wagons that carry the [grapevine](/content/species/flora/grapevine) harvest from the vineyards to the presses, and the oxen of the vale are the animals that are trained to the narrow paths and the steep slopes of the vineyard country. The oxen of the vale are lighter and more agile than the oxen of the heavy soil regions, and the oxen of the vale are valued for the sureness of the foot that the animal displays on the uneven ground.

## The Yoke and the Harness

The yoke that the ox wears is the wooden beam that rests on the neck of the animal and that transfers the pull of the animal to the implement that the ox draws. The yoke is made from the seasoned timber of the [oak](/content/species/flora/oak) or the [ash](/content/species/flora/ash), and the yoke is shaped to fit the neck of the animal without the chafing that would cause the sores and the discomfort. The yoke is fastened to the horns or the neck of the ox with the bows and the straps that hold the yoke in the place.

The harness that connects the yoke to the plough or the wagon is the chain or the rope that transfers the pull of the team to the implement. The harness must be strong enough to withstand the strain of the pull and the flexible enough to allow the team to move with the freedom that the work requires. The harness is the equipment that the farmer checks and maintains with the care, for the failure of the harness in the field can mean the loss of the day and the injury to the animal.

## The Team and the Driver

The team of the oxen is the pair or the group of the animals that work together to pull the plough or the wagon, and the team is the unit that the driver manages through the commands and the voice that the oxen have been trained to obey. The team of the oxen is the pair for the light plough and the four for the heavy plough, and the team of the oxen for the wagon can be the six or the eight for the heaviest of the loads.

The driver of the team is the person who walks beside the plough or rides on the wagon and who directs the team through the words and the goad that the oxen respond to. The driver who knows the team and the animals can coax the best from the oxen, and the driver who is skilled in the handling of the team is the person who is valued by the farmer and the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the ploughmen.

## The Ox in the Culture of the North

The ox holds the place in the culture and the traditions of the northern peoples that reflects the importance of the animal to the economy of the region. The [first-grain-feast](/content/culture/first-grain-feast) that marks the beginning of the harvest in the settlements of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) includes the blessing of the ox that will pull the wagon of the harvest, and the ox that is selected for the blessing is the finest of the team that the farmer owns.

The ox is also the animal that appears in the folklore and the stories of the north, where the patient and the strong ox is the symbol of the endurance and the reliability that the peoples of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm) value. The ox that serves the farmer for the years of the work is the animal that earns the respect and the affection of the household, and the ox that dies is the animal that is mourned by the family that the animal has sustained.
