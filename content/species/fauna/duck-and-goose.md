The ducks and the geese are the birds of the wetter regions of the known world, and the waterfowl are kept for the eggs, the meat, and the feathers by the households that have access to the ponds and the streams.

## The Ducks and the Geese of the Wetlands

The ducks and the geese that are kept in the settlements of the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) and the other wetland regions are the birds that are adapted to the conditions of the watery environment. The ducks and the geese are kept for the eggs, the meat, and the feathers, and the birds are also valued for the down that is used in the production of the bedding and the garments that keep the people warm in the winter.

The ducks are the more common of the waterfowl in the domestic flocks, and the ducks are kept in the yards and the gardens that have access to the water. The ducks are fed on the grain and the scraps, and the ducks also forage in the ponds and the streams for the insects and the plants that supplement the diet.

The geese are the larger of the waterfowl, and the geese are kept for the meat and the feathers and the down that are the most valued of the products of the waterfowl. The geese are also used as the guardians of the farms, for the geese are alert and noisy and will raise the alarm when the strangers approach the property.

## The Breeds of the Domestic Duck

The ducks that are kept in the domestic flocks of the known world are of several breeds, each of which has been developed for the purposes that the breed serves. The common duck of the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) is the breed that is kept for the eggs and the meat, and the common duck is the bird that forages in the ponds and the streams for the insects and the plants that supplement the feed that the household provides.

The larger duck of the [Vintners Vale](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/vintners-vale) is the breed that is kept for the meat, and the duck of the vale is the bird that grows to the greater weight on the grain and the greens that the farmer feeds. The duck of the vale is the bird that is served at the feasts of the wealthy households, and the duck that is fattened for the table is the bird that is valued for the tender and the rich meat that the bird provides.

## The Geese of the Wetlands and the Fields

The geese that are kept in the domestic flocks are the descendants of the wild geese that were captured and tamed in the centuries past, and the domestic goose is the bird that retains the wariness and the alertness that the wild goose possesses. The domestic goose is the bird that is kept in the fields and the pastures where the bird grazes on the [clover](/content/species/flora/clover) and the grass, and the goose is the bird that requires the water for the bathing and the drinking that the health of the animal demands.

The goose that is kept for the down is the bird that is plucked in the spring and the autumn, and the down that is gathered from the breast of the goose is the material that is used in the production of the bedding and the garments that keep the people warm in the winter. The down of the goose is the lightest and the warmest of the fillings that the bedding-makers use, and the quilts and the pillows that are filled with the goose down are the bedding that the wealthy households of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) prefer.

## The Feathers and the Quills

The feathers of the ducks and the geese are the materials that are used in the production of the goods that the craftsmen and the households require. The flight feathers of the goose are the quills that the scribes and the clerks use for the writing, and the quill pen that is cut from the feather of the goose is the instrument that the [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the scribes and the notaries supply to the offices of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth).

The feathers of the waterfowl are also used in the production of the arrows that the archers and the hunters use, and the flight feathers of the goose and the duck are the feathers that the fletchers prefer for the vanes of the arrows. The feathers are also used in the production of the cushions and the mattresses and the other goods that the upholsterers and the bedding-makers produce.

## The Waterfowl in the Trade and the Economy

The ducks and the geese are the birds that are traded in the [markets and fairs](/content/economy/markets-and-fairs) of the settlements, and the live birds and the eggs and the meat and the feathers are the products that the poultry sellers offer. The [guilds](/content/economy/guilds) of the poultrymen in the cities of the wetland regions regulate the trade in the waterfowl, and the guilds set the standards for the quality and the freshness of the birds and the eggs that are sold in the markets.

The down and the feathers of the waterfowl are the products that are traded between the regions, and the down of the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) is exported to the markets of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) where the cold of the winter creates the demand for the warm bedding that the down provides.

## The Waterfowl in the Culture of the Wetland Peoples

The ducks and the geese hold the place in the culture and the traditions of the peoples who live in the wetland regions of the known world. The [halflings](/content/species/sapient/halflings) of the [Greywash Reed Marshes](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/reed-marshes-of-the-greywash) are the peoples who have developed the most elaborate of the traditions that involve the waterfowl, and the halflings keep the ducks and the geese as the animals of the household that provide the food and the materials that the wetland life requires. The geese of the halfling settlements are the birds that are led to the pasture in the morning and the returned to the pens in the evening, and the goose that is fattened for the [first-grain-feast](/content/culture/first-grain-feast) is the bird that is the centrepiece of the table of the feast.

The waterfowl are also the birds that appear in the folklore of the wetland peoples, and the duck and the goose are the animals that the storytellers of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) and the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league) use in the tales that the [Winter Telling](/content/culture/winter-telling) preserves. The goose that lays the golden egg is the tale that the storytellers tell, and the duck that becomes the swan is the story that the children of the wetland settlements hear in the gatherings of the long nights.
