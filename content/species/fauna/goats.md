## The Goats of the Kharesh Margins

The goats that are kept by the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) and the other peoples of the desert margins are the hardiest of the domesticated animals, capable of surviving in the conditions where the sheep and the cattle cannot live. The goats of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) are a small and agile breed that can climb the rocky slopes and feed on the sparse vegetation of the desert, and the goats provide the milk, the meat, and the hair that the desert peoples require.

The milk of the Kharesh goat is rich and nutritious, and the milk is consumed fresh or fermented into the yoghurt that is a staple of the desert diet. The meat of the goat is the principal meat of the desert settlements, and the goat is slaughtered for the feasts and the special occasions.

The hair of the Kharesh goat is used in the production of the cloth and the tents that the desert peoples use. The hair is coarse and strong, and the cloth that is woven from the goat hair is water-resistant and durable, providing the material for the tents that shelter the desert peoples from the sun and the wind.

## The Mountain Goats of the Morvind Uplands

The mountain goats of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are a domesticated breed that has been developed from the wild goats of the region, and the mountain goats are kept by the dwarven and the human communities of the uplands for the milk and the wool and the meat that the animals provide.

The mountain goats are adapted to the steep and rocky terrain of the uplands, and the goats are pastured on the slopes where the sheep cannot graze. The wool of the mountain goat is the cashmere that is the finest and the most valuable of the animal fibres, and the cashmere that is produced in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is exported to the markets of Aethelar and [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana).

## The Husbandry of the Goats

The husbandry of the goats is the principal concern of the herding communities of the desert margins and the uplands, and the herders have developed the practices that are adapted to the conditions of the regions where they live. The goats are moved across the rocky slopes and the desert margins in patterns that follow the availability of the browse and the water.

The breeding of the goats is the second of the principal concerns of the husbandry, and the herders select the billy goats that will be the sires of the next generation. The selection of the breeding animals is based on the qualities that the herders value, including the quality of the hair and the cashmere, the size and the conformation of the animal, and the hardiness and the resistance to the disease.

## The Use of the Goats in the Economy

The goats are used in the economy of the marginal regions for the multiple purposes that the animals serve. The hair of the Kharesh goat is the principal textile fibre of the desert settlements, and the cashmere of the mountain goat is the most valuable of the animal fibres that is traded between the continents.

The milk of the goats is the foundation of the dairy industry of the desert and the upland regions, and the cheese and the yoghurt that are produced from the goat milk are the staples of the diet of the herding communities. The meat of the goat is the principal meat of the desert settlements and the upland communities.

## The Goats of the Sahrani Coast

The goats that are kept in the [Green Crescent](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/green-crescent) and the [Sahrani Coastal Strip](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/sahrani-coastal-strip) are a breed that has been adapted to the warm and the dry conditions of the southern continent. The Sahrani goat is a lean and tall animal with the long ears and the short coat that allows the animal to tolerate the heat of the coast, and the Sahrani goat feeds on the browse of the [acacia](/content/species/flora/acacia) and the [saltbush](/content/species/flora/saltbush) that grow in the marginal lands of the interior.

The milk of the Sahrani goat is the foundation of the cheese-making of the southern settlements, and the cheese that is produced from the goat milk of the Green Crescent is a sharp and the salty cheese that is aged in the caves and the cellars of the coastal towns. The cheese of the Sahrani goat is exported to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), where the southern cheese is valued for the distinctive flavour that the herbs of the coastal browse impart to the milk.

The goats of the Sahrani coast are also kept for the meat, and the kid that is slaughtered in the spring is the meat that is served at the feasts of the [Sahrani Caravan Feast](/content/culture/sahrani-caravan-feast). The meat of the Sahrani goat is lean and mild, and the kid is roasted with the herbs and the spices that the Sahrani cooks use in the preparation of the dishes of the feast.

## The Goats in the Forest and the Heath

The goats that are kept in the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) and the [Ashen Heath](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/ashen-heath) are the breeds that can thrive on the browse of the woodland and the heath. The forest goat of the Brackenshield Wood is a small and dark animal that can navigate the undergrowth and the fallen timber of the forest floor, and the forest goat feeds on the leaves and the shoots of the [oak](/content/species/flora/oak) and the [beech](/content/species/flora/beech) and the [gorse](/content/species/flora/gorse) that grow in the woodland.

The goats of the heath are kept by the communities of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) who graze the animals on the [thistle](/content/species/flora/thistle) and the [gorse](/content/species/flora/gorse) of the open heath. The goats of the heath are valued for the hardiness and the ability to clear the land of the scrub, and the goats are used to prepare the ground for the cultivation of the crops that the farmers of the marches plant after the goats have browsed the land.

## The Sacred and the Ceremonial Goats

The goat holds the place in the ceremonies and the traditions of the peoples of the known world. The [centaurs](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) of the [Great Grass Plains](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/great-grass-plains) keep the goats as the animals of the herd that accompany the horse-herds, and the centaurs sacrifice the goat at the ceremonies of the [first-grain-feast](/content/culture/first-grain-feast) that marks the beginning of the harvest. The [satyrs](/content/species/sapient/satyrs) of the woodland regions are the peoples who are associated with the goat in the mythology of the known world, and the satyrs keep the goats as the animals of the forest that provide the milk and the meat that the satyr communities consume.
