## The Camels of the Kharesh

The camels of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) desert are the most important of the desert beasts of burden, and the camel is the animal that makes the crossing of the desert possible for the caravans that carry the trade between the interior and the coast. The camel is a large and distinctive animal that is adapted to the conditions of the arid environment, and the camel is capable of carrying the heavy loads for the long distances without the water.

The camel of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) is a dromedary with the single hump that stores the fat that the animal draws upon when the food and the water are scarce. The camel can travel for the weeks without the water in the cool season, and the animal can carry the loads of the four hundred pounds or more on the journeys that last for the months.

## The Breeds of the Desert Camels

The camels of the Kharesh are of several breeds, each of which has been developed for the different purposes that the animal serves in the desert economy. The riding camel of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) is a lean and fast animal that is bred for the speed and the endurance, and the riding camel is the mount of the [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) guides who lead the caravans across the desert.

The pack camel of the caravan trade is a heavier and slower animal that is bred for the strength and the carrying capacity. The pack camel can carry the loads of the five hundred pounds or more, and the pack camel is the animal that carries the [spices and dyes](/content/economy/spices-and-dyes) and the other goods that are traded between the interior and the coast.

The herd of the camels that is kept by the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) is the foundation of the wealth of the tribe, and the size and the quality of the herd are the measure of the prosperity of the tribe. The camels are the animals that provide the milk, the meat, the hair, and the transport that the desert peoples require.

## The Care and the Management of the Camels

The care and the management of the camels is the principal concern of the desert peoples, and the knowledge of the camel is the most highly valued of the skills that the desert guide possesses. The camel is a complex and demanding animal, and the camel requires the care and the attention that the experienced camel keeper knows how to provide.

The feeding of the camel is the challenge of the desert travel, for the camel must be fed on the sparse vegetation of the desert and the water that the camel consumes must be carried in the skins and the vessels that accompany the caravan. The camel can survive on the dried grass and the leaves of the desert shrubs, and the camel that is well fed before the journey can travel for the weeks without the food.

The watering of the camel is the most important of the practices of the camel management, and the camel that is fully watered can drink the equivalent of the third of the body weight in a single session. The camel is watered at the oases and the wells that are spaced along the caravan routes, and the schedule of the watering determines the pace of the travel across the desert.

## The Training of the Camel

The training of the camel begins in the calfhood, when the young animal is handled and taught to accept the saddle and the load. The training of the camel to respond to the commands of the rider takes months, and the camel that is well trained is the animal that is the most valuable of the possessions of the desert traveller.

The camel is trained to kneel for the mounting and the loading, and the camel is trained to rise when the rider is seated and the load is secured. The camel is also trained to respond to the voice and the touch of the rider, and the bond between the camel and the rider is developed through the long hours of the travel across the desert.

## The Milk and the Meat of the Camel

The milk of the camel is the principal food of the desert peoples, and the camel that is in the milk can produce the several pints of the milk per day even when the water and the pasture are scarce. The milk of the camel is rich and thick, with the flavour that is different from the milk of the [cattle](/content/species/fauna/cattle) and the [goats](/content/species/fauna/goats), and the milk is consumed fresh or fermented into the sour drink that the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) value for the sustenance that the drink provides on the long journeys.

The meat of the camel is the food of the feasts and the ceremonies of the desert peoples, and the camel is slaughtered only on the occasions of the greatest importance. The young camel that is slaughtered for the meat provides the tender flesh that is roasted over the fire, and the older camel that is no longer useful for the work is slaughtered for the meat that is dried and preserved for the journeys. The fat of the hump is the richest of the parts of the camel, and the fat is rendered into the oil that is used in the cooking and the lamps.

## The Hair and the Hide of the Camel

The hair of the camel is the material that is used in the production of the cloth and the tents and the ropes that the desert peoples require. The camel sheds the hair in the spring, and the hair that is gathered from the ground and the brushed from the animal is spun into the yarn that is woven into the coarse cloth of the tents and the blankets. The cloth of the camel hair is the material that shelters the desert peoples from the sun and the wind, and the tents of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) are made from the panels of the camel-hair cloth that are stitched together and supported on the poles.

The hide of the camel is used in the production of the leather that the craftsmen of the desert settlements shape into the bags and the water-skins and the sandals that the desert peoples use. The leather of the camel is tough and durable, and the water-skins that are made from the camel hide are the vessels that carry the water on the journeys across the desert.

## The Camels in the Caravan Trade

The camel is the animal that makes the caravan trade of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) possible, and the caravans that cross the desert are the trains of the camels that carry the goods between the interior and the coast. The [Kharesh Caravan Way](/content/location/route/trade/kharesh-caravan-way) and the [Sahrani Desert Trail](/content/location/route/trade/sahrani-desert-trail) are the routes that the camel caravans travel, and the camels that are used on these routes are the animals that are bred and trained for the endurance and the strength that the long journeys demand.

The caravans are organised by the [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) guides who know the routes and the water-holes of the desert, and the guides are the masters of the camels that the caravan uses. The guides determine the pace and the schedule of the travel, and the guides are responsible for the welfare of the camels and the safety of the goods. The caravan that loses the camels to the disease or the exhaustion is the caravan that may not reach the destination, and the guide who preserves the health of the animals is the guide who is most valued by the merchants.
