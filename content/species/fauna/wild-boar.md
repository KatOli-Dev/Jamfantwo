## The Wild Boar of the Brackenshield Wood

The wild boar of the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) and the forests of the northern [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) is the most dangerous of the game animals, and the hunting of the boar is the sport of the brave and the skilled hunter. The boar is a large and powerful animal with the tusks that can inflict the fatal wounds, and the boar is not a prey that the hunter can take without the risk.

The hunting of the boar is conducted with the spears and the dogs, and the dogs are the essential partners of the hunters in the pursuit of the boar. The dogs track the boar and hold the animal at the bay, and the hunter dispatches the boar with the spear when the animal is occupied with the dogs.

The meat of the boar is dark and rich, and the boar is valued for the flavour that the meat has. The boar is hunted in the autumn, when the animal has fattened on the acorns and the beech nuts of the forest, and the meat of the autumn boar is the finest of the meats of the forest.

## The Physical Characteristics of the Wild Boar

The wild boar is the large and the powerful animal that stands at the height of the three and the half feet at the shoulder, and the adult male can weigh the three hundred pounds or more. The body of the boar is the compact and the muscular form that is covered with the coarse and the dark bristles that stand erect along the spine of the animal when the boar is angered. The head of the boar is the large and the wedge-shaped head that tapers to the snout that the animal uses to root in the soil for the roots and the bulbs and the grubs that the boar eats.

The tusks of the boar are the most formidable of the weapons of the animal, and the tusks of the mature male can grow to the length of the several inches and curve upward from the jaw with the sharpness that can tear the flesh and the pierce the armour. The tusks of the female are smaller than the tusks of the male but are still the weapons that the female will use in the defence of the young, and the boar that is cornered or wounded is the animal that will charge the hunter with the speed and the ferocity that can kill the man or the [dog](/content/species/fauna/dog) that stands in the path of the charge.

## The Diet and the Habits of the Boar

The wild boar is the omnivore that feeds on the wide range of the foods that the forest provides. The boar roots in the soil for the roots and the tubers and the bulbs that the animal digs with the snout, and the boar eats the [oak](/content/species/flora/oak) acorns and the [beech](/content/species/flora/beech) mast and the [walnut](/content/species/flora/walnut) and the other nuts that fall from the trees in the autumn. The boar also eats the [truffle](/content/species/flora/truffle) and the other fungi that grow in the soil of the forest, and the boar is the animal that the truffle hunters use to locate the valuable fungi that the animal can scent through the earth.

The boar also eats the animal matter that the forest provides, and the boar will take the eggs of the ground-nesting birds and the young of the [hare and rabbit](/content/species/fauna/hare-and-rabbit) and the frogs and the lizards and the insects that the boar encounters in the foraging. The boar that lives near the settlements will raid the [root vegetable](/content/species/flora/root-vegetable) gardens and the [wheat](/content/species/flora/wheat) fields, and the boar is the animal that the farmers regard as the pest that must be hunted and the driven from the cultivated land.

## The Sounder and the Breeding

The wild boar lives in the groups that are called the sounders, and the sounder is the family group that consists of the sows and the young and the sub-adult males. The mature male of the boar is the solitary animal that joins the sounder only in the breeding season, and the male that seeks the sows will fight the other males for the access to the females. The fights between the males are the clashes of the tusks and the charges that can injure or kill the weaker animal, and the male that wins the control of the sounder will mate with the sows that are ready for the breeding.

The sows bear the piglets in the spring, and the litter of the sow can number the four or the six or the eight young. The sow builds the nest of the branches and the leaves and the grasses in the secluded spot that the sow selects for the farrowing, and the piglets remain in the nest for the first weeks of the life before the sow leads the young to join the sounder. The piglets are the striped animals that bear the stripes that provide the camouflage in the dappled light of the forest floor, and the stripes fade as the piglet grows and the coat of the young boar turns to the dark colour of the adult.

## The Boar in the Culture of the Peoples

The wild boar holds the place in the culture and the traditions of the peoples of the known world. The boar is the animal that appears in the tales and the legends of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) settlements, and the boar is the beast that the heroes of the old stories hunt and the overcome in the adventures that the [Winter Telling](/content/culture/winter-telling) preserves. The boar is the animal that the [orcs](/content/species/sapient/orcs) of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) regard as the totem of the clan, and the boar that is taken in the hunt is the animal that is honoured in the ceremonies of the [first-hunt-rite](/content/culture/first-hunt-rite) that the orc communities practice.

The boar hunt is also the sport of the nobility of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), and the lords who maintain the packs of the boar hounds organise the hunts that are the events of the season for the nobility of the court. The boar hunt is the sport that requires the courage and the skill of the hunter, and the hunter who kills the boar with the spear is the person who earns the respect of the peers and the story that the [feasting and dining](/content/culture/feasting-and-dining) of the hall will celebrate.
