## The Mountain Goat of the Morvind Uplands

The mountain goat of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) is the game animal of the high elevations, and the hunting of the mountain goat is the pursuit of the hunters of the dwarven and the human settlements of the uplands. The mountain goat is a cautious and elusive animal that lives in the steep and the rocky terrain, and the hunter must be skilled in the climbing and the stalking to take the goat.

The meat of the mountain goat is lean and gamey, and the meat is valued by the northern peoples for the flavour and the nutrition that the meat provides. The hide of the mountain goat is used in the production of the warm garments that the northern peoples wear, and the horns of the goat are used in the production of the tools and the ornaments.

## The Mountain Goats of the Crown Range

The mountain goats of the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range) are the most typical of the mountain animals, and the mountain goats are the herbivores of the high elevations that graze on the sparse vegetation of the alpine zone. The mountain goat is a sure-footed and agile animal that can climb the steepest and the most treacherous of the slopes with the ease that is remarkable for the size of the animal.

The mountain goat is a stocky and powerful animal with the thick coat of the white hair that protects the animal from the cold and the wind of the high elevations. The mountain goat lives in the herds that are led by the older females, and the herds move through the range in the patterns that follow the growth of the vegetation and the availability of the shelter.

## The Physical Characteristics of the Mountain Goat

The mountain goat is a robust and the compact animal that stands at the height of the three to the four feet at the shoulder, and the weight of the adult male can reach the two hundred pounds. The coat of the mountain goat is the dense and the long hair that hangs from the body of the animal in the shaggy strands that shed the water and the snow, and the undercoat of the goat is the fine and the soft wool that provides the insulation against the cold.

The hooves of the mountain goat are the most remarkable of the features of the animal, for the hooves are the cloven and the padded feet that grip the rock and the ice with the sureness that allows the goat to climb the cliffs and the ridges that no other animal of the size can negotiate. The toes of the hoof can spread and grip the irregular surfaces, and the hard outer rim of the hoof provides the edge that catches on the smallest of the ledges.

The horns of the mountain goat are the short and the black horns that curve backward from the head of the animal, and the horns are present in both the males and the females. The horns are used in the clashes between the males during the rutting season, and the horns are also the weapons that the goat uses to defend itself against the predators that pursue the animal on the high slopes.

## The Diet and the Foraging of the Mountain Goat

The mountain goat feeds on the sparse vegetation of the alpine zone, and the diet of the goat includes the [Shadow Moss](/content/species/flora/shadow-moss) and the mosses that grow on the rocks, the [thistle](/content/species/flora/thistle) and the [gorse](/content/species/flora/gorse) that cling to the slopes, and the shoots of the [pine](/content/species/flora/pine) and the [spruce](/content/species/flora/spruce) that grow at the tree line. The goat can digest the tough and the fibrous plants that the other herbivores cannot utilise, and the goat can extract the nutrition from the plants that grow in the thin soils of the alpine zone.

The foraging of the mountain goat follows the seasonal pattern that is dictated by the retreat and the advance of the snow. The goats move to the higher elevations in the summer when the snow has melted and the vegetation is growing, and the goats descend to the lower slopes and the valleys in the winter when the deep snow covers the higher ground. The goats can dig through the snow to reach the vegetation that lies beneath, and the goats can survive on the browse that the wind has cleared of the snow.

## The Hunting of the Mountain Goat

The hunting of the mountain goat is the pursuit that requires the skill and the endurance of the most experienced of the hunters. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) are the peoples who have developed the most refined of the traditions of the mountain-goat hunting, and the dwarven hunters know the routes and the habits of the goats that pass through the ranges of the holds. The hunt is conducted in the late summer and the autumn, when the goats are at the higher elevations and the meat is at the best.

The hunters of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) and the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) settlements also pursue the mountain goat in the ranges of the north, and the hide and the horns of the goat are the materials that the northern peoples value for the garments and the tools that the goat provides. The horns of the mountain goat are used in the production of the handles of the knives and the tools, and the horns are also carved into the ornaments and the implements that the craftsmen of the northern settlements produce.

## The Mountain Goat and the Domesticated Goat

The mountain goat of the wild and the domesticated goat of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are the related animals, and the domesticated goats that are kept by the herders of the uplands are the descendants of the wild goats that were captured and tamed in the centuries past. The wild mountain goat is the larger and the more powerful animal, and the wild goat retains the agility and the wariness that the domesticated goat has lost through the generations of the selective breeding.

The herders of the uplands sometimes cross the domesticated goats with the wild goats to introduce the hardiness and the vigour of the wild animal into the domesticated herd, and the kids that are produced from the crossing are the animals that combine the size and the temperament of the domesticated goat with the resilience and the climbing ability of the wild goat.
