## The Hares and the Rabbits of the Fields

The rabbit is a more sedentary animal that lives in the burrows in the banks and the slopes of the fields. The rabbit is hunted with the ferrets that drive the animal from the burrow, and the rabbit is caught in the nets that are placed at the entrances of the burrow.
