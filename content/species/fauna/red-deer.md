The red deer is the most important game animal of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood), and the hunting of the red deer is the principal pursuit of the hunters of the forest settlements.

## The Red Deer of the Aethelar Heartwood

The red deer of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) is the most important of the game animals of the northern continent, and the hunting of the red deer is the principal pursuit of the hunters of the forest settlements. The red deer is a large and graceful animal that lives in the herds in the forest and the woodland margins, and the deer is valued for the meat and the hide and the antlers that the animal provides.

The hunting of the red deer is conducted in the autumn and the winter, when the deer are in the season and the meat is at the best. The hunters track the deer through the forest, using the knowledge of the signs and the habits of the animal that the experienced hunters possess. The deer is killed with the arrow or the spear, and the meat of the deer is carried to the settlement for the distribution among the households.

The hunting of the red deer is regulated by the authorities of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), who grant the licences and the permits to the hunters who may take the deer from the forest. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the Heartwood have their own traditions of the hunting that are based on the principle of the taking of only what is needed and the respect for the spirit of the animal.

## The Physical Characteristics of the Red Deer

The red deer is the largest of the deer of the northern continent, and the adult male stands at the height of the four and the half feet at the shoulder and can weigh the five hundred pounds. The coat of the red deer is the reddish-brown in the summer and the greyish-brown in the winter, and the change of the colour is the adaptation that allows the animal to blend with the colours of the forest through the seasons. The male of the red deer bears the antlers that are shed and the regrown each year, and the antlers of the mature male can span the four feet and carry the points that are the sign of the age and the vigour of the animal.

The antlers of the red deer grow from the knobs on the skull of the male, and the growth of the antlers is the process that occurs in the months of the spring and the summer when the velvet covers the growing bone. The velvet is the skin that supplies the blood and the nourishment to the growing antler, and the velvet is shed in the late summer when the antler has reached the full size and the bone has hardened. The shedding of the velvet is the sign that the rutting season is approaching, and the male that bears the largest and the most elaborate antlers is the male that will dominate the rivals in the clashes of the autumn.

## The Rutting Season and the Breeding

The rutting season of the red deer occurs in the autumn, and the season is the time when the males compete for the control of the herds of the hinds. The male that seeks the herd will challenge the male that holds the herd, and the clash of the antlers and the roaring of the males are the sounds that echo through the forest in the weeks of the rut. The males that win the control of the herd will mate with the hinds, and the males that lose the clash will withdraw and seek the smaller herds that the weaker males may hold.

The hinds of the red deer bear the calves in the late spring, and the calf is born in the secluded spot that the hind selects for the birth. The calf lies hidden in the vegetation for the first weeks of the life, and the hind returns to the calf to feed the young animal and to lead the calf to the herd when the young deer is strong enough to follow. The calves are the prey of the [fox](/content/species/fauna/fox) and the [golden eagle](/content/species/fauna/golden-eagle) and the other predators that hunt the young of the deer, and the mortality of the calves is the highest in the first weeks of the life.

## The Red Deer of the Other Regions

The red deer is not confined to the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood), and the deer is found in the forests and the woodlands of the other regions of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The red deer of the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) are the deer that are hunted by the communities of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), and the deer of the Brackenshield Wood are the smaller and the darker animals that are adapted to the denser forest of the northern region.

The red deer of the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) uplands are the deer that are found in the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the [Crown Range](/content/location/natural/feature/crown-range), and the deer of the Morvind mountains are the larger and the heavier animals that are adapted to the colder conditions of the high latitudes. The red deer of Morvind are hunted by the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) and the humans of the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans), and the hide and the antlers of the Morvind red deer are the materials that the northern peoples value for the garments and the tools.

## The Use of the Deer Products

The meat of the red deer is the food that is valued in the settlements of every region where the deer is hunted, and the venison is the meat that is served at the feasts of the wealthy households and the inns of the cities. The hide of the red deer is the material that is used in the production of the leather and the garments that the craftsmen of the settlements produce, and the leather of the red deer is the supple and the durable material that is valued for the gloves and the boots and the bags that the leatherworkers make.

The antlers of the red deer are the material that is used in the production of the handles and the tools and the ornaments that the craftsmen produce. The antlers are also the source of the material that the apothecaries grind into the powder that is used in the preparation of the remedies that the healers prescribe for the ailments of the bones and the joints.
