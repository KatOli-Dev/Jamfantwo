The reindeer of the Morvind Uplands is the most important of the northern herd animals, sustaining the peoples of the high latitudes with the meat, the milk, the hides, the antlers, and the transport that the animal provides.

## The Reindeer of the Morvind Uplands

The reindeer of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) are the most important of the northern herd animals, and the reindeer are the animals that sustain the peoples of the high latitudes. The reindeer is a large and hardy deer that is adapted to the conditions of the cold and the snow, and the reindeer is the animal that can survive in the regions where the cattle and the sheep cannot live.

The reindeer are herded by the human communities of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) and the upland settlements, and the herds of the reindeer are moved in the seasonal migrations that follow the growth of the vegetation. The reindeer feed on the lichens and the mosses that grow in the uplands, and the reindeer can dig through the snow to reach the food that lies beneath.

The reindeer provide the meat, the milk, the hides, and the antlers that are the foundation of the material culture of the northern peoples. The meat of the reindeer is the principal food of the winter, and the hides of the reindeer are used in the production of the warm garments and the bedding that the northern peoples require.

## The Reindeer of the Sledge

The reindeer are also used for the transport in the northern regions, and the reindeer are the animals that pull the sledges across the snow and the ice of the winter. The reindeer are faster and more agile than the dogs in the deep snow, and the reindeer are the animals that the northern travellers use for the long journeys across the uplands.

The sledge reindeer are the larger and the stronger of the herd, and the sledge reindeer are trained to respond to the commands of the driver. The sledge reindeer are the most valued of the animals of the northern herds, and the owner of the sledge reindeer is the person of the status and the prosperity in the northern communities.

## The Seasonal Migration of the Reindeer

The reindeer that are herded by the communities of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) follow the seasonal migration that takes the herds from the winter pastures in the sheltered valleys to the summer pastures on the open uplands. The migration is the movement that the herders and the reindeer make together, and the migration is the event that defines the rhythm of the year for the peoples of the high latitudes.

The winter pastures are the areas where the snow is shallow and the [Shadow Moss](/content/species/flora/shadow-moss) that the reindeer feed on are accessible through the snow. The herders move the herds to the winter pastures in the late autumn, and the herds remain in the valleys until the spring when the snow begins to retreat and the vegetation begins to grow on the higher ground. The summer pastures are the open uplands where the grasses and the herbs that the reindeer prefer are abundant, and the herds gain the weight and the condition on the summer pasture that sustains the animals through the winter.

## The Products of the Reindeer

The reindeer provides the products that are the foundation of the material culture of the northern peoples. The meat of the reindeer is the principal food of the winter, and the meat is dried and the smoked and the preserved in the fat that the northern peoples store for the months of the cold. The tongue and the marrow of the reindeer are the delicacies that are valued by the peoples of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) and the Frostwall Clans, and the tongue is the part of the animal that is served at the feasts of the [long-night-festival](/content/culture/long-night-festival).

The hides of the reindeer are used in the production of the warm garments and the bedding and the tents that the northern peoples require. The hide of the reindeer that is taken in the autumn is the thickest and the warmest of the hides, and the autumn hide is the material that is used in the production of the winter garments that the herders wear. The hide of the reindeer that is taken in the summer is the lighter and the thinner hide that is used in the production of the summer garments and the bags and the other goods that the northern peoples use.

The antlers of the reindeer are the material that is used in the production of the tools and the implements and the ornaments that the craftsmen of the northern settlements produce. The antlers are shed each year by the male and the female reindeer, and the shed antlers are gathered from the ground by the herders who know the places where the animals drop the antlers. The antlers are carved into the handles of the knives and the points of the arrows and the ornaments that the northern peoples wear.

The milk of the reindeer is the product that the herders of the [Valdmark](/content/government/national/valdmark) collect from the domesticated does during the summer months when the animals are on the pasture and the milk is flowing. The milk of the reindeer is rich and the thick, and the milk is consumed fresh or made into the cheese that is a staple of the diet of the herding communities. The cheese of the reindeer milk is the food that is stored for the winter and consumed in the months when the milk is no longer available.

## The Reindeer and the Peoples of the North

The reindeer is the animal that defines the culture and the identity of the peoples of the high latitudes. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) keep the herds of the reindeer on the slopes above the holds, and the reindeer are the animals that provide the food and the materials that the dwarves of the high holds require. The humans of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) and the [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) are the peoples whose life is organised around the herding of the reindeer, and the seasonal migration of the herds is the event that structures the year of the community.

The reindeer also holds the place in the mythology and the traditions of the northern peoples, where the reindeer is the animal that the spirits of the land have given to the peoples for the sustenance and the companionship. The [Winter Telling](/content/culture/winter-telling) is the tradition of the storytelling that the northern peoples practice in the months of the dark, and the stories of the reindeer and the herders are the tales that are told and retold in the gatherings of the winter.
