## The Great Venomous Reptile of the Deep Wood

The basilisk is a great reptilian beast of the deep forest and the broken cave country, a creature of considerable size and singular reputation whose range extends across the wooded interior of the three continents. Naturalists of the academies of [Amurra](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) hold it to be the most venomous of the reptiles of the world. The creature is the subject of an extensive body of cautionary lore among the foresters of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) and the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood). Encounters with a basilisk are recorded in the annals of forest communities as singular events of the gravest kind, and hunters who have taken a basilisk are honoured with a distinction second to none among the orders of the chase.

## Heavy Scales and Lethal Gaze

The basilisk is a heavily built reptile, with a body set upon four short powerful legs and a broad flattened head in the manner of the great lizards. The thick hide of small scales is of a dark green or blackish hue; the scales of older animals are tipped with a pale yellow that foresters have likened to the colour of old bone. The head bears a pair of small eyes of pale yellow, the source of the creature's most celebrated attribute: the gaze of the basilisk is held by forest traditions to be lethal to the creatures upon which it falls. The tail is long and heavy, not prehensile, and is used for balance as the creature moves through the underbrush.

Size varies with subspecies and range, but a mature male may measure twelve feet from the tip of the snout to the root of the tail, with a body heavy in proportion. The basilisk is not swift, for the legs are short and the body heavy, and it is not capable of pursuing fast-moving prey. It is, however, a creature of considerable strength; the bite is sufficient to break the bones of a small deer, and the venom delivered is of a potency that the naturalists of the academies have not been able to equal in any apothecary preparation.

## Forest Lairs and Cave Country

The basilisk inhabits the deep forest and broken cave country of the three continents, with the largest populations in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and a smaller population in the broken country of the [Ashen Heath](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/ashen-heath) of the eastern continent. The basilisk requires the cover of deep forest or the concealment of a cave for its lair and is not found in open country or high mountain. The lair is typically situated in a cave that opens into a dense thicket or in a hollow beneath the roots of a great tree; the entrance is marked by a peculiar musty odour that foresters have learned to recognise as a sign of the creature's presence.

The basilisk is solitary, and mature animals hold territories marked by scent left upon prominent rocks and by trails worn through the underbrush. A territory may extend for several miles through the deep wood, and the creature ranges widely in search of prey. It is most active at dusk and dawn, when it emerges from the lair to lie in wait upon game trails, and returns to the lair before the full light of day, when the heat of the sun would render it sluggish.

## Ambush Predator of Game Trails

The basilisk is an ambush predator that takes a wide range of prey according to opportunity. The principal prey is smaller mammals of the forest, including hares and squirrels; a mature basilisk will take several such animals in the course of a week. It also takes birds of the forest floor, reptiles of the underbrush, and the fawns of forest deer encountered upon game trails. The basilisk does not ordinarily attack [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans), but accounts of the forest communities include a number of incidents in which a basilisk has bitten a forester who unwarily approached the lair; foresters take care to mark the lairs of basilisks and give them a wide berth.

The hunting technique is to lie in wait upon a game trail and seize the prey with a sudden strike of the heavy head as it passes within reach. The prey is held in the jaws until the venom has done its work; the basilisk then follows the trail of the stricken animal to where it has fallen. The venom acts upon the musculature of the prey and leaves the carcass untainted; the meat of an animal killed by a basilisk is held by foresters to be fit for the table provided the flesh is well cooked. The gaze of the basilisk is the subject of substantial legend; foresters of the deep wood hold that the gaze is sufficient to kill a man who meets it directly, though the naturalists of the academies have debated the reality of this attribute.

## Cautionary Lore and the Hunt

The basilisk occupies a singular place in the traditions of the forest communities and is the subject of a body of lore among the most elaborate of the cautionary traditions of the woodland. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the deep forest hold that the basilisk is a creature of the old wood and that its gaze may be avoided by the use of mirrors of polished silver. Elven wardens of the deep forest are reputed to be able to lead a party through the territory of a basilisk without disturbing the creature. The [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) of the forest margins regard the basilisk with a dread not accorded to any other beast; the forester who has killed a basilisk is honoured with a title second to none among the orders of the chase.

The hunting of the basilisk is a recognised pursuit of the forest communities; hunters take a small number each year under licence from local authorities. The hunt is conducted with the heavy spear or the crossbow. The hunter must have the skill and courage to bring the creature down before it can close the distance, for the strike of the basilisk is of considerable speed and the venom of the bite is lethal in the greater part of cases. The basilisk is not held in particular reverence; it is regarded as a hazard of the deep wood and is hunted with the same practical purpose that foresters bring to the hunting of the wolf and the bear.

## The Deep Wood's Most Feared Creature

The basilisk is the foremost of the venomous reptiles of the deep forest, and its presence in the wooded interior of the three continents is a measure of the wildness that endures in the deep wood. It is the predator that keeps the populations of smaller mammals in balance with the carrying capacity of the woodland; the loss of the basilisk from any district would be followed by a deterioration of the forest. The basilisk endures in the deep forest and broken cave country of the three continents and in the cautionary lore of the foresters who share the world with the creature as a symbol of the wildness that persists in the deep wood. The musty scent of the lair upon the still air of the forest is a reminder that the interior of the wood is not empty of life nor subject to the order of the settled lands beyond.
