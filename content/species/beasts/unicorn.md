## Overview

The unicorn is a great beast of the deep forests of the known world, a creature of singular beauty and of singular reputation whose range extends across the mature woodlands of the three continents. The unicorn takes its name from the single horn that the creature bears upon the forehead, and the animal is held by the naturalists of the academies of [Amurra](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) to be one of the most singular of the beasts of the forest. The chronicler notes that the unicorn is not a creature of the open country or the settled lands, for the animal requires the cover of the mature forest and the shelter of the deepest glades that are found there, and the unicorn is found in greatest numbers in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and in the deep forest of the central continent that lies beyond the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden).

## Form and Appearance

The unicorn is a creature of singular beauty, with a body that is set upon four slender legs in the manner of the great horse and a head that is long and graceful and that bears upon the forehead a single horn of considerable length. The body of the unicorn is covered in a coat of pure white that the chroniclers of the forest have likened to the colour of the fresh snow of the high country, and the coat is of a fineness and a softness that is the admiration of the naturalists who have observed the creature in the wild. The mane of the unicorn is long and is of a silvery hue that shades to a pale gold at the tips, and the tail of the unicorn is long and is carried in the manner of the great horses of the plains.

The horn of the unicorn is the creature's most celebrated feature, and the horn is a spiral of a pearly white that is twisted in the manner of the horn of the ibex of the high country. The horn is set firmly upon the forehead of the creature and rises to a height of some two feet in the mature animal, and the horn is said by the chroniclers of the forest to be possessed of a singular purity that is not found in any other horn or tusk of the world. The eyes of the unicorn are large and of a deep and lustrous violet that the foresters have likened to the colour of the wood-violet of the deep glades, and the eyes of the unicorn are the source of the creature's most celebrated attribute, for the gaze of the unicorn is held by the traditions of the forest to be a thing of singular power.

The size of the unicorn is that of a great horse of the plains, and a mature unicorn may stand as high as a [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) at the shoulder. The unicorn is not a heavy creature, and the body of the animal is of a slenderness that is suited to the swift passage through the glades of the forest, and the unicorn is capable of a speed that is considerable for a beast of its size. The hooves of the unicorn are cloven in the manner of the deer, and the unicorn is a creature of the soft ground of the forest floor, the hooves of the creature leaving a mark that the foresters have learned to recognise as a sign of the presence of the unicorn in the district.

## Habitat and Range

The unicorn inhabits the deep forests of the three continents, with the largest populations in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and in the deep forest of the central continent that lies beyond the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden), and a smaller population in the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The unicorn requires the cover of the mature forest and the shelter of the deepest glades, and the creature is not found in the open country or in the settled lands. The unicorn ranges widely through the deep forest in the course of the seasons, and the creature is known to follow the same paths through the wood year after year, the paths being marked by the singular purity of the air that the foresters have attributed to the passage of the creature.

The unicorn is a solitary creature outside the brief season of the breeding, and the mature animals hold ranges that they mark by the singular purity of the water of the streams at which the creature drinks and by the scent that the unicorn leaves upon the prominent trees. The range of a mature unicorn may extend for many miles through the deep forest, and the creature ranges widely in search of the herbage that sustains it. The unicorn is most active in the hours of dawn and the dusk, when the creature emerges from the deeper glades to drink at the forest streams, and the unicorn is a shy creature that is rarely seen by the traveller of the forest.

## Diet and Behaviour

The unicorn is a grazer and a browser, feeding upon the grasses and the herbs of the forest floor in the spring and the summer, and upon the bark, the buds, and the fallen mast of the trees in the autumn and the winter. The unicorn is fond of the herbs of the deepest glades, and the creature is known to seek out the rare and the precious plants of the forest for which the apothecaries of the woodland have a particular esteem. The unicorn is a creature of the early morning and the late afternoon, and the unicorn rests during the heat of the day in the cool of the deeper glades, a habit that the foresters use to their advantage when the wind is right and the forest is still.

The social order of the unicorn is most visible in the breeding season of the late spring, when the mature unicorns gather at the traditional meeting grounds of the deep forest and the males display to the females in a manner that the chroniclers of the woodland have likened to a tournament. The fights between the males for the possession of the females are conducted with a deliberation and a gravity that is in keeping with the singular nature of the creature, and the victor of a contest assumes the herd and drives the younger males to the periphery of the meeting ground. The foal is born in the following spring and is cared for by the mother through the summer of its first year, and the foal is capable of independent foraging before the first snow of the winter.

## The Unicorn in the Traditions of the Forest

The unicorn occupies a singular place in the traditions of the forest communities, and the creature is the subject of a body of lore that is among the most elaborate of the legendary traditions of the woodland. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the deep forest hold that the unicorn is a creature of the old wood and that the creature was old when the first of the elven kind set foot upon the world, and the elves regard the unicorn with a reverence that is not accorded to any other beast. The [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) maintains a tradition of the unicorn hunt, the hunt being conducted according to the strictest of the conventions of the chase and the quarry being released unharmed at the conclusion of the hunt, and the chroniclers of the Vaelic court have preserved accounts of the unicorn hunt that are among the most celebrated of the traditions of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood).

The horn of the unicorn is the subject of a substantial body of legend, and the horn is held by the apothecaries of the woodland to be possessed of singular virtues in the preparation of medicines and the counter of poisons. The chroniclers of the forest have long debated the reality of the virtues that are attributed to the horn, and the naturalists of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) have prepared a number of essays upon the subject, but the consensus of the forest communities is that the horn is a thing to be regarded with the greatest of respect. The hunting of the unicorn for the horn is forbidden by the customary law of the forest communities, and the hunter who takes a unicorn for the horn is held to be guilty of an offence that is second to none among the transgressions of the chase.

## Significance

The unicorn is the foremost of the great beasts of the deep forest, and the presence of the creature in the mature woodlands of the three continents is a measure of the wildness that endures in the country of the old wood. The unicorn is a grazer and a browser that contributes to the maintenance of the diversity of the forest floor, and the loss of the unicorn from any district would be followed by a deterioration of the understory that the chroniclers have noted in the records of the forest communities. The chronicler notes that the unicorn endures, in the deep forest of the three continents and in the legendary lore of the foresters who share the world with the creature, as a symbol of the wildness that persists in the country of the old wood, and the singular purity of the air upon the path of the unicorn is a reminder that the interior of the forest is not empty of life nor subject to the order of the settled lands beyond.
