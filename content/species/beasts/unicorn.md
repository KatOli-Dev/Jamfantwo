## Overview

The unicorn is a great beast of the deep forests of the known world, a creature of singular beauty and singular reputation whose range extends across the mature woodlands of the three continents. It takes its name from the single horn it bears upon the forehead. Naturalists of the academies of [Amurra](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) consider it one of the most singular of the beasts of the forest. The unicorn requires the cover of mature forest and the shelter of the deepest glades; it is found in greatest numbers in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and in the deep forest of the central continent beyond the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden).

## Form and Appearance

The unicorn is a creature of singular beauty, with a body set upon four slender legs in the manner of the great horse and a long graceful head bearing upon the forehead a single horn of considerable length. The body is covered in a coat of pure white that chroniclers have likened to the colour of fresh snow of the high country; the coat is of a fineness and softness admired by naturalists who have observed the creature in the wild. The mane is long and of a silvery hue shading to pale gold at the tips; the tail is long and carried in the manner of the great horses of the plains.

The horn is the most celebrated feature: a spiral of pearly white twisted in the manner of the horn of the ibex of the high country. It is set firmly upon the forehead and rises to a height of some two feet in the mature animal; the horn is said to possess a singular purity not found in any other horn or tusk of the world. The eyes are large and of a deep lustrous violet that foresters have likened to the colour of the wood-violet of the deep glades; the gaze is held by forest traditions to be a thing of singular power.

The size is that of a great horse of the plains; a mature unicorn may stand as high as a [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) at the shoulder. It is not a heavy creature; the body is of a slenderness suited to swift passage through the glades of the forest, and it is capable of considerable speed. The hooves are cloven in the manner of the deer, suited to the soft ground of the forest floor; they leave a mark that foresters have learned to recognise as a sign of the unicorn's presence.

## Habitat and Range

The unicorn inhabits the deep forests of the three continents, with the largest populations in the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood) of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the deep forest of the central continent beyond the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden), and a smaller population in the [Brackenshield Wood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/brackenshield-wood) of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). It requires the cover of mature forest and the shelter of the deepest glades; it is not found in open country or settled lands. The unicorn ranges widely through the deep forest in the course of the seasons; it follows the same paths through the wood year after year, the paths marked by a singular purity of the air that foresters have attributed to its passage.

The unicorn is solitary outside the brief breeding season; mature animals hold ranges marked by the singular purity of the water of streams at which it drinks and by scent left upon prominent trees. The range may extend for many miles through the deep forest; the creature ranges widely in search of herbage. It is most active at dawn and dusk, emerging from deeper glades to drink at forest streams; it is a shy creature rarely seen by the traveller of the forest.

## Diet and Behaviour

The unicorn is a grazer and browser, feeding upon grasses and herbs of the forest floor in spring and summer and upon bark, buds, and fallen mast in autumn and winter. It is fond of the herbs of the deepest glades and is known to seek out rare and precious plants of the forest for which apothecaries have particular esteem. The unicorn is a creature of early morning and late afternoon, resting during the heat of day in the cool of deeper glades.

The social order is most visible in the breeding season of late spring, when mature unicorns gather at traditional meeting grounds of the deep forest and males display to females in a manner likened to a tournament. Fights between males for possession of females are conducted with deliberation and gravity in keeping with the singular nature of the creature; the victor assumes the herd and drives younger males to the periphery. The foal is born the following spring and is cared for by the mother through the summer of its first year; it is capable of independent foraging before the first snow of winter.

## The Unicorn in the Traditions of the Forest

The unicorn occupies a singular place in the traditions of the forest communities and is the subject of a body of lore among the most elaborate legendary traditions of the woodland. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the deep forest hold that the unicorn is a creature of the old wood, old when the first elven kind set foot upon the world; they regard the unicorn with a reverence not accorded to any other beast. The [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) maintains a tradition of the unicorn hunt, conducted according to the strictest conventions of the chase and the quarry being released unharmed at the conclusion; chroniclers of the Vaelic court have preserved accounts of the unicorn hunt that are among the most celebrated traditions of the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood).

The horn is the subject of substantial legend; it is held by apothecaries of the woodland to possess singular virtues in the preparation of medicines and the countering of poisons. The reality of the virtues attributed to the horn has long been debated; naturalists of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) have prepared essays upon the subject, but the consensus is that the horn is a thing to be regarded with the greatest respect. The hunting of the unicorn for the horn is forbidden by customary law; the hunter who takes a unicorn for the horn is held guilty of an offence second to none among the transgressions of the chase.

## Significance

The unicorn is the foremost of the great beasts of the deep forest, and its presence in the mature woodlands of the three continents is a measure of the wildness that endures in the country of the old wood. It is a grazer and browser that contributes to the maintenance of the diversity of the forest floor; the loss of the unicorn from any district would be followed by a deterioration of the understory. The unicorn endures in the deep forest of the three continents and in the legendary lore of the foresters who share the world with the creature as a symbol of the wildness that persists in the country of the old wood. The singular purity of the air upon the path of the unicorn is a reminder that the interior of the forest is not empty of life nor subject to the order of the settled lands beyond.
