## Overview

Bjornsholm is a coastal trading settlement on the northern coast of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated on a broad, sheltered bay that is one of the few natural harbours on a coast where the mountains leave little room for settlement. The town is smaller than [Nordhaven](/content/nordhaven) or [Skaldshavn](/content/skaldshavn), but it is a prosperous and well-situated community, and its harbour is a valued stopping point for vessels trading along the coast and across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea). Bjornsholm is known for the [ursine](/content/ursines) community that inhabits the forests behind the town, and the trade between the coastal humans and the forest ursines is a distinctive feature of the local economy.

## The Harbour and the Coast

The harbour of Bjornsholm is a broad, shallow bay that is sheltered by a pair of headlands that enclose the water on three sides, and the approach is straightforward in fair weather, though the shallows at the entrance require careful navigation. The quays are built along the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses that line the waterfront are the centre of the town's commerce. The fishing grounds off Bjornsholm are productive, and the catch is a valued supplement to the trade that flows through the harbour.

The coast around Bjornsholm is one of the few stretches of the Morvind shore where the mountains leave room for a settlement, and the shelf of land between the cliffs and the sea is broad enough to support the town and the farms that surround it. The forests that cover the slopes behind the town are among the most extensive on the northern coast, and the timber and the game that they provide are a significant part of the local economy.

## Trade and the Ursine Connection

The trade of Bjornsholm is a mixture of coastal commerce and the exchange between the coast and the forest. The fishing and the coastal trade connect the town to the other ports of Morvind and the markets of Aethelar, and the goods that pass through the harbour include the metalwork of the interior and the manufactured goods of the south. The trade between the coastal humans and the [ursine](/content/ursines) community of the inland forests is the distinctive feature of the local economy, and the fur, the meat, and the forest products that the ursines provide are exchanged for the fish, the grain, and the manufactured goods that the coast offers.

The ursines of the forests behind Bjornsholm are the most accessible community of their kind in Morvind, and the trade between the two peoples is among the most sustained and the most peaceful of any contact between the coastal humans and the forest peoples. The ursines are occasional visitors to the market square of Bjornsholm, and their presence in the town is a familiar and a welcome sight.

## The Town

Bjornsholm is a small, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the coastal tradition of Morvind, with sturdy buildings of dark stone, steep roofs, and narrow streets that are sheltered from the wind. The town is oriented toward the harbour, and the market square is situated at the head of the main quay, where the coastal trade and the forest trade meet. The population of Bjornsholm is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a small but visible community of [ursines](/content/ursines) who are involved in the forest trade.

## Significance

Bjornsholm is a valued port of the northern coast of Morvind, and its harbour is an important stopping point for vessels trading along the coast and across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea). The town is a member of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/kingdom-of-nordhaven), and its trade with the ursine community of the inland forests is a distinctive and a valuable component of the local economy. The prosperity of Bjornsholm is modest but secure, and the community is a model of the coexistence between the coastal and the forest peoples that is characteristic of the Morvind coast.
