## Overview

Caerhaven is an Aethelaran-side town of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), situated on the southern shore of the strait to the east of [Southport](/content/southport), where the strait opens into the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The town is the easternmost of the Aethelaran strait settlements, and its position at the mouth of the strait gives it a role in the strait trade that is complementary to Southport's. Where Southport is the principal point of pilotage and toll collection, Caerhaven is the primary haven and staging point for vessels that are preparing to enter the strait from the Auric side or that have just completed the passage and are in need of resupply.

## The Harbour and the Strait

The harbour of Caerhaven is a deep, natural anchorage that is sheltered by a curved headland that extends from the eastern end of the town, and the approach is straightforward in most weather, though the currents that run at the mouth of the strait can affect the passage. The headland is the most prominent feature of the Aethelaran shore at this point, and its summit is crowned by a lighthouse that is among the oldest and most important navigation aids on the strait. The light is visible for many miles across the Auric, and it serves as the primary landmark for vessels that are approaching the strait from the east.

The quays line the inner shore of the anchorage, and the warehouses, the chandleries, and the inns and taverns that serve the mariners are situated along the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels that are waiting for a favourable tide before entering the strait, or that have just passed through and are taking on supplies before continuing their voyage across the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The town is a natural stopping point for the trade that flows between the strait and the eastern ports of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and the vessels that call at Caerhaven are a mixture of the strait traders and the ocean-going merchants who work the Auric routes.

The Aethelaran shore at Caerhaven is a coast of low cliffs and sheltered coves, and the town is built upon a raised terrace that overlooks the harbour. The terrain is more open than the western reaches of the strait, and the land rises gently inland toward the forests and river valleys of the northeastern Aethelaran coast. The climate is mild, warmed by the currents of the Auric, and the weather is more clement than the wind-swept Morvind shore across the strait.

## Trade and the Compact

Caerhaven is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), the joint governance of the strait towns, and its role in the compact is focused on the provision of supplies, repairs, and shelter for vessels at the Auric end of the strait. The town is not a principal point of pilotage or toll collection, for those functions are centred on [Southport](/content/southport) and [Veldenwatch](/content/veldenwatch) at the narrowest reaches of the channel, but Caerhaven contributes to the compact through the staging and resupply services that it provides to the strait trade. The council of the compact includes a representative from Caerhaven, and the town's interests are represented in the regulation of the strait and the division of its revenues.

The trade of Caerhaven is a mixture of the strait commerce and the Auric Ocean trade, and the goods that pass through the harbour include the metalwork of [Morvind](/content/morvind) that is bound for the eastern Aethelaran ports, the grain and cloth of the Aethelaran interior that is bound for Sahrana, and the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana) that are bound for the markets of the north. The town is a centre for the transhipment of goods between the strait traders and the ocean-going vessels, and the warehouses of Caerhaven are filled with the commerce that is transferred between the two routes.

The shipyards and chandleries of Caerhaven are a significant part of the local economy, and the town is known for the quality of its provisions and the reliability of its supplies. The vessels that navigate the strait are often in need of fresh water, salted provisions, and replacement cordage and sailcloth after the difficult passage, and the merchants of Caerhaven are skilled in the rapid provisioning that the strait trade demands. The boatbuilders of Caerhaven are also noted for their skill in the construction of sturdy pilot cutters and harbour craft, and the pilot guilds of the strait maintain a number of vessels that were built in the Caerhaven yards.

## The Town

Caerhaven is a well-built, prosperous town, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the strait and ocean trade have brought, with substantial buildings of pale stone, well-paved streets, and a commercial district that is oriented toward the harbour. The buildings rise three and four storeys high, their upper floors projecting over the narrow streets to maximise the use of space within the town walls. The architecture is a blend of the Aethelaran coastal tradition and the strait style, with the lighter arcades and courtyards of the eastern coast combined with the sturdy stone walls that the strait weather demands, and the buildings are constructed to withstand the winds and the salt spray that the open strait brings.

The population of Caerhaven is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people are a mixture of mariners, merchants, and craftsmen who identify more closely with the strait and the Auric trade than with the interior of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar). A small community of [gnomes](/content/gnomes) has established itself in the town, and their workshops produce fine instruments and navigational tools that are valued by the mariners who pass through. The [merfolk](/content/merfolk) of the strait waters are occasionally encountered by the fishermen of Caerhaven, and a cautious but peaceful coexistence has developed between the two peoples.

## Significance

Caerhaven is the easternmost Aethelaran settlement of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), and its role as the primary staging point and haven for vessels at the Auric end of the strait is a significant component of the economy of the strait region. The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), and its position at the mouth of the strait gives it a commercial importance that is complementary to the pilotage and toll functions of [Southport](/content/southport) and [Veldenwatch](/content/veldenwatch). The prosperity of Caerhaven is built upon the trade that flows through the strait and across the Auric, and the town is a key point in the trade network that connects Aethelar to the wider world.
