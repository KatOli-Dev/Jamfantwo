## Overview

Amurra is a wealthy port city-state on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), renowned throughout the known world as the centre of the spice trade. The city stands on a natural harbour that is sheltered by a headland of rock, and its position on the coast near the mouth of a river that descends from the interior highlands has made it the primary port for the export of the spices that are grown in the foothills and the upland valleys of the Sahrani interior. Amurra is a prosperous, cosmopolitan city, and its wealth is built upon the trade in the rare and the valuable spices that are found only in Sahrana.

## The Harbour and the Spice Trade

The harbour of Amurra is a deep, sheltered bay that is protected by the headland that extends from the northern end of the city, and the approach is marked by a lighthouse that is visible for many miles across the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses that are situated along the waterfront are filled with the spices that are the city's primary export. The spice trade of Amurra is the most valuable single trade in the known world, for the spices that are grown in the Sahrani interior are prized in every continent, and the demand for them is constant and growing.

The spices that pass through Amurra include the pepper, the cinnamon, the clove, and the other aromatic spices that are cultivated in the foothills and the upland valleys of the Sahrani interior, and the trade in these spices is the foundation of the city's wealth. The spices are brought to the city by the caravans that travel the river valley from the interior, and they are loaded onto vessels at the harbour for transport to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/morvind). The return cargo includes the grain, the iron, and the manufactured goods of the northern continents, and the exchange of spices and northern goods is the primary commerce of the city.

## The River and the Caravans

The river that connects Amurra to the interior is the primary route of the spice trade, and the caravans that travel its valley are the lifeline of the city. The caravans bring the spices from the upland gardens and the foothill plantations where they are grown, and the journey from the interior to the coast is a matter of many days of travel through the foothills and the river valley. The caravan trade is managed by the merchants of Amurra, who organise the transport, the provisioning, and the exchange of goods between the coast and the interior, and the caravan routes that connect Amurra to the interior are among the most valuable trade routes in Sahrana.

## The City

Amurra is a beautiful, well-built city, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the spice trade has brought, with elegant buildings of light-coloured stone, shaded courtyards, and the gardens and fountains that are the hallmark of the Sahrani architectural tradition. The streets are broad and well-paved, and the public buildings are among the most refined in Sahrana, with the temples, the markets, and the council halls that are the centre of the city's life. The population of Amurra is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a diverse community of traders and merchants from every continent.

## Significance

Amurra is the centre of the spice trade and one of the wealthiest cities in Sahrana, and its prosperity is built upon the rare and valuable spices that are its primary export. The city is governed by the [Amurra Republic](/content/amurra-republic), a political system that is noted for its merchant-led governance, and it is a leading member of the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast). The significance of Amurra lies in its control of the spice trade, which is the most valuable single commerce in the known world, and the city is a symbol of the wealth and the refinement of the Sahrani coast.
