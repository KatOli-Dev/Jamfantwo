## Overview

Qassir is a port city-state on the Auric coast of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), situated on the eastern shore where the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) meets the Sahrani coast. The city stands on a natural harbour that is sheltered by a headland of rock, and its position on the eastern coast gives it a role in the Auric trade that is distinct from the cities of the northern coast. Qassir is a prosperous, well-situated city, and its wealth is built upon the trade that flows through its harbour and the maritime connections that link the eastern Sahrani coast to the islands and the northern continents.

## The Harbour and the Auric Trade

The harbour of Qassir is a deep, sheltered bay that is protected by the headland that extends from the northern end of the city, and the approach is marked by a lighthouse that is visible for many miles across the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The quays line the inner shore of the bay, and the warehouses and trading houses that serve the Auric trade are situated along the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels that are travelling between Sahrana and the islands of the Auric, and the trade that flows through the port includes the spices and the dyes of the Sahrani coast and the products of the islands, which include the exotic fruits, the shells, and the other materials that are found only in the island communities.

The trade of Qassir is a mixture of the Sahrani exports and the goods that are imported from the islands and the northern continents, and the city is a natural stopping point for vessels that are travelling between the Sahrani coast and the islands. The merchants of Qassir are skilled in the management of the island trade, organising the voyages, the provisioning, and the exchange of goods between the coast and the islands, and the island routes that connect Qassir to the island communities are among the most valued trade routes in the Auric.

## The Islands and the Trade

The islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) are the primary trading partners of Qassir, and the relationship between the city and the island communities is one of mutual benefit and long-standing co-operation. The [Auric Isles](/content/auric-isles) are the closest of the island communities to the Sahrani coast, and the trade between Qassir and the isles is the most valuable component of the city's commerce. The goods that are imported from the islands include the exotic fruits, the shells, and the other materials that are found only in the island communities, and these are exported to the markets of the northern continents, where they command high prices.

The [tortolans](/content/tortolans) of the warm coasts and islands are regular visitors to the harbour of Qassir, and the trade between the coastal humans and the tortolan communities is a valued part of the city's commerce. The tortolans provide the products of the islands and the warm coasts, and the city provides the manufactured goods and the grain that the tortolan communities need, and the exchange is conducted in a spirit of mutual respect and benefit.

## The City

Qassir is a well-built, prosperous city, and its architecture reflects the character of the eastern coast, with buildings of light-coloured stone, shaded courtyards, and the gardens and fountains that are the hallmark of the Sahrani architectural tradition. The streets are broad and well-paved, and the public buildings are substantial and well-maintained. The harbour is the heart of the city, and the waterfront is the busiest and the most prosperous district. The population of Qassir is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a diverse community of traders and merchants from the islands and the northern continents.

## Significance

Qassir is the primary port of the eastern Sahrani coast and the main point of contact between Sahrana and the islands of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and its trade is a valued component of the economy of the Sahrani coast and the continent as a whole. The city is a member of the [League of the Northern Coast](/content/league-of-the-northern-coast), and its position on the eastern coast gives it a commercial significance that is distinct from the cities of the northern coast. The significance of Qassir lies in its control of the island trade, which is a valuable and a distinctive component of the Sahrani commerce, and the city is a symbol of the maritime connections that link Sahrana to the wider world.
