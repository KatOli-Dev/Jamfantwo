## Overview

Vargvik is a remote coastal town on the northern coast of [Morvind](/content/morvind), situated on a stretch of shore that is among the most isolated and most inhospitable in the known world. The town stands at the head of a narrow inlet that cuts deep into the cliffs, and its harbour is sheltered but difficult to reach, for the approach requires navigation through a maze of reefs and shoals that have wrecked many vessels. Vargvik is the northernmost permanent settlement in Morvind, and its people are among the most hardy and the most self-reliant in the known world.

## The Harbour and the Isolation

The harbour of Vargvik is a deep, narrow inlet that is carved into the cliffs of the coast by a glacier that once occupied the valley, and the entrance is so constricted that it is invisible from more than a few hundred yards offshore. The inlet provides excellent shelter from the storms, but the reefs and the shoals that surround the entrance make the approach dangerous, and the pilots of Vargvik are among the most skilled in Morvind, for they must navigate a passage that is barely wider than the vessels that use it.

The isolation of Vargvik is the defining feature of the town, for the settlement is cut off from the rest of Morvind by the mountains that rise behind it and the sea that lies before it. The overland routes that connect Vargvik to the other settlements of the coast are few and difficult, and the town is dependent on the sea for its contact with the wider world. The vessels that call at Vargvik are few, and the trade that passes through the harbour is modest, but the town is self-sufficient in the essentials of life, and the people of Vargvik have learned to live on what the land and the sea provide.

## Fishing and Survival

The fishing grounds off Vargvik are productive, for the cold waters of the northern [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) sustain the shoals that are the primary food source of the town, and the catch is supplemented by the hunting of the marine mammals that inhabit the coast, including the seals and the walruses that are a valued source of food, oil, and material. The hunting of these animals is a dangerous and demanding activity, and the hunters of Vargvik are among the most skilled and the most courageous in Morvind.

The town is also sustained by the hunting of the [lupines](/content/lupines) of the cold steppes that lie behind the mountains, and the occasional trade between the coastal humans and the steppe lupines is a valued if rare event. The lupines provide the fur and the meat of the steppe in exchange for the fish and the oil of the coast, and the exchange is conducted at the highland passes that connect the coast to the interior during the brief summer.

## The Town

Vargvik is a small, rugged town, and its buildings are constructed of the dark stone of the cliffs, with walls that are thicker and roofs that are steeper than those of any other settlement in Morvind, for the weather here is the most severe in the known world. The streets are narrow and winding, and the town is built into the cliff, with the buildings climbing the slope from the harbour to the plateau above. The population of Vargvik is small and entirely [human](/content/humans), and the people are known for their extraordinary hardihood and their deep attachment to the remote coast that is their home.

## Significance

Vargvik is the northernmost permanent settlement in Morvind, and its people are the guardians of one of the most remote and most inhospitable coastlines in the known world. The town is a member of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/kingdom-of-nordhaven), though its distance from the capital means that the town is largely self-governing. The significance of Vargvik lies in its survival, for the town is a testament to the capacity of humans to endure and to thrive in the most demanding environments that the world can offer.
