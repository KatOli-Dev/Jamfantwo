## Overview

The Grey Coast Holdings is an alliance of the western coast fishing and whaling towns of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), united by their common dependence on the [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean) and their shared interest in the fisheries and the whaling grounds that sustain them. The holdings are not a kingdom but a commercial and a defensive alliance, and its member towns retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, navigation, and defence. The Grey Coast Holdings is the primary political entity of the western coast, and its influence extends along the full length of the cliff coast, from the northern headlands to the southern edge of the cliff country.

## The Member Towns

The Grey Coast Holdings includes the principal fishing and whaling towns of the western coast, and the membership is drawn from the settlements that share in the fisheries and the whaling grounds of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean). [Greycliff](/content/greycliff) is the largest and the most influential member of the holdings, and its harbour and its whaling fleet are the primary assets of the alliance. [Misthaven](/content/misthaven) is a valued member, known for its fog-shrouded harbour and the skill of its mariners in navigating the difficult conditions of the western coast. [Saltreach](/content/saltreach) is the southernmost member, and its fishermen work some of the most dangerous waters on the coast.

The holdings are governed by a council of representatives from each member town, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the fisheries, the whaling, the navigation, and the defence of the western coast. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the holdings have the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the holdings is a model of co-operation among independent towns, and the council is among the most effective political institutions on the western coast.

## Trade and the Fisheries

The trade of the Grey Coast Holdings is dominated by the products of the fisheries and the whaling grounds, and the dried fish, the whale oil, the ambergris, and the other products of the Greyring are exported to the inland markets and the eastern coast through the overland routes that connect the western coast to the interior. The holdings co-ordinate the management of the fisheries and the whaling grounds, ensuring that the resources of the Greyring are used sustainably and that the interests of the member towns are balanced. The holdings also maintain the lighthouses, the bells, and the navigation aids that are essential to the safety of the western coast, for the fog and the swell that characterise the Greyring make the western coast the most dangerous in the known world.

## The People and the Defence

The population of the Grey Coast Holdings is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people of the western coast are known for their hardihood and their independence. The [cancrids](/content/cancrids) of the rocky intertidal zone are occasional contacts of the member towns, and the trade between the coastal humans and the cancrid communities is a minor but valued part of the local economy. The [goblins](/content/goblins) of the ruins and the caves along the cliff are a recurring concern, and the defence of the member towns against goblin raiding is one of the functions of the holdings' collective force.

The defence of the western coast is maintained by a collective fleet that is funded by the contributions of the member towns, and the fortifications of the individual towns are maintained by each member. The fleet protects the fishing and the whaling vessels from the dangers of the open ocean, and it is the primary instrument of the holdings' power and influence.

## Significance

The Grey Coast Holdings is the primary political entity of the western coast of Aethelar, and its co-operation among the fishing and whaling towns is a model of the kind of association that has sustained the western coast for centuries. The holdings' management of the fisheries and the whaling grounds is a significant component of the economy of the western coast, and its trade connects the Greyring to the markets of the interior. The significance of the Grey Coast Holdings lies in its resilience and its capacity for collective action in the face of the demanding conditions of the western coast, and the holdings are a symbol of the hardihood and the independence of the people of the Greyring shore.
