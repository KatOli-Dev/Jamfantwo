## Overview

Vashari is an inland city-state of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), situated at the terminus of the great caravan routes that cross the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh) from the coastal cities to the interior. The city stands at the edge of the desert, where the caravan routes emerge from the sands and meet the foothills of the interior highlands, and its position at the meeting of the desert and the highland has made it the primary hub of the inland trade that connects the coast to the interior. Vashari is a prosperous, well-situated city, and its wealth is built upon the trade that flows through its markets and the caravans that pass through its gates.

## The Caravan Routes

The caravan routes that converge on Vashari are the great trade routes of the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh), and the caravans that travel them are the primary means of commerce between the coastal cities and the interior. The routes cross the desert from the northern coast to the interior, following the paths that have been established over centuries of travel, and they are navigated by the experienced guides who know the desert and its water sources. The caravans that arrive at Vashari carry the spices, the dyes, and the precious stones of the interior, and they depart with the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods of the coast.

The caravan trade of Vashari is the primary commerce of the city, and the caravans that pass through its gates are the lifeblood of the inland economy. The merchants of Vashari are skilled in the management of the caravan trade, organising the transport, the provisioning, and the exchange of goods between the coast and the interior, and the caravan routes that connect Vashari to the coastal cities are among the most valuable trade routes in Sahrana. The city is also a centre for the breeding and the training of the camels and the pack animals that are used by the caravans, and the stables and the corrals of Vashari are among the largest in the known world.

## The Foothills and the Water

The position of Vashari at the edge of the desert and the foothills of the interior highlands gives the city access to the water that is the most precious resource in the region. The springs that emerge from the base of the highlands provide a reliable supply of water that sustains the city and the caravans that pass through it, and the water is managed carefully, for it is the foundation of the city's survival and its prosperity. The gardens and the orchards that are cultivated around Vashari, using the water from the springs, produce the fruits and the vegetables that supplement the diet of the community and the provisions of the caravans.

## The City

Vashari is a substantial, well-built city, and its architecture reflects the character of the inland region, with buildings of light-coloured stone and sun-baked clay, thick walls, and narrow streets that provide shade and shelter from the heat and the wind. The city is walled, for the desert is a source of danger as well as trade, and the gates are guarded and controlled, for the caravans that enter and leave the city are the primary source of its wealth. The market square of Vashari is one of the largest in the inland region, and the market that is held there is a daily affair, for the constant traffic of the caravans ensures a steady flow of goods and customers.

The population of Vashari is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [salawa](/content/salawa) who are involved in the caravan trade and the guidance of the desert routes. The relationship between the settled humans and the nomadic salawa is one of mutual dependence, for the salawa provide the knowledge of the desert that the caravans require, and the city provides the goods and the market that the salawa need.

## Significance

Vashari is the primary hub of the inland caravan trade of Sahrana, and its position at the terminus of the great caravan routes gives it a commercial importance that is unmatched by any other inland city. The city is the leading member of the [Vashari Pact](/content/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities at the desert's edge, and its trade is a vital component of the economy of Sahrana. The significance of Vashari lies in its control of the caravan trade, which is the primary means of commerce between the coast and the interior, and the city is a symbol of the wealth and the endurance of the inland cities.
