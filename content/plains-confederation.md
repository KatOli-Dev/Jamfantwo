## Overview

The Plains Confederation is an alliance of the central grassland cities and the pastoral peoples of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), united by their common interest in the management of the grasslands and the trade that flows across them. The confederation is not a kingdom but a co-operative association, and its members include both the settled cities of the plains and the nomadic [centaur](/content/centaurs) kindreds that range across the open grass. The Plains Confederation is the primary political entity of the central grasslands, and its influence extends across the full extent of the plains, from the foothills of the mountain spine to the river valleys of the east.

## The Member Cities and the Centaurs

The Plains Confederation includes the principal cities of the central grasslands, and the membership is drawn from the settlements that share in the agricultural and the pastoral economy of the plains. [Avaros](/content/avaros) is the largest and the most influential member of the confederation, and its granaries and its market are the primary institutions of the grassland economy. [Goldmeadow](/content/goldmeadow) is a valued member, commanding the meeting of river and road at the centre of the plains, and [Stagford](/content/stagford) is a significant member, controlling the primary river crossing of the central grasslands. [Sunmere](/content/sunmere) is the largest city of the southern plains, and [Dustford](/content/dustford) and [Redhollow](/content/redhollow) are the frontier members, marking the southern edge of the confederation's territory.

The [centaur](/content/centaurs) kindreds of the grasslands are full members of the confederation, and their participation is a distinctive and a valued feature of the alliance. The centaurs are represented in the council by the elders of their kindreds, and their knowledge of the plains and their management of the grazing lands are a critical component of the confederation's governance. The relationship between the settled cities and the nomadic centaurs is one of mutual benefit, for the cities provide the market and the manufactured goods that the centaurs need, and the centaurs provide the wool, the leather, and the management of the grazing that the cities depend upon.

## Governance and the Council

The Plains Confederation is governed by a council of representatives from each member city and each centaur kindred, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the trade, the agriculture, and the defence of the grasslands. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the confederation has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective force in times of need. The governance of the confederation is a model of co-operation between settled and nomadic peoples, and the council is among the most inclusive political institutions in Aethelar.

## Trade and the Grasslands

The trade of the Plains Confederation is dominated by the agricultural products of the grasslands, and the grain, the wool, and the leather that are produced on the plains are exported to the markets of the coast and the interior through the river and the road network that connects the confederation to the rest of the continent. The confederation co-ordinates the management of the grazing lands and the agricultural land, ensuring that the plains are used sustainably and that the interests of the settled and the nomadic peoples are balanced. The confederation also maintains the roads and the bridges that are the arteries of the grassland trade, and the tolls on these routes are a significant source of revenue.

## Significance

The Plains Confederation is the primary political entity of the central grasslands, and its co-operation between the settled cities and the nomadic centaurs is a model of the kind of accommodation that has shaped the culture and the economy of the plains. The confederation's agricultural wealth and its management of the grasslands are a significant component of the economy of Aethelar, and its trade connects the interior of the continent to the markets of the coast. The significance of the Plains Confederation lies in its inclusiveness and its capacity for co-operation between peoples of different ways of life, and the confederation is a symbol of the balance between settled and nomadic peoples that is characteristic of the central grasslands.
