## Overview

Queen Sera of the Amber Vale is the most celebrated ruler of the central kingdoms of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the sovereign who united the warring principalities of the interior river valleys under a single crown and established the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-vael) as a stable and prosperous realm that has endured for more than six centuries. Her name is recorded in the chronicles of every nation of the continent, and her reign is remembered as a golden age of law, of commerce, and of learning in the regions that her authority encompassed. The chronicler must note that the accounts of Queen Sera's life are among the most extensively documented of any figure of the ancient age, for the royal archives of the Kingdom of Vael preserve the records of her reign in unusual detail, and the chroniclers of the neighbouring realms corroborated the main events of her life with independent testimony.

## Rise to Power

Sera was born in the year of the founding of the city of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), though the precise date is a subject of debate among the scholars who have studied the early records of the Vaelic dynasty. Her father was a minor lord of the Amber Vale, a fertile region of the central river valley that was contested by the larger kingdoms that surrounded it, and her childhood was spent in the fortified manor that commanded the crossing of the principal river of the region. The Amber Vale was so named for the colour of the wheat that ripened in its fields in the late summer, and the Vale was the richest agricultural district of the central lowlands, producing grain that was exported to the cities of the coast and the highlands alike.

The central lowlands of Aethelar were divided in the age of Sera's youth among a number of small kingdoms and independent principalities that were engaged in a cycle of raid and counter-raid that had persisted for generations. The [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) held the eastern coast, the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) controlled the western grasslands, the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) guarded the approaches to the mountain passes, and the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) extended along the river valleys that descended toward the warm coasts. The Amber Vale lay at the meeting of these competing spheres, and the lords of the Vale had maintained their independence by playing the larger powers against one another, a strategy that required constant vigilance and a willingness to shift alliances as the balance of power changed.

Sera's father was killed in a raid by a neighbouring lord when she was sixteen years of age, and she assumed the governance of the Amber Vale at an age when most rulers of the period were still under the authority of regents. The chronicles of the period record that she assembled the principal families of the Vale and addressed them with a speech that impressed even the most sceptical of the elders, declaring that the Vale would no longer be a pawn in the games of the larger powers but would itself become a power that the kingdoms of the lowlands would have to reckon with.

The first campaign of Sera's military career was the punishment of the lord who had killed her father, and she led the forces of the Vale in a winter campaign that caught the neighbouring kingdom unprepared and resulted in the capture of the lord and the annexation of his territory to the Amber Vale. The speed and the decisiveness of the campaign established Sera's reputation as a military commander, and the lords of the surrounding valleys began to send emissaries to negotiate alliances with the rising power of the central lowlands.

## Reign

The reign of Queen Sera is dated from the year in which she was crowned in the hall of the Amber Vale, and it extended over a period of forty-seven years during which the territory under her authority expanded from a single river valley to a kingdom that stretched from the foothills of the eastern mountains to the western tributaries of the great river. The process of unification was achieved through a combination of military conquest, diplomatic marriage, and the negotiation of treaties that granted the conquered principalities a measure of autonomy within the framework of the new kingdom.

Sera's method of governance was as significant as her military achievements, for she understood that the conquest of territory was meaningless without the establishment of institutions that would hold the disparate regions together. She established a system of royal governors who were appointed to administer the provinces of the kingdom, and she instituted a code of law that was based on the customary practices of the Amber Vale but that incorporated the legal traditions of the conquered regions where they did not conflict with the authority of the crown. The code of Sera is preserved in the royal archives of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), the city that she founded at the confluence of the two principal rivers of the central lowlands, and it is studied by the jurists of the present age as a model of the reconciliation of diverse legal traditions under a single sovereign authority.

The economic policies that Sera pursued were designed to encourage the flow of trade across the boundaries that her conquests had erased, and she invested heavily in the construction of roads, bridges, and market towns that would connect the regions of the new kingdom. The Amber Road, which ran from the eastern foothills to the western tributaries and passed through the heart of the Vale, was the most ambitious engineering project of her reign, and it became the principal artery of commerce in the central lowlands. The road allowed the grain of the Amber Vale to reach the markets of the coast, and it brought the iron of the mountain kingdoms and the cloth of the eastern cities to the towns of the interior.

The diplomatic achievements of Sera's reign were equal to her military and administrative accomplishments, for she negotiated treaties with the [Kingdom of Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth), the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), and the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) that established the boundaries of the new kingdom and guaranteed the free passage of merchants across the borders of the realms. The treaties that she negotiated were notable for their durability, for the boundaries that she established have remained largely unchanged through the centuries, and the terms of the agreements have been renewed by each successive generation of rulers.

The court that Sera established at Vaelmouth was the most refined of its age, and the poets, the scholars, and the artisans of every region of the continent were drawn to the city by the reputation of the queen's patronage. The chroniclers of the period record that the queen herself was a scholar of considerable attainments, fluent in the languages of the neighbouring kingdoms and knowledgeable in the history and the customs of the peoples over whom she ruled. She maintained a correspondence with the scholars of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), and she received emissaries from the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) and the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) in her court at Vaelmouth.

## Legacy

The legacy of Queen Sera of the Amber Vale is the Kingdom of Vael itself, the most stable and the most prosperous of the central kingdoms of Aethelar, and the institutions that she established have endured through the centuries that followed her death. The code of law that she instituted remains the foundation of the legal system of the kingdom, and the system of royal governors that she created has been maintained by her successors, adapted to the changing circumstances of the age but recognisably the same in its essential structure.

The city of Vaelmouth, which she founded at the confluence of the two rivers of the central lowlands, grew into the capital of the kingdom and one of the great cities of the continent, a centre of trade, of learning, and of governance that has never been captured by a foreign army. The palace that she built on the high ground above the confluence is the residence of the rulers of Vael to the present day, and the hall in which she received the emissaries of the neighbouring kingdoms is still used for the ceremonies of state.

Sera died in the sixty-eighth year of her life, and her body was interred in a tomb on the hill that overlooks the city of Vaelmouth, where the waters of the two rivers meet below the walls of the palace. The tomb is a place of pilgrimage for the people of the kingdom, and the inscription upon it records the achievements of her reign in the measured language of the Vaelic chronicles. The chronicler notes that the figure of Queen Sera is invoked by the rulers of Vael in every age as the model of the just and the effective sovereign, and the memory of her reign is the standard by which the success of her successors is measured. The unification that she achieved has proved enduring, and the Kingdom of Vael stands as the monument of her ambition and her wisdom, a realm that was forged from the warring principalities of the central lowlands by the determination of a single ruler who understood that the power of a kingdom rests not upon the strength of its armies alone but upon the justice of its laws and the prosperity of its people.
