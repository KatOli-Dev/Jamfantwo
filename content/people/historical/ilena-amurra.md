## The First Merchant of Thirty Years

Ilena Amurra is recorded in the chronicles of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and every principal city of the three continents as the First Merchant who negotiated the [Twin-Reaches Peace](/content/history/twin-reaches-peace) concluding the [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars) and who presided over the beginning of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace). Ilena held the office of First Merchant, elected by the senate of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) for a customary term of two years, for fifteen consecutive terms spanning more than thirty years — a tenure without precedent before or since, for the office customarily rotates among the great trading families to prevent any one house from dominating the republic's governance. The senate's willingness to set the custom of rotation aside, term after term, is itself remembered as a measure of the trust her conduct of the war and the peace had earned.

## From Junior Senator to First Merchant

Ilena was a junior senator of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) with no experience of war when the senate elevated her to First Merchant in the midst of the [Twin-Reaches Wars](/content/history/twin-reaches-wars). She had served for several years as senator for the southern district, where she gained a reputation for fair judgment in trade disputes and careful management of the district's accounts, and the senate turned to her at a moment of crisis in the hope that her caution would prove wiser than the ambitions of the war party. She had no expectation of a long tenure and was recorded as saying, upon her first election, that she hoped only to serve until the senate could agree upon someone better suited to the work of ending the war.

Ilena quickly demonstrated a capacity for administration and a willingness to consult the other senators of the republic. She appointed experienced senators to lead the principal offices of state, including the treasury, the harbour, and foreign relations, and established a regular council of advisers that met weekly to review the affairs of the republic. Her manner impressed the senate sufficiently that, when her first term expired, she was re-elected without serious opposition, and the pattern repeated at every election that followed for a generation.

## Mediating with the Vashari Pact

The negotiations with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact) were the most important undertaking of Ilena's early terms and occupied the first decade of her tenure. She opened the negotiations through the mediators of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), scholars who brought a knowledge of the ancient accords and a reputation for impartiality trusted by both parties. The negotiations proceeded in rounds, with the mediators travelling between the Republic and the Pact to carry proposals and counter-proposals.

The negotiations were complicated by each party's insistence upon the rights it claimed at the beginning of the war. The Republic insisted upon the customs dues by virtue of the ancient accord the founders of the Republic had made with the founders of the cities of the warm coast. The Pact insisted upon the right to export its goods without hindrance by virtue of the natural liberty of trade. The mediators proposed a compromise that abolished the customs dues and replaced them with a small transit fee shared between the two parties. The compromise was accepted by Ilena and by the council of the Pact, and the peace was signed at the temple of the [Sea Mother's Court](/content/religion/sea-mothers-court) at [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) as the [Twin-Reaches Peace](/content/history/twin-reaches-peace).

## Restoring Fleets and Towns

The years following the peace were a period of rebuilding over which Ilena presided with care and patience across her later terms. The Republic rebuilt the fleets broken by [Thessa Nordhaven](/content/people/historical/thessa-nordhaven) and the towns damaged in the wars. Ilena appointed commissioners to oversee the rebuilding, who worked in concert with the senators and merchants of the principal towns, giving first attention to the harbours and granaries essential to the resumption of trade. The rebuilding was the work of two generations.

Ilena also oversaw the construction of the roads and bridges that the [Laying the Stone Roads](/content/history/laying-the-stone-roads) required and established the office of the road wardens that has maintained the roads ever since. The transit fees the peace had established provided the revenue, and Ilena used it with care, setting aside a portion each year for maintenance rather than spending the entire sum on new construction.

## Endowment of the Khalisar Academy

Ilena supported the founding of the Academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in her later terms and appointed senators to arrange for scholars of the Republic to serve in it. The academy was housed in a complex of buildings the council of Khalisar set aside for the purpose and was endowed by Ilena with a portion of the transit fees the Republic received. The first curriculum included the study of the chronicles, the mathematics of trade, and the natural philosophy of the tides and the stars. The academy soon attracted scholars from every city of the three continents and became the principal centre of the learned traditions of the present age.

The academy has served as the principal source of mediators in every subsequent dispute between the Republic and the Pact. It is the legacy of Ilena's long tenure.

## Death and the Return to Rotation

Ilena died in the winter of her fifteenth term, after a brief illness the physicians of the Republic were unable to treat, and was mourned by the senators and merchants of every city of the three continents. She was buried in the senate's tomb at [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra), marked by a stele bearing an inscription composed by the senate in her honour. Her death, unexpected and without a chosen successor prepared for the office, left the senate to decide for the first time in a generation who should hold the First Merchant's chair, a contest remembered as the [Amurra Succession](/content/history/the-amurra-succession).

The memory of Ilena is preserved in the chronicles of every city of the three continents. The principal public observance of the Republic is the anniversary of her first election, attended by the senators and principal citizens of every city of the Republic.

## Model of Just Rule

Ilena Amurra is one of the most admired First Merchants of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), and her tenure is the foundation of the public order of the Republic and of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace) for several centuries. The peace she negotiated, the rebuilding she oversaw, the roads she financed, and the academy she endowed are the legacy of her thirty years in office. Ilena is the measure by which the senators and councils of the Republic judge public acts.
