## Overview

Oren, Magistrate of the Greywash is recorded in the chronicles of the [Vintners and Cellarers](/content/government/local/vintners-and-cellarers) and of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) as the magistrate who negotiated the [Greywash Compact](/content/history/greywash-compact). Oren was born into a household of magistrates in a small town of the upper river in the generations before the treaty, and Oren was trained in the law of the river before being appointed to the position of magistrate of the principal district. Oren served the council of the river towns for more than thirty years, and Oren's treaty is the foundation of the public order of the river for several centuries.

Oren is held in particular esteem by the magistrates and the merchants of the cold north, and Oren's treaty is consulted by every magistrate who must judge a dispute upon the river. The chronicler notes that Oren is one of the most useful of the magistrates of the present age, and Oren's treaty is the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates of the river towns.
