## The Admiral Who Cleared the Stormfall Sea

Thessa Nordhaven is the most celebrated naval commander of the late maritime age of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the officer who established the standing fleet of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) and led the northern squadrons to a decisive victory over the corsair fleets of the western reaches during the wars of the third age. Her name is recorded in the chronicles of every maritime nation of the known world, and her campaigns are studied by naval officers of the present age as models of the conduct of a campaign upon the difficult waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The accounts of Thessa's life are among the most extensively documented of any figure of the maritime age, for the archives of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) preserve the records of her commands in unusual detail, and the chronicles of the corsair ports that she humbled corroborate the main events of her career with the reluctant testimony of the defeated.

## Learning the Sea in Skaldshavn

Thessa was born in the coastal town of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) on the southern shore of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), the daughter of a shipwright who served the fishing fleet and a mother who was the daughter of a merchant of the coastal trade. The year of her birth is recorded in the chronicles of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) as the seven hundred and forty-second year of the kingdom, a period when the Stormfall Sea was troubled by the corsair fleets of the western reaches and the coastal communities of the southern shore were accustomed to suffer raids conducted with the speed and ferocity that the shallow waters favoured. Thessa's early years were spent in the boatyards of the town, where she learned the construction of vessels from the direct instruction of her father, gaining knowledge of the qualities of a ship that would serve her well in the campaigns to come.

The young Thessa was not content with the knowledge the boatyards could provide and was accustomed to follow fishermen upon their voyages to the fishing grounds off the coast. These voyages gave her an intimate acquaintance with the waters of the Stormfall that no amount of study in the boatyards could match; she learned to read the currents and winds of the shallow sea with the precision that the experienced mariner acquires through a lifetime of practice. She was capable of commanding a vessel before reaching the age of twenty, and the master of the boatyard where her father worked was accustomed to say that she knew the Stormfall better than any sailor of twice her years.

## Enlistment and Rapid Promotion

The naval service of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) was a small force at the time of Thessa's youth, consisting of a handful of war vessels maintained for the defence of the coast and the suppression of the corsairs that infested the waters. Thessa enlisted in the service at the customary age of seventeen and was assigned to one of the smaller vessels maintained for patrol of the coastal waters. Her early service was unremarkable in the formal records of the kingdom, but the chronicles of her contemporaries record that she distinguished herself in several engagements with corsairs encountered in the course of her duties.

Thessa's promotion through the ranks was rapid, for the qualities she had displayed in the boatyards of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) were precisely those the naval service most required. She was promoted to lieutenant at twenty-four, to captain at thirty-one, and given command of one of the larger vessels at thirty-four. The vessel she commanded was a longship of the Morvind design, swift and seaworthy, capable of operating in the shallow waters that corsair vessels favoured. This command was the foundation of the reputation Thessa would later acquire.

## Building a Force of Twenty Vessels

The standing fleet of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) was established in the seven hundred and seventy-ninth year of the kingdom, a period when corsair raids upon the coastal communities had reached a severity that the existing patrol vessels could no longer contain. The decision to establish a standing fleet was taken by the council of the kingdom upon the recommendation of the captains of the patrol vessels, supported by the testimony of the coastal communities that had suffered most from the raids. Thessa was appointed to the command of the new fleet, the first occasion upon which a woman had held the rank of admiral in the service of the kingdom.

The fleet that Thessa was given to command was modest in size, consisting of no more than twenty vessels of war and a number of smaller craft serving as messengers and scouts. The vessels were built in the yards of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) under Thessa's supervision, designed to specifications that her experience of the Stormfall had led her to prefer: shallow draught and high freeboard, suited to the conditions of the sea that corsair vessels favoured. The vessels were manned by crews that Thessa selected from experienced fishermen of the coastal towns. The fleet was the most formidable force the kingdom had ever assembled upon the water.

## Three Years of Pursuit and Engagement

The campaign against the corsairs was conducted over three years, the most sustained naval operation the waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) had witnessed. The strategy that Thessa pursued was based on intelligence provided by merchants of the coastal towns, allowing her to anticipate the movements of corsair fleets and position her own vessels at customary points of ambush. The first major engagement was fought off the coast of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn), where a corsair fleet that had gathered for a raid upon the fishing boats was intercepted by the [Northern Fleet](/content/government/local/northern-fleet) and defeated in a battle that lasted the better part of a day.

The victory at Skaldshavn was followed by a series of engagements along the length of the southern coast, driving the corsair fleets from the waters they had infested for a generation. Thessa's tactics were marked by a patience and precision that the corsair commanders had not anticipated, allowing her to choose the moment and place of every engagement. The corsair vessels were swift and well handled, but they were no match for the discipline of the [Northern Fleet](/content/government/local/northern-fleet). The campaign concluded with a final engagement in the western reaches, where the last of the corsair fleets was defeated and the corsair leaders were taken into custody.

## Consolidation and the Treaties

The later years of Thessa were spent consolidating the gains the campaign had achieved. The standing fleet she had established was maintained at the strength the campaign had demonstrated to be necessary, employed in patrol of the waters the corsairs had previously infested. The patrols were conducted according to customs Thessa established, including regular inspection of coastal waters and maintenance of customary signals by which the vessels communicated with the towns of the coast.

Thessa was also instrumental in negotiating the treaties that the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) concluded with the corsair ports of the western reaches, establishing conditions under which corsair vessels might return to lawful trade. The treaties were notable for their moderation; Thessa was convinced that the corsairs had been driven to their trade by poverty of the western ports and that the suppression of the corsairs would not be lasting unless the conditions that produced them were addressed. The treaties included provisions for exchange of prisoners taken in the campaign and for restoration of vessels captured in the course of raids.

## The Fleet That Endures

The legacy of Thessa Nordhaven is the standing fleet of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) and the security of the coastal waters of the southern shore of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), a security that has endured for more than two centuries since the conclusion of the campaigns she led. The fleet she established has been maintained by her successors, adapted to the changing conditions of the maritime age but recognisably the same in its essential structure. The customs of the service that she instituted are still the customs by which officers of the fleet are trained and vessels commanded.

The admiral died in the city of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) in the seventy-third year of her life. Her body was interred in the maritime cemetery overlooking the harbour from the headland to the north. The tomb is a place of pilgrimage for sailors of the kingdom. The inscription upon it records the achievements of her career in the measured language of the maritime chronicles. Thessa is invoked by every naval officer of the present age as a model of the qualities the naval service most requires, and the standing fleet she established is the foundation upon which the maritime security of the Stormfall Sea continues to rest. The waters she cleared of the corsair fleets are those that merchants and fishermen of the coast now use in safety.
