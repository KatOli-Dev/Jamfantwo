## The Founding King of the Karhold Realm

Thrain Ironbrow is the founding king of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), the dwarven sovereign who united the scattered clans of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and carved the great halls of [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) from the living rock. He is reckoned among the greatest of the dwarven kings, and his name is recorded in the inscriptions that line the walls of the deepest chambers of the city he founded. The dwarven records are the primary source for the details of Thrain's life, for the [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) are the longest-lived of the sapient peoples and their oral traditions are among the most faithfully preserved of any species. The human chronicles of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) corroborate the main events of his reign with independent testimony.

## Childhood in the Ironbrow Clan

Thrain was born in a clan-hold of the southern range of the mountain spine, a modest settlement of miners and smiths situated in a valley where a vein of iron ore had been worked for several generations before his birth. The year of his birth is recorded in the dwarven inscriptions as the four hundredth and twenty-second year of the reign of the Stone King, a dating system difficult to correlate with the calendars of the [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) chroniclers, but scholars of the present age place his birth in the middle centuries of the second age, when the dwarven clans of the mountain spine were divided among independent holds frequently in conflict over control of the richest veins of ore.

The clan of Thrain's birth was the Ironbrow clan, a lineage of smiths and miners whose name derived from the dark band of stone that appeared in the forehead of the founder of the line, a distinctive marking held to be a sign of the favour of the deep earth. Thrain bore the Ironbrow mark upon his own forehead, and the seers of the clan interpreted this as a sign that he was destined for greatness, though the young Thrain showed no particular distinction in his early years beyond a strength of body and a stubbornness of will noted by the elders of the hold.

Thrain's father was the master smith of the clan. Thrain was trained in the working of iron and steel from the earliest age at which he could lift a hammer. He forged his first blade at the age of twelve, and the blade was judged by the elders of the clan to be of exceptional quality, a sign of the skill that would later make his name known throughout the mountain spine. The forge he worked in his youth was a humble chamber in the upper levels of the clan-hold, lit by the glow of smelting fires and ventilated by shafts that carried smoke to the surface — the same conditions that every dwarven smith had endured since the founding of the first holds.

Thrain was also trained in the arts of war, for the clans of the mountain spine were frequently in conflict, and every dwarven warrior was expected to fight with the axe, the hammer, and the spear. Thrain distinguished himself in skirmishes between the Ironbrow clan and the neighbouring Greyrock clan and was given command of a war-band before reaching the age of thirty, a sign of the confidence the elders placed in his abilities.

The event that set Thrain on the path to kingship was the destruction of the Ironbrow hold by a combination of a cave-in that collapsed the upper levels of the settlement and a raid by the Greyrock clan that took advantage of the disorder to seize the richest workings of the Ironbrow miners. The survivors gathered around Thrain, who had led the defence of the lower levels and prevented the Greyrock raid from extending into the deepest chambers where women and children had taken shelter. The elders of the surviving clansmen declared Thrain the leader of the Ironbrow remnant and swore to follow him wherever he chose to lead them.

## The Founding

Thrain led the surviving Ironbrow clansmen northward through the mountain spine, travelling for many weeks through passes and valleys, seeking a site where the remnant could establish a new hold safe from the raids of the Greyrock clan and other hostile powers of the southern range. The journey was arduous; many refugees died from exposure, hunger, and attacks by creatures that inhabited the deeper passes. Thrain held the remnant together through the force of his will and the example of his endurance.

The site Thrain chose for the new hold was a valley in the northern range of the mountain spine, where a cliff of granite rose from the forest floor and a river descended from the high peaks to the east. The valley was rich in iron ore, and the granite of the cliff was of a quality that would support the carving of the great halls Thrain envisioned. Thrain struck the cliff with his hammer and declared that here the Ironbrow clan would build a hold that would be remembered for a thousand years.

The construction of Karhold began with the carving of the first hall, a chamber cut into the granite of the cliff by miners and masons of the remnant working with iron tools salvaged from the destruction of the old hold. The work was slow and dangerous, for the remnant was small and resources limited, but Thrain drove the work forward with a determination that inspired the clansmen to labour beyond the limits of their endurance. The first hall was completed after three years of labour, and the remnant moved into chambers carved from the rock, protected from the weather and the creatures of the mountain for the first time since the destruction of the old hold.

The founding of Karhold attracted the attention of other clans of the mountain spine. [Dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of neighbouring holds began to travel to the new settlement to trade for the iron and steel produced in the forges of the Ironbrow smiths. Thrain used the wealth that trade brought to expand the halls, carving new chambers and passages that extended deeper into the mountain and further along the face of the cliff. The reputation of Karhold grew, and clansmen of smaller and weaker holds began to seek the protection of the Ironbrow king, pledging allegiance in exchange for the safety that the growing fortress offered.

Thrain did not merely accept the allegiance of smaller clans but actively sought to bring the entire mountain spine under his authority, leading campaigns against holds that resisted his unification of the dwarven peoples. The campaigns were conducted with the same skill and determination that had marked his earlier military efforts. The Greyrock clan, whose raid had destroyed the Ironbrow hold, was the last of the southern clans to fall. Thrain himself led the assault on the Greyrock fortress, breaking through the defences and capturing the Greyrock king in his own hall.

## The Ironbrow Dynasty and the Deep Realm

The legacy of Thrain Ironbrow is the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), the united dwarven kingdom of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the city of [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) itself, which grew from a single hall carved into a granite cliff into the greatest dwarven city of the continent. The institutions Thrain established — including the council of clan chieftains that advised the king and the system of mining rights regulating extraction of ore from the veins of the mountain — have endured for centuries, and the laws he enacted remain the foundation of the legal system of the realm.

The Ironbrow dynasty that Thrain founded has ruled the Deep Realm for more than eight hundred years. The kings who have succeeded him have maintained the traditions he established, expanding the halls of Karhold and extending the authority of the realm to the limits of the mountain spine. The forge that Thrain himself built in the first hall of Karhold is still in use, maintained by smiths of the Ironbrow line, and the hammer he used to strike the granite cliff on the day of the founding is preserved in the great hall as the symbol of the authority of the king.

Thrain died in the great hall of Karhold at the age of three hundred and seventy-seven years, according to the dwarven records. His body was interred in the deepest chamber of the city, a tomb carved from the rock of the mountain by the masons who had worked under his command. The tomb is sealed by a door of iron and stone inscribed with the record of his achievements; the kings of the Ironbrow line have added their own inscriptions to the walls of the chamber in the centuries since his death. Thrain Ironbrow is remembered not only as the founder of the Deep Realm but as the embodiment of the dwarven virtues of endurance, skill, and loyalty to the clan, and his name is spoken with reverence in every hall of the mountain spine.
