## Overview

Mira, Lady Healer of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) is recorded in the chronicles of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) as the healer who maintained the practice of medicine in the lower Greywash through the worst years of the [Stone Plague](/content/history/stone-plague). Mira was born into a household of healers in [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) in the generations before the plague, and Mira was trained in the practice of the [Plague and Remedy](/content/belief/science/physical/plague-and-remedy) before being appointed to the position of chief healer of the magistrates. Mira served the magistrates through the years of the plague, and Mira's records of the cases that she attended are the foundation of the modern understanding of the disease.

Mira is held in particular esteem by the healers and the magistrates of the cold north, and Mira's records are consulted by every healer who must judge a question of public health. The chronicler notes that Mira is one of the most useful of the healers of the present age, and Mira's records are the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara).

## Early Life

Mira was born into a household of healers who had served the magistrates of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) for several generations, and Mira's mother was a senior healer of the public hospital at the time of Mira's birth. Mira grew up among the wards and the dispensaries of the hospital, and Mira learned the names and the virtues of the plants of the [Plant-Lore](/content/belief/science/physical/plant-lore) before Mira learned to read. Mira was sent to the academy of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) at the customary age, and Mira excelled in the studies of the remedies and of the management of disease. Mira graduated from the academy with the highest honours of Mira's year, and Mira was appointed to the position of junior healer in the public hospital.

Mira served the public hospital for several years, and Mira compiled the catalogue of the remedies that the hospital used with care. Mira's catalogue was more complete than any that the hospital had previously produced, and Mira's catalogue was adopted by the magistrates for the use of the healers of the region. The chronicler notes that the adoption was the foundation of Mira's reputation, and the reputation is the measure of Mira's public spirit.

## The Plague

The plague reached [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) in the second year of its spread, and the magistrates appointed Mira to the position of chief healer of the public response. Mira worked in concert with the commissioners of public health that the magistrates had appointed, and Mira oversaw the separation of the sick from the well, the purification of the air and the water, and the careful disposal of the dead. The chronicler notes that Mira worked tirelessly through the years of the plague, and Mira's work is the foundation of the modern practice of the management of disease.

Mira kept careful records of the cases that she attended, and Mira entered the records in the public register that the magistrates maintained. The records included the name and the condition of the patient, the remedy that Mira used, and the outcome of the case. The chronicler notes that the records are the most complete of the records of the plague, and the records are the foundation of the modern understanding of the disease. Mira's records were consulted by the healers of every city of the three continents, and the records are the measure of Mira's public spirit.

## The Recovery

Mira continued to serve the magistrates through the years of recovery that followed the plague, and Mira oversaw the reopening of the public hospital and the resumption of the practice of healing that the plague had interrupted. Mira also advised the magistrates upon the measures that the recovery required, and Mira's advice was followed by the magistrates in every case. The chronicler notes that Mira's advice was the foundation of the careful management of the recovery, and the management is the source of much of the prosperity of the towns of the cold north.

Mira also compiled a new catalogue of the remedies that the hospital used, and the new catalogue incorporated the lessons that the plague had taught. The chronicler notes that the new catalogue was the most complete of the catalogues of the cold north, and the new catalogue was adopted by the magistrates of every town of the region.

## Later Life

Mira retired from the service of the magistrates in the fiftieth year of Mira's life, and Mira spent the remaining years in the academy of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) in the company of the other healers who had been Mira's colleagues. Mira died in the seventieth year of Mira's life, and Mira was buried in the cemetery of the academy with the honours that the magistrates had decreed. The chronicler notes that Mira was mourned by the healers and the magistrates of every city of the cold north, and the mourning was the foundation of the public memory of Mira's life.

## Legacy

Mira's records of the cases that she attended have been the foundation of the modern understanding of the [Stone Plague](/content/history/stone-plague), and the records are consulted by every healer who must judge a question of public health. The records have been preserved in the archives of the academy of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and the academy has maintained the records with care. The chronicler notes that the records are the most useful of the productions of the academy, and the records are the measure of the public spirit of the healers and the magistrates of the cold north.

The public memory of Mira is preserved in the chronicles of the academy and in the public observances that the magistrates conduct each year on the anniversary of the end of the plague. The chronicler notes that the observances are among the most important of the public observances of the cold north, and the observances are attended by the magistrates and the principal citizens of every city of the region.

## Significance

Mira, Lady Healer of [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) is one of the most useful of the healers of the present age, and Mira's records are the foundation of the modern practice of the management of disease. The records, the catalogue of the remedies, and the legacy of Mira's service to the magistrates are the inheritance that Mira has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that Mira is held in particular memory by the healers and the magistrates of the cold north, and Mira is the measure by which the magistrates and the councils of the region judge the public acts of their own day.
