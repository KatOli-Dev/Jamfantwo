## Overview

Admiral Thessa of the [Northern Fleet](/content/government/local/northern-fleet) is the most celebrated naval commander of the late maritime age of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), the officer who established the standing fleet of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) and led the northern squadrons to a decisive victory over the corsair fleets of the western reaches during the wars of the third age. Her name is recorded in the chronicles of every maritime nation of the known world, and her campaigns are studied by the naval officers of the present age as models of the conduct of a campaign upon the difficult waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The chronicler must note that the accounts of Admiral Thessa's life are among the most extensively documented of any figure of the maritime age, for the archives of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) preserve the records of her commands in unusual detail, and the chronicles of the corsair ports that she humbled corroborate the main events of her career with the reluctant testimony of the defeated.

## Early Life

Thessa was born in the coastal town of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) on the southern shore of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), the daughter of a shipwright who served the fishing fleet of the town and a mother who was the daughter of a merchant of the coastal trade. The year of her birth is recorded in the chronicles of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) as the seven hundred and forty-second year of the kingdom, a period when the Stormfall Sea was troubled by the corsair fleets of the western reaches and the coastal communities of the southern shore were accustomed to suffer the raids that the corsairs conducted with the speed and the ferocity that the shallow waters favoured. The early years of Thessa were spent in the boatyards of the town, where she learned the construction of vessels from the direct instruction of her father, and the instruction gave her a knowledge of the qualities of a ship that would serve her well in the campaigns that were to come.

The young Thessa was not content with the knowledge that the boatyards could provide, and she was accustomed to follow the fishermen upon their voyages to the fishing grounds that lay off the coast. The voyages gave her an intimate acquaintance with the waters of the Stormfall that no amount of study in the boatyards could match, and she learned to read the currents and the winds of the shallow sea with the precision that the experienced mariner acquires through a lifetime of practice. The chronicles record that she was already capable of commanding a vessel before she had reached the age of twenty, and the master of the boatyard where her father worked was accustomed to say that the young woman knew the Stormfall better than any sailor of twice her years.

## The Naval Service

The naval service of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) was a small force at the time of Thessa's youth, consisting of a handful of war vessels that the kingdom maintained for the defence of the coast and the suppression of the corsairs that infested the waters. The young Thessa enlisted in the service at the customary age of seventeen, and she was assigned to one of the smaller vessels that the kingdom maintained for the patrol of the coastal waters. The early service of Thessa was unremarkable in the formal records of the kingdom, but the chronicles of her contemporaries record that she distinguished herself in several engagements with the corsairs that the patrol vessels encountered in the course of their duties.

The promotion of Thessa through the ranks of the service was rapid, for the qualities that she had displayed in the boatyards of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) were the qualities that the naval service most required. She was promoted to the rank of lieutenant at the age of twenty-four and to the rank of captain at the age of thirty-one, and she was given command of one of the larger vessels of the service at the age of thirty-four. The vessel that she commanded was a longship of the Morvind design, swift and seaworthy and capable of operating in the shallow waters that the corsair vessels favoured. The chronicler notes that the command of the longship was the foundation of the reputation that Thessa would later acquire, and the reputation was the foundation of the admiral's commission that was to follow.

## The Standing Fleet

The standing fleet of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) was established in the year that the chronicles record as the seven hundred and seventy-ninth year of the kingdom, a period when the corsair raids upon the coastal communities had reached a severity that the existing patrol vessels were no longer able to contain. The decision to establish a standing fleet was taken by the council of the kingdom upon the recommendation of the captains of the patrol vessels, and the recommendation was supported by the testimony of the coastal communities that had suffered most from the raids. Thessa was appointed to the command of the new fleet, and the appointment was the first occasion upon which a woman had held the rank of admiral in the service of the kingdom.

The fleet that Thessa was given to command was modest in size, consisting of no more than twenty vessels of war and a number of smaller craft that served the fleet as messengers and scouts. The vessels were built in the yards of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) under Thessa's supervision, and the vessels were designed to the specifications that her experience of the Stormfall had led her to prefer. The vessels were of shallow draught and high freeboard, suited to the conditions of the sea that the corsair vessels favoured, and the vessels were manned by crews that Thessa had selected from the experienced fishermen of the coastal towns. The chronicler notes that the fleet was the most formidable force that the kingdom had ever assembled upon the water, and the assembly was the foundation of the campaigns that were to establish Thessa's reputation throughout the known world.

## The Campaign Against the Corsairs

The campaign against the corsairs was conducted over a period of three years, and the campaign was the most sustained naval operation that the waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) had witnessed. The strategy that Thessa pursued was based upon the intelligence that the merchants of the coastal towns provided, and the intelligence allowed her to anticipate the movements of the corsair fleets and to position her own vessels at the customary points of ambush. The first major engagement was fought off the coast of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn), where a corsair fleet that had gathered for a raid upon the fishing boats was intercepted by the northern fleet and defeated in a battle that lasted the better part of a day.

The victory at Skaldshavn was followed by a series of engagements along the length of the southern coast, and the engagements drove the corsair fleets from the waters that they had infested for a generation. The chronicles of the campaign record that Thessa's tactics were marked by a patience and a precision that the corsair commanders had not anticipated, and the patience allowed her to choose the moment and the place of every engagement. The corsair vessels were swift and well handled, but they were no match for the discipline of the northern fleet, and the discipline was the foundation of the success that the campaign achieved. The campaign was concluded by a final engagement in the western reaches, where the last of the corsair fleets was defeated and the corsair leaders were taken into custody.

## The Later Years

The later years of Admiral Thessa were spent in the consolidation of the gains that the campaign had achieved, and the consolidation occupied the remainder of her active service. The standing fleet that she had established was maintained at the strength that the campaign had demonstrated to be necessary, and the fleet was employed in the patrol of the waters that the corsairs had previously infested. The patrols were conducted according to the customs that Thessa had established, and the customs included the regular inspection of the coastal waters and the maintenance of the customary signals by which the vessels of the fleet communicated with the towns of the coast.

Thessa was also instrumental in the negotiation of the treaties that the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) concluded with the corsair ports of the western reaches, and the treaties established the conditions under which the corsair vessels might return to the lawful trade of the coastal waters. The treaties were notable for their moderation, for Thessa was convinced that the corsairs had been driven to their trade by the poverty of the western ports and that the suppression of the corsairs would not be lasting unless the conditions that had produced them were addressed. The treaties included provisions for the exchange of the prisoners that the campaign had taken and for the restoration of the vessels that had been captured in the course of the raids.

## Legacy

The legacy of Admiral Thessa of the Northern Fleet is the standing fleet of the [Kingdom of Nordhaven](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-nordhaven) and the security of the coastal waters of the southern shore of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), a security that has endured for more than two centuries since the conclusion of the campaigns that she led. The fleet that she established has been maintained by her successors, adapted to the changing conditions of the maritime age but recognisably the same in its essential structure. The customs of the service that she instituted are still the customs by which the officers of the fleet are trained and the vessels are commanded.

The admiral died in the city of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) in the seventy-third year of her life, and her body was interred in the maritime cemetery that overlooks the harbour from the headland to the north. The tomb is a place of pilgrimage for the sailors of the kingdom, and the inscription upon it records the achievements of her career in the measured language of the maritime chronicles. The chronicler notes that the figure of Admiral Thessa is invoked by every naval officer of the present age as a model of the qualities that the naval service most requires, and the standing fleet that she established is the foundation upon which the maritime security of the Stormfall Sea continues to rest. The waters that she cleared of the corsair fleets are the waters that the merchants and the fishermen of the coast now use in safety, and the safety is the monument of a career that established the standing fleet of Nordhaven as a force to be reckoned with throughout the known world.
