## The Founder of the Karhold Realm

Thane Karhold is the legendary founder of the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), the dwarven kingdom of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). He is the figure from whom the realm takes its name, the leader under whom the scattered dwarven communities of the mountains were united into a single political entity, and the author of the Compact of the Deep that established the principles of governance that have sustained the realm through the centuries that followed. The historical Thane is difficult to separate from the legends that have grown around his name, but the core of the tradition is attested in the records of the realm and the chronicles of the dwarven people.

The dating of Thane's life is placed by the chronicles of the realm in the early centuries of dwarven settlement of Aethelar, approximately eight centuries before the present age. The period was one of fragmentation and conflict among the dwarven communities of the mountain spine, who were divided by clan loyalties and competing claims over the rich mineral deposits of the mountains. The communities had no central authority, and disputes were resolved through negotiation, feud, or the intervention of the elders of powerful clans.

Thane was born into a clan of miners and smiths who had established their settlement in a deep valley of the central mountain spine, at the location that later became [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold). The clan was not the largest or the most powerful among the dwarven communities, but it occupied a strategic position at the meeting of the principal routes through the mountains. The location provided the clan with advantages in trade and communication that compensated for its limited numbers.

The education that Thane received was typical of a dwarven youth of the period: instruction in the arts of mining and smithing, the history and traditions of his clan, and the use of the weapons that were necessary for the defence of the community. He distinguished himself in his youth by his skill as a smith and his ability to mediate disputes between members of his clan, and he was recognised as a leader by his peers at an earlier age than was customary.

## The Crisis of the Western Mines

The crisis that made Thane's reputation occurred when the western regions of the mountain spine were threatened by an invasion of orc and goblin war bands from the deep caves beneath the range. The war bands emerged from the subterranean passages that connected the deep caves to the mines of the dwarven communities, overwhelming the defences of the western settlements and driving the survivors eastward toward the central mountains.

The dwarven communities of the central mountains, including Thane's clan, were faced with the choice of fleeing eastward toward the coast or making a stand against the invaders. The clans that had been forced from their settlements in the west argued for a unified military response, but the eastern clans were reluctant to commit their forces to a campaign that they did not regard as their own concern.

Thane, who had been chosen as the leader of his clan, argued that the defence of the mountains required a unified command and a pooling of resources. The clans that did not resist the invasion would face the same fate as the western settlements when the invaders had consolidated their gains and turned eastward. The argument was persuasive but not sufficient to overcome the divisions among the clans, and Thane recognised that stronger measures were required.

## The Compact of the Deep

The agreement that Thane proposed was the Compact of the Deep, a formal compact among the clans that established a unified command for the duration of the crisis and a framework for co-operation that would continue after the threat had passed. The compact proposed that the clans would accept the authority of a single leader, to be chosen from among the clan chiefs, who would command the combined forces of the dwarven communities and represent the clans in dealings with external powers.

The negotiation of the compact required months of discussion and debate. The clan chiefs were suspicious of any agreement that would limit their authority, and the memory of past conflicts between the clans made trust difficult. Thane's reputation as a mediator and his skill in addressing the concerns of each clan were essential to the success of the negotiations. He proposed that the compact include provisions for the protection of clan autonomy in local matters, and that the leader chosen under the compact would be subject to confirmation by a council of elders.

The compact was agreed at a gathering of the clan chiefs at the central meeting place that later became the site of the great hall of Karhold. The ceremony of the signing is described in the chronicles of the realm: the chiefs gathered in a circle, each placing a hand on the stone that had been set up at the centre of the gathering, and they spoke the words of the compact in unison. The stone was carved with the terms of the agreement and set up as a monument that was later incorporated into the foundations of the great hall.

## The Campaign of Liberation

The campaign that followed the signing of the compact was the first military operation conducted by a unified dwarven force in the history of the mountain spine. Thane, who had been chosen as the leader of the combined forces, commanded the army that marched into the western mountains to drive out the invaders. The campaign was conducted over several months and involved battles at a dozen locations, each of which is commemorated in the traditions of the realm.

The strategy that Thane employed was based on the systematic clearing of the underground passages that the invaders had used to penetrate the dwarven defences. The passages were sealed or fortified, preventing the invaders from using them again, and the settlements that had been lost were retaken and refortified. The campaign was conducted with a discipline that was unusual for the period, and the soldiers of the combined forces fought as a single army rather than as contingents of separate clans.

The final battle of the campaign was fought at the entrance of the deep passages in the westernmost mountains, where the main force of the invaders was gathered. The dwarven army defeated the invaders in a battle that lasted for three days, and the survivors of the defeated force fled into the deep caves and did not return. The victory was celebrated by the dwarven communities of the mountain spine, and the reputation of Thane as a military commander was established.

## The Founding of the Realm

The success of the campaign gave Thane the authority to establish the permanent institutions that he had envisioned. The compact that had been negotiated as a temporary measure was made permanent, and the institutions of the realm were established. The great hall of Karhold was built at the site where the compact had been signed, and Thane was installed as the first Deep King of the Karhold Realm.

The title of Deep King was chosen to distinguish the ruler of the underground realm from the kings of the surface nations, and the authority that Thane claimed was modelled on the authority he had exercised during the campaign: supreme command of the military forces of the realm, final authority in matters of external relations, and the power to resolve disputes between the clans. The authority was not absolute, for the compact established that the Deep King's decisions could be reviewed by the council of elders and that certain matters, including the succession and the amendment of the compact, required the consent of the clans.

The system of governance that Thane established was a compromise between the authority of a single ruler and the autonomy of the clans. The clans retained control of their internal affairs, their mines, and their forges, but they accepted the authority of the Deep King in matters that affected the whole realm. The balance that Thane struck between central authority and local autonomy was the foundation of the realm's stability, and the institutions that he established have endured with modification through the centuries that followed.

## The Building of Karhold

The construction of Karhold was the most ambitious project of Thane's reign. The city was carved from the rock of the central mountain, expanding the natural caves and passages into a network of halls, chambers, and passages that could accommodate the population of the realm's capital. The great hall, where the Deep King held court and the council of elders met, was the centrepiece of the city, a vast chamber carved from the living rock and supported by pillars that were decorated with scenes from dwarven history.

The building of Karhold required the labour of thousands of miners, masons, and craftsmen over a period of many years. The resources that were required for the construction were drawn from all the clans, and the contributions that each clan made were recorded in the chronicles of the realm. The completion of the city was celebrated with ceremonies that lasted for a full season, and the representatives of every clan gathered in the great hall to acknowledge Thane's achievement.

The city that Thane built became the centre of the dwarven world in Aethelar, the seat of the Deep King and the meeting place of the council of elders. The mines of the surrounding mountains provided the wealth that sustained the realm, and the forges of the city produced the metalwork that was traded to the lowland kingdoms. The city was the symbol of the unity that Thane had established and the foundation of the realm's prosperity.

## The Later Years and the Succession

Thane reigned as Deep King for more than four decades, and the later years of his reign were devoted to the consolidation of the realm and the preparation of his successor. The laws and institutions that he had established were codified and recorded, and the boundaries of the realm were defined through agreements with the neighbouring human kingdoms and the elven communities of the northern forests.

The succession was a matter of careful planning. The compact that Thane had established required that the successor be chosen from the hereditary line of the Deep King, but that the choice be confirmed by the council of elders. Thane's eldest son had been trained in the governance of the realm from childhood, and he had commanded the army in the later campaigns of his father's reign. The council of elders confirmed the succession without dissent, and the transfer of power upon Thane's death was smooth and unchallenged.

Thane died in the great hall of Karhold, surrounded by his family and the elders of the realm. He was buried in the royal crypt that had been carved beneath the hall, and the stone that sealed his tomb was inscribed with the terms of the Compact of the Deep and the chronicle of his achievements. The chronicle records that he was the first Deep King, the unifier of the clans, and the builder of Karhold.

## The Legacy of the Founder

Thane Karhold is remembered as the founder of the Karhold Realm, the unifier of the dwarven clans of the Aethelar mountain spine, and the author of the institutions that have sustained the realm for centuries. The Compact of the Deep that he negotiated remains the foundation of the realm's governance, and the balance between central authority and clan autonomy that he established has been maintained by his successors without interruption.

The memory of Thane is preserved in the annual observance of Compact Day in Karhold, when the terms of the compact are recited in the great hall and the clan chiefs renew their oaths to the Deep King. The observance is the most important ceremonial event in the realm's calendar, and it is attended by representatives of every clan and every community of the mountain spine. The observance serves as a reminder of the unity that Thane established and of the principles that have sustained the realm through the centuries that followed.

Historians of other nations have recognised Thane's achievement in unifying the dwarven clans, comparing his work to the consolidation of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) by [Elara Stoneheim](/content/people/historical/elara-stoneheim) and the unification of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) by [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) Ambervale. The institutions that Thane established have been studied by scholars of governance as a model of the balance between central authority and local autonomy, and the compact that he negotiated has been cited as an example of the agreements that can sustain a political community through centuries of change.
