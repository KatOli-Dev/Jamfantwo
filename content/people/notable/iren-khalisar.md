## Overview

Iren Khalisar [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) holds the position of chief cartographer of the academy of Khalisar, succeeding a line of scholars that reaches back to [Lien Wind](/content/people/historical/lien-wind). Iren was born in [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) more than forty years ago and was educated in the academy before being appointed to the office. Iren's principal work has been the revision of Lien's atlas in light of new information that the pilots of the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch) have brought back from their voyages. Iren's revisions are the foundation of navigation along the warm coast at the present day.

Iren is held in particular esteem by the pilots and merchants of the warm coast, and Iren's charts are consulted by every pilot who must set a course across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). Iren is one of the most useful of the scholars of the present day, and Iren's charts are a measure of the public spirit of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

## Early Life

Iren was born into a household of pilots who had served the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch) for several generations. Iren's mother was a junior pilot of the Watch at the time of Iren's birth. Iren grew up among the charts and instruments of the Watch and learned the names and bearings of the coasts and islands of the warm coast before learning to read. Iren was sent to the academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) at the customary age and excelled in studies of geography and the stars. Iren graduated with the highest honours of the year and was appointed to the position of junior cartographer in the office of the chief cartographer. The master of the academy noted that Iren's ability to measure latitude from the noon sun and to correct for the magnetic variation of the compass was without equal among the students of the year.

Iren served the office for several years, compiling charts of the harbours of the southern coast with care. These charts were more accurate than any the office had previously produced and were adopted by the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch) for the use of the pilots of the fleet. The adoption was the foundation of Iren's reputation.

## The Atlas

The revisions Iren has made to Lien's atlas have been the work of more than fifteen years and have been entered in the copies that the academy keeps. The revisions incorporate new information that the pilots have brought back from voyages to the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and new charts of the harbours of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). Each revision is checked against the logs of three separate voyages before it is entered, a standard that Iren established upon taking office. These are the most comprehensive revisions the atlas has received and are the foundation of navigation along the warm coast at the present day.

Iren has also compiled a new atlas of the harbours of the warm coast for the use of the merchants. The new atlas marks the depths of the approaches, the locations of safe anchorage, and the facilities available at each port, including the quality of the piers and the availability of fresh water and provisions. It is in preparation at the academy and is expected to be published within the next year, with public ceremonies according to the customs established by the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

## Current Duties

Iren serves as the chief cartographer of the academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and as the principal adviser to the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch) upon the navigation of the warm coast. Iren oversees the work of the junior cartographers of the office and reviews the charts that the pilots of the Watch bring back from their voyages. Iren's oversight has been fundamental to the accuracy of the academy's charts and the safety of navigation along the warm coast.

Iren also serves as the principal correspondent of the academy with the academies of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and other principal cities, exchanging charts and observations with scholars of other academies. This correspondence has contributed to the careful management of navigation of the three continents.

## Public Standing

Iren is held in particular esteem by the pilots and merchants of the warm coast and is consulted by every magistrate who must judge a question of navigation. Iren is a familiar figure at the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and attends sessions when matters of navigation are under discussion.

Iren also serves as the principal tutor of the junior cartographers of the academy. Iren's students have gone on to serve in offices of the principal cities of the three continents. Iren's teaching has been fundamental to the training of cartographers of the present day and is the source of much of the accuracy of the charts of the academies.

## The Atlas of the Kharesh

Iren has also supervised the compilation of a new atlas of the harbours of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), in preparation at the academy. The atlas incorporates new information that the consul of the [Kharesh Anchorage](/content/location/settlement/outpost/kharesh-anchorage) has sent back from the trade and is the first comprehensive chart of the harbours of the island. The atlas is expected to be published within the next year, with public ceremonies according to the customs established by the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

The atlas is being compiled by junior cartographers under Iren's supervision, drawing upon the records of the pilots of the Watch and the consul of the anchorage. It is the most comprehensive production of the office and will be the foundation of navigation of the warm coast and the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) for the next generation.

## Significance

Iren Khalisar [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is one of the most useful of the scholars of the present day, and Iren's charts are the foundation of navigation along the warm coast. The revisions of Lien's atlas, the new atlas of the harbours, and the legacy of Iren's service to the academy are the inheritance Iren has bequeathed to the present age. Iren is held in particular memory by the pilots and merchants of the warm coast and is the measure by which the magistrates and councils of the coast judge the public acts of their own day.
