## The Leader of the Isles

Pavo Auric is the current presiding representative of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), the confederation of island communities of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). He is one of the most influential figures in the maritime life of the ocean, and his leadership of the confederation in the present age has been marked by the careful management of the relationship between the member islands and the maintenance of the trade routes that connect the Auric to the surrounding continents.

Pavo was born on the island of Tidesmeet, the largest and most influential member of the confederation, into a family of boatbuilders and sailors who had served the confederation for several generations. The family was among the most respected in the island community, and its members had served as representatives on the council of the confederation in earlier generations.

The education that Pavo received was the practical training of a sailor and a boatbuilder, supplemented by the formal instruction in the governance of the confederation that the sons of the leading families of the islands received. He learned to handle the vessels that plied the waters of the Auric, to navigate by the stars and the currents, and to build the boats that were the foundation of the island economy.

## The Path to Leadership

The path that led Pavo to the position of presiding representative was marked by his service to the confederation in a variety of capacities. He served as a representative of Tidesmeet on the council of the confederation, and he served as the captain of the vessels that patrolled the trade routes of the Auric Ocean.

The reputation that Pavo established was based on his knowledge of the ocean and his skill in the management of the affairs of the confederation. He was known for his ability to find common ground among the representatives of the different islands, whose interests did not always align, and his fairness in dealing with the members of the confederation was recognised by all who worked with him.

The appointment of Pavo as the presiding representative of the confederation was made by the council, which recognised that he possessed the qualities that the leadership of the confederation required. The position of presiding representative is not one of absolute authority, for the confederation is governed by the council of representatives from the member islands, and decisions are made by vote. The presiding representative is responsible for convening the council, overseeing its deliberations, and representing the confederation in its dealings with external powers.

## The Governance of the Confederation

The governance of the Auric Isles is based on the council of representatives from the member islands, which meets regularly at Tidesmeet to coordinate trade, navigation, and mutual support. Pavo, as the presiding representative, chairs the meetings of the council and is responsible for the implementation of its decisions.

The style of leadership that Pavo has adopted is one of consultation and consensus. He seeks the advice of the other representatives before making decisions, and he works to build the agreements that the confederation requires to function effectively. The approach has been effective in maintaining the cohesion of the confederation during a period when the competing interests of the member islands could have led to fragmentation.

The issues that the council addresses include the coordination of the trade routes, the maintenance of the navigation aids, the management of the collective fleet, and the resolution of disputes between the member islands. Pavo's knowledge of the ocean and his experience in the management of the confederation's affairs have been valuable in the deliberations of the council.

## The Trade of the Auric

The trade of the Auric Isles is the foundation of the confederation's economy, and the regulation of the trade is one of the principal concerns of the council. The goods that flow through the harbours of the confederation include the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), the grain and metalwork of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the products of the islands themselves.

Pavo has promoted the policies that support the trade, including the maintenance of the lighthouses, the pilotage services, and the navigation aids that are essential for the safety and efficiency of the ocean routes. The policies that he has advocated have contributed to the growth of the trade, and the volume of goods passing through the harbours of the confederation has increased during his tenure.

The relationship with the merchants of the northern and southern continents is also a concern of the council. The confederation must maintain good relations with the powers that control the ports of the surrounding continents, for the trade that flows through the Auric depends on the access that the island vessels have to those ports. Pavo has maintained the diplomatic relationships that the confederation requires, and the trade has continued without interruption.

## The Relationship with the Merfolk

The relationship between the island communities of the Auric and the merfolk of the waters is one of the distinctive features of the confederation's life. The merfolk are the inhabitants of the waters of the Auric Ocean, and they share the same seas as the island peoples. The relationship between the two peoples is one of cautious but peaceful coexistence.

Pavo has maintained the relationship with the merfolk that his predecessors established. The representatives of the merfolk are not members of the council of the confederation, but they are consulted on matters that affect the waters of the Auric. The agreements that have been reached with the merfolk govern the navigation of the vessels through the waters that the merfolk regard as their own, and the exchange of goods between the island communities and the underwater settlements.

The relationship with the merfolk is not without its tensions, for the two peoples have different needs and different ways of life. The maintenance of the relationship requires the constant attention of the council and the willingness of both sides to compromise. Pavo's diplomatic skills have been essential to the continuation of the relationship, and the stability that the waters have enjoyed during his tenure is a testament to his abilities.

## The Collective Fleet

The defence of the Auric Isles is maintained by the collective fleet, the naval force that protects the trade routes of the confederation from pirates and raiders. The fleet is composed of vessels contributed by the member islands, and it is commanded by a captain who is appointed by the council.

Pavo has supported the maintenance of the collective fleet, recognising that the security of the trade routes is essential for the prosperity of the confederation. The fleet that patrols the waters of the Auric is among the most capable in the ocean, and its presence on the trade routes deters the raiders who might otherwise prey on the vessels of the island communities.

## The Challenges of the Present Age

The Auric Isles faces challenges in the present age that the founders of the confederation could not have anticipated. The competition from the shipping of the northern kingdoms, whose vessels are larger and more efficient than those of the island communities, is reducing the share of the trade that the confederation controls. The costs of the maintenance of the fleet and the navigation aids are increasing, and the resources of the member islands are limited.

Pavo has responded to the challenges by promoting the diversification of the confederation's economy. The island communities are being encouraged to develop industries that do not depend on the trade routes, including the processing of the fish and the products of the sea that the waters of the Auric provide.

## The Reputation of the Leader

Pavo Auric is held in high esteem by the representatives of the member islands of the confederation and by the merchants and the captains who trade in the waters of the Auric Ocean. The fairness and the diplomatic skill that he has demonstrated in his leadership of the confederation have earned him the respect of all who deal with him.

The chronicles of the Auric Isles will record Pavo as the presiding representative who maintained the cohesion of the confederation through a period of change, sustaining the institution that the founders of the alliance established through the challenges of the present age. The work that he is doing is a continuation of the tradition of co-operation and mutual support that is the foundation of the confederation, and the stability that the islands have enjoyed during his tenure is a testament to his leadership.
