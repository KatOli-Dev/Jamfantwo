## The Guildmaster of the Strait Pilots

Brona [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch) is the present chief of the pilot guild of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden), the brotherhood of mariners who guide larger vessels through the narrow and treacherous waters of the channel. She was elected guildmaster by the fellows of the guild in the four hundred and seventeenth year of the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact), succeeding her predecessor, who had held the office for the customary term of five years. Brona is forty-nine years old. Her tenure has been marked by a tightening of guild standards and the acquisition of two new pilot vessels that have extended the reach of the guild's services.

## Apprenticeship on Her Father's Boat

Brona was born in the town of [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch), the daughter of a strait pilot and a midwife who served the town and neighbouring villages. Her father took her upon the pilot boat from the time she was old enough to be of use, and she learned the waters of the strait through direct instruction. She showed a precocious aptitude for reading currents and winds, and her father was accustomed to say that she knew the strait better than any pilot of her age.

Training of a strait pilot is conducted by apprenticeship. Brona was bound to her father's boat at fourteen, the customary age at which apprentices begin their service. The apprenticeship lasts seven years, during which the apprentice learns the channels and shoals, the reading of water and sky, and the customs of pilots who serve foreign vessels. Brona completed her apprenticeship at twenty-one and was admitted to the fellowship after a probationary year. She served for fifteen years on the boats before being elected to the guild council, and she served on the council for eight years before her elevation to the guildmastership.

## The Chief Magistracy of the Pilot Guild

The guildmaster is the chief magistracy of the pilot guild. The guildmaster is responsible for regulating the pilots, maintaining the pilot boats, and negotiating the terms of the pilot service with the merchants who use the strait. The guildmaster is elected by the fellows for a term of five years and may be re-elected for a further term. The election is conducted by the council of the guild, composed of senior pilots and representatives of the associated towns.

The guildmaster's authority is considerable. The pilot service is essential to navigation of the strait, and the guildmaster's decisions on the conduct of pilots and assignment of boats are binding upon the fellowship. The guildmaster is also the principal officer of the guild in its dealings with the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact), the council of strait towns that governs the affairs of the channel, and represents the guild at the compact's sessions. Regular communication with the harbormasters of the principal ports is among the most important occasions upon which guild policy is determined.

## Guiding Vessels through the Channel

The pilot service is conducted from towns that line the channel. The principal stations are at [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch), at [Caerhaven](/content/location/settlement/town/caerhaven), and at smaller harbours situated at strategic points of the passage. Pilot boats are small, sturdy vessels designed to operate in the heavy weather of the strait, each carrying a senior pilot and a crew of three or four oarsmen. Pilots put out from the stations when a vessel is reported approaching the channel, and the pilot boards the vessel at the appropriate point to guide her through.

The fee for the pilot service is fixed by the guild and approved by the [Velden Compact](/content/government/local/velden-compact). The fee is the same for all vessels of comparable size, regardless of flag. Proceeds are distributed among the pilots and boats according to a schedule established by long custom. The guildmaster is responsible for maintaining the boats and paying the crews. The guild's accounts are audited annually by the compact council.

## Examinations, Suspensions, and Records

The guildmaster enforces discipline upon the pilots and has the authority to suspend a pilot for breach of regulations or failure of skill. Standards are high. Pilots admitted to the fellowship must demonstrate thorough knowledge of channels and shoals, reliable judgment of weather, and steady nerve in the conduct of vessels. The guildmaster conducts examinations of apprentices and probationers; the success rate of candidates reflects the rigour with which standards are maintained.

The most serious disciplinary matters involve pilots involved in the loss of a vessel. The guildmaster investigates such cases and determines whether the pilot was at fault. Findings may be appealed to the guild council, which may confirm or reverse the decision. The guildmaster is also responsible for maintaining the records of pilots' service, kept in the guild hall at [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch), which are among the most complete in the known world.

## Firm Administration and Broad Correspondence

Brona is a leader of considerable energy and firm but equitable administration. She pays close attention to the conduct of the pilot service and is accustomed to be upon the boats when the weather is at its worst, insisting upon observing the work of the pilots in conditions that would deter a less committed officer. She receives foreign merchants who come to the guild hall with courtesy, and foreign visitors speak well of the welcome they receive.

Brona's reputation extends beyond the strait towns. She has corresponded with harbormasters of the great ports of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The reputation of the guild of [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch) is a credit to the strait and to the compact. She is held in high esteem by pilots and merchants alike, and the standards she has maintained during her tenure are among the most rigorous the guild has known.

## Brona's Service to the Strait Commerce

Brona is the present chief of the pilot guild of the strait, and the standards she maintains are those by which the navigation of one of the busiest waterways of the known world is conducted. The pilot service is essential to the commerce of the three continents. Her stewardship of the service contributes to the prosperity of the strait towns and to the trade that flows through the channel. She is a worthy successor to the long line of pilots who have guided the vessels of the strait.
