## The Captain of the Pact Guard

Sael Pactguard is the present commander of the collective force of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). He was appointed to the command by the council of the pact in the three hundred and forty-first year of the pact, succeeding the previous captain, who had held the command for the customary term of six years. Sael is forty-two years old. His command of the guard has been marked by a substantial improvement in the discipline of the force and the construction of two new watchtowers upon the caravan routes that pass through the deep desert.

## From Market Boy to Lieutenant

Sael was born in the city of [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari), the principal member of the pact and the seat of the council. He is the son of a merchant of the caravan trade and a mother who was herself the daughter of a soldier of the pact guard. His early years were spent in the markets of the city, where he learned the customs of the caravan trade and the names of the merchants who operated the routes crossing the desert. He showed an early aptitude for the management of caravans and the conduct of the guards who accompanied them. He was enlisted in the pact guard at seventeen, the customary age at which the young men of the inland cities enter the service.

His early service was spent upon the caravan routes, accompanying many caravans upon the crossing of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the journey to the inland oases. He showed a precocious talent for the command of small parties detached from the main body of the guard for the protection of caravans. He was promoted to sergeant at twenty-four and to lieutenant at thirty. He served for a further seven years upon the routes before being appointed to the staff of the guard at [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari), and he served upon the staff for three years before his elevation to the command of the force.

## The Collective Force of the Member Cities

The pact guard is the collective force of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), composed of soldiers and guards contributed by the member cities of the pact. The force is stationed at the principal points of the caravan routes, and the patrols conducted by the force are the principal means by which the routes are kept secure from raiders and the wild peoples of the deep desert. The force is commanded by a captain, appointed by the council for a term of six years. The captain is responsible for the discipline of the force, the conduct of patrols, and the defence of the member cities in the event of attack.

The captain is the principal military officer of the pact, and the captain's authority extends over the soldiers of all member cities detached for the service of the collective force. The captain appoints the officers of the force; lieutenants and sergeants are selected from among soldiers recommended by the commanders of individual cities. The captain is also responsible for the maintenance of the watchtowers and shelters that the guard maintains upon the caravan routes, and inspections are a regular feature of force management.

## Keeping the Desert Passages Secure

The principal concern of the captain is the security of the caravan routes that cross the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and connect the inland cities to the coast. The routes are the lifeline of the pact, and their security is the foundation of the prosperity of the member cities. The captain is responsible for the disposition of patrols conducted along the routes. Lieutenants of the guard are stationed at the principal watchtowers and shelters that the guard maintains at intervals along the routes. Patrols are conducted both by day and by night, and the soldiers are accustomed to the demands of the desert service.

Under the present captain, the routes have been the subject of substantial attention. He has directed the construction of two new watchtowers upon the routes that pass through the deep desert, situated at points where the routes are most exposed to the threat of raiding. The construction has been a significant undertaking requiring the labour of soldiers of the guard and craftsmen of the member cities. The watchtowers are now in operation, and the patrols conducted from them have substantially reduced the incidence of raiding upon the routes they command.

## The Reserve and Militia Coordination

The captain is also responsible for the defence of the member cities in the event of attack and maintains a reserve of the guard at [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari) that may be dispatched to the relief of any threatened city. The reserve is composed of the most experienced soldiers, equipped with arms and provisions required for a campaign in the deep desert. The captain is also responsible for coordinating the militias of individual cities; lieutenants of the guard are in regular communication with militia commanders to ensure defences are in a state of readiness.

The captain maintains a careful record of the strength and disposition of the forces available for defence. Reports submitted to the council are among the most important documents that come before sessions, including an assessment of threats from raiders and wild peoples of the interior. Recommendations made by the captain are taken into consideration by the council in determining the contributions that member cities are required to make to the support of the collective force.

## Courage, Welfare of Soldiers, and Merchant Esteem

Sael is a commander of considerable courage and firm but equitable administration. He pays close attention to the welfare of the soldiers under his command and is accustomed to be seen at the watchtowers and shelters the guard maintains upon the routes. He receives deputations of merchants who come to the guard hall at [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari) with courtesy, and the merchants speak well of the welcome they receive.

Sael's reputation is not confined to the inland cities. Captains of caravans that operate the routes have corresponded with him upon matters concerning the security of the passages, and the reputation of the guard is a credit to the pact and to the council. He is held in high esteem by soldiers and merchants alike, and the discipline he has maintained during his command is among the most rigorous that the long history of the force records.

## Sael's Command as Foundation of Commerce

Sael is the present commander of the pact guard and the principal military officer of the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact). His command has been marked by a substantial improvement in the discipline of the soldiers and the construction of two new watchtowers upon the caravan routes. The office of the captain is one of the most important of the public positions of the pact, and the security of the caravan routes that the captain provides is a foundation upon which the commerce of the inland cities depends.
