## The Speaker of the Sylvan Court

Caelindis Sylvancourt is the present speaker of the elven council of the great forest of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the chief magistrate of the woodland communities governed by the traditions of the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves). She was raised to the speakership by the council in the two thousand three hundred and forty-second year of the elven reckoning, succeeding her predecessor, who had held the office for the customary term of twenty years. Caelindis is in the one hundred and ninety-seventh year of her life. Her tenure has been marked by careful stewardship of the forest and a renewal of contacts between the woodland communities and the human kingdoms that border the wood.

## Deep Forest Lineage and Long Apprenticeship

Caelindis was born in a community of the deep forest situated in the heart of the wood, several days' walk from any clearing made by the hand of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans). Her lineage is of considerable antiquity in the wood, and her family has been associated with the governance of the woodland communities for many generations. Her early education included instruction in the lore of the forest, the traditions of the council, and the arts that are the heritage of her people. She showed an early aptitude for reading the wood and mediating disputes that arise between the forest communities.

Training of an elven speaker is conducted through a long apprenticeship to senior members of the council. Caelindis was bound at the age of forty to the speaker who then held the office, an elven lord of great age and wide repute. The apprenticeship lasted for thirty years, during which she learned the traditions of the council, the management of the forest, and the conduct of relations with the human kingdoms that border the wood. She accompanied the speaker upon circuits of the council and embassies to the courts of neighbouring peoples, experience that has served her well in her subsequent tenure.

## The Council beneath the Great Beech

The [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) is the council of the woodland communities of the great forest. The speaker is the chief magistrate of the council. The court meets in a clearing at the heart of the forest, where a great beech tree of remarkable age and stature serves as the meeting place of the elders. The court is composed of representatives chosen by the communities for their wisdom and knowledge of the wood. The decisions of the court are binding upon the communities, and the speaker is responsible for executing those decisions and representing the forest in its dealings with the outside world.

The powers of the speaker are considerable, though tempered by the traditions of the council and the customs of the woodland communities. The speaker presides over sessions of the court, and the speaker's voice is the deciding one in the event of a tie. The speaker is also the principal officer of the court in its dealings with the human kingdoms that border the wood. Embassies exchanged between the court and the courts of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are conducted under the speaker's authority. The speaker is the guardian of the forest, and regulations governing the cutting of timber and the hunting of game are enforced under the speaker's authority.

## Timber, Game, and Stream Management

The principal concern of the speaker is the stewardship of the forest. Regulations governing the use of the wood are among the most important matters before the council. The forest is the home of the elven communities, and the management of timber, game, and streams is the foundation of woodland life. The speaker enforces regulations that limit cutting to trees marked by foresters and governs hunting of deer and boar. The speaker works closely with foresters and keepers of the streams, and inspections conducted by the speaker are a regular feature of forest management.

Under the present speaker, stewardship has been marked by careful attention to the renewal of timber and the maintenance of streams. She has directed the planting of new trees in areas that have been cut, and regeneration of the forest has proceeded at a pace the foresters consider satisfactory. She has also directed the cleaning of streams and the restoration of fish passages damaged by spring floods, work carried out by the communities under the supervision of foresters. The forest under the present speaker is in good heart, and the management of the wood is a model of the stewardship that elven traditions prescribe.

## Trade at the Forest Margin

Relations between the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the human kingdoms that border the forest are among the most important matters before the council, and the speaker is the principal officer in the conduct of these relations. The forest borders the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth). Treaties negotiated between the court and these kingdoms govern the use of the forest margin and the conduct of trade between woodland communities and human towns. The treaties are renewed at intervals of twenty years, and the present speaker has been responsible for the negotiation of one such renewal.

Trade between the forest and the human kingdoms is conducted under the speaker's supervision. The forest provides timber of the great trees, furs of forest creatures, and herbs gathered for the apothecaries of human towns. The forest receives metalwork from the smiths, cloth from the weavers, and salt and grain that the forest communities require. Trade is conducted at specified points upon the forest margin, where merchants are received by the speaker's deputies and entertained according to the customs of the wood.

## Wisdom and First-Hand Oversight

Caelindis is a speaker of considerable wisdom and firm but patient administration. She pays close attention to the affairs of the forest and is accustomed to be seen in the company of foresters and keepers of the streams, insisting upon observing the work of stewardship at first hand. She receives human ambassadors who come to the court with courtesy, and foreign visitors speak well of the welcome they receive in the clearing at the heart of the wood.

Her reputation extends throughout the elven communities of the continent. Speakers of other elven courts of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) have corresponded with Caelindis upon matters concerning the woodlands of the central continent. She is held in high esteem by the elders of the council and by the forest communities, and the stewardship of the wood during her tenure is among the most careful that the long history of the court records.

## Caelindis's Tenure in the Long History

Caelindis is the present speaker of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the chief magistrate of the woodland communities of the great forest of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). Her tenure has been marked by careful stewardship of the forest and a renewal of relations with the human kingdoms that border the wood. The office of the speaker is one of the most ancient of the public positions of the elven peoples, and the stewardship of the forest under the present speaker is a continuation of the long tradition of the council that has governed the woodland communities for many centuries.
