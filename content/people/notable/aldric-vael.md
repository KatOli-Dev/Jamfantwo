## The Current Ruler of the Vaelic Dynasty

Aldric Vael is the present ruler of the central river kingdom of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and the thirty-seventh monarch of the Vaelic dynasty, founded by [Sera Ambervale](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) more than six centuries ago. He ascended to the throne in the nine hundred and eleventh year of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic), succeeding his father [Halric](/content/people/historical/halric-vael), who died after a reign of thirty-eight years. Aldric is fifty-six years old. His reign has been marked by careful stewardship of the kingdom's affairs and close attention to the counsel of the valley lords and the capital guilds.

## Royal Birth and Education at Court

Aldric was born in the royal palace at [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), overlooking the confluence of the two rivers, the eldest son of Halric by his queen, a lady of the great house of the eastern marches. The Vaelic lineage is unbroken through the long succession of the dynasty, and the descent from [Sera Ambervale](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) through a chain of marriages and births is recorded in the rolls of the court without any irregularity of succession. Aldric received the customary education of princes: instruction in law and governance, the history of the dynasty and the continent, and the arts of sword and bow.

At sixteen, he was sent to the court of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), where he spent two years as a guest of that realm's king and formed acquaintances among the eastern lords and ladies, a stage then customary in princely education. He returned at eighteen with knowledge of the eastern court and customs that has served him well in his reign. At twenty-four, he was invested with the governance of the western marches and administered them for twelve years before his father's death summoned him to the capital. During those years in the marches, he gained direct experience of the administration of justice, the collection of revenues, and the command of the border garrisons that patrol the frontier with the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation).

## Stewardship and Prudent Management

Aldric's reign has been characterised by careful attention to the kingdom's affairs and prudent management of relations with neighbouring realms. He has maintained the institutions established by his predecessors, and the code of law instituted by [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) remains the foundation of the legal system. Governors appointed from among the valley lords and capital scholars administer the provinces.

Foreign policy has focused on relations with the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation); treaties negotiated by previous kings have been renewed and extended. Customary relations with the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) have been maintained through regular embassies. Aldric has not engaged in any war of conquest, and the peace prevailing throughout his reign accords with the dynasty's traditions.

## The Officers and the Council

The court is maintained at [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth) in the palace built by [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) above the river confluence. Aldric is attended by the great officers of the household: the lord high steward, the lord chamberlain, and the master of the horse. The council consists of the valley lords, the chief judges, and the guild representatives. It meets regularly under the king's presidency, and its decisions are binding.

Aldric listens patiently to contrary opinions before deciding. His judgment is held in high esteem by the realm's lords, and his appointments to great offices have been made with careful attention to merit. The queen, a lady of the house of the southern reach, is a patron of the arts and learning. The court is noted for the refinement of its entertainments and the dignity of its ceremonies.

## Governors, Judges, and the King's Bench

The kingdom is administered through the system of royal governors and local councils established by [Sera](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale). Governors, appointed by the king for fixed terms, are responsible for justice, tax collection, and the maintenance of roads and bridges. They are drawn from the valley lords and the capital scholars.

The law code is administered by judges of the royal court, appointed by the king from among scholars trained at the academy of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), one of the oldest institutions of the kingdom. The king's own court is presided over by the lord chief justice; appeals from lower courts are heard by the king's bench, which meets in the hall of justice adjoining the palace. Aldric takes a close personal interest in the administration of law and attends court sessions on occasions of particular importance. Petitioners from all classes of the kingdom are permitted to present their cases before the king's bench, and the sessions are open to the public.

The treasury is administered by the lord high treasurer under the king's direction. Royal revenues derive from taxes on trade passing through the river confluences, from the agricultural output of the valley lands, and from the tolls collected at the bridges and the city gates. Expenditure is recorded in ledgers that are audited annually by a committee of the council, and the king reviews the accounts in person at the close of each year.

## Temperament and Standing Abroad

Aldric is a ruler of moderate and considered temperament, neither given to the extravagance of more theatrical predecessors nor to the parsimony of the more cautious. He is industrious and attentive to the affairs of the kingdom; reports submitted by governors and the council are read carefully and answered with deliberation. His religious observances are regular, and his daily attendance at the palace chapel sets an example of monarchial piety.

Abroad, the king's reputation is favourable. Envoys of neighbouring realms speak well of their reception at his court. He is courteous to strangers and attentive to the customs of foreign courts. He has been married for thirty-four years, and the queen has borne him three children: two sons and a daughter, all living, two married into houses of neighbouring kingdoms. The heir, the eldest son, has been invested with the governance of the eastern marches and is being prepared for his future responsibilities.

## Aldric's Place in the Long Succession

Aldric is the present ruler of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the representative of the longest unbroken dynasty of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). His reign has been marked by careful stewardship, and the peace and prosperity that have prevailed throughout his years on the throne reflect the prudence with which he has conducted the government. He is held in high esteem by his subjects and by the rulers of neighbouring realms, and the stability of the kingdom during his reign provides a foundation for the commerce and culture of the central continent.
