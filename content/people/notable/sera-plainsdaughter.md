## The Elder of the Council

Sera Plainsdaughter is a centaur elder of the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation) and a member of the council that governs the alliance of the grassland cities and the nomadic kindreds of the central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) plains. She is one of the most respected elders among the centaur kindreds, and her voice in the council carries the weight of the generations of knowledge that her people have accumulated.

Sera was born into the kindred that ranges across the southern plains of the confederation, the region where the grasslands meet the river valleys of the east. The kindred was among the original signatories of the founding charter that [Darian of the Plains](/content/people/historical/darian-plains) had negotiated, and the traditions of the confederation were deeply ingrained in the family from which Sera descended.

The education that Sera received was the traditional training of a centaur elder. She learned the history and the traditions of the kindreds and the confederation, the patterns of the seasons and the movements of the herds, and the arts of mediation and governance that the elders of the kindreds practised. The elders who trained her recognised that she possessed the qualities that the confederation required in its representatives, and they prepared her for the role that she would later assume.

## The Path to the Council

The path that led Sera to the council of the confederation was gradual. She served as an elder of her kindred for many years, mediating disputes among the members and representing the kindred in dealings with the other kindreds and the settled communities of the plains. The reputation that she established as a wise and fair mediator led to her appointment as a member of the council.

The council of the confederation meets three times each year, at the spring, summer, and autumn gatherings that are held at the traditional meeting places marked by the stone circles of the plains. Sera attends the meetings as the representative of her kindred, and she participates in the deliberations that shape the policies of the confederation.

The style of governance that Sera practises is based on the centaur tradition of consensus, which seeks to find a resolution that all parties can accept rather than imposing a solution on the unwilling. She is known for her patience in listening to the concerns of others and for her skill in proposing compromises that address the interests of both the settled communities and the nomadic kindreds.

## The Role in the Council

The role that Sera plays in the council of the confederation is that of advocate for the interests of the nomadic kindreds. The confederation is composed of both settled cities and nomadic centaurs, and the balance between the interests of the two groups is a constant concern of the council. Sera, speaking from the experience of the kindreds, ensures that the concerns of the nomadic peoples are heard in the deliberations.

The concerns that Sera brings to the council include the allocation of grazing lands, the management of water sources, and the regulation of the movement of the kindreds across the plains. The interests of the nomadic peoples do not always align with the interests of the settled farmers and merchants, and the council must find resolutions that balance the needs of both.

Sera also serves as a mediator between the kindreds and the settled communities when disputes arise. The disputes that occur between the two groups are varied in nature, involving the boundaries of grazing lands, the damage caused by herds to crops, and the terms of the trade between the kindreds and the towns. Sera's knowledge of both the nomadic and the settled ways of life makes her an effective mediator in these disputes.

## The Knowledge of the Plains

The knowledge of the plains that Sera possesses is the foundation of her authority in the council. The centaur kindreds have accumulated knowledge of the grasslands over centuries of inhabitation, and Sera is among the most knowledgeable of the current generation of elders.

The knowledge includes the understanding of the patterns of the weather, the movements of the herds, and the conditions of the grazing. Sera can predict the coming of the rains and the droughts, the movements of the wild animals that share the plains, and the health of the grasslands that sustain the kindreds. The knowledge is based on direct observation and the oral traditions of the kindreds, and it is essential for the management of the plains.

The knowledge also includes the understanding of the water sources that are the lifeline of the plains. The wells and the springs that provide water for the kindreds and the settlements are distributed across the grasslands, and the management of the water sources requires the knowledge of their locations, their capacities, and the patterns of their replenishment. Sera's knowledge of the water sources is a resource that the council values in its deliberations on the management of the plains.

## The Relationship with the Settled Communities

The relationship between the centaur kindreds and the settled communities of the confederation is one of the defining features of the politics of the plains. The two groups are bound together by the institutions of the confederation, but the relationship is not without tensions. The expansion of the settled communities into the grazing lands, the competition for water sources, and the differences in the ways of life are all sources of friction.

Sera has devoted much of her attention to the management of the relationship with the settled communities. She has established relationships with the representatives of the principal cities of the confederation, and she works with them to find resolutions to the disputes that arise. The relationships that she has built are based on mutual respect and the recognition that the prosperity of both the nomadic kindreds and the settled communities depends on the continuation of the confederation.

## The Challenges of the Present Age

The Plains Confederation faces challenges in the present age that the founders of the alliance could not have anticipated. The expansion of the settled communities into the grazing lands of the kindreds is increasing, as the population of the confederation grows and the demand for agricultural land rises. The pressure on the water sources and the grazing is intensifying, and the competition between the kindreds and the farmers for the resources of the plains is becoming more acute.

Sera has responded to the challenges by advocating for the strengthening of the institutions that regulate the use of the plains. The council has revised the rules that govern the allocation of grazing lands and water sources, and it has increased the resources that are devoted to the enforcement of the rules. The measures have been effective in reducing the incidence of disputes, but the underlying pressures continue to grow.

The challenge of the relationship between the kindreds and the settled communities is also evolving. The younger generations of both the nomadic and the settled peoples are less connected to the traditions that sustained the confederation, and the bonds that hold the alliance together are weakening. Sera has devoted much of her energy to the education of the younger generations, teaching them the history and the principles of the confederation, and working to ensure that the traditions that Darian of the Plains established are preserved for the future.

## The Reputation of the Elder

Sera Plainsdaughter is held in high esteem by the kindreds of the centaur people and by the representatives of the settled communities of the confederation. The wisdom and the fairness that she has demonstrated in her service to the council have earned her the respect of all who deal with her, and she is recognised as one of the most important figures in the governance of the confederation in the present age.

The chronicles of the confederation will record Sera as the elder who defended the interests of the nomadic kindreds through a period of change, sustaining the traditions that Darian of the Plains established through the challenges of the present age. The work that she is doing is a continuation of the tradition of co-operation between the settled and the nomadic peoples that is the foundation of the confederation, and the stability that the plains have enjoyed during her service is a testament to her wisdom and her dedication.
