## The Chieftain of the Frostwall Clans

Brynja Frostwall is the current chieftain of the Frostwall Clans, the confederation of [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) clans of the deep interior of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). She is one of the most respected leaders of the dwarven people in the present age, and her authority commands the loyalty of the clans that inhabit the most remote and demanding region of the continent.

Brynja was born in the fortress-city of [Grimhold](/content/location/settlement/city/grimhold), the principal stronghold of the Frostwall Clans, into a family that had produced chieftains for several generations. The family was among the most powerful in the confederation, and its influence extended through the network of alliances and relationships that bound the clans together.

The education that Brynja received was the traditional training of a dwarven warrior of the Frostwall Clans. She learned the use of the axe, the spear, and the bow that were the weapons of the interior, and she learned the tactics of the defence of the passes that were the principal military concern of the clans. She also learned the history and the traditions of the clans, the laws that governed the relationships between them, and the arts of leadership that the chieftain required.

## The Path to Leadership

The path that led Brynja to the position of chieftain was marked by her service in the defence of the interior passes. The clans faced constant threats from the orc and lupine war bands that inhabited the deep interior, and the defence of the passes was the principal responsibility of the warriors of the clans.

Brynja served in the garrisons that guarded the passes, rising through the ranks to command a company of the warriors who defended the northern approaches. Her skill in the command of the company, her courage in the face of the enemy, and her fairness in dealing with the warriors under her command attracted the attention of the chieftain who then held the position.

The appointment of Brynja as the successor to the chieftain was confirmed by the council of the clan chiefs, who recognised that she possessed the qualities that the leadership of the confederation required. She was formally invested as chieftain in the great hall of Grimhold, in a ceremony that was attended by representatives of all the clans of the confederation.

## The Defence of the Interior

The defence of the deep interior is the principal responsibility of the chieftain of the Frostwall Clans, and Brynja has devoted much of her attention to the maintenance of the fortifications and the garrisons that protect the passes. The threat from the orc and lupine war bands is constant, and the clans must maintain a state of readiness that would be unnecessary in the more settled regions of Morvind.

Brynja has strengthened the fortifications of the principal passes, reinforcing the walls and the towers that command the approaches to the interior. She has also increased the size of the garrisons that guard the passes, ensuring that the warriors who serve there are well trained and well equipped.

The strategy that Brynja has pursued in the defence of the interior is based on the combination of the fixed fortifications and the mobile patrols. The fortifications guard the principal passes, while the patrols range across the approaches, monitoring the movements of the war bands and providing early warning of any incursion.

## The Council of the Clan Chiefs

The governance of the Frostwall Clans is based on the council of the clan chiefs, which meets at Grimhold to deliberate on matters that affect the whole confederation. Brynja, as the chieftain, presides over the council and is responsible for the execution of its decisions.

The council meets at intervals determined by the needs of the confederation, and the sessions are occasions of ceremony and deliberation. The chiefs who attend are the leaders of the individual clans, and they bring to the council the concerns and the perspectives of their communities.

The decisions of the council are reached by consensus, for the tradition of the clans requires that all the chiefs agree before a decision is binding. The process of reaching consensus is often slow, requiring patience and the willingness to compromise. Brynja's skill as a mediator, developed through years of service in the defence of the interior, has been essential to the maintenance of the consensus that the confederation requires.

## The Relationship with the Stoneheim Holds

The relationship between the Frostwall Clans and the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) is one of the defining features of the politics of the Morvind interior. The two dwarven realms are connected by the geography of the mountains and the trade routes that cross the passes.

Brynja has maintained the relationship with the Stoneheim Holds that her predecessors established. The trade between the clans and the holds flows through the passes, with the metalwork and the stone of the interior exchanged for the grain and the manufactured goods of the east. The relationship is one of mutual benefit, but it is also a source of tension, for the two realms have different interests and different traditions of governance.

The negotiations with the Stoneheim Holds are conducted through the envoys that the chieftain dispatches to the eastern capital. Brynja has appointed experienced warriors and traders as her envoys, and they have maintained the channels of communication that the relationship requires.

## The Challenges of the Present Age

The Frostwall Clans face challenges in the present age that the earlier generations of the confederation did not face. The war bands of the deep interior are becoming more organised, and the threat they pose to the passes is more serious than it was in earlier decades.

Brynja has responded to the challenges by strengthening the defences of the passes and by seeking allies among the other peoples of the interior. The [ursines](/content/species/sapient/ursines) of the forests and the [lupines](/content/species/sapient/lupines) of the steppes have been approached for alliances, and some have agreed to co-operate with the clans in the defence of the passes.

The challenge of the changing climate is also a concern. The winters of the deep interior are becoming longer and more severe, and the passes are closed for longer periods than they were in earlier generations. The clans must rely on their own resources for longer periods, and the stores of food and fuel that they maintain must be larger than those of earlier generations.

## The Reputation of the Chieftain

Brynja Frostwall is held in high esteem by the warriors and the elders of the Frostwall Clans, and by the representatives of the Stoneheim Holds and the other peoples of the interior. The courage and the wisdom that she has demonstrated in the leadership of the confederation have earned her the respect of all who deal with her.

The chronicles of the Frostwall Clans will record Brynja as the chieftain who strengthened the defences of the interior, maintained the unity of the confederation through a period of challenge, and preserved the traditions of the clans for the generations that follow. The work that she is doing is a continuation of the tradition of leadership that the chieftains of the Frostwall Clans have maintained for centuries, and the stability of the interior during her tenure is a testament to her skill and her dedication.

Brynja continues to lead the clans in the present age, commanding the respect of the warriors and the chiefs who serve under her authority. The passes of the deep interior are still guarded by the warriors she commands, and the traditions of the clans are still maintained under her leadership. She is the chieftain of the Frostwall Clans, and her name will be remembered by the dwarven people of the interior for the generations that follow.
