## The Chief Healer of Vassholm

Joren Vassholm holds the position of chief healer of the public hospital of Vassholm, succeeding a line of healers that reaches back to [Mira Nessara](/content/people/historical/mira-nessara). Joren was born in [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) more than fifty years ago and was trained in the practice of the Plague and Remedy before being appointed to the position. Joren's principal work has been the supervision of the public hospital and the compilation of a new catalogue of the remedies the hospital uses. Joren's catalogue is the foundation of the practice of healing in the cold north at the present day.

Joren is held in particular esteem by the healers and magistrates of the cold north, and Joren's judgment is consulted by every healer who must decide a question of public health. Joren is one of the most useful of the healers of the present day, and Joren's catalogue is a measure of the public spirit of the magistrates of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm).

## Brought Up in the Hospital Wards

Joren was born into a household of healers who had served the public hospital of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) for several generations. Joren's mother was a senior healer of the hospital at the time of Joren's birth. Joren grew up among the wards and dispensaries of the hospital and learned the names and virtues of the plants of the Plant-Lore before learning to read. Joren was sent to the academy of Vassholm at the customary age and excelled in studies of remedies and the management of disease. Joren graduated with the highest honours of the year and was appointed to the position of junior healer in the public hospital.

Joren served the public hospital for several years, compiling records of the cases the hospital had attended with care. These records were more complete than any the hospital had previously produced and were adopted by the magistrates for the use of the healers of the region. The adoption was the foundation of Joren's reputation.

## The Most Comprehensive Record of Remedies

The catalogue of remedies that Joren has compiled has been the work of more than fifteen years and has been published by the academy of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) for the use of the healers of the cold north. The catalogue includes remedies tested since the time of [Mira Nessara](/content/people/historical/mira-nessara) and new remedies Joren has tested in the course of service to the hospital. Each entry records the symptoms for which the remedy is effective, the method of preparation, the dosage, and any adverse effects observed in practice. The catalogue is the most comprehensive of the cold north and is the foundation of healing practice in the region.

Joren has also revised the rules of practice of the hospital, and these revisions have been entered in the register maintained by the magistrates. The revisions have contributed to the careful management of the hospital and the health of the people of the cold north.

## Oversight of Healers and Inter-Hospital Correspondence

Joren serves as the chief healer of the public hospital of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and as the principal adviser to the magistrates of the cold north upon the management of disease. Joren oversees the work of the junior healers of the hospital and reviews the cases that the healers bring to Joren's attention. Joren's oversight has been fundamental to the accuracy of practice at the hospital and the health of the people of the cold north.

Joren also serves as the principal correspondent of the hospital with the hospitals of other principal cities, exchanging records and observations with healers of other hospitals. This correspondence has contributed to the careful management of public health across the three continents.

## Joren's Esteem among Healers

Joren is held in particular esteem by the healers and magistrates of the cold north and is consulted by every magistrate who must decide a question of public health. Joren is a familiar figure at the council of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and attends sessions when matters of public health are under discussion.

Joren also serves as the principal tutor of the junior healers of the hospital. Joren's students have gone on to serve the hospitals of the principal cities of the three continents. Joren's teaching has been fundamental to the training of healers of the present day and is the source of much of the accuracy of practice at the hospitals.

## A Dedicated Space for Chronic Cases

Joren has overseen the construction of a new ward of the public hospital, opened to receive patients in the past year. The new ward contains additional beds for treatment of chronic cases that the hospital has admitted in increasing numbers since the recovery from the [Stone Plague](/content/history/stone-plague). The Stone Plague left many survivors with lasting debilities that require ongoing care, and the hospital had been forced to turn away patients for lack of space before the new ward was built. The ward includes a separate pharmacy, a room for the preparation of poultices and ointments, and a heated storage cellar for remedies that must be kept from the frost. It is the best equipped of the wards of the cold north and has contributed to the careful management of chronic cases and the recovery of patients.

The new ward was financed by the council of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and by contributions of the principal merchants of the city, contributions solicited by Joren in person. Joren prepared a detailed proposal setting out the expected costs, the number of patients who would be served, and the projected savings from reduced mortality among the chronic cases, and presented this proposal before the council. The merchants were moved by the argument that a healthier workforce would improve the productivity of the northern trade routes. These contributions have been essential to the careful management of the hospital and the health of the people of the cold north.

## Joren's Legacy to the Healing Arts

Joren of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) is one of the most useful of the healers of the present day, and Joren's catalogue is the foundation of the practice of healing in the cold north. The catalogue, the revised rules of the hospital, and the legacy of Joren's service to the magistrates are the inheritance Joren has bequeathed to the present age. Joren is held in particular memory by the healers and magistrates of the cold north and is the measure by which the magistrates and councils of the region judge the public acts of their own day.
