## Overview

Thane Korin of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/government/national/holds-of-stoneheim) is the present chief magistrate of the dwarven mountain communities of the eastern ranges of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/the-morvind-uplands), and the senior member of the council of the mountain holds. He was elected to the thaneship by the council of the holds in the year that the chroniclers of the mountain communities record as the nine hundred and forty-seventh year of the Stoneheim reckoning, succeeding his predecessor, who had held the office for the customary term of twelve years. Thane Korin is in the one hundred and twelfth year of his life, and his tenure of the office has been marked by a substantial expansion of the trade in the metals and the stone of the mountain and by a careful attention to the maintenance of the fortifications of the highland passes.

## Background and Lineage

Korin was born in the deep hall of Stoneheim, the principal hold of the mountain community, and he is the eldest son of the previous thane of Stoneheim, who held the office for a period of sixteen years before his retirement. The lineage of Korin is among the most ancient of the mountain holds, and the family to which he belongs has been associated with the governance of the holds for many generations. The early years of Korin were spent in the customary education of the dwarven young of the principal families, including instruction in the lore of the mountains, the traditions of the council, and the crafts of the smith and the mason that are the heritage of his people. The young Korin showed an early aptitude for the management of the metal trade and for the conduct of the embassies that the holds exchange with the lowland towns.

The training of a thane of the holds is conducted through a long apprenticeship to the senior members of the council, and the young Korin was bound at the age of forty-five to the thane who then held the office, a dwarven lord of great age and wide repute in the affairs of the mountain. The apprenticeship lasted for a period of twenty-five years, during which Korin learned the traditions of the council, the management of the metal trade, and the conduct of the relations with the lowland towns that purchase the products of the mountain. The apprentice accompanied the thane upon the circuits of the holds and upon the embassies to the courts of the lowland princes, and the experience that he gained during these years has served him well in his subsequent tenure of the office.

## The Holds of Stoneheim

The [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/government/national/holds-of-stoneheim) are the principal dwarven communities of the eastern ranges of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/the-morvind-uplands), a federation of mountain holds that are linked by tunnels and by the paths that wind through the high country. The holds are governed by a council of the senior members of the principal families, and the thane is the chief magistrate of the council and the principal officer of the federation. The council meets in the great hall of Stoneheim, where the decisions of the federation are taken and the embassies from the lowland princes are received. The council is composed of the lords of the principal holds and the representatives of the craftsmen who supply the metals and the stone that are the wealth of the mountain.

The powers of the thane are considerable, though they are tempered by the traditions of the council and by the customs of the mountain holds. The thane presides over the sessions of the council, and the thane's voice is the deciding one in the event of a tie. The thane is also the principal officer of the federation in its dealings with the lowland kingdoms, and the embassies that are exchanged between the holds and the courts of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/kingdom-of-aldreth) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) are conducted under the authority of the thane. The thane is responsible for the maintenance of the fortifications of the highland passes, and the inspections that the thane conducts of the watchtowers and the gates are a regular feature of the management of the mountain.

## The Metal Trade and the Stone

The principal concern of the thane is the management of the metal trade and the stone quarries that are the wealth of the mountain. The metals that the holds produce include the iron and the copper that are the foundation of the dwarven metalwork, and the stone that the holds produce includes the granite and the marble that are used in the construction of the lowland towns. The trade is conducted through the merchants who come to the mountain from the lowland towns, and the merchants are received at the gates of the holds by the deputies of the thane and are entertained according to the customs of the mountain.

The trade under the present thane has been marked by a substantial expansion of the markets for the metals and the stone, and the thane has directed the opening of new workings in the deeper levels of the mountain and the establishment of new quarries in the high country. The expansion has been accompanied by a careful attention to the welfare of the miners and the masons who labour in the workings, and the thane has directed the improvement of the ventilation of the deeper mines and the strengthening of the props that support the roofs of the workings. The chronicler notes that the metal trade is the foundation of the prosperity of the holds, and the management of the trade under the present thane is among the most careful that the long history of the federation records.

## The High Country and the Fortifications

The holds of Stoneheim are situated in the eastern ranges of the Morvind Uplands, and the high country that surrounds the holds is the responsibility of the thane. The highland passes are the approaches to the mountain from the lowland towns, and the watchtowers and the gates that the dwarves have built at the strategic points of the passes are the foundation of the defence of the holds. The thane is responsible for the maintenance of the fortifications, and the inspections that the thane conducts of the watchtowers and the gates are a regular feature of the management of the mountain. The thane is also responsible for the relations with the highland communities of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) and [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) that inhabit the lower slopes of the mountain, and the thane receives the deputations of these communities at the council of the holds.

The fortifications of the high country have been strengthened under the present thane, and the watchtowers that command the principal passes have been rebuilt and enlarged. The thane has also directed the construction of a new watchtower at the head of the eastern pass, a position that commands the approach to the mountain from the lowland towns of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), and the watchtower is among the most impressive of the fortifications of the mountain. The chronicler notes that the high country is well defended under the present thane, and the security of the mountain communities is a matter of which the thane may justly be proud.

## Character and Reputation

Thane Korin is described by the lords of the council as a magistrate of considerable industry and of firm but equitable administration. The thane is known for the close attention that he pays to the affairs of the holds, and the lords of the council are accustomed to see him in the company of the smiths and the masons who labour in the workings, for the thane insists upon observing the work of the mountain at first hand. The thane is also known for the courtesy with which he receives the foreign ambassadors who come to the council, and the foreign visitors speak well of the welcome that they receive in the great hall of Stoneheim.

The reputation of the thane extends throughout the mountain communities of the continent, and the magistrates of the [Deep Realm of Karhold](/content/government/national/deep-realm-of-karhold) and the other dwarven polities of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) have corresponded with Korin upon the matters that concern the mountain. The chronicler notes that the thane is held in high esteem by the lords of the council and by the communities of the holds, and the management of the mountain during his tenure of the office is among the most careful that the long history of the federation records.

## Significance

Thane Korin is the present chief magistrate of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/government/national/holds-of-stoneheim) and the senior member of the council of the mountain holds. His tenure of the thaneship has been marked by a substantial expansion of the metal trade and by a careful attention to the maintenance of the fortifications of the highland passes. The chronicler notes that the office of the thane is one of the most ancient of the public positions of the dwarven peoples, and the stewardship of the mountain during the present tenure is a continuation of the long tradition of the council that has governed the holds for many centuries.
