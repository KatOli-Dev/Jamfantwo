## Overview

Aldric, King of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) is the present ruler of the central river kingdom of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the thirty-seventh monarch of the Vaelic dynasty that was founded by [Sera, Queen of the Amber Vale](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) more than six centuries ago. He ascended to the throne of his ancestors in the year that the chroniclers of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) record as the nine hundred and eleventh year of the republic, succeeding his father [Halric](/content/people/historical/king-halric-of-vael), who died after a reign of thirty-eight years. Aldric is in the fifty-sixth year of his life, and his reign has been marked by a careful stewardship of the kingdom's affairs and by a close attention to the counsel of the lords of the valley and the guilds of the capital.

## Lineage and Early Life

Aldric was born in the royal palace that overlooks the confluence of the two rivers at [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), and he is the eldest son of Halric by his queen, a lady of the great house of the eastern marches. The lineage of the king is unbroken through the long succession of the Vaelic dynasty, and the chroniclers of the court have taken care to record the descent of the king from the founder of the line through a chain of marriages and of births that is unbroken by any irregularity of succession. The early years of Aldric were spent in the palace at Vaelmouth, and he received the customary education of the princes of the realm, including instruction in the law and the governance of the kingdom, in the history of the dynasty and the continent, and in the arts of the sword and the bow.

The prince was sent at the age of sixteen to the court of the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), where he spent two years as a guest of the king of that realm and where he formed the acquaintance of the young lords and ladies of the eastern kingdom. The sojourn was a customary stage in the education of a prince of the line, and the prince returned to Vaelmouth at the age of eighteen with a knowledge of the court and the customs of the eastern kingdom that has served him well in his subsequent reign. The prince was invested with the governance of the western marches at the age of twenty-four, and he administered the marches for a period of twelve years before the death of his father summoned him to the capital to assume the responsibilities of the crown.

## Reign

The reign of Aldric has been marked by a careful attention to the affairs of the kingdom and by a prudent management of the relations with the neighbouring realms. The king has maintained the institutions of the kingdom that were established by his predecessors, and the code of law that was instituted by [Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) remains the foundation of the legal system of the realm. The king has appointed governors to the provinces of the kingdom from among the lords of the valley and the scholars of the capital, and the system of royal governance that has been maintained throughout the long history of the dynasty has been carefully preserved.

The foreign policy of the reign has been marked by a close attention to the relations with the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and the [Plains Confederation](/content/government/national/plains-confederation), and the treaties that were negotiated by the king's predecessors have been renewed and extended during the present reign. The king has also maintained the customary relations with the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches), and the embassies that have been exchanged between Vaelmouth and the courts of these neighbours have been among the most regular of the diplomatic correspondences of the continent. The king has not engaged in any war of conquest during his reign, and the peace that has prevailed throughout his years upon the throne is in keeping with the traditions of the dynasty.

## Court and Counsel

The court of Aldric is maintained at [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth) in the palace that was built by [Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) above the confluence of the rivers. The king is attended by the great officers of the household, including the lord high steward, the lord chamberlain, and the master of the horse, and the council of the king is composed of the lords of the valley, the chief judges of the realm, and the representatives of the guilds of the capital. The council meets regularly under the presidency of the king, and the decisions that are taken at the council are binding upon the kingdom.

The king is known for the patience with which he hears the counsel of his advisors, and the chronicles of the court record that the king will listen to the most contrary of opinions before he makes his decision. The king's judgment is held in high esteem by the lords of the realm, and the appointments that he has made to the great offices of the kingdom have been marked by a careful attention to the merit of the candidates. The queen, a lady of the house of the southern reach, is a patron of the arts and the learning, and the court is known for the refinement of its entertainments and the dignity of its ceremonies.

## Governance and the Law

The administration of the kingdom is conducted through the system of royal governors and local councils that was established by [Sera](/content/people/historical/queen-sera-of-the-amber-vale) and that has been maintained by her successors. The governors are appointed by the king and are responsible for the administration of justice, the collection of the taxes, and the maintenance of the roads and the bridges of the kingdom. The governors are drawn from the lords of the valley and the scholars of the capital, and the appointments are made for a term of years, after which the governor is recalled and replaced.

The code of law of the kingdom is administered by the judges of the royal court, who are appointed by the king from among the scholars of the law that have been trained in the academy of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth). The academy of law is one of the oldest institutions of the kingdom, and the scholars who are trained there are the foundation of the legal system. The king's own court of justice is presided over by the lord chief justice, and the appeals from the decisions of the lower courts are heard by the king's bench, which meets in the hall of justice that adjoins the royal palace. The king takes a close personal interest in the administration of the law, and he is known to attend the sessions of the court upon occasions of particular importance.

## Character and Reputation

Aldric is described by the chroniclers of his court as a ruler of moderate and considered temperament, neither given to the extravagance of the more theatrical of his predecessors nor to the parsimony of the more cautious. The king is known for his industry and his attention to the affairs of the kingdom, and the reports that are submitted to him by the governors and the council are read with care and answered with deliberation. The king is also known for the regularity of his religious observances, and the chapel of the royal palace is the scene of a daily attendance that the court has noted as an example of the piety that is expected of the monarch.

The reputation of the king abroad is favourable, and the envoys of the neighbouring realms speak well of the reception that they receive at his court. The king is known for his courtesy to strangers and his attention to the customs of the foreign courts, and the embassies that have been exchanged during his reign have been conducted with a smoothness that is in keeping with the traditions of the dynasty. The king has been married for thirty-four years, and the queen has borne him three children, two sons and a daughter, all of whom are living and two of whom are married into the houses of the neighbouring kingdoms. The heir to the throne is the king's eldest son, who has been invested with the governance of the eastern marches and who is being prepared for the responsibilities that will fall to him in due course.

## Significance

Aldric is the present ruler of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the representative of the longest unbroken dynasty of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). His reign has been marked by a careful stewardship of the affairs of the kingdom, and the peace and the prosperity that have prevailed throughout his years upon the throne are a measure of the prudence with which he has conducted the government. The chronicler notes that the king is held in high esteem by his subjects and by the rulers of the neighbouring realms, and the stability of the kingdom during his reign is a foundation upon which the commerce and the culture of the central continent may continue to flourish.
