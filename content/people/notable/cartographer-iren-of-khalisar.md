## Overview

Cartographer Iren of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) holds the position of chief cartographer of the academy of Khalisar, succeeding in that office a line of scholars that reaches back to [Cartographer Lien of the House of Winds](/content/people/historical/cartographer-lien-of-the-house-of-winds). Iren was born in [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) more than forty years ago, and Iren was educated in the academy before being appointed to the office. Iren's principal work has been the revision of Lien's atlas in light of the new information that the pilots of the [Watch of the Inner Sea](/content/government/local/watch-of-the-inner-sea) have brought back from their voyages, and Iren's revisions are the foundation of the navigation of the warm coast at the present day.

Iren is held in particular esteem by the pilots and the merchants of the warm coast, and Iren's charts are consulted by every pilot who must set a course across the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The chronicler notes that Iren is one of the most useful of the scholars of the present day, and Iren's charts are the measure of the public spirit of the academies of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

## Early Life

Iren was born into a household of pilots who had served the [Watch of the Inner Sea](/content/government/local/watch-of-the-inner-sea) for several generations, and Iren's mother was a junior pilot of the Watch at the time of Iren's birth. Iren grew up among the charts and the instruments of the Watch, and Iren learned the names and the bearings of the coasts and the islands of the warm coast before Iren learned to read. Iren was sent to the academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) at the customary age, and Iren excelled in the studies of geography and of the stars. Iren graduated from the academy with the highest honours of Iren's year, and Iren was appointed to the position of junior cartographer in the office of the chief cartographer.

Iren served the office for several years, and Iren compiled the charts of the harbours of the southern coast with care. Iren's charts were more accurate than any that the office had previously produced, and Iren's charts were adopted by the [Watch of the Inner Sea](/content/government/local/watch-of-the-inner-sea) for the use of the pilots of the fleet. The chronicler notes that the adoption was the foundation of Iren's reputation, and the reputation is the measure of Iren's public spirit.

## The Atlas

The revisions that Iren has made to Lien's atlas have been the work of more than fifteen years, and the revisions have been entered in the copies that the academy keeps. The revisions include the new information that the pilots have brought back from the voyages to the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) and the new charts of the harbours of the [Strait of Velden](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden). The chronicler notes that the revisions are the most comprehensive of the revisions that the atlas has received, and the revisions are the foundation of the navigation of the warm coast at the present day.

Iren has also compiled a new atlas of the harbours of the warm coast for the use of the merchants, and the new atlas is in preparation at the academy. The chronicler notes that the new atlas is expected to be published within the next year, and the publication will be marked with the public ceremonies that the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) has established for the purpose.

## Current Duties

Iren serves as the chief cartographer of the academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and as the principal adviser to the [Watch of the Inner Sea](/content/government/local/watch-of-the-inner-sea) upon the navigation of the warm coast. Iren oversees the work of the junior cartographers of the office, and Iren reviews the charts that the pilots of the Watch bring back from their voyages. The chronicler notes that Iren's oversight has been the foundation of the accuracy of the charts of the academy, and the accuracy is the source of much of the safety of the navigation of the warm coast.

Iren also serves as the principal correspondent of the academy with the academies of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and the other principal cities, and Iren exchanges the charts and the observations of the academy with the scholars of the other academies. The chronicler notes that the correspondence has been the foundation of the careful management of the navigation of the three continents, and the management is the measure of the public spirit of the academies.

## Public Standing

Iren is held in particular esteem by the pilots and the merchants of the warm coast, and Iren is consulted by every magistrate who must judge a question of navigation. Iren is a familiar figure at the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), and Iren attends the sessions of the council when matters of navigation are under discussion. The chronicler notes that Iren's attendance is the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates and the pilots of the warm coast.

Iren also serves as the principal tutor of the junior cartographers of the academy, and Iren's students have gone on to serve the offices of the principal cities of the three continents. The chronicler notes that Iren's teaching has been the foundation of the careful training of the cartographers of the present day, and the training is the source of much of the accuracy of the charts of the academies.

## The Atlas of the Kharesh

Iren has also supervised the compilation of a new atlas of the harbours of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and the atlas is in preparation at the academy. The atlas incorporates the new information that the consul of the [Kharesh Anchorage](/content/location/settlement/outpost/kharesh-anchorage) has sent back from the trade, and the atlas is the first comprehensive chart of the harbours of the island. The chronicler notes that the atlas is expected to be published within the next year, and the publication will be marked with the public ceremonies that the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) has established for the purpose.

The atlas is being compiled by the junior cartographers of the office under Iren's supervision, and the atlas draws upon the records of the pilots of the Watch and the consul of the anchorage. The chronicler notes that the atlas is the most comprehensive of the productions of the office, and the atlas will be the foundation of the navigation of the warm coast and the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh) for the next generation.

## Significance

Cartographer Iren of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is one of the most useful of the scholars of the present day, and Iren's charts are the foundation of the navigation of the warm coast. The revisions of Lien's atlas, the new atlas of the harbours, and the legacy of Iren's service to the academy are the inheritance that Iren has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that Iren is held in particular memory by the pilots and the merchants of the warm coast, and Iren is the measure by which the magistrates and the councils of the coast judge the public acts of their own day.
