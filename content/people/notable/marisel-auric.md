## Overview

Marisel Auric is the present chief chronicler of the confederation of the island communities of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and the principal keeper of the archives and records of the isles. She was elected to the office of high chronicler by the assembly of the isles in the seven hundred and eighteenth year of the confederation, succeeding her predecessor, who had held the office for the customary term of nine years. Marisel Auric is fifty-seven years old. Her tenure has been marked by a substantial extension of the archives and the publication of a comprehensive chronicle of the isles that has become the standard reference for students of the maritime history of the known world.

## Background and Early Life

Marisel Auric was born on the largest of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles), the island that serves as the seat of the assembly and the home of the principal archives of the confederation. Her father was a fisherman of the coastal village where she was born, and her mother was the daughter of a chronicler who had served the assembly for several decades. Her early years were spent upon the water and in the household of her maternal grandfather, who introduced her to the lore of the isles and to the records that the chroniclers maintained. She showed a precocious aptitude for reading and copying the chronicles, and her grandfather was accustomed to say that she would one day serve the assembly in his stead.

Education of a chronicler of the isles is conducted through a long apprenticeship to the senior members of the college of chroniclers. Marisel Auric was bound at sixteen to the high chronicler who then held the office. The apprenticeship lasted nine years, during which she learned the traditions of the college, the methods of the compilation of the chronicles, and the conduct of relations with the foreign princes whose representatives visit the isles. She accompanied the high chronicler upon circuits of the islands and upon voyages to the principal ports of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), gaining experience that has served her well in her subsequent tenure.

## The Chroniclers' College

The college of chroniclers maintains the archives and the records of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles). The high chronicler is the chief magistrate of the college. The college is composed of chroniclers who serve the individual islands of the confederation, appointed by the assemblies of the islands for their knowledge of traditions and their skill in compiling records. The high chronicler is elected by the assembly of the confederation for a term of nine years and is responsible for the maintenance of the central archives, the publication of the chronicles of the confederation, and the conduct of relations with the chroniclers of other nations.

The archives of the college are housed in a building adjoining the hall of the assembly on the principal island and contain the records of the confederation from its foundation to the present day. The records include treaties negotiated with foreign princes, accounts of voyages conducted upon the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and chronicles compiled of the events of the confederation. The high chronicler is responsible for the maintenance of the archives and the publication of the chronicles that record the events of the confederation. The work of the high chronicler is the foundation of the historical record of the isles.

## The Chronicle of the Isles

The most substantial of the publications of the present high chronicler is the comprehensive chronicle of the isles published under her direction. The chronicle is a work of many volumes that sets forth the history of the confederation from its foundation to the present day, compiled from the records preserved in the archives and from the oral traditions maintained by the elders of the islands. It is the standard reference for students of the maritime history of the known world and has been received with acclaim by the scholars of the [Amurra Republic](/content/government/national/amurra-republic) and other academies of the continent.

The chronicle has been published in several instalments, distributed to the chroniclers of the individual islands and to scholars who visit the isles from foreign academies. The work is being continued under the direction of the high chronicler, and the remaining volumes are expected to be published over the next several years. The publication of the chronicle is one of the most significant events in the intellectual life of the isles and is a credit to the college of chroniclers and to the confederation the college serves.

## The Assembly and the Confederation

The assembly of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) is the governing body of the confederation. The high chronicler is the principal officer of the assembly in the conduct of relations with foreign princes whose representatives visit the isles. Ambassadors of foreign princes are received by the high chronicler upon their arrival at the principal island, and she is responsible for the conduct of negotiations during the visit. The high chronicler is also responsible for the maintenance of the seals of the confederation; treaties negotiated by the assembly are sealed with the seals in her custody.

The high chronicler maintains a regular correspondence with chroniclers and archivists of foreign princes, and these letters are preserved in the archives as a record of the diplomatic relations of the confederation. She is the principal channel of communication between the assembly and foreign princes in matters that touch upon records and chronicles, and her influence in the conduct of these relations is considerable.

## Character and Reputation

Marisel Auric is a scholar of considerable industry and firm but patient administration. She pays close attention to the work of the college, and the chroniclers of the staff are accustomed to find her at her desk in the archives at the earliest hours of the morning. She receives foreign scholars who visit the archives with courtesy, and foreign visitors speak well of the welcome they receive at the hall of the college.

Her reputation extends throughout the known world. Scholars of foreign academies have corresponded with Marisel Auric upon matters concerning the history and records of the isles. She is held in high esteem by the chroniclers of the college and by foreign scholars who have visited the archives, and the work she has directed during her tenure is among the most significant that the long history of the college records.

## Significance

Marisel Auric is the present chief chronicler of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) and the principal keeper of the archives of the confederation. Her tenure has been marked by a substantial extension of the archives and the publication of a comprehensive chronicle that has become the standard reference for students of the maritime history of the known world. The office of the high chronicler is one of the most ancient of the public positions of the isles, and the stewardship of the archives during the present tenure is a continuation of the long tradition of the college that has served the confederation for many centuries.
