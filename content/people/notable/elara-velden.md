## The Master Pilot of the Strait

Elara Velden is a master pilot of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden), the narrow channel that connects the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) to the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) between the southeastern coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the northeastern tip of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). She is one of the most respected pilots in the history of the strait, and her knowledge of its currents, tides, and hazards is the foundation of her reputation.

Elara was born in the city of [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden), the settlement that commands the principal crossing of the strait and that serves as the home of the pilots who navigate its waters. The family into which she was born had served as pilots of the strait for several generations, and the knowledge of the channel had been passed down through the family since the early centuries of the city's history.

The education that Elara received was the traditional training of a Velden pilot. She learned to read the currents and the tides of the strait, to navigate the channels that shifted with the seasons, and to handle the vessels that passed through the narrow waters. She began her training as a child, accompanying her father on the voyages that carried the goods of the northern world through the strait.

## The Strait and Its Challenges

The Velden Strait is one of the most challenging waterways in the known world. The channel is narrow, the currents are strong, and the tides are unpredictable. The waters of the Stormfall Sea and the Auric Ocean meet in the strait, and the meeting of the two bodies of water creates conditions that can be dangerous for the unwary.

The navigation of the strait requires knowledge of the conditions at every season of the year. The currents vary with the tides and with the winds, and the channels shift with the movement of the sediments that the currents carry. The pilot must know the strait in all its moods, and the knowledge that Elara has accumulated through decades of experience allows her to navigate the strait under any conditions.

The vessels that pass through the strait are varied in design and size. The small boats of the local fishermen, the merchant vessels of the [Auric Isles](/content/government/national/auric-isles) and the northern kingdoms, and the warships of the [Northern Fleet](/content/government/local/northern-fleet) all require the services of the pilots who know the strait. Elara is qualified to pilot vessels of all types, and she is in demand from the captains who require her services.

## The Career of the Pilot

The career of Elara Velden has spanned more than three decades, and she has piloted vessels through the strait on hundreds of voyages. She began as an apprentice on the boats that carried the goods between the settlements of the [Velden Coastlands](/content/location/settlement/region/velden-coastlands), learning the channels and the hazards of the strait. She advanced to the position of pilot on the merchant vessels that passed through the strait, and she eventually qualified to pilot the largest vessels that navigate the channel.

The reputation that Elara established was based on her skill in handling vessels in difficult conditions and her knowledge of the strait's hazards. She was known for her ability to navigate the strait in conditions that other pilots would avoid, and her record of safe voyages was among the best in the guild.

The guild of the Velden pilots is the organisation that regulates the profession, establishing the standards for the qualification of pilots and the rules that govern the navigation of the strait. Elara served as the head of the guild for many years, contributing to the development of the standards that the guild enforces.

## The Knowledge of the Strait

The knowledge of the Velden Strait that Elara possesses is the product of decades of direct observation and the oral traditions of the pilots who preceded her. The knowledge includes the locations of the channels at different seasons, the behaviour of the currents at different tides, and the hazards that are hidden beneath the surface of the water.

The channels of the strait shift with the seasons and the years, as the currents deposit and remove the sediments that shape the channel. The pilot must know the location of the channels at the time of each passage, and the knowledge must be updated constantly through direct observation. Elara has maintained a personal chart of the strait, marked with the observations she has made on each voyage, and the chart is the most detailed record of the strait that exists.

The currents of the strait are complicated by the meeting of the two bodies of water, the shape of the channel, and the influence of the tides. Elara's knowledge of the currents allows her to predict the behaviour of the vessel in different conditions and to choose the courses that make the best use of the flow of the water.

The hazards of the strait include rocks that are exposed at low tide, sandbars that shift with the currents, and the wrecks of vessels that have sunk in the channel. Elara's knowledge of the hazards is based on direct observation and the reports of other pilots, and she updates her knowledge constantly through the exchange of information within the guild.

## The Role in the Velden Compact

The governance of the Velden Strait is based on the Velden Compact, the agreement among the settlements of the coastlands that regulates the pilotage, the tolls, and the maintenance of the navigation aids. The compact is administered by a council of the representatives of the principal settlements, and the pilots of the strait are represented on the council.

Elara has served as the representative of the pilots on the council of the compact for many years, contributing to the decisions that affect the navigation of the strait. The influence that she exercises in the council is based on the respect that her knowledge of the strait commands, and her advice on matters of navigation is valued by the other members of the council.

The maintenance of the navigation aids, the lighthouses, the beacons, and the markers that guide the vessels through the strait, is one of the responsibilities of the compact. Elara has advised the council on the placement and the maintenance of the aids, ensuring that they serve the needs of the pilots and the vessels that pass through the strait.

## The Reputation of the Master Pilot

Elara Velden is one of the most respected pilots in the history of the strait, and her reputation extends beyond the community of the Velden Coastlands to the captains and the merchants who rely on the safe passage of the channel. The trust that the captains place in her is based on her record of safe voyages and her knowledge of the strait.

The chronicles of the guild of the Velden pilots will record Elara as one of the most skilled and most experienced pilots in the history of the strait. The knowledge that she has accumulated through her decades of service is a resource that the guild values, and the service she has rendered to the guild and to the trade of the strait is a contribution that will be remembered by the generations of pilots that follow.

Elara continues to serve as a pilot in the present age, despite the advanced years that the length of her career suggests. The strait that she has navigated for three decades remains her calling, and the knowledge that she has accumulated is still in demand from the captains who require her services. The waters of the Velden Strait flow as they have always flowed, and Elara Velden is still the pilot who knows them best.
