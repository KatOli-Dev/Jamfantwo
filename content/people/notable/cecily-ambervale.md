## The Administrator of the Vale

Cecily Ambervale is a noblewoman of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) kingdom and the administrator of the [Amber Vale](/content/location/settlement/region/amber-vale), the agricultural heartland of the realm that was the birthplace of the Vaelic dynasty. She holds the title of Keeper of the Amber Vale, an office that was established by [Sera Ambervale](/content/people/historical/sera-ambervale) at the founding of the kingdom, and she is responsible for the management of the valley's agricultural production, the maintenance of its infrastructure, and the administration of justice within its boundaries.

Cecily was born in the city of [Vaelmouth](/content/location/settlement/city/vaelmouth), the capital of the Vael kingdom, into a branch of the royal family that had held the office of Keeper of the Amber Vale for several generations. The family had served the crown in the administration of the valley since the time of Sera Ambervale, and the tradition of service was the foundation of Cecily's upbringing.

The education that Cecily received was the training that the nobility of the Vael kingdom provided for those who would serve in the administration of the realm. She studied the law and the governance of the kingdom, the management of the agricultural estates, and the history of the valley that she would be responsible for administering. The instruction that she received was supplemented by the direct experience of working with the farmers and the officials of the valley during the summers of her youth.

## The Office of the Keeper

The office of Keeper of the Amber Vale is one of the most important positions in the administration of the Vael kingdom. The valley is the agricultural heartland of the realm, and the grain and wool that it produces are the foundation of the kingdom's food supply and a significant component of its export trade. The Keeper is responsible for the management of the valley's resources, the maintenance of the infrastructure that supports its agriculture, and the administration of justice in the disputes that arise among the farming communities.

The responsibilities of the Keeper are defined by the laws that Sera Ambervale established at the founding of the kingdom. The Keeper is appointed by the crown and serves at the pleasure of the king, but the office has traditionally been held by a member of the family that has administered the valley since the time of Sera. The Keeper is assisted by a staff of officials who oversee the different aspects of the valley's administration, including the management of the river and the drainage channels, the regulation of the markets, and the collection of the taxes that are due to the crown.

Cecily assumed the office of Keeper after the retirement of her father, who had served in the position for more than three decades. The transition of authority was smooth, for Cecily had been trained in the duties of the office from childhood, and the officials of the valley were familiar with her and respected her competence.

## The Management of the Valley

The management of the Amber Vale requires constant attention to the conditions that affect the agricultural production of the valley. The river that flows through the valley is the source of the water that sustains the crops, and the management of the river and the drainage channels is essential for the prevention of both flooding and drought.

Cecily oversees the maintenance of the dykes and drainage channels that control the flow of water through the valley. The system of channels was established in the early centuries of the kingdom, and its maintenance is the responsibility of the office of the Keeper. The channels must be cleared of silt and vegetation at regular intervals, and the dykes must be reinforced when the river rises beyond its normal level.

The regulation of the markets is another of Cecily's responsibilities. The grain and wool that the valley produces are sold in the markets of the surrounding towns and cities, and the Keeper is responsible for ensuring that the trade is conducted fairly and that the taxes that are due to the crown are collected. The regulation of the markets also includes the inspection of the weights and measures that are used in the transactions and the resolution of disputes between buyers and sellers.

## The Administration of Justice

The administration of justice in the Amber Vale is one of the most important functions of the office of the Keeper. The disputes that arise among the farming communities, over the boundaries of fields, the rights to water, and the ownership of livestock, are brought before the Keeper for resolution.

The approach that Cecily takes to the administration of justice is based on the principles that Sera Ambervale established in the law code of the Vael kingdom. The parties to a dispute are heard in the presence of the Keeper, and the witnesses are examined before a judgment is delivered. The judgments that Cecily delivers are based on the law and on the customs that have governed the valley for centuries, and they are recorded in the registers of the office.

The reputation that Cecily has established as a judge is based on her fairness and her knowledge of the conditions of the valley. She is familiar with the farming communities and their customs, and she is able to apply the law in a manner that respects the traditions of the valley while maintaining the standards that the kingdom requires. The parties who appear before her accept her judgments as fair and well considered.

## The Relationship with the Crown

The relationship between the Keeper of the Amber Vale and the crown of the Vael kingdom is one of mutual dependence. The crown relies on the valley for the grain and wool that sustain the kingdom, and the valley relies on the crown for the protection and the legal framework that allow the trade to continue.

Cecily maintains regular contact with the court at Vaelmouth, reporting on the conditions of the valley and consulting with the king and the council on matters that affect the administration of the realm. The relationship is conducted with the formality that the traditions of the Vaelic dynasty require, but the underlying trust between the Keeper and the crown is strong.

The queen of the Vael kingdom, the consort of [Aldric Vael](/content/people/notable/aldric-vael), is a patron of the valley and takes an interest in the work that Cecily does. The relationship between the two women is one of mutual respect, and the queen's support for the valley has been a factor in the continuation of the policies that have sustained its prosperity.

## The Challenges of the Present Age

The Amber Vale faces challenges in the present age that the founders of the kingdom could not have anticipated. The competition from the grain producers of the eastern plains, who can transport their goods to the markets of the coast more cheaply than the farmers of the valley can transport theirs, has reduced the prices that the valley's grain can command. The pressure on the resources of the valley, including the water of the river and the fertility of the soil, is increasing as the population grows.

Cecily has responded to the challenges by promoting the diversification of the valley's agriculture. The farmers of the valley are being encouraged to cultivate crops that are not produced in the eastern plains, including the fruits and vegetables that thrive in the valley's climate and the specialty grains that command higher prices in the markets of the kingdom.

The response to the challenges is still unfolding, and the outcome is uncertain. Cecily is confident that the valley will adapt to the changing conditions, as it has adapted to the changes of earlier centuries, and that the agricultural heartland of the Vael kingdom will continue to sustain the realm.

## The Reputation of the Keeper

Cecily Ambervale is held in high esteem by the farmers and the officials of the Amber Vale, and by the nobility and the crown of the Vael kingdom. The fairness and the competence that she has demonstrated in the administration of the valley have earned her the respect of all who deal with her, and she is recognised as one of the most effective administrators of the present age.

The chronicles of the Vael kingdom will record Cecily as the Keeper who maintained the prosperity of the Amber Vale through a period of change, sustaining the agricultural heartland of the realm through the challenges of the present age. The work that she is doing is a continuation of the tradition that Sera Ambervale established at the founding of the kingdom, and the valley that she administers remains the foundation of the Vael kingdom's prosperity.
