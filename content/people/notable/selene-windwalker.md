## The Voice of the Sylvan Court

Selene Windwalker is an elven practitioner of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court), the council of elders that governs the ancient northern forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). She is one of the most influential voices in the court in the present age, and her knowledge of the forest and its ways has made her a trusted advisor to the council and a respected mediator between the elven communities and the human settlements of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches).

Selene was born in the deep forest that is the territory of the Sylvan Court, in a community that had maintained the traditional elven way of life for as many generations as the oral traditions record. The forest of her childhood was the ancient woodland that the elves have inhabited and shaped for centuries, and she learned the names and properties of the trees, the habits of the animals, and the patterns of the seasons through the direct experience that is the foundation of elven knowledge.

The education that Selene received was the traditional training of an elven practitioner, including the study of the history and traditions of the elven people, the cultivation of the plants and fungi that are used for food and medicine, and the techniques of communication with the creatures of the forest. She distinguished herself by her ability to understand the language of the birds and the movements of the deer, and her instructors recognised that she possessed the sensitivity to the natural world that the court valued in its members.

## The Path to the Court

The path that led Selene to the Sylvan Court was not direct, for she did not seek a position of influence but was recognised by the elders of her community as possessing the qualities that the court required. She was invited to attend the meetings of the court as an observer, and her observations and advice were noted by the elders who presided over the council.

The reputation that Selene established in the court was based on her knowledge of the boundary between the elven forest and the human settlements of the Northern Marches. She had spent much of her early adulthood in the regions where the forest gave way to the cleared lands of the human frontier, and she had established relationships with the foresters and farmers who lived and worked at the edge of the woodland. The knowledge that she had acquired of the human communities and their practices was valued by the court, which sought to maintain the peaceful coexistence that had characterised the relationship between the two peoples for centuries.

The appointment of Selene to a formal position in the court occurred in the aftermath of a dispute between a community of elves and a settlement of human loggers over the boundaries of the forest. The dispute had threatened to escalate into conflict, and the court sent Selene to mediate. She met with the leaders of both communities, listened to the concerns of each side, and proposed a resolution that addressed the needs of both the elven community and the human settlement. The resolution was accepted by both parties, and Selene's reputation as a mediator was established.

## The Work of Mediation

The work of mediation between the elven communities and the human settlements of the Northern Marches has been the central focus of Selene's service to the Sylvan Court. The relationship between the two peoples is governed by agreements that were established in the early centuries of the court's history, but the interpretation and application of the agreements in particular cases require the kind of knowledge that Selene possesses.

The disputes that Selene mediates are varied in nature. Some concern the boundaries of the forest, with human settlers seeking to extend their fields or pastures into land that the elves regard as part of the woodland. Others concern the use of the forest resources, with human loggers and hunters seeking access to timber and game that the elves wish to protect. Still others concern the movement of people through the forest, with human travellers and traders seeking passage through elven territory.

Selene's approach to mediation is based on the elven tradition of consensus, which seeks to find a resolution that all parties can accept rather than imposing a solution on the unwilling. She meets with each party separately to understand their concerns and interests, and she then brings them together to discuss the possibilities for resolution. The process is patient and time-consuming, but the resolutions that it produces are more durable than those that are imposed by authority.

## The Knowledge of the Forest

The knowledge of the forest that Selene possesses is the foundation of her authority in the Sylvan Court. The elven people have accumulated knowledge of the woodland over centuries of inhabitation, and Selene is among the most knowledgeable of the current generation of practitioners.

The knowledge includes the properties of the trees and plants that grow in the ancient forest. Selene knows which trees can be harvested for timber without damaging the health of the woodland, which plants have medicinal properties and how they should be prepared, and which fungi are edible and which are poisonous. The knowledge is passed down through the oral tradition of the elven communities, and Selene has made it her responsibility to ensure that the tradition is preserved and transmitted to the next generation.

The knowledge of the forest also includes the understanding of the animals that inhabit it. Selene can read the signs that the animals leave in the forest, the tracks in the soil, the marks on the trees, and the calls that echo through the woodland. The understanding of the animals and their behaviour allows Selene to predict the movements of the deer and the patterns of the birds, and to advise the court on the management of the forest that is the home of both the elven people and the creatures of the woodland.

## The Role in the Council

The role that Selene plays in the council of the Sylvan Court is that of advisor and mediator, a position of influence that derives from the respect that her knowledge and her judgment command among the elders. She does not hold a formal title in the court, for the elven tradition does not assign titles in the manner of the human kingdoms, but her voice carries weight in the deliberations of the council.

The council meets at intervals determined by the needs of the forest and the community, and the meetings are held in the clearing deep within the ancient woodland that has served as the meeting place of the court for as many centuries as the tradition records. Selene attends the meetings and presents the observations and recommendations that arise from her work in the forest and her mediation between the communities.

The decisions that the council makes are reached by consensus, and the process of deliberation can be lengthy. Selene's role in the process is to ensure that the voices of the different communities within the forest are heard, and that the knowledge that has been accumulated through the experience of the elven people is brought to bear on the questions that the council considers.

## The Relationship with the Northern Marches

The relationship between the Sylvan Court and the Northern Marches is one of the defining features of the politics of the northern forest. The two entities have coexisted for centuries, and the agreements that govern their relationship have been maintained through the work of mediators like Selene.

The relationship is not without tensions. The human settlements of the Northern Marches are growing, and the pressure on the forest resources is increasing. The loggers and farmers who work the edge of the woodland seek access to the timber and land that the elves regard as their own, and the elves seek to protect the forest that is the foundation of their way of life. The management of the tensions requires the constant attention of the court and the representatives of the human communities.

Selene's work in maintaining the relationship is focused on the practical questions that arise in the daily life of the forest margin. The allocation of timber rights, the regulation of hunting, the management of the land that lies between the forest and the settled areas are matters that require negotiation between the two communities. Selene's knowledge of both the elven and the human perspectives allows her to find resolutions that respect the interests of both sides.

## The Legacy in the Making

Selene Windwalker is still active in her service to the Sylvan Court, and the full measure of her legacy will not be known until her work is complete. The knowledge that she possesses and the relationships she has built are the foundation of the contribution that she is making to the life of the forest and the elven people.

The training of the next generation of elven practitioners is one of Selene's principal concerns. The knowledge that she has accumulated through her experience of the forest and her work of mediation must be transmitted to those who will follow her, and she devotes a portion of each season to the instruction of the young elves who will take her place in the court.

Selene is held in high esteem by the elders of the Sylvan Court and by the representatives of the Northern Marches with whom she works. The respect that she commands is based on the knowledge she possesses, the judgment she exercises, and the patience and persistence with which she pursues the resolutions that the communities of the forest require. She is one of the most important figures in the governance of the ancient forest in the present age, and her influence on the relationship between the elven people and the human settlements of the north will be felt for generations after her service to the court is complete.
