## Overview

Caelindis, of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) is the present speaker of the elven council of the great forest of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the chief magistrate of the woodland communities that are governed by the traditions of the [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves). She was raised to the speakership by the council in the year that the chroniclers of the central woodlands record as the two thousand three hundred and forty-second year of the elven reckoning, succeeding her predecessor, who had held the office for the customary term of twenty years. Caelindis is in the one hundred and ninety-seventh year of her life, and her tenure of the speakership has been marked by a careful stewardship of the forest and a renewal of the contacts between the woodland communities and the human kingdoms that border the wood.

## Background and Early Life

Caelindis was born in a community of the deep forest that is situated in the heart of the wood, several days' walk from any clearing that has been made by the hand of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans). Her lineage is of considerable antiquity in the wood, and the family to which she belongs has been associated with the governance of the woodland communities for many generations. The early years of Caelindis were spent in the customary education of the elven young, including instruction in the lore of the forest, the traditions of the council, and the arts that are the heritage of her people. The young Caelindis showed an early aptitude for the reading of the wood and the mediation of the disputes that arise between the communities of the forest.

The training of an elven speaker is conducted through a long apprenticeship to the senior members of the council, and the young Caelindis was bound at the age of forty to the speaker who then held the office, an elven lord of great age and wide repute. The apprenticeship lasted for a period of thirty years, during which Caelindis learned the traditions of the council, the management of the forest, and the conduct of the relations with the human kingdoms that border the wood. The apprentice accompanied the speaker upon the circuits of the council and upon the embassies to the courts of the neighbouring peoples, and the experience that she gained during these years has served her well in her subsequent tenure of the office.

## The Sylvan Court

The [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) is the council of the woodland communities of the great forest of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the speaker is the chief magistrate of the council. The court meets in a clearing at the heart of the forest, where a great beech tree of remarkable age and stature serves as the meeting place of the elders. The court is composed of the representatives of the communities of the forest, and the representatives are chosen by the communities for their wisdom and their knowledge of the wood. The decisions of the court are binding upon the communities, and the speaker is responsible for the execution of the decisions and for the representation of the forest in its dealings with the outside world.

The powers of the speaker are considerable, though they are tempered by the traditions of the council and by the customs of the woodland communities. The speaker presides over the sessions of the court, and the speaker's voice is the deciding one in the event of a tie. The speaker is also the principal officer of the court in its dealings with the human kingdoms that border the wood, and the embassies that are exchanged between the court and the courts of the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) are conducted under the authority of the speaker. The speaker is the guardian of the forest, and the regulations that govern the cutting of the timber and the hunting of the game are enforced under the speaker's authority.

## Stewardship of the Forest

The principal concern of the speaker is the stewardship of the forest, and the regulations that govern the use of the wood are among the most important of the matters that come before the council. The forest is the home of the elven communities, and the management of the timber, the game, and the streams is the foundation of the life of the woodland. The speaker is responsible for the enforcement of the regulations that limit the cutting of the timber to the trees that have been marked by the foresters, and the regulations that govern the hunting of the deer and the boar are also enforced under the speaker's authority. The speaker works closely with the foresters and the keepers of the streams, and the inspections that the speaker conducts are a regular feature of the management of the wood.

The stewardship of the forest under the present speaker has been marked by a careful attention to the renewal of the timber and the maintenance of the streams. The speaker has directed the planting of new trees in the areas that have been cut, and the regeneration of the forest has proceeded at a pace that the foresters consider satisfactory. The speaker has also directed the cleaning of the streams and the restoration of the fish passages that have been damaged by the spring floods, and the work of the restoration has been carried out by the communities of the forest under the supervision of the foresters. The chronicler notes that the forest under the present speaker is in good heart, and the management of the wood is a model of the stewardship that the elven traditions prescribe.

## Relations with the Human Kingdoms

The relations between the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the human kingdoms that border the forest are among the most important of the matters that come before the council, and the speaker is the principal officer of the court in the conduct of these relations. The forest borders the [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) and the [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), and the treaties that have been negotiated between the court and these kingdoms govern the use of the margin of the forest and the conduct of the trade between the woodland communities and the human towns. The treaties have been renewed at intervals of twenty years, and the present speaker has been responsible for the negotiation of one such renewal.

The trade between the forest and the human kingdoms is conducted under the supervision of the speaker, and the goods that the forest provides to the human markets include the timber of the great trees, the furs of the forest creatures, and the herbs that the foresters gather for the apothecaries of the human towns. The goods that the forest receives from the human markets include the metalwork of the smiths, the cloth of the weavers, and the salt and the grain that the forest communities require. The trade is conducted at specified points upon the margin of the forest, and the merchants who come to the forest for the trade are received by the speaker's deputies and are entertained according to the customs of the wood.

## Character and Reputation

Caelindis is described by the elders of the council as a speaker of considerable wisdom and of firm but patient administration. The speaker is known for the close attention that she pays to the affairs of the forest, and the elders are accustomed to see her in the company of the foresters and the keepers of the streams, for the speaker insists upon observing the work of the stewardship at first hand. The speaker is also known for the courtesy with which she receives the human ambassadors who come to the court, and the foreign visitors speak well of the welcome that they receive in the clearing at the heart of the wood.

The reputation of the speaker extends throughout the elven communities of the continent, and the speakers of the other elven courts of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) have corresponded with Caelindis upon the matters that concern the woodlands of the central continent. The chronicler notes that the speaker is held in high esteem by the elders of the council and by the communities of the forest, and the stewardship of the wood during her tenure of the office is among the most careful that the long history of the court records.

## Significance

Caelindis is the present speaker of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the chief magistrate of the woodland communities of the great forest of central [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). Her tenure of the speakership has been marked by a careful stewardship of the forest and a renewal of the relations with the human kingdoms that border the wood. The chronicler notes that the office of the speaker is one of the most ancient of the public positions of the elven peoples, and the stewardship of the forest under the present speaker is a continuation of the long tradition of the council that has governed the woodland communities for many centuries.
