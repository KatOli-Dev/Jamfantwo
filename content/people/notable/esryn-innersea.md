## The Commander of the Inner Sea Watch

Esryn Innersea holds the position of commander of the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch), succeeding a line of pilots that reaches back to the founding of the Watch in the early generations of the [Long Peace](/content/history/long-peace). Esryn was born in [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) more than fifty years ago and was trained as a pilot in the Watch before being appointed to the command. Esryn's principal work has been supervision of the patrol of the southern waters of the strait and prosecution of the wreckers who have preyed upon the commerce of the coast. Esryn's vigilance is the foundation of the safety of navigation along the warm coast at the present day.

Esryn is held in particular esteem by the pilots and merchants of the warm coast, and Esryn's judgment is consulted by every magistrate who must decide a question of navigation. Esryn is one of the most useful of the pilots of the present day, and Esryn's service is a measure of the public spirit of the Watch.

## Raised among the Pilots of Khalisar

Esryn was born into a household of pilots who had served the Watch for several generations. Esryn's father was a senior pilot of the Watch at the time of Esryn's birth. Esryn grew up among the barges and lighthouses of the Watch and learned the names and bearings of the coasts and islands of the warm coast before learning to read. Esryn was sent to the academy of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) at the customary age and excelled in studies of navigation and the stars. Esryn graduated with the highest honours of the year and was appointed to the position of junior pilot in the Watch.

Esryn served the Watch for several years, rising through the ranks to senior pilot and then to lieutenant of the inner division. As a lieutenant, Esryn was responsible for the patrol of the waters between the mainland and the outer islands, a stretch of coast known for its concealed rocks and shifting currents. The careful navigation of this division under Esryn's supervision reduced the number of vessels lost to grounding in that sector by a substantial margin, and the record of this achievement was noted by the council of Khalisar when the question of the command arose.

## The Command

Esryn was appointed to the command of the Watch by the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in the third year of service as a lieutenant and has served in that capacity for the past five years. Esryn's principal work has been the supervision of patrol of the southern waters of the strait and personally leading several prosecutions of wreckers who have preyed upon the commerce of the coast. These prosecutions have been central to the management of wreckers and the safety of navigation along the coast. The wreckers operate by showing false lights that lure vessels onto the rocks, where they can be plundered. The patrols under Esryn's command have captured several such groups and have driven others from the waters entirely.

Esryn has also overseen the maintenance of the lighthouses and beacons that the Watch maintains along the coast. The accuracy of the lights that pilots rely upon depends on this oversight. The maintenance has been essential to the safety of navigation of the strait. Esryn introduced a regular schedule of inspection under which each lighthouse is visited twice monthly, with the condition of the lantern, the reflectors, and the fuel supply recorded in a log that is reviewed at the commander's office.

## Oversight of the Watch and Correspondences

Esryn serves as the commander of the Watch and as the principal adviser to the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) upon the navigation of the warm coast. Esryn oversees the work of the lieutenants and pilots of the Watch and reviews the reports that pilots bring back from their cruises. Esryn's oversight has been fundamental to the accuracy of the Watch's reports and the safety of navigation along the warm coast.

Esryn also serves as the principal correspondent of the Watch with the pilots of other ports of the warm coast, exchanging reports with the pilots of [Sahran](/content/location/settlement/city/sahran), [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden), and other principal towns. This correspondence has contributed to the careful management of navigation along the coast.

## Esteem among Pilots and Magistrates

Esryn is held in particular esteem by the pilots and merchants of the warm coast and is consulted by every magistrate who must decide a question of navigation. Esryn is a familiar figure at the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) and attends sessions when matters of navigation are under discussion.

Esryn also serves as the principal tutor of the junior pilots of the Watch. Esryn's students have gone on to serve in offices of the principal cities of the warm coast. Esryn's teaching has been fundamental to the training of the pilots of the present day and is the source of much of the accuracy of navigation along the coast.

## Founding the First Formal Training Institution

Esryn has established a small pilot school at the principal watchpost of the Watch. The school will offer instruction to the sons and daughters of the pilots of the fleet in the principles of navigation and the management of vessels. It will be the first formal institution of the Watch for the training of pilots. The school is expected to open within the next year, with public ceremonies according to the customs established by the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar).

The school was authorised by the council of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) in recognition of Esryn's service to the Watch, and the council has provided funds for the construction of the schoolroom and dormitory. The school is the most significant of the recent works of the Watch and will be the foundation of training for the pilots of the next generation.

## Esryn's Legacy to Navigation

Esryn Innersea of the [Inner Sea Watch](/content/government/local/inner-sea-watch) is one of the most useful of the pilots of the present day. Esryn's service is the foundation of the safety of navigation along the warm coast. The patrol, the prosecutions, and the maintenance of the lighthouses are the inheritance Esryn has bequeathed to the present age. Esryn is held in particular memory by the pilots and merchants of the warm coast and is the measure by which the magistrates and councils judge the public acts of their own day.
