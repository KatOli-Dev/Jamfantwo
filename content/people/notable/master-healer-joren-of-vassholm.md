## Overview

Master Healer Joren of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) holds the position of chief healer of the public hospital of Vassholm, succeeding in that office a line of healers that reaches back to [Lady Healer Mira of Nessara](/content/people/historical/lady-healer-mira-of-nessara). Joren was born in [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) more than fifty years ago, and Joren was trained in the practice of the [Study of Plague and Remedy](/content/belief/science/physical/the-study-of-plague-and-remedy) before being appointed to the position. Joren's principal work has been the supervision of the public hospital and the compilation of the new catalogue of the remedies that the hospital uses, and Joren's catalogue is the foundation of the modern practice of healing in the cold north at the present day.

Joren is held in particular esteem by the healers and the magistrates of the cold north, and Joren's judgement is consulted by every healer who must decide a question of public health. The chronicler notes that Joren is one of the most useful of the healers of the present day, and Joren's catalogue is the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm).

## Early Life

Joren was born into a household of healers who had served the public hospital of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) for several generations, and Joren's mother was a senior healer of the hospital at the time of Joren's birth. Joren grew up among the wards and the dispensaries of the hospital, and Joren learned the names and the virtues of the plants of the [Counting of Plants and their Virtues](/content/belief/science/physical/the-counting-of-plants-and-their-virtues) before Joren learned to read. Joren was sent to the academy of Vassholm at the customary age, and Joren excelled in the studies of the remedies and of the management of disease. Joren graduated from the academy with the highest honours of Joren's year, and Joren was appointed to the position of junior healer in the public hospital.

Joren served the public hospital for several years, and Joren compiled the records of the cases that the hospital had attended with care. Joren's records were more complete than any that the hospital had previously produced, and Joren's records were adopted by the magistrates for the use of the healers of the region. The chronicler notes that the adoption was the foundation of Joren's reputation, and the reputation is the measure of Joren's public spirit.

## The Catalogue

The catalogue of the remedies that Joren has compiled has been the work of more than fifteen years, and the catalogue has been published by the academy of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) for the use of the healers of the cold north. The catalogue includes the remedies that have been tested since the time of [Lady Healer Mira](/content/people/historical/lady-healer-mira-of-nessara) and the new remedies that Joren has tested in the course of Joren's service to the hospital. The chronicler notes that the catalogue is the most comprehensive of the catalogues of the cold north, and the catalogue is the foundation of the modern practice of healing in the region.

Joren has also revised the rules of the practice of the hospital, and the revisions have been entered in the register that the magistrates maintain. The chronicler notes that the revisions have been the foundation of the careful management of the hospital, and the management is the source of much of the health of the people of the cold north.

## Current Duties

Joren serves as the chief healer of the public hospital of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and as the principal adviser to the magistrates of the cold north upon the management of disease. Joren oversees the work of the junior healers of the hospital, and Joren reviews the cases that the healers bring to Joren's attention. The chronicler notes that Joren's oversight has been the foundation of the accuracy of the practice of the hospital, and the accuracy is the source of much of the health of the people of the cold north.

Joren also serves as the principal correspondent of the hospital with the hospitals of the other principal cities, and Joren exchanges the records and the observations of the hospital with the healers of the other hospitals. The chronicler notes that the correspondence has been the foundation of the careful management of the public health of the three continents, and the management is the measure of the public spirit of the healers and the magistrates.

## Public Standing

Joren is held in particular esteem by the healers and the magistrates of the cold north, and Joren is consulted by every magistrate who must decide a question of public health. Joren is a familiar figure at the council of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm), and Joren attends the sessions of the council when matters of public health are under discussion. The chronicler notes that Joren's attendance is the measure of the public spirit of the magistrates and the healers of the cold north.

Joren also serves as the principal tutor of the junior healers of the hospital, and Joren's students have gone on to serve the hospitals of the principal cities of the three continents. The chronicler notes that Joren's teaching has been the foundation of the careful training of the healers of the present day, and the training is the source of much of the accuracy of the practice of the hospitals.

## The New Ward

Joren has also overseen the construction of a new ward of the public hospital, and the new ward has been opened to receive patients in the past year. The new ward contains additional beds for the treatment of the chronic cases that the hospital has admitted in increasing numbers since the recovery from the [Plague of Stone](/content/history/plague-of-stone-and-the-years-of-recovery), and the new ward is the most modern of the wards of the cold north. The chronicler notes that the new ward has been the foundation of the careful management of the chronic cases, and the management is the source of much of the recovery of the patients.

The new ward was financed by the council of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) and by the contributions of the principal merchants of the city, and the contributions were solicited by Joren in person. The chronicler notes that the contributions have been the foundation of the careful management of the hospital, and the management is the source of much of the health of the people of the cold north.

## Significance

Master Healer Joren of [Vassholm](/content/location/settlement/city/vassholm) is one of the most useful of the healers of the present day, and Joren's catalogue is the foundation of the modern practice of healing in the cold north. The catalogue, the revised rules of the hospital, and the legacy of Joren's service to the magistrates are the inheritance that Joren has bequeathed to the present age. The chronicler notes that Joren is held in particular memory by the healers and the magistrates of the cold north, and Joren is the measure by which the magistrates and the councils of the region judge the public acts of their own day.
