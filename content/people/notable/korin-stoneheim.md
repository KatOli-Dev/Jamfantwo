## The Thane of the Stoneheim Holds

Korin Stoneheim is the present chief magistrate of the dwarven mountain communities of the eastern ranges of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands) and the senior member of the council of the mountain holds. He was elected to the thaneship by the council of the holds in the nine hundred and forty-seventh year of the Stoneheim reckoning, succeeding his predecessor, who had held the office for the customary term of twelve years. Korin is in the one hundred and twelfth year of his life. His tenure has been marked by a substantial expansion of the trade in the metals and stone of the mountain and by careful attention to the maintenance of the fortifications of the highland passes.

## Eldest Son of the Previous Thane

Korin was born in the deep hall of Stoneheim, the principal hold of the mountain community, the eldest son of the previous thane of Stoneheim, who held the office for sixteen years before his retirement. Korin's lineage is among the most ancient of the mountain holds, and his family has been associated with governance of the holds for many generations. He received the customary education of dwarven young of the principal families: instruction in the lore of the mountains, the traditions of the council, and the crafts of the smith and the mason that are the heritage of his people. He showed an early aptitude for the management of the metal trade and for the conduct of embassies that the holds exchange with the lowland towns.

Training of a thane of the holds is conducted through a long apprenticeship to senior members of the council. Korin was bound at the age of forty-five to the thane who then held the office, a dwarven lord of great age and wide repute. The apprenticeship lasted twenty-five years, during which Korin learned the traditions of the council, the management of the metal trade, and the conduct of relations with the lowland towns that purchase the products of the mountain. He accompanied the thane upon circuits of the holds and upon embassies to the courts of lowland princes, experience that has served him well in his subsequent tenure.

## The Federation and the Council

The [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) are the principal dwarven communities of the eastern ranges of the [Morvind Uplands](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/morvind-uplands), a federation of mountain holds linked by tunnels and paths that wind through the high country. The holds are governed by a council of senior members of the principal families, and the thane is the chief magistrate of the council and the principal officer of the federation. The council meets in the great hall of Stoneheim, where decisions of the federation are taken and embassies from lowland princes are received. The council is composed of the lords of the principal holds and representatives of the craftsmen who supply the metals and stone that are the wealth of the mountain.

The powers of the thane are considerable, though tempered by the traditions of the council and the customs of the mountain holds. The thane presides over sessions of the council, and the thane's voice is the deciding one in the event of a tie. The thane is the principal officer of the federation in its dealings with the lowland kingdoms. Embassies exchanged between the holds and the courts of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) and the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches) are conducted under the thane's authority. The thane is responsible for the maintenance of the fortifications of the highland passes, and inspections of the watchtowers and gates are a regular feature of mountain management.

## Iron, Copper, Granite, and Marble

The principal concern of the thane is the management of the metal trade and the stone quarries that are the wealth of the mountain. The metals the holds produce include iron and copper, the foundation of dwarven metalwork. The stone includes granite and marble used in the construction of lowland towns. Trade is conducted through merchants who come to the mountain from the lowland towns, received at the gates of the holds by the thane's deputies and entertained according to mountain customs.

Under the present thane, trade has been marked by a substantial expansion of the markets for metals and stone. Korin has directed the opening of new workings in the deeper levels of the mountain and the establishment of new quarries in the high country. The expansion has been accompanied by careful attention to the welfare of miners and masons labouring in the workings. He has directed the improvement of the ventilation of the deeper mines and the strengthening of the props that support the roofs of the workings. The metal trade is the foundation of the prosperity of the holds, and its management under the present thane is among the most careful that the long history of the federation records.

## Watchtowers and Defence of the Passes

The holds of Stoneheim are situated in the eastern ranges of the Morvind Uplands, and the high country that surrounds them is the thane's responsibility. The highland passes are the approaches to the mountain from the lowland towns. The watchtowers and gates the dwarves have built at strategic points of the passes are the foundation of the defence of the holds. The thane is responsible for the maintenance of these fortifications and conducts regular inspections. The thane is also responsible for relations with the highland communities of [humans](/content/species/sapient/humans) and [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) that inhabit the lower slopes, receiving their deputations at the council.

The fortifications of the high country have been strengthened under the present thane. The watchtowers commanding the principal passes have been rebuilt and enlarged. Korin has directed the construction of a new watchtower at the head of the eastern pass, commanding the approach from the lowland towns of the [Northern Marches](/content/government/national/northern-marches). This watchtower is among the most impressive of the fortifications of the mountain. The high country is well defended under the present thane, and the security of the mountain communities is a matter of which the thane may justly be proud.

## Industry and Equitable Administration

Korin is a magistrate of considerable industry and firm but equitable administration. He pays close attention to the affairs of the holds and is accustomed to be seen in the company of the smiths and masons labouring in the workings, insisting upon observing the work of the mountain at first hand. He receives foreign ambassadors who come to the council with courtesy, and foreign visitors speak well of the welcome they receive in the great hall of Stoneheim.

Korin's reputation extends throughout the mountain communities of the continent. Magistrates of other dwarven polities of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) have corresponded with him upon matters concerning the mountain. He is held in high esteem by the lords of the council and by the communities of the holds, and the management of the mountain during his tenure is among the most careful that the long history of the federation records.

## Ties with the Karhold Realm

In the administration of the holds, Korin maintains close relations with the dwarven communities of the [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) mountain spine through the [Karhold Realm](/content/government/national/karhold-realm), whose envoys are received at the great hall of Stoneheim when questions of common interest arise. The smiths of the two great dwarven polities exchange metalwork and techniques across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), and the treaties that govern the trade are among the most enduring of the diplomatic instruments of the continent.

## Korin's Stewardship of the Mountain

Korin is the present chief magistrate of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) and the senior member of the council of the mountain holds. His tenure of the thaneship has been marked by a substantial expansion of the metal trade and by careful attention to the maintenance of the fortifications of the highland passes. The office of the thane is one of the most ancient of the public positions of the dwarven peoples, and the stewardship of the mountain during the present tenure is a continuation of the long tradition of the council that has governed the holds for many centuries.
