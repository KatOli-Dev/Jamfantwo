## Overview

Southport is the principal Aethelaran-side town of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), situated on the southern shore of the strait where it connects the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) to the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea). The town is the counterpart of [Veldenwatch](/content/veldenwatch) on the Morvind shore, and the two towns together form the core of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), the joint governance that regulates passage through the strait. Southport is the primary point of Aethelaran-side pilotage and toll collection, and its harbour is the first or the last port of call for vessels navigating the strait from the southern shore.

## The Strait and the Pilotage

The strait that Southport commands is the only passage between the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and the waters that flow through it are among the most treacherous in the known world. The currents are strong and unpredictable, the channel is narrow, and the tidal range is considerable, and the pilots of Southport are specialists who are trained from youth in the particular conditions of the strait. The pilotage service is the primary function of the town, and the pilots of Southport are organised into a guild that regulates training, sets fees, and maintains the standards of the profession.

The harbour of Southport is a broad, sheltered cove that is cut into the low cliffs of the Aethelaran shore, and the approach is marked by lighthouses that are among the oldest on the strait. The quays line the inner shore of the cove, and the warehouses, the pilotage offices, and the toll houses that serve the strait trade are situated along the waterfront. The harbour is busy with vessels that are waiting for a pilot, for the tide to turn, or for the weather to clear, and the town is a gathering point for the mariners and the merchants who travel the strait.

The Aethelaran shore of the strait is less dramatic than the Morvind side, where the mountains descend almost to the water's edge. The coast here is a line of low cliffs and sheltered coves that provide anchorage for vessels waiting for the tide to turn, and the terrain is more gentle than the steep and rocky northern shore. The town is built upon a raised shelf of rock that overlooks the cove, and the streets slope down toward the harbour in a series of terraces that give the buildings a view of the strait and the shipping that passes through it.

## Trade and the Compact

The trade of Southport is dominated by the tolls and the pilotage fees that are collected from the vessels that pass through the strait, and the town is a principal beneficiary of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), the joint governance of the strait towns that regulates passage and divides the revenue between the two shores. The compact is the foundation of the town's prosperity, and the governance of the strait is the primary occupation of the officials and the merchants of Southport. The council of the compact meets alternately in Southport and [Veldenwatch](/content/veldenwatch), and the two towns share the administration of the pilotage guilds, the toll collection, and the maintenance of the navigation aids that line the strait.

The town is also a centre for the repair and the supply of vessels that have navigated the strait, and the shipyards and the chandleries that line the harbour are a significant part of the local economy. The vessels that pass through the strait are often in need of repair, and the shipwrights of Southport are skilled in the rapid and the reliable maintenance that the strait trade demands. The trade goods that flow through Southport include the grain and cloth of the Aethelaran interior, the metalwork of [Morvind](/content/morvind), and the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), and the warehouses of the town are filled with the commerce of three continents.

## The Town

Southport is a compact, well-built town, and its architecture reflects the wealth that the strait trade has brought, with substantial buildings of pale stone, well-paved streets, and a commercial district that is oriented toward the harbour. The buildings rise three and four storeys high, their upper floors projecting over the narrow streets to maximise the use of space within the confined walls. The architecture is a blend of the traditions of both continents, with the lighter arcades and courtyards of the Aethelaran coast softened by the heavier stone walls that are characteristic of the strait towns, and the buildings are constructed to withstand the winds and the salt spray that the strait weather brings.

The population of Southport is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people identify more closely with the strait and its trade than with the interior of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), and their loyalties are shaped by the sea and the commerce it brings. A small community of [gnomes](/content/gnomes) has established itself in the town, and their workshops produce fine instruments and mechanisms that are valued by the mariners who pass through, including navigational tools, clockworks, and the intricate mechanisms that operate the lighthouses and the signal towers of the strait.

## Significance

Southport is the primary Aethelaran-side settlement of the [Strait of Velden](/content/strait-of-velden), and its role in the pilotage and the toll collection of the strait trade is a significant component of the economy of the strait region. The town is a member of the [Velden Compact](/content/velden-compact), and its position on the southern shore of the strait gives it a strategic and a commercial importance that is disproportionate to its modest size. The prosperity of Southport is built upon the trade that flows through the strait, and the town is a key point in the trade network that connects Aethelar to the wider world.
