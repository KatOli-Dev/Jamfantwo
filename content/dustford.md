## Overview

Dustford is a southern river town of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the edge of the grasslands where the cultivated land gives way to the warm, dry country of the far south. The town stands at a crossing of the river that marks the boundary between the fertile plains and the arid margins, and its position at the edge of the grasslands has made it a frontier settlement and a gateway to the warm plains beyond. Dustford is smaller and less prosperous than the cities of the central grasslands, but it is a thriving and self-sufficient community, and its trade connects the grasslands to the regions that lie beyond.

## The River and the Frontier

The river that flows past Dustford is the same that waters the southern plains, but at this point the current is slower and the banks are lower, and the water is warm and turbid with the silt of the dry country upstream. The crossing at Dustford is the last reliable ford on the river before it enters the arid margins, and the bridge that spans the water is a modest structure that is maintained by the town for the convenience of the traffic that crosses between the grasslands and the dry country.

The frontier that Dustford marks is not a political boundary but a geographical one, for the cultivated land of the southern plains ends and the warm, dry country begins at the river crossing. The traffic that passes through the town includes the grain and the livestock of the grasslands, which are exported to the dry country, and the exotic products of the warm regions, which are imported to the grasslands and the markets of the north.

## Trade and the Town

The trade of Dustford is a mixture of the agricultural products of the grasslands and the goods that are imported from the warm regions beyond the frontier. The town is a centre for the livestock trade, and the stockyards that line the riverbank are the gathering point for the cattle and the sheep that are driven from the grasslands to the markets of the dry country. The wool and the leather that are produced in the surrounding region are also traded at Dustford, and the town is known for the quality of its leather, which is tanned using the bark of the trees that grow along the river.

Dustford is a small, rugged town, and its buildings are constructed of the light-coloured stone and the sun-baked clay that are the materials of the dry country, with thick walls and small windows that provide insulation against the heat. The streets are broad and dusty, for the town is at the edge of the grasslands and the dust of the dry country is a constant presence. The population of Dustford is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a small community of [centaurs](/content/centaurs) who are involved in the livestock trade.

## Significance

Dustford is the primary gateway between the southern grasslands and the warm, dry country beyond, and its trade connects the agricultural economy of the plains to the regions that lie to the south. The town is the southernmost settlement of the [Plains Confederation](/content/plains-confederation), and its position at the edge of the grasslands gives it a commercial significance that belies its modest size. The prosperity of Dustford is built upon the trade that passes through the town, and the people of Dustford are proud of their role as the frontier community of the southern plains.
