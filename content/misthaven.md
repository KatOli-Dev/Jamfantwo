## Overview

Misthaven is a western port of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), known throughout the known world for the fog that shrouds its harbour for much of the year. The town stands on a low, rocky shore of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean), where a natural inlet provides a sheltered anchorage that is seldom seen clearly, for the cold water of the ocean meets the warmer air of the land in a perpetual bank of mist that gives the town its name. Misthaven is smaller than [Greycliff](/content/greycliff), but it is an important port of the western coast, and its harbour is a vital refuge for vessels caught in the fog and the swell of the open ocean.

## The Harbour and the Fog

The harbour of Misthaven is a broad, shallow inlet that penetrates the coast for some distance, and it is sheltered by low headlands on either side that are visible only when the fog lifts. The approach to the harbour is marked by a series of bells and horns that are placed along the shore, for the fog is often so thick that no landmark can be seen, and the mariners of Misthaven navigate by sound, following the bells to the entrance of the inlet. The quays are built along the inner shore of the inlet, and the waterfront is occupied by the warehouses and the offices of the merchants who manage the trade of the port.

The fog that gives Misthaven its name is a feature of the local climate, and it is produced by the meeting of the cold [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean) current with the warmer air of the land. The fog is most persistent in the autumn and the winter, when the contrast between the water and the air is greatest, and it may blanket the harbour for days at a time, reducing the port to a world of grey shadow and muffled sound. The people of Misthaven are accustomed to the fog, and they navigate their town and their harbour with a confidence that seems uncanny to visitors, for they know the position of every building, every quay, and every channel by heart.

## Trade and Industry

The trade of Misthaven is modest compared to that of the eastern ports, for the western coast is less densely settled and the [Greyring Ocean](/content/greyring-ocean) offers fewer trade routes. The primary commerce of the town is the export of fish and whale products to the inland markets, and the import of grain and manufactured goods from the interior. The fishing grounds off Misthaven are productive, and the fog that shrouds the harbour also shrouds the fishing boats, allowing them to approach the shoals undetected and to take a larger catch than the clearer waters of the open coast would allow.

Misthaven is also a centre for the processing of the kelp and the seaweed that are gathered from the rocky shores of the inlet, and the kelp is dried and burned to produce a substance that is used in the manufacture of soap and glass, a trade that is small but valuable. The people of Misthaven are known for their skill in the working of driftwood, which is collected from the beaches in great quantities after the storms, and the furniture and the carved objects of Misthaven are prized for their weathered beauty and their salt-cured durability.

## The Town

Misthaven is a small, close-built town, and its buildings are constructed of the dark grey stone of the coast, with low profiles and small windows that minimise the exposure to the wind and the fog. The streets are narrow and winding, and they are paved with cobblestones that are often slick with moisture, and the town is oriented toward the harbour, with all the main streets leading down to the waterfront. The population is chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people are known for their reserve and their quiet manner, qualities that visitors from the east often mistake for coldness.

## Significance

Misthaven is a valued member of the [Grey Coast Holdings](/content/grey-coast-holdings), and its harbour is the primary refuge for vessels navigating the fog-bound western coast. The town is smaller and less wealthy than [Greycliff](/content/greycliff), but its position on a stretch of coast where safe harbours are few gives it an importance that belies its size. The fog that shrouds its harbour is both a hindrance and a protection, for it has on many occasions concealed the town from the fleets that have approached the western coast with hostile intent, and the people of Misthaven regard the fog as a gift rather than a curse.
