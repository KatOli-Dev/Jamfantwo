## Overview

Vaelmouth stands at the mouth of the River Vael, the greatest waterway of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), where the river empties into the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) on the eastern coast. The city is the principal port of the [Kingdom of Vael](/content/kingdom-of-vael), and its harbour is the gateway through which the goods of the Vael valley reach the wider world. The River Vael is navigable for much of its length, and the trade that flows downriver from the interior passes through Vaelmouth on its way to the markets of the coast and the oceans beyond, making the city a natural point of exchange between the river and the sea.

## The River and the Harbour

The harbour of Vaelmouth is formed by the broad mouth of the River Vael, where the current slows and the water spreads wide before meeting the sea. The harbour is sheltered by a pair of headlands that enclose the bay, and the depth of the water is sufficient for the largest vessels, though the sandbanks that shift with the current require the services of experienced pilots. The quays line both banks of the river, and the waterfront is a continuous stretch of warehouses, granaries, and the offices of the merchants who manage the trade between the river and the sea.

The River Vael is the lifeblood of Vaelmouth, and the city's prosperity is built upon the goods that travel its waters. Grain, timber, and the produce of the Vael valley flow downriver to the harbour, where they are loaded onto seagoing vessels for transport to the markets of the eastern coast and the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean). The return cargo includes the goods of [Sahrana](/content/sahrana) and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea), which are carried upriver to the interior, and the exchange of upriver and downriver goods is the foundation of the city's commerce.

## The City

Vaelmouth is a well-built and prosperous city, smaller than [Eldreth](/content/eldreth) but no less proud of its heritage. The city is laid out along both banks of the river, connected by a series of bridges that are among the finest in Aethelar, and the oldest portions of the city stand on the higher ground above the floodplain, where the first settlements were established. The streets of the older quarters are narrow and winding, while the newer districts are broader and more regular, reflecting the planned expansion of the city during its period of greatest growth.

The population of Vaelmouth is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with smaller numbers of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) and [gnomes](/content/gnomes) who are drawn by the trade and the opportunities of the port. The city is the seat of the court of the Kingdom of Vael, and the administrative buildings, the barracks, and the residence of the king are situated in the oldest quarter, overlooking the harbour and the river.

## Significance

Vaelmouth is the commercial and political centre of the Kingdom of Vael, and its harbour is the primary outlet for the produce of the Vael valley, the most fertile region of Aethelar. The trade that flows through the city connects the interior of the continent to the markets of the coast and the oceans, and the wealth that accumulates in Vaelmouth funds the maintenance of the river, the roads, and the defences of the kingdom. The city is a rival of [Eldreth](/content/eldreth) in the trade of the eastern coast, and the competition between the two ports is a driving force in the economy of the continent.
