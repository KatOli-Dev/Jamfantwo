## Overview

Khazgar is a [dwarven](/content/dwarves) underground city in the interior ranges of [Morvind](/content/morvind), renowned throughout the known world for the quality of its metalwork and the skill of its smiths. The city is carved into a rich vein of ore that runs through the mountains, and its halls and forges are situated close to the deposits that sustain them, making Khazgar one of the most efficient and productive mining settlements in the dwarven world. The city is smaller than [Stoneheim](/content/stoneheim), but its reputation for craftsmanship rivals that of the greater city, and the metalwork that is produced in its forges is among the finest in Morvind.

## The Halls and the Forges

The halls of Khazgar are arranged around the great vein of ore that is the reason for the city's existence, and the forges are situated close to the mine workings, for the ore that is extracted from the depths is smelted and worked in the same complex of chambers and passages. This arrangement is unusual among dwarven cities, where the mines and the forges are typically separated by some distance, and the efficiency of the Khazgar system is one of the reasons for the city's productivity. The great hall of Khazgar is smaller than that of Stoneheim, but it is the centre of the city's metallurgical industry, and the forges that line its walls are among the hottest and the most active in the dwarven world.

The metalwork that is produced in Khazgar includes weapons, tools, armour, and the decorative objects for which the city is renowned, and the smiths of Khazgar are noted for their skill in the working of steel and the precious metals. The steel that is produced in the forges of Khazgar is regarded as the finest in Morvind, and the weapons that are made from it are prized by the warriors and the soldiers of every continent. The city's decorative metalwork, including the jewellery and the ceremonial objects that are produced in the upper forges, is noted for its beauty and its precision, and the smiths of Khazgar are among the most sought-after craftsmen in the known world.

## Mining and Trade

The mining operations of Khazgar are focused on the great vein that runs through the mountains, and the ore that is extracted includes iron, copper, and the precious metals that are the raw material of the city's metalwork. The mines are worked continuously, for the demand for Khazgar's products is great and the vein is rich, and the miners of the city are among the most skilled and the most productive in Morvind. The ore that is extracted is smelted in the forges and worked into the finished products that are the city's primary export, and the metalwork of Khazgar is traded to every continent through the overland routes that connect the interior to the coast.

The trade that connects Khazgar to the coast is managed through [Stoneheim](/content/stoneheim) and the network of routes that link the dwarven cities of the interior to the ports of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/stormfall-sea). The metalwork of Khazgar is transported overland to the coast, where it is loaded onto vessels for shipment to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and beyond, and the return cargo includes the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the city cannot produce for itself.

## The City

The population of Khazgar is entirely [dwarven](/content/dwarves), and the community is devoted to the metallurgical industry that is the reason for the city's existence. The dwellings of Khazgar are carved into the rock above the forges, and the living quarters are warmer than those of most dwarven cities, for the heat of the forges permeates the surrounding stone. The city is a dependency of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim), and its governance is integrated with that of the larger realm, though the day-to-day management of the city is in the hands of the local council of smiths and mine overseers.

## Significance

Khazgar is the premier centre of metalwork in Morvind, and its products are among the most valued in the known world. The city's reputation for craftsmanship is the foundation of its prosperity, and the metalwork that is produced in its forges is a significant contribution to the economy of the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/holds-of-stoneheim) and the continent as a whole. The significance of Khazgar lies in the quality of its output and the skill of its people, and the city is a symbol of the dwarven mastery of metal that is the defining achievement of the dwarven civilisation.
