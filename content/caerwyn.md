## Overview

Caerwyn is an eastern port city of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), renowned throughout the known world for its shipyards and the vessels they produce. The city stands on a rocky promontory that juts into the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean), and its harbour is sheltered by a natural breakwater of rock that forms a deep, calm basin within sight of the open sea. The combination of a fine harbour, a ready supply of timber from the inland forests, and a long tradition of shipbuilding has made Caerwyn the foremost centre of maritime construction on the eastern coast, and its ships are the backbone of the merchant fleets and the naval forces of Aethelar.

## The Shipyards

The shipyards of Caerwyn are the largest and most extensive on the eastern coast, and they line the inner shore of the harbour in a continuous stretch of slipways, dry docks, and workshops. The construction of vessels is the dominant activity of the city, and the sound of sawing, hammering, and the caulking of seams fills the air from dawn to dusk. The ships built in Caerwyn are noted for their strength, their speed, and their seaworthiness, and the techniques of their construction have been refined over centuries of experience with the conditions of the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean).

The timber for the shipyards is brought downriver from the inland forests, where the great trees of the northern woodland are felled and floated to Caerwyn in rafts that are a common sight on the river. The selection and seasoning of timber is a skilled practice, and the shipwrights of Caerwyn are knowledgeable in the properties of the different woods, matching the timber to the function of each part of the vessel. The shipyards also produce the masts, spars, oars, and the smaller craft that are essential to the maritime trade of the coast, and the output of the yards is a significant export in its own right.

## The Harbour and Trade

The harbour of Caerwyn is a deep, sheltered basin that accommodates both the ships under construction and the vessels that call at the port for trade. The trade of Caerwyn is secondary to its shipbuilding, but it is nonetheless substantial, for the city is a natural stopping point for vessels travelling along the eastern coast, and the goods that pass through its harbour include the produce of the interior, the materials for the shipyards, and the provisions for the vessels that are fitted out and supplied at the port.

The population of Caerwyn is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [dwarves](/content/dwarves) who are drawn by the demand for metalwork, anchors, and the iron fittings that the ships require. The [gnomes](/content/gnomes) of Caerwyn are noted for their skill in the making of navigational instruments and the fine mechanisms that are used aboard the larger vessels, and their workshops are a valued part of the shipbuilding complex.

## Significance

Caerwyn is the primary centre of shipbuilding on the eastern coast of Aethelar, and its ships are the foundation of the maritime power of the continent. The vessels built in its shipyards carry the trade of Aethelar across the [Auric Ocean](/content/auric-ocean) to [Sahrana](/content/sahrana), patrol the coast against raiders, and explore the waters beyond the known trade routes. The reputation of Caerwyn's ships extends to every port in the known world, and the city is a valued ally of the trading cities and a respected rival of the other shipbuilding centres of the coast.
