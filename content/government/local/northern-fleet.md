## Overview

The Northern Fleet is the standing fleet of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven), the principal naval force of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the guardian of the maritime trade that connects the mineral wealth and the crafted metalwork of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond. The Fleet was established by [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet) in the year that the chronicles of the kingdom record as the seven hundred and seventy-ninth year of the kingdom, and the Fleet has been maintained at the strength that the campaigns against the corsairs demonstrated to be necessary. The Fleet is composed of the warships of the line, the patrol vessels, and the smaller craft that serve the Fleet as messengers and scouts, and the Fleet is commanded by an admiral who is appointed by the king upon the recommendation of the council of captains. The chronicler of the maritime age records that the Fleet is the most formidable naval force that the northern waters have known, and the Fleet is the foundation of the maritime security of the Stormfall Sea.

## Establishment and the Admiral

The Northern Fleet was established in the year that the chronicles of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) record as the seven hundred and seventy-ninth year of the kingdom, a period when the corsair raids upon the coastal communities of the southern shore of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) had reached a severity that the existing patrol vessels were no longer able to contain. The decision to establish a standing fleet was taken by the council of the kingdom upon the recommendation of the captains of the patrol vessels, and the recommendation was supported by the testimony of the coastal communities that had suffered most from the raids. The council appointed [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet) to the command of the new fleet, and the appointment was the first occasion upon which a woman had held the rank of admiral in the service of the kingdom. The chronicler records that the appointment was contested by several of the senior captains of the patrol service, and the appointment was confirmed only after the council had heard the testimony of the merchants and the fishermen of the coastal towns.

The fleet that Thessa was given to command was modest in size at its establishment, consisting of no more than twenty vessels of war and a number of smaller craft that served the fleet as messengers and scouts. The vessels were built in the yards of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) under Thessa's supervision, and the vessels were designed to the specifications that her experience of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) had led her to prefer. The vessels were of shallow draught and high freeboard, suited to the conditions of the sea that the corsair vessels favoured, and the vessels were manned by crews that Thessa had selected from the experienced fishermen of the coastal towns. The chronicler notes that the fleet was the most formidable force that the kingdom had ever assembled upon the water, and the assembly was the foundation of the campaigns that were to establish Thessa's reputation throughout the known world.

## Composition and the Vessels

The Northern Fleet is composed of the warships of the line, the patrol vessels, and the smaller craft that serve the Fleet as messengers and scouts. The warships of the line are the principal vessels of the Fleet, and the warships are the vessels that are employed in the major engagements that the Fleet may be called upon to fight. The warships of the line are built in the yards of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) and [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) to the design that [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet) established, and the warships are among the most capable vessels that sail the waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The chronicler records that the warships are distinguished by their shallow draught and their high freeboard, qualities that give them a stability in the rough water of the Stormfall and a speed in the light air that the deeper-draughted vessels of the southern ports do not possess.

The patrol vessels are the second component of the Fleet, and the patrol vessels are the vessels that are employed in the routine patrol of the coastal waters and the customary points of the trade. The patrol vessels are smaller than the warships of the line, and the patrol vessels are built for speed and for endurance in the conditions of the Stormfall. The patrol vessels are manned by crews that have been selected from the experienced seamen of the coastal towns, and the crews are the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet for the security of the coastal waters. The chronicler notes that the patrol vessels are the most numerous of the vessels of the Fleet, and the patrol vessels are the foundation of the daily presence of the Fleet upon the waters of the Stormfall.

The smaller craft that serve the Fleet as messengers and scouts are the third component of the Fleet, and the smaller craft are the vessels that carry the signals and the intelligence that the Fleet requires. The smaller craft are built for speed, and the smaller craft are manned by the most experienced of the sailors of the Fleet. The smaller craft are stationed at the principal harbours of the coast, and the smaller craft are the foundation of the rapid communication that the Fleet maintains with the coastal towns and with the court at [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven). The chronicler records that the smaller craft have been the foundation of the success of the Fleet in the campaigns that the Fleet has conducted, and the smaller craft are the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet for the speed of its response to any threat upon the waters.

## The Bases and the Yards

The Northern Fleet maintains its principal base at the harbour of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven), and the harbour of Nordhaven is the headquarters of the admiral and the senior officers of the Fleet. The harbour is one of the finest on the coast of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the harbour is fitted with the docks, the warehouses, and the repair yards that the Fleet requires. The chronicler records that the harbour of Nordhaven has been the foundation of the maritime power of the kingdom, and the harbour is the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet as the principal naval force of the Stormfall.

The secondary bases of the Fleet are the harbours of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) and [Bjornsholm](/content/location/settlement/town/bjornsholm), and the secondary bases are the stations from which the patrol vessels operate along the southern coast. The harbour of Skaldshavn is the principal shipbuilding centre of the kingdom, and the vessels that are built in the yards of Skaldshavn are among the most capable of the fleet. The harbour of Bjornsholm is the base from which the patrol vessels operate along the central coast, and the harbour is the foundation of the security of the trade with the inland communities of the [ursine](/content/species/sapient/ursines) forests. The chronicler notes that the three bases are the foundation of the disposition of the Fleet, and the three bases are the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet as a force that is present along the entire coast.

The shipyards of the Fleet are the yards of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) and [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn), and the yards are the source of the vessels that the Fleet has maintained since the days of [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet). The yards are staffed by the shipwrights who have inherited the tradition of careful construction that the kingdom has maintained, and the yards are the foundation of the quality of the vessels of the Fleet. The chronicler records that the yards are the source of the vessels not only of the Fleet but also of the merchant marine of the kingdom, and the yards are the foundation of the maritime tradition of the coast.

## The Customs of the Service

The Northern Fleet maintains a set of customs of the service that [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet) established in the campaigns against the corsairs, and the customs are the foundation of the discipline of the Fleet. The customs include the regular inspection of the vessels, the maintenance of the customary signals, and the conduct of the patrols according to the patterns that the admiral established. The chronicler records that the customs have been observed without interruption since the establishment of the Fleet, and the customs are the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet for the security of the coastal waters.

The inspection of the vessels is conducted by the senior officers of the Fleet at the customary intervals, and the inspection is the formal occasion for the assessment of the readiness of the vessels for the campaigning season. The inspection includes the examination of the hull, the rigging, the sails, and the arms of the vessel, and the inspection is recorded in the register of the Fleet. The chronicler notes that the inspection is the foundation of the maintenance of the vessels, and the inspection is the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet for the quality of its vessels.

The customary signals of the Fleet are the signals that the vessels exchange with the coastal towns and with one another, and the signals are the foundation of the communication that the Fleet maintains. The signals include the day signals and the night signals that the admiral established, and the signals are the formal vocabulary of the Fleet. The chronicler records that the signals are the most carefully maintained of any maritime institution in the known world, and the signals are the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet for the rapid communication of its intentions.

## Patrols and the Coastal Defence

The Northern Fleet conducts a continuous patrol of the waters of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the coastal waters of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven), and the patrol is the foundation of the security of the maritime trade. The patrol vessels are stationed at the principal points of the trade, and the patrol vessels keep a steady watch for any sign of disturbance. The patrol is conducted both by day and by night, and the patrol vessels are equipped with the arms and the provisions that the conditions of the Stormfall require. The chronicler records that the patrol is the most visible sign of the Fleet's presence upon the waters, and the patrol is the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet as the guardian of the maritime trade.

The Fleet is also responsible for the defence of the coast in the event of an attack, and the Fleet maintains a reserve of warships at the harbour of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) that may be dispatched to the relief of any of the coastal towns that may be threatened. The reserve is composed of the most experienced crews of the Fleet, and the reserve is equipped with the arms and the provisions that a campaign upon the Stormfall would require. The chronicler records that the reserve has been called upon on several occasions during the long history of the Fleet, and the reserve has been the foundation of the defence of the coast in the most serious of the crises that the kingdom has faced.

## The Stormfall Tradition

The Northern Fleet is the principal naval force of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), and the Fleet is the foundation of the maritime tradition of the sea. The Stormfall is a difficult water, and the ships that sail it must be built to withstand the spirited weather systems that move across the sea in the autumn and the winter months. The Fleet is the custodian of the knowledge of the Stormfall that the [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet) established, and the Fleet is the foundation of the reputation of the kingdom as a maritime power. The chronicler records that the Stormfall tradition is one of the most distinctive of the maritime traditions of the known world, and the tradition is the foundation of the security of the trade that connects the two continents that the Stormfall separates.

The Fleet maintains a close cooperation with the maritime communities of the coast, and the cooperation is the foundation of the security of the trade. The fishermen of [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) and the merchants of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) are the principal collaborators of the Fleet, and the collaboration is the foundation of the intelligence that the Fleet employs in the conduct of its patrols. The chronicler records that the cooperation is one of the most distinctive features of the institution, and the cooperation is the foundation of the reputation of the Fleet as a force that is rooted in the maritime communities of the coast.

## Significance

The Northern Fleet is the standing fleet of the [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven), and the Fleet is the principal naval force of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The Fleet was established by [Thessa, Admiral of the Northern Fleet](/content/people/historical/admiral-thessa-of-the-northern-fleet) in the late maritime age, and the Fleet has been maintained at the strength that the campaigns against the corsairs demonstrated to be necessary. The Fleet is the foundation of the maritime security of the kingdom, and the Fleet is the foundation of the prosperity of the trade that the kingdom conducts with the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond. The chronicler finds in the Northern Fleet one of the most capable and least displaced of the naval forces of the known world, maintained not by a ruling house or a priesthood but by the steady discipline of the officers and the crews who have continued the work of the admiral through the centuries. The Fleet endures, in the harbours and upon the waters, as the living inheritance of those whose work and whose life are bound up with the maritime trade of the Stormfall.
