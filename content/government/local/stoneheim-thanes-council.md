## The Advisory Council of the Dwarven High King

The Thanes' Council of the [Stoneheim Holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) is the advisory body of the dwarven high king of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), composed of the thanes who govern the individual holds that constitute the realm. The council is not a legislative body nor a check upon the king's authority, for the high king of [Stoneheim holds](/content/government/national/stoneheim-holds) absolute power over the realm, and the council's advice is not binding. The council is nonetheless an institution of considerable influence, for the thanes who sit upon it are the leaders of the individual holds, and their co-operation is essential for the effective governance of the realm.

The council meets in the great hall of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim), the largest and most ancient of the dwarven cities of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The great hall is carved from the living rock of the mountain, its walls lined with pillars that rise to a vaulted ceiling adorned with the emblems of the holds and the deeds of the kings of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim). The council chamber occupies a side chamber of the great hall, a space carved from the stone and furnished with a great table of polished granite where the thanes sit according to the seniority of their holds.

## Composition and Appointment

The Thanes' Council is composed of the thanes of the principal holds of the Stoneheim realm. Each hold is a self-governing community that manages its own affairs under the authority of the crown, and the thane is the hereditary ruler of the hold. The thane is the oldest member of the founding family of the hold, and the succession passes from parent to eldest child, subject to confirmation by the king. A thane who is judged unfit by the king may be replaced, though such replacement is rare and is undertaken only in extreme circumstances.

The number of thanes on the council is not fixed. The council includes the thanes of all holds that are of sufficient size and importance to warrant a seat, and new thanes are added as new holds are established or existing holds grow in significance. The council currently includes the thanes of twelve holds, including Stoneheim itself, [Khazgar](/content/location/settlement/city/khazgar), [Ironhollow](/content/location/settlement/town/ironhollow), [Deepreach](/content/location/settlement/town/deepreach), and the other principal settlements of the realm. The thanes of the smaller holds do not sit upon the council but are represented by the thane of the nearest principal hold.

The thanes serve on the council for as long as they hold the thaneship of their holds. There is no fixed term of membership, and a thane who is removed from the thaneship by the king also loses the seat on the council. The council is thus composed of the thanes who currently hold the king's favour, and the membership of the council changes gradually over time as the king confirms new thanes or removes those who have lost confidence.

## Powers and Responsibilities

The Thanes' Council of Stoneheim has no formal legislative authority, for the king alone makes law in the realm. The council's function is advisory, and its powers are limited to the authority the king chooses to delegate. The council is consulted by the king on matters of significance, including the allocation of mining rights, the assessment of contributions for the defence of the realm, the negotiation of trade agreements, and the management of relations with the other peoples of Morvind. The king may accept, reject, or ignore the council's advice as the circumstances demand.

In practice, the council's influence is considerable. The thanes are the rulers of the individual holds, and they control the resources, the population, and the military forces of their communities. The king cannot govern the realm effectively without the co-operation of the thanes, and a king who consistently ignored the advice of the council would face resistance and, in extreme cases, rebellion. The history of the Stoneheim Holds includes several periods in which a king who lost the support of the thanes was replaced by a successor who commanded the confidence of the council.

The council also serves as a forum for the resolution of disputes between the thanes. Disputes over the boundaries of mining claims, the allocation of water resources, and the rights to trade routes are common in the dwarven realm, and the council provides a forum in which such disputes can be resolved by negotiation and compromise. The king may mediate a dispute that the thanes cannot resolve themselves, and the king's decision in such matters is final.

## The Thanes and the Administration of the Holds

Each thane is the absolute ruler of the hold they govern, and the authority of the thane within the hold is subject only to the authority of the king. The thane is responsible for the maintenance of the hold's forges and mines, the administration of justice, the collection of the king's taxes, and the provision of soldiers for the realm's defence. The thane is assisted by a council of elders drawn from the most experienced and respected members of the hold's community, and this council advises the thane on the management of the hold's affairs.

The relationship between the thane and the hold's elders is similar in character to the relationship between the king and the council of thanes. The elders advise the thane, but the thane alone decides. An elder who opposes the thane may be dismissed, and the thane who consistently ignores the advice of the elders risks losing the confidence of the community. The elders also serve as a channel of communication between the thane and the ordinary inhabitants of the hold, and they bring the concerns and grievances of the community to the thane's attention.

The thanes are drawn from the founding families of the holds, and their authority is rooted in the tradition and history of the dwarven people. The founding families have governed the holds for many centuries, and their names are recorded in the annals of the realm and the genealogies that are preserved in the archives of Stoneheim. The thanes are expected to uphold the traditions of their holds and to maintain the forges, mines, and halls that are the inheritance of their families.

## The Council in Session

The Thanes' Council convenes at the summons of the king, and the meetings are held in the council chamber of the great hall at Stoneheim. The meetings are formal occasions, conducted according to the traditions and protocols that have been established over the centuries of the realm's history. The king presides, and the thanes sit according to the seniority of their holds, determined by the date of the hold's establishment or its admission to the council.

The agenda of the council is set by the king, and the thanes are informed of the matters to be discussed before the meeting. The thanes may request the addition of items to the agenda, and the king generally accedes to such requests if the matter is of genuine significance to the realm. The discussions are conducted in a formal manner, with each thane speaking in turn and the king responding to each point before moving to the next.

The decisions of the council are not taken by vote, for the king's authority is absolute and the council's advice is advisory. The king may ask the thanes for their views on a matter, and the king may sum the views of the council and announce a decision that reflects the consensus. A thane who disagrees with the king may state the disagreement openly, but the thane must accept the king's decision once it is announced. A thane who persists in opposing the king after the decision has been made may be dismissed from the council and, in extreme cases, from the thaneship.

## The Council and the Defence of the Realm

The defence of the Stoneheim Holds is a matter of collective concern, and the Thanes' Council plays a significant role in the coordination of the realm's defences. Each thane is responsible for the defence of the territory and approaches of the hold, and the council coordinates the allocation of soldiers and resources to meet threats that affect the realm as a whole. The king commands the realm's military forces, but the thanes provide the soldiers and the supplies that sustain the forces in the field.

The council also coordinates the maintenance of the roads and passes that connect the holds to each other and to the coast. The roads of the Stoneheim realm are among the most carefully maintained in the known world, for they are essential for the movement of ore, metalwork, and supplies between the holds and the ports. The council allocates the responsibility for the maintenance of each section of the road network among the thanes, and the king holds the thanes accountable for the condition of the roads within their territories.

## The Significance of the Thanes' Council

The Thanes' Council of Stoneheim is a distinctive institution of the dwarven realm, combining the authority of the king with the collective wisdom and experience of the thanes who govern the individual holds. The council is not a democratic body, for the thanes are not elected but inherit their positions, and the king's authority is absolute. The council is nonetheless a forum in which the leaders of the holds can make their voices heard and influence the decisions of the crown. The council has sustained the stability and prosperity of the Stoneheim Holds for many centuries, and it is a foundation of the dwarven political order in Morvind.
