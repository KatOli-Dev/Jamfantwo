## The King's Advisory Body and the Administration of the Realm

The Royal Council of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) is the principal advisory body to the crown of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth), composed of the most senior officials, merchants, and military commanders of the kingdom. The council is not a legislative body nor a check upon the king's authority, for the monarch of Aldreth holds absolute power over the realm and may accept, reject, or ignore the council's advice as the circumstances demand. The council is nonetheless one of the most influential institutions in the kingdom, for its members are drawn from the families and offices that control the trade, finance, and defence of the eastern coast, and no king of Aldreth has governed for any length of time without the co-operation of the council and the interests it represents.

The council meets in the royal palace at [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), in a chamber adjoining the king's audience hall. The chamber is a long, timber-panelled room lined with maps of the known world and portraits of previous monarchs and councillors. A great table of dark wood, carved from a single trunk of timber imported from the [Aethelar Heartwood](/content/location/natural/ecosystem/aethelar-heartwood), occupies the centre of the room, and the councillors sit according to their rank on either side of it. The king sits at the head of the table upon a raised dais, and the proceedings are conducted in a formal manner that reflects the gravity of the matters discussed.

## Composition and Appointments

The Royal Council of Aldreth is composed of twelve members, each appointed by the king and serving at his pleasure. The membership includes the Lord Chancellor, who oversees the administration of the realm and the maintenance of the royal seal; the Lord Treasurer, who manages the collection of taxes and tolls and the disbursement of funds from the treasury; the Lord Admiral, who commands the royal fleet and oversees the defence of the coast; the Lord Chamberlain, who manages the royal household and the administration of the palace; the Lord Justice, who oversees the courts and the administration of justice throughout the realm; and six senior merchants chosen from the great trading families of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth). The final seat is held by the Master of the Customs, the official responsible for the collection of tolls and duties at the harbour of Eldreth, the single largest source of royal revenue.

The appointment of councillors is a matter entirely within the king's discretion, and there is no fixed term of service. A councillor serves until dismissed by the king, until death, or until retirement. In practice, most councillors serve for many years, and the council is characterised by continuity of membership. The great merchant families of Eldreth regard a seat on the council as the highest honour the crown can bestow, and competition for appointment is intense. The king may appoint a councillor from outside the established families if the circumstances require, and such appointments have occurred when the crown has judged that new perspectives or particular expertise are needed.

The Master of the Customs holds a unique position on the council, for the customs house at Eldreth generates more revenue than any other single source in the kingdom. The Master is typically a career official who has risen through the customs service, and the appointment is based on experience and competence rather than family connections. The Master's detailed knowledge of the trade flowing through the harbour and the merchants who conduct it makes the holder of the office an indispensable source of information for the council and the crown.

## Powers and Responsibilities

The Royal Council of Aldreth has no formal legislative authority, for the king alone makes law in Aldreth. The council's function is advisory, and its powers are limited to the authority the king chooses to delegate. The council is responsible for the administration of the realm on a day-to-day basis, and its members oversee the departments of government that collect taxes, maintain the fleet and fortifications, administer justice, and manage the royal estates. The council meets weekly to review the affairs of the realm, and its recommendations are presented to the king, who decides.

In practice, the council's influence is considerable. The king relies upon the council for the information and expertise necessary to govern a realm as extensive and complex as Aldreth. The councillors are the heads of the departments that carry out the king's orders, and the king cannot act effectively without their co-operation. The great merchant families represented on the council control the trade that is the foundation of the kingdom's prosperity, and their support is essential for the collection of loans, the negotiation of commercial treaties, and the maintenance of the commercial confidence upon which the economy depends.

The council also serves as a court of appeal for certain categories of dispute, particularly those involving the customs service, the administration of the royal estates, and the conduct of royal officials. The king may refer a dispute to the council for hearing, and the council's recommendation carries weight, though the king retains the authority to override it. The council's role in the administration of justice is limited, for Aldreth maintains a separate system of royal courts that handle the great majority of civil and criminal matters.

## The Merchant Faction and the Crown's Authority

The relationship between the crown and the merchant families represented on the council is the defining dynamic of Aldreth politics. The merchants control the trade that is the foundation of the kingdom's prosperity, and the crown depends upon their co-operation for the collection of revenue, the maintenance of credit, and the negotiation of commercial treaties. The merchants, for their part, depend upon the crown for the protection of the trade routes, the enforcement of contracts, and the maintenance of the legal and commercial framework that sustains the eastern coast trade.

The merchant faction on the council is not a formal organisation but a loose alignment of interests. The six merchant councillors are drawn from the most prominent trading families of Eldreth, and they represent the collective interests of the commercial community. The merchants do not vote as a bloc on every matter, for their individual interests sometimes diverge, but they are united in their support for policies that favour trade, maintain the stability of the currency, and protect the maritime routes that are the lifeblood of the kingdom's economy.

The crown's authority in relation to the merchant faction is absolute in law but constrained in practice. The king may dismiss any councillor at will, but the dismissal of a senior merchant from the council would be a significant act with consequences for the relationship between the crown and the commercial community. The dismissal of a merchant councillor would be taken as a signal that the king had lost confidence in the merchant faction, and the reaction of the commercial community could include a withdrawal of co-operation, a refusal to extend credit, or an attempt to negotiate with the king's rivals in [Vael](/content/government/national/vael) or the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league).

## The Role of the Lord Admiral

The Lord Admiral of Aldreth commands the royal fleet, which is among the largest and most capable in [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The fleet is divided into two squadrons, the northern and the southern, and the Lord Admiral is responsible for their readiness, their deployment, and the defence of the coast. The Lord Admiral is also responsible for the protection of merchant shipping, the suppression of piracy, and the maintenance of the lighthouses and navigation aids that line the eastern coast.

The Lord Admiral is typically a senior naval commander appointed from the officer corps of the fleet, though the king may appoint a merchant or a noble if the circumstances require. The Lord Admiral sits on the council as the principal military adviser and provides the king with detailed assessments of the kingdom's maritime defences, the state of the fleet, and the threats facing the trade routes. The Lord Admiral also maintains close relationships with the admirals of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league) and the other maritime powers of the eastern coast.

## The Council in Times of Crisis

The Royal Council of Aldreth has played a central role in the kingdom's responses to the crises that have marked its history. During the period of raiding that threatened the eastern coast in the early centuries of the kingdom, the council co-ordinated the construction of the watchtowers and fortifications that still line the shore. During the succession disputes that followed the death of a king without a direct heir, the council advised on the selection of a successor and maintained the administration of the realm during the interregnum. During the occasional famines that have afflicted the continent, the council managed the distribution of grain from the royal granaries and negotiated with the merchant families for the importation of food from abroad.

The council's role in times of crisis is both administrative and political. The councillors are the officials responsible for the departments of government that must respond to the crisis, and their co-ordination is essential for an effective response. The council also serves as a forum in which the king can consult the leaders of the merchant community and maintain their confidence and co-operation. A king who loses the support of the council in a time of crisis faces not only the practical difficulty of governing without the co-operation of the administration but also the political challenge of ruling against the advice of the most influential figures in the kingdom.

## The Customs House and the Harbour Administration

The customs house at Eldreth is the largest and most important administrative office in the kingdom, and management of the customs service is a significant responsibility of the council. The Master of the Customs, who sits on the council, oversees a staff of hundreds of officials who inspect cargoes, assess duties, and maintain the records of the trade flowing through the harbour. The customs house is situated on the waterfront at Eldreth, and its stone buildings contain the offices, the record rooms, and the secure warehouses where goods held for inspection or awaiting clearance are stored.

The customs service is the primary instrument of the kingdom's commercial regulation. The officials of the service inspect every consignment of goods that enters or leaves the harbour, assess the duties payable according to the schedules established by the crown, and issue the permits and certificates that govern the movement of goods through the kingdom. The customs service also enforces the standards of [weights and measures](/content/economy/weights-and-measures) that the kingdom has established, and the seals of the Aldreth customs service are recognised and trusted in every port of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean).

The harbour administration, which falls under the authority of the Lord Chamberlain, manages the day-to-day operation of the port of Eldreth. The harbourmaster is responsible for the allocation of berths, the co-ordination of pilotage services, and the maintenance of the quays and warehouses that line the waterfront. The harbour administration also maintains the dredging and maintenance of the harbour channels, ensuring that the port remains accessible to the largest vessels that ply the Auric trade.

## The Enduring Institution

The Royal Council of Aldreth has been a feature of the kingdom's governance for as long as the realm has existed. The council's composition and functions have changed over the centuries, but its essential character as an advisory body of senior officials and merchants has remained unchanged. The council is one of the most stable and effective administrative institutions in Aethelar, and its continuity has been a significant factor in the prosperity and stability of the realm. The council's members, drawn from the most capable and experienced figures in the kingdom, provide the crown with the expertise and advice necessary to govern a realm of Aldreth's complexity and commercial importance. The council is not a check upon the king's authority but a partner in the exercise of it, and the effectiveness of the partnership has sustained the kingdom through the centuries of its history.
