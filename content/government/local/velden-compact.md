## The Joint Governance of the Strait Towns

The [Velden](/content/location/settlement/city/velden) Compact is the joint governance of the [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) towns, encompassing the settlements on both the [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind) and the Aethelaran shores of the strait. The compact is not a kingdom but a commercial and regulatory agreement, its purpose to ensure the safe and efficient passage of vessels through the strait, the fair collection of tolls and pilotage fees, and the maintenance of the navigation aids and pilotage services essential to the strait trade. The Velden Compact is one of the most effective and enduring political institutions in the known world, and its governance of the strait has sustained the trade between the two oceans for many centuries.

## Veldenwatch, Southport, Caerhaven, and the Others

The [Velden Strait](/content/location/natural/feature/strait-of-velden) is the only passage between the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), and all vessels travelling between the two must pass through these waters. The compact governs the towns on both shores, and its members include [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch) on the Morvind side and [Southport](/content/location/settlement/town/southport) on the Aethelaran side, the two principal towns that sit astride the narrowest reach of the channel. [Caerhaven](/content/location/settlement/town/caerhaven), at the Auric mouth of the strait on the Aethelaran shore, is a full member whose role is the staging and resupply of vessels at the eastern end of the passage. [Hafnir](/content/location/settlement/city/hafnir) and [Torshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/torshavn) are associated members, situated near the strait on the Morvind coast, their trade connected to the strait through the compact.

The compact is governed by a joint council of representatives from each member town, meeting regularly to co-ordinate the pilotage, toll collection, navigation aids, and defence of the strait. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the compact has the power to regulate the passage of vessels, set the tolls and pilotage fees, and dispatch a collective force in times of need. The governance of the compact is a model of co-operation between settlements of two different continents, and the council is among the most effective and respected political institutions in the known world.

## The Commerce and the Training of the Pilots

The trade flowing through the strait is the commerce of two oceans, and the goods that pass through the channel include the metalwork of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), the grain and cloth of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The tolls and pilotage fees collected by the compact are the primary source of revenue for the member towns, and the fair and efficient collection of these tolls is the foundation of the compact's prosperity. The compact ensures that the tolls are collected uniformly and that the passage of vessels is not delayed or obstructed, for the trade flowing through the strait is vital to the economies of all three continents.

The pilotage service is the other great function of the compact, and the pilots of the strait are trained and regulated by the compact's guild. [Brona Veldenwatch](/content/people/notable/brona-veldenwatch) is the present chief of the pilot guild, elected by the fellows in the four hundred and seventeenth year of the compact. The pilots are drawn from both shores and from the families that have navigated the strait for generations, their knowledge a closely guarded inheritance passed from parent to child and refined by each generation's experience. The compact ensures that the pilotage service is available to all vessels that require it and that the fees are fair and the standards maintained.

The training of a pilot begins in childhood, and the sons and daughters of the pilot families are taught the currents, tides, and hazards of the strait from the earliest age. The training is conducted on the water, and apprentice pilots accompany licensed pilots on their passages, learning the channel by experience rather than instruction. The qualification of a pilot is determined by an examination conducted by the guild, among the most rigorous in the known world, for the safety of the vessels and commerce passing through the strait depends upon the skill and knowledge of the pilots.

## The Accord of the Waters and the Steady Centuries

The Velden Compact was established in the aftermath of a period of conflict and disorder that threatened the strait trade, when the absence of a common governance led to disputes over tolls, pilotage, and the right of passage. The compact was negotiated by the leaders of the towns on both shores, who recognised that the prosperity of all depended upon the fair and efficient regulation of the strait, and the agreement was sealed by a treaty that has been renewed and reaffirmed many times over the centuries. The founding of the compact is recorded in the traditions of the strait towns as the Accord of the Waters, and the anniversary of the signing is celebrated in the member towns as a day of festivity and remembrance.

The history of the compact has been marked by a remarkable stability, for the shared interest of the member towns in the strait trade has sustained co-operation through periods of tension and change. The compact has faced challenges from time to time, including the attempts of outside powers to control the strait and occasional disputes between member towns over the division of revenue. These challenges have been resolved through the council, and the compact has endured without major disruption to the strait trade. The most serious challenge in the compact's history was a dispute between [Veldenwatch](/content/location/settlement/town/veldenwatch) and [Southport](/content/location/settlement/town/southport) over the allocation of pilotage fees, resolved by a revision of the revenue-sharing formula that has governed the compact's finances ever since.

## The Strait People and the Transcontinental Commerce

The population of the strait towns is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the strait identify more closely with the channel and its trade than with the inland nations that claim their allegiance. The communities on both shores share a common identity as the people of the strait, and their co-operation in the governance and defence of the channel has created a bond that transcends the political division between the two continents. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of Morvind maintain agents in the strait towns to manage the flow of metalwork through the channel, and a few [gnomes](/content/species/sapient/gnomes) have established themselves as craftsmen and merchants of particular skill.

The Velden Compact is the primary governance of the most important waterway in the known world, and its fair and efficient regulation of the strait trade is a critical component of the economy of all three continents. The compact's co-operation between the settlements of two different continents is a model of accommodation that has sustained the strait trade for many centuries, and its endurance and effectiveness are a testament to the power of shared interest and mutual respect. The significance of the Velden Compact lies in its role as the guardian of the strait and the regulator of the trade that flows through it, and the compact is a symbol of the co-operation and commerce that connect the continents through the waters of the world.
