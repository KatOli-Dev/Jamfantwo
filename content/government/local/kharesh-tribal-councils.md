## The Councils of the Desert Nomads

The Kharesh Tribal Councils are the governing bodies of the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), the confederation of nomadic peoples of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), the vast desert interior of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The tribal councils are the primary political institutions of the confederation, responsible for the coordination of the movement of the tribes across the desert, the management of the water sources and grazing lands, the regulation of the caravan trade, and the resolution of disputes between kindreds and families. The councils are not a permanent governing body but a flexible system of assemblies and consultations that adapt to the movement of the tribes across the desert.

The tribal councils meet at the oases and upland settlements that serve as gathering points for the nomadic peoples. The meeting places are determined by the movement of the tribes and the condition of the water sources, and the councils convene when the tribes come together for the exchange of goods, the celebration of festivals, and the conduct of the business of the confederation.

## The Council of Elders

The primary institution of governance among the Kharesh Tribes is the council of elders, composed of the elders chosen by the consent of their kindreds. Each kindred, whether of the [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) or the [human](/content/species/sapient/humans) peoples of the desert, selects its elders according to its own traditions. The elders are those who have demonstrated the deepest knowledge of the desert, the greatest skill in negotiation and mediation, and the most complete understanding of the traditions and laws of the people.

No elder may hold a seat on the council unless the adult members of the kindred have affirmed their authority, either by open vote at a kindred gathering or by the absence of sustained objection over the course of a season. An elder who loses the confidence of the kindred may be set aside and replaced at any gathering. The council of elders is thus a body whose membership is constantly subject to the consent of the kindreds, and the elders who sit upon the council are those who command the respect and trust of their people.

The council of elders does not have a fixed membership or a permanent presiding officer. The elders who attend a given council are those whose kindreds are within reach of the meeting place, and the council is presided over by the eldest elder among those present. The decisions of the council are made by consensus, and any elder who dissents may delay a decision until the council finds a resolution acceptable to all.

## The Sands' Assembly

The most important gathering of the Kharesh Tribal Councils is the [Sands' Assembly](/content/culture/sands-assembly), the great council of the confederation that is held at a designated oasis at a time determined by the movement of the tribes and the condition of the water sources. The Sands' Assembly is the principal occasion for the conduct of the affairs of the confederation, attended by elders of every kindred and family within reach of the gathering place.

The Sands' Assembly is open to every adult member of the tribes, not only the elders. Any member of a kindred may attend the Assembly, speak at the designated times, and bring concerns before the assembled elders. The Assembly lasts for several days, during which the elders discuss the affairs of the confederation, resolve disputes between kindreds, and plan the management of the desert for the coming season.

The opening of the Sands' Assembly is marked by the recitation of the oral traditions of the tribes. The elders and the storytellers recite the histories of the kindreds, the accounts of the journeys across the desert, and the laws and customs that govern the confederation. The recitation is followed by the formal business of the Assembly, which includes the discussion of water rights, the allocation of grazing lands, the coordination of the caravan trade, and the resolution of disputes.

The decisions of the Sands' Assembly are binding on all members of the confederation. A decision reached without the attendance of the affected kindreds is considered invalid, and the council must reconvene until every kindred with a stake in the matter has been heard. The decisions of the Assembly are recorded in the oral traditions of the tribes, and they are cited as precedent in future deliberations.

## The Management of Water Rights

The management of water rights is one of the most important functions of the Kharesh Tribal Councils. The wells and springs of the Kharesh are the most precious resource of the desert, and the rights to use them are carefully regulated by the councils. Each kindred holds custodianship of specific wells and springs, and the councils allocate the rights to use the water sources according to the needs of the kindreds and the condition of the sources.

The laws governing water access are recited at every Sands' Assembly and are enforced by the collective authority of the council. A kindred that violates the water laws may be summoned before the council and required to pay compensation or accept restrictions on its access to water sources. The enforcement of the water laws is essential for the survival of the tribes, for the overexploitation of a water source can lead to its depletion and the loss of a vital resource for the entire confederation.

The councils also manage the maintenance of the wells and springs. The kindreds that hold custodianship of a water source are responsible for its maintenance, and the councils coordinate the allocation of labour and resources for the repair and improvement of the wells. The councils also maintain the knowledge of the locations and conditions of the water sources, and the elders share this knowledge at the Assemblies to ensure that all kindreds have access to the information they need for their journeys across the desert.

## The Allocation of Grazing Lands

The allocation of grazing lands is another important function of the Kharesh Tribal Councils. The desert margins and the upland pastures are the seasonal grazing lands for the herds of the nomadic peoples, and the councils allocate the grazing rights according to the movement of the tribes and the condition of the pastures.

The councils coordinate the seasonal rotation of grazing grounds across the kindreds to prevent overgrazing and to maintain the fragile ecology of the desert margins. The rotation is planned at the Sands' Assembly, and the elders agree on the areas that each kindred will use during the coming season. The rotation ensures that the pastures have time to recover between the grazing seasons, and it has maintained the stability of the desert ecology for centuries.

## The Regulation of the Caravan Trade

The regulation of the caravan trade is a further function of the Kharesh Tribal Councils. The tribes are the guides and guardians of the caravan routes that cross the desert, and the councils coordinate the provision of guidance and protection to the caravans that travel the Kharesh. The councils negotiate the fees that the caravans pay for the services of the guides, and they allocate the responsibility for the guidance and protection of the caravans among the kindreds.

The councils also coordinate the relationship between the tribes and the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities. The councils negotiate the terms of the co-operation between the tribes and the pact, and they resolve the disputes that occasionally arise between the kindreds and the merchants of the cities. The relationship between the tribes and the pact is one of mutual dependence, and the councils work to maintain the co-operation that is essential for the prosperity of both.

## The Resolution of Disputes

The resolution of disputes between kindreds and families is a constant function of the Kharesh Tribal Councils. Disputes over water rights, grazing lands, the movement of herds, and the obligations of hospitality are common in the desert, and the councils provide a forum in which such disputes can be resolved by negotiation and compromise.

The elders who hear a dispute are drawn from kindreds that are not party to the dispute, ensuring that the hearing is impartial. The elders hear the arguments of the parties and the testimony of the witnesses, and they propose a resolution that is consistent with the traditions and laws of the confederation. The resolution is binding on the parties, and a kindred that refuses to accept the decision of the elders may be subject to sanctions imposed by the council.

## The Significance of the Tribal Councils

The Kharesh Tribal Councils are one of the most distinctive political institutions in the known world. Their governance by consensus, their openness to every adult member of the tribes, and their adaptation to the nomadic way of life make them a unique example of democratic governance in the most demanding environment of the known world. The councils have sustained the Kharesh Tribes for many centuries, and they are a testament to the capacity of nomadic peoples to govern themselves effectively through the institutions of consultation and consensus.

The Kharesh Tribal Councils are also a model of the accommodation between the nomadic peoples and the settled communities of the desert margins. The councils coordinate the relationship between the tribes and the Vashari Pact, and they maintain the caravan trade that is the foundation of the economy of the desert. The councils have demonstrated that the nomadic and the settled can coexist and co-operate for their mutual benefit, and their success is a model for the accommodation between different ways of life in a world where such accommodation is rare.
