## The Governing Body of the Eastern Port Confederation

The Thessaran League Council is the governing body of the [Thessaran League](/content/government/national/thessaran-league), the confederation of eastern coast port cities of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar). The council is the primary political institution of the league, responsible for the coordination of trade, navigation, and defence among the member cities. The council is composed of representatives from each member city, and its decisions are binding on all members of the league. The council's governance is built upon the principles of representation, rotation of office, and collective action, and it is one of the most effective political institutions on the eastern coast.

The council meets in the great hall of [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), a building donated to the league by the city in the early years of the alliance. The hall is a substantial stone building situated near the harbour, its architecture reflecting the maritime character of the league. A great chamber on the upper floor houses the council table, a long oak surface around which the representatives of the member cities gather for their deliberations. The hall also contains the offices of the league's administration, the archives in which the records of the council are preserved, and the chambers of the presiding officer.

## Composition and Representation

The Thessaran League Council is composed of representatives from each member city. The number of representatives from each city is fixed by the founding charter of the league: each member city sends two representatives to the council. This equality of representation is a deliberate feature of the league's constitution, designed to ensure that the interests of the smaller member cities are not overshadowed by the larger. The representatives are chosen by each member city according to its own customs, and the methods of selection vary from city to city.

The representatives serve for a term of three years and may be re-appointed without limit. There is no formal qualification for membership beyond the citizenship of a member city and the appointment by the city's governing body. The representatives are expected to attend every council session, and a representative who misses two consecutive sessions without good cause may be replaced by the member city. The council maintains a register of representatives, and the credentials of each representative are verified at the opening of each session.

The methods of selection of representatives vary among the member cities according to their internal governance. In [Eldreth](/content/location/settlement/city/eldreth), the representatives are appointed by the king of [Aldreth](/content/government/national/aldreth) from among the merchants and officials of the city. In [Caerwyn](/content/location/settlement/city/caerwyn), the representatives are elected by the shipbuilders and merchants who constitute the city's governing council. In [Thornhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/thornhaven), the representatives are chosen by the guilds that dominate the city's economy. In [Sallowmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sallowmere), the representatives are elected by popular vote of all free adult citizens. This diversity of selection methods reflects the diversity of the member cities and their traditions of governance.

## The Thessar and the Administration

The council elects from among its members a presiding officer, known as the Thessar, who serves for a term of three years and is responsible for the conduct of the council's business and the carrying out of its decisions. The Thessar is chosen from among the representatives by a majority vote, and the office rotates among the member cities to ensure that no single city dominates the governance of the league. The rotation is organised according to a fixed schedule established by the council, and each member city holds the office for a single term before passing it to the next city in the rotation.

The Thessar is assisted by a body of administrators who manage the day-to-day affairs of the league. The chief administrator is the Clerk of the League, who maintains the records of the council's proceedings, the registers of members and contributions, and the archives of the league. The Clerk is appointed by the council and serves for a term of five years, renewable by the council's decision. The Clerk is assisted by a staff of scribes, accountants, and messengers who manage the correspondence, the accounts, and the communications of the league.

The administration of the league also includes a number of officials responsible for specific functions. The Master of the Lighthouses oversees the maintenance of the lighthouses and navigation aids that line the eastern coast. The Master of the Pilots coordinates the pilotage services in the harbours of the member cities. The Master of the Fleet oversees the maintenance and deployment of the league's collective fleet. The Master of the Tariffs coordinates the collection of the unified port tariffs that the league has established. These officials are appointed by the council and serve at its pleasure.

## Functions and Powers

The Thessaran League Council exercises a range of powers that are essential for the coordination of the maritime trade and defence of the eastern coast. The council's primary function is the coordination of trade among the member cities. The council establishes the unified port tariffs that apply in all member harbours, ensuring that no member city can undercut the others by offering lower fees. The council also establishes the standards for weights and measures, the inspection of cargoes, and the documentation required for the movement of goods through the league's harbours.

The council also coordinates the defence of the eastern coast through the maintenance of the collective fleet. The fleet is composed of vessels contributed by the member cities in proportion to their share of the trade, and the fleet is commanded by an admiral appointed by the council. The admiral serves for a term of three years, and after each term a new commander is chosen from a different member city to ensure that no single city dominates the naval command. The fleet protects the trade routes from pirates and raiders, escorts merchant convoys when necessary, and provides assistance to vessels in distress.

The council also coordinates the maintenance of the navigation aids that are essential for the safety of the trade routes. The lighthouses, beacons, and buoys that line the eastern coast are maintained by the league, and the council allocates the contributions of the member cities for this purpose. The council also coordinates the pilotage services in the harbours of the member cities, ensuring that pilots are available for vessels that require them and that the fees for pilotage are regulated to prevent exploitation.

The council has the power to levy contributions on the member cities to fund the activities of the league. The contributions are assessed annually on the basis of the volume of trade passing through each member city's harbour, and the council may adjust the assessments if the circumstances require. The council also has the power to negotiate treaties with external powers on behalf of the league, and any treaty negotiated by the council is binding on all member cities.

## Decision-Making and Voting

The Thessaran League Council reaches its decisions through a process of deliberation and voting. Each representative has one vote, and decisions are made by a simple majority of those present, except for matters of particular importance, which require a two-thirds majority. The matters that require a two-thirds majority include the admission of new members, the amendment of the league's charter, the declaration of war, and the negotiation of treaties with external powers.

The council meets in regular session four times each year, at the equinoxes and solstices. Special sessions may be convened by the Thessar at the request of a member city or when the circumstances require. The sessions are open to the representatives of the member cities and to the officials of the league, and the proceedings are recorded by the Clerk and preserved in the league's archives.

The council also maintains a system of committees that handle matters requiring detailed consideration. The committees include the Trade Committee, which reviews the tariffs and trade regulations; the Defence Committee, which reviews the state of the fleet and the fortifications; the Navigation Committee, which reviews the condition of the lighthouses and navigation aids; and the Finance Committee, which reviews the contributions and expenditures of the league. The committees are composed of representatives and officials with particular expertise, and the committees report their recommendations to the full council for decision.

## The Relationship with Aldreth

The relationship between the Thessaran League Council and the kingdom of Aldreth is one of the defining features of the league's governance. The city of Eldreth is both the capital of Aldreth and the largest member of the league, and the king of Aldreth is the most powerful political figure on the eastern coast. The king's influence in the league is considerable, but the league's constitution and the tradition of rotation of office prevent the kingdom from dominating the league entirely.

The dual role of Eldreth as a league member and a kingdom's capital is a source of complexity in eastern coast politics. The representatives of Eldreth on the league council are appointed by the king, and they naturally represent the interests of the kingdom. The other member cities are aware of the potential for domination by Aldreth, and the league's institutions have been designed to guard against this. The rotation of the Thessar among the member cities ensures that Aldreth does not hold the presiding office permanently, and the requirement for a two-thirds majority on matters of particular importance ensures that Aldreth cannot act unilaterally on matters affecting the whole league.

The relationship between the league and Aldreth is nonetheless a productive one. The league benefits from the naval power and commercial influence of Aldreth, and Aldreth benefits from the coordination of trade and defence that the league provides. The two institutions have coexisted for many centuries, and the accommodation between them is a model of the layered governance that characterises the politics of the eastern coast.

## The Council and the Maritime Courts

The Thessaran League Council also exercises a judicial function through the league's admiralty court, which sits in Eldreth under a presidency that rotates with the Thessar. The admiralty court hears disputes involving maritime contracts, insurance claims, collisions at sea, and the salvage of vessels and cargoes. The court's decisions are binding on all member cities and are enforced by the collective authority of the league.

The admiralty court has developed a body of maritime law that is cited as precedent in ports as distant as [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) and [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven). The court's decisions are recorded by the Clerk and preserved in the league's archives, and they are consulted by merchants, shipowners, and mariners who seek guidance on the rights and obligations that govern the maritime trade of the eastern coast. The admiralty court is one of the most respected institutions of the league, and its reputation for fairness and consistency has contributed to the confidence that the merchants of the wider world place in the trade of the eastern coast.

## The Council and the Trade Routes

The coordination of the trade routes of the eastern coast is the most visible function of the Thessaran League Council. The league maintains a system of signal towers along the coast that can relay information about approaching vessels, cargo availability, and market prices between member cities in a matter of hours, giving the league's merchants a significant advantage in timing their trades. The league also maintains the lighthouses and beacons that guide vessels into the harbours of the member cities, and the league's pilots are available to guide vessels through the approaches to every member harbour.

The council also coordinates the protection of the trade routes through the collective fleet. The fleet patrols the waters off the eastern coast, and its presence deters pirates and raiders who might otherwise threaten the trade routes. The fleet also escorts merchant convoys when necessary, and the council coordinates the scheduling of convoys to ensure that vessels can travel in safety through sections of the coast where the threat of attack is greatest. The coordination of the trade routes by the council has been a significant factor in the prosperity of the eastern coast and the security of the commerce that flows through its harbours.
