## An Alliance of the Western Fishing and Whaling Towns

The [Grey Coast](/content/location/settlement/region/grey-coast) Holdings is an alliance of the western coast [fishing and whaling](/content/economy/fishing-and-whaling) towns of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), united by their common dependence on the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean) and their shared interest in the fisheries and whaling grounds that sustain them. The holdings are not a kingdom but a commercial and defensive alliance, and its member towns retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, navigation, and defence. The [Grey Coast](/content/location/settlement/region/grey-coast) Holdings is the primary political entity of the western coast, its influence extending along the full length of the cliff coast from the northern headlands to the southern edge of the cliff country.

## Greycliff, Misthaven, and the Rotating Council

The Grey Coast Holdings includes the principal fishing and whaling towns of the western coast, drawn from the settlements that share in the fisheries and whaling grounds of the [Greyring Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/greyring-ocean). [Greycliff](/content/location/settlement/town/greycliff) is the largest and most influential member, its harbour and whaling fleet the primary assets of the alliance. [Misthaven](/content/location/settlement/town/misthaven) is valued for its fog-shrouded harbour and the skill of its mariners in navigating the difficult conditions of the western coast. [Saltreach](/content/location/settlement/town/saltreach) is the southernmost member, its fishermen working some of the most dangerous waters on the coast.

The holdings are governed by a council of representatives from each member town, meeting regularly to co-ordinate the fisheries, whaling, navigation, and defence of the western coast. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the holdings have the power to levy contributions, negotiate treaties, and dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the holdings is a model of co-operation among independent towns, and the council is among the most effective political institutions on the western coast.

The council rotates its meeting place among the member towns, and the representatives are chosen by the local governance of each town. Terms of service are fixed, and the representatives are expected to report back to their communities on the council's decisions and matters under consideration. The council maintains a small permanent staff, drawn from the member towns on a rotating basis, overseeing the collection of contributions, maintenance of lighthouses and navigation aids, and co-ordination of the collective fleet.

## Dried Fish, Whale Oil, and the Navigation Aids

The trade of the Grey Coast Holdings is dominated by the products of the fisheries and whaling grounds. Dried fish, whale oil, ambergris, and other products of the Greyring are exported to inland markets and the eastern coast through overland routes connecting the western coast to the interior. The holdings co-ordinate the management of the fisheries and whaling grounds, ensuring that the resources of the Greyring are used sustainably and that the interests of the member towns are balanced. The holdings also maintain the lighthouses, bells, and navigation aids essential to the safety of the western coast, for the fog and swell of the Greyring make it the most dangerous coast in the known world.

## The Mariner Population, the Cancrids, and the Collective Fleet

The population of the Grey Coast Holdings is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the western coast are known for their hardihood and independence. The [cancrids](/content/species/sapient/cancrids) of the rocky intertidal zone are occasional contacts of the member towns, and trade between the coastal humans and the cancrid communities is a minor but valued part of the local economy. The [goblins](/content/species/sapient/goblins) of the ruins and caves along the cliff are a recurring concern, and the defence of the member towns against goblin raiding is one of the functions of the holdings' collective force.

The defence of the western coast is maintained by a collective fleet funded by the contributions of the member towns, while the fortifications of the individual towns are maintained by each member. The fleet protects the fishing and whaling vessels from the dangers of the open ocean and is the primary instrument of the holdings' power and influence. The fleet is composed of vessels contributed by the member towns, and the crews are drawn from the experienced mariners of the western coast, among the most skilled in handling ships in the difficult conditions of the Greyring.

## From Founding Charter to Goblin Raids

The Grey Coast Holdings was established in the early centuries of the settlement of the western coast, when the fishing and whaling towns recognised that the common challenges of the Greyring required a common response. The founding charter was agreed by the representatives of the original member towns, and the principles of co-operation and collective defence established at that time have been the foundation of the alliance ever since. The holdings have been expanded on several occasions as new towns were founded and admitted to membership, and the council has been reformed to accommodate the growing number of members.

The history of the holdings has been marked by the recurring challenge of goblin raids from the caves and ruins along the cliff coast. The collective force has been dispatched on numerous occasions to repel the raiders and secure the settlements. The most serious of these incursions was repelled by a combined force from all the member towns, and the victory is commemorated in the traditions of the holdings and in the records of the council. The holdings have also been called upon to assist vessels in distress on the western coast, and the rescue operations conducted by the fleet are a valued and recognised part of the holdings' function.

## The Symbol of Co-operation on the Greyring Shore

The Grey Coast Holdings is the primary political entity of the western coast of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and its co-operation among the fishing and whaling towns is a model of the kind of association that has sustained the western coast for centuries. The holdings' management of the fisheries and whaling grounds is a significant component of the economy of the western coast, and its trade connects the Greyring to the markets of the interior. The significance of the Grey Coast Holdings lies in its resilience and capacity for collective action in the face of the demanding conditions of the western coast, and the holdings are a symbol of the hardihood and independence of the people of the Greyring shore.

The holdings are a symbol of the capacity of the western coast communities to sustain themselves and co-operate in the face of the most demanding conditions the known world can offer, and the alliance is a testament to the values of hardihood, independence, and mutual support that are the hallmark of the people of the Greyring.
