## The Scholarly Governing Body of the Far Southern Academies

The [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach) Academy Council is the governing body of the academies of the [Southern Reach](/content/government/national/southern-reach), the federation of communities in the far south of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The academy council is a unique institution that combines the functions of a scholarly governing body with those of a political administration, for the academies of the Southern Reach are the primary institutions of learning and research in the far south, and their governance is closely intertwined with the governance of the federation itself.

The academies of the Southern Reach are situated in the fertile highlands and on the shores of the inland seas that are the heart of the federation. The principal academy is the Great Academy at [Vereth](/content/location/settlement/town/vereth), the largest settlement of the Southern Reach, situated on the shore of the [Greater Inland Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/greater-inland-sea). The Great Academy is a complex of buildings that includes lecture halls, libraries, laboratories, and residential quarters for the scholars and students. The academy council meets in a chamber within the Great Academy, a room lined with bookshelves and manuscripts that reflect the scholarly character of the institution.

## Composition and Selection

The Academy Council of the Southern Reach is composed of the most distinguished scholars and teachers of the academies, together with representatives of the communities that support the academies. The council includes the heads of the principal faculties of the Great Academy at [Vereth](/content/location/settlement/town/vereth), the heads of the smaller academies at [Ashaar](/content/location/settlement/town/ashaar) and [Torem](/content/location/settlement/town/torem), and a number of senior scholars elected by the faculties of the academies.

The representatives of the communities are chosen by the councils of the settlements that host the academies. These representatives are typically drawn from the elders and officials of the communities, and they serve as a bridge between the academic world of the academies and the practical world of the farming and fishing communities that support them. The community representatives ensure that the academies remain responsive to the needs of the communities and that the benefits of the scholarship and research conducted in the academies are shared with the wider population.

The heads of the faculties and the senior scholars serve on the council for life, subject to good behaviour and continued scholarly activity. The community representatives serve for a term of five years and may be re-appointed by their communities. The council thus combines the continuity of the academic leadership with the periodic renewal provided by the community representatives.

## The Presiding Officer and Administration

The academy council elects from among its members a presiding officer, known as the Chancellor of the Southern Reach, who serves for a term of seven years and is responsible for the conduct of the council's business and the carrying out of its decisions. The Chancellor is chosen by a majority vote of the council, and the office is open to any member of the council who has served for at least five years.

The Chancellor is assisted by a body of administrators who manage the day-to-day affairs of the academies. The chief administrator is the Master of the Academy, who oversees the operation of the Great Academy at Vereth and coordinates the activities of the smaller academies. The Master is appointed by the Chancellor and confirmed by the council, and the Master serves for a term of five years.

The administration of the academies also includes a number of officials responsible for specific functions. The Keeper of the Library oversees the great library of the Great Academy, which contains the most comprehensive collection of texts on the Art and the natural philosophy of the known world. The Master of the Observatories oversees the astronomical observatories that the academies maintain for the study of the heavens. The Master of the Surveys oversees the expeditions and surveys that the academies dispatch to study the geography and the peoples of the far south.

## Functions and Responsibilities

The primary function of the Academy Council is the governance of the academies and the coordination of their scholarly activities. The council determines the curriculum of the academies, the admission of students, and the appointment of teachers. The council also awards the degrees and certifications that the academies confer, and the degrees of the Southern Reach academies are respected throughout the known world for their rigour and comprehensiveness.

The council also coordinates the research activities of the academies. The scholars of the Southern Reach are engaged in the study of the Art, the natural sciences, history, and the cultures of the known world. The council allocates the resources of the academies among the different fields of study, and it determines the priorities for research expeditions and surveys. The council also reviews the findings of the scholars and publishes the results of the research for the benefit of the wider scholarly community.

The council also manages the relationship between the academies and the federation of the Southern Reach. The academies are the primary institutions of learning and research in the far south, and the federation depends upon the academies for the education of its officials, the training of its [practitioners of the Art](/content/magic/practitioners), and the documentation of its history and culture. The council coordinates with the federation council at Vereth to ensure that the activities of the academies are aligned with the needs of the communities.

## The Academies and the Art

The study of the Art is one of the principal activities of the Southern Reach academies. The practitioners of the Southern Reach are attuned to the patterns of the mountains and the inland seas, and their discipline is shaped by the peculiar geography of the far south. The academy council oversees the training of practitioners of the Art, the documentation of workings, and the investigation of the laws of the Art.

The academies of the Southern Reach maintain the most systematic treatments of [the four laws](/content/magic/the-four-laws-detailed) of the Art that any tradition has produced. The scholars of the academies have developed a terminology of the Art that is more precise than that of the northern traditions, and they have published detailed critiques of the doctrines of the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court) and the [Heartwood Fellowship](/content/government/local/heartwood-fellowship). The academy council coordinates the correspondence between the Southern Reach and the other traditions of the Art, and the exchange of texts between the south and the north has enriched the study of the Art in both regions.

The academy council also maintains the archives of workings and experiments that are preserved in the library of the Great Academy. The archives include the records of successful workings and the accounts of failed experiments that are among the most valuable sources of knowledge for practitioners of the Art. The archives are open to scholars of all traditions, and the academy council encourages the exchange of knowledge and the collaboration between practitioners of different traditions.

## The Academies and the Federation

The academy council also plays a role in the governance of the federation of the Southern Reach. The academies are the primary institutions of learning and research, and the scholars of the academies are often called upon to advise the federation council on matters of significance. The Chancellor of the Southern Reach sits on the federation council as an adviser, and the scholars of the academies provide the technical expertise that is necessary for the management of the water resources, the planning of the caravan routes, and the coordination of the defence of the basin.

The academies are also the institutions through which the history and culture of the Southern Reach are documented and preserved. The scholars of the academies maintain the archives of the federation, which contain the records of the council's decisions, the histories of the communities, and the accounts of the explorations and surveys that have revealed the geography of the far south. The archives at Vereth contain records spanning more than a dozen centuries, kept on prepared hides that resist the decay that afflicts paper in humid climates, and they constitute the longest continuous written record in the southern hemisphere.

## The Significance of the Academy Council

The Southern Reach Academy Council is one of the most distinctive political institutions in the known world. Its combination of scholarly governance with political administration reflects the unusual character of the Southern Reach, where the academies are the primary institutions of learning and research and the federation depends upon them for the education of its officials and the documentation of its culture. The academy council has sustained the intellectual and cultural life of the far south for many centuries, and its commitment to scholarship and the pursuit of knowledge is a model for the other traditions of the Art and the other institutions of learning in the known world.

The academy council is also a testament to the value that the people of the Southern Reach place on learning and the pursuit of knowledge. The academies are supported by the contributions of the farming and fishing communities, and the communities take pride in the achievements of the scholars and the reputation of the academies. The academy council embodies the principle that learning and governance are complementary activities, and its success has demonstrated that a society that values knowledge can sustain both its material prosperity and its intellectual vitality over many centuries.
