## Overview

The Vashari Pact is the alliance of the inland caravan cities at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), the vast desert interior of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The pact is not a kingdom but a commercial and a defensive alliance, and its member cities retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, caravan management, and defence. The Vashari Pact is the primary political entity of the inland Sahrani, and its collective power in the caravan trade that connects the coast to the interior is the foundation of the economy of the desert regions.

## The Member Cities

The Vashari Pact includes the principal inland cities and settlements at the edge of the Kharesh, and the membership is drawn from the communities that share in the caravan trade. [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari) is the largest and the most influential member of the pact, and its position at the terminus of the great caravan routes gives it a commercial importance that is unmatched by any other inland city. [Shurra](/content/location/settlement/village/shurra) is a valued member, the oasis settlement deep in the Kharesh that is a vital waypoint on the caravan routes. [Kheled](/content/location/settlement/village/kheled) is the upland settlement of the desert massifs, and its minerals and its springs are a valued resource for the caravans. [Dunesend](/content/location/settlement/outpost/dunesend) is the caravan stop at the edge of the Kharesh, and its services are a critical component of the caravan trade.

The pact is governed by a council of representatives from each member city, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the caravan trade, the management of the routes, and the defence of the inland cities. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the pact has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective force in times of need. The governance of the pact is a model of co-operation among independent cities, and the council is among the most effective political institutions in the inland region.

The council of the pact meets at [Vashari](/content/location/settlement/city/vashari), and the sessions are attended by the representatives of the member cities and the caravan masters who operate the routes. The council sessions are occasions of negotiation and exchange, and the representatives who attend the sessions carry with them the news and the concerns of their communities. The council maintains a registry of the caravan routes and the water sources, and the registry is the official record of the trade and the infrastructure that sustains the caravan commerce. The council also maintains a system of waybills and seals that authenticate the caravans and their cargoes, and the system is a critical tool in the regulation and the protection of the trade.

## The Caravan Trade

The caravan trade is the foundation of the Vashari Pact's economy, and the goods that flow through the caravan routes include the spices, the dyes, and the precious stones of the interior, which are carried by the caravans from the oases and the upland settlements to the markets of the coast, and the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that are carried in the opposite direction. The pact co-ordinates the caravan trade, managing the routes, the provisioning, and the protection of the caravans that travel the Kharesh, and its regulation of the trade ensures that the goods flow efficiently and safely between the coast and the interior.

The pact's relationship with the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), the confederation of nomadic peoples of the desert, is one of mutual dependence, for the tribes provide the knowledge of the desert that the caravans require, and the pact provides the goods and the market that the tribes need. The co-operation between the pact and the tribes is the foundation of the caravan trade, and the two entities work together to maintain the routes, the water sources, and the shelters that sustain the traffic between the coast and the interior.

## Defence and the People

The defence of the inland cities is maintained by a collective force that is funded by the contributions of the member cities, and the fortifications of the individual cities are maintained by each member. The desert is a source of danger as well as trade, and the defence of the caravan routes against raiders and the wild peoples of the deep desert is a constant concern of the pact. The population of the pact is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) who are involved in the caravan trade and the guidance of the desert routes.

The collective force of the pact is composed of the soldiers and the guards who are contributed by the member cities, and the force is commanded by a captain who is appointed by the council. The force is stationed at the points along the caravan routes where the threat of raiding is most acute, and the patrols that are conducted by the force are a deterrent to the raiders who might otherwise prey on the caravans. The force is supplemented by the salawa guides who accompany the caravans and who provide the knowledge of the desert that is essential for the safe passage of the routes.

## History

The Vashari Pact was established in the early centuries of the caravan trade, when the inland cities recognised that the common challenges of the desert and the trade required a common response. The founding charter of the pact was agreed by the representatives of the original member cities, and the principles of co-operation, mutual support, and collective defence that were established at that time have been the foundation of the alliance ever since. The pact has been expanded on several occasions as new settlements were founded and admitted to the membership, and the council has been reformed to accommodate the growing number of members.

The history of the pact has been marked by the recurring challenge of the desert raiders and the wild peoples of the deep interior, and the collective force of the pact has been dispatched on numerous occasions to suppress the threat and to secure the routes. The most serious of these threats was a confederation of raiders that was based in the deep Kharesh, and the campaign that was conducted by the pact's force against the raiders is commemorated in the traditions of the inland cities and in the records of the council. The pact has also been called upon to mediate disputes between the member cities and the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), and the mediation of the council has been a significant factor in the maintenance of the peace and the co-operation that sustain the caravan trade.

## Significance

The Vashari Pact is the primary political entity of the inland Sahrani, and its collective power in the caravan trade that connects the coast to the interior is the foundation of the economy of the desert regions. The pact's co-operation with the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes) in the caravan trade is a critical component of the economy of Sahrana, and its regulation of the caravan routes is a vital function that sustains the commerce between the coast and the interior. The significance of the Vashari Pact lies in its capacity for collective action and its control of the caravan trade, and the pact is a symbol of the endurance and the enterprise of the inland cities of the Sahrani desert.

The pact is a symbol of the capacity of the inland cities to sustain themselves and to co-operate in the face of the vast and the demanding environment of the Kharesh, and the alliance is a testament to the values of endurance, enterprise, and mutual support that are the hallmark of the people of the desert interior. The caravan trade that is managed by the pact is a critical component of the economy of Sahrana, and the goods that flow through the routes connect the interior to the coast and the wider world in a network of commerce that is vital to the prosperity of the continent.
