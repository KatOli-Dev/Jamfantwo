## Overview

The [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) Republic is the political entity that governs [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra), the wealthy port city-state that is the centre of the spice trade on the northern coast of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana). The republic is not a monarchy but a merchant-led government, and its governance is based on the principles of commercial representation and the shared interest of the spice traders who are the foundation of the city's wealth. The Amurra Republic is one of the most prosperous and the most influential political entities in Sahrana, and its control of the spice trade gives it a commercial power that is disproportionate to its modest size.

## The Republic and the Governance

The Amurra Republic is governed by a council of merchants that is elected from the spice-trading families of the city, and the council is the primary political institution of the republic. The council meets in the council hall, a substantial building that is situated near the harbour, and the decisions of the council are made by vote. The council oversees the administration of the city, the regulation of the spice trade, the maintenance of the harbour and the caravan routes, and the administration of justice, and its decisions are binding on all the inhabitants of the city. The council is composed of twelve members, each of whom is elected by the spice-trading families for a term of five years, and the elections are held in the council hall with the participation of the heads of all the trading families that are eligible to vote.

The governance of the republic is based on the principles of commercial representation, and the officials are chosen from the merchants who are the economic foundation of the city. The council's primary concern is the regulation and the protection of the spice trade, for the spices that pass through the harbour of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) are the most valuable single commodity in the known world, and the council's management of this trade is the foundation of the city's prosperity. The council regulates the caravans that bring the spices from the interior, the shipping that carries them to the northern continents, and the quality and the authenticity of the spices that are traded in the city's markets. The inspectors who are appointed by the council examine every consignment of spices that passes through the harbour, and the seals that they place on the approved containers are a guarantee of quality that is recognised throughout the known world.

The republic maintains a system of courts that are responsible for the administration of justice, and the judges are appointed by the council from among the senior merchants and the legal scholars of the city. The courts handle the disputes that arise from the spice trade, the commercial conflicts between the trading families, and the ordinary civil and criminal matters that are the concern of any urban community. The legal system of the republic is noted for its fairness and its efficiency, and the reputation of the Amurra courts is a factor in the confidence that the merchants of the wider world place in the trade of the city.

## The Spice Trade and the Economy

The spice trade is the foundation of the Amurra Republic's economy, and the spices that pass through the city are the most valuable single commodity in the known world. The caravans that bring the spices from the foothills and the upland valleys of the [Sahrani](/content/language/spoken/sahrani) interior are the lifeline of the city, and the republic's regulation of the caravan trade is a critical function of its governance. The spices are loaded onto vessels at the harbour for transport to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), and the return cargo includes the grain, the iron, and the manufactured goods that the city needs.

The wealth that accumulates in the treasury of the republic is used to fund the maintenance of the harbour, the caravan routes, and the fortifications, and the republic's investment in the infrastructure of the spice trade is a significant component of its governance. The republic is a member of the [Northern Coast League](/content/government/national/northern-coast-league), the alliance of the Sahrani coastal city-states, and its spice trade is a valued component of the league's commerce.

## The People and the Defence

The population of [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the city are a mixture of merchants, traders, and the craftsmen who serve the spice trade. The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) of the desert are occasional visitors to the city, and the trade between the coastal humans and the desert salawa is a minor but valued part of the republic's commerce. The salawa bring the goods of the deep desert, including the rare minerals and the salt that are valued in the markets of the coast, and they trade these for the spices and the manufactured goods that the city can provide. The defence of the city is maintained by a standing force that is funded by the treasury, and the fortifications of Amurra are maintained in good order. The garrison is composed of professional soldiers who are recruited from the citizens of the republic, and the commander of the garrison is appointed by the council and is responsible for the defence of the harbour, the walls, and the caravan routes.

The caravan routes that connect Amurra to the interior are protected by a force of guards who are maintained by the republic, and the guards escort the caravans that carry the spices from the foothills and the upland valleys to the harbour. The routes pass through the country that lies between the coast and the interior, and the guards are necessary to deter the bandits and the raiders who are an occasional threat to the caravans. The guards are mounted on the horses that are bred for the purpose, and they are armed with the weapons that are suited to the defence of a caravan, including the bows and the lances that are effective at range and in the open country that the routes cross.

## Significance

The Amurra Republic is the centre of the spice trade and one of the wealthiest political entities in Sahrana, and its control of the most valuable single commerce in the known world gives it a significance that is disproportionate to its modest size. The republic's merchant-led governance is a model of the kind of political order that has sustained the Sahrani city-states, and its regulation of the spice trade is a critical function that ensures the quality and the reliability of the most valued product of the Sahrani coast. The significance of the Amurra Republic lies in its commercial power and its effective governance, and the republic is a symbol of the wealth and the enterprise of the Sahrani coast. The spices that pass through the harbour of Amurra are the foundation of the culinary and the medicinal traditions of every continent, and the republic's role in the spice trade ensures that its influence extends far beyond the borders of the city.
