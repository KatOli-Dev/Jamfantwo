## Frontier Principality of the Northern Forests

The Northern Marches is a frontier principality of the northern forests of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), encompassing the settled lands between the ancient forests of the north and the open country of the central grasslands. The principality is the northernmost political entity of Aethelar, and its territory includes the river valleys, forest margins, and foothills that form the transition between the temperate north and the wilder regions beyond. The Northern Marches is a frontier realm whose primary functions are the management of the boundary between settled land and forest, the defence of the northern approaches, and the maintenance of the trade routes connecting the north to the rest of the continent.

## Between Woodland and Settlement

The ancient forests that cover the northern reaches of Aethelar are the defining feature of the Northern Marches, and the relationship between the principality and the forest is the central concern of its governance. The [elves](/content/species/sapient/elves) of the deep forest are the neighbours of the principality, and co-operation between humans and elves in the management of the forest is a model of the accommodation that has sustained the northern frontier for centuries. Logging and hunting conducted by the principality are managed in consultation with the elves, and the boundary between settled land and forest is maintained by mutual agreement. The compact governing this relationship was established in the early years of the principality and has been renewed and revised by each generation of leaders, a living document reflecting the accumulated wisdom of centuries of coexistence.

The frontier that the Northern Marches guards is not a political boundary but a geographical one. The forest is the home of the elves, the [ursines](/content/species/sapient/ursines), and other woodland peoples, and the principality is the guardian of the transition between the forest and the settled land. The defence of the frontier is maintained by a standing force funded by the principality, and the fortifications of the northern settlements are kept in good order. The frontier force consists of rangers skilled in the woodcraft and tracking necessary for patrol of the forest margin, and the discipline of the frontier is embodied in the [Standing Watch](/content/government/local/standing-watch), the body of watchmen who maintain the holding of the beacon and the patrol of the border, supplemented by fortified posts such as [Northern Watch](/content/location/settlement/outpost/northern-watch) upon the outer ridge. The rangers are drawn from the communities of the frontier, for the people who have grown up at the edge of the forest are most familiar with its ways.

The ursines of the deep forest are a less frequent presence in the settlements than the elves, but the relationship between the ursines and humans is one of mutual respect. The ursines are a solitary people who inhabit the deepest portions of the woodland, rarely seen in the towns and villages of the frontier. The rangers maintain contact with the ursine kindreds through signals and markers that the two peoples have developed for communication across the forest, and the ursines provide warning of any threat that emerges from the deeper woodland.

## Thornwick and the Frontier Council

The principal city of the Northern Marches is [Thornwick](/content/location/settlement/city/thornwick), the oldest human settlement in the northern river valleys and the administrative centre of the principality. [Eldergrove](/content/location/settlement/city/eldergrove) is the other significant city, situated at the edge of the ancient forests, and its trade in timber and forest products is a valued component of the economy. [Ashford](/content/location/settlement/city/ashford) and [Fernmoor](/content/location/settlement/town/fernmoor) are the other members of the principality, and their positions at the river crossing and the forest boundary respectively give them strategic significance within the frontier.

The principality is governed from Thornwick by a prince appointed by the council of the northern cities. The council consists of representatives of the member cities, chosen by each city according to its own customs, and the prince's authority is limited to frontier matters, leaving the internal affairs of each city to its own governance. This arrangement places the Northern Marches closer to the authoritarian kingdoms than to the elective confederations, yet the council's power to choose and replace the prince provides a check absent in the hereditary monarchies of the south. The administration is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the collection of taxes, maintenance of roads and fortifications, and administration of justice. The governance of the Northern Marches is oriented toward the frontier, and the administration is concerned chiefly with the management of the boundary and defence of the northern approaches.

## River Barges and Forest Products

The trade of the Northern Marches is dominated by the products of the forest and the agriculture of the river valleys. Timber, game, and grain produced in the principality are exported to the markets of the south through the river and road network that connects the north to the rest of the continent. The population is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), with a significant community of elves involved in the management of the forest and trade between the principality and the elven communities of the deep woodland. The agricultural surplus of the river valleys is transported southward along the rivers, and the grain of the northern valleys is a valued contribution to the food supply of southern cities.

The river trade of the Northern Marches is conducted on flat-bottomed barges built at the boatyards of Ashford. The barges carry timber, grain, and other products of the principality to the markets of the central grasslands and the coast. The return cargo includes manufactured goods, metalwork, and other products imported from the south. This trade is a vital connection between the frontier and the more settled regions of the continent. The merchants who manage the river trade are organised into companies that operate the barges and warehouses, and these companies are among the most prosperous institutions in the principality.

## Balance of Wilderness and Settlement

The Northern Marches is the frontier principality of the northern forests, and its management of the boundary between settled land and forest is a critical function that has sustained the northern frontier for centuries. The principality's trade in timber and forest products is a significant component of the economy of Aethelar, and its co-operation with the elves of the forest is a model of accommodation between peoples characteristic of the northern frontier. The significance of the Northern Marches lies in its frontier role and its capacity for co-operation with the forest peoples. The principality symbolises the balance between settlement and wilderness that defines the northern reaches of the continent, and its endurance is a testament to the wisdom of its founders, who established a political order that has preserved the peace of the frontier through centuries of growth and change. The [Memory Houses](/content/government/local/houses-of-memory) of the Shield and Song, quiet establishments of the song-half of the calling, are found in the larger market towns of the marches, where the singers maintain the local songs and traditions of the frontier communities.

The system of forest management developed in the Northern Marches, under which sections of the woodland are rotated between selective logging and regeneration in cycles of several decades, has maintained the health of the forest while providing a steady supply of timber for construction and shipbuilding. The principality's treaty with the elven communities, renewed every seven years at the Midsummer Compact ceremony in Eldergrove, specifies the boundaries within which human activity is permitted and establishes quotas for game and timber that are reviewed annually. The ranger corps maintains a network of patrol cabins and observation posts along the forest margin, and each ranger is responsible for a designated section of the frontier, keeping records of game movements, signs of fire, and any incursions from the deeper woodland. The principality's laws regarding poaching and illegal logging are enforced by the rangers with the authority to confiscate tools and game and to bring offenders before the frontier courts.
