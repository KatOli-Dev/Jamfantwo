## Overview

The Holds of [Stoneheim](/content/location/settlement/city/stoneheim) is the greatest [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) realm in [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), encompassing the underground cities, mining settlements, and mountain territories inhabited and governed by the dwarven people of the continent. The realm is the largest and most powerful dwarven political entity in the known world, and its capital is Stoneheim, the greatest dwarven hall of Morvind. The Stoneheim Holds are the foundation of dwarven civilisation in Morvind, and their mineral wealth and craftsmanship are the basis of the continent's prosperity and reputation.

## The Territory and the Holds

The territory of the Stoneheim Holds extends across the interior ranges of Morvind, encompassing the vast underground networks that are the home of the dwarven people. The realm includes Stoneheim, the capital and greatest city, and [Khazgar](/content/location/settlement/city/khazgar), the city renowned for its metalwork, as well as [Ironhollow](/content/location/settlement/town/ironhollow), the industrial settlement that is the primary source of iron feeding the forges of the realm. The holds are the individual dwarven communities that constitute the realm, and each hold is a self-governing entity that manages its own affairs while contributing to collective defence and commerce.

The [geography](/content/geography) of the interior ranges determines the distribution of the dwarven population. The holds are situated where veins of ore and natural cave systems provide the resources and space that the dwarven way of life requires. The deepest and most extensive cave systems are those of the central interior, where Stoneheim and Khazgar are situated, and the eastern ranges, where Ironhollow is located, are the richest in iron ore.

## Mining and Metalwork

The mineral wealth of the Stoneheim Holds is the greatest of any political entity in the known world. Ore extracted from the mines includes iron, copper, tin, silver, gold, and rarer metals found only in the deepest veins of the Morvind ranges. The metalwork produced in the forges of Stoneheim and Khazgar is the finest in the known world. Weapons, tools, and decorative objects produced by the dwarven smiths are exported to every continent. Iron produced in the smelteries of Ironhollow feeds the forges of the realm, and the quantity and quality of Ironhollow output is unmatched by any other source in Morvind.

The smiths of Stoneheim are renowned for the quality of their work, and techniques employed in the forges of the capital have been developed and refined over centuries. Weapons produced in the forges of Stoneheim are among the most prized in the known world. Swords, axes, and armour bearing the mark of Stoneheim smiths are sought by warriors and rulers of every continent. Decorative metalwork produced in the forges of Khazgar is likewise renowned, and the jewellery, ceremonial objects, and ornamental ironwork produced by Khazgar smiths are among the most valued products of the dwarven craft tradition.

Trade connecting the Stoneheim Holds to the rest of Morvind and the wider world is managed through overland routes connecting the interior to the coast. Metalwork and stone are transported overland to the ports of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), where they are loaded onto vessels for shipment to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond. The return cargo includes grain, cloth, and manufactured goods that the dwarven communities cannot produce for themselves. The trade routes connecting the interior to the coast are maintained by the realm, and their security is a constant concern of the administration, for the wild peoples of the mountains are a recurring threat to caravans travelling the routes.

## Governance and the King

The Stoneheim Holds are governed by a king chosen from the hereditary ruling family of Stoneheim, and the king is the supreme authority of the realm. The court is situated in the great hall of Stoneheim, and the council of hold-elders that advises the king is drawn from the leaders of individual holds throughout the realm. The administration is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee mining operations, maintenance of halls and roads, and administration of justice. The governance of the Stoneheim Holds is the most stable of any political entity in Morvind, and the dwarven tradition of hereditary rule and council governance has sustained the realm for many centuries.

The king of Stoneheim is advised by the council of hold-elders on all matters of significance, and decisions are made in consultation with the council. The council is composed of elders chosen by individual holds to represent their interests at court, and the elders are among the most respected and experienced members of the dwarven community. The council meets in the great hall of Stoneheim, and proceedings are conducted according to traditions and protocols established over the centuries of the realm's history.

## History

The Stoneheim Holds were established in the early centuries of dwarven settlement of Morvind, and the founding of the realm is attributed to dwarven explorers who discovered the vast cave systems and rich mineral deposits of the interior ranges. The first hold was Stoneheim itself, established in the largest and most extensive cave system, where the great hall was carved from the living rock and the foundations of the realm were laid. The other holds were established in the centuries that followed, as the dwarven population expanded and mineral deposits of the interior were discovered and developed.

The realm has endured for many centuries, and the governance of the kings and councils has been a factor in its stability and prosperity. The Stoneheim Holds have maintained good relations with [Nordhaven](/content/government/national/nordhaven) on the coast, and trade between the interior and the coast has been a significant component of the economy of the realm. The [Frostwall Clans](/content/government/national/frostwall-clans) of the deep interior are allies, and defence of the interior ranges is a shared responsibility.

## Significance

The Stoneheim Holds is the greatest dwarven realm in the known world, and its mineral wealth and craftsmanship are the foundation of the economy and reputation of Morvind. The realm's trade connects the interior to the coast and the wider world, and its metalwork is among the most valued products in the known world. The significance of the Stoneheim Holds lies in its scale and quality, for it is the largest and most productive dwarven political entity, and its long history and stable governance are a model of the political order that has sustained the dwarven people for centuries.

The cultural influence of the Stoneheim Holds extends throughout the dwarven world. The architecture, craft traditions, and governance of the realm have been adopted and adapted by other dwarven peoples of Morvind and the wider world. The realm is the centre of dwarven civilisation, and the great hall of Stoneheim is the symbol of dwarven achievement in building and maintaining a secure and prosperous society in the depths of the earth.

The forges of Stoneheim operate continuously, with shifts of smiths working around the clock in a system of production that has been refined over a thousand years. The ventilation shafts that supply air to the deepest forges are among the marvels of dwarven engineering, drawing fresh air through carved channels that rise hundreds of feet to the mountain surface. The hold-elders maintain a code of mining law that governs the rights to mineral veins, the sharing of water drainage, and the procedure for settling boundary disputes between holds. The high road that connects Stoneheim to the Stormfall ports is paved with stone flags fitted without mortar, a dwarven technique that allows the road to shift with the freezing and thawing of the ground without buckling, and it is maintained by gangs of workers from the holds through whose territory it passes.
