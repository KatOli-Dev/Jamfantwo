## Overview

The Kingdom of Nordhaven is the human kingdom of the Morvind coast, encompassing the coastal lowlands and the settlements that line the shores of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The kingdom is the primary human political entity on the continent, and its capital is [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven), the largest port and the hub of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) trade. The Kingdom of Nordhaven is a maritime power, and its prosperity is built upon the trade that flows through its harbours, connecting the mineral wealth and the metalwork of the Morvind interior to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and beyond.

## The Coast and the Trade

The coastal lowlands of Morvind are narrow, seldom extending more than a few miles inland before the mountains rise, but the shore is lined with the settlements that are the lifeblood of the kingdom. The trade that flows through the harbours of the kingdom is dominated by the exchange of Morvind and Aethelaran goods, and the mineral wealth and the crafted metalwork of the interior are exported through the ports of the kingdom in exchange for the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods of the south. The kingdom's revenue is drawn chiefly from the tolls and the taxes on this trade, and the wealth that accumulates in the treasury funds the maintenance of the fleet, the construction of public works, and the defence of the coast.

The fleet of Nordhaven is the primary instrument of the kingdom's power, and it is among the largest and the most capable in the northern waters. The fleet protects the trade routes of the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea) and the coast, and it is the guardian of the kingdom's maritime interests. The shipyards of [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) and [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) are the primary sources of the vessels that serve the fleet, and the maritime tradition of the kingdom is the foundation of its military and its commercial power.

## The Member Settlements

The Kingdom of Nordhaven includes the principal settlements of Morvind's Stormfall coast and maintains ties to more remote northern ports. [Nordhaven](/content/location/settlement/city/nordhaven) is the capital and the largest city, and its harbour is the primary hub of the continental trade. [Skaldshavn](/content/location/settlement/town/skaldshavn) is a valued southern member, known for its fishing fleet and its boatbuilders, and [Bjornsholm](/content/location/settlement/town/bjornsholm) commands the trade with the [ursine](/content/species/sapient/ursines) communities of the inland forests. [Vargvik](/content/location/settlement/town/vargvik) is the kingdom's northernmost member, linked to the capital by a long coastal passage and largely self-governing because of its remoteness.

The kingdom is governed from Nordhaven by a king who is chosen from the hereditary ruling family of the city, and the court is the centre of the political and the social life of the kingdom. The administration is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the collection of taxes, the maintenance of the fleet and the fortifications, and the administration of justice. The individual settlements retain a measure of self-governance, and the local councils that administer the towns and the cities are drawn from the prominent families and the merchant communities of each settlement.

The relationship between the crown and the member settlements is one of mutual benefit, for the crown provides the fleet, the defence, and the commercial framework that sustains the trade, and the settlements provide the revenue, the manpower, and the resources that sustain the kingdom. The kings of Nordhaven have traditionally pursued a policy of consultation and accommodation with the member settlements, and the stability of the kingdom has been a factor in the prosperity of the coast.

## The People and the Defence

The population of the Kingdom of Nordhaven is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the coast are known for their hardihood and their maritime skills. The [dwarves](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) of the interior are present in the coastal cities, where they maintain agents to manage the flow of metalwork through the ports, and their counting houses are among the most substantial buildings on the waterfronts. The [lupines](/content/species/sapient/lupines) of the cold steppes are occasional visitors to the coastal settlements, and the trade between the coast and the steppe is a minor but valued part of the kingdom's commerce.

The defence of the kingdom is maintained by the fleet and by a standing army that is funded by the treasury, and the fortifications of the coastal cities are maintained in good order. The kingdom has been at peace with its neighbours for many generations, but the occasional tensions with the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/government/national/holds-of-stoneheim) over the trade between the coast and the interior require the maintenance of a credible military force. The fleet is the primary instrument of the kingdom's power, and the shipyards of Nordhaven and Skaldshavn produce the vessels that serve both the fleet and the merchant marine, and the maritime tradition of the kingdom is the foundation of its military and its commercial strength.

## History

The Kingdom of Nordhaven was established by the human settlers who colonised the Morvind coast in the early centuries of the continental settlement. The first settlements were the fishing villages and the trading posts that were established along the shore, and the growth of the trade with the interior and the southern continents provided the foundation for the expansion of the kingdom. The unification of the coastal settlements under the rule of the kings of Nordhaven was a gradual process that was achieved through a combination of negotiation, alliance, and the occasional exercise of force, and the kingdom that emerged from this process has been the primary human political entity on the continent for many centuries.

The relationship between the kingdom and the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/government/national/holds-of-stoneheim) has been a defining feature of the history of the coast, for the trade between the coast and the interior is the foundation of the economy of both entities, and the terms of the trade have been a subject of negotiation and occasional dispute throughout the history of the kingdom. The kingdom has maintained a policy of co-operation with the dwarven realm, and the trade routes that connect the interior to the coast have been maintained and protected by both parties, for the mutual benefit of the trade is a powerful incentive for co-operation.

## Significance

The Kingdom of Nordhaven is the primary human political entity in Morvind, and its maritime power and its commercial influence are the foundation of its significance. The kingdom's trade connects the mineral wealth of the Morvind interior to the markets of Aethelar and beyond, and its fleet is the guardian of the northern trade routes. The significance of the Kingdom of Nordhaven lies in its maritime tradition and its commercial power, and the kingdom is a symbol of the hardihood and the enterprise of the human people of the Morvind coast.

The cultural influence of the kingdom extends throughout the Morvind coast and the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea), for the maritime traditions, the shipbuilding techniques, and the commercial practices of the kingdom have been adopted and adapted by the other settlements of the coast. The kingdom is a centre of the human civilisation of Morvind, and the port of Nordhaven is a meeting point of the peoples and the cultures of the continent and the wider world.
