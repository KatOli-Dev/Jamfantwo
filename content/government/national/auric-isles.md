## Overview

The Auric Isles is a confederation of island communities of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean), encompassing the settlements on the volcanic islands and remote landmasses that rise from the deep waters of the central and eastern Auric. The confederation is not a kingdom but a commercial and cultural alliance. Its member communities retain individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, navigation, and mutual support. The Auric Isles is the primary political entity of the ocean, and its collective power in maritime trade is a significant component of the economy of the known world.

## The Member Islands

The Auric Isles includes the principal island settlements of the Auric Ocean. [Tidesmeet](/content/location/settlement/village/tidesmeet) is the largest and most influential member, and its harbour is the primary refuge and resupply point for vessels travelling the Auric trade routes. [Windward](/content/location/settlement/village/windward) is the most remote member, situated on a distant island of the eastern Auric, and its participation is nominal because of its distance from the other islands. The smaller islands of the chain, though not individually named in continental records, are full members of the confederation and are represented in the council.

The confederation is governed by a council of representatives from each member island. The council meets regularly at Tidesmeet to co-ordinate trade, navigation, and mutual support. Its decisions are binding on all members, and the confederation has the power to levy contributions, negotiate treaties, and dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the confederation is a model of co-operation among isolated communities, and the council is the primary institution of the island peoples.

The council maintains a registry of member islands and their communities, the official record of the confederation and its membership. Representatives who serve on the council are chosen by individual communities, and the methods of selection vary from island to island, reflecting the diversity of traditions and governance among the members. Council sessions are occasions of ceremony and social gathering. Representatives who travel to Tidesmeet carry the news and goods of their islands, and the sessions provide a valued opportunity for exchange of information and renewal of the ties that bind the island communities together.

## Trade and the Ocean

The trade of the Auric Isles is built upon the position of the islands astride the trade routes of the Auric Ocean. The goods that flow through the harbours of the confederation include the spices and dyes of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), the grain and metalwork of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the products of the islands themselves, which include tropical fruits, shells, and other materials found only in the island communities. The confederation co-ordinates maritime trade, managing lighthouses, pilotage services, and navigation aids essential to the safety and efficiency of the ocean routes.

The [merfolk](/content/species/sapient/merfolk) of the Auric waters are regular contacts of the island communities, and the relationship between the islanders and the merfolk is one of cautious but peaceful coexistence, for the two peoples share the same waters and the same dependence on the sea. The [cyclopes](/content/species/sapient/cyclopes) of the remote islands are occasional contacts, and the relationship is distant but respectful, for the cyclopes are solitary and the islanders are few.

## Defence and the People

The defence of the Auric Isles is maintained by a collective fleet funded by contributions of the member islands. The fleet protects the trade routes from pirates and raiders. The population of the confederation is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the islanders are known for their seamanship and boatbuilding. Their vessels are a common sight in the ports of both Aethelar and Sahrana. The [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) of the warm coasts and islands are full members of the confederation, and their participation is a valued feature of the alliance.

The collective fleet consists of vessels contributed by member islands, crewed by experienced mariners of the island communities. The council co-ordinates the fleet, and its operations are directed against pirates and raiders who prey on the trade routes. The fleet also provides assistance to vessels in distress, and its rescue operations are a valued and recognised part of the confederation's function. The reputation of the fleet is a significant factor in the security of ocean trade, and the presence of confederation vessels on the trade routes deters raiders who might otherwise threaten island commerce.

## History

The Auric Isles was established as a confederation in the early centuries of ocean trade, when the island communities recognised that the common challenges of the sea and trade required a common response. The founding charter was agreed by representatives of the original member islands, and the principles of co-operation, mutual support, and collective defence established at that time have been the foundation of the alliance ever since. The confederation has been expanded on several occasions as new islands were discovered and settled, and the council has been reformed to accommodate the growing number of members.

The history of the confederation has been marked by recurring challenges from pirates and raiders who prey on the trade routes. The collective fleet has been dispatched on numerous occasions to suppress these threats. The most serious such threat was a confederation of raiders based on a remote island of the central Auric, and the campaign conducted by the confederation's fleet against them is commemorated in the traditions of the island communities and the records of the council.

## Significance

The Auric Isles is the primary political entity of the Auric Ocean, and its collective power in maritime trade is a significant component of the economy of the known world. The confederation's co-operation among isolated island communities is a model of the political order that has sustained the island peoples for many centuries. Its regulation of ocean trade and navigation aids ensures the safety and efficiency of routes that connect the continents. The significance of the Auric Isles lies in its capacity for collective action and its role as guardian of the ocean trade.

The confederation symbolises the capacity of the island peoples to sustain themselves and co-operate in the vast and demanding environment of the open ocean. The alliance is a testament to the values of seamanship, independence, and mutual support that are the hallmark of the people of the Auric. The confederation is a critical component of the economy of the known world, and the trade that flows through the harbours of the member islands connects the continents and islands in a network of commerce and communication vital to the prosperity of the world.

The navigational knowledge accumulated by the island peoples over centuries of voyaging is codified in the council's pilot books, which contain detailed charts of currents, winds, reefs, and safe anchorages across the entire Auric Ocean. These books are among the most valuable possessions of the confederation, and access to them is restricted to member communities and trusted allies. The council also maintains a system of signal stations on the headlands of the larger islands, where trained lookouts watch for vessels in distress and relay warning beacons along the chain. The island boatbuilders have developed hull forms and rigging adapted to the long swells and sudden squalls of the open ocean, and the double-canoe designs used for the longest voyages are capable of carrying provisions for months at sea, enabling the confederation to maintain contact with even the most distant members.
