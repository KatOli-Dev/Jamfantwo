## Overview

The League of the Northern Coast is the alliance of the Sahrani coastal city-states, united by their common interest in the maritime trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and their shared dependence on the sea for their prosperity. The league is not a kingdom but a commercial and a defensive alliance, and its member cities retain their individual governance while co-operating on matters of trade, navigation, and defence. The League of the Northern Coast is the primary political entity of the Sahrani coast, and its collective power in the maritime trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) is among the greatest of any political entity in the known world.

## The Member Cities

The League of the Northern Coast includes the principal city-states of the Sahrani coast, and the membership is drawn from the settlements that share in the Auric trade. [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar) is the largest and the most influential member of the league, and its [Council of Khalisar](/content/government/local/council-of-khalisar) is the primary political institution of the city. [Amurra](/content/location/settlement/city/amurra) is a valued member, renowned for its spice trade, and [Taretha](/content/location/settlement/city/taretha) is a significant member, known for its libraries and its scholarship. [Drahima](/content/location/settlement/city/drahima) is the centre of the dye trade, and [Nessara](/content/location/settlement/city/nessara) is the northernmost member, marking the edge of the fertile strip. [Qassir](/content/location/settlement/city/qassir) is the easternmost member, and its trade with the islands of the Auric is a valued component of the league's commerce.

The league is governed by a council of representatives from each member city, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the trade, the defence, and the navigation of the Sahrani coast. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the league has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective fleet in times of need. The governance of the league is a model of co-operation among independent city-states, and the council is among the most effective political institutions in Sahrana. The council meets in the city of [Khalisar](/content/location/settlement/city/khalisar), which serves as the de facto capital of the league, and the council hall is a building that is maintained jointly by the contributions of all the member cities.

The membership of the league has changed over the centuries, and the council has the authority to admit new members and to expel those that fail to meet their obligations. The criteria for membership are based on the contribution that a city can make to the collective trade and defence of the coast, and the admission of a new member requires the assent of a majority of the existing members. The league has been stable for many generations, and the current membership has remained unchanged for over a century, a testament to the effectiveness of the arrangement and the prosperity that it has brought to the member cities.

## Trade and Navigation

The trade of the League of the Northern Coast is the collective commerce of its member cities, and the goods that flow through the harbours of the league include the spices, the dyes, the precious stones, and the other products of Sahrana, which are exported to the markets of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind). The league co-ordinates the maritime trade of the Sahrani coast, managing the lighthouses, the pilotage services, and the navigation aids that are essential to the safety and the efficiency of the trade routes. The league also maintains a collective fleet that protects the trade routes from pirates and raiders, and the fleet is funded by the contributions of the member cities.

The league's trade with the [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities, is a vital component of the Sahrani economy, for the goods that are brought by the caravans from the interior are the primary exports of the coast, and the league's co-ordination of the coastal trade ensures that the goods flow efficiently between the coast and the interior.

## Defence and the People

The defence of the Sahrani coast is maintained by the collective fleet of the league and by the individual fortifications of the member cities, and the league's military power is a significant force in the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean). The population of the league is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the people of the coast are among the most cosmopolitan and the most prosperous in Sahrana, for the trade that flows through the harbours of the league brings peoples and goods from every continent. The collective fleet is commanded by an admiral who is appointed by the council, and the admiral serves for a term of three years, after which a new commander is chosen from a different member city to ensure that no single city dominates the naval command.

The fleet is composed of vessels that are contributed by the member cities in proportion to their share of the trade, and the vessels are crewed by the sailors of the contributing cities. The fleet patrols the trade routes of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) and the coast, and its primary function is the suppression of the piracy that has been a recurring problem in the waters off the Sahrani coast. The fleet also serves as a deterrent against any larger threat, and the reputation of the league's naval power is sufficient to discourage all but the most determined adversaries.

## Significance

The League of the Northern Coast is the primary political entity of the Sahrani coast, and its collective power in the maritime trade of the [Auric Ocean](/content/location/natural/feature/auric-ocean) is the foundation of the economy of the northern coast and the continent as a whole. The league's co-operation among independent city-states is a model of the kind of political order that has sustained the Sahrani coast for many centuries, and its effective governance and its commercial power are recognised throughout the known world. The significance of the league lies in its capacity for collective action and its control of the most valuable trade routes in the southern world, and the league is a symbol of the wealth and the refinement of the Sahrani civilisation. The endurance of the league is a testament to the wisdom of its founders, who established an institution that has served the common interest of the Sahrani coast through centuries of change and challenge.
