## Overview

The Southern Reach is a political entity of the far southern reaches of [Sahrana](/content/location/natural/continent/sahrana), encompassing the fertile highlands and the inland seas that lie beyond the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh). Unlike the city-states of the northern coast, the Southern Reach is a federation of settled communities that have grown up around the inland waters and the temperate uplands of the deep south, and its existence has been confirmed by the expeditions and the oral traditions that have reached the northern world. The Southern Reach is the most remote of the known political entities, and its distance from the northern coast and the continents beyond has preserved its isolation while allowing a limited trade to flow between the south and the north through the caravan routes of the Kharesh.

## The Far South

The far south of Sahrana is a region that lies beyond the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and its geography is radically different from the desert that precedes it. As one travels south through the deep desert, the dunes gradually give way to rocky plateaus, and the climate becomes cooler and more temperate, until the land rises into a range of highlands that are the gateway to the Southern Reach. The highlands are the first sign of the change, for their summits are clothed in vegetation and their valleys are watered by streams that flow from the interior, and the contrast with the desert is abrupt and striking.

Beyond the highlands, the land descends into a broad basin that is dominated by two inland seas, the Greater and the Lesser, which are the central features of the Southern Reach. The inland seas are bodies of fresh water that are fed by the rivers of the surrounding highlands, and their shores are the most fertile and the most densely settled lands in the far south. The climate of the basin is mild and temperate, with warm summers and cool winters, and the rainfall is sufficient to sustain agriculture without irrigation in most years. The vegetation is a mosaic of open woodland, grassland, and cultivated fields, and the landscape is a patchwork of farms, orchards, and the towns and villages of the Southern Reach.

The inland seas are navigable, and the communities that dot their shores are connected by a network of boat routes that are the primary means of travel and trade within the Southern Reach. The boats are shallow-draught vessels that are well suited to the calm waters of the inland seas, and the boatmen of the Southern Reach are skilled in the navigation of the waters and the routes that connect the settlements. The seas are rich in fish, and the fishing fleets of the lakeside towns are a significant part of the economy of the Southern Reach.

## The Federation and the Governance

The Southern Reach is a federation of the settled communities that inhabit the shores of the inland seas and the surrounding highlands, and its governance is based on a council of representatives from each community. The council meets at [Vereth](/content/location/settlement/town/vereth), the largest of the Southern Reach settlements, which is situated on the shore of the Greater Inland Sea, and the decisions of the council are made by consensus. The federation is not a kingdom, for no single ruler holds authority over the whole, but the council co-ordinates the management of the water resources, the defence of the basin, and the trade that flows between the south and the north.

The governance of the Southern Reach is built upon the traditions of the settled communities that constitute it, and each community is self-governing in its internal affairs, with its own council or elders that administer the local matters. The federation is the instrument of co-operation between the communities, and its authority is limited to the matters that concern the whole: the management of the inland seas and their fisheries, the maintenance of the caravan routes that connect the south to the Kharesh, the regulation of the trade that flows through the routes, and the defence of the basin against the threats that may arise from the desert or the surrounding wilds.

The council of the Southern Reach is composed of representatives who are chosen by their communities according to their own traditions, and the representatives are drawn from the elders, the merchants, and the craftsmen of each settlement. The council meets twice each year, once in the spring and once in the autumn, and the sessions are the principal occasions for the conduct of the federation's business. The decisions of the council are recorded in writing, and the archives of the Southern Reach are maintained at Vereth, where the records of the council and the history of the federation are preserved.

## The Trade and the Caravans

The trade that flows between the Southern Reach and the northern world is carried by the caravans that cross the [Kharesh](/content/location/natural/feature/kharesh), and the caravan routes are the only link between the south and the north. The caravans that travel the southern routes are guided by the [Kharesh Tribes](/content/government/national/kharesh-tribes), whose knowledge of the desert extends to the edges of the southern highlands, and the journey from the northern coast to the Southern Reach is the longest and the most demanding of any caravan route in Sahrana. The caravans carry the goods of the south, including the wool and the leather of the highland herds, the preserved fish of the inland seas, and the medicinal herbs that grow in the temperate uplands, and they bring back the grain, the cloth, the metalwork, and the manufactured goods that the south cannot produce for itself.

The trade between the Southern Reach and the north is small in volume but high in value, for the goods of the south are rare and prized in the northern markets, and the goods of the north are equally prized in the south. The [Vashari Pact](/content/government/national/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities at the edge of the Kharesh, is the primary intermediary in this trade, for the caravans that travel the southern routes pass through the Vashari cities on their way to and from the coast. The relationship between the Southern Reach and the Vashari Pact is one of mutual benefit, for the trade that flows through the caravan routes is a source of wealth for both, and the co-operation between the two entities is the foundation of the commerce between the south and the north.

## The Peoples

The population of the Southern Reach is chiefly [human](/content/species/sapient/humans), and the humans of the far south are distinct in appearance from their northern kin, being taller and more slender, with darker skin and dark hair that is adapted to the sun of the southern latitudes. Their civilisation is ancient, though its history is poorly known to the northern peoples, and their traditions of agriculture, craftsmanship, and governance are well developed and self-sufficient. The humans of the Southern Reach are settled farmers and fishermen, and their communities are the most permanent and the most prosperous of any in the far south.

A small population of [tortolans](/content/species/sapient/tortolans) is found on the shores of the inland seas, where the warm, sheltered waters and the mild climate provide an environment that suits their long-lived and deliberate nature. The tortolans of the Southern Reach are few, but their knowledge of the waters and their long perspective on the history of the basin are valued by the human communities, and the relationship between the two peoples is one of quiet co-operation.

The [salawa](/content/species/sapient/salawa) of the deep desert are the most frequent visitors to the Southern Reach from the north, and their oral traditions contain the most detailed accounts of the far south that have reached the northern world. The salawa who travel to the edges of the southern highlands are the intermediaries between the Southern Reach and the Kharesh Tribes, and their role in the trade and the communication between the south and the north is a significant one. The salawa do not settle in the Southern Reach, for their way of life is bound to the desert, but their visits are a regular and a valued feature of the life of the southern communities.

## The Settlements

The Southern Reach is home to several settled communities, of which the three principal settlements are [Vereth](/content/location/settlement/town/vereth), [Ashaar](/content/location/settlement/town/ashaar), and [Torem](/content/location/settlement/town/torem). Vereth is the largest and the seat of the council of the federation, situated on the shore of the Greater Inland Sea. Ashaar is the southernmost of the settlements, situated on the shore of the Lesser Inland Sea, and it is the centre of the fishing and the agriculture of the southern basin. Torem is the gateway to the Southern Reach, situated in the highlands at the head of the pass that descends from the Kharesh, and it is the first settlement that the caravans from the north encounter upon entering the Southern Reach.

## Significance

The Southern Reach is the most remote of the known political entities, and its significance lies in its existence as a settled, organised civilisation in the far south of Sahrana, beyond the desert that has long been regarded as the boundary of the known world. The federation's trade with the northern world through the caravan routes of the Kharesh is a small but valued component of the economy of Sahrana, and the goods of the south are among the rarest and most prized in the markets of the north. The significance of the Southern Reach lies in its demonstration that the world is larger and more various than the records of the northern peoples can convey, and the federation is a symbol of the endurance and the adaptability of the settled peoples who have made their home in the most remote corner of the known world.
