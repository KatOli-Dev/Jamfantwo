## Overview

The Plains Confederation is an alliance of the central grassland cities and the pastoral peoples of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), united by their common interest in the management of the grasslands and the trade that flows across them. The confederation is not a kingdom but a co-operative association, and its members include both the settled cities of the plains and the nomadic [centaur](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) kindreds that range across the open grass. The Plains Confederation is the primary political entity of the central grasslands, and its influence extends across the full extent of the plains, from the foothills of the mountain spine to the river valleys of the east.

## The Member Cities and the Centaurs

The Plains Confederation includes the principal cities of the central grasslands, and the membership is drawn from the settlements that share in the agricultural and the pastoral economy of the plains. [Avaros](/content/location/settlement/city/avaros) is the largest and the most influential member of the confederation, and its granaries and its market are the primary institutions of the grassland economy. [Goldmeadow](/content/location/settlement/city/goldmeadow) is a valued member, commanding the meeting of river and road at the centre of the plains, and [Stagford](/content/location/settlement/city/stagford) is a significant member, controlling the primary river crossing of the central grasslands. [Sunmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sunmere) is the largest city of the southern plains, and [Dustford](/content/location/settlement/town/dustford) and [Redhollow](/content/location/settlement/town/redhollow) are the frontier members, marking the southern edge of the confederation's territory.

The [centaur](/content/species/sapient/centaurs) kindreds of the grasslands are full members of the confederation, and their participation is a distinctive and a valued feature of the alliance. The centaurs are represented in the council by the elders of their kindreds, and their knowledge of the plains and their management of the grazing lands are a critical component of the confederation's governance. The relationship between the settled cities and the nomadic centaurs is one of mutual benefit, for the cities provide the market and the manufactured goods that the centaurs need, and the centaurs provide the wool, the leather, and the management of the grazing that the cities depend upon.

The centaur kindreds that are members of the confederation include the principal kindreds of the central grasslands, and the elders of each kindred are the representatives who sit on the council. The kindreds are nomadic, and their movements across the plains are governed by the seasons and the availability of the grazing, but the elders gather for the council meetings at the designated meeting places that are situated at the crossroads of the grassland routes. The meeting places are marked by the stone circles that the centaurs have built at the traditional gathering points, and the stone circles are the most distinctive monuments of the plains.

## Governance and the Council

The Plains Confederation is governed by a council of representatives from each member city and each centaur kindred, and the council meets regularly to co-ordinate the trade, the agriculture, and the defence of the grasslands. The decisions of the council are binding on all members, and the confederation has the power to levy contributions, to negotiate treaties, and to dispatch a collective force in times of need. The governance of the confederation is a model of co-operation between settled and nomadic peoples, and the council is among the most inclusive political institutions in Aethelar.

The council meets three times each year, and the meetings are held at the meeting places that are situated at the crossroads of the grassland routes. The spring council is the most important, for it is the occasion at which the plans for the coming season are made and the arrangements for the grazing and the planting are agreed. The summer council is a shorter affair, concerned with the management of the trade and the resolution of any disputes that have arisen during the season. The autumn council is the occasion at which the harvest is assessed, the contributions are levied, and the preparations for the winter are co-ordinated.

## Trade and the Grasslands

The trade of the Plains Confederation is dominated by the agricultural products of the grasslands, and the grain, the wool, and the leather that are produced on the plains are exported to the markets of the coast and the interior through the river and the road network that connects the confederation to the rest of the continent. The confederation co-ordinates the management of the grazing lands and the agricultural land, ensuring that the plains are used sustainably and that the interests of the settled and the nomadic peoples are balanced. The confederation also maintains the roads and the bridges that are the arteries of the grassland trade, and the tolls on these routes are a significant source of revenue.

## History and the Foundation

The founding of the Plains Confederation is dated to the year 340, when the cities of the central grasslands and the centaur kindreds of the plains first agreed to the co-operative management of the grasslands and the trade that flows across them. The founding of the confederation was a response to the pressures that had arisen from the unregulated expansion of the settled agriculture and the unmanaged grazing of the nomadic herds, and the agreement that established the confederation was a compromise that balanced the interests of the settled and the nomadic peoples. The founding council was composed of the representatives of the principal cities and the elders of the principal kindreds, and the council has met regularly since that first gathering.

The history of the confederation is a history of gradual expansion, for the alliance has grown over the centuries as additional cities and kindreds have joined the council. The admission of [Sunmere](/content/location/settlement/city/sunmere) in the year 412 was a significant event, for the inclusion of the largest city of the southern plains extended the influence of the confederation to the full extent of the grasslands. The admission of the frontier members, [Dustford](/content/location/settlement/town/dustford) and [Redhollow](/content/location/settlement/town/redhollow), in the following century extended the territory of the confederation to the southern edge of the plains, and the confederation now encompasses the full extent of the central grasslands from the foothills of the mountain spine to the river valleys of the east.

## Significance

The Plains Confederation is the primary political entity of the central grasslands, and its co-operation between the settled cities and the nomadic centaurs is a model of the kind of accommodation that has shaped the culture and the economy of the plains. The confederation's agricultural wealth and its management of the grasslands are a significant component of the economy of Aethelar, and its trade connects the interior of the continent to the markets of the coast. The significance of the Plains Confederation lies in its inclusiveness and its capacity for co-operation between peoples of different ways of life, and the confederation is a symbol of the balance between settled and nomadic peoples that is characteristic of the central grasslands.

## The Roads and the Trade Routes

The roads and the bridges that the confederation maintains are the arteries of the grassland trade, and the network of routes that connects the member cities and the centaur kindreds is the most extensive and the best-maintained of any in the interior of Aethelar. The roads are built and maintained by the contributions of the member cities and the labour of the centaur kindreds, and the tolls that are collected on the routes are a significant source of revenue for the confederation. The roads are broad and well-paved, for the traffic that uses them is heavy and constant, and the caravans that travel the routes carry the grain, the wool, and the leather that are the primary products of the plains to the markets of the coast and the interior.

The trade routes of the confederation connect the member cities to each other and to the markets of the surrounding regions, and the network of routes is the foundation of the economy of the central grasslands. The eastward routes connect the confederation to the cities of the eastern coast, the southward routes connect to the frontier members and the regions beyond, and the westward routes connect to the mountain passes and the regions beyond the mountain spine. The city of [Emberfall](/content/location/settlement/city/emberfall) is the primary hub of the southern routes, and the trade that flows through the junction at Emberfall is a significant component of the commerce of the confederation.
