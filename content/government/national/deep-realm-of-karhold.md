## Overview

The Deep Realm of [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) is the [dwarven](/content/species/sapient/dwarves) kingdom of the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), encompassing the underground cities, the mining settlements, and the highland territories that are inhabited and governed by the dwarven people of the continent. The realm is the oldest and the most extensive dwarven political entity in Aethelar, and its capital is [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold), the greatest dwarven city of the mountain spine. The Deep Realm is a significant power in the politics and the economy of Aethelar, and its mineral wealth and its craftsmanship are the foundation of its influence.

## The Mountain Spine and the Territory

The territory of the Deep Realm extends along the full length of the mountain spine of Aethelar, from the northern peaks where the range begins to the southern foothills where it subsides into the plains. The realm encompasses the underground cities and the mining settlements that are carved into the rock of the mountains, and the highland territories that are claimed and managed by the dwarven communities. The [geography](/content/geography) of the mountain spine determines the distribution of the dwarven population, and the settlements of the realm are situated where the veins of ore and the natural cave systems provide the resources and the space that the dwarven way of life requires.

The realm includes [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold), the capital and the greatest city, and [Deepreach](/content/location/settlement/town/deepreach), the mining settlement of the central highlands, as well as the smaller communities that are scattered throughout the range. The [Highgate](/content/location/settlement/city/highgate) pass, though governed independently, lies within the territory of the realm, and the relationship between the Deep Realm and the city of Highgate is one of mutual respect and co-operation, for the pass is the primary route of trade between the two coasts and the dwarven cities are the primary beneficiaries of the traffic that flows through it.

## Mining and Metalwork

The mineral wealth of the mountain spine is the foundation of the Deep Realm's economy, and the ore that is extracted from the mines of the realm includes iron, copper, tin, silver, gold, and the precious stones that are among the most valued products of the continent. The metalwork that is produced in the forges of Karhold and the other cities of the realm is noted for its quality and its durability, and the weapons, the tools, and the decorative objects that are produced by the dwarven smiths are exported to every continent. The reputation of the Deep Realm's metalwork is the foundation of its commercial power, and the smiths of the realm are among the most skilled and the most respected craftsmen in the known world.

The trade that connects the Deep Realm to the rest of Aethelar is managed through the overland routes that cross the mountain spine, and the metalwork and the stone of the realm are traded to the lowland cities and the coast in exchange for the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the dwarven communities cannot produce for themselves. The [Brackenford](/content/location/settlement/town/brackenford) trade is the primary point of exchange between the Deep Realm and the western interior, and the Highgate pass is the primary route to the eastern coast.

## Governance and the King

The Deep Realm is governed by a king who is chosen from the hereditary ruling family of Karhold, and the king is the supreme authority of the realm. The court is situated in the great hall of Karhold, and the council of elders that advises the king is drawn from the leaders of the dwarven communities throughout the range. The administration of the realm is managed through a network of officials and local councils that oversee the mining operations, the maintenance of the halls and the roads, and the administration of justice. The governance of the Deep Realm is the oldest and the most stable of any political entity in Aethelar, and the dwarven tradition of hereditary rule and council governance has sustained the realm for many centuries.

The king of the Deep Realm holds the title of Deep King, a designation that distinguishes the ruler of the underground realm from the kings of the surface nations. The Deep King's authority extends to all the dwarven communities of the mountain spine, from the smallest mining camp to the great cities of [Karhold](/content/location/settlement/city/karhold) and [Deepreach](/content/location/settlement/town/deepreach), and the loyalty of the communities is sustained by the tradition of council governance that ensures the voice of each community is heard at the court. The succession of the Deep King is determined by heredity, but the confirmation of the successor requires the assent of the council of elders, a safeguard that has prevented the accession of rulers who lacked the confidence of the realm.

## History

The Deep Realm of Karhold traces its origins to the earliest dwarven settlements in the mountain spine of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), which were established during the first expansion of the dwarven people into the continent. The discovery of rich veins of ore in the mountains drew the dwarven prospectors deeper into the interior, and the cities that grew around the mines became the foundation of the realm. The unification of the cities under a single king is recorded in the dwarven traditions as the Compact of the Deep, an agreement among the leaders of the communities to accept the authority of a single ruler in exchange for the protection and the co-ordination that a unified realm could provide.

The history of the Deep Realm has been marked by a long and a remarkable stability, for the dwarven tradition of hereditary rule and council governance has proved to be a resilient and an effective system of government. The realm has faced challenges from time to time, including the periodic incursions of ores and goblins from the deeper caves and the remote highlands, and the occasional disputes with the human settlements of the lowlands over the access to the mountain passes and the trade routes. These challenges have been met with the characteristic dwarven combination of military preparedness and diplomatic prudence, and the realm has endured without a major disruption to its governance or its prosperity.

## Relations with Neighbours

The Deep Realm maintains a complex network of relationships with the other political entities of [Aethelar](/content/location/natural/continent/aethelar), and the dwarven kingdom is a significant power in the politics and the economy of the continent. The realm's primary relationship is with the human kingdoms of the lowlands, to which the metalwork and the stone of the realm are traded in exchange for the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that the dwarven communities cannot produce for themselves. The [Brackenford](/content/location/settlement/town/brackenford) trade is the primary point of exchange between the Deep Realm and the western interior, and the Highgate pass is the primary route to the eastern coast.

The realm also maintains a relationship with the [Holds of Stoneheim](/content/government/national/holds-of-stoneheim) of [Morvind](/content/location/natural/continent/morvind), the other great dwarven political entity of the known world, and the two realms are connected by the trade that flows across the [Stormfall Sea](/content/location/natural/feature/stormfall-sea). The relationship between the two realms is one of mutual respect and commercial co-operation, and the exchange of metalwork and techniques between the dwarven smiths of the two continents is a valued component of the trade. The Deep Realm also maintains contact with the [Sylvan Court](/content/government/local/sylvan-court), the elven governance of the northern forests, and the relationship between the two entities is one of cautious coexistence, for the dwarves and the elves have different claims upon the mountain and the forest, but the boundary between the two territories is managed by agreement and has not been a source of conflict.

## Significance

The Deep Realm of Karhold is the primary dwarven political entity in Aethelar, and its mineral wealth and its craftsmanship are a significant component of the economy of the continent. The realm's trade connects the mountain spine to the lowlands and the coast, and its metalwork and its stone are valued commodities in every market. The significance of the Deep Realm lies in its endurance and its stability, for the dwarven kingdom is the oldest continuous political entity in Aethelar, and its long history and its stable governance are a model of the political order that has sustained the dwarven people for centuries.
