## Origins

The origins of the goliaths are a matter of speculation, for the goliath body is plainly shaped by the highest peaks of the world, and their affinity for thin air and cold is the defining feature of their kind. Some naturalists have proposed a kinship between goliaths and [trolls](/content/trolls), citing the great size and the mountain habitat shared by both peoples, though the goliaths are far more specialised for altitude and far less broadly built. Others have argued for a connection to [giants](/content/giants), pointing to the enormous stature and the remote, high-altitude range that both share. The goliaths themselves preserve oral traditions that speak of their having been born from the stone of the highest peaks, and they regard the summits as their rightful home.

What is certain is that goliaths have inhabited the highest reaches of the mountain ranges of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/morvind) for as long as any people can account for. Their remains are found at altitudes that no other sapient people can tolerate, and the evidence suggests that the goliath body has been shaped by the thin air and the cold of the highest peaks over a very long period.

## Anatomy and Physiology

Goliaths are a large people, standing taller than [orcs](/content/orcs) and approaching the height of [trolls](/content/trolls), though they are leaner and more narrowly built than either. Their frames are tall and angular, with long limbs and a deep, broad chest that houses lungs of exceptional capacity. The ribcage is wider than that of any other people, and the lungs are larger and more efficient, adapted to extract oxygen from air that is too thin for [humans](/content/humans) or [dwarves](/content/dwarves) to breathe comfortably. This adaptation is the defining feature of the goliath body, for it allows them to live and labour at altitudes where no other sapient people can endure for long.

Goliath musculature is lean and built for endurance rather than explosive strength, suited to sustained effort in conditions where the air is thin and the body must work harder for every breath. Their bones are dense and strong, heavier than those of a human of comparable build, and their joints are large and well reinforced, for the goliath body must withstand the rigours of a high-altitude environment where the cold is intense and the terrain is steep and rocky. Their skin is thick and weathered, ranging from greyish to dark tan, and it is adapted to the intense ultraviolet light of the high peaks, tolerating exposure that would burn a lowland people.

Goliath senses are adequate but undistinguished. Their vision is keen at distance, adapted to the open vistas and the bright light of the high peaks, though they are less capable in low light than the cave-dwelling peoples. Their hearing is competent, and their sense of smell is moderate. The goliath body is not specialised by sense but by resilience, for the capacity to endure thin air, intense cold, and high altitude is the quality that sets the goliaths apart from all other peoples.

## Life Cycle

Goliaths are long-lived, with a typical lifespan of one hundred to one hundred and fifty years under favourable conditions. They mature slowly, reaching full stature and physical adulthood by their late twenties, and they retain their vigour well into their second century. Goliath women carry their young for approximately twelve months before giving birth, and births are invariably single. The infant is large and well-developed, though it requires many years of care before it is fully independent, and the thin air of the high peaks means that the young grow slowly, their bodies gradually adapting to the altitude over the first decade of life.

Goliath populations are very small and grow at a glacial pace, for the high-altitude environment can support only a limited number of individuals, and births are infrequent. The low rate of reproduction is balanced by the longevity of adults and by the remarkably low rate of death among those who have reached maturity, for a fully grown goliath has few natural predators and is well adapted to the conditions that would threaten a lesser people. Goliath communities are small and widely dispersed, separated by the vast distances and the difficult terrain of the high peaks.

## Habitat and Distribution

Goliaths are found in the highest reaches of the mountain ranges of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar) and [Morvind](/content/morvind), at altitudes above the snowline where the air is thin and the cold is intense. They favour the high plateaus, the ridges, and the upper valleys of the great ranges, where the terrain is open and the views are vast, and their settlements are typically situated in locations that are inaccessible to any people less adapted to the altitude. The [geography](/content/geography) of each range determines the distribution of goliath populations, and they are found only where the mountains rise high enough to provide the conditions their bodies require.

In Aethelar, goliaths inhabit the highest peaks of the mountain spine, above the [dwarven](/content/dwarves) settlements and the [troll](/content/trolls) ranges. In Morvind, they are present on the tallest summits of the interior ranges, in places where even the dwarves do not venture. Goliaths are absent from the lowlands, the forests, and the coasts, for their bodies are too specialised for altitude to tolerate the dense air and the warm temperatures of the low country, and they experience discomfort and illness when they descend below the elevations to which they are adapted.

## Diet

The goliath diet is limited by the scarcity of food at high altitudes, and goliaths are opportunistic omnivores who make use of whatever the upper reaches of the mountains can provide. The staple is meat, provided by the hunting of the hardy mammals that share the high peaks, including mountain goats, hares, and the large birds of the uplands. Goliaths are patient and methodical hunters, capable of sustained pursuit over difficult terrain, and their great stamina serves them well in the chase.

Plant foods are scarce at the altitudes where goliaths dwell, but the alpine meadows that bloom during the brief summer provide a variety of roots, herbs, and berries that are gathered and consumed fresh or dried for storage. Goliaths do not practise agriculture, for the growing season at their altitudes is too short and the terrain too steep for cultivation, and they rely entirely on what the high country provides. Their metabolism is efficient and adapted to a sparse diet, and a goliath can sustain long periods of exertion on relatively little food, a necessity in an environment where nourishment is never abundant.

## Ecology

Goliaths occupy the niche of a large-bodied, high-altitude omnivore in the extreme environments of the highest peaks. Their impact on their surroundings is minimal, for their numbers are very small and their range is vast, and the goliath presence in an area is often detectable only through the faint traces of their passing. The goliaths and the high-altitude ecosystem exist in a state of equilibrium that has persisted for centuries, the goliaths taking what they need and the mountain providing what it can.

Goliaths have almost no contact with other sapient peoples, owing to the extreme altitude and remoteness of their habitat. [Trolls](/content/trolls) and goliaths occasionally share the same mountain ranges, but the two peoples separate by elevation, with trolls below the snowline and goliaths above it, and encounters between them are rare. [Dwarves](/content/dwarves) are aware of the goliaths from the upper reaches of their own ranges, but the two peoples have little interaction, for the dwarves cannot tolerate the altitudes where the goliaths dwell.
