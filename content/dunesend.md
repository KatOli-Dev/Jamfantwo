## Overview

Dunesend is a caravan stop at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh), situated where the great caravan routes emerge from the desert and meet the foothills of the Sahrani interior. The settlement is the last outpost before the deep desert, and its function is to serve the caravans that prepare to enter the Kharesh or that have just emerged from it. Dunesend is small and purpose-built, and its prosperity is built upon the services it provides to the caravan trade, including provisioning, shelter, and the exchange of goods between the desert routes and the inland cities.

## The Caravan Stop

Dunesend stands at the point where the foothills of the interior highlands give way to the sands of the Kharesh, and the settlement is the last reliable source of water and provisions before the caravans enter the deep desert. The water that sustains the settlement is drawn from a well that is fed by the same aquifers that supply the oases of the interior, and the water is managed carefully, for it must sustain the community and the caravans that pass through. The caravans that stop at Dunesend are preparing for the crossing of the Kharesh, and the provisioning that they receive here is the last they will obtain before they reach the oases of the deep desert.

The caravans that arrive at Dunesend from the desert are weary and in need of rest, and the settlement provides shelter, water, and food for the travellers and their animals before they continue to the inland cities. The inns and the stables of Dunesend are the primary institutions of the settlement, and the services that they provide are the foundation of the local economy. The caravans that stop at Dunesend are the primary contact between the settlement and the wider world, and the trade that flows through the settlement is the lifeline of the community.

## Trade and the Routes

The trade of Dunesend is modest, for the settlement is small and its production is limited, but the services that it provides to the caravans are the foundation of its economy. The tolls and the fees that are levied on the caravans are a significant source of income, and the sale of provisions, water, and shelter to the travellers is the primary commerce of the settlement. The goods that pass through Dunesend include the spices, the dyes, and the precious stones of the interior, which are carried by the caravans from the oases and the upland settlements to the markets of the coast, and the grain, the cloth, and the manufactured goods that are carried in the opposite direction.

The [salawa](/content/salawa) of the desert are the most frequent visitors to Dunesend, and the relationship between the settlement and the nomadic salawa is one of mutual dependence, for the salawa provide the knowledge of the desert that the caravans require, and the settlement provides the water and the provisions that the salawa need. The salawa are welcomed at the settlement, and their presence is a familiar and a valued part of the life of the community.

## The Settlement

Dunesend is a small, compact settlement, and its buildings are constructed of the stone of the foothills and the sun-baked clay that is made from the desert earth, with thick walls and small windows that provide insulation against the heat and the wind. The streets are broad and dusty, for the caravans that pass through the settlement require room for their animals and their wagons, and the market square is the centre of the community, where the caravans gather to trade and to rest. The population of Dunesend is small and chiefly [human](/content/humans), and the people are known for their hospitality and their skill in the management of the caravan trade.

## Significance

Dunesend is the primary caravan stop at the edge of the [Kharesh](/content/kharesh), and its services are a vital resource for the caravans that cross the desert. The settlement is a dependency of the [Vashari Pact](/content/vashari-pact), the alliance of the inland caravan cities, and its role in the caravan trade is a critical component of the economy of the desert interior. The significance of Dunesend lies in its position at the edge of the desert, where it serves as the last outpost before the deep Kharesh and the first refuge for those who emerge from it, and the settlement is a testament to the endurance and the hospitality of the people who live at the desert's edge.
