## Overview

Eldergrove is a northern city of [Aethelar](/content/aethelar), situated at the edge of the ancient forests that cover the northern reaches of the continent. The city stands where the forest meets the open country of the river valleys, and its position at the boundary of the woodland and the cultivated land has made it a meeting point of the settled and the forest peoples. Eldergrove is among the older cities of Aethelar, and its growth has been shaped by the proximity of the great forest, which has provided timber, game, and the materials of a woodland economy for centuries.

## The Forest and the City

The ancient forests that lie to the north of Eldergrove are among the oldest and most extensive woodlands in the known world, and their canopy is so thick that the forest floor is in perpetual shade. The [elves](/content/elves) who inhabit the deepest portions of the forest have a long and complex relationship with the city, and the two peoples have coexisted for centuries, with the elves maintaining the forest and the humans maintaining the clearing and the settlement. The boundary between the forest and the city is not a sharp line but a gradual transition, and the outermost districts of Eldergrove are built among the trees, their houses set in gardens that merge with the woodland.

The timber of the ancient forest is the primary resource that has sustained Eldergrove, and the logging operations of the city are carefully managed, for the forest is valued not only for its wood but for the game, the fungi, and the other products that it provides. The loggers of Eldergrove are skilled in the selection and the felling of trees, and they work in consultation with the elves, who guide the management of the forest and ensure that the cutting does not damage the broader woodland.

## Trade and Industry

The trade of Eldergrove is dominated by the products of the forest, including timber, bark, resin, and the various fungi and herbs that are gathered from the woodland floor. The timber is exported to the southern cities and the coast, where the great trees of the northern forest are prized for their straight grain and their durability, and the bark and the resin are used in the tanning and the sealing that are essential to the construction and the shipbuilding of the continent. The game of the forest, including deer, boar, and the smaller animals, is a secondary export, and the smoked and salted meat of Eldergrove is a valued commodity in the markets of the south.

The city is also a centre for the woodworking that is the natural industry of a forest settlement, and the carpenters, the carvers, and the cabinet-makers of Eldergrove are among the most skilled in Aethelar. The furniture and the carved objects of the city are noted for their quality and their beauty, and they are exported far and wide.

## The City

Eldergrove is a well-built city, and its architecture reflects the woodland setting, with timber-framed buildings that are clad in the dark wood of the forest, their roofs pitched steeply to shed the snow and the rain, and their windows fitted with shutters that are closed against the winter cold. The streets are broad and lined with trees, and the city has the character of a settlement that has grown in harmony with the forest rather than against it. The population is chiefly [human](/content/humans), with a significant community of [elves](/content/elves) who are involved in the management of the forest and the trade between the city and the woodland.

## Significance

Eldergrove is the primary gateway between the settled lands of Aethelar and the ancient forests of the north, and its trade in timber and forest products is a significant component of the economy of the continent. The city is the most important settlement of the [Northern Marches](/content/northern-marches), and its position at the edge of the forest gives it a strategic importance that is disproportionate to its size. The relationship between the city and the elves of the forest is a model of coexistence, and the careful management of the woodland that the two peoples have developed over centuries is a source of prosperity for both.
