## 2026-07-28

Category index pages now show short descriptions alongside each entry. The mobile navigation menu properly hides on small screens, and the search field works even when JavaScript is unavailable.

## 2026-07-27

Site search added — browse or search the full archive from any page. New culture pages covering festivals and celebrations, and new history pages on treaties and alliances. Duplicate religion pages removed, prose streamlined across all content, and cross-references added throughout. Roadmap added.

## 2026-07-26

New sections added: art, law, military, mythology, and nature (twenty flora pages, eighteen fauna pages). Economy expanded with banking, currency, guilds, prices, and trade. Governance expanded across nations. A prejudices page added and limifelids introduced as a sapient species. Religion restructured under its own category. Existing culture, history, location, magic, and people pages expanded. Anachronistic and modern terminology removed from all pages.

## 2026-07-25

Period-appropriate prejudices woven into background across thirty pages. All content copy-edited for concision. Undersized pages expanded and the outpost page rewritten. Wikipedia-like prose style adopted.

## 2026-07-22

Cross-references now link on first mention.

## 2026-07-21

Fifteen spoken-language pages added (Aethelari, Ashari, Frostic, Karathi, Kameni, Khari, Kharoshi, Morvindi, Nordhal, Quessan, Reachi, Sahrani, Stormic, Thessari, Vashari). Deity, feature, and institution pages added, and the content taxonomy extended. World content expanded broadly.

## 2026-07-19

The homepage now lists categories with links to each section. Navigation menus improved. Site styles refined for typography, accessibility, and readability.

## 2026-07-14 — Initial

Project started with Jekyll. First content covering geography, species, and world overview. Style guide and table of contents added. Minimal working design established.
