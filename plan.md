## Assignments

This is a living document which details incomplete or planned work. Completed assignments are removed from this file entirely. In the below sub-sections, if there are any, you can find which tasks need to be completed.

### Language Content

The `content/language/` folder and its `pseudo/` subfolder hold no pages. Create language entries for the tongues spoken across the world, to include at least one pseudo language: systems of meaning that fall outside ordinary speech, such as the expression of emotion through gesture, sign, and bearing.

### People Content

The `content/people/` folder and its `historical/` subfolder remain unwritten. Add pages for notable living individuals and for figures of the past whose deeds still shape the present age.

### Non-Sapient Species

The `content/species/` folder contains only `sapient/`. Compose entries for the beasts, monsters, and other non-sapient creatures that share the world's lands and seas.

### Location Content for Sparse Folders

Several location subfolders are near or wholly empty. The `settlement/region` folder holds no pages, `settlement/outpost` holds but one, and `natural/ecosystem` and `route/trade` stand empty. Populate each with fitting entries that round out the world's geography.
